A locked-off shot from the same early-2000s television studio recording. The man shakes the thin tree hard with one hand, twice. The banknotes come off the twigs and drop out of the bottom of the frame. He looks back at the table and says: Yes. He lets go of the trunk. The banknotes are old British pound notes. Nobody else is in the studio and nobody reacts. Audio: only his voice, close and dry, and the faint hum of the empty room. No music.
