A locked-off shot from the same early-2000s television studio recording. The man breathes, blinks and reads aloud from the card in his hand. He says: From Barnsley, a coal miner, killed at the Somme aged nineteen. He lifts his eyes from the card to someone on the right of the frame and holds the look. The banknotes on the tree are old British pound notes and they hang still. Nobody else is in the studio and nobody reacts. Audio: only his voice, close and dry, and the faint hum of the empty room. No music.
