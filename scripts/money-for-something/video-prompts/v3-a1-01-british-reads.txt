A locked-off shot from the same early-2000s television studio recording. The man breathes, blinks and reads aloud from the card, slowly, one finger moving under the words. The sleeves of his tunic are plain khaki cloth. He says: To make Britain a fit country for heroes to live in. He lowers the card, looks at someone out of frame to the right and says: Go on, then. The banknotes on the tree are old British pound notes and they hang still. Nobody else is in the studio and nobody reacts. Audio: only his voice, close and dry, and the faint hum of the empty room. No music.
