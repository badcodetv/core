A locked-off shot from the same early-2000s television studio recording. The man breathes, blinks and leans forward and speaks to someone just to the right of the lens. He says: Spoken for by what? He waits with his eyebrows up. The banknotes on the tree are old British pound notes and they hang still. Nobody else is in the studio and nobody reacts.
