A locked-off shot from the same early-2000s television studio recording. The man breathes, blinks and reads from the card in his hand, then looks up to the right of the frame. He says: Keynes. Probably. Nobody can find where he said it. He turns the card over, looks at its blank back and shrugs. The banknotes on the tree are old British pound notes and they hang still. Nobody else is in the studio and nobody reacts. Audio: only his voice, close and dry, and the faint hum of the empty room. No music.
