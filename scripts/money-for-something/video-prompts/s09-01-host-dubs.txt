A locked-off shot from the same early-2000s television studio recording. The man breathes, blinks and looks past the lens as if watching a screen. He says: Shake. Nobody else is in the studio and nobody reacts. Audio: only his voice, close and dry, and the faint hum of the empty room. No music.
