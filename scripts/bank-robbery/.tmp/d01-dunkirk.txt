A 1940 war photograph at Dunkirk, taken from the waterline. The camera sits half a metre above grey-green surf, water droplets on the lens. Foreground, sharp: a young British soldier in soaked khaki battledress and a dented steel helmet drags an exhausted mate by the webbing towards us through waist-deep water, face screwed up with effort, mouth open, shouting. Midground: lines of soldiers wading out to a small wooden fishing boat with a rust-red sail. Background: a long beach under black oil smoke, a burning ship, a dive bomber dropping out of a white sky. Look: bleach-bypass colour, drained except the khaki, the rust sail and an orange fire glow; hard film grain, every droplet and grain of sand sharp. Nobody is wounded. 16:9. Thanks.
