A back room with bare brick walls. An old cast-iron printing press runs in the middle, a ribbon of paper already feeding through it, sheets of new banknotes stacking up in its tray. Around a bare table beside it the five men from the character references, no masks, relaxed: one pours whisky, two clink glasses, one leans on the press. Camera low on the floor in the corner, the press's iron wheel huge and out of focus in the right foreground. Light: one warm bare bulb overhead, and a green glow from a fire-exit sign.
