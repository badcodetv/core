Night, a bare brick back room. Close and low, at the level of the steel feed board of a big old printing press: a heavy cut-glass tumbler of amber whisky stands on the scuffed steel, a smear of purple ink beside it, rings trembling on the whisky's surface. Behind, out of focus, the dark bulk of the press and a blur of pale paper sheets. One hard white work lamp in a wire cage lights it from the right; everything else falls to near black. Fingerprints and dust on the glass. Unretouched 35mm film still, rich saturated colour, fine grain, a little underexposed. 16:9. Thanks.
