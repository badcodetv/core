From high up between the stone columns of a grand old bank at dusk, looking down at the street in front of it. Two crowds of ordinary people press against two lines of steel barriers, facing each other across an empty strip of wet tarmac where a thin line of police in yellow jackets stands with their backs to the bank. Every face is turned to the other crowd; nobody looks up at the bank. Foreground: the edge of a stone column, out of focus. Light: orange sodium street lamps and the white glare of a police floodlight from the left. No flags, no coloured smoke, only a few handwritten cardboard signs too small to read. Unretouched 35mm film still, rich saturated colour, fine grain. 16:9. Thanks.
