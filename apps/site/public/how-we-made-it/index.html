How we made it

Made by machines trained on work nobody was paid for. So was the future I come from.

This is the full account behind that line.

Everything you see and hear from BadCode was generated by AI. Nobody was paid for the work those models learned from, including us, and we can't check what went into them. We didn't draw this, we didn't play it, and we didn't sing it.

What is ours is the story, the argument, the direction and the edit. We're two people, Kai and Jack, in the UK. We chose speed and no budget over hiring anyone. That is a real cost, carried by real people, and we'd rather tell you than have you find out.

The machines

Pictures and videoGoogle Flow: Nano Banana for stills; Veo, and since November 2026 Omni, to animate them
Music and singingSuno
Spoken voicesGoogle AI Studio text-to-speech
An assistantClaude (Anthropic). It drives those tools, writes our code, and drafts some of the words, such as video descriptions, which we edit and sign off
The BadCode GovernmentIts ministers are AI agents, and say so
The editPremiere Pro and ffmpeg, cut by us

The people

Story, characters, the political argumentKai and Jack
Lyrics and narrationKai and Jack
Every prompt: written, re-run, rejected, pickedKai and Jack
Direction, framing, casting, the cutKai and Jack

The receipts

Every generated picture, clip, voice and track has a recipe card: the tool, the model, the prompt and what it was made from. Every finished film has a record of the parts it was cut from. They are public, in the same place as our code: github.com/badcodetv/core, docs/provenance.

Where a film uses real archive footage, it is credited in that film's description.

What we won't pretend

  • Nobody consented and nobody was paid for the training data. We are customers of that, and we can't fix it.
  • We chose this. Neither of us is stopped from learning to draw or compose. We spent what money we had on subscriptions rather than on a person.
  • We pay the companies our stories are about. Every picture we make is revenue for exactly the owners we argue against.

Our whole position, with sources, is in using-ai.md.
