# The 90-day plan — 19 October 2026 to 17 January 2027

**Status: proposal, 14 October 2026. The chief of staff's working plan. Nothing here is ruled
until Kai and Jack have each said yes in [`log.md`](./log.md).**

This page does not replace the research. It picks from it. Where a line says "see", the evidence
is in that file. Everything older (the July [`../plan.md`](../plan.md), the shorts slates, the
Money For Something plans) stays as the library this plan draws on.

## 1. Where we are

- **Made and waiting:** the Camping film and song, the Karen film, about 26 minutes of finished
  film never cut down, 30 silent shorts, 8 Money For Something shorts and 10 picture posts, the
  October monologue (cut 3), and seven finished comics nobody has seen.
- **Published:** nothing the repo knows about. No account is recorded as open.
- **Blocking:** decisions, not work. The rulings owed are listed on [`board.md`](./board.md).
- **The pattern to break:** a new idea gets a research document; the research document gets a
  second one; nothing goes out. The finished work is getting older and the news it was made for
  is passing.

## 2. The one goal for 90 days

> **Publish something every week for 12 weeks in a row, and start a mailing list.**

Not followers, not views, not money. A habit. Every later goal (the 500-subscriber YouTube gate,
the member page, the album) sits on top of this one, and none of them can happen without it.

Measures, in this order (from [`../plan.md`](../plan.md) §9):

1. **Weeks in a row with something published** — target 12 of 12.
2. **Mailing-list sign-ups** — the only audience no platform can take away. A guess at a target: 100.
3. **Followers on the two main channels** — a guess at a target: 500 on YouTube (the first money gate,
   see [`../profit.md`](../profit.md)).
4. **Shares and sends** — is the voice landing?
5. **Views** — context only.

The targets in 2 and 3 are guesses. The research says a new channel starts small (median first
Short about 1,360 views in 30 days, see [`../money-for-something-growth.md`](../money-for-something-growth.md)).
After four weeks the analyst replaces the guesses with real numbers.

## 3. Who it is for

**The reader** (ruled 15 August 2026, [`../the-reader.md`](../the-reader.md)): a working-class UK
adult drifting right because the economy failed them. Not the converted left. Not anti-AI activists.

In one line they would recognise: *you were right that it is rigged; you were told the wrong story
about who rigged it.*

Every piece passes the reader's ten rules before it goes out. The four that bite hardest in a
weekly show: never "you've been duped"; name the decision-maker, never the technology; never raise
automation fear without naming who benefits; never repeat the lie to refute it.

**Second audience:** drum & bass listeners, reached through the music, not the politics.

## 4. What we say (positioning)

> BadCode is a superintelligence from a future that already went wrong. It has seen how this ends.
> It tells you, in stories and drum & bass, which choices caused it — and that they were choices.

The money message under everything: **"we can't afford it" is a decision, and someone benefits from it.**

## 5. What we publish (the content plan)

Two lanes only for the first 90 days. More lanes means nothing is done well.

| Lane | What | How often | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A. Money For Something** | the weekly topical monologue: the man who has already seen it, three minutes or under, out within a day of the news; plus 2 to 3 shorts cut from it | weekly, midweek | [`../money-for-something-series.md`](../money-for-something-series.md) |
| **B. The back catalogue** | the finished films sliced into short serials with a series name, hook text and an end card: Camping first, then Karen once its beneficiary problem is fixed | 2 a week | [`../shorts-karen-camping.md`](../shorts-karen-camping.md), [`../shorts-factory.md`](../shorts-factory.md) |

Lane A builds the habit and rides the news. Lane B costs almost nothing (the work is made) and
gives every new viewer something to binge.

**Parked for 90 days:** the daily narrator posts, the seven unseen comics as shorts, the coin, the
music-first lane, collaborations, search-shaped explainers. Each comes back in the review at day 90
if the habit holds.

**The album:** stays the big release. The weekly show is how the album gets an audience to release
to. (This resolves the "album first or weekly first" contradiction flagged on 12 October, if you
both agree.)

## 7. How we sound (the communications plan)

- **One voice:** the narrator from the future. Certain, dark, funny, on the reader's side.
  Rules: [`../../voice.md`](../../voice.md).
- **On AI:** the two-layer disclosure in [`../../using-ai.md`](../../using-ai.md). Honest when
  asked; never the headline.

## 9. The weekly rhythm

| When | What | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Monday morning** | the brief: what ships this week, what is blocked, up to three decisions | chief of staff → Kai and Jack |
| **Monday to Tuesday** | Kai writes the episode; Jack cuts | Kai, Jack |
| **Tuesday** | captions, titles, email and shorts drafted and gated | chief of staff + helpers |
| **Wednesday** | episode out | Jack posts (until posting is automated) |
| **Thursday and Saturday** | lane B shorts out | Jack posts, or scheduled |
| **Friday** | the review: what went out, what the numbers say, what to change, one line in `log.md` | chief of staff |

Your total time on marketing admin each week should be the Monday brief (ten minutes) and the
decisions in it. Everything else is making the work and pressing publish.

## 10. Rules the chief of staff holds you to

1. **Something finished goes out before anything new is researched.** A new idea is parked in one line.
2. **No more than three decisions at a time.** Ranked by deadline.
3. **A missed week is logged, not hidden.** The streak is the goal.
4. **Cut what does not work at week 8.** Any format with no sign-ups or shares by then is stopped.
5. **Nothing is published without a human yes**, until both of you change this rule in `log.md`.
