## 2. Six formats — pick a lane, name it, repeat it

Research is blunt on this: channels that break out pair AI production with **a consistent
character and a real point of view**, and faceless Shorts with a consistent voiceover retain
**~58% better** than stock-footage-plus-narration. Meanwhile YouTube is actively demoting
mass-produced AI content. So: the narrator is the product, and volume without a lane is the
thing being punished.

| | Format | Shape | Length | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ① | **DISPATCH** | The museum-from-the-future voice over panels: *"Exhibit four. They called it a shortage."* The [BadCode Future History](../ideas/badcode-future-history.md) idea, now with art | 20–40s | one caption |
| ② | **ONE PANEL** | A single panel, slow push in, one narrator line, ident tail. The cheapest thing we can make and the most on-brand | 10–20s | minutes |
| ③ | **THE BIT** | A self-contained comic beat lifted whole from Karen or Camping | 20–45s | a cut |
| ④ | **COLD OPEN** | 30–50s of a film that ends **mid-sentence**, pinned comment to the whole thing. The actual funnel | 30–50s | a cut |
| ⑤ | **ARTEFACT** | One object, one caption. Posts as image or 4s loop. The only tier that can react to news the same day | 3–8s | minutes |
| ⑥ | 🔑 **STYLE SWAP** | The same ten seconds of story told twice, in two of our seven registers, cut back to back. *"Same story. Which one did you believe?"* | 15–25s | a cut |

## 3. Thirty short ideas

> ### ✅ All thirty are cut — `animation/shorts/batch-1/`, 2026-09-18
>
> 30 files · 1080×1920 · BT.709 limited-range tagged · 6m47s total · 10–28s each · 190 MB.
> 28 rendered from stills with [`scripts/shorts/panel.sh`](../../scripts/shorts/panel.sh),
> 2 cut from film with [`cut.sh`](../../scripts/shorts/cut.sh).

> **Every one is built on the house two-step** ([`../voice.md`](../voice.md)): the **sarcastic
> hook** lands at 0s and carries the diagnosis; the **straight payoff** arrives at 55% and names

> diagnoses and the care re-aims, and the beneficiary is always a person, never "technology".
>
> 🔴 **They are silent.** Measured −70 LUFS. Nothing on the drive is an instrumental bed and
> nobody has recorded narration for these sets, so batch 1 ships as picture-and-type. That is
> the one open decision — §5.
>
> ⚠️ **Panel numbers are not file numbers.** `fast food` has no `9.png` and does have a

> panel before this was caught. `Fire sale` (`7.1`, `15.1`) and `Out Of The Jungle` (`3a`, `4a`)
> shift too. **Always eyeball the frame after rendering** — the filename will lie to you.

## 5. Where the ideas came from, and what's unproven

**Verified 2026 research behind §2:**
- ✅ Faceless channels that break out pair AI production with **a consistent character and a real
  point of view**; generic AI-narration-over-stock is being demonetised. Our narrator is the
  entire moat.
- ✅ Shorts with a consistent voiceover retain **~58% better** than stock-plus-narration.
- ✅ Animation lets political argument land that live action makes people flinch at — it reads
  as *this is play*, not *this is a lecture*. Every one of these seven sets is animation.
- ◻ AI-made dystopian **worldbuilding lore** series (e.g. @gossipgoblin) are a proven 2026
  format — a consistent invented world, one dispatch at a time. That is ① DISPATCH exactly,
  and the Storyverse/GPOM canon is far deeper than anything those channels invented.
- ◻ AI videos imagining a dystopian **future Britain** have gone viral repeatedly. We have the
  canon, the voice and the art for that and have never posted one.

**Open:**
- 🔴 **Audio is the one real gap.** Batch 1 is silent. Three ways out, cheapest first: a low
  bed pulled from our own tracks (free, instant, but nobody has picked the section — and it
  cannot be picked by ear here, there is no `GEMINI_API_KEY` so `listen_describe` will not
  run); a narrator VO through AI Studio ([`../ai-studio/`](../ai-studio/README.md), the first
  engine that sounded human on take one); or leave them mute and let the type carry it.
- ⬜ **A still cannot hold 30–60s**, which is where the research says Shorts perform. These run
  10–28s deliberately — a single image stretched to 45s would tank the completion rate that
  actually drives distribution. Multi-panel DISPATCH cuts are how we reach the good band.
- ⬜ **Five of the seven sets have no canon file** — `general ai`, `Lights, camera`,
  `Biometric Steal` and `Scripted` are not in [`../ideas/`](../ideas/README.md) at all. They
  should be parked with `new-idea` before anything ships, or we will lose what they were about.
- ⬜ **Panel order is inferred from filenames**, not from a recorded storyboard. Check with Jack
  before cutting a multi-panel sequence — 7.1 and 15.1 in `Fire sale` suggest inserts.
- ⬜ **`Styles/` inside `Out Of The Jungle` holds 111 more images** (characters, references).
  Unreviewed.
- ⬜ **The 300-clip ident bank** in `badcode logo designs/` is still only being used for tails.
  There is probably a whole ⑤ ARTEFACT series in there.
