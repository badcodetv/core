  ignores most of it and grabs keywords. One story's rule (e.g. "35mm film") leaks into every story, which is why
  everything looks the same. Kai: "Kai's made a s*** mop, and for nine months we've cleaned the floor with it."

The plan:
1. **Audit first.** Ask Claude what its base context is when it does a task (what loads every time, and from where),
   so both of them know what's on the map before anything is removed.
2. **Archive everything, lose nothing.** Move the current context into an archive (working name "BadCode messy
   context") so the slate is empty but the value is kept.
3. **Rebuild `CLAUDE.md` small.** Only what is true of every task: the functional stuff (how the repo runs, where
   things live) and the few global rules: there's a logo; the point is to propose solutions, not just complain;
   democracy means you can change how government works, not just vote for the next dickhead; there are more
   possibilities than materialism.

   you're working on a story, read its `badcode.md`." Songs live one level below their story.
5. **A library** (not called "skills"): generic material that might serve more than one story, e.g. the 35mm look,
   a cartoon look. Bank Robbery story and Bank Robbery music video are separate top-level stories that may share

6. **Jack's rule: each new project asks what goes in its context.** When a story or short starts, Claude proposes
   which library pieces to inject; Jack and Kai pick. The story keeps its own copy, so changing it never touches the
   library ("a tool shed").
