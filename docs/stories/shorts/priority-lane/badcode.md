## What it is
A BadCode Short, vertical 9:16, ~20-26 s. A dying man waits so that a minister's convoy doesn't have to; never said
out loud, the beneficiary is on screen. Beats: inside the ambulance at ER pace ("BP 60 over 30… we're gonna lose
him!") → his SAVE OUR NHS poster seen sideways out of the ambulance's left window, held ~3 s → the cordon: police hold
the junction, radio "Constable 97, junction blocked, ready for VIP" → held at the light, an outrider's raised glove →
the minister crosses in the back of his car on the phone: "Hey Jeffrey, what time's the plane to the island? ... I'm on
my way to fetch Andrew." → the monitor flatlines, black, the BadCode logo. State: rough cut r5 on the timeline page,
awaiting Jack's notes; the earlier agreed cut r4 is in Premiere (`shorts-1-priority-lane-r4`). Resume at RESUME.md.

## Look
**In use; look.md calls it EXPLORING.** Night in the rain, like a real 35 mm film photo taken from the roadside, not a
CGI render: soaking wet tarmac with long red tail-light reflections, sodium-orange street lamps blooming in fog, cold
teal-green shadows, real grain, soft focus, slightly underexposed and handheld; blue emergency lights cut through it.
Inside the ambulance the light is harsher: cold strip lights and the monitor's green glow against the blue outside.
- Reference: `reference/look-ref-rainy-night-traffic.png` (match light, grain, colour, not the place).
- **Method (Jack):** every still is built on a real Unsplash photo attached as a reference: "Keep the real layout,
  perspective and lighting of the reference photo; [then what to add or change]". Shot facts first, under ~200 words.
- Junction and traffic obey UK keep-left geometry: `cleanroom/r18/geometry.md`.
- Picks in the r5 cut: LS18-b, PB18-a, CD18-a, HD19-b, BM18-a, FL18-a. Stills Nano Banana 2; clips Omni 1.2 Flash,
  9:16, Frames, 8 s. Flow project `57538600-142a-45b8-ac4c-e79e663de0c6`.
- Never: glossy, clean, daylight or "cinematic CGI". No readable text except the poster's SAVE OUR NHS.

## Story-only rules
- No speech in any Flow clip. The paramedics wear surgical masks, so no mouths to sync.
- The minister is a **Boris-alike**: invented, unmistakable look, never named, never his real face (poster too).
- 🔴 The "Jeffrey… Andrew" line needs the marketing-gatekeeper legal check before release.
- Swearing is said in full, never bleeped; if an engine refuses a word, swap the word.
- The logo at the end is the official file laid on in the edit, never drawn by Flow. No balaclava in this cut.
- No early car shot and no poster-to-car dissolve.
