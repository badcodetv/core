# Facts about the job

- A 20-second comedy short for YouTube Shorts / TikTok / Reels: **vertical 9:16**.
- Each shot is made in two steps in Google Flow: a still with **Nano Banana 2**, later animated by an image-to-video model. This round makes the stills only.
- The person making it pastes your prompts into Flow exactly as written.
- No readable words anywhere in the picture (no readable number plates, signs or logos), except the BadCode mask described below.
- Nobody in any still is talking: mouths closed or neutral. Voices are added later.

# The story, in plain words

Night, in the rain, in a British city. A police motorcycle outrider holds up a gloved hand and stops an ambulance at a red traffic light, blue lights flashing, so that a politician's motorcade (a few dark saloon cars) can sweep through the junction. Inside the ambulance the two crew are working on a patient and watching the light. Inside the back of the motorcade's car, a minister (an invented man, not any real politician) is on the phone, furious about something trivial: he asked for raspberry jam and they gave him lemon curd. On the pavement, a TV news reporter is doing a live piece to camera with all of this happening behind him. The reporter is BadCode.

# The BadCode reporter (fixed rules)

- A full knitted balaclava, a large **white ring** stitched round the whole face, and a **red triangle over the wearer's LEFT eye**, high inside the ring (so, facing the camera, it sits on the right of the picture, at about 2 o'clock).
- **No skin anywhere:** the balaclava has no eye, nose or mouth holes; the neck tucks into a collar; gloves on both hands. **No robot features**: no metal, no lights, no visor. Cloth and gloves only.
- Otherwise dressed and standing exactly like a normal British TV news reporter: a rain jacket or coat, a handheld microphone, played completely straight.

# The four shots for this round

A. **Inside the ambulance**: the crew (two paramedics) with the patient, the red light and the stopped traffic through the windscreen.
B. **Inside the politician's car**: the minister in the back seat on the phone, the ambulance or blue lights glimpsed through the rain-streaked window.
C. **The whole outside view**: the junction, the outrider's raised glove, the ambulance held at the red light, the motorcade coming through.
D. **The BadCode reporter outside**: on the pavement, mic in hand, to camera, with the ambulance and motorcade behind.
