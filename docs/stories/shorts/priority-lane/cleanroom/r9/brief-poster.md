# Shot brief PS9: his campaign poster, seen out of the ambulance's driver's-side window

Write **4 prompts** to `prompts-poster.json` with ids `PS9-a` .. `PS9-d`. A reference image of the minister WILL be
attached in Flow. **Do not describe his face or body at all**. Write "the man from the reference image". You may
describe his pose, hand, eyes and expression on the poster, and any photo-op prop (a hard hat, hi-vis).

**Research first (the web):** what you see from the driver's seat of a UK front-line ambulance (a box-bodied van,
often Fiat Ducato / Mercedes Sprinter / Ford Transit based) looking out of the DRIVER'S side window. In England traffic
drives on the LEFT and the driver sits on the RIGHT, so the driver's window faces the oncoming lane and, beyond it, the
far pavement. How high is the seat? What's on a UK ambulance dashboard and door (radio handset, the data terminal on
its arm, sat-nav, the door's grab handle, wing mirror)? What does a London bus shelter (the glass "Adshel"-type with
an illuminated ad panel at one end) look like from across a road?

- **What the shot does:** while the paramedics shout that the patient is dying, we glimpse the minister's own campaign
  poster, SAVE OUR NHS, as the ambulance races past it. Held ~3 seconds: slogan and face must read at once on a phone.
- **The physics (the director's main note):** the ambulance is driving STRAIGHT down its own lane, never toward a
  kerb. The camera is in the cab, at the high driver's seat height, turned to look out through the RIGHT-HAND driver's
  window at 90 degrees to the direction of travel. Across the oncoming lane (empty, or one car's lights), on the far
  pavement, a bus shelter stands parallel to the road, seen square-on, its ad panel carrying the poster. The shelter is
  a few metres away across the road, so it looks a little lower than the camera (we are high up in a van).
- **Ambulance cues in frame:** the driver's door: window frame, grab handle, the big wing mirror at the edge, a bit of
  dashboard or the data terminal; maybe the driver's green-sleeved arm on the wheel, blurred. Blue strobe light from our
  own roof bar washing the wet road and the shelter. Nobody could mistake it for a private car.
- **The poster:** a glossy campaign photo of the man from the reference image above the words **SAVE OUR NHS** in huge
  heavy capitals, spelled exactly, nothing else written. Vary across the 4: beaming portrait; staged hospital visit in a
  hard hat and hi-vis, thumbs up; shaking a nurse's hand while looking at the camera; pointing at the viewer.
- **Light:** the shelter's backlit panel, a sodium street lamp, our blue strobe, rain on the window glass.
- Motion: the near road and kerb streak slightly; the poster stays readable.
