# Shot brief BM9: the minister in his car

Write **4 prompts** to `prompts-minister.json` with ids `BM9-a` .. `BM9-d`. No reference image is attached: you create
his face, so describe it fully. Look at the last round first: `/home/jackt/.cache/pl-refs/BM8-d.jpg` (open it).

**The director's note:** "please come up with more boris-like politicians, keep the hair and him being fat and posh
looking, just slightly change the face."

- **Rules:** he is an INVENTED man of an instantly recognisable type: the floppy-haired, overweight, upper-class
  English politician. Never name or describe a real person ("looks like X" is banned). Keep: a huge shock of unbrushed
  straw/white-blond hair falling over his brow; fat, heavy-jowled; posh (expensive but rumpled navy suit, loosened tie,
  maybe an old school or club tie, signet ring). Change the face a little in each prompt so it is HIS face, not a real
  one: different nose, eyes, mouth, jaw, age (fifties to sixties), complexion.
- **Staging:** slumped in the back seat of a large black luxury car at night, phone to his RIGHT ear with his right
  hand, talking (mouth visible, mid-word: he says two lines on camera later). A small silver tray of canapés on the
  armrest. Medium close-up at eye height across the back seat; vertical frame, his head in the upper half.
- **Light:** a warm overhead reading lamp on his hair; the phone's glow; cold blue emergency strobes through the
  rain-streaked windows (we never see the vehicles).
- Vary across the 4: the face as above, his mood (jovial, conspiratorial, bored, impatient), lens distance.
