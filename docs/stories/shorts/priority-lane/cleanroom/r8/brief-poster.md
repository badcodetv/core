# Shot brief PS8: his campaign poster, seen sideways out of the ambulance

Write **4 prompts** to `prompts-poster.json` with ids `PS8-a` .. `PS8-d`. A reference image of the minister WILL be
attached in Flow. **Do not describe his appearance at all** (no age, hair, face, clothes, build). Write "the man from
the reference image". You may describe his pose, hand position, eyes and expression on the poster, and anything he
wears that is part of the poster's photo-op (see below).

- **What the shot does:** while the paramedics shout that the patient is dying, we glimpse the minister's own
  campaign poster, SAVE OUR NHS, as the ambulance passes it. It is held about 3 seconds, so the slogan and his face
  must read instantly on a phone screen.
- **The problem we're fixing:** the last version looked like it was shot from inside a private car, and the angle
  made it look as though the vehicle was driving into the pavement. So:
  - The camera is INSIDE A UK AMBULANCE, pointing straight out of its LEFT side window, at 90 degrees to the direction
    of travel. Research what the inside of a UK ambulance looks like at the side windows (frosted lower band, rubber
    seal, white-grey clinical wall panels, grab rails, equipment straps, green paramedic uniforms, blue nitrile gloves)
    and put two or three of those things in the foreground so nobody could mistake it for a car.
  - Outside, a bus shelter stands on the pavement PARALLEL to the road, seen square-on, with the poster in its
    illuminated ad panel. The ambulance is passing it in the road lane, not turning toward it.
- **The poster:** a glossy campaign photo of the man from the reference image above the words **SAVE OUR NHS** in
  huge heavy capitals, spelled exactly so, nothing else written. Vary the photo-op across the 4: a beaming portrait;
  a staged hospital visit in a hard hat and hi-vis vest, thumbs up (the director wants a hat in it); shaking a nurse's
  hand without looking at her; pointing at the camera.
- **Light:** the shelter's own backlit panel, a sodium street lamp, the ambulance's own blue strobe washing the shelter
  and the glass, rain on the window.
- Vary across the 4: how much ambulance interior is in frame, whether a paramedic's sleeve or gloved hand is blurred at
  the edge, motion blur on the foreground pavement vs a sharp poster.
