# Shot brief BM8: the minister in his car

Write **4 prompts** to `prompts-minister.json` with ids `BM8-a` .. `BM8-d`. No reference image is attached: you
create his face, so describe it fully.

- **What the shot does:** this is the first time we see him properly, and he must be unforgettable at a glance: an
  instantly recognisable *type* of British politician. He will later talk on camera (two lines on the phone), so his
  mouth must be clearly visible, slightly open mid-word.
- **The man (invented):** late fifties, a big shock of unbrushed white-blond hair flopping forward over his brow and
  sticking out in every direction, a large ruddy round face, small eyes, a rumpled navy suit straining at the button,
  a tie loosened and askew, a white shirt with the collar half up. Jovial, entitled, a bit dishevelled.
- **The director's note:** "We'll give him a hat." In ONE or TWO of your prompts (not all), give him a hat that suits
  this type (for example a hard hat or a flat cap on the seat beside him or on his head with the hair spilling out).
- **Staging:** slumped in the back seat of a large black luxury car at night, holding a phone to his RIGHT ear with
  his right hand. Beside him on the leather armrest a small silver tray of fancy canapés. Medium close-up at eye
  height across the back seat; vertical frame, his head in the upper half.
- **Light:** a warm overhead reading lamp on his hair and face; the phone's glow; through the rain-streaked side and
  rear windows, cold blue strobing light from emergency vehicles outside (we never see them).
- **Mood:** his warm private bubble against the cold blue world outside.
- Vary across the 4: lens distance, which window the blue comes through, his expression (chuckling, impatient,
  conspiratorial whisper, bored), the hat or no hat.
