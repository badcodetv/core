# Shot brief CD8: the police cordon at the junction

Write **4 prompts** to `prompts-cordon.json` with ids `CD8-a` .. `CD8-d`.

- **What the shot does:** spell out WHY the ambulance has stopped: the police have closed a whole junction so a very
  important person's car can pass. It plays under a police radio voice: "Constable 97, junction blocked, ready for VIP."
  Research how the UK's police escort VIP motorcades (motorcycle outriders who leapfrog to hold junctions, the "rolling
  road block", the vehicles, hi-vis jackets with blue-and-yellow chequer, white helmets).
- **Camera:** HIGH and WIDE, as if from a first-floor window across a London junction at night, the whole junction in
  the frame. It is the only high angle in the film, so the change of scale is the point.
- **In the frame:** at the bottom, the held ambulance at the stop line (its roof, light bar and yellow-green chequer,
  blue lights on); a line of police motorcycles and officers in hi-vis across the mouth of the junction, one with a
  raised gloved hand; the junction beyond them strangely EMPTY and cleared; far side, the headlights of a small convoy
  of black cars approaching. A stopped double-decker bus and the traffic lights give the scale. Other traffic held
  too.
- **Focal point:** the empty cleared junction: the space that has been given to one man.
- **Light:** blue strobes from the police bikes and the ambulance, sodium-orange street lamps, a red traffic light,
  all doubled on the wet tarmac; rain visible against the lamps.
- Keep the people still and few enough that it reads; nobody's face needs to be seen.
- Vary across the 4: the exact vantage (window, bridge, rooftop), how much of the ambulance shows, how far away the
  convoy is, how many officers.
