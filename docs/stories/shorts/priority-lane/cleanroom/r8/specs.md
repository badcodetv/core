## BM8: the minister in his car (beat C, the motorcade)

- **Job:** make him unmistakable, so the poster and the car are obviously one man, and give him a face that can say two
  lines (the clip is a prompted-words Omni clip, muted and dubbed; no LatentSync).
- **Look:** look.md, inside a car: warmer than the street, his own little bubble of comfort.
- **The Boris-alike:** a shock of unbrushed white-blond hair flopping forward over his brow, a big ruddy round face, a
  rumpled navy suit straining at the button, tie loosened and askew. Nobody is named. Jack: "We'll give him a hat."
  One option keeps a hat (a flat cap or a hard hat on the seat beside him, or on his head with the mop sticking out).
- **Depth:** foreground: the leather armrest with a little silver tray of canapés (keeps the old joke as a prop);
  midground: him slumped, phone to his right ear; background: the rain-streaked side window with blue light outside.
- **Focal point:** his face, won by the warm reading lamp and the phone's glow.
- **Light:** a warm overhead reading light on his hair; cold blue strobing through the rear and side glass.
- **Camera:** eye height across the back seat, medium close; mouth clearly visible (he talks).
- **Withheld:** the ambulance (it's only blue light on the glass).
- **Moves:** his mouth and the light; camera locked.

## PS8: his poster, sideways out of the ambulance (beat A2, ~3 s)

- **Job:** while the crew shout, we glimpse what he promised: SAVE OUR NHS, his face. Kai: twice as long as before,
  "composed in a way that makes sense to the brain". It must read as **from inside the ambulance**, never his car.
- **Physics:** the camera points straight out of the ambulance's **left side**, at 90° to the direction of travel. The
  shelter sits on the pavement **parallel** to the road and slides past right to left in frame (looking out of the
  left side while travelling forward, the world moves backward). Nothing drives toward the kerb.
- **Ambulance cues in the foreground:** a UK ambulance side window (frosted lower band, dark rubber seal), the clinical
  white-grey wall panel and a grab rail at the edge, a green paramedic sleeve or blue-gloved hand blurred at the frame
  edge, the ambulance's own blue strobe washing the shelter.
- **Midground/focal point:** the bus-shelter poster, square-on, his face (the BM8 man, from the reference) above SAVE
  OUR NHS in huge capitals, readable on a phone. Option: the classic photo-op poster, him in a hard hat and hi-vis on
  a hospital site, thumbs up (Jack's hat).
- **Background:** wet pavement, a dark shopfront, sodium lamp.
- **Light:** sodium lamp over the shelter, the poster's own backlight, the blue strobe.
- **Moves:** lateral slide (done in the edit as a slow pan across a wide still, or Veo with the camera locked to the
  ambulance and the world passing).

## CD8: the cordon (new, between A and B)

- **Job:** spell out the cause: police have closed the junction for a VIP, and the ambulance is one of the held.
  Kai: "a wide shot of all the police", under a radio voice "Constable 97, junction blocked, ready for VIP".
- **Camera:** high and wide, from a first-floor window across the junction (the only high angle in the short, so the
  scale change is the point); the whole junction in frame.
- **Depth:** foreground: the stop line, the held ambulance's roof and light bar; midground: a line of police
  motorcycles and a van across the junction, officers in hi-vis, one with a raised glove; background: the empty
  junction and, far side, the headlights of three black cars coming.
- **Focal point:** the empty, cleared junction (the space is what's being given to him).
- **Scale reference:** the double-decker bus and the traffic lights.
- **Light:** blue strobes from police and ambulance, sodium lamps, one red traffic light; wet tarmac doubling it all.
- **Withheld:** the minister (just headlights).
- **Moves:** the world only (lights, rain, the headlights growing); officers stand still. Few movers on purpose.
