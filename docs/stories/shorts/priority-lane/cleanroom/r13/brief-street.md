# Shot brief PS13: his poster on a real bus stop, seen out of the ambulance's driver's window

Read `research-likeness.md` here first (why the face drifted before, and the rules). Write **4 prompts** to
`prompts-street.json`, ids `PS13-a` .. `PS13-d`. In Flow each prompt gets ONE reference image: a finished poster
artwork (the man's face already big on it). Refer to it only as "the poster from the reference image, reproduced
exactly, the same man's face". Do not describe the poster, the man, or his face. No real person's name, no
politician/minister/campaign words.

**The director's note:** "make it more obvious that it is him and do a few different bus poster stills but on the
actual bus stop."
- **On the actual bus stop:** research what a UK (London) bus shelter looks like: glass panels, a roof, a bench, the
  bus-stop flag on its pole, a timetable case, and the illuminated advertising panel built into the END of the shelter
  (the poster lives there). Make it unmistakably a bus stop. No readable route numbers, stop names or other text.
- **More obvious it is him:** the poster must be big and clear in the frame: his face must read instantly on a phone.
  Bring the shelter's end panel close: a near pass, the panel taking up a large part of the window (more than half
  the window's height in at least three of the four). Poster sharp, evenly lit, square-on, no glare across the face.
- **Physics (unchanged, the director's earlier notes):** UK traffic keeps left; the driver sits on the right. Camera
  at the high driver's seat of a UK ambulance (a tall box-bodied van) driving STRAIGHT down its lane, looking straight
  out through the RIGHT-HAND driver's door window at 90 degrees to travel, across the oncoming lane to the far
  pavement, where the shelter stands parallel to the road. Cab cues: window seal, grab handle, grey door trim, the big
  wing mirror at the LEFT (forward) edge. Our blue roof strobe on the wet road, a sodium street lamp, rain on the
  glass; near road streaks with motion.
- Vary across the 4: how much of the shelter shows (end panel only vs the panel plus the flag pole and a bit of the
  bench), window glass up vs half down, how strong the blue strobe is, a person waiting at the stop (back to us, face
  unseen) in one.
