# Shot brief, round 12: the poster, in two steps

Read `research-likeness.md` in this folder first: it explains why the last rounds' posters kept turning into a real,
famous man, and the rules that fix it. Follow those rules. Look at the face reference: `/home/jackt/.cache/pl-refs/BM9-b-face.jpg` (the
invented man; it will be the ONLY reference attached in Flow for step 1).

## Step 1: the poster artwork alone (`prompts-art.json`, ids `PA12-a` .. `PA12-d`)
The printed advert, filling the whole frame, flat and square-on, as a photo of the printed poster (so it can later be
put into a street scene). His face is large: at least a third of the image height.
- He is "the man from the reference image". Keep his face identical to it. Following the research, you MAY add his
  distinguishing features as the reference shows them (nose, eyes, freckles, age, expression) to anchor him; do not
  describe the hair (leave it to the reference).
- Words that must NOT appear: politician, minister, MP, campaign, election, English, British, posh, upper-class, London,
  bus. No hard hat, no hi-vis, no thumbs up, no pointing, no handshake, no glossy studio head-and-shoulders "official
  portrait" look. Say what you want, never "not X".
- The joke still needs a political-advert feel: him plus a big slogan. Slogan: **SAVE OUR NHS** in huge heavy capitals,
  spelled exactly, nothing else written. Vary the visual idea across the 4 in ways the research says are safe (for
  example a candid-looking photo of him at a hospital bedside, holding a cup of tea in a ward, a close crop of his
  face against a flat colour, him with a stethoscope draped as a prop), each different.
- Vertical 9:16 (a portrait advert panel).

## Step 2: the street shot (`prompts-street.json`, ids `PS12-a` .. `PS12-b`)
Two prompts. In Flow, the reference attached will be the PICKED step-1 poster image (not the face). Describe the scene
and say the advert panel shows "the poster from the reference image, reproduced exactly". Do not describe the poster
or the man at all.
- The scene (the director's physics, already right last round): UK traffic drives on the left; the driver sits on the
  right. From the high driver's seat of a UK ambulance (a tall box-bodied van) driving STRAIGHT down its lane, the
  camera looks straight out through the RIGHT-HAND driver's door window at 90 degrees to travel. Across the oncoming
  lane, on the far pavement, a pavement advertising panel / shelter stands parallel to the road, square-on, carrying
  the poster, big in the window. Cab cues: window seal, grab handle, grey door trim, the big wing mirror at the LEFT
  (forward) edge. Our blue roof strobe on the wet road, a sodium lamp, rain on the glass. Near road streaks with motion,
  poster sharp.

Common rules: see brief-common.md. Reply with one line per prompt.
