# Shot brief PB14: from the bus-stop seat, his poster beside us, the road in front

Write **3 prompts** to `prompts-poster.json`, ids `PB14-a` .. `PB14-c`. Read `research-likeness.md` first. In Flow
ONE reference image is attached: a finished poster artwork with the man's face big on it (for example
`/home/jackt/.cache/pl-refs/PA12-b.jpg`; open it). Refer to it only as "the poster from the reference image, reproduced exactly, the same
man's face". Never describe the poster, the man or his face; no real person's name; no politician/minister/campaign
words.

**The director's note:** "we need to see it from the angle of the bus stop seat, then seeing the ambulance drive past
in the video."
- **The camera** sits at seat height on the narrow bench inside a London bus shelter at night in the rain (research
  the TfL shelter: glass sides and roof, a perch bench, the illuminated advertising case built into the end of the
  shelter, often double-sided so it also faces inward). The poster in that end case is big and sharp in the frame
  (one side of the picture), square or at a slight angle, his face reading instantly.
- **Through the shelter glass / open front**, the wet road and kerb right in front, lit by sodium lamps, with space
  for an ambulance to pass. This still is the FIRST frame of a clip in which a UK ambulance (yellow-green chequered,
  blue lights) will race past along the road, left to right or right to left. In the still the road is empty but the
  blue glow of the approaching ambulance is starting to light the rain and the wet tarmac from one side. UK traffic
  keeps left: an ambulance on the near side of the road, travelling past the stop, moves from right to left as seen
  from the seat facing the road. Make that consistent.
- Light: the poster case's own white backlight, sodium street lamps, the first wash of blue, rain on the shelter glass.
- Vary across the 3: poster on the left vs right of frame, how much of the shelter's glass and roof shows, how close
  the camera is to the poster. No readable text anywhere except the poster.
