# line-of-duty-circling-piano: what shaped it

- **The Line of Duty sound.** Carly Paradis's score is built on a four-note motif. Radio Times describes the melody "circling round and round, whipping up tension" with the piano at the centre. Classical Music adds "racing rhythms", electronic beats with tuned and untuned percussion, and Clint Mansell-style shimmering strings and electronic shifts (she played piano on Mansell's *Moon*). That gave the prompt its circling four-note minor piano figure, racing electronic percussion and thin high-string shimmer. Sources: https://www.classical-music.com/features/tv-and-film-music/line-of-duty-on-bbc-who-wrote-the-music-and-can-you-buy-the-soundtrack , https://apricots-coral-h24b.squarespace.com/words/2021/3/21/obsessed-with-the-music-of-line-of-duty-meet-composer-carly-paradis?format=amp , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carly_Paradis
- **Bodyguard.** The cue titles ("Attack", "Dread", "The Trap") and a Gold Derby headline about scoring "what does panic sound like" point to panic-pulse writing. I could not read the interview itself, so the "fight-or-flight" wording comes from the brief, not from that source. Source: https://filmmusicreporter.com/2018/10/24/soundtrack-album-for-bbc-netflix-series-bodyguard-released/
- **Accents.** Community guides say Suno reads the first words of the style box most heavily, and that a region-specific tag plus a stronger delivery description helps a faint accent. So "British … English accents only" comes first, each crew voice is named by place (Brummie, Black Country, South London), and "American accent" goes in Exclude. Suno says its own Speech beta does not hold accents fixed: a British voice can drift to Australian and back. Expect drift and roll several takes. Sources: https://howtopromptsuno.com/making-music/specific-vocal-styles , https://medium.com/@aitooldiscovery/suno-reddit-tips-and-prompts-what-r-sunoai-users-actually-do-65b59d51bcad , https://blog.dubspot.com/suno-speech-2026
- **Spoken, not sung.** Guides say spoken word in normal v6 Custom mode is unreliable, and that `[Spoken]` tags are an experiment, not a documented control. Lyric shape matters more than style-box instructions. Each line is therefore tagged `[Spoken, shouted …]`, singing and melody go in Exclude, and every line is kept as one short shout. Sources: https://jackrighteous.com/es/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/suno-ai-spoken-narration-workflow , https://sunowatermark.com/blog/suno-v6-vocal-prompts-2026/
- **Making the minister sound different.** The style box says "a different voice entirely" and gives him the opposite of the crew on every axis: class (received pronunciation, Home Counties), age (fifties), temper (smooth and petulant against frantic) and space (muffled behind glass against an open, shouting ambulance). The music change does the same job: the piano stops mid-phrase and drops to a single hum, so his line lands in a different acoustic world.
- **Length.** Duration is 14 s as the story fixes. The lyrics end in a hard `[Silence]` then `[End]`, and "fade out" is excluded, to discourage the long runs of r3 (Suno ran 0:15 to 1:55 then).
- **Settings.** v6 rather than Wild, with variety normal and weirdness 35, because the genre is tightly defined. Style influence is 70 to keep the British thriller palette. Vocal gender is null because the cast is mixed.
