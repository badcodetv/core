# bbc-drama-strings-two-worlds — writer notes (r4, 2026-11-13)

What shaped it:

- **Accent first, region named, country words plus local names.** A r/SunoAI user reports accents land best when the Style names the country and the accent and the lyrics give the singers names typical of that place, so each speaker tag carries a regional English name (Shirley, Gaz, Dean, Sir Rupert). Another user's style pairs "Close-mic British vocal" with "No American accent"; here "American accent" sits in the exclude box instead. Accent tags are guidance, not guaranteed (unverified for v6).
  - https://redlib.hyperreal.coffee/r/SunoAI/comments/1igcs0k/different_voices
  - https://macmyths.com/best-ai-song-generators-for-a-us-midwest-accent-2026/
  - https://howtopromptsuno.com/making-music/specific-vocal-styles
- **Making Suno speak, not sing.** Jack Righteous' spoken-narration guide: put `[Spoken ...]` cues in the lyrics, keep melodic/hook/chorus words out of Styles, state "no singing", and write short punctuated lines with breaks. So: no genre word that implies song, and every line is cued as spoken.
  - https://jackrighteous.com/fr/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/suno-ai-spoken-narration-workflow
- **Keep the two worlds apart.** Speaker labels are guidance, not commands (one tutorial found Suno swapped male/female tags on its first try), so the minister is described in the style box as a separate person with clashing traits: elderly, deep baritone, cut-glass RP, bored rather than shouting, and acoustically muffled. That gives four contrasts against the crew: age, class, energy and room sound.
  - https://lilys.ai/en/notes/suno-ai-20260102/suno-ai-secrets-part-1
- **What "plummy/posh" sounds like.** RP descriptions: clipped consonants, long vowels, non-rhotic; the heightened "cut-glass" form is mostly heard in older speakers, hence *elderly*.
  - https://dialectsfortheatre.soc.northwestern.edu/british/received-pronunciation
  - https://www.adamsmith.org/blog/come-awf-it
- **The score.** Casualty's theme is Ken Freeman's, built around a heartbeat figure, which is why the synth pulse runs at heartbeat tempo under the strings.
  - https://Www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ken_Freeman_(composer)
  - https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/news/whos-first-credits-clash-classic-tv-theme-tunes-1975559
- **Settings.** v6 core, not Wild, for consistency on spoken delivery (narration guide); Variety normal; style influence high so the accents and the cut-dead structure hold; weirdness low; vocalGender left null because the cast is mixed; Duration 14 as fixed.
- Lyrics are word-for-word; only bracketed tags were added.
