# viola-tremolo-dread: what shaped it

- **Genre first, then players, then technique, then mood.** Film-score prompting guides put the genre at the front and name concrete instruments over adjectives; vague prompts get "statistically average" output. So the box opens "avant-garde string chamber score" and names the exact players (2 violas, 2 cellos, bass) rather than "strings". https://hookgenius.app/learn/suno-film-score-prompts/ , https://medium.com/@aitooldiscovery/suno-reddit-tips-and-prompts-what-r-sunoai-users-actually-do-65b59d51bcad
- **Technique words.** Sul ponticello brings out high overtones, "glassy, fuzzy", "eerie"; tremolo plus glissando is the classic chill, and slow glissandi read as more menacing than fast ones. Those three are the engine of the cue, described with their sound ("glassy and metallic") as well as their name, because Suno's own glossary only defines tremolo and nothing confirms it understands "sul ponticello" (unverified). https://hub.yamaha.com/strings/s-how-to/eight-spooky-string-techniques , https://sharmusic.com/blogs/all/a-symphony-of-horror , https://help.suno.com/en/articles/9010177
- **Arc in words, not timestamps.** Frantic from bar one (the ambulance), an abrupt drop to one sliding viola (the red light / the car), then a unison bow-stop. Suno has no reliable timing control, so the 10.5 s cut point will still be found in the edit.
- **The hard stop.** No official Suno wording guarantees a hard stop; community guides say to describe it in plain language ("cut off at once, dead silence, no ring") and keep "fade out" and "reverb tail" out. Both are in the exclude box. https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/suno-ai-end-prompt-guide , https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/fix-suno-song-repeats-wont-end
- **Room for voices.** "Sparse", "low-mid", "under shouted dialogue" follow the background-use advice (subtle, supporting role). The dry, close-miked sound also keeps a hall reverb from washing over the dialogue. https://hookgenius.app/learn/suno-film-score-prompts/
- **Settings.** v6 (the story fixes it) at 20 s. Weirdness 60 to push past a polished trailer-strings default towards the scratchy and avant-garde; style influence 80 so the instrument list and no-percussion rule hold; variety normal; no vocal gender (instrumental); Max Mode off.
- Excludes: vocals, choir, humming, synth, drums, piano (from the brief), plus percussion, brass, electronic, orchestral swell and epic trailer (the most likely way it drifts into a big Hollywood string bed), melody, uplifting and comedic (the story's Never list).
