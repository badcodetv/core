# percussion-only-ensemble: notes

What shaped the prompt:

- **Genre first, then players, then one dynamics sentence.** Suno guides put the genre tag up front and tell you to name the instruments you want. For a percussion-only part that means naming the drums and textures and leaving out every melodic word, because guides that add "melodic elements" to beats would pull a tune in. https://howtopromptsuno.com/making-music/instrumental-tags , https://jackrighteous.com/fr/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/instrumentation-and-arrangement-in-suno-v5
- **Say instrumental in two places.** "instrumental only" goes in the style box as well as the exclude list, and the lyrics stay empty. The guides say an exclusion works best alongside a positive descriptor. https://howtopromptsuno.com/style-lyrics/how-do-i-make-instrumental , https://hookgenius.app/learn/suno-negative-prompting/
- **Short, specific exclude list.** I kept only the seven terms the brief named, because the negative-prompting guide warns that piling on exclusions confuses the model and can leave the track thin. Nothing in the list removes drums. https://hookgenius.app/learn/suno-negative-prompting/
- **Bowed cymbal and pounding low drums as procedural-drama tension.** Library cues written for crime and procedural drama pair throbbing low drums and bowed cymbal swells with metal textures. That gave me the scraped metal and the bowed cymbal over the floor toms. https://sonoton.com/en/track/SCDV146821 , https://sonoton.com/es/track/SCDV146829 , https://www.rareformaudio.com/blog/percussion-in-media-music
- **Floor toms as the cinematic low end.** Floor toms, timpani and gran casa are named as the classic big-drum choices. I chose hand-played floor toms and frame drums, not taiko, so it sounds like a body being worked on rather than an epic trailer. https://lunacy.audio/?p=158834
- **Room for shouted dialogue.** "Sparse midrange" and "dry close-miked" are there to keep the voice band clear.

Unverified: Suno cannot be told a time in seconds, so I can't say whether the drop lands near 5.5 s or whether the cold end lands near 10.5 s.
