# broken-cardiac-electronica — notes

What shaped it:

- **Short tag list, then one scene sentence.** Several guides say v6 responds to concrete instrument names and to sections described by what they *do*, not by stacked adjectives, and to naming the transition and "the last thing playing" (here: the tape-stop, then silence). Keep the style box under ~100 words. Sources: https://sunowatermark.com/blog/suno-v6-instrument-prompts-2026/ , https://howtopromptsuno.com/style-lyrics/how-do-i-make-instrumental , https://www.layer3labs.io/guides/how-to-write-suno-prompts
- **Instrumental:** set the Instrumental switch on as well as writing "instrumental" in the style box (howtopromptsuno). Lyrics left "" as the brief asks.
- **The palette:** glitch production swaps hi-hats for electrical blips and fragments, built on randomness (https://www.ujam.com/tutorials/how-to-design-a-glitch-beat/). Library thriller cues pair "heartbeat bass" with "glitchy percussion" and "distorted noise FX" (https://www.intervox.de/albums/rsm575-darktronic-3 , https://us.audionetwork.com/browse/m/track/heartwork_1103632), and a kick drum used as a heartbeat is a known scoring move (https://collab.sundance.org/catalog/Your-City-Sleeps-Project-MKHEXE-Owen-s-House-2025-09-30-223153).
- **Room for voices:** "hollow empty midrange" plus "heavy sub low end, brittle sharp highs" asks for the mids to stay clear for the shouted dialogue. Unverified whether Suno honours mix words; check the takes.
- **Tape-stop:** no source documents a Suno tag for it (untested), so it is described in the scene sentence. "dead silence at ten seconds, nothing after" covers the flatline cut; take generated at 20 s, so the cut can be made in the edit regardless.
- **Settings:** v6 (not Wild) so the arc is followed; weirdness 55 for a broken-machine feel without losing the shape; styleInfluence 70 to hold the palette; variety normal; maxMode off.
- **Exclude:** the brief's list plus "cheerful" (story's Never list).
