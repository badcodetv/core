# heartbeat-drums: what shaped it

- **Speech, not singing.** Guides say Suno's spoken delivery is steered by stacking signals: a speech cue in the style box, `[Spoken]`-type tags in lyrics, short punctuated lines, no chorus/hook language, and "no melody" phrasing. So every line carries a `[Spoken/Shouted …]` tag naming the speaker, and the style says "spoken voices barked in time with the drum hits". If a tag gets sung aloud, move it into Styles and delete it. Sources: https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/suno-ai-spoken-narration-workflow , https://sunowatermark.com/blog/suno-v6-vocal-prompts-2026/
- **Speech beta exists (Oct 2026)** but takes one voice and a music bed; it does not cover multiple speakers or SFX, so this experiment stays in the v6 music model. Source: https://jackrighteous.com/de/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/suno-speech-beta-spoken-audio-background-music
- **Tempo is a target, not a lock**, and the arc is described in Styles with short local cues in lyrics. So the style gives a starting 150 bpm plus rhythm words (lub-dub double strikes, skipping, dragging), and the lyrics carry the "stumbles / almost gone / flatline / silence" cues at their place. Style order follows tempo → genre → rhythm → instruments → mood. Sources: https://jackrighteous.com/en-us/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/suno-ai-build-intensity-prompt , https://medium.com/@aitooldiscovery/suno-reddit-tips-and-prompts-what-r-sunoai-users-actually-do-65b59d51bcad
- **Heartbeat tempo as tension control** is a standing film device (speed up/slow down to raise/drop tension); taiko rim for high clicks, head for low thuds. Sources: https://www.tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Main/Heartbeatsoundtrack , https://cinematiccomposing.com/tc-course-composing-a-tension-cue
- **Deliberate wording choices:** the brief's "like a drill" is NOT in the style box (it would summon UK drill); it became "barked in time with the drum hits", and `uk drill, rap flow, trap hi-hats` sit in exclude. No uniformity words. "fade out" excluded so it ends on the tone, then silence.
- **Settings:** v6 (steadier timing than Wild for a 14 s cue), durationSec 14 (v6 obeys a set duration), vocalGender null (three voices, two genders), weirdness 55 / styleInfluence 70 to hold the percussion-only brief.
