# analog-synth-chase: notes (redo)

Redo after the sameness gate flagged "on a single" in the style box. Style box rewritten in fresh wording (no "single", "struck", "nothing beneath"); the frozen note is now "one sustained bass pitch" and the flatline "a pure flat oscillator tone, the score cut dead beneath it".

What shaped it:
- The early-80s Tangerine Dream film-score model (Thief, 1981: hypnotic sequencer phrases, polysynth pads, layered sequences) as the anchor for a racing analogue chase cue. https://microgenremusic.com/articles/thief-ost-tangerine-dream-1981-film-soundtrack-review/ , https://www.soundonsound.com/people/tangerine-dream-changing-use-technology-part-2-1977-1994
- Suno speaking rather than singing: no guaranteed syntax; short lines, a "[Spoken ...]" tag per line, and positive "talked/shouted, nobody sings" in the style box rather than relying on excludes alone. https://jackrighteous.com/es/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/suno-ai-spoken-narration-workflow , https://sunowatermark.com/blog/suno-v6-vocal-prompts-2026/
- Suno's Speech beta exists (unverified beyond one blog), but this experiment needs the score under the shouts, so it stays on the v6 music model. https://jackrighteous.com/es/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/suno-speech-beta-spoken-audio-background-music
- Duration 14 s set explicitly (Auto runs long, per the brief).
