# fly-on-the-wall-cab — writer's notes (2026-11-13)

**What shaped it**

- **Spoken, not sung.** Asking for spoken delivery is only a tendency in the v6 song model. Bracketed "[Spoken]" tags are experiments, not a documented command, and saying "no singing" is weaker than giving a positive spoken cue. So the prompt gives the positive cue in three places: the style ("spoken dialogue only"), the first lyric tag, and an exclude list naming singing, choir, chorus, melody and hook. Sources: https://jackrighteous.com/es/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/suno-ai-spoken-narration-workflow and https://jackrighteous.com/es/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/suno-speech-beta-spoken-audio-background-music
- **Voice and music described apart, and the music kept under the words.** The Speech beta guide's advice is to describe the narrator and the music separately and keep the music restrained so every word stays clear. That is why the underscore is only a hum, the beeps and the rain. No melody line competes with the shouts. Source: the Speech beta link above.
- **Line shape beats adjectives.** The v6 vocal guide says phrasing and timing come mostly from the lyric, and delivery words in the style box only sometimes work. The fixed lines cannot change, so each line gets its own speaker and level tag (woman steady then cracking, man quiet then suddenly loud, driver off-mic, minister through glass). That way the timing is carried by the structure. Source: https://sunowatermark.com/blog/suno-v6-vocal-prompts-2026/
- **The documentary register.** UK observational A&E documentaries are recorded on many fixed mics, not by actors on a scored set. That is the "fly-on-the-wall" quality aimed for here: dry, close, breathy and understated. Source: https://www.gtc.org.uk/media/fm/Zerb%20articles/AandE%20spreads%20web.pdf (GTC ZERB piece on the 24 Hours in A&E rig). One weak, possibly spammy page claims these shows often use no dramatic music, just a monitor and silence. It is unverified, but it matches the brief.

**Settings.** v6 rather than wild, because the minimal bed needs restraint, not drift. Weirdness is a low 30 and styleInfluence 70, so the documentary and no-melody instructions hold. vocalGender is null because there are both a woman and men. Duration is 14 s, because a set Duration is obeyed. Max Mode is on, because Suno lists vocal consistency among its aims (unverified for speech).

**Unverified risks.** Suno may still sing a line, may not switch speakers cleanly, and may not produce a pure sine flatline. If the swearing is refused, swap the word.
