# Round 3 (Suno, all audio): research notes for the ideas writer, 2026-11-13

All sources are third-party (no official Suno tag grammar exists); treat every tag as something to test.

- **Suno Speech is a separate beta, launched 2026-10-01, not a v6 mode.** Simple mode (describe it) and Advanced mode (custom script, voice gender, speaking style, variety); background music optional and described separately from the voice. No duration control and no word-level timing; accents drift, pauses can run long. Tips: describe voice and music in separate directions, keep music restrained, say "no sung chorus". Multi-speaker and sound effects not documented.
  https://jackrighteous.com/fr/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/suno-speech-beta-spoken-audio-background-music
  https://unite.ai/suno-launches-speech-beta-pairing-voice-and-music-in-one-model
  https://www.aimusicpreneur.com/ai-tools-news/suno-speech-beta-spoken-word-music-one-take-launch/
- **Spoken lines in v6 Custom Mode:** tags like [Spoken Word], [Monologue], [Spoken narration] are steering cues only; Suno may still drift melodic. Performance is driven mainly by the lyric field (line length, syllables per bar), identity (timbre, register) by the style box. Style-box adjectives ("emotive", "powerful") barely change phrasing.
  https://jackrighteous.com/de/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/suno-ai-spoken-narration-workflow
  https://sunowatermark.com/blog/suno-v6-vocal-prompts-2026/
- **Shouting and sound effects:** tag lists include [Shouted Vocals], [Heartbeat], [Alarm Siren] (no bare [Siren]); UPPERCASE lyric text reportedly pushes shouted delivery; dialogue in parentheses reportedly reads as speech. Keep tags sparse (2-3 in a row, not 5+); tags matter most at the top and at section changes.
  https://www.scribd.com/document/882613915/Suno-META-Tags
  https://musci.io/blog/suno-tags
  https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/how-to-use-suno-ai-special-tags
- **Several voices in v6:** no documented duet syntax; reports conflict (voices swap mid-line). What helps: name each voice in the style box as a map ("Voice A = ..., Voice B = ...") and repeat the name as a line-level tag; short lines; never two voices on different words at once.

- **What hospital-drama underscore is made of (library cue descriptions):** heartbeat pulse, "tense medical bleeps", clock percussion, deep bass pulse, low drone, sparse piano, cello harmonics. Casualty's recent score is by Justine Barker (album 2023); theme by Ken Freeman.

- **Tension pulse craft:** build from the bottom, spiccato strings plus double basses marking the pulse gives "a functional pulse with a certain sense of anxiety".
  https://cinematiccomposing.com/tc-course-composing-a-tension-cue
- **String jabs (Kai's "quick jab on a violin"):** Bartók (snap) pizzicato = a percussive crack, distinct from an accented pizz; col legno battuto = bow wood struck on the string, dry and percussive; sforzando = the stab's attack.
