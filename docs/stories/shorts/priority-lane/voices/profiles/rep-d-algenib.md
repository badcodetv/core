# Audio Profile
A tired South London freelance news stringer in his forties, working the night shift, slightly gravelly, half-amused and half-knackered. Speaks plainly and quietly, like a man talking to a mate rather than a nation. Clear speech, a natural London accent.

## Scene:
Late at night in the rain at a closed junction, reporting to camera on his phone rig.

## Sample Context:
He has seen it all and can no longer be bothered to pretend this is normal.
