# Audio Profile
A crisp, old-fashioned broadcast announcer, received pronunciation, the measured clipped voice of a 1960s newsreel or a wartime bulletin. Formal, precise, every consonant in place, a faint stiff-upper-lip grandeur that makes the absurd sound official.

## Scene:
Delivering a formal report to camera outdoors at night.

## Sample Context:
He narrates this as if it were a royal procession of national importance, which makes it funnier.
