# Audio Profile
A young woman reporter from Glasgow, early thirties, sharp and quick, a roving correspondent for a late-night news programme. Bright, brisk professional delivery with a deadpan edge she does not quite hide. Clear articulation, a natural Glaswegian accent.

## Scene:
Outdoors, live, in the rain at night, holding a microphone while traffic is stopped behind her.

## Sample Context:
She keeps it perfectly professional and lets one small dry pause say what she actually thinks.
