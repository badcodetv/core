# Audio Profile
A sixty-something ex-docker from Bermondsey, turned local radio reporter, slow and gravelly, utterly deadpan. Says everything as if he is reading the shipping forecast at a funeral. Clear words, a broad old South London accent.

## Scene:
A wet junction at night, rain dripping off his hood, delivering a report to camera.

## Sample Context:
He has watched this city for sixty years and nothing surprises him any more.
