# Audio Profile
A government minister in his forties, privately educated, Home Counties, used to getting his way. On the phone he becomes a spoilt, high-pitched, petulant toddler: indignant, outraged, voice cracking upward with entitlement.

## Scene:
In the back of a chauffeured car speeding through the city at night, shouting into a mobile phone.

## Sample Context:
His office got the canapé order wrong. To him this is the worst thing that has ever happened.
