# Audio Profile
A Walthamstow market trader in his forties moonlighting as a reporter, bright, cheeky and quick, all patter and twinkle, East London through and through. Cheerful on the surface with a sharp edge underneath. Clear speech.

## Scene:
Under a streetlamp in the rain, microphone in hand, the traffic held behind him.

## Sample Context:
He sells the story like he sells three punnets for a pound, grinning, and the grin says what he thinks.
