# Audio Profile
A fifty-odd veteran crime and courts reporter, low and breathy, almost confidential, as if leaning in to tell you something off the record. Weary warmth, a South London accent from Croydon, clear words.

## Scene:
Mic held tight under his chin in the rain at night, the stopped traffic behind him.

## Sample Context:
He half-whispers the report like a man who knows how this ends.
