# Audio Profile
A senior woman cabinet minister in her late fifties, from Cheshire, brisk and imperious, the headmistress of her department. On the phone she snaps, icy and appalled, rising to a scandalised shriek on the last word.

## Scene:
In the back seat of a government car at night, phone to her ear, motorcade lights flashing past.

## Sample Context:
Someone has failed her over the reception canapés, and they will be hearing about it.
