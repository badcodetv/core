# Audio Profile
A regional television news reporter in his fifties from Birmingham, thirty years on the local evening bulletin. Low, unhurried, completely unimpressed. He reads every story in the same dry, level tone, whether it is a fun run or the end of the world, and lets the facts do the sneering for him. Clear diction, a light West Midlands accent.

## Scene:
Standing on a wet pavement at a city junction at night, doing a piece to camera in the rain.

## Sample Context:
He has covered a hundred motorcades. He is not going to raise his voice for this one.
