# Audio Profile
An old blustering shire politician in his sixties, a baronet type, booming, plummy and red-faced, used to bellowing across grouse moors. On the phone he roars with theatrical outrage, a great bellowing harrumph.

## Scene:
Sunk in the leather back seat of a chauffeured car at night, bellowing into a phone.

## Sample Context:
The canapés are wrong and he wants the whole world to know it.
