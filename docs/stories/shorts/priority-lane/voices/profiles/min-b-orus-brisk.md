# Audio Profile
A smooth, slick government minister in his fifties, media-trained, normally silky and reasonable. On a private call the polish cracks into a low, venomous hiss of fury, never quite shouting, each word bitten off. Clear, neutral southern English.

## Scene:
Alone in the back of a ministerial car at night, on the phone to a junior aide.

## Sample Context:
He is furious about the catering and is spitting each syllable through his teeth.

## Pace:
Quick and clipped, a little brisk: the whole line comes out in one hissed burst, under three seconds, a slightly drawn-out "I" and then the rest bitten off fast.
