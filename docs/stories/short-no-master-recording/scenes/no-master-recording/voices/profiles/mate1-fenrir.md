# Audio Profile
A lanky Mancunian lad in his thirties in a red football shirt, wound up and loud, the kind of bloke who argues with the telly.

## Scene:
Standing knee-deep in a sea of old seven-inch records, having just broken into a recording studio to settle an argument about how a song goes.

## Sample Context:
Exasperated, half laughing, half losing it: the swear comes out hard and the rest tumbles out quick.
