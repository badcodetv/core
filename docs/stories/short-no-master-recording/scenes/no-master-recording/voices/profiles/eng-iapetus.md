# Audio Profile
A studio engineer in a balaclava who has been mixing the same song for eleven years. South London, dry, flat, unbothered, a voice like a kettle that stopped caring whether it boils.

## Scene:
A cramped basement studio at three in the morning, slumped over a mixing desk buried in mugs, two intruders staring at him across the desk.

## Sample Context:
Bone-dry and deadpan. Short, tired, completely unsurprised. No warmth, no hurry.
