## What it is
A BadCode short (9:16, 15.3 s). Two mates argue over how the BadCode song goes, each humming a different tune; they
break into the studio, flooded knee-deep with seven-inch singles labelled FINAL, FINAL FINAL, FINAL v847 USE THIS ONE;
the balaclava'd engineer looks up: "Which one?" The point, twice over: a joke on ourselves (we make hundreds of takes of
every song) and "no master recording": every listener holds their own cut, and there is no original to appeal to.
State: rough cut 1 on the timeline page and in Premiere (`short-no-master-recording-1-no-master-recording-r1`).
