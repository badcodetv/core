## The idea (slate #52, made meta by Jack 2026-11-12)

Two mates argue over how the BadCode song goes, each humming a different tune (4) → they break into the studio to check
the original: a wall of a thousand records labelled `final`, `final 2`, `FINAL final`, `final v847 USE THIS ONE` (5) →
a balaclava'd engineer looks up from the desk: "Which one? There's no master. There's only the one you heard." (4) **13s.**
Draft words `by: claude`; Jack writes the final jokes.

**Point, twice over:** a joke on ourselves (we really do make hundreds of takes of every song: 400+ for Bank Robbery),
and the Storyverse's "no master recording" (`docs/stories/storyverse/doctrine.md`, simplification 1): every listener
holds their own cut, and there is no original to appeal to. Part of the parked Storyverse batch (#50–82).
