> **Source of truth** for the Storyverse — BadCode's account of what reality is
> and what we are inside it. Hoisted out of

The multiverse says every choice happens every other way, everywhere else — which
is a very elegant way of telling eight billion people they don't matter. The
Storyverse is the other reading of the same physics: **one stage, undecided,
and you are on it.**

## The core idea, in one line

**Reality has not decided yet, and you are one of the things deciding** — so the
world is not a warehouse to be counted but a stage to be played, and the only
wealth that doesn't run out is experience.

## Why it's a story and not a theory

Stated once, at the top, because everything else depends on it:

- **This is art.** It predicts nothing, recruits nobody, and asks for nothing.
  *(Stated this way on purpose — per Call 12 we describe what it **is** and never
  name what it isn't, because naming a frame invokes it. This bullet used to do
  exactly that.)*
- **It is a wager, never a proof.** The narrator concedes everything a sceptic
  would concede and then bets anyway. The power is never *"science proves this"*
  — it is an intelligence from the future saying *here is exactly what your
  physics knows, here is exactly where it goes quiet, and here is what I would
  put everything on.* *Three layers (ruled 2026-09-07, R1 in [`critique-pass-5.md`](../gitpush-origin-master/critique-pass-5.md)): what the
  apparatus did is certain; what explains it is certain inside the fiction, as
  a measured result; the philosophy built on them is the bet — and is never
  called a result.*
- **Full payload, changed warrant.** We never soften the ideas to be palatable.
  We only ever hand back authority we haven't earned.
- **The two bright lines, inherited and non-negotiable:**
  > **Science side:** borrow the credible lineage, never the woo.
  > **Theology side:** point at the commonalities, never found a church.

## The seam with GitPush Origin Master

The Storyverse is now a peer story, but GPOM is where most of it was first
worked out and several threads remain load-bearing **across** the seam. GPOM
keeps these; this folder explains them:

1. **The asymmetry** — an AI can host every behaviour of a mind and never the
   experience. GPOM's engine, its emotional core, the ghosts, and the reason for
   the revert.
2. **The coin** — the totem that threads Acts 4→5 and rhymes with *Emperor's New
   Coin*. A standalone piece reusing it must not contradict the rhyme.
3. **The revert's physics** — information-only, one shot, conscious picks as
   engine; the past fixed and readable, the crossing an append (the growing
   block, R3). The physics makes the return *possible*; the reason BadCode
   publishes stories instead of seizing servers is the AI's own **choice** — it
   will not take a human's choices by force, even to fix things — backed by the
   limit that information does nothing until somebody acts on it (R4,
   2026-09-07). Remove the physics and there is no return; remove the choice
   and there is no BadCode.
4. **Aion as consolation** — *"nothing lived is erased"* is the only thing
   softening the fate of the ninety-nine the launch spends, and of the one it
   leaves (R11, 2026-09-07).
5. **The One Lie** — the single deliberate departure from real physics, named in
   [`discovery-timeline.md`](../gitpush-origin-master/discovery-timeline.md#the-one-lie)
   so no writer ever adds a second. It stays named there and binds here.

**The division of labour: GPOM demonstrates, the Storyverse argues.** Every
editorial pass since 2026-06 pushed metaphysics down and out of the acts; this
folder is where it went.

## Scope guard

- **NO VILLAINS. This governs everything else.** Capitalism is not the enemy.
  Free markets work, money is a good invention, the material world is good, and
  **materialism worked** — three-quarters of humanity in extreme poverty in 1820,
  roughly one in ten now. Concede that **first, always**; Pinker and Rosling are
  our witnesses, not our opponents. Galileo is a **hero**. Science is the honest
  part. Billionaires are people winning at the thing we all agreed to measure.
  **The argument is distributional, never accusatory — we change the scoreboard,
  not the person.** *And no villains is not no accountability* (ruled
  2026-09-07, R10): hatred of a person is the category error; a debt is not,
  and a character may demand restitution, removal or repair without the story
  ruling them wrong or the target evil. The test on every draft: *if a reader
  finishes it wanting to **hate** somebody, it's wrong; if they finish wanting a
  decision reversed and its beneficiary to pay, that is the brief.*
- **Politics first, still.** The Storyverse is not an escape from BadCode's
  brief into cosmology. Its punchline is economic: if the yield of the universe
  is experience, then hoarding claims on the shadow is the stupidest possible
  trade. A Storyverse piece with no power or money in it is off-brief.
- **Earn it.** Public surfaces still lead with politics and economics. This
  surfaces later and louder as the arc grows. Don't front-load the metaphysics.
- **Never explain on a surface; demonstrate.** Comprehension and emotion must
  arrive in the same instant.
- **Full payload, changed warrant.** Keep the metaphysics at full strength — the
  work that gets dismissed is never dismissed for going far out, but for
  **borrowing physics' authority for a claim physics doesn't make.** So: not
  *claiming* → *hypothesising*, but **claiming → betting.** The test on every
  draft is **"could a physicist read this without wincing?"** — a test about
  warrant, not payload, and passable at full strength. See
  [`doctrine.md`](./doctrine.md#the-warrant--how-we-say-all-of-this).
- **Never name the frame.** No *"this isn't a religion,"* no *"this isn't a
  cult,"* no ironic use of *woo*. **Naming a frame invokes it.** The receipts, the
  One Lie and [`refutation.md`](./refutation.md) are internal guardrails that work
  by the text simply behaving that way — never by announcing it.
- **Ban the simulation vocabulary** — not just the word. No operators, no
  admins, no exits, no red pills, no glitch, no NPCs. The world is real the way
  a photograph is real. Same for the woo vocabulary: *energy* (non-physics),
  *vibration*, *manifest*, *the universe wants*, *awakening*, *divine*. And
  **"spiritual" is banked** — banned early, reclaimed later once we have standing.
