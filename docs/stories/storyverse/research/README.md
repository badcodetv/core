# Storyverse research base

Ten deep-research briefs, commissioned 2026-08-07 as a parallel fan-out to put
real, cited scholarship under the Storyverse's fiction. Each brief is written for
writers, not for reviewers: dense, quotable, with sources inline, claims tiered
by how settled they are, and — the part that matters most — **the attack surface
marked**, so we know exactly where a sceptic would hit us and can write around it
rather than get caught by it.

**These are evidence, not canon.** They are raw and occasionally contradict each
other. What we actually assert lives in [`../doctrine.md`](../doctrine.md); the
open calls live in [`../decisions.md`](../decisions.md).

## How to use these

- **Check the tier before you assert.** Every brief labels its claims. Anything
  above a minority-but-published tier is a wager and must be written as one.
- **Read the attack surface before you write the line.** Each brief ends with the
  strongest objection a specialist would make. If a line can't survive it,
  rewrite the line, not the objection.
- **Attribute in the text.** "Hoffman argues," "on Kastrup's account," "in
  Drake's much-quoted translation." Fiction that quotes its sources accurately is
  unfalsifiable *as fiction*.
- **They disagree with each other, and that's useful.** Kastrup vs Goff, IIT vs
  Global Workspace, Seth vs Hoffman on identical phenomenology. Real intellectual
  movements are full of people who despise each other — more interesting *and*
  more accurate than a united front of visionaries.
