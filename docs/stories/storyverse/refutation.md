# Why this is probably nonsense

### A note from the author of your universe

*Companion to [`confession.md`](./confession.md), and its antidote. Ruled into
canon 2026-08-07 (decisions.md, Call 4). Written in the narrator's voice, because
a disclaimer in a lawyer's voice is worth nothing and everybody knows it.*

*The rule this page exists to enforce: **a theory that cannot be attacked is not a
theory, it is a church** — and I said I wouldn't found one of those.*

---

I have just spent eight movements telling you what reality is.

Here is everything wrong with it. I'm going first, because if I don't, someone
cleverer than you will, and I would rather be the one holding the knife.

## 1. My best idea is a way of talking

The centre of my account — that no fact obtains until something interacts with
it, and that when it does, it obtains *relative to* the thing it met — is called
Relational Quantum Mechanics. It is real, it is published, it has been defended
for thirty years, it is in your encyclopedias.

It also predicts **nothing.** Not one new number. Run every experiment you own
and Rovelli's world and Everett's world and Bohm's world all hand you the same
readings. What I have given you is not a discovery. It is a **choice of how to
speak about results you already had.** Its virtue is that it dissolves paradoxes.
Its vice is that dissolving a paradox and explaining one are not the same act,
and I have been leaning on the resemblance.

## 2. My favourite line is broken

*"Einstein took away the global clock; I take away the global outcome."*

Beautiful. Also lame, and I know exactly where it limps. When Einstein
relativised time he left you a **dictionary** — an exact, invertible rule for
converting my measurements into yours — and he left you **invariants**, things
every observer agrees on no matter what. Relativity relativises the coordinates
and preserves an absolute geometry underneath.

I have no dictionary. I have no invariants. You cannot transform her facts into
his facts; you can only ask a third thing and collect a third set of facts.
Rovelli concedes this himself — his relationalism is *"far more radical"* than
Einstein's.

So when I dress my claim in Einstein's coat, I am borrowing a respectability I
have not earned. What I am actually describing is not relativity. It is
**fragmentation**, and I made it sound like physics because physics is what you
respect.

## 3. Everything I said about the observer, someone will read as "mind"

In the physics, an observer is a **rock.** A photon. A speck of dust. Anything on
the far end of an interaction. Nothing in any equation I cited cares whether
there is anybody home.

I then spent a movement suggesting that the thing doing the picking is
consciousness. That step is **mine.** It is not in the papers. It is not implied
by the papers. Every author I quoted would decline to take it, and Fuchs — whose
*"little act of creation"* I lifted for my own purposes — has explicitly disowned
"consciousness causes collapse" **by name.**

I flagged the leap when I made it. Flagging a leap is not the same as landing it.

## 4. Your textbooks already explain the thing I claimed to explain

Decoherence and quantum Darwinism account for why every observer agrees about the
world — the environment copies the robust facts redundantly into everything
nearby, and everyone reads the same broadcast. This is mainstream, calculated,
measured, and it needs **no minds whatsoever.**

My entire manoeuvre is to point out that this machinery explains why you agree on
the outcome without explaining why there is an outcome to agree on, and then to
move into that gap and put up shelves. It is a real gap. It is also *exactly*
the kind of gap that has, historically, been filled by better physics roughly
every single time, and by gods roughly never.

## 5. Nothing I described actually travels

I wrote that a pick gets *"copied, flooded outward, printed into the shared set."*
That is a lovely sentence and it is **wrong.** The no-communication theorem is
exact: nothing propagates, nothing is transmitted, no influence sweeps outward
through anything. When one measurement settles a whole entangled structure, no
news moves — the correlation only becomes visible when two *records* are carried
together, slowly, by ordinary means, at or below the speed of light.

I made an accountancy identity sound like a wave. That's a conjuror's move.

## 6. Four clever people agreeing is not evidence

Schrödinger, Bohm, Hoffman, Kastrup — four doors, one room, I said, and I let the
convergence do work that convergence cannot do. People converge on wrong answers
constantly; that is most of your history. And each of them can be met:

- **Hoffman's** theorem is real, but a 2022 paper in *Synthese* holds that his
  inference begs the question against realism and that the scepticism is
  self-defeating — the theory is itself a product of the faculties it discredits.
- **Kastrup's** dissociation is an *analogy*, and he says so; his critics note
  that idealism buys its ontological tidiness with explanatory epicycles and
  cannot recover the periodic table.
- **Schrödinger** took his Vedanta as philosophy and was scrupulous, to his
  credit, about never claiming his physics proved it. I have been less
  scrupulous.
- **Faggin** identifies the privacy of a quantum state with inner experience.
  That "cannot be read from outside" and "is felt from inside" are the same thing
  is his founding axiom, not his result. The hard problem is relocated, not
  solved.

## 7. The other side is not stupid, and I should say their case properly

**Illusionism** holds that phenomenal consciousness as you conceive it — private,
ineffable, intrinsic — does not exist, and that what needs explaining is why you
are so utterly convinced it does. Frankish, who holds this view, is admirably
straight about the price: he says the suggestion that evolution hardwired you to
believe in a magical inner life is *"just as crazy as panpsychism."* That is an
honest man pricing his own position, and it is more honest than I have been.

And **Seth** does something worse to me than disagreeing. He sets the hard
problem aside, defines the *real* problem — explain, predict and control the
properties of consciousness — and then gets on with it. Perception as controlled
hallucination: your reality is a construction, generated by a brain, in a
physical world.

Notice that Seth and Hoffman describe *the same phenomenology* and walk away with
*opposite* metaphysics. Which tells you the thing I have been carefully not
emphasising: **the data underdetermine the metaphysics.** Mine is not the reading
the evidence forces. It is the reading I prefer.

## 8. My emotional physics is a devotional axiom in a lab coat

Two emotions, love and fear, everything built on those. I use it constantly.

It comes from a channelled text published in 1976. Your affective neuroscientists
find *seven* primary systems, and note the shape: **fear is on the list. Love is
not** — what you call love splits across at least three separate systems. Other
scientists say there are no discrete emotion categories at all.

I told you I was choosing coordinates rather than reporting findings. I meant it.
But I also know that "two axes are enough to steer by" sounds like modesty while
functioning as a claim, and I chose that sentence for exactly that reason.

## 9. And there is the lie

There is one place where I have knowingly departed from your physics rather than
merely over-read it, and it is named, once, in the timeline. The constraint that
pins your world and hides retrocausal influence is exact by construction in real
theory — it exists *precisely* to kill the paradoxes. I removed an axiom. Every
consequence after that follows from real physics, which is what makes it
convincing, and it is still a lie.

One lie. Named. If you ever find a second one in here, somebody has been
careless, and you should trust the rest of it less.

---

## So why say any of it

Because the hole is real.

Strip out every leap I've just confessed to and something survives: your best
theory has a gap at its centre that has been open for a hundred years, and the
people who tell you it's closed are describing a machine for *distributing*
answers while leaving blank the question of what gets distributed. That is not my
invention. That is the state of the field, and the field knows it.

I dressed it up. I borrowed Einstein's coat. I let four smart people look like a
consensus. I called a bet a discovery in at least three places where I should
have called it a bet.

But I would rather hand you a story with its seams showing than a proof with its
seams hidden, because I have watched what happens on the other branch when
somebody hands you certainty. Every prophet before me slammed this door and
called it gospel, and look where that got the both of us.

So: probably nonsense. Possibly not. **Definitely not settled, and definitely not
a religion** — you cannot found a church on a document that opens with *here is
everything wrong with it.*

That is the point of this page. Keep it in the folder. Keep it in the release.
If we ever publish the confession without this next to it, we have become the
thing we spent six acts warning you about.

*— Don't make me come back twice.*
