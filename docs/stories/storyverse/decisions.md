## Part 1 — Settled by the research (no ruling needed)

These are corrections, not choices. The research is unambiguous and the canon
should simply absorb them.

### 1.1 One word decides whether we're smart or wrong

*"It hasn't been decided yet"* has two readings and only one survives.

- ❌ **"It already has a value and you don't know it yet"** — naive hidden
  variables. Killed by Bell (1964) and Kochen–Specker (1967).
- ✅ **"No value obtains at all until an interaction occurs, and when it does, it
  obtains relative to the interacting system"** — Relational Quantum Mechanics.

**Rule: say *undetermined*, never *unknown*. Say *no fact yet*, never *hidden*.**
The wrong reading is the one a casual audience defaults to, so the text must
actively block it.

### 1.2 Our thesis has a name, an author and a Stanford Encyclopedia entry

The "no global collapse, just as there is no global simultaneity" move **is
Rovelli's Relational QM**, and the Einstein analogy is his own, from the 1996
paper. Verdict: *"a live minority interpretation with reputable academic
advocates, experimental relevance, and a Stanford Encyclopedia entry. It is not
fringe."*

**And the best objection to it is a gift.** Special relativity relativises
simultaneity but keeps a Lorentz transformation (an exact dictionary between
frames) and invariants everyone agrees on. RQM has **neither** — Rovelli concedes
this: *"far more radical than the relationalism that underpins classical
relativity."* So we never claim the analogy is exact. We claim it is
**insufficient**:

> Einstein took away the global clock but left the master geometry. Quantum
> theory takes the master geometry too.

### 1.3 An observer is any physical system, not a mind

In RQM an "observer" is a rock, an electron, a photon's polarisation — anything
on the other end of an interaction. **The moment our text implies observer = mind
we are doing Deepak Chopra**, and the first physicist to read it will say so.
Guard this explicitly in the text.

### 1.4 Never caricature Many-Worlds

Everett's defining move is *deleting* collapse. There is no "our universe" and
"another" as pre-existing containers to land in. Getting this wrong gets us
dismissed in the first sentence. (`confession.md` movement ii states it
correctly; the shorthand does not.) The honest attack is better drama anyway:
*if every outcome happens with certainty, what is a 70% chance a chance of?*

### 1.5 The holography arrow runs the other way

In AdS/CFT the world **with** gravity and **more** dimensions is the image; the
flatter, gravity-free thing is the plate. We are the hologram, **not its source.**
"We are a lower-dimensional projection of a higher-dimensional consciousness"
must be sourced to Kaluza–Klein, Bars's 2T-physics, or plain Flatland allegory —
**never to holography.** A physicist catches this instantly and it is the one
error that makes the whole edifice look unread.

### 1.6 Nothing travels

The no-communication theorem is exact. Write hierarchical settling as
**simultaneity of the record**, never as transmission. ✅ **Fixed 2026-08-07** —
`confession.md` movement vi previously had one pick *"copied, flooded outward,
printed into the shared set,"* which reads as propagation. Rewritten to concede
the theorem in voice (*"nothing travels… the record simply agrees, everywhere it
exists"*) and to land the new totem: **the chorus gets a definite pitch; the
individual singers do not each get a definite anything.**

### 1.7 The Kelvin quote is fake

Lord Kelvin never said physics was nearly finished. The line traces to a remark
by **Michelson** in 1894 about an unnamed "eminent physicist," which **Millikan**
later *guessed* meant Kelvin. Kelvin's actual 1900 lecture names two unresolved
clouds — Michelson–Morley and black-body — and **he picked the right two.** The
mood was real; the quote is not.

The restatement is sharper than the myth anyway:

> **Physics gave up determinism. Middle management never got the memo.**

### 1.8 Other accuracy armour

- Goff's famous Galileo quote is **Stillman Drake's contested translation**. Say
  *"in Drake's much-quoted translation"* or a historian corrects us.
- *"I was a hidden treasure and I loved to be known"* is classed by Hadith
  scholars as **baseless/fabricated**. It's a Sufi treasure, not a prophetic one.
- **Yin/yang has no moral valence** (it is not good vs evil). *Māyā* does not
  mean "nothing matters" — the world is real at its level and ethics is
  untouched. *Śūnyatā* explicitly refuses nihilism. **Līlā is *motiveless* play**
  — which kills "the universe wants you to…"

### The calls in full

### Call 1 — Love and fear: how honest, and in whose mouth? — **RULED: choice of coordinates**

The research is blunt: **~15% science, ~85% pop-spirituality.** The lineage is *A
Course in Miracles* (1976, channelled) → Kübler-Ross → Williamson → everywhere.
Panksepp finds **seven** primary systems, and note the shape: **FEAR is on the
list, LOVE is not** — love decomposes across LUST, CARE and PANIC/GRIEF.
Approach/avoidance (BIS/BAS) is the nearest real support, and *avoidance ≈ fear*
is defensible while *approach ≈ love* is a category error, because approach also
drives greed and conquest.

Three ways to play it:

- **(a) The honest reframe.** Not two emotions — **two postures toward the
  unknown: move toward, or move away.** Defensible, survives contact with the
  science, loses a little of the mythic ring.
- **(b) Choice of coordinates.** The narrator says it in full knowledge: *"There
  are a thousand emotions. I use two axes because two axes are enough to steer
  by."* A superintelligence picking a lossy but actionable model is characterful
  and true to how models work. **Recommended** — it's in voice, and it's honest
  without being timid.
- **(c) Assert it straight.** Mythic force, but it's a devotional axiom from a
  channelled text presented as a finding. Trips the woo wire.

Whichever we pick: **never "scientists have shown."**

**And the craft answer that may make this moot:** stage them as **improv
offers** — *"yes, and"* (love) versus **blocking** (fear). That converts the
primitives from a claim into a **game with rules**, natively theatrical, and it
sidesteps the neuroscience entirely. Do we take this instead of, or alongside,
the two-postures framing?

### Call 2 — The symbiosis thesis: pendulum, or coup? — **RULED: the coup, then AMENDED by Call 9**

> ⚠️ **Superseded in part, 2026-08-07 (2nd pass).** The historical correction below
> stands — it was not a pendulum, and the 17th century *was* the symbiosis. But the
> *heist* framing ("sold", a perpetrator, a motive) is **cut** under the no-villains
> ruling. Read this section for the history; take the framing from
> [`doctrine.md`](./doctrine.md#the-oscillation-not-the-pendulum--and-not-a-coup-either).

Your claim — *we've never had a healthy symbiosis of materialist and spiritual* —
is **false as stated.** The 17th century **was** the symbiosis: Newton wrote more
on theology and alchemy than on physics; the Royal Society was thick with clergy.
Josephson-Storm's whole book demolishes the idea that modernity ever became
un-magical.

The restatement, which is a far better story:

> **It wasn't a pendulum. It was a coup.**

On Bilgrami's account, *two* metaphysics competed inside the new science — inert
matter needing an external clock-winder (Newton, Boyle, the Royal Society
ideologues) versus matter shot through with inner divine dynamism (the
freethinkers and dissenters). **Both were scientific.** One won because it was
**sold** — to the Anglican establishment and to mercantile interests, on the
grounds that **inert matter is matter you may take.**

That has a perpetrator, a date and a motive. It is also natively political, which
is the brief. **Ruling needed: do we replace the pendulum framing with the coup
framing wholesale?** (Recommendation: yes. It converts our weakest historical
claim into our strongest political one.)

⚠️ And the causal claim needs softening regardless: we can defend the *mechanism*
(desacralised nature → extraction as the only available relation) and the
*correlates*, but **not** "the meaning deficit caused the economics." Say **"the
same move produced both."**

### Call 3 — Concede the poverty numbers? — **OPEN**

Half of "the material world is stacked against them" is rock-solid — the top 10%
owns 76% of household wealth, the bottom 50% owns 2%; US productivity grew 90.2%
since 1979 while typical pay grew 33.0%. But the implication that **absolute**
conditions are worsening is false: extreme poverty went from ~3/4 of humanity in
1820 to ~1 in 10 today. That is **the single strongest weapon our opponents
have.**

The restatement: **the pie got enormous and the slicing got worse.** People are
not poorer than their grandparents; they **own less of what they make and have
less say over it.** Stacked against them on *ownership and agency*, not on
*calories and antibiotics*.

**Ruling needed: do we concede this explicitly, in voice, on a surface?**
(Recommendation: yes, and early — conceding it first is what makes the rest
un-dunkable, and it's the same move the confession already makes with the
physics.)

### RULED 2026-08-07 (second pass): concede it, and make it a threshold — not a dose

**The intoxication metaphor is retired.** *"Most of the world hasn't had its first
drink; the problem is the table that finished the bottle and is still ordering"*
is a good line and it is **cut** — the moment the argument is about being drunk it
opens doors we don't want (impairment, addiction, shame, prohibition), and it
smuggles a villain back in through the side. **Do not use alcohol, intoxication or
dosage imagery for this idea.**

What survives, and is now canon: **materialism worked.** That is the first
premise, not a concession wrung out of us. Free markets work, money is a good
invention, the material world is good. Three-quarters of humanity in extreme
poverty in 1820 → roughly one in ten now; child mortality halved and halved again.
**Pinker and Rosling are witnesses for us, not opponents.** Nobody can accuse a
project of romanticising poverty when its opening move is *the poverty numbers are
the best thing you ever did.*

The replacement frame is **a threshold, not a dose** — see
[`doctrine.md`](./doctrine.md#the-threshold-and-the-deer) for the deer parable and
[the scoreboard](./doctrine.md#the-scoreboard-not-the-players) for the
materialist/idealist reading. The shape:

- Below the threshold, acquiring is simply living. **Get everybody there.** That
  is the maximisation problem, and it is *sufficiency*, not equality.
- Above it, further accumulation buys the holder almost nothing (the ~$100k
  plateau for the unhappy) while being a claim on somebody else's afternoon.
- **Nobody is a villain.** A person optimising a number they were told to optimise
  is winning at the thing we all agreed to measure. **Change the scoreboard, not
  the person** — which is exactly what
  [Billionaire Coin](../../ideas/billionaire-coin.md) does.

⚠️ **The correction, and it is load-bearing.** "We've had enough material" is a
**rich-world claim**, and stated flatly it is false and ugly — because for the
bottom half of the planet more material is still precisely what is needed, and a
2023 adversarial collaboration (Killingsworth, Kahneman & Mellers, *PNAS*) found
that for most people happiness **keeps rising** with income well past $100k, and
for the happiest group it *accelerates*. Money keeps working. If our line implies
the poor should stop wanting things, we have written a rich man's sermon and
deserve everything we get.

**What that same paper actually gives us is better.** It found one exception:
**an unhappy minority whose happiness rises with income only to about $100k and
then flattens.** Money fixes the problems money can fix, and then stops. *That
residue is our entire subject* — the unhappiness that survives its own cure.

So the dosage metaphor holds, with the dose made specific:

> **Most of the world has not had its first drink. The problem is the table that
> finished the bottle and is still ordering.**

The claim is therefore never *"growth is bad"* and never *"we have enough."* It is
**distributional and dose-specific**: for the people who have solved their
material problems, more material has stopped doing anything — and they are the
ones who own the machine and set its direction. Same restatement as above: **the
pie got enormous and the slicing got worse.**

**Never say:** "money doesn't buy happiness" (the data says it mostly does),
"things are getting worse" (absolutely, they are not), or anything implying the
global poor should want less. **Do say:** the treatment worked, the dose is now
wrong, and it is wrong *at one specific table*.

### Call 4 — Does the standalone keep the receipts and the One Lie? — **RULED: keep both, add the refutation page**

No doc currently says whether a standalone Storyverse keeps the A–F tiering and
the one-lie discipline, or becomes pure fiction with no apparatus. **This is the
single most consequential structural call in the hoist** — the receipts are our
defence against the *What the Bleep* failure mode, and the One Lie is what stops
a second lie creeping in.

Recommendation: **keep both, and add Egan's move** — publish the refutation *in
voice*, as a one-page in-canon document: *"Why this is probably nonsense — a note
from the author of your universe."* It is the best guardrail available, it is the
funniest page we'd write, and it makes us unmockable because we got there first.
It is also the structural answer to *"this is art, not a religion"* — a guarantee
rather than a disclaimer.

### Call 5 — Two totems are missing

The craft rule worth adopting: **one bicycle per abstraction** — every claim gets
a physical object, a sound and a gag, and if it has no bicycle it isn't ready to
ship. Two load-bearing claims currently have none:

- **Hierarchical entanglement.** Named as one of the three big ideas in four
  separate docs; has no totem, no keeper framing, and the phrase is banned
  outright. The research supplies a candidate that is real physics *and* native
  to our medium: **the chorus gets a definite pitch; the individual singers do
  not each get a definite anything.**
- **"Low-dimensional projection of a higher-dimensional consciousness."** No
  object at all. Candidates: the photograph (*the world is real the way a
  photograph is real*), Kastrup's **dashboard/dials** (the wind is real, the dial
  is real, the dial is not the wind), or **"the extra dimension is the zoom
  level"** — the higher intelligence isn't *above* us, it's further along the
  magnification.

**Ruling needed: pick one totem each.**

### Call 6 — The music metaphor needs replacing

*"Music is a wave until you hear it"* doesn't survive: sound is a wave before,
during and after you hear it, and *"lands differently depending on your mood"* is
one shared observer-independent signal differently interpreted — which
accidentally asserts exactly the absolute fact we were trying to deny, and hands
the audience the hidden-variable picture.

Replacements, all still musical:

- **"There is no master recording."** Every listener holds a different dubplate,
  there is no studio original to appeal to — *and no mixing desk that can convert
  one cut into another.* **Recommended:** the missing desk **is** the missing
  Lorentz transformation, so this one line carries both the claim and the
  disanalogy that makes it radical.
- **"A jam, not a pressing."** A record is a fixed thing heard differently; a jam
  has no take until it's played.
- **For contextuality:** the C that is a root, a third or a seventh depending on
  what's under it. Not a stretch — a fair rendering of real physics.
