## What it is
BadCode's account of what reality is and what we are inside it: **reality has not decided yet, and you are one of
the things deciding**, so the world is a stage to be played, not a warehouse to be counted, and the only wealth that
doesn't run out is experience. Its punchline is economic: hoarding claims on the shadow is the stupidest trade. It is
art, and a wager, never a proof. Told as a **history lesson**: we never assert the metaphysics; we show that the
people who built the instruments never accepted the boundary. Written as text (confession, refutation, doctrine);
no song, comic or video made.

## Story-only rules
- **No villains.** Concede first, always: markets work, money is a good invention, materialism worked. The argument is
  distributional, never accusatory. No villains is not no accountability: if a reader finishes wanting to *hate*
  somebody, it's wrong; wanting a decision reversed and its beneficiary to pay is the brief.
- **Full payload, changed warrant:** claiming → betting. Test: "could a physicist read this without wincing?"
  Three layers: what the apparatus did is certain; what explains it is certain inside the fiction; the philosophy is the bet.
- Science side: borrow the credible lineage, never the woo. Theology side: point at the commonalities, never found a church.
- **Never name the frame** ("this isn't a religion / cult", ironic "woo"): naming a frame invokes it.
- **Banned words:** simulation vocabulary (operators, admins, exits, red pills, glitch, NPCs) and woo vocabulary
  (energy as non-physics, vibration, manifest, the universe wants, awakening, divine). "Spiritual" is banked.
- Never explain on a surface; demonstrate. Don't front-load the metaphysics; lead with politics and economics.
- Never publish the confession without the refutation next to it. The confession is never script source for a surface.
- The era is "the Age of Natural Philosophy", never "the Gilded Age" (telling.md).
