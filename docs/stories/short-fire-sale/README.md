# Fire Sale

A BadCode short (Jack, 2026-11-21): "Fire Sale itself, as a short: but in a realistic style, use everything from the
original fire sale, then us unsplash to recreate the realistic style".

**The original:** `docs/ideas/fire-sale.md` (mined from the old Storyteller repo) and Jack's 18 painted stills and 6
character sheets in `Desktop\Youtube Vids\animation\Fire sale` (Oct–Nov 2025): the British car-boot treatment.

**The point:** democracy is for sale through "donations"; whoever buys the most gets the result.
**The way out (Claude's draft, no government policy on record):** donations capped at the same small amount for everyone,
so the auction collapses and the rich man can't outbid a pensioner.
