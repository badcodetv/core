## What it is
A BadCode short (9:16, aim ~25 s): Jack's Fire Sale (2025, painted) remade real-looking. A rainy English car-boot sale
where Jim the wheeler-dealer flogs the country's institutions as framed pictures off his pasting table. Phineas, a
grotesque rich man, arrives in a Rolls with his sweating clerk Larry and buys the lot. Fiona, the weary bargain hunter,
goes home; her phone says Phineas narrowly beat Brian in the polls. Source: `docs/ideas/fire-sale.md` and the 18 stills
and 6 character sheets in `Desktop\Youtube Vids\animation\Fire sale` (the composition and cast reference).
State: script draft 1 ([script.md](./script.md)), nothing generated.

## Look
Jack, 2026-11-21: "in a realistic style … use unsplash to recreate the realistic style". Real-looking photographs of an
English car-boot sale in the rain (gazebos, pasting tables, muddy field, vans), a cosy terraced front room, an auction
tent. The painted stills are used for composition, beats and cast only, never their painted look. Flow project: none yet.

## Sound
Not decided. Any Suno track is music and sound only; every spoken line is an AI Studio voice. Voices must sound English:
design them with an English region, never just "British".

## Cast (from the original character sheets, made real; invented faces only)
- **Fiona:** a woman of about 30, long auburn hair, tired eyes, an olive parka, a dark green scarf, a backpack.
- **Jim:** the wheeler-dealer, about 50, round red-cheeked face, a flat cap, a black jumper and dark jacket.
- **Phineas:** the buyer, about 65, heavy, bald, jowly, a three-piece navy pinstripe, a gold signet ring, a cane; arrives in a black Rolls with a chauffeur.
- **Larry:** his clerk, about 45, thin, big black glasses, a crumpled suit and lanyard, sweating, a PURCHASES clipboard.
- **Brian:** the candidate who loses (seen only as a name on a phone; maybe a leaflet).
