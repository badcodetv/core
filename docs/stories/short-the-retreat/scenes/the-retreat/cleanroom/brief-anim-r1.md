# Brief: clips round 1 (image to video)

You write motion prompts for Google Flow's video model (Omni 1.2 Flash, "Frames" mode: the still is the first frame;
8 seconds; 9:16). Each prompt says what MOVES, in order, from the first frame, and what stays still. Research first
(how image-to-video prompts are best written for a locked or slow camera), then write.

Write `anim-r1.json` in this folder: a list of `{"key", "still", "motion"}`, two DIFFERENT takes per still
(`<still>a`, `<still>b`, e.g. `R01a`, `R01b`). Look at each still (in the reference folder given to you) before writing.

## The short
A comedy played straight, real-looking, ~20 seconds. An expensive "detox from materialism" retreat in a bamboo resort
where nobody is detoxing from anything; then, behind the resort, an old groundskeeper on his break watches the wind in
the trees and pets his dog, the only calm person in the film. The cut uses about 2 to 4 seconds of each clip, so the
action must start within the first second.

## Rules for every clip
- Nobody's mouth moves except where a still below says so; no new talking.
- Words in a picture stay readable and spelled the same; no new text appears.
- The man in the balaclava keeps it exactly as it is (white ring, red triangle over his own left eye), and no skin
  ever shows on him: navy roll-neck at the neck, gloves at the wrists.
- No new people enter. Faces keep their identity. No cuts inside a clip.
- Plain description of the motion; no camera brand words.

## The stills
- `R01` (the quote): a golden sunset through bamboo. Gentle: leaves stir, light shimmers, a slow drift; the empty sky
  at the top stays clear (a quote is added there later).
- `R02` (the sign): the carved wooden sign in the jungle. Leaves sway around it, dappled light moves; a slow push-in.
- `R03` (the circle): the bro in the foreground, eyes shut, whispering into a phone hidden in his cuff, his lips moving
  a little behind his hand; the guru behind him, soft, palms open, speaking calmly; other guests still.
- `R04` (the gong): the guru has just struck the gong, which shimmers and trembles; the man in the balaclava pushes the
  card machine a little closer to the camera and holds it there, patient.
- `R05` (the gift shop): an empty, hushed shop of £80 stones. Very slow push-in towards one stone; light glints.
- `R06` (the groundskeeper): he sits on his rock with his tea, looking up as the wind moves through the tall trees,
  leaves streaming; he takes a slow sip. Calm. Camera locked.
- `R07` (the dog): the terrier has its paws on his knee, tail wagging; he scratches its head, still looking up at the
  trees with a small smile. Camera locked.
