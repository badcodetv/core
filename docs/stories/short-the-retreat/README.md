# The Retreat

A BadCode short (Jack, 2026-11-21): "i want to take the piss out of materilistic people adn spritiual people then find a
middle ground", then "like ram dass's quote, if you think you're enlightnened spend a day with your family. this
encapsulates it perfectly".

**The idea:** a £3,000 "Detox From Materialism" retreat. A hedge-fund bro sneaks a deal through during meditation; the
guru takes card payments for the closing gong; the exit is through a gift shop selling £80 rocks. Then they go home for
Sunday dinner, both "enlightened", and lose it at the family within seconds.

**The point:** the materialist and the spiritual escapist are both dodging the same thing: ordinary life with other people.
**The middle ground:** money for what money fixes, then time with the people who wind you up. The test of either is Sunday dinner.
