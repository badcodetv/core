## What it is
A BadCode Short, portrait 9:16, 27.6 s. If the rules worked, the street would show it; it doesn't, so you have every
right to rewrite them. The balaclava gang look up at a stack of paper, THE RULES → a sunny island, a filing cabinet
asleep in a hammock (the profits have a bed) → out of the window, people sleeping rough → the gang tear up the rules →
one holds out a blank page and a pen: your turn. Good rules stay; the target is rules that shield their writers (tax
havens) and deference to the people who write them. **CUT** (Kai): Premiere `the-rules.prproj`, sequence
`short-the-rules-1-rules-r3`, frozen to `scenes/the-rules/final.json`. Owed: the overlay fact check, upload.

## Look
**LOCKED.** A windowless council archive basement seen from the floor: ultra-wide worm's-eye, steep converging
verticals, steel shelving like canyon walls, bare fluorescent tubes (one stuttering), cold greenish white, documentary
realism, fine grain. Modern: the gang in muted modern workwear. Every beat keeps this light and grain except the island,
a deliberate break into sunlight. Anchors: `ST1-a` (the picked look; its masks are wrong) and `ST3-a-a` (the opening's
fixed still). Stills from round 5 on are Nano Banana 2.1.

Mask preamble (LOCKED, the fixed opening line of every prompt with the gang in it):

```
Every gang member wears a knitted balaclava covering the whole head, with no eye, nose or mouth holes; a large white ring is stitched round the face, and a red triangle sits high over the wearer's left eye (on the right of the picture, at about 2 o'clock); gloves on both hands, collars and cuffs closed, no skin showing anywhere.
```

## Story-only rules
- The overlay must say what's on the islands. Fact-check before it goes on screen: "little or no tax", never "no tax";
  "flies our flag" is true for Overseas Territories, not Crown Dependencies.
- No caption ever says the missing tax causes rough sleeping; the picture sits next to the rule and the viewer joins it.
- People sleeping rough are shown with dignity: the camera never laughs at them.
- Cut on the words (rough sleepers on "bed", an empty purse on "purse", the bin on "bin").
