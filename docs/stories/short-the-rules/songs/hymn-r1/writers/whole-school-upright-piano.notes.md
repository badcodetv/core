# whole-school-upright-piano — what shaped it

- **The room, not the genre.** The style box describes the event: a 1980s English primary assembly, kids cross-legged on a polished floor, words on the overhead projector, one teacher at the piano. These details come from people's memories of assembly: "the polished floors of assembly halls, the thumping introductory chords on the school piano" (Tes staffroom thread, https://www.tes.com/news/tescoukstaffroom-12), and the older teacher at the piano with words on the OHP (https://fwhittaker.substack.com/p/when-a-knight-won-his-spurs). Come and Praise (BBC, 1978; volume 2 in 1988) sets the era (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Come_and_Praise). The tunes were plain and folksy, and a whole hall sang them together (https://www.christiantoday.com/news/when-a-knight-won-his-spurs-the-lost-genius-of-the-1980s-school-hymn).
- **The roughness is spelled out:** everyone on one melody, behind the beat, flat, loud boys at the front, little ones mumbling. The exclude list removes what Suno would otherwise reach for: trained choir, harmony parts, organ, cathedral reverb and a pop band.
- **How the style box is written.** The main idea goes first, then the feel, then the instrument, the voices and the texture. Negatives go in Exclude, not in Style. Variety is set to off so Suno keeps the wording closer (https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/optimize-suno-v6-style-of-music-prompt). The prompt is v6 rather than Wild because the brief wants realism.
- **The ending.** Guides agree [End] is a hint, not a command, and that describing the ending works better (https://jackrighteous.com/en-gb/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/suno-ai-end-prompt-guide, https://howtopromptsuno.com/common-problems/how-do-i-end-the-song). So the stop is said three times: in the style box ("held on a single piano chord, then dead silence"), as a bracket direction, and as [End]. Exclude adds "fade out, second verse, instrumental outro". Duration is set to 22 s.
- **Gender is null** because a mixed hall of children is the whole point.
