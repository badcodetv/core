# one-small-girl-nervous — notes (redo)

Rewritten after the sameness check flagged "on a single" in the style box: every phrase is new wording; the word "single" is gone.

What shaped it:
- **One voice only.** Suno adds choirs and backing voices when the prompt hints at a group ("assembly", "school" could). Borrowed the tested fix: a plain "one clear lead singer" clause in the style box, a `[Verse - Solo Lead Vocal Only]` tag in the lyrics, and an exclude list of choir, group, backing, harmony, layered and singalong terms. Exclude improves the odds and does not guarantee. https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/stop-suno-adding-crowd-vocals-choirs-backing-voices
- **Child-voice keywords.** Community guides list "girl child vocal" / "child voice" style words and suggest a [solo] marker; they note child voices are a weak spot (they can sound uncanny), so the age and the untrained nerves are said plainly rather than stacked as adjectives. https://sunohk.pages.dev/suno-tutorial/suno-timbre-vocal-prompt-guide/ · https://medium.com/@aitooldiscovery/suno-reddit-tips-and-prompts-what-r-sunoai-users-actually-do-65b59d51bcad
- **Concrete instrument over adjectives.** Same Reddit digest: name the instrument. So "gedackt flute pipe": the gedackt is the soft, bland, flute-like stopped stop that hymn-accompaniment teaching uses under a soloist, and a chamber organ has few stops and often no pedals, so no bass. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gedackt · https://sites.google.com/view/organtrainingcourse/lessons/lesson-9-refining-registration · https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/502327
- **Dry.** "Raw and dry like a home video" in place of documentary wording; reverb and hall reverb excluded, piano and strings out so the organ is the only bed.
- Settings kept from the brief's intent: v6, variety off, weirdness 30, style influence 80, female, 20 s, no max mode. The ending is carried by the last lyric tag (organ lets go, then nothing).
