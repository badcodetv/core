# edwardian-sunday-school-78 (redo) — what shaped it

Redo for the round's shared-wording flag ("the last word" in the lyric directions). Every bracket and the style box are reworded from scratch; the closing cue now names the word itself ("bin") instead of describing its position.

- **The recording.** Acoustic-era horn capture covered roughly 250 Hz to 2.5 kHz (Sound On Sound) or about 100 to 2,500 Hz (Library of Congress), with the low end weakest. Hence "thin, nasal, honky, nothing below the tenor range", plus excluding bass and low end. Horn recording suited small groups, so a class of about twenty is plausible.
  - https://www.soundonsound.com/node/4921200
  - https://loc.gov/loc/lcib/1106/records.html
  - https://www.ksl.com/article/50015992/how-the-tabernacle-choir-made-its-first-recording-110-years-ago
- **Real precedent.** The Discography of American Historical Recordings lists a 1910 Gramophone session by a children's chorus "with harmonium", so the line-up is true to the period.
  - https://adpprod2.library.ucsb.edu/index.php/date/browse/1910-08-05
- **The harmonium.** A treadle pump organ gets louder the faster it's pumped, and its pitch wanders because the wind pressure is hard to hold steady. That gave "wind rises and sags with the treadles, pitch leans flat when the pumping slows". UK instruments were usually suction reed organs.
  - https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Pump_Organ
  - https://classical-music.com/features/instruments/harmonium-history-pump-organ
- **Suno v6 tags.** Brackets are cues, not commands, and a full sentence carries intent better than a bare tag. Keep the cues short and give each section one clear exit. Ending tags aren't guaranteed, so the silence after the hymn sits in the brackets and in the 20 s duration, and can be trimmed afterwards with Crop.
  - https://jackrighteous.com/en-gb/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/suno-ai-4-5-metatag-tutorial
  - https://jackrighteous.com/de-gb/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/suno-ai-end-prompt-guide
- **Settings.** v6 (not Wild), so the period recording stays on brief. Weirdness 40 allows a little wobble without pulling it toward modern pop. Style influence 70 holds the shellac sound. No vocal gender, because it's a mixed children's class.
