# Research notes: hymn-r1 ideas (2026-11-10)

- British school assembly hymns: the BBC's *Come and Praise* (1978; vol. 2 1988) was the standard book; singing was led from the school piano, "thumping introductory chords", an older teacher at the piano, words on an overhead projector, children cross-legged in the hall. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Come_and_Praise · https://fwhittaker.substack.com/p/when-a-knight-won-his-spurs
- The singing itself was ragged: some pupils kept going after everyone stopped, lines got raucous, verses skipped. https://www.christiantoday.com/news/when-a-knight-won-his-spurs-the-lost-genius-of-the-1980s-school-hymn
- Victorian hymnals were written for "a simple Organ, or, for school use, Harmonium"; hymns were the folk song of Victorian Britain, sung at home, school and church; later diatonic tunes were often sung in unison. https://www.victorianweb.org/victorian/mt/dbscott/5.html · https://jesus4u.co.uk/study-sheets/victorian_hymns
- The lyric is close to common metre (8.6.8.6), the commonest hymn metre. https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/Hymn_tune
- Boy treble / chorister vocals: "boy soprano", "solo chorister boy, pure vibrato-free treble", cathedral reverb as a separate production cue; Suno drifts to clean adult soprano, so steer with exclusions. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boy_soprano · https://sunohk.pages.dev/blog/suno-vocal-prompt-palette-guide/ · https://tts-agi-moss-voice-profiles.static.hf.space/vocals/genre_choirboy_solo.html
- Hymn style strings in the wild: "hymn-like, congregational vocal, piano or organ, steady tempo, minimal FX, natural room reverb". Describe what the organ DOES (sustained chords, carries melody, tone: warm pipe vs bright church organ), not just name it. https://jackrighteous.com/en-gb/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/suno-ai-worship-inspirational-tag-guide-v5 · https://suno.com/id/music-library/instruments/organ
- Keep the style prompt short (under ~100 words); first words weigh most; "live", "raw", "unison" push towards imprecise, natural singing. https://medium.com/@aitooldiscovery/suno-reddit-tips-and-prompts-what-r-sunoai-users-actually-do-65b59d51bcad
- Short pieces: no long intro, start on the hook, three or four landmarks, a resolved ending, judge the first and last three seconds. https://www.nemovideo.com/blog/ai-video-workflows/suno-v6-short-video-music
- Endings on v6: [End] is not a guaranteed command; say the ending in plain words ("resolve on the tonic, no fade, let the last chord ring briefly, then stop"; give a length in bars or seconds); Crop in the editor trims anything after a good ending. https://jackrighteous.com/en-gb/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/suno-ai-end-prompt-guide
- No fixed 20-second setting is documented for v6 (up to 8 minutes per generation); expect to crop. https://help.suno.com/en/articles/13924929
