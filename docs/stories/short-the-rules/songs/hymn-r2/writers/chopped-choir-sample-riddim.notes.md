# chopped-choir-sample-riddim — notes

What shaped it:

- **Grime riddim grammar.** Grime sits at about 140 bpm (138–142), snare/clap on two and four; the core is one raw unfiltered square wave playing short stabs, one repeated melodic idea (a cold stab or a *string preset*), dry "eski clicks" on the off-beats, and the beat dropping to almost nothing so a bar can land; the DJ "reload" (spinning the record back) is part of the culture. Early grime was made on FruityLoops on home PCs, so cheap presets are part of the sound: hence "bedroom", "cheap string-preset stab", "dry eski clicks", "reload rewind spinback". https://orphiq.com/resources/what-is-grime-music , https://www.masterclass.com/articles/grime-music-guide , https://www.attackmagazine.com/technique/tutorials/grime-synth-basics/2/
- **The sample conceit.** No grime track sampling a children's hymn turned up (search returned only sample-pack listings and drill "children's choir" loops on Looperman), so the prompt describes the sample physically instead of by reference: crackly 1950s assembly record, pitched up a semitone, tinny mono, far away, sliced. https://www.looperman.com/loops/detail/388827/uk-drill-x-central-cee-type-loop-by-landsharkszn-free-138bpm-uk-drill-choir-loop
- **Suno prompting.** Community guides agree the opening words of the style box weigh most (so "bedroom grime instrumental 140 bpm ... sampled children's choir" leads), keep vinyl/lo-fi artifact words to one or two (only "crackly" and "dusty"), describe sounds rather than naming artists, and put per-section direction in bracket tags in the lyrics. https://medium.com/@aitooldiscovery/suno-reddit-tips-and-prompts-what-r-sunoai-users-actually-do-65b59d51bcad , https://jackrighteous.com/de-us/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/suno-ai-vinyl-sound-prompts-warm-analog-style-music , https://frankx.ai/guides/suno-prompt-playbook
- **Structure for 20 s.** Lines 1–2 over the riddim; line 3 alone in the drop-out (the "beat drops to almost nothing so a bar can land" move, given to the choir instead of an MC); a rewind + gunshot hit to slam back for the punchline; hard stop into the silent window.
- **Settings.** v6 (not Wild) to keep the four lines intact; weirdness 62 / styleInfluence 72 to push the odd pairing while holding the brief; vocalGender null, because "female" risks an adult woman instead of children; exclude carries "MC, rap, spoken word" so Suno does not add a grime MC over the choir. Untested guess: Suno may still render the choir as live voices rather than a chopped sample.
