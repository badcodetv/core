# assembly-over-eskibeat — what shaped it

- **The beat in eskibeat's own terms.** Complex's tour of Wiley's eskibeat gives the vocabulary used in the style box: "gliding squarewave basslines" with "hollow, digital rumbles", the "Eski click" as a "ping-ponging sound effect straight out of an 8-Bit Nintendo game", "plinky" melodies off Korg Triton / E-mu romplers, and "sheer minimalism and emptiness", "frosty, digital", "cold, mechanical, strange". So: hollow square bass, 8-bit ping-pong clicks, one frosty plinking stab, long silences.
  https://www.complex.com/music/2019/08/a-journey-through-wiley-eskibeat/
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eskimo_(grime_beat)
- **The children.** Another Brick in the Wall's choir was a north London school (Islington Green), 23 kids overdubbed 12 times to sound like a crowd. That's why the prompt says "two hundred kids in unison", for mass without polish, plus the echoey hall.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Another_Brick_in_the_Wall
- **Prompt shape.** Jack Righteous's v6 guide: one lead genre, then rhythm → two or three signature instruments → vocal role → mood → production; unwanted traits go in Exclude, not Styles; Variety toward 0 keeps the wording closer. So grime leads, the choir is the vocal role, and Variety is off. Without that, Suno might "fix" the mismatch by putting the kids on the beat.
  https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/optimize-suno-v6-style-of-music-prompt
- **Exclude.** Exclude is a nudge, and a style box that asks hard for group vocals can override it, so "solo singer" is excluded and the unison crowd is stated positively.
  https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/stop-suno-adding-crowd-vocals-choirs-backing-voices
- **Separating the two worlds.** The reverb goes on the children only and the beat stays bone-dry, so the two sound like different rooms and the mismatch reads as deliberate.
- **Settings.** v6 rather than Wild, because the oddness is already in the brief. Weirdness 55 lets it accept the clash. Style influence 70. Duration 20 s, which leaves a one-bar intro (about 1.7 s at 140) so the beat is heard bare before the school comes in. "[End: beat cuts dead, no tail]" asks for the dead stop after "bin".
