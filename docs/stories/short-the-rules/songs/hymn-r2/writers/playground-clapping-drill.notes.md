# playground-clapping-drill: notes

The idea: five or six ten-year-olds doing the hymn as a clapping and skipping rhyme, over a UK drill beat that belongs to a much harder song. The kids' bounce and the beat's menace are meant to stay in two separate worlds.

- **Beat wording.** The prompt says "UK drill", not plain "drill", because guides say that pulls the London sound. It names the sliding/gliding 808 (the genre's most cited marker) and the broken, gappy "skippy" hats. It puts kick and snare at half the hat rate, which is where the half-time feel comes from. Tempo is 142 bpm, inside the 140-145 range the guides give. Snare placement is the least agreed detail between sources, so the prompt leaves "snare on 3" out and describes the half-time feel instead.
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UK_drill
  - https://beatstorapon.com/blog/how-to-make-a-drill-beat/
  - https://ampedstudio.com/blog/what-is-drill-music/
  - https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/what-is-drill-music-suno-ai-guide (it says to describe drill as a set of relationships: sparse base, mobile sub, minimal eerie motif, dry low-end mix)
- **Child voices.** Community guides warn that a bare "children's choir" comes out uncanny or choral. So the prompt asks for "real ... imperfect, unpolished ... 5 or 6 ... in unison", the wording from the Suno-hosted examples found. It says "NOT a rapper" and excludes "male rapper" and "choir", because drill pulls in a rapper and group energy pulls in a choir.
  - https://sunohk.pages.dev/blog/suno-vocal-prompt-palette-guide/
  - https://hookgenius.app/learn/suno-kids-music-prompts/
  - https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/stop-suno-adding-crowd-vocals-choirs-backing-voices
- **The rhyme itself.** In skipping, the rhyme sets the timing of the rope. Clapping games keep palm-to-palm time to the chant. That is why claps and the rope slapping the tarmac are the only acoustic sounds, and why the rope lands on the beat.
  - https://www.schoolhousemuseum.org.au/resources/chants-and-rhymes/
  - https://pgpedia.com/c/clapping-games
- **Settings.**
  - Model v6, not v6-wild: the clash has to be exact, and Suno pitches v6 as "precise ... when you know what you want".
  - Variety "normal": a guide says lower Variety keeps the tags in control, and we still want some room.
  - 15 s, ending with "beat stops dead" so the silence for the tear lands.
  - vocalGender null, because the kids are a mixed group.
  - https://datanorth.ai/news/suno-launches-v6-v6-wild-and-v6-mini
