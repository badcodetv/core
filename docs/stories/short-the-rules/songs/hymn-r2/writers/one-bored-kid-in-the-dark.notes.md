# one-bored-kid-in-the-dark: notes

What shaped the prompt:

- **Eskibeat as the cold grime template.** Wiley's early "eskibeat" is cold, sparse, synthetic, heavy on bass with abrupt rhythm; the template is a raw, unfiltered square wave on short stabs or held notes. Hence "raw detuned square-wave sub bass", "icy metallic ping", and nothing else melodic. Sources: https://www.complex.com/music/a/son-raw/a-journey-through-wiley-eskibeat , https://orphiq.com/resources/what-is-grime-music , https://passionweiss.com/2015/04/23/sub-bass-space-squares-introducing-iglew
- **Name the nearest wrong answer and exclude it.** The grime genre-control guide says the neighbours that take over are hip-hop loop logic, drill drum grammar, trap hat rolls and lush pads. Those go in Exclude (trap/drill hi-hats, snare rolls, pads, strings) along with the brief's own list. Source: https://jackrighteous.com/en-us/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/grime-ai-music-genre-control-guide
- **Half-time 70 bpm instead of 140.** Grime sits around 140, but the guide says BPM is not proof of genre. Half-time keeps the grime pulse while leaving room for a bass that moves only on the downbeat, so four lines fill about 20 seconds.
- **Style Influence high, Weirdness middling.** Strong Style Influence makes the descriptors mandatory, which an unusual pairing (a child's hymn over sublow) needs. Weirdness stays near the middle so the structure stays plain. Source: https://blakecrosley.com/blog/suno-style-field-style-influence
- **Voice in both places.** Guides disagree on whether vocal identity belongs in Style or in lyric tags, so the broad identity (eight-year-old London schoolboy, bored, off-key, dry) goes in Style and the delivery goes in the verse tag. Vocal gender is left null: "male" risks an adult man, so "schoolboy" carries the gender. "Male child voice" and "boy soprano" are the documented keywords; boy soprano was avoided because it pulls towards a choirboy, the opposite of bored. Sources: https://jackrighteous.com/en-gb/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/where-to-put-your-suno-prompt-guide , https://sunohk.pages.dev/blog/suno-vocal-prompt-palette-guide/
- **Exclude is a boundary, not a guarantee.** It does not contradict Style: there is no piano, choir or pad anywhere in the positive prompt. Source: https://jackrighteous.com/en-gb/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/negative-prompting-suno-v5-guide
- **Risk.** No source covers a reliable child voice; one guide says child voices are hard to get. If takes come back as an adult or a choirboy, try "small boy, school assembly, unenthusiastic" in the verse tag before changing the music.
