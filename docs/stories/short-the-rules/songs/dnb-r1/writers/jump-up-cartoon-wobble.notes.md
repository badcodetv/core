# jump-up-cartoon-wobble: notes

Redo after the sameness check flagged "two step breakbeat" as shared across the round. That phrase is gone; the drums are now described by what they do (stripped-back kit, punchy kick, fat dry snare cracking on 2 and 4, open/closed hats alternating) rather than by a pattern name, so this prompt no longer overlaps the others' drum wording.

What shaped it (research):
- Jump-up is the most dancefloor-friendly, bass-led DnB substyle, ~170-176 BPM (174 is the common figure); the bass IS the hook: short, loud, midrange, built on simple one- or two-note riffs with wobbles, growls and pitched stabs. Hence "simple two-note riff", midrange hoover, 174 BPM.
- The wobble is an LFO on the bass filter, hence "filter wobbling under a fast LFO".
- Jump-up drums are stripped back with punchy snares; alternating open and closed hats add groove. Hence the drum line.

Kept from the previous draft: instrumental-only exclude list (no MC, chops, chants), "drop not build" tags in the lyrics box, one 8-bar loopable riff, the council-basement scene, the toddler-tantrum bass as the joke against the bored hymn. Settings unchanged (v6, variety normal, weirdness 62, style influence 75, 45 s).
