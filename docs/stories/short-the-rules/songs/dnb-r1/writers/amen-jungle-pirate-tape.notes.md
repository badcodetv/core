# amen-jungle-pirate-tape: notes

Redo: the earlier lyric direction ("from the first beat") matched wording other writers used. The [Break] cue now reads "Amen roll already running, S950 crunch", and the style says "drums already rolling at bar one". Nothing else changed.

What shaped it:
- The Amen break (The Winstons) is the backbone of jungle, chopped and micro-edited hit by hit on the sampler: https://reverb.com/news/the-samplers-behind-90s-jungle-and-drum-and-bass
- The Akai S950's time-stretch gave stretched breaks their odd metallic grain, and it was standard kit for 1993-94 tracks (Renegade "Terrorist", Leviticus "Burial"): https://djmag.com/longreads/how-renegades-terrorist-created-blueprint-jungle and https://djmag.com/features/how-leviticus-burial-epitomised-jungles-melting-pot-of-influences
- Jungle lived on pirate radio and white labels until 1994, which is why the mix is "taped off a pirate broadcast" (hiss, saturation): https://musicradar.com/news/everything-you-need-to-know-about-jungle
- No MC and no ragga samples, per the brief, so ragga jungle's vocal habits are excluded outright.
