# live-band-rock-dnb — notes (redo)

Redo: the previous draft shared "two step breakbeat" with most of the round. That phrase is gone; the beat is now described as what a drummer's hands do ("double-time kick-snare shuffle", "snare like a gunshot") rather than by DnB sub-genre name, which also keeps it away from the machine-made experiments.

What shaped it:
- Real live-DnB acts play the genre on acoustic kits with dry, cracking snares to imitate breaks (2KINETIK, TOWNS' punk-meets-jungle drumming, Rudimental/Sigma/High Contrast live drummers): https://undergroundsound.eu/industry/elements-of-a-killer-live-dnb-performance/ , https://dreamcymbals.com/pages/dan-steinert , https://musicradar.com/news/drums/paul-jones-on-the-drum-gear-that-powers-drum-n-bass-maestros-sigma-638987
- Suno community advice: name each player by role, keep the style box short (~100 words), use recording-character words ("live room", "small-room", "raw" as performance, not poor clarity): https://medium.com/@aitooldiscovery/suno-reddit-tips-and-prompts-what-r-sunoai-users-actually-do-65b59d51bcad , https://jackrighteous.com/pages/top-music-genres-rock-suno-ai
- Instrumental reliability: "instrumental" in the style box plus an [Instrumental] tag, and vocals in the exclude box, since the switch alone still lets vocals leak: https://howtopromptsuno.com/style-lyrics/how-do-i-make-instrumental
- Cut to a three-piece (drums, fuzz bass, noise guitar) to match the brief exactly; "no overdubs", "bleed", "clipping" push the physical, door-kicked feel. Excluded synth/drum machine so it stays human. Settings kept: v6-wild, weirdness 45, styleInfluence 75, 60 s for loop room, maxMode on.
