# ragga-soundsystem-dub — notes (redo)

Rewritten to clear the sameness gate: dropped "the lead is a…" phrasing; the bass is now described as "carries the tune".

What shaped it:
- Ragga jungle peaked 1994–95 (Shy FX "Original Nuttah", Remarc "Sound Murderer", Conquering Lion "Code Red"); Reynolds' "postmodern dub music on steroids"; sound-system roots. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jungle_music
- Jungle basslines sit at half the drum tempo (~80–88 BPM, reggae territory) under Amen pitched to 160–170, so the bass is phrased like a reggae riff at half time: "half the drum tempo". https://orphiq.com/resources/what-is-jungle-music
- Dub prompting is about subtraction and return (what drops out, what stays, when it comes back), plus tape echo / delay tags — so the desk moves are spelled out as dropouts and the break slamming back. https://jackrighteous.com/en-us/pages/top-music-genres-2025-reggae-suno-guide , https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/what-is-drum-and-bass-music-suno-ai-guide
- Put the key descriptors at the start and end of the style box (middle gets lost): genre/tempo/bass first, "Drums, bass and echo only" last. https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/instrumentation-and-arrangement-in-suno-v5
- Banned words (vocals, MC, etc.) kept out of the style box and put in exclude, with synths/keys/siren/airhorn to kill the high-pitched layer Kai disliked.

Settings: v6, variety normal, weirdness 40, style influence 80 (stick to the box), 60 s, instrumental.
