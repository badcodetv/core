# reese-hardstep-terrorist: notes

**Aim:** answer Kai's "too many high-pitched synthesizers" by putting the weight in the low mids. The Reese leads, the Amen sits under it, and nothing goes above the cymbals.

**What shaped it**
- Renegade's "Terrorist" (Ray Keith, Moving Shadow, 1994) is the hardstep anthem. It paired the Reese with an Amen left clean, "probably the biggest amen track of them all". Hence "played almost straight for the first bars, then edited and rolling". https://drumtrip.co.uk/?p=796 · https://djmag.com/longreads/how-renegades-terrorist-created-blueprint-jungle (403 to the fetcher; title and search summary only)
- The Reese is two slightly detuned saws, and the techstep crews ran it through overdrive to push the mid-range. Hence "two detuned saw basses phasing ... overdriven". https://mixmag.net/feature/kevin-saunderson-reese-bassline-transformed-uk-dance-music-jungle-speed-garage-drum-n-bass · https://www.musicradar.com/how-to/classic-reese-bass-renegade-terrorist
- Hardstep put a kick under a clean, dense Amen, which gives it extra weight. Hence "a hard kick doubled beneath". http://12edit.com/hardstep/
- Suno prompting advice: name the substyle, break and bass type; put the genre first; give each phrase a musical job and don't stack adjectives; write "instrumental". https://suno.com/music-library/genres/drum-and-bass · https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/what-is-drum-and-bass-music-suno-ai-guide · https://hookgenius.app/learn/suno-dnb-prompts/
