# two-instruments-only (redo) — what shaped it

Redo after the sameness gate flagged "the very first" / "very first downbeat"; both are gone ("from bar one"). The rest of the prompt was reworded too.

- Suno tends to fill empty space and slip in an intro or a breakdown, so the prompt fences it in twice: a space rule in the style box (nothing above the hi-hats) and an exclude list of synth, pad, stab, hoover, riser, intro and breakdown. Users report that Suno "will cut off the beat… or add an ambient break", and that an element you don't name you won't get. https://usesuno.pages.dev/guide/genre/electronic-edm/drum-and-bass · https://medium.com/@aitooldiscovery/suno-reddit-tips-and-prompts-what-r-sunoai-users-actually-do-65b59d51bcad
- "Instrumental" goes in the style box and [Instrumental] in the lyrics box. https://howtopromptsuno.com/style-lyrics/how-do-i-make-instrumental
- The sub only answers the kick. That idea comes from jungle production, where producers duck the sub on the kick and place the bass notes so "a kick or a snare hit on most of the bass notes". https://www.edmprod.com/how-to-make-jungle-music/ · https://mixedinkey.com/captain-plugins/wiki/how-to-make-drum-and-bass-the-complete-guide/
- The "every fourth bar" re-sequenced fill copies the jungle turnaround: chop up the loop and change it in the last bar of a phrase. https://www.edmprod.com/how-to-make-jungle-music/
- The Amen was sped up to 160–170 BPM in UK jungle, so 167 BPM is used here. Dubplate and sound-system framing: https://loopmasters.com/genres/112-Jungle/products/15702-Chopstick-Dubplate-Jungle-Soundclash-Vol-1
- Weirdness is 35 and style influence 85: the low weirdness discourages added extras, and the high style influence holds the subtraction rule.
