# tower-block-sub-boom: notes

Experiment: Kai's dnb-r2 hardstep pick with the Reese torn out and nothing growling in its place. The only bass is a clean pitched-down 808-style boom that drops and slides each bar; the Amen stays raw with a doubled kick.

What shaped it:
- Hardstep is named for bringing the kick drum back under the break, and for using the clean, barely processed Amen (Renegade's "Terrorist", 1994; DJ SS "Black", 1995 is called the clearest example: repeated drums, heavy kick). That is why the style says "barely chopped Amen" and "hard kick doubled". The same page credits Ray Keith with bringing the Reese growl in, replacing a shorter dub-style bass, so that growl is what this take removes (exclude: reese, growl bass, detuned saw, wobble). http://12edit.com/hardstep/
- Jungle low end is commonly sine-wave sub or 808-style bass, and the TR-808 kick is a down-pitched sound. That is why I asked for an "808 kick hum" that drops and slides. https://splice.com/blog/?p=38712 , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drum_and_bass
- Jungle bass was rooted in reggae and dancehall (Wikipedia). Kai dropped that influence, so ragga, reggae and dancehall go in the exclude box, and the style avoids anything dubby. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jungle_music
- Third-party Suno guides say tags near the front carry the most weight. So the style opens with a short tag run (genre, Amen, sub boom, BPM, instrumental, no intro) and the prose follows. https://roo.beehiiv.com/p/complete-suno-ai-prompt-guide-2026-301-styles-the-exact-formula-and-why-your-outputs-sound-random , https://jackrighteous.com/en-gb/blogs/music-creation-process-guide/style-driven-suno-prompts-brand-identity
- Settings: v6, weirdness 55 (up from 35–40, as asked), style influence 80 to keep it in the hardstep lane, variety normal. "Odd lopsided swing" gives the extra weirdness something to bend. Duration is 45 s, leaving room to cut an 11 s drop that loops cleanly.
- "Council estate" is carried by the words "bare concrete tower-block weight, cold, stark" and an empty top end, not by any sample request.
