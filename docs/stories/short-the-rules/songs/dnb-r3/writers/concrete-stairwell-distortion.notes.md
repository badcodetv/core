# concrete-stairwell-distortion: what shaped it

- **Distorted sub, not a Reese.** Mid-90s accounts put the heavy, darker sub (Back 2 Basics' "Dread Bass") and the 1995 race to ever more distorted, "twisted" bass in hardstep/early techstep. The Reese came after techstep, so I asked for one clipped, fuzzy sub and excluded the detuned-saw sound by name. Sources: https://www.timeout.com/london/music/the-20-best-jungle-tracks-ever-made , https://EN.wikipedia.com/wiki/Techstep , https://wikipedia.com/wiki/DnB
- **Hardstep Amen.** Hardstep came from the Amen's heavy kick, so I doubled a kick under it and asked for stuttering rolls (Remarc-style frantic chops). Sources: https://ukf.com/read/20-essential-jungle-tunes-everyone-should-know-about/ , https://reverb.com/news/the-samplers-behind-90s-jungle-and-drum-and-bass
- **Concrete slapback.** Producer guides put the break in a small, tight room, band-limited and run in parallel, so it doesn't sound dry. I turned that into "snare cracking off raw concrete with a short tight slapback". Sources: https://www.edmprod.com/how-to-make-jungle-music/ , https://www.musicradar.com/news/try-these-12-banging-drum-processing-tips
- **Materials after the genre words.** Stairwell, steel shutters, lift shaft and strip light describe the place in sentences, not as a tag list. That follows the brief.
- **Sliders.** In v6, Variety rewrites the style prompt, so I set it to off to keep these words intact. Weirdness runs from Safe to Chaos, with 50 as the normal result, so 75 is a real push. Style influence is at 70, so the higher weirdness has room to wander. Model is plain v6, not Wild, so that weirdness is the only push toward the strange. Sources: https://sunowatermark.com/blog/suno-v6-variety-style-influence-weirdness/ , https://jackrighteous.com/en-gb/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/how-to-use-suno-s-advanced-sliders-weirdness-style-audio-influence
- **Exclude.** It drops the reggae and ragga markers, the dub siren and skank guitar, the Reese and detuned saw, rave stabs, and anything that builds up or fades in. 166 BPM sits in the mid-90s range, a little slower than the modern 174.
