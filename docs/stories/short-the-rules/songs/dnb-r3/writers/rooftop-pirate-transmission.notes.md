# rooftop-pirate-transmission: notes (redo)

Redo after the sameness gate flagged "165 BPM instrumental", which most of the round's style boxes shared. The tempo now reads "Tempo sits near 166" in the middle of the scene. The instrumental instruction is carried by the `[Instrumental]` lyrics and the exclude box.

What shaped it:
- **Broadcast framing.** Jungle pirates sent their weekend shows from makeshift studios in flats, through aerials on tower-block roofs. Kool FM ran about 70 hours a week from the Nightingale Estate towers in Hackney, and its Sundays followed the party nights. Rinse FM started in 1994 with an aerial taped to a broom handle. Sources: https://eurozine.com/mutualism-massive-and-the-city-to-come , https://vice.com/en/article/kool-fm-jungle-pirate-london-945 , https://wikipedia.com/wiki/Kool_FM
- **The reversed sub.** Drumtrip's jungle history names Dead Dred's "Dred Bass" (Moving Shadow, 1994) as "commonly referred to as the first reversed sub bass line in Jungle", so the prompt names the Dred Bass trick. My earlier draft credited the trick to Renegade's "Terrorist", but that track's bass is a sample of the Reese bass, which Kai ruled out, so I took the name out. Sources: https://drumtrip.co.uk/?p=796 , https://djmag.com/longreads/how-renegades-terrorist-created-blueprint-jungle , https://www.reasonstudios.com/news/post/jungle-101-lets-talk-bass
- **No midrange.** Kai asked for "without the Reese", so "detuned saw bass" is also in the exclude list. The only bass is the backwards sub, and the drums carry the lead.
- **Settings.** Weirdness 65 and style influence 65, as the brief asked. v6, Variety off, 60 s, so an 11 s loop can be cut from the middle.
