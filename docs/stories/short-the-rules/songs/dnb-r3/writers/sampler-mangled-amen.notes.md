# sampler-mangled-amen: notes (redo)

Redo after the sameness gate flagged "165 BPM, instrumental" as shared with the round. Tempo is now "tempo 165"
and the style box no longer says "instrumental" (the [Instrumental] lyric tag carries it; "drums and sub only" says the rest).

What shaped it:
- The Akai S950's time-stretch let producers radically change a break's tempo and pitch, and it was used on breaks in 1994 jungle (Dred Bass):
  https://reverb.com/news/the-samplers-behind-90s-jungle-and-drum-and-bass
- 12-bit sampling (S950, SP-1200) is where the grit comes from, because 16-bit converters were costly:
  https://loopcloud.com/cloud/blog/5185-Unlocking-Vintage-Vibes-Mastering-Classic-Sampler-Techniques-in-Modern-Music-Production
- Mid-90s jungle used a heavily fragmented, chopped Amen as the lead rhythmic voice:
  https://www.musicradar.com/news/the-history-of-breaks
- Asked as gear and technique (sampler model, bit depth, sample rate, retrigger rate, repitch intervals) rather than mood, as the brief says.
  The wildest prompt in the round: weirdness 80, style influence 60, model v6, variety high.
