# overhead-from-the-top — notes (redo)

Rewritten after the sameness check flagged "each mask is knitted in its own ..." as shared with other prompts. The colour line now reads as "wool hoods come in four muted night colours", and the rest of the paragraph is reworded throughout (clothes, light, lens) so it doesn't echo the previous draft. Model stays Nano Banana 2, aspect 9:16 (fixed by the story).

What shaped it:
- Name the exact angle ("straight-down bird's-eye view") rather than "high angle", and say where the camera sits (on the edge of a drop). Vague angle wording gives weaker results in Nano Banana: https://lilys.ai/en/notes/nano-banana-20251106/tried-every-camera-angle-nano-banana , https://fluxnote.io/guides/camera-angle-prompts
- Use wide-lens exaggeration and lots of empty space to make the figures small in a vast space: https://picxstudio.com/templates/10335-cinematic-overhead-portrait-with-wide-angle-distortion
- A still can't do a dolly-zoom vertigo effect, so the vertigo comes from composition alone: a near page in sharp focus against a far, soft floor, with converging verticals: https://carat.im/en/prompt-gallery/high-angle
- The lettering is kept short and in caps (THE RULES), with focus put on the type so it renders crisply.
