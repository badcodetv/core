What shaped it:
- Google's own Nano Banana Pro tips: prompt like a cinematographer (framing, f-stop for shallow focus,
  named light such as golden-hour, colour grading, aspect ratio), and state the exact words you want
  rendered with their look (here: heavy black print, THE RULES, on one sheet near the top).
  https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/gemini/prompting-tips-nano-banana-pro/
- Community guides repeat the photoreal template (shot type + subject + action + setting, then lens and
  key textures) and the 85mm f/1.8 shallow-focus portrait recipe; texture is named concretely
  (rib knit, raised cord ring, raking light making each rib cast a shadow) because the brief says the
  picture is about the mask's texture.
  https://www.leonardo.ai/news/nano-banana-prompt-guide
  https://www.veed.io/learn/nano-banana-pro-prompts
- Triangle placement restated as "high and off to one side, not centred" because earlier Bank Robbery
  masks drifted to a centred triangle; the left-shoulder camera puts it on the near cheek.

  one line and soft-focus, which NB2 handles. Aspect 9:16 as the story fixes.
