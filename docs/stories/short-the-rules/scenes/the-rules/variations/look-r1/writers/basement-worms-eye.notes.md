# basement-worms-eye — notes

- **Angle and lens.** Community Nano Banana prompts for a worm's-eye view put the camera on the floor and name the lens: 14–18mm ultra-wide gives the towering foreshortening the experiment wants (8–12mm tips into fisheye, which would bend the shelving too far). Also named "almost flat on the floor" plus "tilted steeply upward" rather than just "low angle". Sources: https://youmind.com/prompts/cinematic-worm-eye-view-portrait-24720 , https://sider.ai/en/blog/ai-image/advanced-camera-angle-prompts-for-nano-banana-pro , https://lilting.ch/en/articles/ai-illustration-camera-angles
- **The light.** Fluorescent tubes read green because of mercury emission spikes and weak red; colours go murky and dull under them. Used that on purpose: the clothes go dull, so only the white rings and red triangles carry, as the brief asks. Sources: https://www.redsharknews.com/production/item/2209-practical-lighting-colorimetry-or-can-i-light-a-scene-with-this-lamp , https://community.usa.canon.com/t5/EOS-DSLR-Mirrorless-Cameras/M50-MKII-Color-shift-in-pictures/m-p/480008
- **The words.** Nano Banana Pro is the model for legible in-image text; Google's guidance is to put the exact words in quotes and name the typeface style. Sources: https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/gemini/prompting-tips-nano-banana-pro/ , https://deepmind.google/models/gemini-image/prompt-guide/
- **One departure from the experiment:** a top sheet seen edge-on can't be read, so "THE RULES" is printed on a paper band wrapped round the stack at head height, where both the gang and the camera can see it.
- **Mask:** spelled out "left eye, toward two o'clock" and "small" to push the triangle off-centre (Bank Robbery masks drifted to the centre), plus gloves with cuffs pulled over the wrists, after the bare-hand round.
- **Settings:** Nano Banana Pro, 9:16 (aspect fixed by the story).
