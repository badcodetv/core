# Look r1 — research notes (ideas writer)

- Nano Banana responds to concrete photographic language: name a lens and aperture (e.g. 85mm f/1.8), name the light and its direction, and state the canvas ("a 9:16 vertical ..."); vague "good lighting" does little. 9:16 is a supported ratio. https://deepmind.google/models/gemini-image/prompt-guide/ · https://blog.google/products/gemini/prompting-tips-nano-banana-pro/ · https://www.veed.io/learn/nano-banana-prompting-guide
- Prompt order that works: subject, technical parameters, style, composition, lighting, then exclusions. https://www.veed.io/learn/nano-banana-prompting-guide
- Pussy Riot's balaclavas: bright, featureless, multicoloured knitted masks (lineage: Guerrilla Girls, Malevich's faceless figures), read as playful anonymity against the black masks of riot police. Supports "different colour per member" and a modern, non-gangster register. https://www.theartstory.org/artist/pussy-riot/artworks/ · https://hasta-standrews.com/scotland/2021/5/10/pussy-riot-protest-art-and-dissensual-aesthetics
- Paper stacks as bureaucracy art: Yumi Janairo Roth's *Effigy 1462* (1,462 identical prints = days in a term, paired with a shredder), Maya Zack's *Paper Work* (paperwork bursting off a shelf into a landscape), Taryn Simon's *Paperwork and the Will of Capital* (stacked pages as a monolith). The stack can be a sculpture, a landscape or a monolith. https://lenscratch.com/2026/01/yumi-janairo-roth-effigy-1462/ · https://maya-zack.squarespace.com/paper-work-2009 · https://www.brooklynmuseum.org/objects/224301
- Jan Banning's *Bureaucratics*: frontal, deadpan portraits of real government offices. A model for a plain British council-office setting. https://www.trendhunter.com/trends/jan-banning
- Martin Parr: daylight flash with a wide lens gives saturated, "garish, cheap" colour; it is the visual language of ordinary modern Britain. https://www.canon.co.uk/pro/stories/martin-parr-style-vision/ · https://petapixel.com/martin-parr/
- Gregory Crewdson: staged tableaux at twilight, every practical light in the frame lit on purpose; ordinary streets turn uncanny and suspended. https://petapixel.com/2016/05/18/interview-gregory-crewdson/ · https://proedu.com/blogs/photographer-spotlight/gregory-crewdson-cinematic-staged-photography-and-surrealism-capturing-suburban-dreamscapes
