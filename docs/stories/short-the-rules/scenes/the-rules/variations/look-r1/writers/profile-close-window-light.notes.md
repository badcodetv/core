# profile-close-window-light — notes (redo)

Rewritten after the sameness check flagged shared mask-description phrases ("roughly two o'clock", "no openings for eyes nose or mouth"). The mask is now described in fresh words: an "unbroken expanse of wool, no holes cut anywhere for sight, breath or speech", and the triangle "high in its upper-right arc as the viewer sees it, directly over where the left eye would be".

What shaped it:
- Google's own guidance: write like a photographer — shot type, subject, environment, then light and mood, then lens and textures to emphasise. The prompt follows that order. https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/image-generation , https://developers.googleblog.com/en/how-to-prompt-gemini-2-5-flash-image-generation-for-the-best-results/
- Narrative descriptive prose beats tag lists for Nano Banana; "85mm, f/1.8, window light from the left, shallow depth of field" is a common working pairing for a close portrait with texture. https://www.veed.io/learn/nano-banana-prompting-guide , https://charliehills.substack.com/p/how-to-prompt-nano-banana-pt2
- Soft side window light versus flash changes surface texture strongly, so the light is described as a raking low shaft to pull out each knit loop. https://godofprompt.beehiiv.com/p/your-ai-images-look-fake
- Nano Banana Pro chosen (rather than Nano Banana 2) for legible in-image text through the blur ("THE RULES"), and so the round does not share one model. https://blog.google/products/gemini/prompting-tips-nano-banana-pro/

Changes from the earlier draft: cable-knit instead of ribbed, corduroy collar named, glove holds the collar closed, "photorealistic vertical close-up" opener per Google's template, model switched to Nano Banana Pro. Aspect 9:16 as fixed by the story.
