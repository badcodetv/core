# The Rules: brief

> Stage 1 (`scene-beats`), 2026-11-10, Kai and Claude. A BadCode Short, its own project (`short-the-rules`).
> Rulings and Kai's words: `picks.md`. Lyrics: `../../songs/lyrics.md`. Sound: `../../sound.md`. Look: `../../look.md`.

**Format:** portrait 9:16, **20 s maximum.**

## The sentence

If the rules worked, the street would show it. It doesn't, so you have every right to rewrite them.
**Who gains from the rules as they are:** the people who wrote them, and the companies that book their profits
on islands that fly our flag and pay little or no tax there. **The target is deference:** working-class people
handing their say on the rules to credentialed establishment figures.
