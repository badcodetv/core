# Pub Quiz: Democracy

A BadCode short, the second Pub Quiz (Jack, 2026-11-21: "Pub Quiz: Democracy let's do this but let's try and make it
in the quickest way possible").

**The idea (Jack, 2026-11-21):** "you can't pick and choose": you wait five years and pick a party instead of voting on
each topic or policy as it happens. Show what picking and choosing looks like, the way the first Pub Quiz got the
facts out through the table arguing, not by listing them.

**The point:** given the choice one question at a time, people don't vote the party line, and in some countries they
already choose that way. The facts and sources: [`../../ideas/you-can-pick-and-choose.md`](../../ideas/you-can-pick-and-choose.md).
