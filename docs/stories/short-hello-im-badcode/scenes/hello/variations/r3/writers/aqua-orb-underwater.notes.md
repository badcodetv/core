What shaped it (web research):
- Frutiger Aero guides list glossy orbs, translucent bubbles, tropical fish, water and sky gradients as
  the core motifs, 2004-2013 "web gloss": https://budgetpixel.com/blog/how-to-design-frutiger-aero-aesthetics-glossy-orbs-tropical-fish-and-sky-gradients
- The "Aqua water" preset of a Frutiger Aero background generator = blue-to-cyan gradient with rays,
  bubbles, orbs and fish layers (the god-rays + rising bubbles + orb build): https://madegooddesigns.com/?p=139812
- Rendering cue: bright rim and a highlight on the top edge of the orb, lit from above (the crescent on
  the sphere's top rim): same budgetpixel guide.
- Palette: icy blues, deep aqua, silver/white with warm orange fish as the accent:
  https://www.designpieces.com/2026/08/frutiger-aero-design-resources/
- Background on the aesthetic's revival as a nostalgia meme: https://www.dazeddigital.com/life-culture/article/58103/1/what-is-frutiger-aero-aesthetic-tikt

No figure (by design), no text, no brands; model and aspect fixed by the story (Nano Banana 2, 9:16).
