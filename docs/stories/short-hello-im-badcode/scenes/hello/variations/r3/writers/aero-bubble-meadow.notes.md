# aero-bubble-meadow — notes

What shaped it (web research, 2026-11-12):

- Frutiger Aero core motifs: clear skies, lush green fields, water droplets, bubbles; glossy textures, bokeh photography, lens flares; tech set against idealised nature — https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Aero , https://wiki.riteme.site/wiki/Frutiger_Aero
- Palette: saturated royal-blue-to-cyan sky gradient, lime sunlit tops with deep emerald shadows, clean whites — https://budgetpixel.com/blog/how-to-design-frutiger-aero-aesthetics-glossy-orbs-tropical-fish-and-sky-gradients , https://www.eggradients.com/palette/frutiger-aero-colors
- Orb/bubble rendering: bright crescent highlight on the top rim, softer secondary on the bottom rim, reflections inside; multi-ringed white/cyan flare near a top corner; soft translucent bokeh circles — budgetpixel guide above.
- The "desktop wallpaper / stock photo of a bright future" framing comes from the style's origin in mid-2000s OS wallpapers and corporate stock imagery — https://trends.daisyui.com/trend/frutiger-aero/

Choices: worm's-eye macro so grass becomes glossy architecture; one pin-sharp mirror bubble as the focal point, the rest as bokeh; "slightly too perfect" so it can turn eerie in the edit. Banned words avoided (no OS or brand names); no text, since meme writing is added in the edit. Settings fixed by the story: Nano Banana 2, 9:16. Ends with "Thanks." per house convention.
