# chill-guy-flat-backdrop — writer notes (redo)

What shaped it:
- The original "I'm just a chill guy" image is a cartoon dog by Phillip Banks in jeans, a sweatshirt and sneakers, standing nonchalant; it circulates as a background-removed PNG pasted over any backdrop, so the meme's grammar is "relaxed figure, cut out, on a plain field, caption on top". The artist has moved to copyright the character, so only the attitude and the template layout are borrowed: our masked figure, our own clothes (oatmeal cardigan, cargos, off-white trainers rather than the dog's sweatshirt, jeans and red sneakers).
  - https://knowyourmeme.com/editorials/whats-the-im-just-a-chill-guy-meme-the-dog-who-was-my-new-character-on-tiktok-explained
  - https://www.kapwing.com/explore/im-just-a-chill-guy-meme-template-background-removed
  - https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/my-new-character-his-whole-deal-is-hes-a-chill-guy-that-lowkey-doesnt-give-a-fuck
- Flat peach paper sweep + soft frontal light = the clean cut-out-on-a-colour look of a meme template; the figure sits low and small so the edit has room for the colourful writing.
