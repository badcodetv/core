# millennial-optimism-loft — writer notes (r3 redo)

Shaped by the "millennial optimism era" trend (roughly 2010-2014, romanticised on TikTok by Gen Z): moustache art, Polaroids, beanies, fairy lights in mason jars, Edison bulbs and string lights in rustic spaces, mason-jar everything, hipster urban flats — and the cost-of-living reading of why people now miss it (cheap rent, everything going to be fine).
