# aero-closing-horizon — redo notes

What shaped it (web research):
- Frutiger Aero palette and motifs — sky blues, grassy greens, glossy glass surfaces, water/bubbles, bokeh, lens flares and auroras, era ~2004-2013: https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Aero , https://budgetpixel.com/blog/how-to-design-frutiger-aero-aesthetics-glossy-orbs-tropical-fish-and-sky-gradients , https://www.dazeddigital.com/life-culture/article/58103/1/what-is-frutiger-aero-aesthetic-tikt
- The "rolling green hill under a perfect sky" default-wallpaper image is the archetype the aesthetic is remembered by; described here as a polished lime mound with a lacquered gradient sky (no product named, per the never-write list).
- Aurora shimmer kept low on the horizon (sources list auroras as an Aero light effect) to give the dusk grade a hint of eeriness without breaking the calm.

Composition: bottom fifth hill, top two thirds clean sky for the logo, figure tiny and centred, telephoto from below so the figure stays small but readable. Model/aspect fixed by the story: Nano Banana 2, 9:16.
