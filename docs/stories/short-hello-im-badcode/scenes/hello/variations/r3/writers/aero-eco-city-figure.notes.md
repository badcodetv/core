What shaped it:
- Frutiger Eco is the environmental branch of Frutiger Aero: renewable energy, sustainability, futuristic architecture, water and nature, a utopian "advanced green future" that also carries anxiety about that future. Hence turbines, greenhouse domes, terraced gardens, a lagoon, and the creeping grey haze as the turn.
  - https://holythrift.com/blogs/news/frutiger-eco-the-glamorization-of-climate-change-nbsp
  - https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Aero
  - https://www.dazeddigital.com/life-culture/article/58103/1/what-is-frutiger-aero-aesthetic-tikt
- Real eco-tower visions (wind turbines built into towers, greenery-covered skyscrapers) informed the petal-stacked white towers.
  - https://burnhamplan100.lib.uchicago.edu/big_bold_visionary/towers/eco_scrapers
  - https://hyperallergic.com/envisioning-eco-friendly-architecture-in-paris.md
