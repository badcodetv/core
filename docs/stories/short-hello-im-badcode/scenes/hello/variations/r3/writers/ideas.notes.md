# r3 research notes: happy nostalgic meme edits (not childhood)

Web research, 2026-11-12. Community wikis and lifestyle blogs are informal sources; dates vary.

- **Frutiger Aero (c. 2004-2013)**: glossy "future we were promised" stock-image look. Macro grass blades with dew, soap bubbles, glass orbs lit from above, bright cyan sky with wispy clouds, lime-green hills, tropical fish (goldfish, clown loach), aqua water, bokeh, lens flare, auroras, bloom and sheen. By 2012 it was called cluttered and cheesy, which is now part of the charm.
  https://trends.daisyui.com/trend/frutiger-aero/ · https://budgetpixel.com/blog/how-to-design-frutiger-aero-aesthetics-glossy-orbs-tropical-fish-and-sky-gradients · https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Aero · https://frutigeraeroarchive.org/history.html
- **Its family**: Frutiger Eco (solar panels, wind turbines, white curved eco-architecture, earth and water, a sustainability-utopia future); Frutiger Aqua (sea, beach, underwater light); Frutiger Metro (flatter, brighter, bold colour tiles, peak to c. 2012). Vaporwave is called "Frutiger Aero's gloomier cousin": same nostalgia, sadder tone. Y2K futurism (chrome, blobs) came just before.
  https://gurlworld.com/frutiger-aero-the-iconic-2000s-tech-and-corporation-aesthetic-and-its-sub-genres/ · https://skeuoss.net/blogs/aesthetics/frutiger-metro · https://medium.com/elevenpm/frutiger-aero-the-trend-of-2024-1be502c0b367
- **"2026 is the new 2016"**: the start-of-2026 trend (10th anniversary), photo dumps of 2016: oversaturated filter presets, outfit laid out on the bed, acai bowls, crop tops, flannel tied round the waist, chokers, aviators, festival boho and flower crowns, matte lip and winged liner, messy unpolished camera-roll dumps, grainy Tumblr-style moody landscapes and indie music. Read as longing for "the last human internet". Peak Dec 2025-Jan 2026, fading since.
  https://www.forbes.com.au/life/lifestyle/why-is-the-internet-obsessed-with-2016/ · https://theweek.com/culture-life/nostalgia-2016-social-media-trend · https://www.trillmag.com/culture/why-2026-is-the-new-2016-took-over-social-media/
- **"I'm just a chill guy"**: Phillip Banks' character (posted Oct 2023, viral Nov 2024): "his whole deal is he's a chill guy that lowkey doesn't give a ...". The attitude is hands in pockets, smirk, unbothered. The artist owns the character and has objected to unlicensed use, so we only borrow the attitude.
  https://earlygame.com/news/entertainment/chill-guy-meme-origin · https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/chill-guy-vietnamese-magazine-cover-plagiarism-hoax
- **"Millennial optimism era"** (InsideHook, "Younger Generations Are Romanticizing the Millennial Optimism Era"): late-2000s/early-2010s montages: exposed-brick apartments, string lights, moustache-print mugs, ukuleles, kickball, cheap rent, indie-folk soundtrack (stomp-clap, "Blood" by The Middle East).
- **Hopecore** (late 2023 on): montages of kind, joyful moments (sport, animals, the elderly) under soulful oldies, captioned "life is beautiful"; its satirical twin is "Hopeless Core".
  https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/hopecore · https://mediacat.uk/corecore-and-hopecore-expressing-the-pessimism-and-optimism-of-the-age/amp/
- **"We used to be a country. A proper country."** (Dec 2021): a mock-patriotic caption over photos of lost everyday things (a 1973 corner shop, 2000s malls, cheap prices, 24-hour supermarkets), turned into a lament for the 90s/00s.
  https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/we-used-to-be-a-country-a-proper-country · https://www.bustle.com/life/we-used-to-be-a-country-meme-origins
- **Global Village Coffeehouse** (late 80s to early 00s): warm earth tones, suns, moons, globes, hand-drawn café décor; a utopian "global village" internet. Revived on nostalgia.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Village_Coffeehouse · https://cari.institute/aesthetics/global-village-coffeehouse

Not found: a single named "colourful text over music nostalgia edit" format; it seems to be the generic edit style across these trends (text is added in the edit anyway).
