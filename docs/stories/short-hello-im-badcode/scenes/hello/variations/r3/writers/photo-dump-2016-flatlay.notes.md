# photo-dump-2016-flatlay — writer notes (r3)

What shaped it:
- The 2016 trend's own vocabulary: flat-lay outfit photos, acai bowl photos, grainy phone photos, chokers, skinny jeans, flower crowns, oversaturated warm edits with grain (True Future Media explainer: https://www.truefuturemedia.com/articles/what-is-the-2016-trend-2026-is-the-new-2016-instagram).
- Chokers as black velvet bands, skinny jeans (https://www.azafashions.com/blog/why-2016-fashion-is-back/?noamp=mobile).
- Trend origin and look (grainy phone shots, saturated filters, flower crowns; some posts ironic): https://www.nbcnews.com/pop-culture/pop-culture-news/2016-nostalgia-internet-instagram-celebrities-influencers-rcna254493 and https://www.thestatesman.com/entertainment/2016-trend-on-instagram-detailed-look-at-why-2016s-pop-culture-memes-music-are-going-viral-again-in-2026-1503542523.html
- Sources disagree on the grade (faded/muted vs loud saturation); the brief asked for both lifted blacks and boosted saturation, which matches the warm Valencia/Clarendon-era presets most explainers name, so the prompt does both (https://www.perfectcorp.com/consumer/blog/photo-editing/2016-photo-trend, https://amateurphotographer.com/latest/opinion/2016-trend-please-bring-back-authenticity-but-leave-the-horrendous-filters-behind/).

Choices: no filter or app names in the prompt (brand ban), the grade described in plain terms; the flower crown is a physical silk crown (the trend's version was a selfie filter); "no hands" added so the engine doesn't put a phone-holder in frame; the off-level tilt and arm's-length phone framing sell the camera-roll dump. Settings fixed by the story: Nano Banana 2, 9:16.
