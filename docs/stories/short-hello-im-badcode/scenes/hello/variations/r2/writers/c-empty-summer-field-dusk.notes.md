# c-empty-summer-field-dusk (redo) — notes

Redo after the sameness gate flagged "anywhere in the", "date stamp", "no text no", "on a cheap". All four are gone; the closing exclusion is now one positive-worded line ("clean of any writing, numbers, overlays or graphics"), and the camera is a "family handycam propped on a garden wall" rather than a cheap camera on a fence post.

What shaped it:
- Camcorder nostalgia look = slightly muted colour, low-res softness, fuzz/grain, first-person home framing (Pulp, "A 60x zoom into camcorder aesthetics": https://www.pulp-usu.com/all-articles/2023/04/17/a-60x-zoom-into-camcorder-aesthetics). Kept the camera locked off instead of handheld because Beat C needs calm and room for the logo.
- Tape-specific artefacts real viewers recognise: chroma speckle/smear and head-switching wobble at the bottom edge of analogue tape (VideoHelp https://forum.videohelp.com/showthread.php?p=2801398 ; DigitalFAQ https://www.digitalfaq.com/forum/video-restore/10952-hi8-video8-issues-print.html). Used lightly: "fizz of chroma speckle", "faint horizontal wobble near the bottom edge".
- CCD/MiniDV-era home-movie look as the core of the 2000s aesthetic (https://dutchthrift.com/blogs/blogs/top-pre-owned-camcorders-for-that-2000s-home-movie-look).
- Why an empty childhood place reads as nostalgic: a transitional space emptied of the people who gave it meaning, recognised as a type rather than a specific memory (https://karenakorli.substack.com/p/the-liminal-space-effect ; https://www.421.news/en/liminal-spaces-definition/). Hence the abandoned bike as the only trace of the kids, and a generic recreation ground with an oak line and a bench.
- Upper half kept explicitly empty (no clouds, birds, wires, aircraft) so the logo can sit there later.
