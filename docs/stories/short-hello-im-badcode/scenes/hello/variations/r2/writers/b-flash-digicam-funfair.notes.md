What shaped it:
- The 2000s compact look is defined by hard frontal xenon flash, punchy colour, imperfect exposure, visible low-light noise and spontaneous framing — so the figure is flash-flat and slightly overexposed, the sky noisy, the frame tilted. https://www.mpb.com/en-us/content/kit-guides/guide-to-y2k-digicam-style-photography , https://www.wayshot.ai/blog/inside-the-2000s-digicam-look
- Early compacts were mostly CCD with off-chip processing giving the over-saturated colour and purple fringing. https://www.adorama.com/alc/?p=117804 , https://www.kfconcept.com/blog/digital-camera-aesthetic
- Night party snapshots in this style: harsh flash, dark background, motion blur — hence slow-shutter light trails from the rides behind a sharp flash-lit subject. https://dev.to/hblai_filmlook/the-bad-photo-look-is-back-heres-the-engineering-behind-it-1h59
- Nostalgia-core compilations lean on bright, saturated, optimistic everyday scenes (arcades, malls, fairs) from roughly 2000-2012; a funfair prize goldfish and candyfloss fit that "happy ordinary memory" register. https://www.eviemagazine.com/post/the-real-reason-gen-z-is-reclaiming-the-y2k-era , https://ccstartup.com/blog/?p=94002
