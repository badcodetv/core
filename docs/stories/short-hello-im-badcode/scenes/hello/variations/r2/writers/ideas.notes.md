# Round 2 ideas: research notes (2026-11-12)

What the "happy 90s/00s nostalgia compilation" videos run on, from web research:

- **The feeling is the idea of the era, not the era.** Commentary pieces agree the comfort comes from imperfection and from "lighter times" when nothing was built for engagement; nostalgia cherry-picks the warm bits and is bittersweet. https://90sbabydoll.substack.com/p/nostalgia-is-a-dirty-liar · https://zeropercentsugar.substack.com/p/vol-1-chapter-3-y2k-kitsch-and-tough · https://theboar.org/2026/09/newstalgic-2000s-online/
- **Nostalgiacore / dreamcore**: revived toys, clothes and tech of the late 90s and early 00s; "pleasant, but also bittersweet"; its liminal cousin shows normally busy places empty. https://medium.com/@tracydraws/nostalgia-aesthetic-7c671b9b7c8e · https://ca.news.yahoo.com/dreamcore-aesthetic-tiktok-unsettling-nostalgia-135245091.html · https://www.infegy.com/insight-brief/nostalgiacore-gen-z-tech-skepticism
- **Summer childhood motifs**: lawn sprinklers, rusty swing sets, bikes dropped on the grass, treehouses. https://theadventurechallenge.com/blogs/news/nostalgic-summer-ideas · https://budgetpixel.com/blog/90s-childhood-reimagined-how-ai-brings-back-the-magic-of-simpler-times
- **UK flavour**: local-paper galleries of 2000s schools, nights out, first-day-of-school uniforms "new uniforms, fresh haircuts and shiny shoes"; a survey rates taking photos on a digital camera and burning CDs as the most nostalgic 2000s moments. https://lep.co.uk/retro/calling-all-2000s-kids-31-awesome-and-cringe-worthy-pics-of-preston-back-in-the-2000s-from-schools-to-clubs-4675284 · https://www.blackpoolgazette.co.uk/heritage-and-retro/retro/back-to-school-34-super-cute-pictures-of-children-starting-school-in-the-2000s-across-the-fylde-coast-including-lytham-ce-norbreck-and-strike-lane-3838068 · https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/read-this/samsung-nostalgia-phone-9001036
- **Compact digital camera look**: small direct flash lights the near subject and drops the background to dark; punchy saturated colour, warm skin; mixed-light colour casts; noise and JPEG softness; blown lamps, dense shadows; off-centre, tilted, busy candid framing. https://www.kfconcept.com/blog/digital-camera-aesthetic · https://gearfocus.com/blog/why-are-digicams-popular-again · https://www.keh.com/expert-advice/?p=59270
- **90s camcorder look**: handheld 4:3 home movie, soft focus, sway, abrupt zoom, flat on-camera light, warm washed colour, interlace combing, chroma bleed; small on-screen text (date stamps) renders badly, so add it in the edit (and our brief bans it anyway). https://blog.picassoia.com/vhs-ai-video-generator-create-the-retro-camcorder-look · https://morphic.com/fr/resources/videos/1990s-camcorder-videos
- **The glossy-optimism look (c. 2004–2013)**: tech in harmony with nature; vivid blue skies, brilliant green grass, water droplets, bubbles, tropical fish in clear water, lens flares, glass and chrome. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frutiger_Aero · https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Aero · https://budgetpixel.com/blog/how-to-design-frutiger-aero-aesthetics-glossy-orbs-tropical-fish-and-sky-gradients

Taken together: the compilations mix *media textures* (camcorder tape, flash digicam, disposable-film prints, glossy stock imagery) with *ordinary small joys* (garden, seaside, birthday, school, summer evening). The nine ideas below give each experiment its own texture and its own joy.
