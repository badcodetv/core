# a-flash-digicam-birthday — notes (redo)

Redo because the earlier draft shared "anywhere in the" with most of the round; the whole paragraph is rewritten in new words, and the no-text line is now phrased differently.

What shaped it:
- Y2K digicam look per resale/review guides: direct on-camera flash as the main light, overexposed foreground against a dark falling-off background, hard shadow outlines, compact point-and-shoot framing. Sources: https://www.mpb.com/en-us/content/kit-guides/guide-to-y2k-digicam-style-photography , https://gearfocus.com/blog/why-are-digicams-popular-again , https://www.oldcamsbyjens.com/blogs/all-about-cams/achieving-the-90s-party-look-best-compact-cameras-for-direct-flash
- Early compacts mishandled mixed/artificial light: warm/yellow casts, sometimes green tints; low-res CCD gives soft images with visible noise. Sources: https://www.keh.com/expert-advice/?p=59270 , https://www.bhphotovideo.com/explora/node/129741 , https://www.vsco.co/learn/digital-camera-effect
- The flaws read as authenticity (anti-perfect), which is the "happy compilation" feeling: so red-eye, tilt, clutter and noise stay in on purpose.
- Caveat: these are trade/retail blogs, not photo history; some search hits were junk and were ignored.
- UK working-class living room details (woodchip wallpaper, artex ceiling, party rings, standard lamp) to place it in the reader's own childhood; no brands named.
