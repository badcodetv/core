# a-camcorder-sprinkler-garden — notes (r2, clean room)

What shaped the prompt:

- **Analogue tape look.** Chroma bleed is colour smearing sideways past edges because colour was stored at far lower detail than brightness, worst on saturated reds; muted/washed colour, lifted blacks, blown highlights and noise are the other common markers. Hence "reds and pinks bleed", "lifted milky blacks", "blown-out white sky", "tape noise".
  - https://doom9.org/capture/chroma_artefacts.html
  - https://www.digitalfaq.com/forum/video-editing/9888-chroma-bleeding.html
  - https://madegooddesigns.com/tools/vhs-effect/
- **Camcorder as memory.** Shaky unstabilised handheld, crusty resolution, slightly muted colours, first-person from the operator's hand; "any core memory I have, it's on the camcorder." Hence waist height, tilt, focus hunting, the parent's forearm in frame.
  - https://www.pulp-usu.com/all-articles/2023/04/17/a-60x-zoom-into-camcorder-aesthetics
- **Why nostalgia compilations work.** Rough, unpolished tape reads as authentic; the montages are happy moments with a bittersweet "simpler time" pull. Hence "unposed, a little clumsy, plainly joyful" rather than staged.
  - https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/internet/videos-high-school-life-decades-stir-nostalgia-debate-rcna77590
  - https://ccstartup.com/blog/?p=94002
- **90s summer garden props.** Yard sprinklers were the must-have of the 90s garden summer; paddling pool and dropped bike added as ordinary clutter (no brands named).
  - https://www.mentalfloss.com/nostalgia/90s/sounds-defined-pool-day-1990s
  - https://www.foxnews.com/lifestyle/90s-theme-summer-activities-nostalgia.amp

Choices: no date stamp (the brief bans timestamps, and camcorder frames invite one, so it is banned explicitly). "Cropped tall" because real tape is 4:3 landscape and the part is 9:16. Settings fixed by the story: Nano Banana 2, 9:16. Ends with "Thanks." per house convention.
