# b-camcorder-party-guest — notes (redo)

What shaped it:

- **Tape camcorder texture** taken from VHS/home-video emulation write-ups. Colour is stored at lower resolution than brightness, so saturated colour smears sideways. Bright areas bleed a red-orange halation. Shadows carry more grain, and the grain crawls. A cheap wide lens bows the edges and leaves a soft vignette. Moving edges comb from interlacing while still areas stay clean, which is why the dancers smear and the still masked guest stays crisp.
  - https://www.strayspark.studio/blog/vhs-found-footage-look-unreal-engine-5
  - https://www.digitalfaq.com/forum/video-restore/9303-chroma-bleed-problem.html
  - https://electronics.alibaba.com/buyingguides/1990-camcorder-guide-how-to-choose-use-vintage-tape-cameras-in-2026 (Hi8/VHS-C: soft, grainy, warm, roughly 250–400 lines)
- **Why nostalgia compilations feel the way they do.** People find them fond and bittersweet, and what sets them off is homely sensory detail: everyday party food, relatives, cosy rooms. Hence the grapefruit hedgehog of cheese-and-pineapple sticks, the jelly, the woodchip wallpaper and the grandad in a paper crown.

- **On-camera lamp.** The flat frontal pool of light keeps the mask and its eye openings evenly lit for the later logo fix, and the room behind falls off to tungsten amber. (My inference from how camcorder top lights behave. The search did not cover on-camera lighting directly, so this is **unverified**.)
