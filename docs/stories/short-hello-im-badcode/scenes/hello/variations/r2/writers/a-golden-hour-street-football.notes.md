# a-golden-hour-street-football — notes (r2 redo)

Rewritten after the sameness gate flagged the shared exclusion tail ("no text, no date stamp, no logos or lettering anywhere", "on a cheap"). The closing exclusion is now in its own words ("clean of any writing, overlay, timecode or emblem; tops are blank, house numbers too blurry to read"), and the camera is described by what it does to the picture, not by "cheap camcorder".

What shaped it:
- "Jumpers for goalposts" is a UK phrase for informal street football with nostalgic weight: kids in the street, simpler times. Kept the jumper goals, the scorer's celebration and the watching neighbour as the happy-memory centre. https://sportspundit.com/soccer/terms/2868-jumpers-for-goalposts/ ; https://forum.testpressing.org/t/jumpers-for-goalposts-childhood-business/5352
- Home-video look as a list of artefacts: soft focus, low horizontal detail, tape noise, highlights blooming into nearby shadows, colour stored at lower resolution than brightness so it bleeds past edges. Hence "colours bleeding past their edges", blown sky eating the rooftops, murky grey shadows. https://magichour.ai/templates/details/cmr4mgpq8017hjh0z1exot0dv-1990s-vhs-home-camcorder ; https://blog.picassoia.com/vhs-ai-video-generator-create-the-retro-camcorder-look
- Golden hour (low sun, softer contrast, warm tones, long shadows) is singled out as the most reliable route to this nostalgic warmth. https://www.insta360.com/blog/tips/film-look-digital-camera-guide.html
- Nostalgia compilations work because remembered childhood summers are idealised blends of many days, a "refuge", with grainy, saturated texture as the recurring cue. Hence candid/unposed, overcooked reds, and the "holidays have weeks left" line. https://contemplationstation.substack.com/p/why-time-felt-slower-when-we-were-128/comments ; https://www.theworldofchinese.com/2025/03/nostalgia-core-why-chinas-gen-z-is-embracing-the-past/ ; https://atmos.earth/art-and-culture/how-climate-anxiety-is-fueling-90s-nostalgia/
- DV and VHS looks differ in resolution and colour; kept it vaguely "tape dubbed to disc" rather than naming a format. https://nofilmschool.com/powell-robinson-interview-vhs
