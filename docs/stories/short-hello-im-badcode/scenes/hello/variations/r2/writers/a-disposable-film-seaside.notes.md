# a-disposable-film-seaside (redo) — notes

Rewritten after the sameness gate flagged shared phrasing ("no text", "no date stamp", "on a cheap"). The no-lettering instruction is now phrased in this prompt's own words ("carries no writing of any kind: clean corners without printed numbers ... nothing lettered on the windbreaks"), and the rest of the wording is new rather than a paraphrase of the first draft.

What shaped it:
- Disposable-camera traits: a warm or greenish-yellow cast, washed-out low contrast, muddy shadows, soft edges, corners darkened by the fixed plastic lens, chromatic fringing, grain. The QuickSnap/Superia lineage points to cyan skies against warm skin. Sources: https://pjrigali.github.io/photo/disposable-camera , https://fujixweekly.com/2023/07/19/getting-that-90s-film-look-with-fujifilm-cameras/ , https://fujixweekly.com/tag/disposable-camera/ , https://versusguy.com/how-to-get-disposable-camera-effect/
- 1990s British seaside snapshot content: ice creams, crowded beaches, deckchairs and the resort-day feel in regional retro galleries. Sources: https://www.blackpoolgazette.co.uk/heritage-and-retro/retro/24-pictures-of-retro-blackpool-through-the-hazy-days-of-summer-in-the-90s-and-00s-3621757 , https://www.yorkshireeveningpost.co.uk/heritage-and-retro/retro/29-brilliant-photos-take-you-back-to-bridlington-in-the-1990s-3832518 , https://www.rmg.co.uk/stories/art-culture/why-do-we-love-seaside
- The nostalgia-compilation feel (an unposed, imperfect, loved family-album print) is kept by the careless framing, the finger intruding and the mid-laugh moment.

Settings: Nano Banana 2, 9:16 (fixed by the story).
