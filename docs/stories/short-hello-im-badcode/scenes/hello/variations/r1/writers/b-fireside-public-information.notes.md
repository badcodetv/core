# b-fireside-public-information — notes (redo)

Rewritten after the sameness check flagged shared triangle wording ("small red triangle with softly rounded corners"). The mark is now described as "a tiny scarlet triangular patch whose three tips are blunted, one tip aimed at the floor", set in "a fine white outline loop"; the rest of the prompt is reworded from scratch (navy balaclava, corduroy chair, hexagon-and-loop paper, fringed drum shade, crocheted mat, gas-bill line).

What shaped it:
- 1970s public information films were seen mainly on television; period living rooms were "swirly-patterned" (BFI/LWLies on the archive films): https://lwlies.com/article/public-information-films-bfi-archives , https://televisionheaven.co.uk/articles/for-your-information
- 16mm reversal was the TV production stock of the era; telecine enhancement made grain more visible (Cinema Canada, Mar 1976): https://lantern.mediahist.org/catalog/cinema-canada-1976-03_0012 , https://www.kodak.com/en/motion/page/100-years-of-16mm-film/
- Reversal stock reads grainier, punchier, more contrasty and saturated than negative: https://www.indepthcine.com/videos/kodak-ektachrome
- 1970s wallpaper: large-scale geometrics (circles, hexagons) in orange, yellow, brown: https://www.borastapeter.com/uk/wallpapers/historical-wallpapers/1960-1970s , https://happywall.com/1970s-hex-orange-brown-wallpaper
