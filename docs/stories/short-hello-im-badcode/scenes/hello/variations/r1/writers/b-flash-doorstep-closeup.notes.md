# b-flash-doorstep-closeup — notes (redo)

Rewritten to drop every phrase the sameness gate flagged (the "two o'clock sits a small red triangle with softly rounded corners" run, "of the frame", "the eyes inside the holes look"). The emblem is now "slim white circle ... tiny crimson triangle, corners rounded off, tip aimed at the ground, up toward the 2 o'clock position".

What shaped it:
- Direct-flash editorial look: crisp, raw, darker ambient, zoomed/gridded flash gives a soft edge vignette and isolates the subject; shooting vertically moves the flash to the side and throws shadows across the face, so a bracket/"Statue of Liberty" hold keeps the shadow falling behind. Hence the speedlight on a bracket straight above the lens, shadow dropping down behind the neck. https://www.slrlounge.com/mastering-editorial-raw-flash/
- Falloff comes from distance ratios: subject much closer to the flash than the background, short focal length exaggerates falloff, underexposed ambient keeps the background black. Hence "barely an arm's length", "short wide lens", door jamb and doorbell the only things surviving. https://www.uniquephoto.com/community/qa/how-can-i-recreate-this-dark-direct-flash-fashion-look-with-a-dslr-and-built-in-flash and https://dailyphototips.com/flash/why-flash-photos-look-bad/
- Night flash isolates the subject in deeper shadow. https://amateurphotographer.com/technique/expert_advice/night-time-fill-in-flash
