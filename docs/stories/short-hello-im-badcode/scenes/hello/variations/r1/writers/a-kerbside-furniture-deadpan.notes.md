What shaped it:
- The deadpan register comes from the New Topographics approach: a neutral gaze, no dramatic light or composition, centred straightforward framing, flat and everyday images. Hence the overcast noon, the "no shadows, no mood" light and the estate-agent-survey plainness. https://fiveable.me/history-of-photography/unit-7/topographics-movement/study-guide/Sa2zkrct2ugredim , https://www.thecollector.com/new-topographics-photography-movement , https://hautlieucreative.co.uk/photo22al/?p=27085
- The large-format camera on a tripod, used to record mundane street detail, is the camera named here (no artist named in the prompt). https://www.artdoc.photo/articles/archaeology-of-the-present
- Nano Banana 2 guides: name real camera gear and use lens/focus language (deep focus) to read as a photograph rather than an AI image, and use concrete detail over decoration; models treat text as texture, so lettering is designed out (no numbers, letterboxes or lettering). https://fliki.ai/blog/nano-banana-2-prompting-guide , https://runware.ai/docs/models/google-nano-banana-2/guides/prompting , https://miraflow.ai/blog/nano-banana-2-realistic-photo-prompts-copy-paste-2026
- Story: what is left when greed won, with nothing explained. A whole street evicted at once, with no people in it, carries that without monsters. UK terraces, because the reader is working-class UK.
