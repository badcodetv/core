# b-grey-tableau-office — notes (redo)

Redo after the sameness gate flagged "of the frame" and "in the middle of the frame". Both phrases removed; the word "frame" no longer appears. Composition is now carried by "exactly on the centre line" and "seen square-on and dead level".

What shaped it (web research on the absurdist Swedish tableau look):
- Locked camera first, set built in front of it — hence "the whole room reads like a stage set built in front of the lens". https://www.bfi.org.uk/features/where-begin-with-roy-andersson
- Light reaching every corner, no shadows to hide in, very pale people — "perfectly even and shadowless", "no dark corners", "skin-pale surfaces". https://www.bfi.org.uk/sight-and-sound/reviews/about-endlessness-roy-andersson-tragic-tableaux
- Desaturated greys, off-whites, washed-out greens/yellows and a particular beige — palette widened to add beige. https://hyperallergic.com/about-endlessness-roy-andersson-absurdist-scandinavian-musings-on-loneliness , https://www.avclub.com/no-one-really-no-one-makes-movies-like-roy-andersson-1798241965
- Static deep-focus long shot at a distance — "sharp from the desk's front edge to the back wall", camera at the far wall. https://www.sensesofcinema.com/2026/cteq/you-the-living/

The only saturated colour is the red triangle, so the mark reads at a glance. No names of films or directors in the prompt, per the brief.
