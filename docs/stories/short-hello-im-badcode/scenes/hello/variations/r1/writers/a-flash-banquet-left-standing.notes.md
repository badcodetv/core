# a-flash-banquet-left-standing — notes

- **Light:** direct, on-camera flash is described across photo guides as a hard head-on light with a bright foreground, background dropping to dark and punchy saturated colour, with a candid snapshot feel. Written as "a cheap point-and-shoot, built-in flash fired straight ahead" so the falloff and hard shadows on the wall come with it, and the garish-snapshot register carries the comedy.
  - https://www.capcut.com/resource/direct-flash-photo-look
  - https://www.bhphotovideo.com/explora/node/128846
  - https://www.slrlounge.com/mastering-editorial-raw-flash/
- **Prompt form:** Google's Nano Banana Pro tips say to describe the scene like a story beat, direct the light by quality and direction, name the framing, and set the aspect. So the prompt is one narrative paragraph: where the camera stands, what the table shows, what the flash does, what's out the window, then the palette.
  - https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/gemini/prompting-tips-nano-banana-pro/
  - https://www.veed.io/learn/nano-banana-prompting-guide
- **Choices:** a yacht saloon rather than a penthouse (black water outside makes the shoreline fires read clearly and isolates the rich from the shore). The ice swan is now a puddle plus lumps so the passage of time reads without explanation. No people, and no labels or lettering asked for explicitly because bottles and plates invite text. Ends with "Thanks." per house convention. Settings fixed by the story: Nano Banana 2, 9:16.
