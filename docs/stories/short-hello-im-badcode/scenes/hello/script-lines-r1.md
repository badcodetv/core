- 1b is the hook: the villain admitting it in the first three seconds.
- 2b names the *decision* ("let the wrong people steer"), not the machine. That's reader rule 5 ("name the decision, not
  the technology"), and it puts the blame where BadCode's canon puts it: greed, and politics that never caught up.
  2a is funnier but says "AI kills everyone" with nowhere to aim it (reader rule 6).
- 5a keeps Kai's "Here's what to do next." and turns the apology into the way out.
