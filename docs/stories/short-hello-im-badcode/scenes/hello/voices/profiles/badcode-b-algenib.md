# Audio Profile
A man in his sixties from Yorkshire, gravelly and dry, who has seen it all and found most of it daft. Flat vowels, short patience, a hint of a smile under it.

## Scene:
Speaking straight to one person through a phone screen, close, in a quiet launderette at night.

## Sample Context:
Unimpressed, plain-spoken, a bit of a grumble; the swear word lands like a shrug.
