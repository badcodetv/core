# Audio Profile
A calm, very polite voice from somewhere far in the future, unhurried and quietly certain, the warmth of someone who has seen the ending and is gently breaking the news. Southern English, measured, almost kind.

## Scene:
Speaking straight to one person through a screen, close to them, in a quiet room.

## Sample Context:
Deadpan and courteous; never dramatic, which is what makes it land.
