# aero-nylon-bossa: notes

- Why bossa on nylon guitar: the Frutiger Aero fan community ties easy-listening bossa nova to the Wii-era interface sound, which was meant to make tech feel approachable (https://forum.frutigeraeroarchive.org/viewtopic.php?p=5910). Write-ups on the Wii channel music describe the same bossa/lounge leaning (https://whyisthisinteresting.substack.com/p/the-wii-tennis-theme-edition, https://www.vice.com/en/article/replaying-the-pretty-synthwave-tunes-of-the-wii-channels). Bossa is played fingerpicked, so the style asks for "fingerpicked nylon-string classical guitar" (https://www.tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Main/BossaNova). I name the gear and the period, not a game or an artist.
- Structure: the prompt is in the order tempo, genre, instruments, mood, and the tempo/section changes are written as plain text with timestamps, because the lyrics box has to stay empty (https://www.layer3labs.io/guides/how-to-write-suno-prompts). It is kept under about 100 words, since community advice says long style prompts get diluted (https://medium.com/@aitooldiscovery/suno-reddit-tips-and-prompts-what-r-sunoai-users-actually-do-65b59d51bcad).
- Mood words go sunny, glossy, aqua, then cold, as the brief asks. The turn at 4 s is the guitar cut plus a pitched-down echo of its last chord, so the dread grows out of the same harmony.
- Settings: variety "normal" and styleInfluence 70 hold the instrument list in place (guides say lower variety keeps tags tighter: https://jackrighteous.com/en-us/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/suno-v6-ignoring-prompt-how-to-diagnose). Weirdness 40 leaves some room for the tape-slow echo. The exclude box adds "drum kit groove" so the bossa stays light, and "upbeat ending" to protect the fade to silence.
