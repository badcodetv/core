# r2-track ideas: research notes

- **Suno prompting for acoustic guitar.** A bare "acoustic guitar" is too vague. Name the guitar and how it is played (fingerpicked nylon, steel-string, room reverb, pick attack or none). Keep the style box short, end it with "instrumental, no vocals", and keep the exclusions to a few clear items. https://howtopromptsuno.com/style-lyrics/how-do-i-make-instrumental , https://jackrighteous.com/es/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/instrumentation-and-arrangement-in-suno-v5 , https://www.blakecrosley.com/guides/suno
- **Acoustic guitar as a meme freeze.** The "To Be Continued" meme uses the acoustic intro of a 1971 prog-rock song. A bright fingerpicked steel-string figure (played on a 1953 Martin 00-18) runs until a freeze-frame just before disaster. That is the same turn this short makes at 4 s. https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/yes-roundabout-to-be-continued , https://gizmodo.com/to-be-continued-is-the-sleeper-meme-hit-of-the-summer-1781860391
- **Folktronica.** Acoustic strings carry the harmony over programmed, glitchy electronic rhythms and synths. The press named it as a scene in the early 2000s, so it fits the warm-00s period. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Folktronica , https://www.masterclass.com/articles/folktronica-music-guide
- **Midwest emo and "twinkle" guitar as a TikTok meme.** The twinkly guitar technique is the joke. Creators dub emo riffs over clips (for example, "Midwest emo scooter kid", 2020), and the genre runs on millennial nostalgia. https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/midwest-emo-intro-parodies , https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/Midwest_emo , https://www.medicineboxmag.com/post/6-music-genres-that-tiktok-has-resurrected
- **Frutiger Aero sound.** It is an aesthetic (about 2004-2013), not a genre. Its music is loosely pianos, guitars and violins with digital sound effects and synths. Fan playlists include bossa nova, ambient and downtempo. https://www.last.fm/tag/frutiger+aero/wiki , https://www.dazeddigital.com/life-culture/article/58103/1/what-is-frutiger-aero-aesthetic-tikt
- **Lo-fi treatment of guitar for a "hopecore" feel.** Adding lo-fi sound design to guitar gives a nostalgic, hopecore feel. https://splice.com/blog/?p=37209
- **The "chill guy" meme sound is piano, not guitar** (a soft piano piece), so the "acoustic guitar + synth" memes Jack means are probably the freeze-frame, emo-riff and lo-fi/Aero edit families above. https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/just-a-chill-guy-my-new-character
- **Spanish guitar in meme edits.** A flamenco-style guitar theme drove a 2021-22 TikTok edit trend. Phonk edits are the other big family, but their trap and cowbell beats clash with this story's exclusions, so not used. https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/la-espada
