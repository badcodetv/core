# freeze-frame-prog-acoustic: notes

- The "To Be Continued" meme runs the acoustic intro of a 1971 prog-rock opener, then freeze-frames on the hit just before disaster. So the turn at 4 s is the meme's own gesture: a freeze (tape-stop plus a short silence), and the dread comes in after it. Sources: https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/yes-roundabout-to-be-continued, https://gizmodo.com/to-be-continued-is-the-sleeper-meme-hit-of-the-summer-1781860391
- Gear and playing are taken from the original intro, with no artist named: a 1953 small-body mahogany flat-top (00 size), played fingerstyle, 12th-fret harmonics, a jig-like figure. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roundabout_(Yes_song), https://totallyguitars.com/lessons/1952-roundabout-irl
- One tab writer says the part sounds better on nylon. "Nylon strings" is in exclude so this prompt stays apart from the aero-nylon-bossa writer and keeps the steel-string sparkle. Source: https://guitartabsexplorer.com/yes/to-be-continued-roundabout-tab
- Suno prompt practice: put the genre and era first, then two or three named instruments; keep phrases short; say "instrumental" in the style box as well as leaving the lyrics empty; Variety lower means closer to the style tags. Sources: https://www.layer3labs.io/guides/how-to-write-suno-prompts, https://howtopromptsuno.com/style-lyrics/how-do-i-make-instrumental, https://jackrighteous.com/en-gb/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/where-to-put-your-suno-prompt-guide
- Settings: v6 and duration 22 as the story fixes them. Variety "normal" and styleInfluence 70 so the two-part shape holds. Weirdness 40 leaves room for the tape-stop. maxMode is on for cleaner acoustic detail. Drum kit and electric guitar are excluded so the meme's band entry stays out and the cut goes straight to the synth.
