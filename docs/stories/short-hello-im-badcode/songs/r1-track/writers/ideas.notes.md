# r1-track ideas: research notes

- Suno weights earlier style tokens more; put tempo and genre first, then rhythm, two or three named instruments, mood. Long one-paragraph specs read as noise. Lower Variety keeps style tags tighter. Suno blocks artist names; describe decade, gear, production instead. https://www.layer3labs.io/guides/how-to-write-suno-prompts , https://medium.com/@aitooldiscovery/suno-reddit-tips-and-prompts-what-r-sunoai-users-actually-do-65b59d51bcad
- For instrumentals, turn the Instrumental switch on even if the style says instrumental (stray "ghost" vocals otherwise); structure tags with no words can sit in the lyrics box, but this brief keeps lyrics as "" so the turn must be described in the style box. https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/advanced-prompt-techniques
- Suno v6: say what leads, what supports and how dense the arrangement is; "epic"/"professional" do not replace musical decisions; tempo misses are fixed by making tempo top-level; v6 Edit can replace one section while keeping the rest (useful if the 4 s turn or the slow-down lands wrong). https://jackrighteous.com/en-us/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/suno-v6-ignoring-prompt-how-to-diagnose
- Frutiger Aero has no fixed music genre; fans describe slow-to-mid tempos, lots of reverb, calm optimistic nostalgia, sustained string/bell synths on major and minor 7th chords (jazz/R&B harmony), pianos, digital sound effects; linked to 2000s electropop, chillout, even early-2000s drum and bass. https://forum.frutigeraeroarchive.org/viewtopic.php?t=550 , https://www.last.fm/tag/frutiger+aero/wiki , https://uwaterloo.ca/computer-museum/exhibits/frutiger-aero-future-2000s
- Synthwave gear: chorus-wide analog polysynth (Juno-style) saw pads with slow attack, fast-attack 8th/16th arpeggios, FM bell and electric-piano tones (DX7-style), LinnDrum / 808 / 909 drum machines, big snare with gated reverb, arpeggio sidechained to the kick. https://modeaudio.com/magazine/synthwave-5-production-essentials , https://violetrecording.com/how-to-make-synthwave/ , https://www.roland.com/uk/products/rc_juno-106_synthwave/
- Trailer dread: the "braam" (huge low synth-horn blast, now standard in trailer sound design); risers climb in pitch into a cut, with a braam as the release; Shepard tone = endless rising/falling glissando, reads as alarming; trailer music is hybrid orchestral plus sound design. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BRAAAM , https://www.rareformaudio.com/blog/sound-design-in-film-trailers , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trailer_music
- Slow-down to nothing: a tape stop drops speed and pitch together and reads instantly as everything grinding to a halt; the curve shape matters. Trailer editors stop music on a natural beat to throw focus onto one line of dialogue (fits "Here's what to do next." on black). https://www.musicradar.com/how-to/how-to-create-a-classic-turntable-stop-effect-with-vengeance-sound-vps-tapestop , https://www.filmeditingpro.com/?p=12897
- Timing note (own arithmetic): at 120 BPM one beat = 0.5 s, the reel's cut length, so a quarter-note hit under the reel lands one hit per shot.
