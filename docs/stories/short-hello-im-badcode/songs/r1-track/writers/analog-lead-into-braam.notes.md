# analog-lead-into-braam: what shaped it

- **Tempo first, short tags up front.** Guides say Suno weights early tokens most and that BPM belongs near the front; tempo changes can be described in the prompt text. Period and gear tags (1980s film-score synths, 2010s revival, analog, drum machine) lead, no artist names. Sources: https://www.layer3labs.io/guides/how-to-write-suno-prompts, https://dev.to/promptmaster/the-anatomy-of-a-suno-prompt-6-parts-that-make-or-break-a-track-1i63
- **Say what leads, what supports, and how sections change.** v6 drifts when instructions conflict, so the style reads in time order (warm opening, then braam, then ostinato, then tape-stop) and names one lead per section. Standard v6 rather than v6-wild for control. Source: https://jackrighteous.com/en-us/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/suno-v6-ignoring-prompt-how-to-diagnose
- **Variety off.** Suno's advice (as quoted in community guides) is that variety at 0 holds the style tags more tightly; with four events packed into 22 s, adherence matters more than surprise. Source: https://medium.com/@aitooldiscovery/suno-reddit-tips-and-prompts-what-r-sunoai-users-actually-do-65b59d51bcad
- **Synthwave gear vocabulary.** The 16th-note repeated bass, chorus-detuned Juno-style saw pads, LinnDrum and gated-reverb snare are the genre's agreed markers, around 108-118 BPM; 120 sits just above and gives a beat every 0.5 s, matching the reel's half-second cuts. Sources: https://modeaudio.com/magazine/synthwave-5-production-essentials, https://www.edmprod.com/how-to-make-synthwave/
- **The braam as the turn.** A dense low brass-style blast, usually synthesised, used to punctuate trailer cuts; "synth-brass braam" asks for it in synth terms so it ties back to the opening's gear. Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BRAAAM, https://www.rareformaudio.com/blog/sound-design-in-film-trailers
- **Exclude box.** The brief's list (EDM drop, trap, autotune, big pop chorus, vocals, choir) plus spoken word and singing to keep it instrumental, dubstep wobble and guitar solo as likely drifts, and fade-in so the lead starts at once. Lyrics left empty.
- **Risk.** 22 s is short for Suno to hit a 4 s turn exactly; expect to trim or nudge in the edit, and the tape-stop may land early or late.
