# piano-to-orchestra-trailer: what shaped it

- **Lead with the main thing and keep it short.** Suno gives earlier tags more weight, and long style prompts make parts read as optional. So the prompt starts with the format, tempo and length, then gives the four sections in time order with rough timings. ([Medium, r/SunoAI tips](https://medium.com/@aitooldiscovery/suno-reddit-tips-and-prompts-what-r-sunoai-users-actually-do-65b59d51bcad))
- **Write how it moves, not just how it sounds.** The cinematic guide says many prompts "name the sound, but not the movement" and suggests verbs in order (start, develop, peak, resolve). Each section names one action: plays, breaks off, climbs, drags, fades. It also says to keep a cue to a few instrument families (here piano/synths, strings, drums, brass) and to state the ending you want. Here that ending is explicitly *no* final hit, against the trailer habit of ending on a last impact. ([Jack Righteous, cinematic guide](https://jackrighteous.com/en-us/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/what-is-cinematic-music-suno-ai-guide))
- **Trailer words.** Staccato/spiccato low-string ostinatos keep it moving between hits, and taiko and anvil hits cut through a dense mix. Stock-library cue notes describe "riser dissolving into silence" and an "abrupt ending following the final impact". I kept the first and excluded the second. ([epicomposer](https://epicomposer.com/trailer-music-production-tips/), [Warner Chappell cue notes](https://de.warnerchappellpm.com/playlist/label-special-glory-oath-blood/MzAyMDk4MS00MmQzOTQ))
- **No choir.** Choir is a standard trailer layer, so it is in the exclude box along with chant, to keep the cue instrumental under the one spoken line.
- **Settings.** Variety is "off" because v6 guides say turning variety down keeps tags tighter, and this cue depends on its timings ([Jack Righteous, v6 framework](https://jackrighteous.com/en-gb/blogs/vip-prompt-support/suno-v6-reusable-style-framework)). Model v6 (the precise option, not wild), weirdness 30, style influence 75. Lyrics are empty and vocal gender is null because the cue is instrumental. Duration is 22 s, as the story fixes it.
- **Risk.** No source I found confirms that Suno follows a ritardando. If takes keep a steady tempo to the end, the fade alone can do the slow-down in the edit.
