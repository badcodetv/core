# slowed-sax-meme-edit: what shaped it

- **Mallsoft / vaporwave production words.** The genre is made by slowing, pitching down, chopping and looping 80s/90s smooth jazz, muzak and lounge, then burying it in reverb and tape wear (wow and flutter, hiss). So the style box leans on "slowed + reverb", "mallsoft", "pitched a little low", "hall reverb", "VHS wow and flutter", "chopped edit" instead of naming any artist. Sources: https://emastered.com/blog/how-to-make-vaporwave-music, https://orphiq.com/resources/what-is-vaporwave, https://futureproofmusicschool.com/blog/what-is-vaporwave
- **Mall-music framing.** Community mallsoft prompt templates use "elevator music slowed", "thick reverb wash" and "easy-listening saxophone". I kept the tempo near 100 BPM for the warm half (as the brief asks) and let the slow-down happen inside the track rather than starting at 60 BPM. Source: https://www.tunee.ai/music-generator/mallsoft
- **Order and tag weight.** Guides say the most important descriptors should go first, and that a genre buried late in a long box gets ignored. So "instrumental" and the slowed-mallsoft core lead, then the instruments, then the timeline. Sources: https://undetectr.com/prompts, https://github.com/mttkllr/suno-field-guide
- **Ending.** Nothing I found gives a reliable tape-stop tag. Guides say to spell out the ending (length, energy, sudden stop or strip-back) in words, and that a track with no ending instruction tends to loop until time runs out. So the box describes the drag and pitch droop to silence in plain words, and the exclude box blocks an upbeat ending. Sources: https://jackrighteous.com/en-gb/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/suno-ai-end-prompt-guide, https://popularaitools.ai/blog/suno-v6-intro-ending-prompts-2026
- **Instrumental.** Lyrics left empty. Vocals, singing and spoken words are excluded. That fits the rule that the short's music is music only, and BadCode's voice goes on top.
- **Settings.** v6, 22 s (both fixed by the story). Weirdness 55 leaves room for the tape degradation without losing the sax hook. styleInfluence 75 keeps the two-halves timeline in charge. variety normal.
