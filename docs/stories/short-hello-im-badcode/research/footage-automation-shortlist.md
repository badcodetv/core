doesn't look like the films. It looks mundane and already here: robots on the pavement, empty driver's seats, tills

**What was looked at, and what wasn't.** Every licence below was read live from the Commons API this session
(`LicenseShortName`, author, date, hidden licence-review categories). What each item shows comes **only from the
Commons 640 px thumbnail**: for a still, that's the picture; for a video, it's the single poster frame Commons picked.
**No video was watched, no in-point was chosen and no 9:16 centre crop was checked.** So the "What it shows" column for
videos describes one frame, and items marked NOT VIEWED weren't seen at all. In-points get chosen once a file is down
(extract a frame, crop 9:16 from the centre, look at it).

**Tiers** follow the skill: 🟢 green = PD/CC0, or CC BY with the credit recorded (we carry it in the video).
🟡 amber = needs a per-item human check before it ships. 🔴 red = don't use.
**US military items** come from Commons (PD, US federal work, uploaded from DVIDS by OptimusPrimeBot), not from
DVIDS itself. As with the FEMA clip in `footage-shortlist.md`, DVIDS's own no-endorsement clause is noted: we
caption nothing as any party's or candidate's view.
**Audio:** strip it from all of them (`-an`). **Brands:** many of these show a company's robot, car or till.
They are objects in a real place and true to life. Keep the logo off the centre of the 9:16 crop, and don't put one
next to a line that names or attacks that company.

## Ambers, and why

- **Robot package handling.webm:** CC BY 3.0 from a YouTube channel, with no Commons licence review. It looks like a
  robot company's own promo, re-uploaded. Burned-in "2x".
- **Home delivery by drone.webm:** CC BY 3.0 from YouTube, Commons review still pending ("License review needed").
- **BLK-HDPTZ12 Security Camera Parking Lot.webm:** a "PD-automated" claim on a camera vendor's own demo video.
- **Self-checkouts in Lidl:** the licence is green, but a young woman (back view) is prominent in the frame. A people call.

## Rejected

- **Combined Resolve 24-2 "drone swarm attack" stills (8436765, 8436770), Freedom Shield 26 (9564944), Expresscare
  Call Center:** green licences, but an identifiable face or group fills the frame.
- **Romanian troops train in counter-drone defence.webm:** credited "Courtesy Video of NATO", so it isn't a US federal
  work and the PD tag doesn't hold for us. A soldier's face too.
- **Vkusno i tochka robot Waiters:** customers' faces and the restaurant's brand.
- **Commons CCTV / "security camera" videos:** nearly all real shootings, crashes and crimes involving identifiable
  people (as in the earlier shortlist). None of them is "mundane automation".
- **archive.org:** no green item on any of these subjects. The hits were community uploads with uploader-asserted
  licences, or BY-NC / BY-ND / BY-SA. DIMOC is almost all pre-1990.
- **DVIDS direct:** the site served a 202 challenge page to curl, and it's amber in `footage-sources.md` anyway. Its
  videos were taken from their Commons copies instead.

## Fetching later

One file at a time, a few seconds apart, and stop on any 429. The direct file URL is in each row. Check the sha1 from
`https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/api.php?action=query&titles=<File:...>&prop=imageinfo&iiprop=url|sha1&format=json`.
Then pull a frame at a candidate in-point, crop it 9:16 from the centre and look at it before writing a description.
