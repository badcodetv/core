# Footage shortlist: AI data centres + "bad things happening now" (horror flash reel)

**For:** Hello, I'm BadCode (short #17). Director's ask: *"real footage of ai data centers and basically evil shit happening now flashing on screen in a horror compilation with cinematic music."*
**Session:** 2026-11-12, run by the `find-footage` skill. **Search and shortlist only. Nothing downloaded** apart from thumbnails for viewing (they're in the session scratchpad, not the repo).
**Liveness:** I fetched every identifier below this session. For Commons that means the API returned the file's licence, size and description page. For archive.org it means `/metadata/` returned files and `/details/` returned 200. For NASA it means `images-api` plus the asset list (the orig mp4 returned 200).

**Tiers** follow the skill: 🟢 green = US federal §105, CC0/PDM, or attribution-only CC BY (we carry the credit). 🟡 amber = needs a per-item human check before it ships. 🔴 red = don't use.
**Audio:** strip it on every clip (`-an`). We score it ourselves.
**People:** read the people note on each item. Lots of these show identifiable real people. A flash of 0.3-0.8 s doesn't change that: the risk sits in the juxtaposition (skill: "the one risk no licence clears"). Pick the frames with no people or faces wherever you can.

1. **AI data-centre construction and the gas turbines powering them** (xAI Colossus in Memphis, Stargate in Abilene, Meta Hyperion): **no free footage found.** Commons has nothing. The press b-roll is corporate-owned and the news footage is paid. Flow is the route, or a push-in on the CC0 Ashburn aerial (#10).

The Frontier stills (#7) plus a Premiere push-in are the best "endless aisle" image. Flow will out-cinema all of these.
3. **UK-specific decline** (boarded-up high streets, To Let signs, food-bank queues, eviction, mass-layoff signage): **zero green hits on Commons.** Leads, unverified this session: Geograph (CC BY-SA, so 🟡 amber) and UK government Flickr under OGL v3 (attribution-only, which would be green with a credit). Not checked. Otherwise use Flow.
4. **Warehouse automation / robots replacing workers:** none free. Amazon's own b-roll is corporate. Flow.
5. **Drones / CCTV menace without real victims:** none clean. Flow (an invented CCTV grid is safer than real surveillance footage of real people anyway).
6. **More ground-level US disaster video** (floods, Helene, National Guard): DVIDS has a lot of it, but DVIDS is 🟡 amber (free key, and an endorsement / commercial clause). Not searched this session.

No spend is suggested. Everything above is free, and the gaps are better served by Flow than by any paid house (and none of those fit the £100 budget anyway).
