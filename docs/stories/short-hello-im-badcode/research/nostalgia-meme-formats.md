# Nostalgia meme formats: research for "Hello, I'm BadCode"

Researched 2026-11-12 from web sources only (no repo files read). Written for short #17, where the line is
*"Hello. I'm from the future. It didn't go well."*, delivered deadpan by a masked figure.

**What Jack asked for (verbatim):** "when i said nostalgia, i didnt mean being a kid. i meant the best things of
those times, they get editied to happy memes, where there is a bunch of colourufl writing to music then happy
nostalgic stuff, or the im just a chill guy meme, stuff like that…"

So the target is **the internet's "it was better back then" edit**, not childhood. Most of it is a montage of the
best bits of an era (2000s, 2012, 2016, the "millennial optimism" years), with captions over music, plus a small
cast of mascot characters (Chill Guy, Gigachad, the wojaks) that people paste into those edits.

**How to read the sources.** Each claim has its source in brackets. KYM = Know Your Meme. Where only weak
sources (fan wikis, SEO blogs, meme.com mirrors) back a claim, it says **(weak source)**. Where I am describing
the look or sound from general knowledge of the format and found no source that states it, it says
**(unverified: my description)**. I did not watch TikToks directly; view counts are as reported by the sources.

## 1. The headline finding: nostalgia is the trend of 2026

Three overlapping waves, all within the last 14 months, are exactly what Jack is describing:

- **"Millennial optimism" (Dec 2025).** Gen Z posting late-2000s / early-2010s throwback photos to The Middle
  East's 2009 song "Blood". [Daily Dot](https://www.dailydot.com/culture/millennial-optimism-trend-tiktok-backlash); [YPulse, 2025-12-04](https://www.ypulse.com/newsfeed/2025/12/04/tiktoks-latest-nostalgia-wave-is-the-millennial-optimism-era/); [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91457548/gen-z-is-jealous-of-millennial-optimism)
- **"The Great Meme Reset of 2026".** A campaign, coined on TikTok in late Sept 2025, to make memes revert to
  2010s formats on 1 Jan 2026, out of a feeling that current memes are "oversaturated and unfunny". KYM's
  meme-of-the-year poll put it 16th for 2025. [KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/the-great-meme-reset-of-2026); [KYM top 20 of 2025](https://knowyourmeme.com/editorials/meme-review/meme-of-the-year-the-top-20-memes-of-2025)
- **"2026 is the new 2016" (from 31 Dec 2025).** A TikTok montage of 2016 moments by @taybrafang is the usual
  origin; people repost 2016 photos, use a TikTok "2016" filter, and Zara Larsson's "Lush Life" and The
  Chainsmokers' "Closer" came back with it. [Forbes Australia](https://www.forbes.com.au/life/lifestyle/why-is-the-internet-obsessed-with-2016/); [The Week](https://theweek.com/culture-life/nostalgia-2016-social-media-trend); [IBTimes UK](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/2016-new-2026-why-viral-movement-reviving-2016-music-trends-pop-culture-1771959); [Fortune, 2026-01-20](https://fortune.com/2026/01/20/what-is-2016-vibes-trend-why-is-everyone-posting-2016-pics-talking-gen-z-nostalgia/)

**Why this matters for BadCode:** commentators read all three as unhappiness with the present, not love of the
past: rising cost of living and housing prices are named in the Millennial Optimism backlash [Daily Dot](https://www.dailydot.com/culture/millennial-optimism-trend-tiktok-backlash);
"discomfort with the present rather than nostalgia about the past" for 2016 [per search summary of 2016 coverage; specific outlet not pinned, treat as unverified];
"grounding ourselves amid rapid technological change, political uncertainty" [Shira's newsletter (weak source)](https://shiras-newsletter.beehiiv.com/p/2026-is-the-new-2016-and-why-we-re-obsessed-with-nostalgia).
That is BadCode's premise in one sentence: *people already feel the timeline went wrong; we're the one who can
confirm it.* The audience is primed.

## 2. The montage formats

### 2.1 "Millennial Optimism" edit
- **What:** Slideshow of late-2000s/early-2010s hipster-era photos captioned as a lost golden age.
- **Looks like:** Mustache tattoos, Apple Photo Booth selfies, owl-print tops, galaxy leggings [Daily Dot](https://www.dailydot.com/culture/millennial-optimism-trend-tiktok-backlash); mustache art, Polaroid selfies, bangs, beanies [Her Campus](https://www.hercampus.com/culture/millennial-optimism-era-tiktok-trend-explained/). Low-res, warm, slightly over-saturated phone and webcam photos; white TikTok caption at top **(unverified: my description)**.
- **Sounds like:** "Blood" by The Middle East (2009), the instrumental opening; critics note the lyrics are actually depressing and the clip used has none [Daily Dot](https://www.dailydot.com/culture/millennial-optimism-trend-tiktok-backlash).
- **Mood:** Sincere longing from people too young to have lived it; millennials reply that they were miserable at the time [Daily Dot](https://www.dailydot.com/culture/millennial-optimism-trend-tiktok-backlash). The gap between the warm picture and the sad song is built in.
- **Examples:** [InsideHook](https://www.insidehook.com/internet/gen-z-millennial-optimism-era-tiktok); [The Tab](https://thetab.com/2025/12/16/right-what-does-the-viral-millennial-optimism-tiktok-trend-actually-mean)
- **Rights:** The format is free. The song "Blood" is a commercial recording: do not use it without a licence.

### 2.2 "2026 is the new 2016" / "Happy 2016"
- **What:** Reposting 2016 photos and videos, often captioned "Happy 2016", or new selfies through a "2016" filter [Forbes AU](https://www.forbes.com.au/life/lifestyle/why-is-the-internet-obsessed-with-2016/).
- **Looks like:** The TikTok "2016" filter mimics the oversaturated, slightly washed-out early-Instagram grade [IBTimes UK](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/2016-new-2026-why-viral-movement-reviving-2016-music-trends-pop-culture-1771959); Snapchat dog-ears filter [IBTimes UK](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/2016-new-2026-why-viral-movement-reviving-2016-music-trends-pop-culture-1771959); overlined lips [same].
- **Sounds like:** "Lush Life" (Zara Larsson), "Closer" (Chainsmokers ft. Halsey) [IBTimes UK](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/2016-new-2026-why-viral-movement-reviving-2016-music-trends-pop-culture-1771959).
- **Mood:** Escapist; backlash videos point out the bad things that happened in 2016 [Forbes AU](https://www.forbes.com.au/life/lifestyle/why-is-the-internet-obsessed-with-2016/). A 2016 poll figure says 32% thought the trend performative [per search summary; outlet not pinned, unverified].
- **Examples:** [NBC DFW](https://www.nbcdfw.com/news/national-international/2016-again-internet-trend/3970368/); [The Varsity](https://thevarsity.ca/2026/01/25/press-rewind-its-2016-on-the-internet-again/); CapCut has a "2016 trend" template page: [CapCut](https://www.capcut.com/explore/2016-trend)
- **Rights:** Format free. Songs licensed. Snapchat lenses are Snap's; recreate the *idea* (dog ears, flower crown) with our own art.

### 2.3 "POV: it's 2013" / "2013 core" / "act like it's 20XX"
- **What:** Clips captioned "POV: it's 2013" (or any year) telling viewers to act like it's that year; going back to at least 2022 [Daily Dot, "Gen Z romanticizing the 2010s"](https://dailydot.com/gen-z-romanticizing-the-2010s).
- **Looks like:** Era-signifier objects (rainbow loom, Vine, iPhone 5, Subway Surfers), on-screen "POV:" caption in TikTok's default font **(unverified: my description)**.
- **Sounds like:** Hits of the year; one widely used sound mashes One Direction "What Makes You Beautiful" with Kelly Clarkson "Stronger" [Daily Dot (search summary)](https://dailydot.com/gen-z-romanticizing-the-2010s). Mic reported teens "faking nostalgia" for the 2010s [Mic](https://www.mic.com/p/teens-on-tiktok-are-faking-nostalgia-for-the-2010s-22917555).
- **Mood:** Wistful, a bit performative.
- **Rights:** Caption format free; songs licensed.

### 2.4 "2000s older brother core" (and other hyper-specific "-core" compilations)
- **What:** Nostalgia compilations of objects tied to an early-2000s older brother: Monster Energy, Xbox 360/PS2, nu-metal and punk CDs, CRT TVs, Tony Hawk's Pro Skater, Doritos [KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/2000s-older-brother-core).
- **Looks like:** Vertical clips of the objects, product shots, CRT glow [KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/2000s-older-brother-core).
- **Sounds like:** Nu-metal and era rock; examples used Saliva "Click Click Boom" [KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/2000s-older-brother-core).
- **Origin:** Phrase first seen 8 July 2021 on X; viral on TikTok June 2023 (@exelyox, 2.3M views) [KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/2000s-older-brother-core).
- **Rights:** Brands and game footage are trademarked/copyrighted; we would make generic stand-ins (an unbranded energy can, a fat grey console).
- **The key lesson:** the format is a *list of objects*. A UK version (Woolworths pick'n'mix, a Nokia 3310, Freeview box, a Blockbuster card, a Tesco Value bag, Top Up TV) would work the same way **(our idea)**.

### 2.5 "Core memory" edits
- **What:** "A new core memory" (from Pixar's *Inside Out*, 2015) captioning a moment someone will remember forever; meme from Reddit, 19 Aug 2019; TikTok trend from late Jan 2022 with an echo voice effect and piano [meme.com (weak source)](https://meme.com/memes/a-core-memory); [KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/a-core-memory); [Bustle](https://www.bustle.com/life/core-memory-meme-meaning-tiktok).
- **Sounds like:** "Cornfield Chase" (Hans Zimmer, *Interstellar*) on piano [meme.com (weak source)](https://meme.com/memes/a-core-memory). Revived by *Inside Out 2* in 2024 [same].
- **Mood:** Sincere, soft.
- **Note:** this is NOT a 2000s nostalgia meme as such; I found no source tying "core memory" to 2000s edits specifically.
- **Rights:** The glowing orb is Pixar/Disney's; the phrase is free. Score is Zimmer's: licensed.

### 2.6 "We used to be a country. A proper country." / "We had 24 hour Walmarts. We had it all."
- **What:** Absurd retro photo (a 1973 7-Eleven) plus the caption; from @DarnelSugarfoo, 2 Dec 2021; spread as mockery of "things were better" nostalgia, then into sincere childhood-nostalgia use [KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/we-used-to-be-a-country-a-proper-country); [Bustle](https://www.bustle.com/life/we-used-to-be-a-country-meme-origins). Spin-off "We Had 24 Hour Walmarts. We Had It All" from late 2023 about lower prices [KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/we-had-24-hour-walmarts-we-had-it-all).
- **Looks like:** One faded, warm, analogue photo; plain text caption **(unverified: my description)**.
- **Mood:** Ironic and sincere at once. **Directly economic** (the Walmart one is about prices), which suits BadCode.
- **Rights:** Phrase free; use our own retro image.

### 2.7 "This is what they took from us" (caution)
- **What:** Catchphrase over an old photo, KYM files it as a cliché of American right-wing nostalgia posts, often with a photo of a woman; parodied with things like a 1970s plane interior [KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/this-is-what-they-took-from-us-and-unbelievable-honkers-right-wing-nostalgia).
- **Note:** The phrase "Look What They Took From You" is a trad catchphrase per [KYM news](https://knowyourmeme.com/news/it-appears-gen-x-has-gone-full-boomer-as-trads-do-look-what-they-took-from-you-to-sixpence-none-the-richer).
- **For BadCode:** Coded right, but the question it asks ("who took it?") is exactly the one BadCode answers (name the decision-maker). Use with care; see idea 8.

### 2.8 Then vs now / "2005 vs 2025" glow-up templates; photo-dump slideshows
- CapCut hosts "2005 vs 2025", "Throwback picture memories", "Nostalgic photos" and "2016 trend" templates [CapCut 2005 vs 2025](https://www.capcut.com/explore/2005-vs-2025-trend-template); [CapCut throwback](https://www.capcut.com/explore/throwback-picture-memories); [CapCut nostalgic photos](https://www.capcut.com/explore/nostalgic-photos). I found no single "memories" song tied to these. The 2019-2020-2021-2022 CapCut template has its own KYM entry [KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/2019-2020-2021-2022-capcut-template).
- **Use:** a year-by-year card ("2006 / 2012 / 2016 / 2026 / 2054") is the obvious BadCode twist.

### 2.9 Hopecore (and Hopeless core)
- **What:** Feel-good montage of wholesome clips, scored with older music (Bob Marley, Neil Diamond, Sinatra); the inspirational twin of corecore [search summary of Artnet/KYM coverage; hopecore details only from meme.com (weak source)](https://meme.com/memes/hopecore). "Hopeless core" is the satirical spin: dire situations in the same style [ComingSoon](https://comingsoon.net/guides/news/1888758-hopeless-core-trend-on-tiktok-explained).
- **Looks like:** Fast cuts of people helping each other, sunsets, animals, sport wins; big centred white caption like "you are going to make it" **(unverified: my description)**.
- **Mood:** Earnest uplift. Hopeless core turns it into a joke. BadCode lives right on the line between the two.

## 3. The aesthetics (the look)

### 3.1 Frutiger Aero
- **What:** The glossy, optimistic tech look of roughly 2004-2013: Windows Vista/7, the Wii, bubbles, water, grass, blue skies, glass buttons. Unnamed at the time; named in 2017 by Sofi Lee of the Consumer Aesthetics Research Institute after the Frutiger typeface and Windows Aero [KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/cultures/frutiger-aero); [Frutiger Aero Archive](https://frutigeraeroarchive.org/history.html); [Dazed](https://www.dazeddigital.com/life-culture/article/58103/1/what-is-frutiger-aero-aesthetic-tiktok-msn-messenger-windows-vista-noughties).
- **Looks like:** Aqua/sky blue and lime green, glossy 3D orbs, lens flare, bubbles, fish, dewdrops on grass, translucent glass panels, humanist sans (Frutiger, Segoe), reflections under everything **(sources above; specific palette is my description)**.
- **Sounds like:** Windows/MSN start-up and notification chimes, Wii menu music, bright synth-pop **(unverified: my description)**.
- **Mood:** "The future we were promised" [Substack essay title (weak source)](https://oneinalillian1.substack.com/p/the-future-we-were-promised); a museum exhibit calls it "The Future of the 2000s" [U Waterloo Computer Museum](https://uwaterloo.ca/computer-museum/exhibits/frutiger-aero-future-2000s). Microsoft's own Windows TikTok posted about it in Aug 2023 [KYM via search summary](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/cultures/frutiger-aero). Corporate Memphis (flat cartoon people) is cast as the thing that killed it [same].
- **Rights:** It is a style, nobody owns it. Do not use actual Windows logos, the Bliss wallpaper or Wii UI; build our own bubbles, glass and grass. **This is the strongest single look for BadCode**: it literally is "the optimistic future" that didn't happen.

### 3.2 Frutiger Metro (and the Wii/Windows 8 flat era)
- **What:** The flatter successor named after Windows 8's Metro (2012): flat tiles, bright solid colours [Aesthetics Wiki / fan sources (weak source)](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Aero); [daisyUI trends](https://trends.daisyui.com/trend/vectordelia-frutiger-metro/). One source says the name was only coined online in 2023 and is historically loose (search summary; weak).
- **Use:** Coloured square tiles flipping in as captions.

### 3.3 Y2K
- **What:** Late-90s/early-2000s techno-optimism: chrome, neon, bubble 3D type [Photoshop Roadmap](https://www.photoshoproadmap.com/how-to-design-with-the-y2k-aesthetic-typography-colors-and-effects); [Essential Addons (weak source)](https://essential-addons.com/y2k-aesthetic-design/). Caption tools sell one-tap "Y2K" text styles [Captions](https://captions.ai/styles/y2k).
- **Looks like:** Electric blue, hot pink, silver chrome, star sparkles, blobby inflated type.

### 3.4 WordArt image macros (the "colourful writing")
- **What:** Images overlaid with Microsoft Word WordArt text, filed by KYM under Weird Facebook / post-ironic shitposting [KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/wordart-image-macros).
- **Looks like:** Rainbow gradient, 3D extruded, wavy/arched text, often in Impact or Comic Sans, drop shadows, sometimes spinning; plus clip-art sparkles, minion-era "good morning" glitter text [KYM entry; specific styling is my description].
- **Mood:** Deliberately "boomer Facebook" / cringe, used ironically.
- **Rights:** The WordArt *look* is free to imitate; use our own type treatment, not Microsoft's product screenshots. **This is probably the "bunch of colourful writing" Jack means**, together with the TikTok default caption styles.

### 3.5 Kidcore (noted only, per brief)
- Primary colours plus pastels, chunky bubble/handwritten fonts, 90s/00s cartoon childhood [Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/uk/express/learn/blog/what-is-kidcore); [Kittl](https://www.kittl.com/blogs/kid-core-design/). Not what Jack wants (he said "not being a kid").

### 3.6 Vaporwave
- **What:** Music and visual genre built on slowed, chopped 80s/90s muzak and mall culture; *Floral Shoppe* by Macintosh Plus (Vektroid), Dec 2011, is the usual landmark; "リサフランク420" is a slowed Diana Ross track [KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/floral-shoppe-%E3%83%95%E3%83%AD%E3%83%BC%E3%83%A9%E3%83%AB%E3%81%AE%E5%B0%82%E9%96%80%E5%BA%97); [Perfect Circuit](https://www.perfectcircuit.com/signal/vaporwave-history); [The Collector](https://www.thecollector.com/vaporwave-art-retro-tech-mall-culture/).
- **Looks like:** Pink/teal gradients, Roman busts, palm trees, grid floors, Windows 95 windows, Japanese text.
- **Mood:** Ironic consumerism; many read it as a critique of capitalism [NYU Gallatin essay](https://confluence.gallatin.nyu.edu/?p=10533). Feels 2015-ish now; useful as a single beat, not the whole look.

### 3.7 Dreamcore / Weirdcore / Nostalgiacore / liminal
- **What:** Low-quality images of half-familiar places (empty pools, playgrounds, hallways) with odd captions; weirdcore is "a glitch in your memory", dreamcore "a fantasy you just woke up from" [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weirdcore_aesthetic); [Creative Bloq](https://www.creativebloq.com/art/digital-art/the-weirdcore-art-trend-is-the-most-unsettling-thing-to-happen-to-nostalgia); [Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/uk/express/learn/blog/what-is-weirdcore). Nostalgiacore described as a creepier offshoot tied to Gen Z tech scepticism [Infegy](https://www.infegy.com/insight-brief/nostalgiacore-gen-z-tech-skepticism).
- **Looks like:** Washed-out, over-sharpened phone photos, eyes, clouds, red text boxes like "you are not supposed to be here", crude MS Paint text.
- **Use:** This is the turn. The happy montage *becomes* weirdcore when the masked figure arrives.

## 4. The mascots

### 4.1 Chill Guy ("my new character")
- **What:** Brown anthropomorphic dog, grey sweater, rolled jeans, red Converse, hands in pockets, smirk [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chill_Guy).
- **Origin and owner:** Drawn by artist **Phillip Banks** (@PhillipBankss), posted 4 Oct 2023 captioned "my new character"; went viral Aug 2024, peaked Nov 2024 [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chill_Guy).
- **How used:** Slideshows with the dog captioned "me being a chill guy who lowkey doesn't give a fuck" style lines, and edits pairing him with "chill" TV quotes (a *Dexter* edit reached 4.5M) [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chill_Guy).
- **Sounds like:** Gia Margaret's piano piece "Hinoki Wood" (hit #2 on the TikTok Billboard Top 50 in Dec 2024); early edits used Roddy Ricch "Ricch Forever" [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chill_Guy).
- **Rights:** **Owned.** Banks announced copyright on 21 Nov 2024 and issued takedowns against unauthorised merch and crypto (a $CHILLGUY Solana coin hit a reported $580M cap) [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chill_Guy); [KYM news](https://amp.knowyourmeme.com/news/the-chill-guy-meme-has-been-copyrighted-by-creator-phillip-banks-who-says-hes-dmca-striking-brands-using-it-for-profit). He later said brand accounts using it "as a trend" were not affected, and was fine with credit [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chill_Guy). A claim that he plagiarised it was a hoax [Plagiarism Today](https://www.plagiarismtoday.com/2024/12/30/the-chill-guy-plagiarism-hoax/). **BadCode must not draw the dog.** We *can* do the *idea*: our masked figure, hands in pockets, unbothered, captioned "just a chill guy from 2054" (the caption is not the artwork). Note: a coin-adjacent brand (BadCode has a coin) touching a meme whose artist fought a meme coin is a bad look; avoid any visual echo.

### 4.2 Gigachad
- **What:** Black-and-white, heavily retouched photos of a chiselled man, from photographer **Krista Sudmalis**'s project "Sleek'N'Tears"; model named as Ernest Khalimov; reached Reddit/4chan 15-16 Oct 2017 [Daily Dot](https://dailydot.com/meme-history-gigachad); [KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/gigachad). KYM's 2021 investigation concluded the man may be a composite/digital art project [KYM guide](https://amp.knowyourmeme.com/editorials/guides/what-is-a-gigachad-the-mysterious-meme-man-explained).
- **Looks like:** High-contrast mono, jawline, side profile; "Average X enjoyer" captions.
- **Sounds like:** Phonk, "Can You Feel My Heart" (Bring Me The Horizon) edits **(unverified: my description)**.
- **Rights:** **Owned photographs.** Do not use. A masked figure shot in high-contrast mono profile is ours.

### 4.3 Doomer / Bloomer / Zoomer (the "-oomer" wojaks)
- **What:** Wojak variants from 4chan, 2018: Doomer (beanie, black hoodie, cigarette, nihilist), Bloomer (the optimistic one), Zoomer (Gen Z) [KYM Doomer](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/doomer); [KYM Zoomer Wojak](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/zoomer-wojak); [KYM field guide](https://knowyourmeme.com/editorials/guides/your-field-guide-to-oomer-wojaks). Dates vary by source.
- **Sounds like:** "Doomer music" mixes are post-punk (Molchat Doma style) slowed and reverbed **(unverified: my description)**.
- **Rights:** Wojak is an anonymous MS Paint drawing passed around freely; no owner claims it that I found, but 4chan origins carry baggage. Doomer/Bloomer is a *template* BadCode can redraw in our own line style.

### 4.4 "This is fine"
- **What:** Dog sitting in a burning room saying "this is fine", from **KC Green**'s 2013 *Gunshow* comic "On Fire" [CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/1.6713594); [KYM KC Green](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/people/kc-green).
- **Rights:** **Owned and actively defended.** Green has tolerated casual use but objected to commercial use; he said AI startup Artisan used it without permission in subway ads (2025) [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/366650/this-is-fine-creator-ai-startup-stole-meme-subway-ads?amp=1); a settlement was reported [NewsBytes (weak source)](https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/entertainment/kc-green-settles-with-artisan-over-this-is-fine-meme/tldr). **For an AI-made BadCode piece this is the worst possible character to copy.** The *phrase* "this is fine" / "everything is fine" is common speech; a masked figure calmly having tea while the world burns behind is our own staging.

### 4.5 Sad Hamster / Violin Hamster
- **What:** Grey hamster with huge eyes, sad violin music behind; appeared on Twitter before TikTok; exact origin unclear [KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/sad-hamster-hamster-with-big-eyes); [Daily Dot](https://dailydot.com/sad-hamster-meme-tiktok-explained).
- **Sounds like:** Sad violin (often linked to a SpongeBob "world's smallest violin" moment; weak) [The Mary Sue](https://themarysue.com/tiktok-sad-hamster-explained).
- **Use:** Captions like "me when …" over the sad face. A real pet photo is someone's; make our own.

### 4.6 3D Saul Goodman (Better Call Saul)
- **What:** A 3D render of Saul Goodman's head flying round the screen to the *Better Call Saul* theme, often bass-boosted; posted by Itsnickford 17 Oct 2021, edit wave early 2022 [KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/3d-saul-goodman).
- **Use:** An "entrance" sting: the character arrives to a theme. **Rights:** AMC/Sony character and Bob Odenkirk's likeness; do not use. Our version: the masked head spinning in to our own theme sting.

### 4.7 Pedro raccoon, Moo Deng and the wholesome-animal wave
- Pedro: a pet raccoon spinning to an EDM remix of Raffaella Carrà's "Pedro", viral April-May 2024 [KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/raccoon-dancing-in-a-circle-pedro-pedro-pedro). Moo Deng: Thai pygmy hippo, viral Sept 2024, on SNL 28 Sept 2024 [Daily Dot](https://dailydot.com/meme-history-moo-deng). Real animals, real owners; use as reference for *energy* only.

### 4.8 Lofi Girl (the "chill" study stream)
- Girl studying at a desk by a window, cat, endless lo-fi beats; the YouTube channel first used a Ghibli character and was taken down, then commissioned artist Juan Pablo Machado (2018) [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lofi_Girl). **Owned** by the channel/its operators (exact assignment not found). The *lo-fi window-desk shot* is a genre; a masked figure studying by a rainy window is ours.

## 5. The ironic and dark cousins (where BadCode's tone actually lives)

### 5.1 Time-traveller formats (most on-brand)
- **Time Traveler Conversation:** a time traveller accidentally reveals the future to someone unaware [KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/time-traveler-conversation); TikTok version via the add-text feature [Daily Dot](https://dailydot.com/?p=608207).
- **"What's that? We're from the future"** has its own KYM page [KYM](https://amp.knowyourmeme.com/memes/whats-that-were-from-the-future).
- **AI time travellers interrupting memes (June 2024):** AI-extended classic memes (Luma Dream Machine), a dark shadowy figure slips in before the punchline, captioned "POV: time traveler comes to stop [meme]"; began 14 June 2024 with the "9 + 10 = 21" Vine extension (29M views on X in four days), the time-traveller caption two days later by @worry_vp [meme.com (mirrors KYM)](https://meme.com/memes/ai-time-travelers-interrupting-memes); [KYM editorial](https://knowyourmeme.com/editorials/guides/where-are-all-the-ai-extended-vines-coming-from-memes-about-time-travelers-stopping-vines-explained).
- **TikTok "time traveller from 2671" accounts** warning of disasters [KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/sites/eno-alaric-tiktok-time-traveler).
- **Why it matters:** "a shadowy figure walks into a beloved nostalgic memory" is a format audiences already read instantly. BadCode's masked figure *is* that figure, except it's honest about why it came.

### 5.2 "The World If" (utopian city macro)
- Futuristic utopia illustration captioned "the world if X" / "society if X"; earliest Feb 2018 (@bhrisgreen) [KYM](https://www.knowyourmeme.com/memes/the-world-if).
- **Looks like:** Gleaming sci-fi skyline, flying cars, waterfalls, often sunset.
- **Use:** Perfect for BadCode's good-branch / bad-branch: "the world if we'd fixed it" vs what actually happened. A leftist economics journal has written on these memes as glimpses of a radical future [New Economy Journal](https://www.neweconomy.org.au/journal/issues/vol2/iss1/memes-as-a-glimpse-of-a-radical-future).

### 5.3 Corecore
- Unrelated clips (news, films, livestreams, memes) collaged to sombre music (soft piano, Aphex Twin); first edit and name credited to Heksensabbat, 16 July 2022 [Artnet](https://news.artnet.com/art-world/corecore-tiktok-explainer-2250235); [KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/cultures/corecore).
- **Mood:** Alienation, late capitalism dread. The "after" side of BadCode's before/after.

### 5.4 Sigma / phonk edits
- Patrick Bateman and the 2019 Joker with glowing "lightning eyes" over Brazilian phonk, placed after a clever/alpha moment; earliest Dec 31 2023, later mocked as cringe [KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/patrick-bateman-and-joker-lightning-eyes). "Sigma face" trend uses a phonk remix of Oliver Tree "Worth Nothing" [Daily Dot](https://dailydot.com/memes/sigma-face/).
- **Looks like:** Mono or crushed contrast, slow-mo, flash frames, eyes glowing, heavy bass hit on the cut.
- **Use:** One beat only: the masked figure's eyes (the triangle) flash as the 808 drops. Ironic, already self-parodied, so the audience is in on it.

### 5.5 Brainrot / Italian brainrot
- "Brain rot" was Oxford's 2024 Word of the Year (usage up 230% 2023-24; first recorded in Thoreau's *Walden*, 1854) [PBS](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/amp/world/brain-rot-is-oxfords-word-of-the-year); [CBS](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/oxford-university-press-word-of-the-year-brain-rot).
- Italian brainrot: AI-generated hybrid creatures (Tralalero Tralala, a shark in Nikes) with rhyming Italian TTS; blew up April 2025; Tung Tung Tung Sahur is Indonesian [KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/editorials/what-does-the-tralalero-tralala-meme-mean-the-origins-of-the-italian-brainrot-tiktok-trend-explained); [PureWow](https://www.purewow.com/family/italian-brainrot).
- **Use:** The *opposite* of the golden era: "how it went" montage beat. Also: the Great Meme Reset was literally a revolt against brainrot [KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/the-great-meme-reset-of-2026).

## 8. How BadCode could use it (9-15 s, "Hello. I'm from the future. It didn't go well.")

The arc in every idea: **warm nostalgic montage (3-6 s) → the masked figure, deadpan (3-5 s) → "Watch this space" (1-2 s).**
The joke is the format whiplash: the audience's own comfort-food edit gets interrupted by the one person who
knows how the story ends.

1. **"2026 is the new 2016" hijack.** 0-5 s: a fast 2016-filter photo dump (warm oversaturated grade, dog-ear
   style doodle, "Happy 2016 🥳" in bubbly caption) to a bright tropical-house sting. Hard cut, music dies:
   masked figure in a flat, cold room. "Hello. I'm from the future." Beat. "It didn't go well." End card:
   "Watch this space." Rides the biggest nostalgia trend of the year.
2. **Frutiger Aero to weirdcore.** 0-5 s: glossy bubbles, lime grass, blue sky, glass "Welcome" orb, a cheerful
   start-up chime, caption in glassy humanist type "the future we were promised ✨". The bubbles pop one by one;
   the sky desaturates into a dreamcore hallway; the masked figure is standing in it. Line. End: a single
   glassy orb with the triangle in it, "Watch this space." *The look itself is the argument: this was the
   optimistic future.*
3. **"Just a chill guy from the future."** Lo-fi piano in the "Hinoki Wood" register. Masked figure, hands in
   pockets, slouching against a wall, no sweater/jeans/red-trainers outfit (no visual echo of the dog), caption
   in lowercase TikTok text: "me, a chill guy who has seen how 2054 goes". Line delivered flat. Caption updates:
   "lowkey not chill about it". "Watch this space." (Caption only. Never the dog.)
4. **Time traveller interrupts the memory.** 0-5 s: a Millennial-Optimism-style montage (Photo Booth selfies,
   owl prints, festival wristbands, a moustache-on-finger photo) to a wistful folk-guitar loop. Then, as in the
   2024 AI time-traveller meme, a dark figure slips into the back of the last photo. Next frame: it's right
   at the lens. Line. "POV: a time traveller comes to stop the vibes." caption on top. "Watch this space."
5. **WordArt golden era.** Rainbow 3D WordArt captions spinning in over each nostalgic still: "CHEAP RENT!!",
   "ONE JOB PAID FOR A HOUSE", "TEXTING 10p", "PICK 'N' MIX 🍬". Bossa-muzak (Wii-shop register, our own track).
   Last WordArt: "WHAT HAPPENED NEXT???" in Comic Sans. Hard cut to the figure, no text at all. Line.
   "Watch this space" in plain white. *Puts the economics into the nostalgia, per the-reader rules (name what
   was lost, not who to blame yet).*
6. **"We used to be a country. A proper country."** One faded photo of a busy 2000s UK high street (our own
   generated image, unbranded: Woolies-style pick'n'mix, a record shop) with the caption. Slow push in. Then a
   second card: the masked figure in the same spot, high street dead behind. "Hello. I'm from the future. It
   didn't go well." Ironic and sincere at once, and the meme is already about prices.
7. **Then / now / then.** CapCut "2005 vs 2025" year-card template: "2006" (Frutiger glow), "2016" (2016
   filter), "2026" (now, slightly grey), "2054" (black). The 2054 card is the masked figure. Line. "Watch this
   space." A known template, read instantly.
8. **"The world if" vs what happened.** "the world if we'd sorted it out" over a gleaming utopian city (the meme's
   own look). Sweet synth swell. Record scratch. "the world we actually got:" Masked figure, deadpan. Line.
   Doubles as the good-branch / bad-branch premise of GPOM.
9. **Hopecore to Hopeless core.** Hopecore cut: sunsets, strangers high-fiving, a dog catching a frisbee, a big
   centred caption "you're going to make it 🫶" over an old-standard crooner feel. Masked figure: "Hello. I'm
   from the future." Beat. "It didn't go well." Caption, still sincere, now in hopecore's font: "but it can."
   "Watch this space." *This is the voice guide's "nurturing underneath the snark".*
10. **Sigma reveal, played straight.** Nostalgia montage, then the phonk 808 drops on a hard cut to the figure in
    crushed mono; the triangle over the eye flares (lightning-eyes style, our own glow). Instead of flexing, it
    says the line in total deadpan. The audience expects a sigma brag and gets bad news. "Watch this space."
    One beat of irony; the format is already self-mocking.

**Picking advice (my judgement, not a finding):** ideas 1, 2 and 5 are the strongest fits for what Jack described
("colourful writing to music then happy nostalgic stuff"): 1 rides the biggest live trend, 2 has the
strongest *look* and says "the future we were promised", 5 is literally colourful writing and carries the
economics. Idea 3 is the "chill guy" ask done without touching Banks's art. Idea 4 borrows the most on-brand
existing format (time traveller interrupting the memory). Run the style through the story's own
`look.md` / `sound.md` before any prompt; nothing here is a house style.
