Every file was checked against its source: Commons sha1 or archive.org md5, and video duration against the declared length.

**Tiers** follow the skill: 🟢 green = PD/CC0, or CC BY with the credit recorded (we carry it in the video).
🟡 amber = needs a per-item human check before it ships. 🔴 red = don't use.
**Audio:** strip it from all of them (`-an`), as the story rules say.
**Date evidence:** for Commons, "EXIF" means the camera's `DateTimeOriginal` that Commons reads from the file.
That is the camera clock, so it's strong evidence but not proof. Where the date comes only from an upload or
publish date, the item says so.
**Logos:** the arcade signs, the Wonder Wheel sign, the iPod and the Razr show brand names and logos. They are
objects in a real place, with no implied endorsement, but don't put them next to a line that criticises the brand.
