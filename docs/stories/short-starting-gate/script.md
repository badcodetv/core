## The facts behind it
- **Wages:** ONS, February 2020 release (December 2019 data): real average weekly earnings excluding bonuses £474, £1
  above the pre-downturn peak of £473 in March 2008 (March 2008 to December 2019 = eleven years and nine months). On CPI,
  or with bonuses, it had not passed it. Sources: [Full Fact](https://fullfact.org/economy/earnings-februrary-2020/),
  [ONS average weekly earnings](https://www.ons.gov.uk/employmentandlabourmarket/peopleinwork/employmentandemployeetypes/bulletins/averageweeklyearningsingreatbritain/october2023/pdf).
  Read from search summaries on 2026-11-22: fetch the ONS figure itself before release.
- **Houses:** the UK average house price regained its September 2007 peak in August 2014 (about seven years). Source:
  [Professional Adviser](https://www.professionaladviser.com/news/1557176/house-prices-return-2008-levels) (secondary,
  from a search summary). Check against the ONS/Land Registry UK HPI before release.
- **Shares:** no sourced recovery date; the script gives SHARES no time.
- **Why assets go first:** Bank of England, "The distributional effects of asset purchases", Quarterly Bulletin 2012 Q3:
  asset purchases pushed up asset prices, and the top 5% of households held about 40% of financial assets.
