# The Starting Gate: where everything is

- An English flat racecourse on grass (turf), grey wet afternoon, white running rails, a grandstand along one side.
- **The starting stalls:** a metal row of narrow boxes on the turf, each with a front gate. Three horses in three
  adjacent stalls, left to right as we face the front of the stalls: SHARES, HOUSES, WAGES. WAGES is always the stall on
  the RIGHT of the picture when seen from the front.
- **Each horse wears a plain cloth (saddlecloth) with one word in big plain capitals:** SHARES, HOUSES or WAGES. Those
  three words are the only readable text in any racing shot. The riders are anonymous, faces not the subject.
- **The race runs away from the stalls towards the camera** in head-on shots, and LEFT to RIGHT in side-on shots.
- **The commentary box** is a small glass-fronted booth high in the grandstand, looking down over the course.
- **The winners' enclosure** is a small railed ring of grass near the grandstand.
