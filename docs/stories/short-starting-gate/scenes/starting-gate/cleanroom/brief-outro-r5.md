# Brief: The Starting Gate, the closing shot, round 5 (common to every writer)

You write prompts for Google Flow: Nano Banana 2.1 for the still (9:16 portrait), then Omni 1.2 Flash in Frames mode for
an 8-second clip that starts on that still. Read ONLY the files named here. Research the web freely for how the real
thing looks and moves (a thoroughbred led at a walk, a tracking shot beside a walking horse).

## The shot's facts first
- A chestnut racehorse with a white blaze walks calmly from right to left of frame along a wet grass course, led at a
  walk. We see him side-on: his left side faces the camera. He is the horse in `still-STALL.jpg` (same coat, same blaze).
- The middle of the frame is his bare flank and shoulder: smooth, clean chestnut coat, no saddle, no saddle cloth, no rug,
  no tack on the body; only a bridle on the head. The flank should be a large, plain, evenly lit area of coat, about the
  middle third of the frame, kept free of anything crossing it (no lead rope across it, no person in front of it).
- Layout: `ref-flank.jpg` (a real photo) shows the framing to aim for: the whole horse side-on, legs mid-stride.
  In 9:16 the horse fills the width; sky and grandstand above, wet grass below.
- Camera: the clip is a smooth tracking shot moving alongside him at his walking pace, at the height of his flank, so
  he stays in the same place in the frame while the background slides past. No cuts, no zoom, no change of angle.
  His body keeps the same size and position for the whole clip; only his legs, head, tail and the muscles under the
  coat move.

## The look: one line
Real-looking photographs of an English racecourse on a grey, wet day.

## Setting (locked for this short)
White running rails, a grey grandstand in the distance, wet turf, soft overcast light.

## Rules for every prompt
- One paragraph, your own words, under ~180 words. 9:16 portrait.
- Describe only what should be in the picture; never name the thing to leave out.
- No readable text anywhere. No people's faces; the handler, if seen at all, is only a sleeve and a hand at the bridle,
  at the far left edge, beyond his head.
- The clip prompt ends with a "Sound:" sentence: hooves on soft wet grass, a snort, rain, a distant crowd, no talking.
- Do not end the prompts with any sign-off.

## Output
Write `outro-r5.json` in this folder: a JSON object {"stills":[{"id","prompt"} x2], "clips":[{"id","prompt"} x2]}.
Two genuinely different still prompts (e.g. different distance and light, both keeping the plain flank in the middle),
and two clip prompts that each work from either still. Reply with one line per prompt.
