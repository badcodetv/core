# Research notes: music-r1 ideas (arcade music, 1980s-arcade TV-scene feel)

(Some source URLs are described in words rather than written out, because they contain the series title, which this brief bans.)

- The 1980s-set series' score is built on vintage analog synths (Prophet-5 style poly synth, Roland SH-2 mono synth, Mellotron, filters) in the style of 1980s synth film scores; influences listed are 1970s-80s German electronic, Italian horror-synth and US horror-film synth composers. Source: English Wikipedia article "Music of [the series]".
- The season 2 arcade appears in two episodes, with cabinets like Dragon's Lair, Missile Command, Pole Position, Ms. Pac-Man. Source: geekdad.com article (Nov 2017) on the arcade in season 2.
- One unconfirmed blog source says 1980s hard-rock radio plays during the arcade game scene, so the scene mixes licensed 80s rock with the synth score. Source: higgypop.com season 2 playlist article; pastemagazine.com "8 Great Musical Moments" for season 2.
- Real 1980s arcade cabinets from 1984 on used the Yamaha YM2151 FM chip (8 FM voices): bright, glassy, "digital" tones; drums were weak in FM and often came from separate sample chips. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamaha_YM2151 , https://fabiensanglard.net/sf2_sound_system/
- Suno instrumentals: use Custom mode plus the Instrumental switch, also write "instrumental" in the style box; vocal hallucinations still happen, so put vocals/choir/spoken words in Exclude. https://howtopromptsuno.com/style-lyrics/how-do-i-make-instrumental
- Suno v6 reads the style box as a producer's brief: name concrete instruments, tempo and arrangement decisions rather than piles of adjectives; keep it under ~100 words; bare section ideas collapse, so give each section a duration and a job. https://pastebin.com/RK06uiKL , https://jackrighteous.com/es/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/optimize-suno-v6-style-of-music-prompt
- Community synthwave recipe: analog synth bass, gated-reverb drums, arpeggiated lead, exclude acoustic instruments; chiptune recipe: retro soundchip, looping structure, no vocals. https://medium.com/@aitooldiscovery/suno-reddit-tips-and-prompts-what-r-sunoai-users-actually-do-65b59d51bcad , https://roo.beehiiv.com/p/the-suno-ai-prompts-list-that-actually-works-2026-genre-artist-style-and-use-case

Applied to this short: ~15-20 s, instrumental, a ~3 s gap mid-track for one spoken line, a losing feel and then a lift into a win at the end. In an instrumental, the "voice" of each idea is its lead instrument.
