# arcade-radio-synth-rock: what shaped it

- The goal is a 1980s rock radio blaring across an arcade floor, so the core sound is mid-80s production: gated-reverb drums (Peter Gabriel III and the AMS RMX16 "Non Lin" sound on records like "Jump"), synth brass stabs, and a guitar lead. "Jump" shows guitar and synth sharing the lead, which is why the synth stab doubles the riff here.

- Suno v6 prompting notes: order the style box as tempo, genre, rhythm, instruments, mood; keep it compact; timestamps are soft targets, so tie each time to an audible change (cut to a single held chord, crash re-entry, hard stop). Negations go in Exclude, not the style box. Exclude also has "fade out" and "long intro" so the 20 s take is all usable.

- Settings: v6 (not wild) with a fairly high style influence (78) and low weirdness (30) to keep the radio-rock genre on track; durationSec 20 to fit the 15-20 s short; maxMode on for quality; lyrics empty (instrumental). The 7-10 s drop keeps the middle free for the spoken line.
