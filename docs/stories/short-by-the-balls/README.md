**Jack, 2026-11-23:** "lets try this, By the Balls: a claw machine grabs the house and goes limp; "It was never broken.

**The pitch, from the shorts list (#18):** an arcade claw machine. The prize in the glass is a little house. A coin goes
in, the claw grabs the house, then goes limp at the top. Again, and again. The claw is labelled with the names of the
owners (bank, landlord, energy company). The line: "It was never broken. It was set." (Jack's). Inspired by George
Carlin's "The Owners"; his audio is never used.

**The point:** the game isn't broken, it is set that way by the people who own the machine.
