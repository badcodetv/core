**What the dial means (for the joke writers, and the caption):** when a council buys a field to build homes on, the law
(Land Compensation Act 1961) pays the owner as if planning permission were already granted ("hope value"), so every
council home costs far more and fewer get built. The BadCode government's draft policy "Homes at Field Price" makes
buying at today's field value the default for social rent; since 2024 councils may already ask for it case by case.
