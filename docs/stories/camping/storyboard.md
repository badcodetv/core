# Camping — canonical storyboard (video)

> **This is the scene-by-scene guide for recording the story video.** It renders
> the locked structure in [`story.md`](./story.md) into shots Jack can produce
> from. Narrator lines marked *(draft)* are placeholders in the agreed spirit —
> improve the words freely; **don't change what the scene does.** Visual
> reference: [`style.md`](./style.md) + the character files in
> [`characters/`](./characters/) (Bob and Tarquin have Flow sheets + Flow
> Character records).
>
> Production rules that shaped every scene (from the master notes):
> - **The narrator carries thoughts and freak-outs** over held images — we never
>   animate a character emoting (that's where the AI-slop uncanny valley lives).
> - **The audio track is a parallel universe** — recorded narration + sound-effect
>   libraries, never model-generated audio.
> - Animation is spent sparingly (the blink, the fire, the embers).

---

## Scene 1 — 2008: meet Tarquin

**Shot:** City of London skyline, the **old NatWest Tower** dominant. A huge
**"2008"** on screen. Then inside: a trading floor, screens everywhere, and
young Tarquin (mid-20s variant — see [`characters/tarquin.md`](./characters/tarquin.md))
mid-deal, cocky, admired.

**Reader's set-up:** before anyone appears they should think *"this is going to
be about the financial crash."*

**NARR (draft):** "Meet Tarquin. 2008. Up-and-coming star of the trading desk.
Betting heavily on leveraged ETFs. A great career ahead of him. He was the
reason 2008 hit."

---

## Scene 2 — 2008: meet Bob

**Shot:** An ordinary car on an ordinary road. Bob (younger, brighter-eyed —
see [`characters/bob.md`](./characters/bob.md)) driving, his wife beside him.
Luggage. A holiday.

**NARR (draft):** "Meet Bob. Nice guy. Middle manager. Normal." — keep the job
deliberately unremarkable; that's the point.

---

## Scene 3 — the crash

**Shot:** The crash. **No narration at all. We do not show or say who died.**
Just the crash — then cut/fade, and let the audience join the dots when they
meet Bob again. Sound design carries this scene (impact, then silence).

**Transition:** fade through black to 2026.

---

## Scene 4 — 2026: the Shard

**Shot:** The **mirror of Scene 1** — same skyline framing, but now **the
Shard**, and the year reads **"2026"**. Inside: Tarquin, older, at the top.

**NARR (draft):** "Eighteen years later. Tarquin runs the biggest private
equity firm in the country. The current plan: buy up every piece of social
housing they can get their hands on. In a lot of cases they don't even bother
renting it out — the asset inflation pays better than the rent ever could.
What a brilliant businessman."

*(Texture option: a "let's circle back…" boardroom bubble.)*

---

## Scene 5 — the drive home: the crack in the armour

**Shots:**
1. Night. The X8 wank tank (see [`characters/wank-tank.md`](./characters/wank-tank.md))
   pulls away from the Shard.
2. Through the glass: homeless people on the streets of London.
3. Tarquin chuckling to himself.
4. **Hold on his face as the smile fades.** No narration during the drop —
   this can run several seconds; the face tells everything.
5. Then the narrator lands the U-turn.

**TARQUIN (draft, thought/bubble):** "Interest rates up 1% next week — you lot
are going to have loads more mates. And I cannot *wait* for my bonus."

**NARR (draft, after the hold — this is a spell-it-out moment):** "There's
another part of Tarquin. One he doesn't show on the outside. And it's just
occurred to him how cold it must be out there — his car heater broke last week.
Maybe he isn't as big a c*** as we all think."

*(The empathy stays small and sideways — cold, not guilt.)*

---

## Scene 6 — Bob outside Waitrose

**Shots:** First sight of the tent (see [`characters/tent.md`](./characters/tent.md))
in the Waitrose car park. Customers stream past Bob to buy their duck breast:
some don't look, some do the apologetic no-change mime, nobody stops. Include a
beat of **Bob's POV from inside the tent looking out** — this framing gets
rhymed in Scene 10, so lock it (door-flap letterbox, low horizon).

**NARR (draft):** "Bob gets three reactions, all day, every day. Ignore.
Politely reject — which is ignoring with a sorry attached. Or engage. Have a
guess which one's rarest." — the audience should recognise *their own* reaction.

---

## Scene 7 — therapy

**Shot:** A tasteful consulting room. Tarquin on the couch, genuinely baffled.
Play it for comedy.

**TARQUIN (draft):** "The other night I drove past some homeless people and I
laughed — obviously — and then I felt… something. What *is* this feeling?"

**NARR (draft):** "The first guilt of Tarquin's entire life. Forty-seven years.
Personal best."

**THERAPIST (draft — the punchline, keep it):** "I don't think there's any
amount of therapy that can help you. You need the most powerful psychedelic
known to man."

---

## Scene 8 — the standoff

**Shots:** The wank tank swings into the Waitrose car park — right beside
Bob's tent *(Easter egg: straddling two bays — "Two parking spaces. For
that.")*. The two men lock eyes. **One short beat of judgement each — no
prolonging** — then Tarquin strides into the shop / drives off.

**TARQUIN (draft):** "Get a job, you lazy c***."
**BOB (draft):** "F***ing wank tanks. You're all pricks. …I wish my wife was
here."

The crack in the armour from Scene 5 slaps shut. Cut.

---

## Scene 9 — the trip

*(The forest-drive scene is dropped — do not shoot it.)*

**Shots:**
1. The Welsh retreat, night: dome tents by a river, a small ceremony fire.
   Keep it minimal — the shaman *(name option: Moonwhisper Ascending)* says her
   piece; Tarquin grabs his knee.
2. Cut straight to the kick-in.
3. The water: Tarquin's reflection. His **eyes close with the scroll/cut…**

**TARQUIN (draft):** "F*** me, that kicked in quick."

**NARR (draft, over the reflection):** "Tarquin has a profound moment about the
fragility of human existence. That the distance between him and that man
outside the supermarket is thinner than he thought." — **No AI mention here.**
The vision is *wind your neck in, d***head* — not a preview. The newspaper does
the reveal later.

---

## Scene 10 — the wake-up

**Shots:**
1. **Eyes open — exactly Bob's POV from Scene 6:** inside the tent, the
   door-flap letterbox, the car park beyond. Same framing, locked.
2. But outside: chaos. Tents, wreckage, frost — and **a fire burning (possibly
   the only animated element in the scene)**.
3. Tarquin crawls out into the ruined Waitrose car park. *(Easter eggs: the
   W-AI-trose sign with only the A and I still lit; the wank tank still there —
   dead, filthy, clamped.)*
4. There's Bob.

**NARR (draft — the narrator does the freak-out, and still thinks it's the
drugs):** "What the f*** is going on. That… must be really strong stuff."

**TARQUIN (draft):** "Bad trip. This is a bad trip. I don't like it. I'm going
to get in my car and go home."

---

## Scene 11 — the newspaper

**Shots:** Face to face. Recognition. Bob picks up / points to the newspaper
and hands it over — **one prop, two jobs: the date (five years gone) and the
headline (AI TAKES THE JOBS — his included).** Tarquin reads. Hold.

**TARQUIN (draft):** "I remember you. I saw you just… yesterday."
**BOB (draft):** "No no no, mate. That was like five years ago. Here — look at
the date."
**BOB (draft, the man-sized empathy):** "Where the f*** have you been, then?
You look in a worse state than when I saw you last. You alright?"

**NARR (draft):** "It's not the drugs." — the moment the trip stops being
deniable, for Tarquin and narrator both. The five missing years are **never
explained**. Bob asks *him*.

---

## Scene 12 — the ending: embers into bad code

**Shots:**
1. The two of them sat by the fire, in the car park (**not** the woods). Their
   faces changed — almost a smile, almost glad of each other.
2. The man-way apology: cans clinked. Nothing said straight.
3. It's cold. **The newspaper goes on the fire.** It burns.
4. Camera pans up with the smoke — **the rising embers resolve into the bad
   code.** Hold. End.

**NARR (draft, closing):** "Well. We were both wrong, weren't we." *(Final line
open — land the shared-humans note without going soft; see story.md decisions
log.)*

*(Optional post-credits stinger: an ATM screen — `EMPLOYMENT COLLAPSE SURVIVAL
MENU / ▸ STAY PUT` — "THANK YOU FOR BANKING WITH US.")*
