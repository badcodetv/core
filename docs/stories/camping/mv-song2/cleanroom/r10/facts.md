# Plain facts for the round-10 writers (2026-11-09)

## The film
A UK hip-hop music video, 220 s, 88 BPM (one beat = 0.682 s, one bar = 2.73 s). Two men:
- **Bob**: a rough sleeper in a supermarket car park.
- **Tarquin**: a posh City man. By this point in the song he has lost his job and everything else, and he lives in the same car park.

They are played two ways:
- **Unmasked** (the real men).
- **Masked**, as two rappers in balaclavas.

Mask rule for both men:
- A plain fabric balaclava covering the whole head.
- A thin white ring around the face area, with one small red triangle high inside it on the picture's right.
- No skin anywhere: full gloves.
- No robot parts.

The rough man's mask is grey duct tape over the balaclava; he wears black wool gloves. The posh man's mask is tweed; he wears tan leather driving gloves.

## Engines
- Stills: Google Nano Banana 2 in Flow, 16:9, two takes, with the reference images attached.
- Clips: Flow "Omni Flash" image-to-video from the still as the start frame, 8 s, 720p. Speech is never generated: no spoken words in a clip's sound, only ambient sound and effects. (Mouths may move; the sound is muted in the edit and the song plays.)
- Every prompt ends with the line `Thanks.`
- One prompt per shot. No text overlays or captions; the only readable lettering allowed is the word BADCODE on the DJ's TV screen.

## Story rules (Jack's, standing)
- Gritty, never pitiful, never fun at the poor man's expense; Tarquin's fall is never a "serves you right" joke. The bridge ends "same side all along".
- Adults only. Never the word "homeless" in a prompt.
- No lens flares, glow or bloom; no advert words (immaculate, glossy, perfect bokeh).
- The car park after the fall: wet tarmac, painted bays, a supermarket front in green, tents in the bays, a burning barrel, night, rain (the lyric: "living in a car park, rained on in the fucking dark").
- Jack picked the rapping style for the masked men: Ghostface Killah — hunched, animated, hands stabbing and sweeping close to the body, head bobbing and jutting, feet planted, never floating.
