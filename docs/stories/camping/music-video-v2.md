# Camping — music video v2 (no lip sync)

**Started 2026-09-27, Jack.** A new music video for the Camping track. It replaces nothing in
[`music-video.md`](./music-video.md) (Kai's Premiere cut), which is left as is.

**The brief (Jack):** Tarquin and Bob walk and rap, filmed from behind like a third-person game camera,
so lip sync never matters. Bob's walks go through his world (underpasses, alleys, the car park), Tarquin's
through his (the City, the golf course, yachts, a sunny beach abroad). Cutaways of each of them doing what
the lyric is saying at that moment. A *Disconnect* (Becky Hill, Chase & Status) visual reference is still to
be pinned down: its on-screen content could not be verified.

## The device

- A camera behind the head, following. Verse 1 is Bob's walk, verse 2 is Tarquin's, and the bridge walks both of them.
- Footfalls land on the kick. Faces are hidden by **geometry, never by a sentence**
  ([`nano-banana-2.md` §18](../../google-flow/nano-banana-2.md)).
- Every wealth location carries a visible cost (a caddie, a deckhand, a cleaner), per the cinematography
  gate 2.
- 🔴 **The walking follow is camera translation, which is Veo's measured regeneration trigger**
  ([`hybrid-method.md`](../../video-fx/hybrid-method.md)). Whether Omni Flash suffers it too is **unverified**.
  Plan the video as short beat-cut follows, or as a locked camera with the move done in post.

## Round 2 — make it a music video (Jack, 2026-09-27)

**Jack on the four stills:** *"cool, but they don't scream music video."* He wants homeless people around the world
(Jamaica, since it's a reggae song), GTA-style third-person following, and DJ sets or clubs.

**Diagnosis:** the four stills are in the **film's** register: muted, documentary, one quiet moment. A music video
needs a device, a pulse and a performance layer. The seven ideas below were researched 2026-09-27, and Jack asked
for them to be saved.

### 1. 🎮 The GTA character switch (the device)

- GTA V switches players by zooming up to satellite view, then dropping down into someone else. That's our transition.
- The camera lifts off Bob in London, crosses the globe and drops behind someone in Kingston, then Lagos, then LA.
  Each drop lands on the next "player", filmed from behind.
- **Each city is a pair:** its Bob and its Tarquin. In Kingston, the uptown hills above downtown. In Lagos, Banana
  Island facing Makoko across the water. The point: *this isn't a British problem, it's the system.* Showing the
  beneficiary every time also satisfies [`the-reader.md`](../../marketing/the-reader.md).
- **A HUD we design ourselves, added in post.** Tarquin's balance ticks up while Bob's "change collected" counter
  crawls. .idk.'s "C.O.P." (April 2026) put GTA graphics over live action
  ([Fader](https://www.thefader.com/2026/04/07/eric-andre-idk-music-video-cop-gta-6)).
- 🔴 **Never use Rockstar's font, logo or "WASTED" screen.** Make our own pastiche. **Whether a pastiche is legally
  safe is unchecked.**

### 2. 🔊 Soundclash (the club layer, and very reggae)

- A soundclash is two sound systems battling, which fits a two-hander song exactly.
- **Bob Sound:** a speaker stack built from scavenged kit in the Waitrose car park, run off a shopping trolley.
- **Tarquin Sound:** a DJ booth on a yacht, champagne, nobody dancing.
- Verse 1 plays out of Bob's sound and verse 2 out of Tarquin's. **The drops are the clash**, cutting between the
  two faster and faster.
- References:
  - Passa Passa, the Tivoli Gardens street dance, and the camera-light look of its DVDs
    ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Passa_Passa))
  - Channel One and Aba Shanti-I at Notting Hill Carnival ([Boiler Room](https://boilerroom.tv/recording/aba-shanti-i))
  - Chase & Status "Blind Faith": a rave shot on VHS with a street-cast crowd
    ([It's Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/articles/my-favourite-music-video-tomas-leach-daniel-wolfe))

### 3. ⏪ The rewind (reggae's own time machine)

- In dancehall, the selector "pulls up" a tune and restarts it when the crowd demands.
- **Here the pull-up also rewinds time.** The needle drags back, and the picture reverses and lands five years on.
  That's Tarquin's jump, told in reggae's own language, and it fits BadCode being a time traveller.

### 4. 🗣 The crowd sings, not the artists

- Steve McQueen's *Lovers Rock*: the DJ pulls the music out and the room keeps singing "Silly Games" a cappella
  ([MovieMaker](https://www.moviemaker.com/lovers-rock-captures-the-silly-games-and-twisty-politics-of-a-house-party/)).
- For us, the sound cuts out in the bridge and the car park crowd carries the hook. No artist lip-sync is needed, and
  crowd singing in a wide shot doesn't need exact sync.

### 5. 🏝 Postcard against yard (Tarquin's "sunny beach abroad")

- His beach is an all-inclusive resort in Jamaica. The camera follows him out of the resort gate into the real town,
  where the sound system is playing.
- This borrows the "Welcome to Jamrock" gap between the tourist Jamaica and the real one
  ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welcome_to_Jamrock_(song))). The life is outside the gate.

### 6. 🚦 The red light

- The X8 stops at a red light in each city and a windscreen washer steps up. Tarquin raises the privacy glass.
- **Show the washer working, not begging.** The Big Issue's frame, "working not begging", won a Cannes Grand Prix in
  2021 ([Ad Age](https://adage.com/creativity/work/cannes-lions-2021-grand-prix-linkedin-campaign-supports-homeless-vendors-big-issue/2314361/)).

### 7. 📼 Show the tool, don't hide it

- Pulp's "Spike Island" (2025) was praised because Jarvis Cocker kept the AI's glitches as jokes and used the band's
  own photos ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spike_Island_(song))).
- Render the world tour in a **game-engine look**, not photoreal. It reads as a music video straight away, it fixes
  lip-sync (game cutscenes never sync anyway), and it avoids fake documentary footage.

### Suggested running order *(proposed, not agreed)*

| Section | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Intro | Satellite view, dropping down onto London |
| Verse 1 | Bob's walk, with switches to the other cities' pairs |
| Drop 1 | Bob Sound fires up in the car park |
| Verse 2 | Tarquin's walk: the yacht, the golf course, the resort gate |
| Drop 2 | The clash, cut faster and faster |
| Bridge | A switch lands wrong and Tarquin's "player" is now homeless; the pull-up rewind does the jump |
| Outro | The crowd sings the hook, then the embers become bad code |

**Open decisions:**
- ⬜ **The spine:** the character switch, the soundclash, or both. The recommendation is both: the switch for the
  verses, the clash for the drops.
- ⬜ **The look:** a game-engine render for the world tour (recommended) or photoreal.

🔴 **Red lines, from the ethics research:**
- Photoreal AI homeless people in real named countries is exactly the October 2025 "AI poverty porn" backlash
  (Alenichev, reported by the Guardian; [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2025/10/21/aid-agencies-are-using-ai-images-instead-of-real-photos/)).
  Models also resist reversing poverty stereotypes ([Lancet 2023](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/langlo/article/PIIS2214-109X(23)00329-7/fulltext)).
- Jamaica already complains about ghetto tourism and foreign vloggers
  ([Trench Town study](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/1206331219871892)).
- Every poor shot is paired with its city's beneficiary.
- Show hustle, joy and sound systems, not misery as scenery.
- Any real people are paid and consenting (as Bugzy Malone did with "Run").

### g1 — player one: Bob's walk, the sound system ahead · still · written 2026-09-27, unrun

**Job:** this frame sets the rules for the whole video. We are the game camera, Bob is player one, and the fire at
the speaker stack at the end of the street is where the video is going. It is also the **look test**: nobody has run
a game-engine register with our Characters, so it is unverified.
- **Game look without slop.** The prompt names a game engine's own artifacts (baked textures, screen-space
  reflections, aliasing on thin wires) instead of the trailer-render words ("Unreal Engine", "ray tracing", "8K",
  "cinematic").
- **No game or brand named.** Naming one pulls its trade dress in.
- **No HUD.** The HUD is ours and goes on in post.

Cast `@Bob`. 16:9 · x2.

```prompt
SCENE:

A frame of gameplay from a current-generation open-world console game set in present-day London, rendered in real time by the game engine: slightly simplified geometry, crisp baked textures, screen-space reflections sliding across the wet pavement, faint aliasing along the thin overhead wires, a light film of rain on every surface. The whole frame is the game world, edge to edge.

Camera and framing: the game's third-person camera, about two and a half metres behind the man from the character reference and a little above his head, looking past him down the street. He is the player character, walking away from us in the lower left third of the frame, sharp, his back squarely toward the camera: the back of his head hides the whole of the rest of his head.
```
