**How these are built** (research brief 2026-09-28, from `docs/google-flow/omni-flash.md`, `docs/video-fx/hybrid-method.md`,
`docs/cinematography/` and a web pass on Omni prompting and the AI-slop tells):
- The motion only, never a re-description of the picture. The still carries the look.
- The camera is locked in every clip (Omni still drifts about 34px over 8s, so stabilise or crop in post). Any push-in is done in Premiere.
- The first verb is a motion verb, at real speed. Freezing on a stillness verb and floaty slow motion are the two big tells.
- One main action plus one small reaction, then the world carrying on. Two beats across the 8s.
- Breathing and one blink for held faces, staggered when there are two people. Mouths closed, no speech ever, and no quote marks or colons after an action (they summon speech and subtitles).
- The card is never called a sign and never gets text. It is held flat and square with steady hands, and the words are typed on in post.
- Audio is ambient only and placed in the world. No music and no voices. The song replaces it in Premiere.

### Lyrics on the signs (2026-09-28, Jack): replaces the captions

**Superseded first:** a full-lyric caption overlay was rendered and placed on V2, and Jack corrected it the same
evening: *"the text was supposed to go on top of the blank places like the cardboard and the gold plates."* It was
removed. The storyboard's rule stands: **each sign's lyric goes onto the sign itself.**

**How:** Premiere's API can't set text, so it was done in Python/OpenCV over the Flow clips:
- the sign's four corners are read off frame 0
- the words are laid out to about 80% of the sign, with a slight tilt per word on cardboard
- they are perspective-warped onto the corners
- the sign is followed frame to frame (ORB features plus RANSAC on the sign region, smoothed over 7 frames, with a
  template-match fallback). This is what keeps s15's drifting plaque locked.
- the ink is blended into the surface, with a touch of grain

The results are in `…\music video\videos with text\NN.mp4`. The Premiere project items were **relinked** to them
(`ClipProjectItem.changeMediaFilePath`), so every cut, trim and the ping-pong stayed where they were. The originals
in `videos\` are untouched.
