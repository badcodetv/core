# What the last clips did (round 6, watched at 4 frames a second, 2026-11-07)

The director's words: "the new sequence has terrible gesturing".

- He no longer floats: feet planted, he stays on the spot. Keep that.
- But the hands are dead: both gloved fists hover at chest height for the whole 8 seconds, making small repeated pumps, like a
  boxer's idle loop or a man warming his hands. The same move over and over.
- He stands dead centre and square to the lens; the body barely rocks; the head barely moves.
- No hand ever comes towards the lens, crosses the frame or leaves it; nothing is aimed at the viewer.
- Moves have no accent or peak: no hit, then a stop, then a different move. It reads as fidgeting, not rapping.
- The prompts asked for counted, tiny moves ("taps his temple twice", "pumps down twice", "subtle handheld"), stacked 4-5 per
  clip, and the model blended them into one small loop.
