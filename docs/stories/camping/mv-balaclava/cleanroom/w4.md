=== DESIGN p4 ===
NAME: Square Mile Shark
A waist-up photograph of a City trader in an underground car park at night, standing beside a black German saloon. He is mid-verse, leaning into the lens and jabbing one finger straight at it, his other hand flat on the car roof. His whole head is covered by a tight slate-grey fine merino balaclava, an unbroken expanse of knit where the face would be, with no eye holes, mouth hole or openings of any kind. A large white ring is embroidered round the face, and on the right-hand side of the picture, at two o'clock inside the ring, sits a small red triangle the size of a large coin. He wears a slim charcoal suit, a white shirt buttoned to the top with the balaclava tucked into it, a long black wool overcoat thrown over his shoulders and black leather gloves, no skin visible. One fluorescent tube overhead, concrete pillars, oil stains, a 28mm lens, high-ISO grain.
WHY: Flash new money that thinks a fast car and a pointed finger make him untouchable.

=== DESIGN p5 ===
NAME: Old Country Squire
A medium close-up of a portly landowner at a country-house garden party, standing by a stone-edged pool on a unkempt lawn, guests with champagne blurred behind him, one cut off by the frame edge. He holds a flute in one hand and chops the air with the other, chin lifted, caught mid-line. His head is wholly covered by a bottle-green cable-knit lambswool balaclava, closed all over with no eye or mouth openings. A white ring is knitted round the face; a small red felt triangle sits high on the right side of the image, at two o'clock on the ring. He wears a faded lovat tweed jacket with suede elbow patches over a quilted gilet, a frayed pale blue shirt, a loosened regimental tie and thin tan deerskin gloves with a gold signet ring over the left little finger, collar up to the jaw, no skin showing. Low warm sun from the side, shot on 35mm colour film.
WHY: Old money so comfortable it lets its expensive clothes go threadbare, lecturing the world from its own lawn.

=== DESIGN p6 ===
NAME: Offshore Idler
A seated close shot of a languid rich man stretched on a teak beach lounger under palm trees, an open laptop on his knees and a cocktail on the sand beside him. He is mid-verse, head tilted back toward the lens, one hand flicking the back of its fingers at the camera in lazy dismissal. His whole head is wrapped in a pale sky-blue loose-knit cotton balaclava, the face an unbroken surface of soft knit with no holes for eyes, nose or mouth. A white ring is stitched round the face, and a small crisp red triangle sits high on the right side of the picture, at two o'clock inside that ring. He wears a white linen shirt buttoned to the collar with the balaclava tucked in, a cream cashmere crewneck knotted on his shoulders, cream linen trousers, boat shoes and thin white cotton gloves, no skin showing. Hard midday sun through palm fronds, dappled shadow, slight film grain.
WHY: Money parked offshore and a man who never has to sit up straight again.
