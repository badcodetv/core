# Plain facts for the writers

## The film

A British music video about a rough sleeper and a rich City man. It is photoreal, like cinematic documentary
footage: real London places, natural motivated light, cold and grey for the rough sleeper, glossy and expensive
for the rich man. The song is rapped. In the current cut, the two men rap straight into the camera in a series
of shots; those shots are being remade with two masked men in their place. Everything else in the film stays.

## The mask (fixed for both men, every time)

- A full-head knitted balaclava with **no holes at all**: no eye holes, no nose or mouth opening. The whole head
  and face are covered in cloth.
- A **large white ring** stitched round the whole face area.
- A **small red triangle** stitched **high inside the ring, over the wearer's LEFT eye**. Facing the camera, that
  puts it on the **right side of the picture, at about 2 o'clock** inside the ring.
- **No skin anywhere**: gloves on both hands (full gloves, never fingerless), cuffs over the wrists, the neck of
  the balaclava tucked into a collar or scarf.
- It is a man in cloth. **No robot features**: no metal, no lights, no visor, no seams that look mechanical.
- The mask's colour and material are free, and should carry who he is.

## The two men

1. **The rough sleeper.** London, winter. Worn, layered, cold, tired, with dignity: never pitiful, never having a
   fun time, never a joke at his expense. Adult. Do not use the word "homeless" in a prompt.
2. **The posh man.** Very rich, British, City money: old-money ease or flash new money, your call. Smug,
   untouchable, comfortable.

## Where they rap now (the shots being replaced)

The rough sleeper's current rap shots:
- a narrow brick alley at night, graffiti, big bins, steam from a vent, close and waist-up
- under a concrete motorway flyover at night, graffiti on the pillars, a fire burning in an oil drum, small tents behind
- a flat rooftop at dusk over a city skyline, standing, hands low
- sitting on the pavement of the Thames embankment at night by the river wall, a small green tent beside him, St Paul's dome behind

The posh man's current rap shots:
- a glass-walled penthouse office high over the City, leaning into the lens
- a fine-dining restaurant table, white cloth, other diners behind
- a rooftop terrace by a glass tower, very close
- the deck of a large white yacht at sea
- a beach lounger under palms, a cocktail and a laptop
- an underground car park beside a black BMW
- a country-house garden party by a pool, guests with champagne behind

## How they rap

Straight into the lens, mid-performance, with big hand gestures. With no mouth showing, the head, shoulders and
hands carry the rap.
