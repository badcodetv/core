=== DESIGN r4 ===
NAME: The Crocheted Ochre
A wiry, narrow-shouldered man stands on a gravel rooftop beside a rusted water tank at dusk, the city's uneven towers falling away behind his right shoulder, a sodium lamp just coming on below. Over his head is a closed balaclava crocheted from reused yarn in faded ochre yellow, open-textured stitches with a seam of salvaged grey wool at the neck: crochet covers the entire face, no eye holes, no mouth opening, nothing open anywhere. A broad white ring is worked round the face, and toward the upper right as the camera sees it, at two o'clock on that ring, a small red triangle sits over the eye nearest the frame's right edge, his left eye. He wears a short black puffer jacket patched with silver tape, a bin-bag poncho knotted at the collar, tracksuit bottoms tucked into worn work boots, thick blue rubber-palmed gloves. Waist-up, slightly low, 28mm: both gloved hands roll over each other in front of him mid-flow, knees bouncing. Light grain, cold dusk air.
WHY: He is quick, light and restless, a man who patches what he has and keeps moving because standing still is how the cold gets in.

=== DESIGN r5 ===
NAME: The Long Black Coat
A tall, lean man stands in a narrow Shoreditch brick alley at night, wheelie bins at his back, steam rising from a wall vent, one sodium streetlamp throwing amber light across him from the right. His head is in a tight, ribbed oxblood acrylic balaclava, faded unevenly, a frayed edge at the neck: knitted cloth across the whole face, no eye holes, no nose or mouth opening at all. A wide white ring is stitched round the face, and high on the right side of the picture, at about two o'clock on the ring, a small red triangle the size of a large coin sits over his left eye. He wears a long black wool overcoat far too big, buttoned to the throat over a grey hoodie, a clear plastic poncho over the shoulders, black leather gloves split at one knuckle, cuffs over the wrists. Low wide angle, 24mm, close: he jabs one gloved finger straight at the lens, foreshortened, head tilted sharp on the punchline. High-ISO noise, crushed blacks.
WHY: He is all edge and posture, a man who dresses like he still has somewhere to be and points the blame straight at you.

=== DESIGN r6 ===
NAME: The Foil Cape
A broad, heavy-set man sits forward on an upturned milk crate under a concrete motorway flyover at night, a fire burning in an oil drum just left of frame lighting him warm orange from below, graffiti on the pillars and two small dome tents dim behind him. His head is in a close-fitting forest-green army-surplus wool balaclava, worn thin and shiny at the crown: smooth knitted wool over the whole face, no eye holes, no mouth hole, no openings of any kind. A white ring is stitched round the face, and on the image's right side, at two o'clock inside the ring, a small red triangle sits high over his left eye. A folded foil blanket hangs round his shoulders like a cape over a quilted navy work jacket; a rolled sleeping bag lies strapped to a rucksack at his boots; thick grey work gloves cover both hands. Seated close, waist-up, eye-level, he holds both open palms out toward the lens, mid-line, shoulders rolled forward. Natural grain.
WHY: He is the steady, heavy centre of a camp who makes the cold look survivable and asks you, palms open, what you would have done.
