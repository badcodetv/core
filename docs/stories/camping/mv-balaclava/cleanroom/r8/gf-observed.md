# Ghostface Killah: what his body does to camera (watched 2026-11-07)

Watched (frame sheets, 2 and 4 fps):
- "Run" ft. Jadakiss (BET version, YouTube eMSY3zfLxRA), 0:35-0:55, his verse to the lens on a street and a fenced walkway. Best source.
- "Cherchez LaGhost" (eIdPfbwNV3w), 0:30-0:50: sofa scenes, extreme wide-lens close-ups.
- "Back Like That" (3zUDcdH3OI4), 0:40-1:00: mostly story scenes, one finger point to lens. Little to-camera rapping.
- Web search found no written account of his gestures; reviews only say "impassioned, aggressive", "presence".

Stance and weight
- Hunched forward from the hips, shoulders up and rounded, knees soft. Weight on the front of the feet, leaning INTO the lens.
- Feet stay put; the body sways and rocks side to side above them, so he drifts left and right across the frame without walking.

Framing and camera
- Low wide-angle lens, waist-up or chest-up, close: the hat and shoulders nearly fill the top of frame, head often near the top edge.
- Often off-centre, sometimes cocked to one side of frame. Background bends a little at the edges (wide lens).
- Cherchez: extreme close-up, face shoved into a fisheye lens, mouth wide.

Hands
- Hands live LOW: belly to waist height, below the chest. Never above the shoulders in these clips.
- Signature move: both hands flat, palms down, fingers splayed, sweeping across the body at waist height, like smoothing a table or calming a crowd, then flicking out.
- One hand chops flat sideways across the belly while the other hangs or hooks at the hip.
- Thumb jabbed back at his own chest ("me") in short repeated pokes.
- One-finger point at the lens (Back Like That), short and quick.
- Hands are busy all the time; one is always moving while the other sets.

Head and shoulders
- Shoulders roll and pump alternately, left then right, on the beat; the whole torso rocks with them.
- Head cocks and dips, chin jutting forward on the stressed words; face scrunched, mouth wide.

How a gesture starts and stops
- Abrupt and choppy: snaps out, holds a beat, snaps to the next shape. No slow, smooth arcs. Flicks at the end of a sweep.
- Gestures overlap the sway, so nothing ever comes to rest.

What he never does
- Never stands square and still; never holds a hand at the temple; never stays dead centre at eye level.
- No microphone in the to-camera music-video shots. Hands not raised high, no big overhead arms.
- Feet do not travel toward the camera.
