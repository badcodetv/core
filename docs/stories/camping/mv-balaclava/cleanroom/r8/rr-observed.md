# Rick Ross to camera: what the body does (watched 2026-11-07)

Watched (frame sheets at 1-4 fps, 480p):
- "Hold Me Back" official video (YouTube HVO5WhIm4uI), 0:30-0:54 and 1:05-1:25: the most to-camera footage.
- "Hustlin'" official video (Ohg5Gx31X2E), 0:40-1:00: raps from the driver's seat, to the side window and lens.
- Web: live reviews stress a "commanding", "boss" presence, controlling the pace, pausing to bask and pose.

Stance and weight
- Planted. Feet barely move; he does not bounce or travel. Weight sits back, belly forward, chest out.
- The energy is in the torso leaning towards the lens, not in the legs. He tips forward into a close low lens, then settles back.

Framing and camera
- Low angle, wide lens, very close: his chest and face fill the frame, slight fisheye bulge. Camera is handheld and
  pushes in on him; he is often off-centre with the crowd or a building behind.
- Medium-close in the car shots: profile/three-quarter, glancing to lens.

Head and shoulders
- Signature: chin lifted, head tipped back, looking down his nose or up at the sky between lines; a slow, satisfied
  roll of the head. Nods are big and slow, on every other beat, not a fast bob. Shoulders rock side to side, heavy.

Hands
- Unhurried, wide, lordly. Open palms raised to shoulder height and turned up ("look at all this").
- Hands pressed together in front of the chest, rubbed slowly (prayer/counting-money rub).
- One index finger lifted beside the face, or flicked at the lens; a fist pumped towards the camera on a hard line.
- Fingers adjust the sunglasses / touch the brim, then the hand sweeps away.
- A gesture starts slow, peaks, and is held a beat (he poses), then drops loosely. Never twitchy, never a full stop.
- The hands work in pairs or mirror each other; the off hand is rarely idle (wrist, chain, palm open).

What he never does
- No mic in these videos. No fast footwork, no jumping, no tight choreography, no hurry. Never looks nervous or
  explains; never tucks his hands away. No presenter-style chest-height hovering.
