# Kendrick Lamar to camera: what the body does (watched 2026-11-07)

Watched (yt-dlp sections, frame sheets at 2 and 4 fps):
- HUMBLE. (official, tvTRZJ-4EyI) 0:40-0:50 robe-under-the-spotlight verse; 1:32-1:40 white hoodie, head on fire, line of men.
- DNA. (official, NLZRYQMLDW4) 2:00-2:40 interrogation table, seated, face to face.
Web: Uproxx and mxdwn on "Rich Spirit" (free, slightly awkward, unchoreographed movement vs. near-motionless SNL set);
Complex / Charm La'Donna: most "Not Like Us" moves were him "just vibing", not choreographed; DJ Booth: body language is
a chosen character, theatrical; Super Bowl coverage: choppy, staccato, glitch-like moves.

Framing and camera
- Favourite to-camera frame (HUMBLE robe): a wide lens very close and LOW, looking up at him, off-centre, lots of dark
  space and a single hard top light. The lens distortion makes the reaching hand enormous and the head small.
- Second mode (HUMBLE fire): locked, centred mid shot, near-deadpan; the performance is all in the head and face.
- DNA: side-on and close, two people almost nose to nose; hands fill the frame between the faces.

Stance and weight
- Body turned three-quarters to the lens, one shoulder leading. Weight forward on the front foot, torso pitched from the
  hips toward the camera. Feet stay put; he leans and lunges from the waist rather than stepping.
- Seated (DNA) he hunches forward over the table, elbows in, shoulders up round his ears.

Hands
- Signature: one arm thrust straight at the lens, palm open, fingers spread wide, then pulled back; the other hand
  curled near the chin or collarbone, fingers splayed or pinching. Big moves: the full arm length, right into the lens.
- DNA: both hands work together in front of the chest and face, fingers spread and clawed, wringing, clapping once,
  flicking outward, then snapping back. Hands are almost never still and almost never symmetrical.
- Fire shot: hands hidden entirely; he lets the head do it.

Head and shoulders
- Head cocked to one side, chin tucked, looking up from under the brow. Small sharp tilts and nods on the stresses,
  not a continuous bob. Shoulders jolt up with the hands; the upper body rocks forward on the hard lines.

How a gesture starts and stops
- Staccato: a move fires fast and STOPS dead, holds a beat, then the next one. No smooth easing, no swaying loop.
- Expression flips from flat deadpan to a sudden grin or snarl and back.

What he never does
- Never the presenter's two open hands at chest height, never a temple-tap, never a still second hand clutching
  clothes, never a wide flat symmetrical stance, no bouncing on the spot, no travelling toward the camera on his feet.
- In these to-camera shots no microphone.
