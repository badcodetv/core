# Method Man to camera: what the body does (watched 2026-11-07)

Watched (frame sheets at 2 and 4 fps):
- "Bring The Pain" (1994), 0:30-0:50 and 1:10-1:30: subway car verse, tunnel crew walk, red-lit close-ups.
- Wu-Tang Clan "Method Man" video, 0:50-1:10: fisheye from below against the sky.

Camera and framing
- The lens is LOW and CLOSE, often below his chest, wide/fisheye, looking up at him. Head and shoulders fill
  the frame, sky or ceiling behind. Never a polite eye-level mid shot.
- He leans DOWN over the lens from the waist, as if looming over someone on the floor. Head pushed forward,
  chin over the camera. The lean is from the hips; the feet stay put.
- Often off-centre: head in the left or right third, shoulder cut by the frame edge; in the subway he is
  sat/slouched against the seat with a shoulder jammed to one side.

Stance and weight
- Weight forward on the balls of the feet, knees soft, hunched. Bounces down on the beat (a dip, not a step).
- In wider shots (subway aisle) one arm hangs off a grab rail, hip cocked, the other hand loose at the waist.

Hands
- Signature: the OPEN HAND thrust straight at the lens, fingers splayed wide, palm forward, so close it fills
  half the frame and blurs. Then it pulls back and comes again on the next bar.
- A fist punched toward the lens, fast, arm fully extended, then yanked back.
- Low sideways flicks and chops at waist height, the hand loose at the wrist, fingers spread, swatting.
- One hand often stays low and loose (hanging, or tapping the chest/jacket) while the other does the work.
  It does not grip anything rigidly; it swings.
- Both hands up, fingers clawed, beside the head on a big line (once), then dropped.

Head and shoulders
- Head ducks and jerks forward on the stressed syllables, a sharp nod down into the lens.
- Shoulders roll and hunch; one shoulder drops as the opposite hand goes out.
- Face scrunched, snarling, mouth wide; he mugs into the lens.

How a move starts and stops
- Moves are fast and snapped: out in one beat, back in the next. Nothing slow or floaty. Between punches he
  keeps bouncing; he never holds a pose.

What he never does
- Never stands square and still at eye level. Never folds his arms or holds a lapel. No mic in these videos.
- Never walks toward the lens while rapping; the approach comes from leaning, not stepping.
