# Eminem to camera: what the body does (watched 2026-11-07)

Watched (frame sheets, 1.6-4 fps, 480p sections):
- "Not Afraid" official video (youtube j5-yKhDd64s), 0:30-0:50 and 1:05-1:25: rooftop and derelict-room to-camera verses.
- "Lose Yourself" video (youtube _Yhyp-_hX2s), 3:00-3:20: 8 Mile battle-stage and car-park footage, with a mic.
- Also skimmed "Rap God" 1:10-1:30 (mostly static suit-and-shades close-ups, little body) and "The Way I Am" 1:00-1:20 (mostly story shots, little to camera).
Web search on his stage presence was thin: "spastic movements", hand-as-gun toward the lens ("Best Friend" analysis), kneeling, pacing.

Stance and weight
- Weight forward, on the front of the feet; shoulders rounded and hunched forward, chest closed. He leans INTO the lens, then pulls back.
- Feet stay planted wide for the to-camera verses; travel happens in separate walking shots, not while rapping.

Framing and camera
- Very close, wide lens: head and shoulders fill the frame, face often in the top third, the lens near enough that a hand thrust at it goes huge.
- Frequently off-centre, slightly low, head tilted; the camera is a person he is arguing with, not an audience.

Hands (the key)
- Big, sudden, one at a time: a pointing finger jabbed straight at the lens (Not Afraid 0:49), a flat hand slapped onto his own chest, arms crossed hard over the chest then flung out wide to both sides (1:19-1:21), one arm swept straight out sideways at shoulder height (1:06).
- With a mic (8 Mile): the mic hand clamps the mic tight to the mouth, elbow up; the free hand chops forward and down at the opponent, flicks fingers, points.
- A gesture starts with a snap, hits a hard stop and holds a beat, then drops or snaps to the next. No smooth looping waves.
- The two hands do different things; the free one is never parked.

Head and shoulders
- Head juts forward on the accent and cocks to one side; chin drives the line. Shoulders jerk up and forward with the stresses; a hunch, not a bounce.
- Eyes locked on the lens, brow down; face scrunched in effort.

What he never does
- No hand to the temple, no presenter "explaining" palms at chest height, no side-to-side dancing sway, no relaxed lean-back cool pose.
- Never stands square and calm at mid distance while rapping; calm only in the walking/brooding cutaways.
