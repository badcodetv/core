=== DESIGN b5 ===
NAME: Heather Fair Isle
A close, waist-up film still on a rooftop terrace in the City of London at grey midday, the curved glass of the Gherkin and the bulging top of the Walkie-Talkie soft behind him. A rich British man in a full-head balaclava of fine wool Fair Isle knitting, bold geometric bands in heather purple, moss green, oatmeal, rust and slate blue, with no eye holes, no mouth hole and no openings: the face is one smooth, unbroken stretch of patterned knit. Round the face runs a large plain ivory knitted ring edged with a narrow band of tiny motifs. On the right-hand side of the picture, high inside that ring at two o'clock, over his left eye, sits a small solid red knitted triangle; nothing else red on the face. He wears a camel cashmere overcoat with a velvet collar, a Fair Isle tank top over an oxford shirt, a cashmere scarf tucked over the balaclava's neck, and tan deerskin gloves covering every finger. Both gloved hands rise slowly from the waist, palms up, mid-verse. Overcast daylight, 35mm lens, light film grain.
WHY: The only patterned knit in the set, a Highland-weekend colour world that makes inherited money look cosy and smug.

=== DESIGN b6 ===
NAME: Luxury Luggage Canvas
A waist-up film still in a dim London underground car park, cold fluorescent tubes overhead, a black luxury saloon car parked close behind him, its paint catching the light. A flash young rich man wears a stiff full-head hood of coated canvas printed all over with a repeated interlocking abstract mark in tan and chocolate brown, with no letters, edged in glossy honey-coloured leather piping with gold stitching. The hood has no eye holes, no mouth opening and no gaps: the face is smooth printed canvas. A large cream leather ring set with small gold rivets circles the face. On the right of the picture, high inside the ring at two o'clock, over his left eye, is a small red patent-leather triangle with a hard shine. He wears a chocolate suede bomber over a black roll-neck that covers the hood's neck, tan leather driving gloves over every finger, a chunky gold chain round one gloved wrist, and a matching canvas holdall at his feet. He jabs a thumb back over his shoulder at the car, chin raised, mid-line. 28mm lens, high-ISO grain, no readable text anywhere.
WHY: Flash new money wearing the most recognisable kind of luggage pattern as his own face.

=== DESIGN b7 ===
NAME: Toile de Jouy Lounger

A large band of plain ivory cotton piped in navy forms a ring round the face. On the right-hand side of the picture, high inside the ring at two o'clock, over his left eye, sits a small solid red silk triangle outlined in navy piping. === DESIGN b8 ===
NAME: Giant Houndstooth City
A waist-up film still in a glass-walled penthouse office high over the City of London at dusk, St Paul's dome and grey towers below, smeared reflections on the glass. A rich man leans over a dark wooden desk toward the lens. His full-head hood is tailored from heavy black-and-white wool cloth in an oversized houndstooth check, each tooth about the size of a thumbnail, cut in shaped panels with a crisp seam down the crown, with no eye holes, no mouth opening and no gaps: the face is unbroken checked cloth. A broad ring of solid white wool felt bound in black ribbon circles the face. On the right of the picture, high inside the ring at two o'clock, over his left eye, is a small solid red felt triangle. He wears a matching houndstooth overcoat over a charcoal three-piece suit, a white shirt buttoned to the hood's neck, a black knitted tie, black leather gloves covering every finger, an umbrella with a cane handle hooked on the desk. Both gloved index fingers point straight down at the desk on the beat. Soft window light from one side, 35mm lens, light grain.
WHY: Graphic black-and-white City power, as sharp as a share chart, and nothing like a knit.
