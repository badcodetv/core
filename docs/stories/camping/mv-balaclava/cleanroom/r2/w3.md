=== DESIGN b1 ===
NAME: Estate Tweed Squire
A waist-up photograph of a man rapping straight into the lens at an English country-house garden party, guests with champagne softly out of focus by a pool behind him, overcast afternoon light. His whole head is covered by a tailored hood of heavy moss-green herringbone tweed with a bold oversized scarlet overcheck, cut in panels like a flat cap; the face area is one unbroken expanse of tweed with no eye holes, no mouth opening, no openings at all. A thick cream suede piping forms a large ring round the face. Inside that ring, high on the right-hand side of the picture at about two o'clock, sits a crisp red leather triangle the size of a large coin. He wears a matching three-piece tweed suit, checked shirt, knitted silk tie, quilted gilet and tan leather gloves covering both hands fully, a gold signet ring over the left little finger. One gloved finger is raised by his temple mid-line, a champagne flute held level in the other hand. Shot on a 35mm lens, natural colour, fine film grain.
WHY: Land money: his head is cut from the same cloth as the grouse moor he owns.

=== DESIGN b2 ===
NAME: Gold Chainmail Hood
A waist-up photograph, slightly low angle, of a man rapping into the lens on the teak deck of a large white yacht at sea, hard midday sun, blue water and white railings behind him. His whole head is wrapped in a hood of fine polished yellow-gold metal chain links draped close over a black silk under-hood, so the face area is a smooth unbroken surface of gold mesh with no eye holes, no mouth opening, no gaps; the sun breaks into hard, scattered pinpoint highlights across the links. A band of brighter white-gold links forms a large ring round the face. Inside the ring, high on the right side of the picture at about two o'clock, is a small triangle of red enamel plates. He wears an open cream linen shirt over a cream cashmere crewneck, white linen trousers, worn leather boat shoes, and white leather driving gloves covering both hands with no skin showing. Arms spread wide, palms up, presenting the sea as if he owns it. Documentary photograph, 28mm lens, natural colour, light grain.
WHY: Armour made literally of money, flashing and untouchable under a hard sun.

=== DESIGN b4 ===
NAME: Black Crystal Couture
A close waist-up photograph of a man leaning right into the lens in a glass-walled penthouse office high above the City of London at night, the lit towers and the river blurred behind the glass, a single desk lamp as the key light. His whole head is covered by a couture mask on a black silk base, thickly set with faceted jet-black crystals that throw hard, sharp pinpoints of light; the face area is a continuous field of black stones, no eye holes, no mouth opening, no openings anywhere. A close band of clear crystals reads bright white as a large ring round the face. Inside it, high on the right-hand side of the picture at about two o'clock, is a small cluster of ruby-red crystals shaped as a triangle. He wears a black velvet dinner jacket over a black silk shirt, black patent leather gloves covering both hands, a slim gold watch at the cuff. He leans on both gloved knuckles planted on the desk, head cocked mid-verse. Shot on a 35mm lens, crushed blacks, fine digital noise.
WHY: Night-time money in a hard glittering skin, the City's lights repeated in every facet.
