=== CLIP m12 ===
Fast, aggressive performance from the very first frame. The camera is locked off and does not move. From the deep lunge he pumps up and down on bent knees to the beat, mic pressed to his mask, and stabs his gloved finger at the lens on every stressed word, driving his chest closer with each jab. His head jolts forward on the downbeat, snaps sideways and whips straight back to the lens as if daring the viewer to look away. His boots stamp the concrete. Sound: traffic rumbling on the flyover above, wind under the bridge, no voices.

Between jumps he struts sideways along the parapet with a rolling swagger, chest thrust out, and jabs his other finger down at the city lights below as if sneering at the people there, mask turned back to the lens.
