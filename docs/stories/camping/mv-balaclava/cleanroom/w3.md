=== DESIGN p1 ===
NAME: The Chairman's Cashmere
A tall, lean British financier stands in a glass-walled penthouse office high over the City of London, leaning into a 28mm lens mid-verse, one gloved forefinger jabbing straight at the camera like a lecture, the other hand flat on a walnut desk. His whole head is covered by a fine-gauge charcoal cashmere balaclava, a smooth unbroken knit where the face would be, no eye holes, no mouth hole, no openings of any kind. A thick white ring of silk embroidery circles the face; high inside it, on the right-hand side of the image at two o'clock as seen by the camera, sits a small crisp red satin triangle. He wears a navy chalk-stripe three-piece, pale blue cutaway shirt, knitted silk tie, thin pale-tan leather gloves, a gold signet ring over the left glove, balaclava tucked under the collar, no skin visible. Flat grey winter daylight from the glass, smeared fingerprints on the pane, towers soft behind, light film grain.
WHY: Old money at the top of the tower, so sure of himself that he lectures the lens like it owes him interest.

=== DESIGN p2 ===
NAME: The Velvet Show-Off
A heavy-set British man sprawls wide in a leather banquette at a fine-dining restaurant table, knees apart, mid-rap toward the camera at eye level, one gloved hand swirling a big glass of red wine, the other held out palm-up in a brash "me?" gesture. His head is entirely covered by a chunky ribbed burgundy merino balaclava, unbroken knit over the whole face, no eye holes, no mouth hole, no openings. A bold white ring is stitched round the face; high inside it, on the right-hand side of the picture at about two o'clock from the camera, is a small bright red triangle. He wears a bottle-green velvet dinner jacket, black silk shirt buttoned to the throat with the balaclava tucked inside, a heavy gold chain over it, black leather gloves under thick gold rings, no skin anywhere. White tablecloth, a half-eaten plate, warm candlelight from below, a diner behind cut off by the frame edge, slightly off-level on-camera flash, grain.
WHY: Flash new money who wants the whole room to see what he paid, and treats the waiter's world as his stage.

=== DESIGN p3 ===
NAME: The Idle Helmsman
A slim, loose-limbed British man stands at the rail on the teak deck of a large white yacht at sea, rapping straight into the lens from a low angle, both arms flung wide in an easy, untouchable shrug, chin tipped up. His whole head is wrapped in a powder-blue slubbed linen-cashmere balaclava with a ribbed rim, the knit unbroken over the face, no eye holes, no mouth hole, no openings of any kind, with a battered straw panama on top. A white ring is stitched round the face; high within it, on the right side of the image at two o'clock as the camera sees it, sits a small red triangle. He wears a cream cable-knit cricket jumper knotted over a crumpled white linen shirt, collar up and a navy silk scarf hiding the neck, frayed cuffs over tan suede driving gloves, no skin showing. Hard late-afternoon sun from the left, glare off the water, salt-dulled brass fittings, horizon slightly tilted, grain.
WHY: Inherited leisure that has never worked a day, so relaxed about owning the sea that the rap is a yawn.
