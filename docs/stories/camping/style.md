# Camping — style notes (thin)

The **global** BadCode house style lives in the `badcode-art-direction` skill
(35mm documentary look, muted cool-neutral palette, observational framing,
environment-as-argument, restraint vs. reveal). **Read that first.** This file is
only the Camping-specific specializations it can't know — read it *alongside*
the skill, never as an override.

## Recurring locations (keep consistent across scenes)

- **The City, 2008** (Scene 1) — the old NatWest Tower skyline, then a 2008
  trading floor: CRT-adjacent screens, shirtsleeves, fluorescent glare.
- **The City, 2026** (Scene 4) — the **same skyline framing** now led by the
  Shard: cold glass, flat fluorescent + grey daylight, self-satisfied, no
  warmth. The two city shots are a matched pair — the skyline is the timestamp.
- **The Waitrose car park** (Scenes 6, 8; ruined in 10–12) — wet tarmac, painted bays, a
  glazed barrel-roofed trolley shelter, the tent pitched **in a bay**, and the **Waitrose
  fascia in its green livery, readable**. Plate:
  [`camera/reference/waitrose-car-park-plate.png`](./camera/reference/waitrose-car-park-plate.png).
  **✅ Ruled 2026-08-26: the real name is on the sign** — the scene-10 `W-AI-trose` gag needs it
  established here, and a blurred fascia would kill the payoff. The same place in its before
  (ordinary, overcast) and after (frost, tents, wreckage, a burning fire).
  Class-coded and recognisable. *(After-state Easter eggs: the W-AI-trose sign
  with only the A and I lit; the clamped dead wank tank.)*
- **The consulting room** (Scene 7) — tasteful, warm-neutral, money-quiet; the
  comedy plays against its calm.
- **The Welsh retreat clearing** (Scene 9) — executive dome tents by a river,
  fog, a small ceremony fire; damp, late-70s film-negative texture on
  exteriors.

## Where warmth is allowed (and only here)

Warmth is an argument in this comic, never decoration:

1. The **wank tank's gold headlights** through the night city (Scene 5) —
   opulence + menace.
2. The **ceremony fire** (Scene 9) — contained, slightly sickly.
3. The **fire in the ruins** (Scenes 10–12) — the one *earned* human warmth,
   the heat the whole story withheld, shared between the two men at the end —
   and the engine of the closing image: the newspaper burns, the camera pans
   up, **the rising embers become the bad code.**

Everywhere else: muted, cool, unforgiving.

## Motifs to hold

- **The matched city pair:** Scene 1 (NatWest + "2008") and Scene 4 (Shard +
  "2026") share framing.
- **The tent** is one object in every appearance (see `characters/tent.md`).
- **The POV rhyme:** Scene 6's Bob-tent-out shot and Scene 10's Tarquin wake-up
  must share the exact door-flap framing and low horizon.
- **The blink** (Scene 9 → 10) is the jump device — eyes close on the trip,
  open in the tent; scroll-driven in the comic, a held cut in the video.
- **Recognisability across time:** young Tarquin (Scene 1), Shard Tarquin
  (Scene 4), ruined Tarquin (Scenes 10–12) are the same bone structure; same
  for young Bob (Scene 2) and car-park Bob.
- **Faces do the emotional work in stillness** — the Scene 5 smile-fade is a
  held shot, not an animation.
