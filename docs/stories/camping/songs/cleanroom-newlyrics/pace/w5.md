=== TITLE ===
Camping (Can't Live Like This)
=== STYLE ===
Dark jungle drum and bass, 174 BPM, minor key, heavy rolling sub bass, chopped breakbeats, instrumental kept exactly as it is. Two male UK MCs rapping, never singing. MC 1: gravelly, hoarse, rough, weathered working-class Liverpool street voice, angry and funny. MC 2: cold, smug, clipped, sneering posh London City voice, a completely different man. Flow: steady MC chat locked to the breakbeat, one syllable on every beat, about three words a second, continuous from the first bar of each verse to the last, no gaps between lines, the end of each line runs straight into the next with no breath, only tiny catch-breaths. Energetic, driving, urgent, tight on the grid, rhythmic spoken rap, not double-time, not half-time. Hooks are a flat, rough group chant over the instrumental drops. No melody, no harmonies, no sung lines.
=== EXCLUDE ===
singing, melody, harmonies, sung hook, crooning, half-time vocal, double-time, chopper, laid-back, relaxed, slow flow, pauses, spoken word poetry, whisper, breathy, auto-tune, female vocal, ad-libs, new instruments
