=== WHY ===
Every verse is re-lined into run-on lines of about two bars with no end punctuation, commas or ellipses, breaking mid-phrase so Suno has no pause signal and has to carry each thought straight into the next line at one word a beat. The style box stays short and only names the beat, the two gravelly MCs and the continuous no-gap flow, while per-section headers pin who raps and the hooks sit over the drops so the verses keep their places.
