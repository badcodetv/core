- Shot ideas that follow from this [inference]:

  - Reflections and glass: shopfronts, car windows, puddles, to split one frame into two worlds.
  - Tower or skyline in the back of every wide, so place is never in doubt.

## 2. Visual satire and class contrast
- Mike Leigh's High Hopes sets three classes side by side; the posh couple bought an ex-council house next door; set dressing of gaudy swans, a glass chess set, a gold-plated fig tree, a brass banana that "only reflects her image back at her" signals an empty material life [reported] https://reverseshot.org/symposiums/entry/2889/high_hopes
- The Riot Club (from the play Posh): the rich gorge on food and wine, deride the staff, and trash the pub; critics found the characters "types" and the critique blunt [reported] https://www.straight.com/movies/418386/lone-scherfigs-riot-club-probes-uk-class-divide ; https://spectrumculture.com/2015/03/30/the-riot-club/
- Common People video: comedy of class tourism, the posh character "roughing it" [reported] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_People
- Takeaways [inference]:
  - The posh man is shown by how he treats space and other people (takes two parking bays, snaps fingers, hands his coat over without looking), not by sneering at camera.
  - His props should be slightly too much: the oversized glass, the decanter, the car key fob held like a remote, the cufflinks. Satire through excess, not caricature costume.
  - Bob is shown by competence and wit under pressure: he knows the city's back routes, fixes his tent in the wind, makes a cup of tea on a tiny stove. The joke is on the system, not on him (fits a nurturing satire).
  - Mirror shots for the peace ending: both doing the same action in their own world (both pouring a drink, both lacing shoes, both checking the weather) then the same action together.
  - Avoid the Riot Club trap: pure grotesque makes the rich man a type; give Tarquin one human tell (a tired rub of the eyes in the car) so the peace is earned.

## 3. Prompting Nano Banana 2 for action stills
- Google's AI Studio docs: photorealistic template "A photorealistic [type of shot] of a [subject] in a [setting]... Shot from a [camera angle] with a [lens type]"; "Describe a scene in rich detail. The more specific you are, the more control you have" [vendor] https://aistudio.google.com/docs/image-generation
- Google tip as reported: "think like a photographer", mention camera angles, lens types, lighting and detail [reported, secondhand] https://www.slashgear.com/1974425/how-to-use-google-gemini-ai-to-create-realistic-photos-guide
- Nano Banana 2 = Gemini 3.1 Flash Image, released 26 Feb 2026 [reported]; Google's own NB2 post covers Image Grounding (model looks up what a real place/subject looks like; locations "accurate to reality"; cannot search for people) and says keep Thinking off unless results are nonsensical [vendor] https://dev.to/googleai/getting-the-most-out-of-nano-banana-2-502k
- Photography terms ("85mm f/1.8", "golden hour") said to raise photorealism [reported] https://www.veed.io/learn/nano-banana-prompting-guide
- Quality buzzwords ("4K, masterpiece, ultra-detailed") said not to help and can harm [reported, attributed to Google, unverified] https://plusai.com/blog/best-nano-banana-pro-prompts
- Restrained blur: blur on background crowd, subject sharp; "slight motion blur, gritty but believable urban" [reported] https://miraflow.ai/blog/nano-banana-2-realistic-photo-prompts-copy-paste-2026 ; blur and parallax work "used with care" [reported] https://eonsr.com/en/nano-banana-motion-and-parallax-prompts-for-energy
- Describing the action precisely ("typing rapidly with motion blur on fingers") claimed for Pro, not NB2 [reported] https://renderfire.com/blog/nano-banana-pro-prompting-guide
- What to do, synthesis [inference]: name a real, specific place and time of day; one verb in progress ("mid-stride", "halfway through pulling", "cork just leaving the bottle"); a real lens and angle; one imperfection that is physical, not filmic (rain on the lens, a scuffed kerb, a smudged window, uneven street light); everyday background people busy with their own lives; positive phrasing rather than lists of bans.
- What to avoid [inference/reported]: generic "cinematic, epic, ultra-detailed"; centred symmetrical hero poses; perfect skin and clean streets; dramatic teal-orange grade by default; more than one action per frame; text on signs (warps when animated, see §3b).

### 3b. Writing a still an image-to-video model can animate
- Match output aspect ratio; meet output resolution; keep the image sharp (no motion blur, compression or upscale artefacts) [reported] https://aifilmcontests.com/guide/first-frame-for-image-to-video
- Pick a pose that can start moving (weight on one foot, hand mid-gesture, head about to turn); "a peak moment has nowhere to go" [reported] (same)
- Hands right or out of frame; the model animates whatever hands are there; Runway: artefacts "may be intensified" [reported/vendor-quoted] (same)
- Leave room on the side the subject moves toward; go wider for a push-in; no text, logos or lens flares (add in edit) [reported] (same)
- With an input image, the motion prompt should describe motion and camera, not re-describe appearance (re-description causes drift); don't stack camera moves [reported] https://www.veo3gen.app/blog/veo-31-image-to-video-this-week-the-first-frame-lock-workflow-for-consistent-cha
- Google's Veo prompt guide covers action/camera language generally [vendor] https://docs.cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/docs/video/video-gen-prompt-guide
- Tension with §3 [inference]: background-only blur in the still may be fine (reads as speed, model continues it), but blur on the subject or hands will be smeared further. Safer: sharp subject, a moving element implied by pose and a direction of light.
