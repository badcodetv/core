## In one paragraph

A **two-hander between two men who must never sound like the same man**: Bob, gruff, weathered and
working-class, and Tarquin, clipped, posh and cold. The released track was dark neurofunk drum & bass at
174 with a Scouse street-poet and a sneering London voice. The re-cut has wandered through drum & bass,
house, trip hop, talking blues and grime, and on 2026-09-24 Jack settled on **slow half-time reggae
hip-hop and reggae dubstep with two ragga dancehall MCs toasting**: loud, unhurried, every word clear,
the hook said flat by a room of men. The words are fixed (Jack's video is cut to them); the cues and the
sound change. The film's narrator is a woman, Nell: low, controlled, sarcastic, north London.

## Rules for this story

- **Two voices that never blend.** Cast by contrast — pitch, texture, tradition, delivery, age/class,
  room — and let the genre tag own the vocalist pool. **A saved Voice binds the whole track**, which is
  what merged Bob and Tarquin on the released take; use one only if each man is generated separately and
  joined in the edit (2026-08-21, Thread 4 of `docs/suno-gpt/suno-voices.md`).
- 🔴 **Never double-time rap** (Jack, 2026-09-24). Urgent is fine; double-time is not.
- **The men never sing.** Every vocal sound is a word from the lyrics: no oohs, no wordless hooks, no
  ad-libs. The hook is spoken flat by a room of men.
- **The words are fixed.** `camping.md`'s `lyrics` block is the guard every runner checks before spending
  a credit; cues may change, words may not. Run the verification after any lyric edit (three words once
  drifted unheard for nine rounds).
- **Each round carries its whole direction in its own boxes**: My Taste not used, Personalize off
  (Kai, 2026-09-10).
- **Accents are copied, not prompted** (narration): pick a voice that already has the accent; prose that
  asks for one comes back a cartoon (Jack, 2026-09-09).

## Never

- One voice for both men.
- Double-time, chopper or speed rap.
- Sung hooks, crooning, oohs, wordless vocals.
- American accents.
- Editing the released lyrics.
- Narration over the crash, the smile fading, or past the last word on the `12d` tilt.
