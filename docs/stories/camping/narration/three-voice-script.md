# The three-voice script

> **What this is:** Jack asked 2026-09-09 for the narration rebuilt as a back-and-forth between
> Bob and Tarquin. This is that script. The job list it answers is
> [`../narration-brief.md`](../narration-brief.md); the voices are in
> [`ai-studio-cast.md`](./ai-studio-cast.md) and [`voice.md`](./voice.md).

## The five devices, so a reader can check them

| | Device | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 🔑 **Bob speaks in past tense. Tarquin speaks in present.** | Bob already knows how it went; Tarquin does not know anything yet. His arrogance happens live, Bob's grief is already finished — **and past tense says *it is over* without ever saying *she died***, which is how the crash stays withheld |
| 2 | 🔑 **The word count reverses.** | Tarquin has most of the words up to scene 8, because power narrates. After the jump he has almost none and Bob has them all. **The audience hears the reversal before it understands it** |
| 3 | 🔑 **Scene 8 is the only place they collide.** | Four lines, same three seconds, opposite sides, neither man hearing the other. It is the whole film, and it is the beat that justifies the device |
| 4 | 🔑 **The machine is funny; the humans are not.** | Every joke stays with the narrator. This permanently closes the *snark at the dying* trap — **Bob cannot be the joke if Bob's voice is never comic** — and the sarcasm keeps a home |
| 5 | 🔑 **Bob never says her name, and never says "I" in scene 2.** | He says *we* until the crash and *I* after it. Nobody mentions her again. In scene 12 he nearly does and stops |

## What the narrator is for now that it is not doing the whole film

**Three jobs, and nothing else.** It opens, it says the things neither man can know, and it closes.

- **The frame** — one opening beat, so the film is still received wisdom from a future that went wrong.
- 🔴 **Scene 10's joke survives intact.** *The narrator does the freak-out and still thinks it is the
  drugs* is the one moment the audience is ahead of the narrator, and it only works from outside
  the two men. It is kept word-for-word in function.
- 🔴 **Scene 11's reach-back.** *"It does not say who owns the machines. Same man who owned the
  flats."* **Neither man knows this** — Bob cannot, and Tarquin will not — which is exactly why the
  narrator's return at that moment *is* the reveal. Its silence through the middle of the film is
  structural, not economy.

## The script

> **Markup:** `{FLAT}` is the narrator's baseline — certain, dry, administrative.
> `{WARM}` is the one crack and it is spent once, in scene 12.
> **BOB** and **TARQUIN** carry no markup: they are people, and they are read straight.

### Scene 1 · 2008, the trading floor

> #### 🔴 The opening was rewritten 2026-09-09 — and the diagnosis generalises
>
> **Jack: *"it is weird and vague."*** He was right. The cut line was
> *"I have run this century forward more times than you have had thoughts. This time I am going to
> let the two of them tell it. They are wrong about almost everything. That is the useful part."*
>
> **Three faults, and the third is the one worth remembering:**
>
> 1. **It explained the device.** *"I am going to let the two of them tell it"* is a note to the
>    writer, not a line — and the device explains itself the instant Tarquin speaks in first
>    person. **Never announce a structure; just start using it.**
> 2. **Nothing in it was concrete.** No year, no place, no person, no object. *"More times than you
>    have had thoughts"* is a brag with no image in it, and it is faintly rude to the audience —
>    which [the reader gates](../../../marketing/the-reader.md) do not allow, however lightly.
> 3. 🔑 **The cosmic sentence was in the wrong place.** The brief prescribes *one long cosmic
>    sentence among the short ones* — and I had spent one in the opening **and** another in scene 6
>    (*"eleven thousand times… that detail has never once stopped being the one"*). **A cosmic brag
>    only lands once the audience cares about something small.** Opening on scale is a boast;
>    arriving at scale later, off a man being judged for his lager, is the joke. **Scene 6 keeps
>    it; the opening does not get one.**
>
> #### ✅ Round 2 is Jack's, 2026-09-09 — his voice, one canon fix
>
> Jack's draft: *"2008 trading floors, a cesspit of madness. Tarquin here, shorting the stocks he
> knows will plummet; a sinking ship mentality may not win over the hearts of the country, but hey
> ho. Nice tie, mate."*
>
> **Better than the writer's version on voice, and three things are kept verbatim:**
> **"but hey ho"** — dry British understatement, exactly the `{FLAT}` register;
> **"Nice tie, mate"** — the *mate* is the upgrade, because it turns the joke from *about* Tarquin
> into a line addressed *at* him, which is funnier and is the narrator's default address;
> and **"Tarquin here"** — introduces him with no ceremony at all.
>
> #### 🔴 ROUND 3 — Jack reaffirmed shorting, and he is right. Canon changes, not the line.
>
> *"Profiting off of the sinking ship. Economic collapse and this prick stands without a scratch."*
> **Jack held the line after the note below and the note was wrong.** Three reasons his version
> beats canon's, and the first is the one that matters:
>
> 1. 🔑 **It is the grievance the target reader actually has.**
>    [`the-reader.md`](../../../marketing/the-reader.md) aims this film at a working-class UK reader
>    who was failed by the economy. **What that reader knows in their bones is not *one trader
>    caused it* — it is *the crash happened, we paid, and the City was fine*.** "He shorted it and
>    walked away clean" is that grievance exactly. "He was the reason 2008 hit" is a claim nobody
>    believes of one man, and disbelief costs more than accuracy buys.
> 2. **It plants scene 10.** *Stands without a scratch* in 2008 is the setup that
>    [the wake-up in the ruins](../shot-list.md) pays off. Consequence arriving eighteen years
>    late is a better shape than consequence never arriving.
> 3. **It makes him the same man twice.** Right about money, wrong about people — in 2008 and again
>    in [scene 4](../narration-brief.md), buying flats and leaving them empty because *asset
>    inflation beats rent*. **One flaw, two decades, no contradiction.** The old version made him a
>    fool with leverage in 2008 and a shrewd operator in 2026, which is two different men.
>
> ⚠️ **This is a canon amendment and it is recorded** in
> [`characters/tarquin.md`](../characters/tarquin.md) — one line in `story.md` still says he was
> *"instrumental in the financial crisis"* and needs Jack's eye.
>
> #### Line edits kept on top of Jack's draft
>
> | | |
> | --- | --- |
> | ✅ **"a cesspit of madness"** | **Restored.** It was cut for failing McKee's VO test and Jack put it back twice — that is a register call and it costs three words. It sets the room before anyone speaks |
> | 🔑 **"shorting the ship he knows is sinking"** | **Merged from two clauses into one.** *"Shorting the stocks he knows will plummet"* and *"profiting off the sinking ship"* are the same idea said twice — [the repetition Pro specifically punishes](../../google-flow/nano-banana-2.md#-descriptive-not-repetitive--and-what-it-costs-us) and the ear punishes too. Shorting **the ship** carries the mechanism and the image in one clause |
> | 🔑 **"does not get his shoes wet"** | Offered in place of *"stands without a scratch"* — it extends the ship metaphor instead of dropping it, and **expensive shoes are class-coded**, which *a scratch* is not. ⬜ Jack's original is fine; this is a proposal |
> | ✅ **"this prick"**, **"but hey ho"**, **"Nice tie, mate"** | Untouched. The three best things in the line |
>
> #### ⬜ One structural note, and it may be a feature
>
> **Nothing in this opening tells the audience the narrator knows the future.** The foreknowledge
> has moved to *Tarquin* — he is the one who knows the ship is sinking.
>
> 🔑 **That is arguably better.** [`narrator.md`](../../../story-craft/narrator.md) says what is
> withheld is the narrator's **identity**, never its wound — so a scene 1 where it reads as just a
> voice with opinions **makes the ember reveal at the end land harder**. It first shows its hand in
> scene 6 (*"eleven thousand times"*) and scene 10 (*"obviously he is still on the drugs"*), which
> is a slower and better disclosure curve than announcing it in the first ten seconds.
> ⬜ **If Jack wants the frame set earlier, one clause does it. Otherwise leave it.**

> 🔴 **The one real problem: shorting is the wrong crime, and it is a canon contradiction.**
> [`characters/tarquin.md`](../characters/tarquin.md) and [`story.md`](../story.md) both have him

> | | Reads as |
> | --- | --- |
> | **Shorting stocks he knows will plummet** | A cynic who **saw it coming and profited**. It makes him *right*, and it makes him a spectator to the crash rather than a cause of it |
> | **Long, leveraged, on borrowed money** | A fool with other people's money who **caused it and did not see it**. Canon's version, and the only one that supports the film's argument about him |
>
> 🔑 **Tarquin's confidence has to be unearned.** A successful short-seller is a clever man; a man
> blown up by his own leverage is this film's man. **The fix is one clause and nothing else
> changes.**
>
> ⬜ **Two smaller cuts, both restorable if Jack disagrees:**
>
> - *"a cesspit of madness"* — energetic, but it is the narrator **editorialising** where the

>   back if the opening feels too dry without it.)*
> - *"a sinking ship mentality may not win over the hearts of the country"* — the idiom is doing
>   the opposite job (a sinking-ship mentality is *abandoning* ship, not causing the sinking), and
>   *"win over the hearts of the country"* has no image in it. **The rhythm is kept, the abstraction
>   is not:** *"Not one for the hearts of the nation, this."*
>
> ✅ **And the foreknowledge is restored in a better form than round 2 had it.**
> *"He will be right about it for another four months"* does in eight words what *"I have checked.
> Repeatedly."* did in five — it establishes that the speaker knows the future, it is funny, it is
> specific, and **it condemns him and dates the crash at the same time.**

## Rows run, and rows not run

**Run:** the reader gates (no *you've been duped*; the beneficiary named in scene 4 and reached
back to in scene 11; the Lee/Coe test on every sarcastic line — all of them are the narrator's and
all are aimed at Tarquin or at the system, never at Bob or a claimant); the narrator rows (flat
baseline, one `{WARM}` spent once and late, one admitted uncertainty — scene 8, *"I have never
found a way to put it that works"*, the grievance said plainly — scene 7, *"I have been on my own a
very long time"*, cosmic scope closing on a human-sized image); McKee's VO test line by line;
the three silent stretches; the last-word timing.

**Not run:** the full ~40-question adversarial pass. **This is a draft to hear, not a finished
script** — the brief's own instruction is to read every line aloud with the picture off, and
nobody has.

## ⬜ Open calls for Jack

1. 🔴 **Scene 2's *"I said there was no rush."*** It gives Bob a private guilt about the timing of
   the drive. It never says anyone died and it never assigns fault — but it is **new**, it is not
   in canon, and it changes what Bob is carrying for the rest of the film. **Cut it and the line
   becomes *"We had four days booked in Wales"* and nothing else.** Deliberate call, not a
   flourish.
2. **Scene 7's pun.** The narrator punning *"car crash of a personality"* over a film whose crash
   killed Bob's wife is the darkest joke in the script. It is aimed at Tarquin, so it passes the
   *never the joke* rule — but it is the line most likely to be judged too far.
3. **Whether the therapist is heard at all.** She is reported by Tarquin here, which is funnier and
   **removes the third-voice dependency entirely** ([`ai-studio-cast.md`](./ai-studio-cast.md) has
   no therapist voice). If Jack wants her heard, that voice has to be built first.
4. **The closing line.** *"Of everything I have ever seen, that is the part I would keep"* replaces
   canon's draft *"Well. We were both wrong, weren't we."* It keeps the shared-humans note without
   going soft, and it costs the narrator something — but the canon draft is still on the table.
5. ⬜ **The single-narrator draft is not deleted.** [`elevenlabs-script.md`](./elevenlabs-script.md)
   stands until Jack rules between them.
