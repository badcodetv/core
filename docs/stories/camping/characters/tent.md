# The Tent

The one object both men inhabit. Bob lives in it in the Waitrose car park
(Scene 6); after the trip, Tarquin **wakes up inside it** (Scene 10). It
carries the POV rhyme — Bob's view out through the door flap in Scene 6 and
Tarquin's wake-up view in Scene 10 are the **same shot from inside this tent**,
so the two men read as one position before anyone says it. Treat it as a
character: **the same tent in every appearance.**

## Visual (house style: hyper-realistic documentary photography)

- A cheap two-person dome tent, originally green and grey, now faded and
  algae-stained, guy-ropes frayed, one pole bowed so a corner sags. A patch of
  duct tape over a tear; the flysheet beaded with rain or frost.
- Pitched in the corner of a Waitrose car park against a low wall and a trolley
  bay — class-coded location, recognisable at a glance.
- **Interior-POV variant (lock the framing):** from inside, the world seen
  through the unzipped door flap — a low, framed letterbox of car park. In
  Scene 6 that view is wet tarmac and the wank tank's wheel; in Scene 10 it is
  the ruined car park — other tents, wreckage, a burning fire. The door-flap
  shape and horizon must match exactly between the two so the rhyme reads.
- Light: flat overcast daylight outside, dim and close inside; the Scene 10
  version colder and bluer, fire-flicker at the edge of frame.
