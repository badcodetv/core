# The world cast: the recurring people of Camping mv v2

Ruled by Jack on 2026-09-29: the people sleeping rough in every city recur through the film, the way Bob and
Tarquin do. They get portrait and full-body plates and a Flow Character each. The images are **Nano Banana 2
only** (Jack), and no video yet.

**Where they appear:** the re-cut, [`../music-video-v2-recut.md`](../music-video-v2-recut.md): the verse-1
cutaways, the chorus match cuts and the final chorus.

## Rules that shaped them
- **Gritty the way Bob is, never fun** (Jack): worn, stained, layered, weather-beaten. Nobody performing.
- **Dignity:** a neutral, level, closed-mouthed look into the lens. Condition is carried by their **clothes,
  kit and skin**, never by a pitiful expression. `[Homeless Hub, Street Roots, 2026-09-29 research]`
- **Each is dressed for their own climate and city**, so the match cuts read as five places, not five costumes.
- 🔴 **The word "homeless" goes in no Flow field**, not a prompt and not a Character Name. Destitution is trigger 3
  (`docs/flow/failure-modes.md` §A2, the `@homeless-Tarquin` lesson). The story calls them what they are; the
  fields don't.
- **Rule zero:** invented people, no real names.
- **Adults only** (the mv2 no-kids rule).

## The method (the one that made `@Tarquin-new`)
1. **Portrait first:** head and shoulders on a plain mid-grey wall in flat frontal light, a record shot, not a
   portrait.
2. **Full body from the portrait:** the portrait is the only reference, and the clothes are described in full.
3. **Upload both as a Flow Character.**
- **Settings:** Nano Banana 2 · 16:9 · x2.
- **Framing:** matches Jack's reference plates for Tarquin (2026-09-29): the camera straight on and centred, a
  grey seamless wall, and the full figure standing with arms at the sides.

```
A plain reference photograph for a casting file: one person, head and shoulders, straight on against a plain mid-grey studio wall.

Camera and framing: An 85mm lens at eye level, about two metres away. The person is centred, framed from just above the top of the head to the middle of the chest, looking straight into the lens.

The person: <DNA block>

Expression: lips closed and relaxed, jaw loose, the eyes steady on the lens, the brow flat. A plain, level, unposed look.

Light: Soft, flat, even daylight-balanced light from the front, with no strong shadows, no rim light, no mood and no atmosphere. This is a plain record shot, not a portrait.

Details: Real, unretouched skin with pores, sun damage, broken veins and uneven stubble where there is stubble. Worn fabric with real dirt, stains and fraying. Fine natural grain.

Constraints: No writing, logos or badges on any clothing.

Compose for a 16:9 frame.

Thanks.
```
