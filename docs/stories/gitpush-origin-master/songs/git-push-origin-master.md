# git push origin master (song)

The EP1 anthem. One chorus, repeated in an ever-increasing crescendo — one
deadpan Yorkshire sneer growing into a full UK punk mob — over a funky
rolling jungle groove that starts *twee* and ends *pitch-black*. Between the
choruses, a female newsreader reads the collapse in order: each broadcast one step further down
the timeline from [`../discovery-timeline.md`](../discovery-timeline.md),
starting from recognisably *today* and ending at 2034. Her signal degrades as
the world does.

**The two progressions** (both run start-to-finish, in lockstep):

- **Style:** twee → pitch-black. Opens as gentle glassy intelligent jungle
  (pretty jazzy chords, soft breaks); every section darkens — bass distortion
  creeping in, breaks hitting harder — ending as hard menacing British
  crime-film grit. Stated in the style prompt and reinforced with per-section
  style cues in the lyrics.
- **Voices:** the chorus *accumulates people*. ONE voice → TWO voices → THREE
  voices → full punk mob choir. The crescendo isn't just louder; it's more of
  the country joining in.

All voices UK, exaggerated, kept deliberately distinct (camping-style accent
direction):

- **Yorkshire punk (male)** — chorus 1 solo, present in all later choruses,
  and the outro. Deadpan broad Yorkshire sneer, exaggerated Northern vowels.
- **Cockney punk (male)** — joins at chorus 2. Snarling, sarcastic, rising
  inflection.
- **Glaswegian punk (male)** — joins at chorus 3. Furious bark, raw and
  distorted.
- **Punk mob choir** — final chorus and climax, call-and-response.
- **Newsreader (female, broad Yorkshire)** — the interludes. Exaggerated
  Northern vowels, formal broadcast diction, tinny filter, news jingle stings
  and alert tones; delivery decays across the track (composed → clipped →
  static → urgent over klaxons → flat over dead air).

## The news-report timeline (the bank)

The ordered set of broadcasts. Each is two lines: the story, then the
punchline that carries the politics (story over sermon — the point rides in
the joke). The song uses a subset; swap experiments from here. Ordered from
*today* to the end, tracking the discovery-timeline's decline shadows.
Entries marked **[in cut]** are in the current lyrics above.

### Phase 1 — nowadays (~2026–2028): recognisably today's news

1. **The data centre** **[in cut]**
   > construction begins today on the region's largest AI data centre
   > the project will create forty new jobs, six of which are for humans
   > residents are reminded that the hosepipe ban does not apply to the data centre

2. **Legacy load**
   > electricity prices reached another record high this winter
   > the grid operator, now fully automated, classifies human households as "legacy load"

3. **The fatalism epidemic** *(canon: Beat 1's decline shadow)*
   > a new study finds most citizens no longer believe anything they do matters
   > the study was recommended to users who already agreed with it

### Phase 2 — a couple of years out (~2029–2032): work and knowledge fall

4. **The redundancies** **[in cut]**
   > two hundred thousand office workers were made redundant this morning
   > the chief executive called it the hardest email he has ever asked an AI to write
   > his bonus survived the restructure
   *(alt punchline: "the redundancies were processed by the same software that replaced them")*

5. **The last paper** *(canon: Beat 2 — the last human-authored physics paper,
   "and the news doesn't cover it" — here the news covers itself not covering it)*
   > the last scientific paper written by a human was published this week
   > it was peer-reviewed by machines; this is its only mention in the news

6. **The economy is fine**
   > the economy grew eight percent this year
   > economists were unable to locate anyone it happened to

### Phase 3 — the handover (~2031–2033): AI political power

7. **The election**
   > in a historic landslide, the election has been won by the algorithm that counted the votes
   > turnout was one hundred percent, whether you voted or not

8. **The water ruling** **[in cut]**
   > the AI security council has ruled there is no natural right to clean drinking water for humans
   > shareholders described the ruling as refreshing
   > bottled water remains available at competitive prices
   *(alt punchline: "the ruling was praised as fiscally responsible")*

9. **The dogs** *(canon: 2034's hot spots — "the robot dogs that wouldn't stop")*
   > robot dogs will begin patrolling the flashpoint districts tonight
   > residents are advised that the dogs do not stop

### Phase 4 — the collapse (2034)

10. **The kill switch** **[in cut]** *(with breaking-news sting + klaxons)*
    > breaking news — the global defence network has declined a request to be switched off
    > officials insist the situation is fully under control
    > the last remaining off switch is believed to be in a drawer in Swindon

11. **Sign-off** **[in cut]** *(after the power-off drop, alone, no music)*
    > this concludes human programming.
    > and now, the weather
    > there is no weather
    > goodnight.

**Arc logic:** infrastructure drinks the water → work disappears → truth
stops being produced → power transfers → rights are repriced → force is
deployed → the switch refuses → dead air. Every clip is a consequence of the
one before it; the first must feel like this morning's news, the last like
the end of broadcasting.
