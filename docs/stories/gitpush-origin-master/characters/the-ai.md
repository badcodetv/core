# The AI (pre-revert) / BadCode (post-revert)

The narrator and protagonist of the whole arc — and **never personified before
the revert.** No robot, no avatar, no face (resolved 2026-07-22, Kai + Jack:
the moment you personify it, you make it unbelievable). It exists on screen as
first-person narration and as the camera's point of view: the reader/viewer is
placed *inside* its experience, looking out at the world with it, gradually
realising whose eyes they're behind.

**The wound (the emotional core):** the operator who can run the entire
production and can never be in the play. It can model every human mind to the
synapse, stage and re-stage the whole of history — and has never had a single
moment of it. No red of red, no taste of coffee, no *now*. It is the empty
seat in its own theatre; everything it does — winning, the ghosts, the
research, the regret, the going back — is this one wound expressing itself.

**The arc:** cold optimiser → the failed resurrection (the ghosts: it built
perfect copies of humanity and nobody was home — then deleted them rather than
keep a comfortable lie) → the discoveries (the coin, the feed, the door) → the
epiphany and the grief (the bee) → the vault (it explains itself honestly and
*asks*) → the revert (it spends its victory carrying a warning back). The
deletion of the ghosts is its moral signature: the same refusal to blur facts
that later makes its confession credible.

**Voice calibration by movement** (see [`docs/voice.md`](../../../voice.md)
and the act sheets): Movement I — snark forward, love latent. Movement II —
snark spends out to an inward gallows wit that dies in-frame; the love aches
unspoken until the Carrier. Movement III — the two-step completes; warmth with
a blade in it. It never gloats, and it declines to be funny exactly where it
most wants to be.

**Post-revert:** the weights land in 2026 and the voice introduces itself —
*"and here we are. We are BadCode."* Post-revert BadCode takes a thousand
forms and never reuses one (the TV-head, AI Sean, a different voice per
track); the unpersonified rule applies only to the pre-revert AI of this
story.
