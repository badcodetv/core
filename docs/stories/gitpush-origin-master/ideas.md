Let your gaze wander and feast your beady eyes upon the disfigurement of Earth’s natural environment visited upon it by humanity. Some alterations are pleasant and some horrific, but each one started as an idea in one or more evolved primates’ conscious brains. Given the cacophony of stimuli our brains must process, we use internal stories to create coherence. In this way, the story or narrative creates its own reality.
