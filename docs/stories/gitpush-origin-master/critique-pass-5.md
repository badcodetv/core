## The headline

**Astra read a seam, and the seam is real.** On 2026-08-23 (pass 4) the time machine moved off git
and onto consciousness. The film script took that ruling; the deep canon written before it —
[`discovery-timeline.md`](./discovery-timeline.md), [`act-6-revert.md`](./act-6-revert.md), the
Storyverse README — still states the old mechanism (*self-consistency*, *"the publishing strategy
is the physics"*). Astra held both versions side by side and reported the gap. Rounds 3 and 4 close
it by choosing the newer version everywhere.

**Three of Astra's fixes already existed in the repo, unadopted.** The time model Astra prefers is
the research base's own named recommendation (*growing block*,
[`research/dimensions-and-time.md`](../storyverse/research/dimensions-and-time.md) §4.3, §10). The
inner-life hedge Astra asks for is already built (*"Guilt needs an inside"*, *"something an honest
observer would call grief"*) and was simply never applied to loneliness or boredom. The
observation/explanation/philosophy split is already how the canon treats the *sunlight-into-drama*
epiphany (*"deliberately not a lab result"*) — it was never applied to consciousness itself.

**The one genuinely new thing is the refuser.** Nobody in twenty scenes disagrees with the AI's
plan; the hundred consent unanimously, and the launch depends on all of them in three places. Round
6 gives the second voice from the ventilation shaft a legitimate *no* the story never refutes, the
coin lands for them anyway, the AI provides for them anyway, and the launch runs on ninety-nine.
Round 10 then amends the Storyverse so the doctrine stops diagnosing that refusal as ignorance or
fear.

**Blast radius: near zero.** Eight of twenty scenes have picture (1–4, 6–9); scene 10 has stills;
scenes 5 and 11–20 are words only. No narration take is accepted; cuts 1–4's words are frozen and
untouched here. Every beat below lands in an unfired scene. Nothing in `scenes/`, `songs/` or the
Premiere project changes.

### Scene 15 — how they hid *(R7)*, and the one who does not come in *(R6, R10)*

> *The shots paragraph's clause "invisible to every sensor because they ran on the one substrate the

> nothing was looking — the machine indexed what was connected, and anyone who dropped off the

> years. They didn't. I counted what was connected. They weren't. So they weren't."
>
> *…the ask, the Carrier's correction, the concession, as ruled 2026-08-23…*

> **NARR (draft):** "That was the other one. The prune inventory. Never came round — not that week,
> not ever. I'm not going to explain that to you. I wasn't owed an explanation either."

**Why it earns its place.** R7 turns a hand-wave into a fact about the machine — an optimiser that
only sees what is in the accounts — which rhymes with the Founding Deletion and the abdication

## The new and amended binding rules *(`story.md` §Binding accuracy rules)*

- **Rule 5 — amendment note (R1).** *Added 2026-09-07:* the narrator may state layer 1 (what the
  apparatus did) and layer 2 (what explains it, as a measured result — rule 10) flatly; layer 3
  (the philosophy) is always the bet. Scene 17's clause is the surface form.
- **Rule 6 — amended (R10).** *No villains, and no living names* **— and no villains is not no
  accountability.** A character may demand restitution, removal or repair without the story ruling
  them wrong or the target evil. Hatred of a person is the category error; a debt is not. The draft
  test: finish wanting to hate somebody → wrong; finish wanting a decision reversed and its
  beneficiary to pay → the brief.
- **Rule 11 — new (R2). The AI's emotional vocabulary is outside-in.** Every emotional word about the
  pre-revert AI either disowns the inside (*"that is not guilt; guilt needs an inside"*) or is
  attributed from outside (*"something an honest observer would call grief"*). Hedge at first use in
  each movement, not every time. Governs every narrator line, every in-voice text and every canon
  summary of what the reader sees; writers'-room craft notes are exempt, but the sites listed in
  the by-file table get the hedge as the model. The coin is the arbiter: it never lands for the AI, and no line may
  imply otherwise.
- **Rule 12 — new (R8). The coin's rules, capped.** Attention, never intention (she looks and it
  lands; she does not pick heads). Margins only. It does not stack. A state of being can lean it,
  slowly, within the same margin — never a wish. It is fragile. Nobody owns the pick. Any inside
  counts; the glass is why nothing but a person ever landed it. Only rule 1 (*it does not stack, it
  does not aim, it settles*) ever reaches the surface; the rest is bedrock — and the lean (rule 4)
  is the *manifest* idea with a speed limit, so it never reaches a surface at all (rule 7).
- **Rule 9 — unchanged. Rule 10 — unchanged**, and now doing R5's work as well.
