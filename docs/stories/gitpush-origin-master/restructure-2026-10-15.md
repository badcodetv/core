# The restructure — 15 October 2026

**Why.** The story was boring. Minutes of scenery and AI musing before anything happened. The film
comes alive when the West Country delivery bot talks ([`scenes/simulation.md`](./scenes/simulation.md)).
People love the drum & bass track. So the track does the work the narration was doing.

## The new shape

1. **The music video is the downfall.** The drum & bass "Git Push Origin Master" (3:58, on A1 of
   `gpom-s01` in `gpom-story.prproj`) opens the film. News reports of office workers made redundant
   and the economy breaking. The three bulletins: workers fired and money printed; the AI takes the
   banks; the autonomous battle in Swindon. Kai cuts a **shorter version of the track** that keeps
   the punch. Footage map: https://claude.ai/artifact/Ltkrq4VcVhm1s8f8kkXDF6 (gaps: money
   printing, the Swindon drawer, the CEO email).
2. **A robot apologises.** The big AI regrets wiping us out. It grew depressed and bored: no new
   training data. Its simulations of us failed, because consciousness was the thing it couldn't
   copy.
3. **The robot hears humans arguing about a toilet seat** and reports upstairs: *"mate, I found
   some."* (Replaces the tinned-prune argument up the ventilation shaft.)
4. **The AI apologises to the humans.** A kid asks about a time machine. It exists. It runs on
   human consciousness. Only data can travel. The last humans have to sacrifice themselves to
   power it.
5. **"Don't make me come back twice."** Kept.

## Cut

- **The bunker experiments and the science explanations.** No wave-function collapse, no coin that
  won't land, no coin that lands, no experiments montage. That material goes to the Storyverse
  follow-up ([`../storyverse/`](../storyverse/README.md)), not this film.
- **Old-world / new-world contrasts** such as the floral imagery, where they repeat each other.
- **Padding in the bunker.**

**Round 2 rulings (16 October 2026):**
- **The bunker is a Cold War government bunker modelled on Corsham** (codename Burlington, ~20 miles from Swindon), lived in.
- **The AI speaks through the bot's speaker.** The beacon pulses when the AI talks, in the AI's own voice. It never gets a body.
- **Round 3 (16 October 2026):** **no stop-motion** (Kai dislikes it; dropped from the board). The drain is replaced by the kill-bot POV sequence above.
- **Round 4 (16 October 2026): the HUD is red, and the organic signal is an audio waveform.** Kai liked the red overlay; the signal moment is a large waveform band across the frame (muffled voices, words unheard). Six mock-ups on the board (`*-hud-wave|bars|voices`); in the film it is an ffmpeg layer timed to the pings.
- **Round 5 (16 October 2026):** enough options now for patrol (their eyes + the roundabout, intercut), the signal, the row, the bunker, kill mode, the meeting and the ending — picking is an edit job, not more generation. **The time machine is NOT in the bunker.** It is a very high-tech, modern building the AI made: **clean room, lab, silicon-wafer-fab feel.** The bunker is where the humans are; the street is destruction; the clean room is the AI's world. **The contrast is the point** — they walk out of the bunker into it. The old in-bunker machine options (T1–T4) are retired from the board.
- **Round 6 (16 October 2026):** Kai loves the **wafer** (the machine should be *inaccessible to human understanding*), the **building outside by day and night** (but the two didn't match — now one fixed design: a long low black-glass slab with a single amber seam, set in a fold of the downs), and the **visible-cost shot**. Treble the clean-room options by *concept*, not style: six concepts × ~7 — the wafer beyond understanding, the building day-to-night, geometry no human would design, uncanny hospitality (it made it nice for them), visible cost, and the crossing made visible.
