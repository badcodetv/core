# GPOM chorus: Skid Row Britain (working title)

The half-chorus that goes back between the intro and the bank downfall: 32 beats at 173.975 BPM, 11.04 s, 265 frames, song 69.583–80.625 s. In "01 GPOM start r2" it is an INSERT at 62.583 s (the intro's zoom into black), so the film gets 11.04 s longer.

Kai, 10 Nov: *"the jump from the black screen into the bank downfall is an anti-climax… the ear expects a drop because it's a drum and bass tune… economic downfall… everybody's homeless… dystopian skid row… the desperate poverty of humanity… not like the death via violence of humanity, which the second chorus does."*

**The one sentence:** the money went, then the homes went; by the time the banks emptied, the whole country was sleeping outside the towers that took it.

**It is the twin of Outbreak Night** (`../chorus-outbreak/`). That chorus is death by violence at night; this one is death by poverty. Same grammar, so the two choruses rhyme:
- the biggest frame on the kick (Kai put the airliner on the kick);
- one shot every 2–4 beats, about 9 shots;
- the scale jumps between city-wide and one hand;
- a held, intimate insert to finish (Outbreak's cup), or the towers as the button into the banks.

## Why the last try failed, and the fix
The "people" chorus (https://claude.ai/artifact/8gX5wpQjdyW3krCtRV14jN, 8 Nov) showed a few people having a hard week: Kai, *"everyone's having a bit of a difficult time, not humanity ended."*
- **The fix is scale.** It is not a hard time for some people; it is the whole country. Motorways that have become towns of tents, a queue a mile long, every park a shanty town, a stadium full of camp beds, every doorway on the high street taken.
- **Seen the way Outbreak Night saw its cities:** wide, real British places, from the air and from a long lens.

## Look
- **Winter, cold and grey:** dusk, flat overcast day or night, offered side by side so Kai picks the light. The 35mm feature-film stock Outbreak Night used, so the two choruses match.
- **Fire is the one warm colour:** braziers, stoves and oil drums (Outbreak's fire rule, turned domestic).
- **Kept from look.md:**
  - British places and pounds.
  - No writing in frame.
  - The AI never has a body.
  - A visible cost in every monumental frame (here the cost IS the frame).
  - No children.
- **Dignity (the-reader rules 1, 2 and 9):**
  - Poverty is done TO people. Nobody is the joke, and nobody is shown as degraded.
  - Faces are tired and particular, never grotesque.
  - The beneficiary (the lit towers, the automated warehouse) is in shot wherever it can be.
