# Audio Profile

BadCode is an adult British male narrator, pitched low within his natural speaking range, with slight gravel and clear formal broadcast diction. He has dry authority and active thought under restraint. He speaks to one living person at roughly 125 words per minute. The warning carries the weight of a debt he has paid. The correction and admission are direct and free of self-pity. One small current of warmth enters when he explains why living humans matter; he then returns to lucid civic resolve. Natural conversational grouping, firm downward endings, and no promotional cadence.

## Scene:

A dry voiceover booth after midnight. Close microphone, no audience, no score. He is recording a private postmortem for one person who can still change what happens next.

## Sample Context:

A restrained British narrator closes a political science-fiction story by surrendering authority and inviting shared work.
