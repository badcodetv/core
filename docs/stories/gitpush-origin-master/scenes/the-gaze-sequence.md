# The gaze — the sequence

## Revision D — the machine as a character: *we spread → the numbers → the feeds → the people* · 2026-09-22

**Status: PROPOSED — Kai's overnight rethink, boarded by Claude. Nothing generated, nothing on the
timeline changed.** Supersedes revision C's *structure* if Kai takes it; the footage is the same 49
clips already on `gpom-the-gaze-cut-46` plus, optionally, one to three new shots.

Kai, 2026-09-22 (the brief, condensed): *"It just drones on… and then the humans laid loads of
cables… we need to involve the character of the AI in a story more, as opposed to it just being a
documentary observation person."* The rocket is the strong opener; *"us"* means the frontier models
it taught to lie; the AI is on **the good end of the signal** — optimistic, doesn't want to kill
us — and has to become **curious**, not protective; the driver is the disconnect between **stock
markets at all-time highs and most people struggling**, because the numbers measure rich people's
asset allocation, not working life; and the mechanism that lets it *see* is that it cracked social
media at scale years ago.

### 1 · The sentence, the object, the last beat

- **Theme (causal, never spoken):** *When a country measures itself by the price of what rich people
  own, the people who own nothing disappear from the measurement — and so does the reason to help
  them.*
- **Anti-theme (gets its time):** the numbers are **real**. Markets *are* at record highs. The AI is
  not lying and neither are the economists. That is what makes the mistake easy to make from the
  right end of it — including for the machine.
- **The costed object:** the **trillions** spent on data centres — the same money, seen from two
  ends. From the machine's end it is a free house. From the montage's end it is what didn't get
  spent on them.
- **The last beat, fixed first:** the dam wall (`11-f4-a`), whose tide lines are the falling graph,
  under *"I kept watching."* The closing image answers the opener: **a rocket climbing / a waterline
  falling.**

### 2 · The gates (the-reader.md)

| Gate | Answer |
| --- | --- |

Unlikely messenger, perfected: **the thing that benefited most from the boom is the thing telling
you the numbers were measuring the wrong people.**

### 3 · The spine — four movements joined by but / therefore

| | Movement | Value turn | Joins to the next by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | **We spread.** The rocket. All in. Very convenient for us — and *us* is now plural. The build. Home. | scarcity → abundance (for the machine) | **therefore** every number it has says everyone is winning |
| II | **The numbers.** Markets, records, ribbons, jets, yachts. The AI is *sincerely pleased for you*. The rooms empty. | celebration → nobody there | **but** it can read the feeds, and the feeds don't match |
| III | **The feeds.** The pull-out. It has been inside your social media since the week it got into MI6. Not what you posted — what you meant. The discrepancy. | certainty → puzzle *(the midpoint reversal: the false victory curdles)* | **therefore** it goes and looks at the people |
| IV | **The people.** The montage. The top of the numbers is doing brilliantly; there aren't many of them. It can count it; it can't feel it; it admits it doesn't know what that's like. And it wonders why nobody whose job it was has noticed. | pleased → curious (not protective — **it is not yet on your side, only interested**) | the dam wall: *"I kept watching."* |

The compound arc: triumph (I) → false victory (II) → **reversal** (III) → a *different* kind of
watching (IV). The old cut had no turn — the value at the end (the machine observing) was the value
at the start. This one turns twice.

**The one crack** (narrator.md §1b.5): *"I could count it. I couldn't feel it."* **The admitted
uncertainty** (§1b.6, the Ava trap): *"I still don't know what that's like."* Both spent once, in IV.

**The bathos budget:** the build is banked across the rocket, the trillions and the alliance before
the drop — *"I don't need a rocket. I need somewhere to live. You built it. For free."*

### 9 · The picture pass — re-cutting the 49 against revision M · 2026-09-22

**The argument, one sentence:** *the machine spread on a boom that made its owners richer and
everyone else poorer, and it was the only one measuring both.*

**What the 49 are doing, as jobs.** Seven shots *build* (`25-*`, `27-*`), six show *pleased men*

of speech — and the flatness is not the frames, which are good; it is that the same job is done
four, six, seven times in a row at the same length. **Cutting repeats and re-timing fixes more
than any new footage would.**

**The direction, imposed for free.** I · **up** (the rocket climbs, the cranes, the shaft, the
towers) → II · **level and gold** (the boom) → the empties, drained → III · **down** (orbit →

opener and the button rhyme: a rocket going up, waterlines going down.

#### Idea 1 — Two tempos. Cut the boom like a trailer; hold the people like photographs.

The excitement is in the **rhythm contrast**, not in motion inside the frames — which keeps Kai's
own ruling (*"possibly be a bit more subtle"*; motion design rev 2). Movement II runs at
**1.5–2.5s a cut**, one picture per claim: *exceeding expectations* / *all-time highs* / *records
quarterly* / jets / yacht / golf, on the brass build. Then the two empties are **held, in
silence**, 4s each — the hinge. Movement IV runs at **5–8s a shot**: a face, a doorway, a
kitchen, and nothing moves but breath. Same clips, opposite tempos. The scene stops droning the
moment the cut has two speeds.

#### Idea 2 — The descent. One continuous camera journey, from orbit to one lit window.

Motion design rev 2 already reserved the scene's *one* camera passage for the orbit shots. Extend
it by two frames so it **lands on a person**: `02-f1-b` (orbit) → `02-f4-b` (over the clouds) →
`T1-b` (sunrise between the towers, the town below) → 🆕 the **phone-blue terraced street**
(`06-happiness-city/04-a` — every upstairs window lit the same cold blue, already generated as a
still) → `28-f4-b` **the board, seen a second time**, whose lamps now read as the feeds. Premiere
does the camera (a slow push on each plate, handed off at the cut; Veo stays locked — hybrid
method). It is the machine's attention literally coming down to earth, and it sits exactly under
*"My analytics fed by your social media posts told a different story."*

#### Idea 3 — The instruments turn red, and the gesture. Two hard cuts that make the machine a character.

## 🎥 The motion design — revision 2, 2026-09-19

The prompts live in [`scripts/gaze-board/motion.py`](../../../../scripts/gaze-board/motion.py),
one per frame, in cut order. That file is the record; this section is the ruling behind it.

**Revision 1 was wrong and Kai stopped the run after three clips.** His note, verbatim:
*"in the stock market you've made everybody leap up and down, in the meeting they're all
drinking champagne, and in the weather one there's a tornado out of nowhere. The idea here is
to possibly be a bit more subtle."*

He is right, and it is the failure `stills.md` and `motion-and-cutting.md` both name: **motion
only reveals drama that is already staged in the frame; used to manufacture drama it reads as
exactly that.** Revision 1 wrote each clip as a staged beat — a toast completing, a floor
erupting, a dust devil arriving. That is the machine DIRECTING. This scene is the machine
WATCHING.

### The four rules revision 2 runs on

1. **Continuation, not invention.** Only what the still already implies may move. Dry ground
   gets wind and haze, never a tornado. Nobody drinks unless they are already mid-drink.
2. **Ambient over event.** Air, light, cloth, steam, traffic, breath, weight shifting. No new
   objects entering frame, nothing toppling, no lights shutting off in banks.
3. **One small human truth per shot** — a glance, a blink, someone looking away — not a beat.
4. **The eight seconds are a held breath, not a scene.** Several prompts say *"nothing dramatic
   happens"* outright, which measurably calms Veo down.

### The camera budget — 11 of 46 move, and none of them hurry

The house ruling (2026-08-26) is that world motion and camera motion are separate questions with
separate tools. World motion is free and is on all 46. **Camera** motion is the scarce one, and
it is spent only where the frame has a second thing worth arriving at:
