# GPOM chorus: Outbreak Night + the Rumbling

The one half-chorus (32 beats at 174 BPM, 11.04 s; "01 GPOM start" 106.92 s) between the bank takeover and the war in Swindon. It replaces "the sky, then the silence" (decisions #44, paused by #45).

**The one sentence:** the night our own machines woke up; the cars we sat in and the cranes that built the city turn on us, and by morning only machines are moving.

Kai, 9 Nov, picking concepts #2 and #1 from the 20-agent research (`../chorus-sky/research/downfall-research.json`): "number two with number one combined… absorb as much cinematography as we can."

## Shape (sequence design, docs/cinematography/motion-and-cutting.md §2)

- **Argument:** the machines we live inside stop serving us, all at once and at every scale.
- **Shot sizes run inside → outward → back inside.** It opens close inside one car, widens beat by beat to the biggest frame in the film (cranes walking over a city), then the button returns to the exact opening frame with the driver's seat empty.
- **Pattern and break:** beats 1-12 are the cars (ground level, our size). Beat 13 breaks scale: the cranes stand up.
- **Withheld:** what the tremor is. The cup of water ripples in beat 1, and the cause is not seen until beat 13.
- **The button** bookends the first frame. The car is now driving itself in a convoy toward a town, and that convoy becomes the army in the war scene.

## Look

- **Night**, continuing chorus 2's night. 35mm photographic.
- **Light:** sodium-orange motorway lamps (they die in LK), cold white LED headlights, fire as the one warm anchor, and one red aviation light on each crane.
- **Departs from look.md on purpose:**
  - The British-places guide: three global one-beat shots, for scale. Kai's ruling #43.
  - "The collapse stays off-screen": explosions are allowed (#43).
- **Kept:**
  - No gore and no harm to a body: people are small, seen from behind, and running away.
  - No children.
  - No writing in frame.
  - The AI is never given a body. The cars and cranes are only its hands.
- **Visible cost** in every monumental frame: the fleeing figures, the abandoned cars, the terraced street.
