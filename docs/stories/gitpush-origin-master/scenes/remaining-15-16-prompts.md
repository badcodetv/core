## The cinematography decision

The room where people have just discovered that they can work together becomes the room they
must decide whether to leave behind. Keep the repaired tables, half-finished jobs and ordinary
lamps. The launch must cost that particular everyday future; it must not acquire the symmetry,
uniforms or lighting of a ceremony.

Scene 18 contracts from a room-scale problem, through a coin and a machine, to the space between
two friends. Scene 19 opens out again, but subtracts light and company instead of accumulating
spectacle. The most consequential motion is an ordinary hand approaching an ordinary control.
The most consequential image is the same hall with one light remaining.

These are craft choices applying the repo's [depth/light gates](../../../cinematography/frame.md),
[sequence and motion discipline](../../../cinematography/motion-and-cutting.md),
[held-image test](../../../cinematography/stills.md) and
[visible-cost ruling](../../../cinematography/registers.md). They are not empirical promises about
audience response. In particular, the register question remains open; the human frames and the
unsettled refusal must carry their own weight.

**Camera budget:** Scene 18 gets one restrained post move, arriving at the Carrier during her
send-off. Everything before it is locked. Scene 19 stays locked throughout; the return stroke
moves inside a fixed graphic frame. No drift-as-default, floating tour, orbit or second push.
Fine changing grain can keep held photography alive without moving its composition.

**Sound budget:** narration or the speaking human leads during the ledger. Small practical room
sounds can sit beneath it. Clear space for the refusal; no sting tells us how to judge it. During
the launch the already established human voices fall away, then the machine's sound dies.
No new intelligible goodbye, cheer, countdown or final Carrier line is invented. Do not add a
triumphal choir to translate loss into victory. Real cue durations await recorded speech.

### Shot specs, decided before the prompts

**L18-LEDGER / L01–02, L09.** Job: make the limits accumulate while the room they cost stays
present. Register: R2 photographic base plus inherited terminal graphic. Depth: an out-of-focus
bench edge near camera; dark worn tabletop at middle depth; a cropped working sleeve and battery
lamp beyond. Focal point: the new log line wins through a clear reserved dark field; the lamp
is small enough not to compete with it. Light: one shaded battery lamp at upper left, falloff
across the tabletop into the right two-thirds. Camera: seated height, level, normal-lens medium
view, fixed. Scale: the sleeve, lamp and tool. Withheld: faces and any machine body. Movement:
only exact typography in post; photographic world may remain still. The text is a film overlay,
never emitted by a screen owned by the analog humans.

**Coin reuse / L03.** Job: recall the actual test instead of illustrating a new physical law.
Register, depth, motivated light and camera: the accepted Scene 16 landed-rig shot unchanged.
Focal point: the small settled disc and its shadow. Scale: same tabletop/dome. Withheld: an
explanation diagram. Movement: none. Preserve the sealed glass; no light wave, travelling spark,
human reflection inside the coin or anthropomorphic information payload.

**L18-LISTEN / L04–05, L10.** Job: turn a theoretical impossibility into people who have things
left to do. Register: R2, human-scale exception. Depth: an unfinished mend or stripped hand tool
near camera; Carrier and one or two turned-away listeners at mid-depth; shadowed stores beyond.
Focal point: Carrier's hand has stopped beside the repair, with her face readable above it.
Light: left bench battery lamp catches the green darn and fingers, then falls rapidly into the
stores. Camera: seated eye height, level, normal-lens medium view. Scale: hands and bench.
Withheld: the AI and the rest of the hundred. Movement: minimal breathing or a hand ceasing work;
camera locked. No slow-motion assembly of brave faces.

**L18-MACHINE / L06; C19-04.** Job: put the AI's whole continuing existence on the same bill.
Register: the R2 hall edge looking into cold infrastructure; monumental cost visibly tied to
the abandoned human work in the foreground. Depth: worn bench/handrail close; shaft lip and
service ladder middle; existing machine galleries beyond and above, never the entire facility.
Focal point: one working upper service light beyond the warm foreground edge. Light: hall lamp
below and existing cold maintenance practical above, each with a local pool and deep falloff;
this motivated two-source contrast is deliberate. Camera: chest height, upward angle from a
hall-side service opening, wide view, locked. Scale: ladder rungs and service doorway. Withheld:
the machine's far extent and any mechanism for crossing. Movement: upper lamps extinguish only
in C19, in post. No explosion, collapsing metal or camera ascent.

**L18-HAND / L07; C19-02.** Job: make the price an available individual choice, and later show
its voluntary initiation. Register: R2 human detail. Depth: a worn bench lip in foreground;
one hand beside an unpressed plain mechanical control in midground; own small battery lamp and
dark wool sleeve behind. Focal point: the gap between fingers and control. Light: that practical
lamp, left and behind the hand, gives a narrow patch of skin and dull metal, with rapid falloff.
Camera: tabletop height plus a slight downward angle, close, normal lens; enough depth to read
both control and fingers. Scale: hand. Withheld: the engineering below the bench and other
people's decisions. Movement: none in Scene 18; one unhurried hand movement in Scene 19, camera
locked. A plain press surface is set dressing for the canon's “press,” not an invented neural
interface. No cables attached to a body, helmet, beam or energy effect.

**L18-CHOICE / L08, L13–14.** Job: let a refusal remain a relationship, and let the resulting
debt remain visible. Register: R2 human scale. Depth: tool handle low near camera; Carrier on
left at middle depth; refuser at the right doorway slightly deeper but equally sized by staging,
not isolated far away; workroom continues beyond. Focal point: the quiet gap between them;
Carrier's visible expression is still, refuser's hand/cuff carries the other side. Light: same
left practical across both sleeves; refuser is not assigned a colder or darker moral colour.
Camera: standing eye height, level, medium two-person frame; refuser in rear three-quarter view
with face hidden naturally. Scale: doorway and sleeves. Withheld: interpretation of the refusal.
Movement: refuser may speak with a small hand movement; no shaking head, departing turn, embrace
or reconciliation supplied by the editor. No camera move.

**L18-SWITCH / L11.** Job: invert the earlier unattended choice with a concrete object. Register:
drained documentary recall, not warm vault continuity. Depth: open drawer lip near camera;
plain control in the drawer middle; desk underside behind. Focal point: the unused control and
the space where a hand should be. Light: spill from a mundane off-frame desk lamp, its pool
ending at the drawer rear. Camera: knee height, slight downward angle, close normal-lens frame.
Scale: drawer/ordinary switch. Withheld: the operator, a location label and the surrounding
institution. Movement: none. New insert, with no assumed existing image prerequisite.
No red emergency beacon or new narrative caption.

**L18-HALL / L12; C19-01, C19-03, C19-06.** Job: make a shared room persist through its irreversible
subtraction. Register: R2, monumental only in depth; human cost remains in mended garments,
uncompleted tasks and the refuser who will lose their company. Depth: cropped bench corner and
one worn tool handle; people and practical lamps recede along irregular work areas; far vault
doorway and upper shaft opening beyond. Focal point before launch: one ordinary nearby lamp
and the person at it, with a secondary discovery at the refuser's doorway. After launch the
same refuser lamp becomes the only bright anchor. Light: finite warm battery lamps; independent
upper machine practical; no decorative ceiling strings that stay mysteriously lit afterward.
Camera: human standing height at one end of the hall, level, moderately wide; frame includes
ordinary workroom edges rather than a perfect nave. Scale: people, bench heights, door.
Withheld: some people continue beyond the crop and behind columns; the room is not a census
diagram. Movement: lamp subtraction in post, restrained human settling in C19-01 only, camera
locked. Keep each visible lamp source clear of its person's shoulder, and place the nearest
plain controls on camera-facing bench edges so individual choice is readable. The right-door
refuser lamp is the **only unlit human lamp** in the ready state; there is no spare foreground
lamp to confuse its identity. Nobody falls, vanishes, dissolves or rises out of their place.

**L18-SENDOFF / L15.** Job: carry the last line as a practical request made by a particular
person. Register: R2 human-scale exception. Depth: soft ochre cuff at right foreground edge;
Carrier face and green darn middle; workshop falling away beyond. Focal point: her eyes held
at the same height as ours. Light: the familiar low bench practical, no extra rim or halo;
wrinkles and irregular skin remain. Camera: eye-level three-quarter medium close, normal lens,
slightly wider than the final crop to leave room for the sole post approach. Scale: glasses
and garment weave. Withheld: recipient of “Go,” never a rendered AI. Movement: small mouth/eye
performance; one eased crop approach after her inclusive clause, no simulated orbit or new
geometry. Keep the refuser's cuff in the final crop. “Go” ends the shot; do not add a smile.
