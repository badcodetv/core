# Chorus: the sky, then the silence (brief)

Kai, 9 Nov (decisions #43, #44): one half-chorus, about 11 s (32 beats at 174 BPM; 01 GPOM start 106.92 s, song 113.917 s), between the banks falling and the war. Swindon comes later (the end of verse 3), so this is not set there.

**The one sentence:** humanity ends on a planet-wide scale, and we never see anyone hurt.

**Shape:**
- **Beats 1-16, the sky (humans):** the world's busiest places, full; the people stop and look up; thousands of lights fill the sky over every city at once; flashes on the horizon. The autonomous army arriving, seen only as light (its first proper reveal stays in the war).
- **The cut, on a music hit:** a white flash, or one beat of silence.
- **Beats 17-32, the silence (no humans):** the same places, framed exactly as before, empty: dust, open car doors, screens playing to nobody, floodlights over empty seats. The last shot holds longest; black; the war.

**Kai's words:** "big, bold, in your face, downfall happening now"; "Godzilla style… as big as that… but not some massive alien foe"; "the bridge between when humans were around and then when they're not". No gore, no violence on people; explosions allowed.

**Look:** GPOM's people register (35mm documentary), night and dusk. Departs from look.md's "British places" guide on purpose: the scale has to be global (Tokyo, New York, Rio) beside London and a British stadium and motorway.

**Making it:** each place's empty frame is an edit of its full frame with the people removed, so the pair matches exactly.
