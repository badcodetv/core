# AUDIO PROFILE
Derek, a working-class man from Liverpool in his fifties, Sandra's husband.

## THE SCENE
A tiled lavatory in a Cold War bunker, then a circle of a hundred survivors being asked to trust the machine that killed everyone. Echo off the tiles.

### DIRECTOR'S NOTES
Style: indignant and exasperated in the row, arms-in-the-air disbelief; in the circle, angry and frightened, the first one to say no.
Pace: sheepish and quick, a placating mumble, the words run together; a long, drawn-out 'I'm' at the start.
Accent: Scouse, as heard in Liverpool. Never American, never Received Pronunciation.

### SAMPLE CONTEXT
The voice you'd cast as the bloke in a British sitcom who is losing an argument and knows it.
