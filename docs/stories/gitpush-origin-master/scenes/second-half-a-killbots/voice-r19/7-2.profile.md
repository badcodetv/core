# AUDIO PROFILE
Sandra, a working-class woman from Bolton, Lancashire in her fifties, married to Derek for thirty years.

## THE SCENE
A tiled lavatory in a Cold War bunker under a hill, five years underground with a hundred people and one toilet. Echo off the tiles.

### DIRECTOR'S NOTES
Style: sharp, sarcastic, every line a jab; not shouting at first, then loud. Funny because she is completely serious.
Pace: snaps straight back, quick and cutting, the words tumbling out fast, no pause after 'ended.'.
Accent: Northern English, as heard in Lancashire, around Bolton. Never American, never Received Pronunciation.

### SAMPLE CONTEXT
The voice you'd cast as the wife in a British sitcom row that the whole street can hear.
