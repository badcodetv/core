# AUDIO PROFILE
Maddie, a small pavement delivery robot with a young woman's voice, late twenties. A real person, never robotic.

## THE SCENE
Outside the steel blast door of a Cold War bunker under a Wiltshire hill, grey daylight, wind. The kill bots that were about to break in have just stood down and are leaving one by one. A small robot speaks quietly up into her boss-hotline antenna. Open air, no echo; her voice close to the microphone.

### DIRECTOR'S NOTES
Emotion for this line: overwhelming relief, shaky, fond; she nearly lost them.
Style: no cheek, no gossip, no jokes: the relief after nearly watching a hundred people she loves be killed; shaky and fond, her voice wobbling, close to tears, a breath of a laugh on the way out.
Pace: fast, clipped, joining phrases with commas; no gaps between phrases.
Accent: West Country, as heard in Bristol. Never American, never Received Pronunciation.

### SAMPLE CONTEXT
The voice you'd cast as the cheerful local in a British comedy who turns out to be the kindest person in it.
