# Choruses with people: brief (6 Nov)

The ideas, the ladder and the limits: [`design/2026-11-05-gpom-meeting/choruses-ideas.md`](../../../../../design/2026-11-05-gpom-meeting/choruses-ideas.md). This scene replaces the robot-army and eye shots in both choruses of `01 GPOM start`.

## Kai's picks (6 Nov): "1A, no eye, black"
- **Chorus 1 = direction A:** the opening's own people a week after the sackings: the trader (MK5-b), the corner-shop woman (BR3-a), a sacked office worker (e03), then who gained (the tower full of servers, or the boardroom TO1T2).
- **No abstract beat:** the robot choir and the eye are gone from both choruses.
- **Chorus 2 ends on black** (not the white flash) into the war's grey morning.

## Slots (174 BPM, 0.345 s a beat)
- **Chorus 1:** 62.58–74.08 (11.5 s). 4 shots, one per chant line.
- **Chorus 2:** 106.92–128.71 (21.8 s). Pass 1 to 117.71 (things stop working, dusk to night); pass 2 (the flight, night).

## The sentence
**The money went first, then the people went: the AI's owners did fine, and by the time the machines fought over Swindon there was nobody left in it.**

## Limits (so the e07 explosion stays the peak)
- No fireball, no hit, no weapon pointed at a person, no injury.
- Fear on faces, never pain.
- Fire only as a glow behind something.
- Flashes at 3 a second or fewer.
- No child in frame (Flow refuses; a dog or an adult takes the back seat).

## Look
- Chorus 1: the opening's 35mm documentary register, rain, cold blue.
- Chorus 2: Swindon, dusk into night, 35mm, sodium and headlights.
- One bright anchor in every dark frame.
