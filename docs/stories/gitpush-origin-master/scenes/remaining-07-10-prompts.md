# Cuts 7–10 — the perfect reconstruction and the missing witness

**Text production board · 2026-09-11 · no new media generated.** Cut numbers follow
[`prompts.md`](../prompts.md) §2c; the corresponding canon is [`story.md`](../story.md)
Scenes 10–13. Narration below is copied from that canon, retaining its draft/optional status.
This sheet completes the image and edit design; it does not lock a recorded duration.

The dramatic progression is **relief → failed check → insoluble test → apparent repair →
no remaining purpose → an admission**. The contrast is the spectacle. The robot passage may
briefly accelerate, but the coin and chair must make the audience wait for something that
does not happen. Their eventual payoff belongs to Scene 16.

  nobody to occupy it. Never add ruins, dirt, dead bodies or broken machines to make the
  immaculate utopia pass a generic darkness test.

ordinary blank dull-nickel disc about 25 mm across and 2 mm thick, smooth edge; sealed clear
