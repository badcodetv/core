## What it argues

> **The machine can build anything except the one thing it needs, so the people it wiped out pay for
> the second chance, knowing exactly who it's for.**

- **The turn.** It opens as comedy in a place the hundred can't understand: suits, tea, the AI's
  creepy hospitality. It closes as grief in that same place, a hundred empty chairs. The joke never
  stops; it turns into the goodbye (*"Seat down?" / "Seat down."*).
- **The midpoint.** Derek asks the small robot whether this is a simulation and whether the AI has
  anything else up its sleeve. The machine does not win the argument with a clever line. It hands it
  back in Maddie's words from scene B's door (*"You don't. Believing it is your bit."*), then admits,
  once, what it does not know (*"No. This is all of it. I don't know if it works."*). That is the trust
  test (4.12): it states its interest, it admits a limit, and the humans decide. Derek sits down
  (D10). Trust is the act, as round 2 ruled: earned by something they can check, never by an argument.
- **The visible cost.** There are three of them, and none is an explosion:
  1. mud on the white (D02): the only dirt in the machine's world is them;
  2. the footprints in the chalk outside (D14): a hundred going in, none coming out;
  3. a hundred empty chairs, with Maddie among them (D16).
- **Who benefits, named, once.** The old lady asks *"What'll you tell them? Back then."* and the AI
  answers, straight, in the care register: *"That the companies cutting the jobs kept the gains. And
  you paid."* That names the chooser and the beneficiary of the downfall in canon's own words (story.md,
  README §Coda), not in pronouns a viewer could fill with anyone (reader rules 5 and 6). *"Back then"*
  fixes where he is going: the past, our present, which scene E's bus then shows. **Kai to rule** on the
  exact words (alt: *"...And who paid."*).
- **Where the AI's voice comes from.** Maddie is in the room all scene. D00 shows her, low among the
  white suits, beacon pulsing, on his first line; she is soft in the background of D07, D08 and D11, and
  Derek and the lady speak *down to her*, never to the ceiling. Her D16 *"Boss?"* lands because we
  watched her relay him.
- **The moral, never stated.** Nobody says sacrifice, hope, humanity, or "don't make our mistake".

## Depends on Kai's vote scene

The vote (between B and C, Kai's to write) is where restructure beat F puts *"I've invented a time
machine. It needs your consciousness to drive it, and it can only send me."* This scene assumes the vote
carries **"it can only send me"**. So D03 does not re-explain: it keeps only *"What it runs on, you
already know."* / *"Us."* / *"You. And your consciousness."*, as recognition. If the vote drops the time
machine, D11's *"Back then"* becomes the only statement of where he is going, and is load-bearing.
Re-check this scene against Kai's when it lands.

**Review round 2 (22 Oct):** a story and a cold-viewer finding (both verified) showed that a cold viewer of this reel never hears what is in it for him. So D03 now says it once, right after *"You. And your consciousness."*: AI *"It only sends one back. Me."* The vote scene can repeat it rather than carry it alone, and *"I don't know if it works"* and *"Back then"* now have an object. Derek's test is now one plain question, *"Does it even work?"*, and Derek sits first, in a room of empty chairs, with the room following him. by: claude, **Kai to rule**.

## Look

Clean, bright and futuristic: cool white, crisp, a faint grain. The style rule for the second half
is that it travels from 16mm to futuristic, and this is the far end of it. The light comes from the
ceiling grid in the hall, the wafer's rainbow on faces, and the pool of light under each chair. D14 is
the one exterior, at dusk, with the amber seam as its only warm light.

## Shot design (shot-craft)

- **Every monumental shot carries a cost in frame.** D02 has the mud. D05 (the empty chair pattern) is
  held only as a set-up for the people who fill it, and it pays in D10 and D16. D13, the spiral, is the
  same pattern with **every chair full**, followed straight away by D14's footprints and D15's objects,
  so full (D13) and empty (D16) frame the cost.
- **The scale steps** from the wide hall to the giant wafer, a face, the overhead pattern, a mug, faces,
  hands, the overhead pattern again, the exterior wide, objects, and a small robot among empty chairs.
- **One lead element per moment.** The crossing (D13–D14) has no words, so music leads. Maddie alone
  (D16) has no music, so silence leads.
- **No explosions as spectacle.** The AI goes as a light running out along a seam.
- **Talking shots** (`lip-sync-dub`): D04 (the lady, one word), D07 and D09 (Derek, two clips from one plate)
  and D11 (the lady). The AI never has a mouth: his source is Maddie's beacon (D00, and soft in D07, D08,
  D11). Maddie has no face, so her lines need no sync.
