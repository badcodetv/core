## The sentence

**The people who won in the opening are gone too: the machine now holds the money, and the government
reads out the machine's script and calls it its own idea.**

Beneficiary, by role: the machine holds the funds; the government takes the credit. The opening
already named the first winners (the CEO, the board, the traders); this scene empties their rooms.

## The steps (ending first)

- **End:** the drop. The bank tower switches to the invader; the takeover is complete and counted.
- **Before it:** the government statement, from the speaker's eyes. The glass prompter carries the
  speech; then the machine's aside types itself in underneath. The words the audience hears are
  the ones the machine wrote.
- **Before that:** the four rooms from the opening, each now empty, each with money leaving on a screen.
- **Start:** the signal goes in (kept clips).

## The prompter wording (ruled)

- Speech, **white capitals**, scrolling with the newsreader: `…ACTUALLY GOOD FOR THE MARKETS.`
- Then the machine's aside, **lowercase green terminal type with a cursor**, typed in on *"this
  statement was its own idea"*: `now tell them it was your idea.`
- No hands, no body. Every word on the glass and on every screen is **ffmpeg**; the plates carry blank glass and blank screens.

rules: darkness reads as depth only with one brighter region (the screen in each room is that region);
flashes from the camera array at 3 a second or fewer.
