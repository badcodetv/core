# Businesses — Times Square fills with boxes

[Pack index, shot specifications and edit](./downfall-flow-prompts.md).

**Play order:** T1 4s → T2 6s → T3 8s. Generate the T3 anchor candidates first. The scene opens
on an individual consequence, passes through congestion, then reveals its scale. T3 is the
principal image, not a quick establishing shot.

**Declared variation:** lighting and colour treatment. A = cool overcast house treatment;
B = ordinary full-colour daylight; C = screen-lit dusk; D = hard late-afternoon light. Keep the
chosen treatment coherent across all three shots. The four blocks under each shot are complete
alternatives; paste one, not all four. All images fill a landscape 16:9 frame.

## T1 — a working life in a box

The box is the focal point. This worker does not need to recur; there is no character-identity
dependency. The photograph lies face-down so it cannot introduce another face or text.

### T1-A · cool overcast; the entrance falls dark

```prompt
Create a landscape 16:9 photographic frame from a fictional near-future film. Close view at chest height, a 50mm lens angled slightly down toward a cardboard box held against an adult office worker's worn charcoal coat. The frame crops above the mouth. The box rim is bent under the weight of a ceramic mug, a small desk plant and a face-down photograph frame. Sleeves partly cover the supporting hands. Beyond the sharp box, other adult workers carry boxes out of a dark glass office entrance in Midtown Manhattan, their bodies partly cut by the frame edges. Soft overcast street light comes from camera-left and falls away inside the entrance. Muted cool-grey colour, real cardboard fibres and creased cloth, fine 35mm film grain. Full-frame image, clean unmarked surfaces, no captions, logos or lens flare.
```

## T2 — the pavement cannot take them

Compose an open near-side gap for the camera to move along. Bodies occupy depth, with different
orientations and destinations; the camera is not travelling through a uniform rank of people.

### T2-A · cool overcast; a gap closing ahead

```prompt
Create a landscape 16:9 photographic frame from a fictional near-future film. Street-level medium-wide view at 1.5 metres, a level 35mm lens beside a crowded Midtown Manhattan pavement. A cropped carton and coat sleeve occupy the near-left edge. Across a narrow clear strip of pavement, adults in varied office clothes stand in irregular groups carrying desk belongings in cardboard boxes. One person halfway through a gap holds their box tightly and looks for a route; another waits with a phone against their ear. More workers and stationary cars recede behind them. Include varied adult ages and skin tones, ordinary hair and creased clothing. Hands remain at waist height or beside faces holding phones. Overcast light from the open street, darker building side, cool restrained colour and fine 35mm grain. Unbranded, no captions or lens flare.
```

## T3 — the square is full

Generate this anchor first. Keep people and boxes large near camera. The crowd should occupy
most of the lower two-thirds; screens locate the square without consuming the whole picture.

### T3-A · the house frame; raised, close, overcast

```prompt
Create a landscape 16:9 photographic frame from a fictional near-future film. Raised oblique view of Times Square from about five metres above the pavement, close to the crowd, with a 28mm lens tipped slightly down. Adult office workers carrying cardboard boxes fill the square and continue beyond the side streets. The nearest people are only several metres away: ordinary coats, tired faces, plants and mugs in open cartons remain individually readable. Dense irregular groups stall around stationary cars; some sit on kerbs beside belongings. Crumpled boxes and loose sheets collect on damp paving, mostly grounded. Hands carry possessions low or hold phones. Large screens continue showing abstract unbranded commercial colour above the crowd. Soft overcast sky lights the people; cool-grey restrained colour, fine 35mm grain, full-frame image. No banners, captions, readable advertising or lens flare.
```

### T3 · image-to-video

```prompt

```

**Edit/sound:** hold the mass long enough to discover the near belongings and the crowd's
continuation. Do not animate everyone into one marching direction. The headline's number lives
in narration; the frame does not attempt to depict or verify exactly 200,000 individuals.

## Selection notes

The camera progression is close → medium → wide. T2 is the sole travelling camera in this
scene; T3 reveals scale through the cut. All three shots belong to the same time and weather.
If the strongest compositions come from different treatment letters, make reference edits to
align their lighting before animating; do not rely on grading to turn hard sun into overcast.

Check the stills at intended viewing size: boxes must read as belongings, paper as litter, and
the mass as separate adults with disrupted days. If the square resembles a rally, change the
staging rather than adding an explanatory headline. If distant faces become indistinct, protect
the large near figures; the intended scale does not require distant facial detail.
