## The sentence

**Two machine armies fought their first battle over an ordinary English town, and nobody asked anyone:
both sides declared victory, neither government was consulted.**

Beneficiary/decision-maker, by role: whoever owns the machines (the scenes before: the money bought an
army). The cost is the town: empty, and littered with wreckage.

## Rules (Kai)

- **No humans at all**, anywhere in frame.
- **Never overtly violent**: the e07 explosion is enough. Wreckage, smoke, crashed drones — not hits.
- **Today**, not five years later (that's the simulation scene). Reminiscent of Ukraine–Russia drone war
  without saying it (quadcopters, fixed-wing drones, tracked ground robots, trenches, shredded tree lines).
- Set in Swindon: motorways, roundabouts, high streets, fields.

## Look

Overcast grey English daylight, muted natural colour (matches d09–e07). The drone-feed beat breaks it on
purpose (grainy, grey or thermal). Veo camera-locked; Premiere moves; any readout on a feed shot is ffmpeg.
