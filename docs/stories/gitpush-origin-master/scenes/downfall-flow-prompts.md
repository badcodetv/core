# Downfall — Flow prompt pack

Three scenes, approximately eighteen seconds each. **Twelve distinct shot designs, eighteen
placements in the edit.** The three network inserts repeat for each city. Their recurrence is
deliberate: the same action arrives in a different financial centre.

## The paste-ready sheets

- [Businesses — boxes through Times Square](./downfall-flow-businesses.md): T1–T3.
- [Banks — travel through the network](./downfall-flow-banks.md): N1–N3, SG, NY, LN.
- [Swindon — sign, wreckage, continuing machine](./downfall-flow-swindon.md): W1–W3.

Each shot has **four complete still prompts** labelled A–D, followed by its image-to-video
prompt. Paste one still block on its own; it already includes its composition and treatment.
The variants are alternative proposals, not four consecutive shots. A is the house treatment;
B deliberately tests fuller natural colour. None is selected by this document.

Use the eventual selected still as the video's **start image**. The motion block describes what
changes, not the appearance the image already provides. No end image is required. Exact cuts,
the signal signature, lettering and final audio belong to the edit.

This completes the prompt-writing brief. Generation and the review of actual candidates are
later work; the prompts have not been tested in Flow.

## Authority and intentional departures

Kai's latest direction governs the [sequence plan](./downfall-to-return-plan.md): crowded Times
Square, travelling through circuits and cables into bank control units, tracking the Swindon
sign with smoke beyond, then damaged and active machines in UK fields. It supersedes the older
off-screen-collapse rule and this sequence's earlier locked sign and shutdown-console ideas.
There is no button, switch, drawer, press conference or office-notification insert in this pack.

The AI remains unpersonified. The combat platforms are machinery in the world, not its body.
The signal is represented by travelling camera and controlled light activity, not a character
or glowing creature. Close human framing is spent on the dismissed workers, as the new brief
requires. Real places locate fictional future scenes; generated crowds are not historical
Lehman footage. Bank architecture stays unbranded.

The macro network inserts cannot individually show the human cost of the requested takeover.
This is a scoped departure from the toolkit's every-machine-frame cost gate, required by the
signal viewpoint: keep these inserts short and within the dismissal/takeover sequence. Do not
pretend that scratched circuitry proves a social consequence. The human scenes, bank thresholds
and damaged farmland carry that consequence visibly.

## The edit

```text
30–31 N1   31–32 N2   32–33 N3   33–36 LN
36–42 W1   42–48 W2   48–54 W3
```

These are edit targets, not requested generation lengths or promises about spoken delivery.
Generate enough source to choose a clean interval. The first circuit passage establishes the
route; subsequent passages can use different clean windows of those same three sources. Keep
travel direction and the arrival signature identical. Do not ask Flow to perform a city change
inside one clip or morph a control cabinet into a skyscraper.

T3 gets the longest uninterrupted hold. Bank travel is fast against relatively still buildings.
W1 slows the camera down again; W2 and W3 let the machines and landscape carry the action. The
sign appears early. The final machine is still engaged when we cut to the later empty world.

## Continuity and generation order

1. **Choose the three visual anchors first:** T3, N3, W2, each from its four written concepts.
   These settle the crowd's light, the network's materials, and the field/vehicle geography.
2. Make the remaining candidate spreads. T1–T2 must resolve to T3's time/weather. Network
   inserts share N3's materials, but need not pretend to be one physically continuous room.
   Bank cities can have different local light; all must leave the facade legible for the same
   takeover event. W1 and W3 inherit W2's light, smoke direction and agricultural setting.
3. W3's four prompts are **reference-led**: attach the selected W2 plate as the single reference
   and retain the actual surviving platform, field and weather. Their declared variable is
   framing. Candidate B also tests fuller colour on this shot; if selected, match the field
   sequence's grade before animation.
4. Check all candidate stills in sequence before video. After a choice, refine that selected
   image by reference. No repeated face is required across T1–T3; they are different people
   within the same event. W2–W3 object and location continuity is required.
5. Animate selected plates using the relevant motion blocks. T2, N1, N2 and W1 require actual
   camera translation. Keep those paths short and legible: repeated geometry can deform during
   a long fly-through. N3, bank exteriors and field shots stay held.

The repo gives both a general locked-camera hybrid method and a more specific correction that
real depth requires parallax. This pack uses the correction for the requested travelling shots;
post remains responsible for exact light timing, text and cuts. A flat zoom is not a substitute
for the Swindon track.

## Exact finishing work

**Signal:** choose one cool-white activity band, travelling left-to-right across N3's indicator
array, then left-to-right across a selected region of each target building's windows. Mask the
actual indicator/window geometry; keep mullions, structure and sky unchanged. The band makes
one pass and the selected windows stay in their new state. No whole-frame flash. Composite it
over held shots; do not depend on generated timing. N1–N2 camera travel conveys movement through
the route; a subtle travelling highlight can reinforce it without introducing an orb or avatar.

**Swindon:** W1 deliberately generates a blank pale sign with a dark border. Add the exact word
**SWINDON** as a separate graphic on its face in finishing. The sign starts front-on and the
camera translates parallel to its plane, so the intended transform is mainly sideways movement,
with small corrections if necessary. Keyframe against the actual plate; do not assume a moving
sign can take a screen-fixed caption. Match perspective, surface texture, exposure and motion
blur. Include a lettered mock-up in the still review so the location read is judged before
animation. If the actual take rotates the sign substantially, use a shorter clean interval or
reshoot the same simple path; do not assume unavailable automatic planar tracking will fix it.

**Sound:** retain generated sound only as scratch. Final layers: cardboard/shoes/overlapping
calls; electrical travel and three low arrival impacts; roadside wind, remote machinery and one
weapon report. Narration is laid separately. No generated dialogue or music is requested.
