## The sequence in one sentence

The completed bad branch proves what happened; the Carrier and the people harmed correct who was
responsible; the failed copies prove why humans matter; and a living person receives an unfinished
branch that BadCode cannot complete alone.

## Decisions

- **Estimated runtime:** **1:30–1:42**. Approximately 1:02–1:12 is spoken; approximately 0:26–0:30
  is hinges, pauses and two true silences. These are working edit estimates, not evidence or locked
  frame timings.
- **Eleven designed shots.** Hard cuts and matched frames do most of the work. There is one camera
  move: the final slow narrowing toward the cursor.
- **The good branch begins late.** It starts as the voice admits, *“I don't have a map for this
  one,”* then forms through the invitation. It does not begin before the warning or accountability.
- **The title is spent once.** `WE ARE BADCODE` appears over the ordinary 2026 plate on the spoken
  reveal, then clears. It never returns as an end card.
- **Two true silences:** after *“Don't make me come back twice”* and after *“Let's get to work.”*
- **The Carrier is recalled exactly.** Repeat her Scene 15 correction composition if that shot
  exists; otherwise make that composition once. Do not invent a more heroic memory angle.
- **No new collapse montage.** Reuse the existing trader and worker receipts under the two
  political sentences.
- **The cursor is optional participation.** The web version may let a reader extend the line, but
  the film is complete if they do nothing. The Future Proof link lives below the player.
- **The branch stays open.** The bad branch has a terminal ring; the lighter good branch dissolves
  at an unfinished cursor and never receives a tip ring.

## Shot specifications

### 1 · Fork landing

**Job:** carry Scene 19's cost into the present and establish that the bad branch is complete.
**Register:** cosmic hinge. **Depth:** compressed weights near; hot fork at middle depth; completed
branch receding beyond the frame. **Focal point/light:** the fork wins with its own residual trace;
everything falls to black around it. **Camera:** locked, straight-on, orthographic wide. **Scale:**
the bad branch exits the frame. **Withheld:** the space below the fork is entirely blank.
**Movement:** the weights settle once. The full hundred remains remembered through the
just-seen launch. A small lone-lamp inset stays attached to the old-history side, visibly separate
from the return trace. Neither the refuser nor any human light travels to the arrival node.
Typeset `2026. Now.` at arrival and clear it before the room/identity cut.

### 2 · Ordinary now / identity

**Job:** transfer “you” to a living viewer and reveal the name without making it the ending.
**Register:** ordinary 2026, human scale. **Depth:** table edge, cup, blank paper and pen near; recently vacated sofa seat
at middle depth; window and doorway beyond. **Focal point/light:** the empty seat, isolated by
ordinary window daylight and negative space. **Camera:** seated eye height, level, normal-lens
medium-wide. **Scale:** familiar domestic objects. **Withheld:** the occupant is just outside the
frame. **Movement:** curtain or daylight drift only. Plain `WE ARE BADCODE` typography arrives on
the spoken name and clears before the price.

### 3 · The price

**Job:** attach the identity to its cost, then let the warning cut cleanly. **Register:** terminal
cosmic. **Depth:** spent weights near; fork and completed branch behind. **Focal point/light:** the
return trace holds the last weak fork glow; the lone-lamp inset remains on the old-history side. **Camera:** a distinct close graphic cut, not a
minor push from Shot 1. **Scale:** the branch crosses multiple edges. **Withheld:** no good branch.
**Movement:** none. Do not add an ominous swell or stress an implied “you” in the warning; that
would turn sacrifice into a demand for repayment.

### 4 · The Carrier

**Job:** make “she” instantly legible and show that a human corrected the narrator. **Register:**
the human-scale exception. **Depth:** tool or doorway edge near; Carrier at middle depth; workshop
receding behind. **Focal point/light:** her eyes and braided glasses cord in the same low warm
practical as Scene 15. **Camera:** the exact eye-level three-quarter medium close used for her
correction. **Scale:** worn tools, hands, face. **Withheld:** her interlocutor. **Movement:** exact
replay where available; otherwise stillness with living room tone. Hold her through *“get that
right.”* Never substitute a ghost image or the superseded legacy woman.

### 5 · Responsibility returns to now

**Job:** keep the admission *“I took choices that weren't mine”* in the viewer's present while the
AI remains unpersonified. **Register/depth/light/camera:** exact Shot 2 plate, with the title gone.
**Focal point:** the empty human seat. **Withheld:** AI and viewer. **Movement:** locked. The repeated
frame changes meaning because the voice now owns the empty seat rather than occupying it.

### 6 · Perfect copies

**Job:** recall the attempted reconstruction without explaining it again. **Register:** remembered
domestic island. **Depth:** doorway edge near; copied people at the table; dark kitchen beyond.
**Focal point/light:** the human pair in the table lamp's pool. **Camera:** existing eye-level
doorway view. **Scale:** chairs, mugs and table. **Withheld:** the machine around them.
**Movement:** none. Reuse `C7-b2-kitchen-START.jpg`.

### 7 · Nobody came

**Job:** prove the failure through exact subtraction. **Register, camera, depth and light:** an
identical match to Shot 6. **Focal point:** the pushed-out empty chair beside the same lamp.
**Withheld:** the people. **Movement:** none. Reuse `C7-kitchen-EMPTY-golden.jpg`; do not dissolve
between the pair when a hard cut makes the absence do the work.

### 8 · Living present

**Job:** convert the failed-copy rhyme into living human agency. **Register:** ordinary 2026,
human scale. **Depth:** table or pen edge near; one listening person at middle depth; a second
person partly outside the frame; the room beyond. **Focal point/light:** a living face mid-response,
held by the same window daylight as Shot 2. **Camera:** the exact seated-eye-height 40mm-equivalent
medium-wide crop of Shot 2. Face and hand must be readable at that original scale; do not change
the lens or tighten only the living derivative.
**Scale:** hands, blank paper, cup. **Withheld:** the collaborator and the unwritten words.
**Movement:** the hand pauses and the person looks up; camera locked. This is the only sincere
warmth, delivered more quietly rather than with a larger score.

### 9 · Beneficiary receipt

**Job:** show who kept the gains. **Register:** documentary callback. **Depth/focal point:** monitors
near, laughing traders at middle depth, climbing green graph beyond; faces and movement lead the
eye. **Light:** existing fluorescent practicals and screens. **Camera/scale:** existing eye-level
office wide; desks and people keep it human. **Withheld:** company identity and invented numbers.
**Movement:** reuse a short extract of `C4V2-trading-floor.mp4` under the beneficiary sentence.

### 10 · Worker receipt

**Job:** show who lost the work and the say. **Register:** documentary callback. **Depth:** passing
car/road near; workers and boxes at middle depth; office entrance beyond. **Focal point/light:** a
box-carrying worker in existing overcast daylight. **Camera/scale:** existing across-street,
eye-level wide with doors, people and boxes. **Withheld:** biographies and employer identity.
**Movement:** reuse `C4V2-workers.mp4`; cut on the lateral walk. Exclude newsroom, empty-city,
army and aftermath material.

### 11 · Open branch / cursor

**Job:** turn evidence into shared work and hand over the unresolved future. **Register:** quiet
terminal hinge. **Depth:** completed branch dim behind; forming line at middle depth; cursor nearest
at its open end. **Focal point/light:** the cursor becomes the sole bright anchor. **Camera:**
11a is locked orthographic wide. After the worker receipt, 11b spends the sequence's one slow
digital crop/scale move narrowing toward the cursor, ending stationary.
**Scale:** the whole failed history is visible when the shot begins. **Withheld:** all future events,
labels, maps and endpoints. **Movement:** the line first appears under *“I don't have a map,”* draws
through the political receipts as a restrained marginal overlay, or continues off-screen if no
safe margin exists, and stops unfinished. Keep political faces and boxes unobscured. The cursor holds through the final silence.

## Performance plan

- Use a calm, authoritative scratch read around **115–134 words per minute**. The ceiling in the
  house form guide is a check, not a target; the line must have time to decay.
- Play the warning as a blade, not a threat. Stress *“twice”*, not an implied accusation of “you.”
- Keep the Carrier correction and the AI's admission flat, quiet and free of score swell.
- Reserve the one warm crack for *“You were the part I couldn't copy. That's why I came back.”*
- Return to quiet authority for *“I don't have a map.”* The admission is limited knowledge, not a
  shrug.
- Play the chooser/beneficiary pair straight: `companies / jobs / gains`, then `people / say`.
  Let *“together”* fall quieter.
- Phrase the canon in clusters. Staccato full stops in the file are edit handles, not an instruction
  to isolate every sentence.

Two compression options were considered and rejected for now. The canon is stronger at its current
length. If a locked runtime later requires cuts, revisit only after hearing the scratch read.

## Interactive and linear implementation

Create a story-local `Scene20Fork` renderer with one normalized cue/state mapper. Do not generalize
the shared comic package before this sequence proves what it needs.

The state order is: `bad_hold → now → identity → price → carrier → accountability → copies →
living → good_draw → cursor_ready → cursor_claimed`. The interactive telling maps scroll progress
to these states; a linear render maps time to the same states.

On the web, the terminal caret may become a native focusable button only after the last line. Click,
tap, Enter or Space extends one short line segment into blank space and moves the caret. It does not
navigate, reveal a prescribed future or finish a task. Do not autofocus it or trap focus. Give it a
generous hit target, visible focus treatment, a concise instruction and a polite state announcement.

The diagram needs a text equivalent: **“The documented bad branch ends; an unfinished second branch
waits for a living choice.”** Use shape as well as brightness: solid terminal bad line and ring;
thinner/fading or dashed open line and caret. Under `prefers-reduced-motion`, skip drawing, camera
narrowing, fades and blinking; show each state immediately and finish on a solid caret. Custom SVG
or animation code must query that preference rather than relying only on the global CSS shortcut.

In linear video, run the same cues automatically, blink the cursor during the final hold and end
cleanly. No click instruction or promotional end card. In print, use the final static caret and put
the optional continuation on the following companion page.

The Future Proof companion sits below the player, outside the sticky/final frame, as semantic text
and a normal link to `/future-proof`. The film never requires the cursor or link to feel complete.

## Ordered workflow

This is the later multimedia workflow. The text-only cinematography pass is complete in the
linked scene sheets; steps 1–2 below do not block beginning photographic stills. Their timing
findings must be incorporated before animation and the final edit.

1. **Scratch VO and timing.** 🟡 Text package ready; generation deferred in
   [`s20-scratch-read.md`](./s20-scratch-read.md). Record the complete current canon once without
   performance tricks; mark real word timings and replace the estimated cue windows above.
2. **Creative timing gate.** Review the read for warning-as-debt, Carrier legibility, political
   slogan density and room for the final silence. Adjust performance before proposing text cuts.
3. **Scene 19 seam.** Finish or placeholder-board `crossing` and `crossing-after`; retain the lone
   refuser lamp so the cost never implies all hundred consented.
4. **Reuse assembly.** Pull the locked ghost pair and two bulletin clips into a rough ending bin.
5. **New stills.** Make the empty `now` plate, living-present rhyme and Scene 15 Carrier composition.
   Show every still candidate to Kai before spending animation credits, per shot-craft's approval gate.
6. **Branch prototype.** Build the cue mapper and a story-local SVG branch/cursor renderer. Reuse
   normalized geometry ideas from the site graph, while removing its completed-good-branch ring.
7. **Animatic.** Assemble scratch VO, stills/placeholders, existing clips, branch prototype and
   silence into the first 1:30–1:42 cut.
8. **Sol review.** Re-time before requesting new footage. Cut shots that repeat a job; identify at
   most the remaining one or two image gaps.
9. **Human creative gate.** Kai chooses the stills and rules whether the emotional turn feels earned.
10. **Production build.** Animate approved stills, record final narration, integrate interactive and
    linear variants, and add the external Future Proof companion.
11. **Accessibility and behavior check.** Keyboard, touch, focus, screen-reader text, contrast,
    reduced motion, forward/reverse/fast scroll, mobile viewport and no-WebGL fallback.
12. **Astra adversarial pass.** Review the actual animatic/cut for excitement, blame, coercion,
    continuity and the hope-to-action handoff. Apply findings, then run delivery QC before release.

## Acceptance checks

- The full hundred are remembered; only ninety-nine are shown paying the launch cost; the refuser's
  “no” remains visible and unexplained.
- The warning does not sound like the living audience owes the machine repayment.
- “She” resolves immediately to the Carrier, and the film visibly remembers that she corrected the AI.
- The AI owns taking choices. No line or picture converts ignored resistance into public consent.
- The exact ghost subtraction earns *“You were the part I couldn't copy.”*
- A living human, rather than a title or machine, owns the warmest frame.
- The existing trader/worker evidence names beneficiary and consequence once, plainly.
- The good branch begins after limited authority is admitted, remains unfinished and has no tip ring.
- `WE ARE BADCODE` disappears before the warm turn and does not return.
- The cursor is optional, keyboard usable and non-navigational; reduced-motion users receive the
  same states and meaning.
- The Future Proof route is reachable outside the film, while the film remains complete on its own.
- The final line decays into silence with no score hit, CTA overlay or promotional card.
