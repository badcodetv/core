## What makes the sequence cinematic

The remaining film changes its way of looking as the narrator loses certainty and encounters
people who can disagree. Large spaces are earned through the ordinary things they cannot supply.
The coin's tiny change and the final ordinary face should compete successfully with the vastest
machine image.

Camera discipline is a budget, not a claim that stillness is inherently profound. There are three

  composite controlled spin/landing with correct occlusion, contact and reflection. No generator
  is asked to discover the physics. Once it lands, keep it landed in the response/recollection.
  Later tests reset only between tests; nobody opens the dome. The blank faces' named outcomes

Before accepting any image sequence, check:

- The defining object or gesture reads at the intended viewing size: seal, empty seat, Carrier's
  eyes and darn, refuser's cuff/lamp, present-day face/hand. Near-black does not mean illegible.
- Every large machine view contains a readable human cost or absence; pristine utopia stays
  pristine. Warmth is motivated by human practical light, then ordinary present-day daylight.
- The AI has no body, face or emotional robot proxy. A human's independent glance, disagreement
  and refusal survive the edit. Nobody is visually blamed for being scared or failing to obey.
- All ninety-nine remain spent, the refuser remains in old history, and the arrival carries
  information alone. Neither the living room nor the graphic suggests human resurrection.
- Existing dialogue and the optional status of draft material are preserved. A shot cue is not a
  replacement narration script. No invented speech, equation or numerical evidence is added.
- Final warmth belongs to a living person capable of replying; political gain and displacement
  remain concrete; identity does not return as a promotional end card.
