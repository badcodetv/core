# Second half — mood board prompts

Look exploration only, on the cheap tier: nothing here is a locked plate.

- **Round 1 (24, style `original`):** each prompt ends with *Photographic 16:9 frame from a fictional near-future British film. Fine 35mm grain, realistic textures, full-frame image, no captions, logos or lettering.*
- **Round 2 (48, Kai: "a lot more variation… try different styles"):** each round-1 look re-run in two of seven styles (1980s BBC 16mm, noir, painted matte, stop-motion miniature, anamorphic blockbuster, handheld documentary, advert-bright), rotated so every scene gets at least four. The full prompt is recorded below.
- **Round 3 (20 + 8 HUD mock-ups, Kai: kill the drain and stop-motion):** the kill-bot sequence — patrol POV, the signal, the row, kill mode. Stop-motion rows deleted from the record. The drain rows stay below but are off the board. HUD mock-ups are drawn by `scripts/gpom/hud-mockup.py`, not generated.
- **Round 5 (20, Kai: the time machine is a clean room the AI built, not in the bunker):** chip-fab amber, white hall, the airlock contrast, exteriors on the downs, pods, the crossing. The old in-bunker machine rows are kept below as `machine-bunker-retired`, off the board. ⚠ L13 timed out as "Failed" but finished late, and the next batch harvested it first, shifting every frame by one; remapped by eye and L20 regenerated alone.
- **Round 6 (40, Kai: treble the clean room by concept, not style):** WF the wafer beyond understanding · BL the building, one fixed design day to night · GE geometry no human would design · HM uncanny hospitality · VC visible cost · CR the crossing made visible. GE1 timed out once and was reworded; every batch was contact-sheet checked against its prompts before naming.
- **What came back:** stop-motion and painted read weakly on several frames (mostly still photographic); X2 drew a humanoid robot.





## bunker

### B1-lived-tunnel · Naturalistic, lived in

A long Cold War government bunker tunnel deep beneath Corsham, Wiltshire: whitewashed quarried-stone walls, green-painted 1950s steel blast doors, a line of cream enamel ceiling lamps receding. Survivors have lived here for five years: washing lines strung between conduit pipes, camp beds, a kettle on a trestle table, a hand-painted chore rota on a board, tomato plants under a grow lamp. Mixed cool fluorescent and warm tungsten light. Level 35mm view at head height.

### B2-one-bulb · GPOM's register: near-black, one light

A Cold War bunker corridor beneath Wiltshire almost entirely in darkness. One bare tungsten bulb in a wire cage hangs over a trestle table with a kettle and mugs; its small warm pool shows whitewashed stone, a green steel blast door and a folded camp bed. Everything beyond the pool falls to near-black, with one faint second lamp far down the tunnel. Quiet, inhabited, hidden. Low 35mm view.

### B3-canteen · Warm community

A 1950s underground government canteen in a Cold War bunker, now home to a hundred survivors: long formica tables, people of all ages in jumpers and coats eating from tin plates, homemade bunting strung from pipes, a tea urn steaming, children drawing on the stone wall. Warm sodium and tungsten light, cosy and crowded under a low vaulted stone ceiling. Wide 28mm view from the serving hatch.

### B4-quarry-cathedral · Monumental: the stone quarry

A vast underground limestone quarry cavern beneath Corsham, Wiltshire, used as a Cold War bunker: enormous rough-hewn stone pillars like a cathedral, and in the middle of it a tiny village of tents, camp beds and strings of fairy lights where survivors live. Small human figures give the scale. Darkness all around, the village the only warm light. Wide 24mm view from a raised walkway.

### B5-the-lavatory · The source of the argument

A Cold War bunker washroom: 1950s white tiles, a row of government-issue toilet cubicles, one door open showing a toilet with its seat left up. A cast-iron drain pipe climbs the wall into the stone ceiling. A man and a woman in jumpers mid-argument by the sinks, pointing at the seat. Flat fluorescent light, one tube flickering. Comic, ordinary. 35mm view at waist height.


## drain

### D2-lane-night · The drain at night

A storm drain grate in a cracked rural Wiltshire lane at night, hedges on either side, no buildings. A faint warm glow and wisps of steam rise from far below the grate. The only light is that glow and a dim blue sky. Low camera almost on the tarmac, the grate large in the foreground.

### D3-shaft-up · Looking up the shaft

Looking straight up a narrow brick and iron drainage shaft from deep underground, iron rungs climbing the wall, to a small square of grey daylight through a street grate far above. Damp, green-stained brick, water drips catching the light. Wide-angle lens, strong perspective.


## killbots

### K1-cctv · Grainy CCTV

A grainy black-and-white security camera still from a high corner camera above a ruined English market-town street: three low tracked autonomous machines with chamfered bodies and compact turrets race around the corner in motion blur, debris kicked up. Fisheye distortion, low resolution, heavy video noise and scan lines, washed-out highlights. No on-screen text.

### K2-bot-pov · From the delivery bot's height

Street-level view from 40 cm high on a ruined English high street: in the soft foreground the amber beacon and grey lid of a small six-wheeled delivery robot; beyond it, three low tracked autonomous combat machines with chamfered bodies and compact turrets speed past in a blur, kicking up ash. Overcast daylight, burned shopfronts, a church tower in the distance.

### K3-night-vision · Night vision

A green night-vision image of a column of low tracked autonomous machines with compact turrets moving fast along a dark Wiltshire lane between hedgerows, their sensors glowing white. Heavy grain, vignette, bloom on hot spots. No on-screen text.

### K4-motorway · Aerial: the column

Aerial view at dusk of an empty, weed-cracked English motorway: a long column of small low tracked autonomous machines streaming along the carriageway in formation, tiny in the frame, dust trailing. Abandoned cars along the hard shoulder, grey sky, the lights of nothing. High oblique view.


## destruct

### X1-chain · The self-destruct

A ruined English high street: a line of low tracked autonomous machines bursting apart one after another in bright white-orange flashes, metal panels flying, smoke rolling, the nearest one only metres from a street drain grate in the foreground. Overcast daylight, no people. Dramatic action-film frame.

### X2-cctv-whiteout · On CCTV

A grainy black-and-white security camera still blown out by a huge white flash in the middle of a street, the silhouettes of machines breaking apart at its edge, video noise, scan lines, the image half lost to overexposure. No on-screen text.

### X3-aftermath · The aftermath

A quiet ruined street just after: three smoking, split-open husks of low tracked machines lie stopped in a row, the last one a metre from a drain grate in the foreground. Smoke drifts. A small six-wheeled delivery robot with an amber beacon sits a little way off, watching. Overcast daylight, still.


## meeting

### M1-emerge · Into daylight

Survivors climbing out of a concrete Cold War bunker entrance set into a grassy Wiltshire hillside, heavy green blast doors swung open, squinting into overcast daylight, wrapped in coats and blankets. A small six-wheeled grey delivery robot with an amber beacon waits on the track in front of them. Wide 35mm view.

### M2-canteen-bot · The bot comes in

Inside a Cold War bunker canteen lit by warm tungsten: a hundred survivors of all ages crowd silently back from a small grey six-wheeled delivery robot with an amber beacon that has rolled into the middle of the room. An old woman in a cardigan stands nearest it, arms folded. Tense, slightly absurd. 28mm view from behind the crowd.

### M3-old-lady · The old lady

Close portrait of a British woman in her seventies in a bobbled cardigan and reading glasses on a chain, arms folded, unimpressed, looking down at something small off-frame at knee height. Warm tungsten light from one side, a whitewashed bunker wall soft behind her. 85mm lens, shallow depth of field.


## machine-bunker-retired

### T1-circle · A circle of people

In a vast dark underground stone quarry cavern, a hundred people sit on chairs in a great circle, holding hands, each with a small old brass lamp at their feet glowing warmly. In the centre of the circle, a small grey six-wheeled delivery robot with an amber beacon. High overhead view, the circle of lights the only light.

### T2-apparatus · A machine built for it

Inside a huge underground stone cavern, a towering improvised machine: racks of computer servers stacked into a monolith, thick cables running out to a hundred worn reclining armchairs arranged in rows before it, people settling into them. Cold blue light from the racks, warm light at the chairs. Wide 24mm view, monumental scale with visible wear and makeshift repairs.

### T3-analog · Homemade, 1950s

A homemade time machine in a Cold War bunker: a 1950s government control console with valves, dials, bakelite switches and patch cables, wired to a ring of a hundred battered headphones hanging from hooks, a hand-written label on masking tape. Warm tungsten light, dust, ordinary and handmade.

### T4-lamps-out · The lamps going out

Long rows of small warm lamps in a vast dark stone cavern, one beside each of a hundred empty chairs; the lamps nearest the camera are already dark, the far ones still glowing, a wave of darkness moving away from us. Elegiac, still, enormous scale.


## ending

### Y1-sofa · Over the shoulder, at home

Over-the-shoulder view of a young man on a sofa in a small British flat at night, watching a laptop. On the laptop screen, a generic video-player web page plays a frame of an underground bunker full of lamps. A mug, a takeaway box, the glow of the screen on his face. No logos or brand names, no readable text.

### Y2-bus · On the phone, on the bus

Over-the-shoulder view of a woman on the top deck of a night bus in an English town, watching a video on her phone: the phone shows a small grey delivery robot with an amber beacon on a ruined street. Rain on the window, streetlights streaking. No logos or readable text.

### Y3-recursion · The infinite screen

A screen inside a screen inside a screen: a laptop on a desk shows a video of a person watching a laptop that shows a video of a person watching a laptop, receding into infinity, each smaller and dimmer, in a dark room lit only by the screens. No logos or readable text.
