# AUDIO PROFILE
Derek, a working-class man from Liverpool in his fifties, Sandra's husband.

## THE SCENE
Walking along a chalk track over the open Wiltshire downs in a long line of a hundred people, grey daylight, wind in the grass, open sky, no echo; a husband and wife talking quietly side by side.

### DIRECTOR'S NOTES
Emotion for this line: weary, gentle, not looking at her; the second sentence quieter, the cost said plainly.
Style: indignant and exasperated in the row, arms-in-the-air disbelief; in the circle, angry and frightened, the first one to say no.
Pace: weary and gentle, a real pause between the two sentences.
Accent: Scouse, as heard in Liverpool. Never American, never Received Pronunciation.

### SAMPLE CONTEXT
The voice you'd cast as the bloke in a British sitcom who is losing an argument and knows it.
