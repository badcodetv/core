# Audio Profile
An older man from Salford, gravelly and low, a weary dad who has driven to collect someone at three in the morning before; flat, unimpressed, warm underneath.

## Scene:
A taxi rank at night, engines ticking over, he is standing right at the young man's shoulder.

## Sample Context:
The young man thinks he can drive because there are infinite versions of him. There aren't. He tells him so, once.
