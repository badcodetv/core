# Audio Profile
An old, burnt-out genius scientist with a raspy, cracked voice, a few drinks in and utterly bored by humanity. Flat, deadpan, dripping with sarcasm, as if explaining something obvious to an idiot for the hundredth time. A small sigh before the words.

## Scene:
A night street outside a pub. He has just done something impossible and cannot believe he has to spell it out.

## Sample Context:
The drunk young man thinks there are infinite versions of himself. The scientist corrects him with total contempt and a little affection, and does not raise his voice.
