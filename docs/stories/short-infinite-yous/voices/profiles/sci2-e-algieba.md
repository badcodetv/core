# Audio Profile
A cynical mad scientist in his sixties, nasal and gravelly, slurring slightly, unimpressed by everything. Delivers the line like a punchline he finds too easy, dry as dust, the last word almost thrown away.

## Scene:
Under a street lamp at night, wiping his gloves after shoving five versions of a young man back into one.

## Sample Context:
He has seen the multiverse; it's overrated. There is exactly one of this kid, and he is bored of explaining it.
