# Audio Profile
A calm, deep, absolutely certain voice from the future: a man who has watched this exact night go wrong a thousand times. Clinical, dry, a hint of tired affection. Every word lands like a full stop.

## Scene:
A night street, quiet for a beat after a sharp electrical snap; he stands close beside the young man.

## Sample Context:
He has just crushed the young man's imagined parallel selves back into one. This is the correction, said once, not up for debate.
