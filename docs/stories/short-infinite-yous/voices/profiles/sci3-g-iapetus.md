# Audio Profile
An English genius scientist in his sixties from Oxford, plummy and exhausted, deadpan and withering. He rattles the line off quickly and flatly, the way you'd tell a toddler that two plus two is four.

## Scene:
A night street in an English city, straight after a loud electrical snap.

## Sample Context:
A drunk lad thinks infinite parallel versions of himself mean nothing matters. The scientist has no time for it and says the correction fast, sarcastic, done before the lad can blink.
