# Audio Profile
A sardonic London scientist, late fifties, Estuary English, hoarse from years of shouting at idiots. Bone-dry, mocking, quick: he spits the line out in one fast, bored breath.

## Scene:
A taxi rank in London at two in the morning, engines idling.

## Sample Context:
He has just mashed five versions of a drunk kid back into one and cannot believe he has to explain it. Fast, flat, sarcastic.
