# solo-violin-jab-music-box — writer's notes

- **Instrumental, said in words, not just an empty lyrics box.** Community guides agree Suno defaults to singing and that "instrumental only, no vocals" should be stated in the style box; the toggle alone can still hallucinate voices. Voice words also go in exclude. ([howtopromptsuno](https://howtopromptsuno.com/style-lyrics/how-do-i-make-instrumental), [Reddit tips round-up](https://medium.com/@aitooldiscovery/suno-reddit-tips-and-prompts-what-r-sunoai-users-actually-do-65b59d51bcad))
- **No drums by naming the opposite.** Guides note a ban works less well than a positive word, so the style carries "completely beatless, rubato, no pulse" as well as drums in exclude. ([hookgenius](https://hookgenius.app/learn/suno-instrumental-music/))
- **One solo instrument, named prominently.** "Solo violin, close-miked and dry" keeps it from blooming into an orchestra; Suno is very sensitive to named instruments. ([Ultimate Suno guide](https://medium.com/@abhisheksd2003/the-ultimate-suno-ai-prompt-guide-with-clear-tested-examples-2d827ffe8b3a), [instrument tags](https://sunoaime.pages.dev/blog/suno-instrument-prompt-tags/))
- **The jab is a scare chord: the stab hits hardest after quiet.** Psycho's violin stab is "heightened by the preceding lack of music", so the prompt asks for pianissimo before and dead silence after the sforzando. ([TV Tropes, Scare Chord](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Main/ScareChord), [NCSU on Psycho](https://classapps.chass.ncsu.edu/com304/?p=1581), [The Conversation](https://theconversation.com:443/what-makes-a-film-score-frightening-expert-explains-the-techniques-that-build-tension-and-make-us-jump-216121))
- **Splitting copies as canon + sul ponticello.** Near-bridge bowing is a standard string unease colour; the canon is the music version of the copies drifting apart, then folding back into one note. ([Yamaha spooky string techniques](https://hub.yamaha.com/strings/s-how-to/eight-spooky-string-techniques))
- **Mood words carry the horror.** A 200-prompt test found dread words ("dread", "dark") steer Suno more than instruments do, so "tender dread" closes the style; "tender" keeps the mum/music box warm. ([roo.beehiiv detective test](https://roo.beehiiv.com/p/i-tested-200-suno-prompts-for-detective-music-30-actually-sound-cinematic-here-s-why))
- **Settings.** v6 + 15 s fixed by the story. Variety normal and weirdness 45: enough drift for the music-box/horn oddity without losing structure in 15 s. styleInfluence 75 so the timeline in the style box is followed. No source covered music box prompting specifically: that part is unverified.
