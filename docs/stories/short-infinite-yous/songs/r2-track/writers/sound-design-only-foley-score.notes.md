# sound-design-only-foley-score: what shaped it

- **Instrumental, no hallucinated voices.** Community guides say Suno defaults to vocals and the instrumental switch does not fully stop hallucinated singing, so the style box opens on "instrumental" and the exclude box bans voice, choir, humming and spoken word as well as "vocals". Lyrics left empty as the brief says.
  https://howtopromptsuno.com/style-lyrics/how-do-i-make-instrumental
  https://medium.com/@aitooldiscovery/suno-reddit-tips-and-prompts-what-r-sunoai-users-actually-do-65b59d51bcad
- **A full Suno track is not a sound-effects timeline** (third-party guide). So the effects are written in the order of the picture and framed as one short "film sting" with silence between hits, rather than as timestamps Suno would not obey.
  https://jackrighteous.com/en-gb/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/suno-ai-sound-effects-asterisks
  https://help.suno.com/en/articles/10625537 (Suno Sounds, the official one-shot effect tool, for comparison)
- **Musique concrète vocabulary**: loops of real recordings turned into rhythm, reversed playback, montage of cut sounds. That gave "foley arranged as rhythm", the keys as a repeated stuttering motif, and the reversed swell.
  https://codehop.com/organizing-sounds-musique-concrete-part-i/
  https://www.britannica.com/art/musique-concrete
- **Trailer sound-design terms** (riser before a cut, whoosh as filtered sweep, hit lands on the cut, long tail): each effect is described by weight, direction and length, and the horn gets a soft attack and long tail so it reads as a final chord, cinematic rather than annoying.
  https://www.ableton.com/en/blog/learn-how-to-make-high-impact-sounds-for-movies-and-trailers/
  https://violetrecording.com/sound-design-for-trailers/
- **Settings**: v6 and 15 s as fixed. Weirdness 60 to push it off conventional song structure; style influence 80 so the sound list is followed; variety normal; no vocal gender; Max Mode off. "Muddy bass", "reverb wash" and "lo-fi hiss" are excluded to keep the low end clean and the hits tight.
- Unverified: whether v6 will actually render a no-music, effects-only bed; this is the experiment.
