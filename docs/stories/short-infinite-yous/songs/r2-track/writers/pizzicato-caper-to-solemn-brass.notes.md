# pizzicato-caper-to-solemn-brass: notes

What shaped it:

- **Instrumental reliability.** Suno guides say the Instrumental switch alone still lets hallucinated vocals through; put "instrumental" in the style box, leave lyrics empty, and keep metatags sparse. So the style opens with "instrumental", lyrics is "", and the exclude box bans every kind of voice (vocals, choir, humming, spoken word). https://howtopromptsuno.com/style-lyrics/how-do-i-make-instrumental
- **Short style box.** Community advice keeps the style prompt under about 100 words, with instruments named plainly ("pizzicato", "bassoon"; bassoon described as reedy, quirky). Mine is about 90 words, in plain orchestration terms. https://medium.com/@aitooldiscovery/suno-reddit-tips-and-prompts-what-r-sunoai-users-actually-do-65b59d51bcad , https://howtopromptsuno.com/making-music/instrumental-tags
- **The caper palette.** Library and review blurbs pair pizzicato strings with bassoon for the classic tiptoeing, mischievous caper (practically made for mickey-mousing), and use a slide whistle glide as the slapstick signal of a twist. That gives the drunk-lad march and the squeeze gesture. https://moviemusicuk.us/2022/03/04/cat-burglar-christopher-willis/ , https://ende.app/en/song/13753-ultimate-comedy-music-vol-29
- **Mickey-mousing as the method.** Cartoon scoring sits between sound design and score, and mimics on-screen actions with instruments. That is why each picture event gets one instrumental hit (bitonal pile-up for the split, slide whistle and snap for the squeeze, timpani thud for the kick) rather than separate foley. https://willdinola.substack.com/p/im-leaving-composing-to-pursue-comedy
- **The turn.** The last 2 s drop to one muted flugelhorn note, rubato, with a distant soft car horn. That carries the message: one of you, alone. I chose flugelhorn over trumpet because it is warmer and less heraldic. No artist names are used, since Suno tends to refuse them.
