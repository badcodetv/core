# r2-track ideas: research notes (2026-11-14)

- Suno's own help page: Suno Sounds is a separate mode for single effects ("One Shot" for hits and whooshes, "Loop" for beds); its example prompt is a "cinematic whoosh transition sound". This means a song-mode prompt has to ask for effects as part of the arrangement, not expect clean isolated foley. https://help.suno.com/en/articles/10625537
- Creator guides: a one-line "cinematic" prompt gets a safe default; name a substyle (suspense underscore, trailer, emotional score) and an arc with dynamics. Pulse/drums make a cause spot feel like a trailer, so whether to use them is a choice. For instrumentals, say instrumental and keep "vocal/singer/lyrics" out of the style box. https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/what-is-cinematic-music-suno-ai-guide , https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/suno-sounds-ai-sound-effects-guide
- Hits/transitions guide: describe each effect as type + direction + weight + duration + job + cleanliness (tight, no muddy low end). Whooshes for movement, impacts for cut points, risers into a transition, reveal accents for a logo. https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/ai-art-visuals-creatives/cinematic-hits-and-transitions-suno-sounds-guide
- Community tips (r/SunoAI roundups): put [Instrumental] at the start; treat structure as a timeline. Not relevant to the lyrics box here (it stays empty), but supports describing the 15 s as a sequence in the style box. https://medium.com/@aitooldiscovery/suno-reddit-tips-and-prompts-what-r-sunoai-users-actually-do-65b59d51bcad
- Car horn advice (stock library, not Suno-specific): describe the press, not the horn: one tap, two short taps, held note; a horn with a pitch slide reads as passing, one pitch reads as parked. Useful for "cinematic, not annoying". https://morphic.com/resources/sounds/car-horn-sound-effect
- Horror/suspense scoring: the sting (timing matters most), sforzando string stabs (Herrmann, Psycho), dissonant scare chords, tremolo and harmonics, silence just before a stab, and music box / lullaby timbres that unsettle by evoking childhood. Maps directly onto Kai's "quick jab on a violin ... meek little child with a little pattern of foreboding music". https://theconversation.com/what-makes-a-film-score-frightening-expert-explains-the-techniques-that-build-tension-and-make-us-jump-216121 , https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Main/ScareChord
- Drink-drive PSA precedent (UK THINK!): pub music slowing to a crawl and dropping to dead silence at the reveal; party tracks used ironically ("Celebration"); Y&R London "A mate doesn't let a mate drink drive" turns a pub singalong into the rescue. Silence and ironic party music are both proven moves. https://www.gigwise.com/videos/95963/ , https://www.thedrum.com/creative-works/project/yr-london-think-mate-doesn-t-let-mate-drink-drive
