# terrace-chant-collapse: what shaped it

- **Terrace chant character** (melodigging football-chant genre page): unison crowd voices stay primary, handheld/bass drum, claps and stomps, brass bands doubling the tune, roughly 90-120 bpm, short loopable phrases. Hence "gang vocals in rough unison", "big bass drum and stomping", "supporters' brass band", 105 bpm. https://www.melodigging.com/genre/football-chant
- **Crowd vocals are hard in Suno**: guides pair stadium chants with brass stabs and stomp-clap, and a Suno playlist example uses "unison crowd shouting, gang vocals". So the style leads with "gang vocals" and the lyrics carry a [Gang Vocals: ...] tag. https://suno.bi/en/blog/how-to-make-phonk-football-anthem-2026 , https://suno.com/playlist/b3233b95-16d6-449a-a39c-63afc22f5f81 , https://www.itechguides.com/?p=860447
- **Spoken line**: one tutorial claims that putting the spoken phrase in parentheses after a [Spoken Word] tag stops Suno singing it (single creator's claim, unverified). Used for the masked figure's line. https://roo.beehiiv.com/p/suno-commands
- **Tags are hints, Style sets the whole sound, earliest tokens weigh most**: the style opens with the chant and brass, then lists the events in the order they happen. https://jackrighteous.com/en-gb/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/suno-ai-4-5-metatag-tutorial , https://jackrighteous.com/en-us/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/suno-v6-ignoring-prompt-how-to-diagnose
- **Tape stop**: I found no source saying Suno honours "tape stop"; it is in both style and a lyric tag as a bet, followed by [Silence] so the dead air lands even if the effect does not. Unverified.
- **Settings**: v6 (controlled, not Wild) at 15 s as fixed; weirdness 62 to let the chant fracture and the comic sounds in; styleInfluence 70 so the brass-and-chant palette holds; vocalGender null because the cast is a male crowd and one woman; variety normal.
- No artist or club names in the style (Suno refuses artist names); the supporters'-band sound is described instead.
