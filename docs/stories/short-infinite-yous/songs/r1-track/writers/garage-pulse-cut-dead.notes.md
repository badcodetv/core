# garage-pulse-cut-dead: what shaped it

- **Groove words.** 2-step sits around 130-135 bpm, with a broken (not four-on-the-floor) kick and swung sixteenth hats carrying the shuffle. So the style names 132 bpm, "skippy swung sixteenth hats, broken kick", and the exclude bans four-on-the-floor house, which UKG prompts drift into. Sources: https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/2-step_garage , https://beatstorapon.com/blog/how-to-make-uk-garage-beats-2-step-swing/ , https://musicradar.com/how-to/uk-garage-tutorial
- **Genre and era go first** in the style box, because community guides say Suno weights early style tokens more (https://jackrighteous.com/en-gb/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/advanced-prompt-techniques). The delivery notes come after. Unverified as an official rule.
- **Spoken, not sung.** Guides say a [Spoken Word]-type tag on its own line steers the line toward dialogue. They also say style-box delivery notes are only partly reliable, and that the shape of the lyric matters more (https://roo.beehiiv.com/p/suno-commands , https://sunowatermark.com/blog/suno-v6-vocal-prompts-2026/). So each line gets its own [Spoken: ...] tag naming the voice. The style box repeats "spoken word, not rapped, not sung", and the exclude bans singing, rap flow and autotune. I left out the parentheses some guides suggest, because the brief allows only square-bracket additions.
- **The cut to silence.** [Pause] and silence tags are a hint, not a guaranteed duration. Exact silence is an edit job (https://jackrighteous.com/ja/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/master-pauses-silence-in-suno-ai-pause-intro-silence-song-flow). So the hard stop is said in plain words in two places, the [Hard stop: dead silence] tag and "hard stop into total silence" in the style box. If the silence leaks, gate it in the edit. The 15 s Duration is set as the brief fixes it.
- **The muffled club door** is written as "low-pass filtered" plus "street air", so the opening sounds like it's outside the club, not in it.
- **Settings.** v6 (fixed); variety normal and weirdness 45 leave some room for the glitch section without losing the genre; styleInfluence 70 holds the 2-step; vocalGender male (both voices are men); maxMode off.
