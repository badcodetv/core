# lab-theremin-documentary: what shaped it

- **Theremin as the "lab" sound.** It became the standard 1950s sci-fi sound (Rocketship X-M, The Day the Earth Stood Still). Its sound is close to a pure sine, and the warble comes from moving the hand. So the style box asks for "hand vibrato" glides, and on "only one" the arpeggio collapses into a pure sine tone: the theremin goes back to its plainest form. Sources: https://reverb.com/news/the-sound-of-early-sci-fi-samuel-hoffmans-theremin, https://electronics.howstuffworks.com/gadgets/audio-music/theremin.htm, https://www.perfectcircuit.com/signal/theremin-history
- **Where things go.** Bracket tags in the lyrics box set the order and the delivery ([Spoken], [Whisper]-type cues). The sound description goes in the Style box, comma-separated. Sound-effect cues are more reliable in the Style box than as lyric tags, so the door thunk and the horn appear in both. Sources: https://roo.beehiiv.com/p/suno-commands, https://sunowatermark.com/blog/suno-prompts-guide-2026/
- **Delivery.** Delivery instructions in the Style box only "sometimes work" on v6. The shape of the lyrics is the stronger lever. So each line gets its own speaker tag right above it, and the Style box says "no singing" while the exclude box bans sung melody, rap and choir. Source: https://sunowatermark.com/blog/suno-v6-vocal-prompts-2026/
- **Settings.** v6 rather than Wild, so the two spoken lines come out clean. Weirdness 55 leaves room for odd lab textures. Style influence 70 holds the theremin and Geiger palette. Male vocal, because both speakers are men. 15 s is fixed by the brief.
- Unverified: whether v6 voices two distinct speakers from tags alone. No source confirmed it for v6.
