## What it is
A BadCode short (9:16, 15 s). A drunk lad at night, car keys in hand: "One version of me gets home fine." Versions of
him split across the screen; the BadCode figure in a lab coat grabs the dividing lines and squeezes them into the one
original ("There's only one."), kicks him into a cab; the logo is lit by taxi headlights. The point: there is one of
you, not a multiverse; "infinite yous, so nothing matters" is the excuse. State: rough cut 4 frozen
(`scenes/infinite-yous/final.json`) and on Drive; figure voice, Premiere build and 1080x1920 export still owed.


**Paused** (Jack and Kai, 17 Nov morning call, [notes](../../misc/2026-11-17-morning-meeting.md)): the hardest to bring up to
quality, and the taxi ride home is too boring. Comes back later as a series, not one short; to be talked about at the pub.

## Story-only rules
- No lyrics in the Suno tracks: music and effects only.
- Never run Suno and Flow at the same time in the one browser (download clash).
- Flow refuses "kick" and violent words: name the leg's path as ordinary movement (dancer, penalty swing, doorman), "nobody hurt", action in the first second, then a hold.
- In Omni clips the figure must stay in profile; when he turns to camera the mask fails. Check the triangle zoomed in on every still and clip.
- The logo is never drawn by Flow: the official file over the plate, lit in the edit.
- He is drunk with keys: the short is against drink-driving; gatekeeper to confirm it reads that way. Taxi livery invented, generic "TAXI" sign.
- Never Flow speech that ends up audible: talking clips said "Thanks" (the prompt sign-off) after the line; mute them and dub.
