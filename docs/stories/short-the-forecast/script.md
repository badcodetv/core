# The Forecast: script draft 1

Claude's draft (2026-11-21). Lines marked `(joke, claude)` are Claude's first go, for Jack and Kai to rewrite. ~20 s.

1. **The forecast (7 s).** A TV weather set; BadCode in the balaclava, smart suit, clicker in a gloved hand, beside a
   big UK map with cartoon clouds and arrows.
   - PRESENTER: "Prices rising across the south. Wages staying cold in the north."
   - PRESENTER: "And a front of efficiency savings moving in from Westminster." *(joke, claude)*
   - A cloud labelled RENT drifts onto London.
2. **The reveal (5 s).** The camera pulls back past the edge of the set: just off-screen, a man in a suit is crouched
   with a desk fan, blowing the clouds across the map. He sees the camera. He keeps blowing.
   - PRESENTER (not looking): "Expect it to continue." *(joke, claude)*
3. **The way out (5 s).** The map again, but every cloud now has a name tag: THE TREASURY on the "cuts" cloud, a
   tag on the RENT cloud, and so on. The man with the fan edges the fan behind his back.
   - PRESENTER: "Forecast's the same. Now you know who's holding the fan."
4. **The button (2 s).** *(joke, claude)* The presenter takes the fan off him and turns it round: the clouds blow back
   onto the man in the suit; his hair blows everywhere.
5. Logo.
