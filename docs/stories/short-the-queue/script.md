# The Queue: script draft 1

Claude's draft (2026-11-21). Lines marked `(joke, claude)` are Claude's first go at the jokes, for Jack and Kai to
rewrite. ~18 s.

1. **The queue (4 s).** A counter with a sign: POWER. A plain queue: a mum with a toddler and a kettle, a pensioner
   with an electric heater under her arm, a lad holding a phone at 1%. Everyone patient.
2. **The barge (4 s).** The door bursts open: a data centre the size of a lorry, humming, fans whirring, lights
   blinking, rolls straight past the queue to the front. Its little screen flashes: DISCOUNT APPLIED.
   - PENSIONER: "There's a queue, love." *(joke, claude)*
3. **The bouncer (5 s).** The bouncer at the counter (BadCode in the balaclava) looks the data centre up and down.
   - BOUNCER: "Brilliant machine. Wrong queue." *(the Energy minister's own line, from the policy)*
   - He points it to the back. It trundles off, sulking; its fans wind down.
4. **The way out (3 s).** The mum steps up; the kettle plugs in; it boils.
   - BOUNCER: "Households first."
   - [JOKE: the button. Angle: the data centre at the back of the queue, doing something small and human: sighing,
     its screen now reading 'PLEASE HOLD', or the lad with 1% plugs his phone into it.]
5. Logo.
