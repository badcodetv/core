# The Queue

A BadCode short (Jack, 2026-11-21: "The Queue lets do this"), one of eight drawn from the BadCode government's policies.

**The idea:** a family queues for power; a huge data centre barges to the front; the bouncer sends it to the back.
"Households first."

**The point:** ministers plan to give big AI data centres cheaper power than you pay; in a shortage, homes should keep
their power first, and big new loads should switch down first and pay for their own wires.
**The way out:** the BadCode government's policy `energy-climate/households-first`
([`../../government/policies/energy-climate/households-first.md`](../../government/policies/energy-climate/households-first.md)).
