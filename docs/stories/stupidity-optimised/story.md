# Stupidity Optimised

Jack's seed: [`docs/ideas/stupidity-optimised.md`](../../ideas/stupidity-optimised.md). Research and
every ending considered: [`docs/misc/2026-10-31-stupidity-optimised-research.md`](../../misc/2026-10-31-stupidity-optimised-research.md).

**Rulings so far (Jack, 2026-10-31):** South Park-style skits. The ending is intelligence people making an
agent that can talk to people and be an asset, then killing him. The reason they kill him: his vlog shows
his handler in the back of shot.

## Key concept

What actually works is free, so someone has to sell you something else. Once it's free and it works, you
are what's being sold. *(Hypothesis. Never spoken in the film.)*

**The carrying object:** the Allow button. A running gag of small permissions, and then, in the same
gesture from another man's thumb, a kill order.

**The pun:** an AI agent makes a human agent. It can profile anyone, and it can't sit in a pub.

## Background

- Young men in the UK really are alone a lot: over two-fifths of waking hours (Resolution Foundation).
- The prophets are composites of real, checkable absurdities (see the research file). No real person
  is named or drawn.
- Unwitting recruits: the Leyton warehouse case (UK men recruited over Telegram, some reportedly unaware
  who it was for) and the spycops (undercover police in over 1,000 groups, by reported figures).
- AI for spotting and pitching to sources is a real think-tank proposal, not a shown practice.
- The UK data-centre water row is real. The site in the film is fictional and explicitly water-cooled.

**Never claim a real service does this.** The contractor, agency and app are all invented.

## Beats

- **Cold open.** The narrator promises that this man actually matters.
- **Act 1, the prophets.** Each disaster causes the next: arm, ambulance, flood, apology, dates, jaw,
  course, holy war. An ad finds him at his lowest.
- **Act 2, it works.** Sleep, then everything, each with a small Allow. A mate, a door that opens, a
  community, love. **Midpoint:** the best day of his life. His journal entry leaves the phone and lands in
  an office. Act 2 replays with its real labels.
- **Act 3, the asset.** Daily goals sink the campaign, and he's proud of each. He starts a vlog to help
  other lads. The handler is in the back of every shot. The AI is the only real viewer: it flags itself.
  A bored man taps Allow. Accidents fail because the app made him too well. So the handler makes a shake.
- **The end.** Same ambulance, same shot, same shake.
- **Coda.** Misadventure. A full funeral. His scheduled videos keep posting, and the neighbour spots the man
  in the back of them. The town wins something, the gilet man gets promoted. New lad, same advice.

## Who the jokes land on

Contempt goes to the sellers: the prophets, the contractor, the gilet man. Brandon is an idiot and never a
loser, he is trying, and his stupidity is what finally exposes them. **Departure from a guide, on purpose:**
`narrator.md` says humans are "never stupid". The title and character require this one, and Kai hasn't
ruled on it. The neighbour and the paramedics are the competent, funny adults, and no joke lands on them.
