# The two paramedics

**Sophie** (named by Jack) is the one who talks to him: "Put that protein shake down, love." The other still
needs a name. Unflappable, kind, and increasingly fond of him. They greet him by name by the second
ambulance. They buy his course out of pity, and miss him when he's well. They're the only people who could
know that his last injury isn't how he breaks things.
