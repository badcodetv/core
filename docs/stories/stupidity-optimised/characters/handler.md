# The handler

*(Name is Jack's.)* Comes into Brandon's life as his "accountability partner". A bad liar on a zero-hours
contract, believed only because Brandon believes everyone. He's alone too: Brandon's the nearest thing he has to
a mate, and he makes the shake anyway, for an ordinary reason. He gets the film's one scene from the other side.
