# r4a ideas: research notes

- Rushed vocals in Suno come mostly from syllable density: too many syllables for the bars available, so each one gets squeezed. Cutting words is one fix. Moving phrase placement, adding rests or spreading a phrase across more beats also works and leaves the lyric unchanged. That matters here because the words are locked.
  https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/music-creation-process-guide/syllable-density-flow-for-ai-songs-prevent-rushed-ai-vocals
- For v6 specifically, the style field governs voice identity (timbre, register, accent) and the lyric field governs performance (phrasing, timing, sustain). Delivery instructions in the style box sometimes work but are less reliable, so use them to refine, not as the only fix.
  https://sunowatermark.com/blog/suno-v6-vocal-prompts-2026/
- Wording that helps pace: "laid-back", "relaxed pacing", "unhurried", "patient delivery", "lingering". Avoid speed words such as "fast-paced", "driving", "energetic" and "rapid". Ellipses in the lyric can signal a pause. Don't mix conflicting directions like "slow" with "rapid-fire".
  https://hookgenius.app/learn/fix-suno-rushed-lyrics/
- State tempo as a number, not an adjective. Keep the descriptor count focused, because piling on production elements can make Suno push the vocal harder.
  https://medium.com/@aitooldiscovery/suno-reddit-tips-and-prompts-what-r-sunoai-users-actually-do-65b59d51bcad
  https://roo.beehiiv.com/p/suno-ai-prompt-guide-2026-copy-paste-templates-the-formula-that-actually-works
- Generations vary, so judge pace across several takes of the same prompt, not one.
  https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/music-creation-process-guide/syllable-density-flow-for-ai-songs-prevent-rushed-ai-vocals
