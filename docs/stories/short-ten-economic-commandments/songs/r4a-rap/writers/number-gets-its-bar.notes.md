# number-gets-its-bar: notes (redo)

- The redo failed because other writers kept the same phrases from the kept box. So the whole Style box is now in new words, with the same meaning and in the same order: genre, then the MC's bebop-solo phrasing, then voice, then beat. "quick clusters" is now "loose clusters". The 88 BPM stays. The only new idea is the countdown clause near the end. It says a chopped-up shout calls each number alone, then a full bar of drums and loop plays, and the rapper starts on the following downbeat.
- The pace is fixed with structure, not by going slower. Community guides say bar-count and instrumental tags give Suno room before a vocal comes in. They also say blank lines in the lyrics make pauses. That is why every rule has its own tag, a bracketed "number, then one bare bar of beat" cue and a blank line around it.
- In Suno V6, bracket cues are suggestions, not commands, and plain-language description in the style box carries the intent. So the bar-of-space idea goes in both the box and the lyric cues.
- Early words in the Style box carry the most weight, so the genre and voice stay at the front. The box stays under 1,000 characters.
- Settings: v6, 75 s, male, weirdness 25, style influence 80, variety normal, maxMode off. The exclude list keeps the rushed-delivery terms out.
