# lazy-behind-the-beat: notes (redo)

- Redo after the shared-wording check. The previous draft had reworded the voice and the barked-number clauses, and that rewording overlapped with other writers. This draft keeps those clauses word for word from the kept box, as the brief asks, and changes only three things:
  1. The drum-solo simile becomes "phrases his verse like a tenor sax strolling home".
  2. "then tumbled out in quick clusters" becomes "stretched and leaned on".
  3. Added: "unhurried, patient delivery, every line finishes before the next number".
- I left out tempo words. The 88 BPM swung breakbeat, the instruments and "no hook" are unchanged. The box is about 925 characters, under the 950 limit.
- Research: the hip-hop prompt guides say to name where the rapper sits against the beat (behind it, laid-back, conversational), and that the lyric has to leave room to breathe. That is why the lyric is the locked words with plain section tags, one rule per section, so each number starts a new block.
- Exclude: "crammed bars" in place of "double-time flow", so no tempo word appears.
- Settings: v6, 75 s, male, weirdness 50, style influence 65, variety normal. Guides warn that higher Variety can drift away from the style direction.
