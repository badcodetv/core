# Research notes for round 3 ideas

- The original track has no chorus and drops the usual 16-bar verse: it is a numbered list, each rule followed by a short witty explanation. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ten_Crack_Commandments
- Its beat is built on a brief guitar slide taken from a 1977 jazz-piano record ("Vallarta"), and it was the last song recorded for its album; the numbers are chopped from a shouted voice sample from another group's record. https://uk.news.yahoo.com/dr-dre-mobb-deep-massive-131418686.html , https://r5gnd.netlify.app/ten-crack-commandments-sample (low-quality mirror, treat with care)
- A tempo database gives the track about 90 BPM (AI-estimated, not verified); boom-bap usually sits at 80-100 BPM. https://musiciwant.com/song/ten-crack-commandments-the-notorious-big
- Suno does not count syllables: it fits a line to a phrase length that suits the style and speeds up delivery when there are too many words. Lyric shape governs timing; the style box governs character. https://sunowatermark.com/blog/suno-v6-vocal-prompts-2026/ , https://popularaitools.ai/blog/suno-v6-vocal-prompts-expression-phrasing-2026
- To slow rushed delivery: words like "unhurried", "laid-back", "patient delivery", "relaxed pacing", and "space" or "breathing room" next to any high-energy words; avoid "fast-paced", "driving", "uptempo". Pauses can be written with ellipses or bracket directions. https://hookgenius.app/learn/fix-suno-rushed-lyrics/
- Delivery wording in the style box is less reliable than lyric shape; change one variable at a time; standard v6 is the more controllable model. https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/suno-v6-ignoring-prompt-how-to-diagnose
- Implication for this round: the words are locked, so pacing must come from bracket tags in the lyric field (a bar of beat after each barked number, a breath before each line) plus style wording that removes "quick clusters" and asks for space. "More like the original" means a sparse, ominous loop around a short jazz-guitar slide stab, hard drums, numbers barked by a separate sampled voice, no hook.
