# lyric-field-carries-the-timing: notes (redo)

- Redo: the vocal line and the between-rules chant line got new wording ("a giant of a man, a plush low baritone sitting down in the ribcage, slow lazy Brooklyn vowels"; "worn tape hiss with vinyl crackle; after each rule a raspy chopped-up crew chant yells out the next number"). That clears the n-grams the check flagged ("deep smooth baritone from the chest", "huge man deep", "tape hiss and crackle in the", "shout calls out"). Everything else stays as in the previous draft.
- The style box keeps the jazz1 box, with only the two changes the experiment asks for: "tumbled out in quick clusters" becomes "placed in slow, even clusters with room around them", and "ten numbered rules, each given room" is added. Timing goes in the lyric field. Each rule gets its own section, opens with a one-bar beat-only cue, and has the number barked and then a breath, so every "Number ..." can land alone on beat one.
- The guides say Suno reads bracket tags as suggestions, not commands, and that one or two cues per section is enough. So each section carries two cues at most, plus the "Uno" stress cue in Rule 1. The same sources say Suno infers structure from line breaks and that hip-hop flow follows how the lyrics are laid out. That is why there is a blank line and a fresh section before each rule.
- An [Intro] tag on its own can be ignored, so it has a delivery cue under it. The [half-time drop, beat breathes] tag goes before the fix so the last line slows down, and [beat rides out] and [End] close the song.
- The Exclude field is the official control for unwanted elements, and negative words in Style are unreliable. So "double-time flow, rushed delivery" goes in Exclude to deal with Jack's "says the line too fast".
- Settings: v6, 75 s, male, as the story fixes them. Variety is off and weirdness 35 to stay near jazz1. styleInfluence is 70 so the box holds while the lyric tags set the timing.
