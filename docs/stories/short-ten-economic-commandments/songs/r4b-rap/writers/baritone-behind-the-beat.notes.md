# baritone-behind-the-beat: notes

What shaped it:

- **Style box order and wording.** The v6 guidance at undetectr says to put the vocal early, to describe the voice by what it does ("laying each line a hair behind the snare", "letting the beat breathe") instead of piling up adjectives, to give each instrument one plain line, and to spell out the arrangement (the loop holds, drum-only gaps, the beat rides out at the end), because the sliders don't control arrangement. The box is about 690 characters, well under 1,000. https://undetectr.com/blog/suno-v6-prompts
- **Exclude rather than negation.** The same guide says that naming an unwanted thing in the style box puts it into play. So trap hats, auto-tune, sung hooks, double-time and 808s go only in Exclude, as short category words, fewer than ten of them.
- **Variety off, Style Influence 80, Weirdness 25.** Suno's FAQ, as quoted there, says Variety rewrites style tags and that at 0 the style tags stay as written. Off keeps the loop and tempo steady. 80/25 holds the model close to the brief without making it stiff. Max Mode is off because this is an exploratory take.
- **Boom bap parts and pocket.** Suno's own genre page calls boom bap "drum-first", with short repeating bass or chord fragments. It says the MC should fit between the kick and the snare and use deliberate pauses. That is the reason for the two-chord piano chop, the two-bar bass loop, and a "[Two-bar drum break, then]" before every rule, so each "Number ..." lands on a fresh downbeat. https://suno.com/music-library/genres/boom-bap
- **Lo-fi texture.** The 12-bit drum grit and the sparse, loop-led arrangement follow the general boom bap references. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boom_bap , https://create.routenote.com/blog/?p=12433
- **Section tags.** There is a short cue at the top of each section, worded to agree with the style box (contradictions thin the arrangement out). Rules 7 and 9 open with a word instead of "Number", so they get "slower, deliberate" to keep them from being rushed. The lyric words are unchanged. Only bracketed tags were added.
- **Settings fixed by the story:** v6, 75 s, male.
