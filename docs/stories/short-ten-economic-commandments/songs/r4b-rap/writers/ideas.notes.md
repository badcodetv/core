# Research notes for the inspiration version (r4b)

- Boom bap sits about 90-100 BPM; kick and a heavy, compressed snare on 2 and 4; loops chopped from funk, soul and jazz records; upright bass samples recur; the MC carries the rhythmic variation while the loop stays steady; deliveries range from laid-back to loud. https://theshfl.com/guide/1990s-boom-bap
- Boom bap = exposed kick ("boom") and hard snare ("bap") over sparse sample-based production; East Coast golden age, peak roughly mid-1990s. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boom_bap , https://repository.uwl.ac.uk/5150/1/Boom%20Bap%20ex%20Machina%20Hip%20Hop%20Aesthetics%20and%20the%20Akai%20MPC.pdf
- Suno v6 Styles: order roughly main genre, rhythmic feel, two or three signature instruments, vocal role, mood, production texture; pair a BPM with a feel word (e.g. 78 BPM slow groove, 84 BPM relaxed pocket); describe voice by register, delivery and presence, never by naming a singer; unwanted traits go in Exclude, not as "no X" in Styles; Variety toward 0 keeps output nearer the wording. https://jackrighteous.com/es/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/optimize-suno-v6-style-of-music-prompt
- Community boom-bap style examples: dusty drums, jazzy piano sample, vinyl crackle, punchy snare, storytelling pace, head-nod groove, 88 BPM; exclude trap hi-hats and auto-tune; Suno blocks many artist names, so describe flow in words; style text must stay under the 1,000-character counter. https://medium.com/@aitooldiscovery/suno-reddit-tips-and-prompts-what-r-sunoai-users-actually-do-65b59d51bcad , https://jackrighteous.com/pages/top-music-genres-create-hip-hop-rnb-suno , https://github.com/mttkllr/suno-field-guide
- Delivery cues in Styles are hit and miss; they hold better as bracket directions in the lyrics right before the line they affect. https://medium.com/@aitooldiscovery/suno-reddit-tips-and-prompts-what-r-sunoai-users-actually-do-65b59d51bcad
- Lyric tags: [Break] = short pause/instrumental gap; [Spoken] before short spoken text; community lists include [Slow Flow], [Boom Bap Flow]; v6 tends to shrink undefined pauses to about a second, so ask for gaps explicitly (e.g. "two-bar drum break"); tags are suggestions, do not stack many. https://niller2005.github.io/suno/guide/meta-tags.html , https://hookgenius.app/learn/suno-metatags-complete-list/ , https://dev.to/promptmaster/suno-metatags-the-structure-tricks-most-people-never-find-3cad
- Implication for "he says the line too fast": slower BPM (82-90), a half-time or behind-the-beat pocket, one rule per short section with an explicit beat gap before each "Number ...", and pacing cues placed in the lyric brackets rather than only in Styles.
