# weathered-ledger-reader: notes (redo)

- The redo rewrites the whole Style box in new words. The old closing sentence ("Voice upfront and close, music tucked underneath...") matched other prompts in the round, so the ending now describes the groove emptying to one Rhodes chord before his closing remark.
- Guides say to give subgenre, BPM, drum character, bass, mood, then vocal flow, in that order. The Style box follows that order (undetectr hip-hop guide).
- Boom-bap guides advise naming the head-nod groove and keeping trap hi-hats and auto-tune out. I added 808 glides, double-time rap and melodic singing to Exclude so the slow, half-spoken read stays clear.
- Community references say v6 bracket tags are suggestions, not commands, and short tags hold best. So the tags stay short ([Slow Flow], [Break], [Spoken, ...]), and the Style box also asks in plain words for a pause before every numbered rule. That answers Jack's note that "he says the line too fast".
- The lyric words are unchanged. Only the bracket directions differ from the last draft.
