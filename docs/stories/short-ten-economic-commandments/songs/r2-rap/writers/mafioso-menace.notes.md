# mafioso-menace (redo) — what shaped it

Redo after the sameness gate flagged "raising his voice" as shared with another prompt. Only that
phrase changed: the calm-authority idea is now "volume level and low the whole time like a man who
has never needed to shout". Tempo set to 90 BPM (one AI-analysed listing puts the original at ~90;
unverified, single source). Everything else stands from the first draft.

- Mood target: the 1997 album's mafioso/crime-film register, sparse and dark, a rulebook read with
  calm menace rather than shouted.
- Beat: dusty sampled jazz keys loop (the original's keys reportedly come from a 1960s jazz record,
  blog source, unverified), hard boom-bap kick and snare, tape hiss, mono. Described in features
  only; no sample, loop or melody reproduced.
- The count between rules: the original's numbers are a scratched-in voice sample (the subject of a
  1998 settled lawsuit over the sampled voice), so here it is an original gruff barked count cut in
  with a scratch, not the original sample.
- No artist, song or producer names used.
