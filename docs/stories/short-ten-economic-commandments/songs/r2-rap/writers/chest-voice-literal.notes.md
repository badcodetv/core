# chest-voice-literal: notes

Experiment: the most literal portrait of the voice, music asked for plainly, low weirdness and high style influence to pin Suno to the era.

What shaped it:

- **Voice words.** NPR's piece on the rapper's voice calls it "plummy, wheezy, humid" and says it seems to come from deeper in the chest than most voices; flow was his main asset. Those exact timbre words went into the style box, along with "audible breaths" (from "wheezy") and "on the beat". A Revolt list of deep-voiced rappers calls his delivery a grovel/grumble, which gave me "thick laid-back grumble".
  - https://www.npr.org/2010/08/02/128916682/biggie-smalls-the-voice-that-influenced-a-generation (search snippet only; the page timed out)
  - https://www.revolt.tv/article/2024-01-15/347754/16-rappers-with-best-deep-voices
- **Beat.** The original is built on a dark 1977 jazz-piano sample, has no chorus and isn't in 16-bar verses (it's a list), and puts a scratched, shouted counting voice between the rules (the producer scratched it, and only up to five). So I asked for a short dark jazz-piano stab, no chorus, and a turntable-scratched shouted number. In line with the parody rule, I didn't name or describe the actual sample, melody or counting source.
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ten_Crack_Commandments
  - https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-lists/dj-premier-my-life-in-15-songs-630261/
  - https://amp.barstoolsports.com/blog/3365431/{{ url }}
- **Tempo.** The only figure I found is 90 BPM, and it's an AI estimate (https://musiciwant.com/song/ten-crack-commandments-the-notorious-big). I used it, but it is unverified.
- **Excludes.** These ban what pulls Suno away from 1997: trap hats, 808s, autotune, sung hooks, a polished modern mix. I also excluded "shouting rapper" so the menace stays quiet.
- **Lyrics.** The words are exactly as given. I only added bracket tags: a scratch cue before each number, a spoken intro, and an outro where the beat drops out. 20 s is tight for eight lines, so it has no extra sections.
- **Settings.** v6, variety off, weirdness 15, style influence 85, male, 20 s, max mode off.
