# r1-rap research notes (2026-11-16)

What the web says, for a parody of the 1997 countdown song's beat, style and voice on Suno v6. Artist, song and producer names are kept out of every prompt.

## The original song (what to evoke, never copy)
- No chorus and no 16-bar verses: a numbered list, each rule followed by a short witty explanation. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ten_Crack_Commandments
- The beat was first a radio promo for a "top five at nine" countdown show; the producer took the "ten" from a spaceship countdown. https://www.complex.com/music/2013/06/dj-premier-the-combat-jack-show-ten-crack-commandments , https://hypebeast.com/2011/1/jeru-the-damaja-10-crack-commandments-original-hot-97-promo
- The numbers between rules are a sampled voice counting one to nine (from another group's record), which led to a lawsuit settled in 1998. So the counted numbers must be OUR vocalist or a new voice, never a sample-like lift. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ten_Crack_Commandments
- Unreliable pages describe it as a steady boom-bap beat with sharp snares and subtle scratches; one lists a jazz record ("Vallarta") as a second sample. Treat as unverified. https://www.shazam.com/el-gr/song/1876498554/10-commandments

## The voice (describe, never name)
- Critics: deep, gravelly; "precision of delivery"; mellow on guest verses; early work in a slightly higher register than later, "searching for the frayed edges of his voice". https://singingcarrots.com/learn-to-sing-like/The%20Notorious%20B.I.G. , https://www.vice.com/en/article/notorious-big-ready-to-die-25th-anniversary-essay/ , https://www.npr.org/2010/08/02/128916682/biggie-smalls-the-voice-that-influenced-a-generation
- A rhythm count of one of his verses found him landing on 60 of 72 beats: mostly ON the beat, not lazily behind it. Laid-back in tone, precise in time. https://rapanalysis.com/?p=100
- Vocal-imitation sites (low quality) say deep, resonant, relaxed, conversational. https://soundcy.com/article/how-to-sound-like-biggie

## Suno craft
- Boom bap ~85-95 BPM; name one signature sound (e.g. jazzy loop + vinyl crackle) or Suno modernises it; 6-10 tags, first tags weigh most; put "rapped, spoken cadence, no melody" in style; [Rapped] before verses. https://hookgenius.app/learn/suno-rap-prompts/ , https://hookgenius.app/learn/suno-hip-hop-prompts/
- Vocal type belongs in the Style field (works better than in lyrics). Stack timbre + delivery + emotion + mic/room in place of an artist anchor. Exclude "Singing", "Melodic Vocals" to keep it rap; 2-3 excludes max. Ad-libs inline in brackets, 2-3 per verse; parentheses = backing vocals. https://github.com/mttkllr/suno-field-guide
- Raspy baritone / dry close-mic baritone spoken rap appear as working vocal descriptors in community prompts. https://medium.com/@abhisheksd2003/the-ultimate-suno-ai-prompt-guide-with-clear-tested-examples-2d827ffe8b3a , https://howtopromptsuno.com/making-music/specific-vocal-styles
- v6 (Sept 2026): Suno claims more controllable vocals and better prompt adherence; user reports say adherence varies and vocals can come out buried, so ask for a dry, upfront vocal. https://the-decoder.com/suno-launches-v6-music-models-built-with-warner-bmg-and-believe/ , https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/suno-v6-reviews-problems-whats-next
