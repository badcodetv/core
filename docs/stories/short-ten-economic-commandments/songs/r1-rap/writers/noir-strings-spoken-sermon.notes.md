# noir-strings-spoken-sermon (redo)

Rewritten after the sameness gate flagged shared lyric directions ("intro spoken low", "out spoken dry", "spoken low and"). The word "spoken" is now gone from every bracket. The sections are named in church terms instead: [Sermon], [Benediction], "pulpit murmur", "talked like scripture". That keeps the preacher idea and gives this prompt its own vocabulary.

What shaped it:
- Mafioso / NY crime rap is boom-bap drums under moody minor-key harmony, with orchestral strings for a noir feel. So: a violin loop plus a cello drone over a sparse kick and a dry snare. https://www.melodigging.com/genre/mafioso-rap , https://www.complex.com/music/2015/06/mafia-culture-influence-on-rap-music
- Film scores as a source for cinematic beats: https://blog.samplefocus.com/blog/the-influence-of-film-scores-on-beat-making-and-sound-design
- Suno delivery: delivery mode, gender, BPM and texture are the controls it follows reliably, and putting the voice description inside each section tag helps. The fine-grained reverb wording is a soft hint at best (vendor/community claims, unverified). https://hookgenius.app/learn/suno-metatags-complete-list/ , https://hookgenius.app/learn/suno-vocal-prompts/ , https://apipass.dev/blogs/7-pro-suno-prompt-hack
- No artist, song or producer is named. The voice is described by its traits: a cavernous bass baritone, Brooklyn vowels, slow, with a dry smirk.
