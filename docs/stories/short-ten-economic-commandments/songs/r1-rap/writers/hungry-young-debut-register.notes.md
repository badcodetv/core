# hungry-young-debut-register: notes

What shaped it:

- **Order by weight**: genre and era first, then vocal character, then delivery, then gear and mix; style kept under ~100 words. Community guides say Suno weights early tokens most and that long style boxes dilute. https://roo.beehiiv.com/p/the-suno-ai-prompts-list-that-actually-works-2026-genre-artist-style-and-use-case , https://medium.com/@aitooldiscovery/suno-reddit-tips-and-prompts-what-r-sunoai-users-actually-do-65b59d51bcad
- **Voice described, never named**: Suno blocks artist names; guides say describe age, timbre (nasal, raw) and delivery separately, and that rap needs an explicit rap-flow cue or Suno drifts melodic. Hence "rap only" in style and "melodic singing, sung chorus" in exclude. https://hookgenius.app/learn/suno-vocal-effects/ , https://roo.beehiiv.com/p/suno-artist-style-prompts
- **Gear words**: the 12-bit grit, swing and punch of golden-age boom-bap came from the SP-1200 (12-bit, ~26 kHz, no reconstruction filter, so aliasing) and the MPC60 (12-bit, famous swing). I used the sound words (12-bit, swung, aliased snare) instead of model names, so Suno doesn't fixate on a brand. https://blog.landr.com/sp-1200 , https://daily.redbullmusicacademy.com/2017/11/mpc-instrumental-instruments , https://attackmagazine.com/technique/character-crunch-replicating-the-sound-of-vintage-samplers/2
- **Exclude**: boom-bap guides suggest excluding trap hats and auto-tune. "Deep baritone" is banned so this take stays in its own higher register, away from the other writers' heavy-voice experiments. https://medium.com/@aitooldiscovery/suno-reddit-tips-and-prompts-what-r-sunoai-users-actually-do-65b59d51bcad
- **Parody, not copy**: there is no counting sample, no reference to the original beat and no melody quote. The piano stab and filtered bass are generic era tools. The numbers are spoken by the rapper inside the lyrics.
- **Settings**: Weirdness is 40, so the take stays in the era without getting strange. Style Influence is 72, so the gear words hold. Variety is normal and the model is plain v6, because Wild would wander off the 90s.

Unverified: whether v6 honours "gasping a quick breath at every line end". That comes from v5-era community tips, not a v6 test.
