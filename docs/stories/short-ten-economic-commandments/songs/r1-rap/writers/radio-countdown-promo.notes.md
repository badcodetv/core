# radio-countdown-promo — notes (redo)

Redo after the sameness gate flagged the lyric direction "shouts the number". Every bracket direction is reworded: the countdown lines now read `[Synth blip up] [Singer B, hype host: "Number N" / Singer A takes the line]`, and the intro/outro directions are new wording. Lyrics unchanged word for word.

What shaped it:
- The original beat began life as a radio promo for a Hot 97 countdown segment, with scratches counting down, before it became the 1997 song. So the bit is built as a station countdown: an ident, a hype host calling chart positions, a tape-stop sign-off. Scratches stay excluded so the parody does not copy the original's counting device; a synth blip does that job instead.

- Suno has no official duet syntax; v6 guides say give each voice a distinct identity in the style box ("Singer A" / "Singer B") and reuse the same labels in the lyric tags as a map, keep the second voice to short compact interjections, and do not have both voices say different words at once. Hence the host only gets the number, and the MC takes the rest. Voice assignment is not guaranteed (unverified for rap specifically).
