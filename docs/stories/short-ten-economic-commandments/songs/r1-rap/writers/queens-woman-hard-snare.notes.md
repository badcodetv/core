# queens-woman-hard-snare: what shaped it

- **Female MC as a described voice, not a name.** Profiles of the Queens/Brooklyn women MCs of that era single out a "slightly husky voice" and a "raspy, incisive, commanding" delivery; that is the vocal brief (low husky alto, clipped, biting). Sources: https://bkreader.com/2019/02/22/feb-22-black-history-maker-mc-lyte/ , https://codamusic.me/artists/mc-lyte (used for descriptors only; no name goes in the prompt).
- **Keep it rapped, not sung.** HookGenius reports Suno melodises verses unless the style asks for "rapped delivery, spoken cadence, no melody"; hence "spoken cadence, no melody" in style, and "singing, melodic hook" in exclude. Same page: boom bap 85-95 BPM, and trap tags override the vintage feel, so trap hi-hats / 808 rolls are excluded. https://hookgenius.app/learn/suno-rap-prompts/
- **Female rap pitch drift.** HookGenius notes Suno struggles with female rapper pitch; vocalGender is set to female and "male lead vocal" excluded so the male crew stays in the back. https://hookgenius.app/learn/suno-prompt-guide-2026/
- **Production spec.** The era's grit is credited to 12-bit samplers (SP-1200), breaks with natural swing, snares pushed forward, 86-100 BPM: "hard compressed drum break, cracking dry snare pushed loud, 12-bit sampler grit", 92 BPM. https://create.routenote.com/blog/?p=12433 , https://www.gearnews.com/boom-bap-beats-perfect-match/ , https://daily.redbullmusicacademy.com/2013/11/in-search-of-boom-bap
- **Style box order and length.** Genre first, then vocal, then instruments/production, kept under ~100 words; unwanted traits go in Exclude. https://jackrighteous.com/en-gb/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/where-to-put-your-suno-prompt-guide , https://medium.com/@aitooldiscovery/suno-reddit-tips-and-prompts-what-r-sunoai-users-actually-do-65b59d51bcad
- **Crew answering the numbers.** Guides say parentheses become backing/echo vocals, but the brief forbids adding words to the lyrics, so the crew is asked for in square-bracket directions ("crew shouts the number back") and in the style line instead. Departure from the experiment's "(parentheses)" wording, made to keep the lyrics word-exact; if the crew never appears, the fallback is parenthesised echoes of the numbers, which would add words. https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/stop-suno-adding-crowd-vocals-choirs-backing-voices
- **Settings.** v6 (fixed), 30 s (fixed). Variety "normal", weirdness 35, styleInfluence 70: tight enough to hold the boom-bap tags (guides say lower variety keeps tags), loose enough that she doesn't come out generic. 92 BPM leaves room to fit 13 lines in 30 s.
- **Parody, not copy.** No original beat, sample, melody or counting sample is asked for; the horn stab and crew shout are new devices standing in for the voice-count of the original. No artist, song or producer named.
