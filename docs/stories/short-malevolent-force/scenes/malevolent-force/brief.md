# The Malevolent Force: brief (beats agreed 2026-11-16)

**The sentence:** Inflation is not weather: someone decides who gets the new money first (whoever stands nearest the
printer), and you meet it last, at the till. The force is never seen and never one person.

**Who benefits:** whoever is in the back room with the printer. It is never shown: only its light, its blank notes on
the floor and the prices it leaves behind.

**Form:** one trip to the corner shop for bread, shot like a haunting. About 15 s plus the end card, 9:16. **No voice**
(Jack, 2026-11-16: "No voice, one end card"). Music and sound only; one line of text at the end, written by Jack.

**Look:** Jack's reference photo `reference/jack-r2-shop-doorway.png` (night, cheap colour film, fluorescent teal).
Stills are his round 2 picks. The board run came out darker and sharper than the photo; a softer/grainier pass can
be done in the grade.
