# siren-and-pulse: notes

What shaped it:

- The reference series' opening uses a distant air-raid siren as the warning that something is coming, later swallowed by industrial thumping that rises to join the wail. Hence: siren as lead, far off, then pushed nearer from 12 s. ([GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/silent-hill-opening/), [GameSpot, "Lessons From Silent Hill"](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/lessons-from-silent-hill/1100-6349148/))
- The ambient tracks are synthetic and sampled sound, manipulated, with heavy reverb, distortion/warping and a grainy lo-fi character; percussion is often sound effects used percussively; full-volume chaos interrupts quiet passages. Hence: "lo-fi grain", "long cold reverb", "rust and static", thuds that read as metal/tube flickers rather than a drum kit, one crashing hit after the quiet. ([VGMonline](https://vgmonline.net/silenthill/))
- The composer has said he chose industrial sound for its "cold and rusty" feeling. Hence "cold", "rust". (quoted via search summary of [deepwavegame write-up](https://deepwavegame.github.io/news/sound-of-silent-hill-akira-yamaoka-industrial-dread), which now 404s, unverified first-hand)
- Dark-ambient practice: a slow low-frequency pulse as a rhythmic anchor mimicking an anxious heartbeat; one sub layer, LFO-shaped; field-recording hums. Hence the 60 bpm sub throb and the fluorescent hum from 0 s. ([Sascha Ende, Low Energy Tension Vol. 7](https://ende.app/fr/song/13767-low-energy-tension-vol-7), [Native Instruments, sub bass](https://blog.native-instruments.com/sub-bass), [Melodigging, dark ambient](https://www.melodigging.com/genre/dark-ambient-2601))
- Round 1 reference ("Damned", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LGD79pQY1kI): page fetched, title only, no description. Avoided its piano/music-box shape entirely.
