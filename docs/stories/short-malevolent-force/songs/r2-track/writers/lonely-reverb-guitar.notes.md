# lonely-reverb-guitar: what shaped it

- **Feelings before instruments.** The composer of the reference says he set out to express "loneliness, sadness, anxiety and the psychological aspects of fear" rather than simply scary music, so the style box opens on mood (lonely, sorrowful, uneasy, too quiet) and only then names the guitar. https://www.musicradar.com/artists/rather-than-simply-writing-scary-music-i-wanted-to-express-loneliness-sadness-anxiety-and-the-psychological-aspects-of-fear-we-speak-to-video-game-horror-soundtrack-godfather-akira-yamaoka-about-the-legacy-of-silent-hill
- **Wet reverb, tremolo, arpeggios, lo-fi grain.** A review of the first soundtrack describes electric guitar arpeggios under a tremolo, guitars "drenched" in wet reverb, and a "distinctively lo-fi, grainy sound", plus non-melodic noise used like industrial percussion. That gave the wet spring reverb, the slow tremolo, "lo-fi, grainy", and the gritty distortion in the echo tail, which stands in for the industrial side without adding drums. https://vgmonline.net/silenthill/
- **Silence as an instrument.** Writing on the second game's score credits its use of silence for the isolation and sadness, so the prompt asks for long silences between notes. https://techraptor.net/gaming/features/why-silent-hill-soundtrack-deserves-much-more-credit (found through search; the page itself was not read)
- **Industrial and Reznor.** The composer names Trent Reznor as his main influence, and the score is built from guitar, ambient noise, dissonance and sirens. I kept this to the creeping distortion; sirens are another writer's job (siren-and-pulse). https://www.tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Music/AkiraYamaoka (search summary)
- **The picture.** About 15 to 17 s long. The sour bend halfway lands near the price-flicker loaf (6-10 s). The muted chord and ring-out cover the end card. No drums, so the flicker hits come from the bend and the grit, not from a beat.
- **Settings.** v6 at normal variety. Weirdness 45 to keep the bend and grit a little off-centre without losing the lone guitar. Style influence 75 holds the instrument line.
