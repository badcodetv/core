# rust-foundry-percussion: what shaped it

- What the reference sound is: the composer of that series' score was also its sound designer, so the environmental noise counts as part of the music. Much of the percussion is non-musical sound used as percussion, as in industrial music ("clanking pipes", "metal banging", "industrial drums"), with radio interference and mechanical noise around it. Sources: search summaries of https://vgmonline.net/silenthill/, https://twinfinite.net/2017/10/twinfinites-endless-playlist-silent-hill-2-soundtrack/, https://www.nme.com/?p=3959312, https://techraptor.net/gaming/features/why-silent-hill-soundtrack-deserves-much-more-credit (403 when fetched, so only the search snippet was read).
- Scrap Metal Archive (https://scrapmetalarchive.blogspot.com/2023/01) describes the ambient industrial pieces as "sequenced scrap metal", "machine samples" and "straight up metal percussion", and the search snippet calls their semi-percussive metal "ritual, hypnotic". That gave the "ritual and hypnotic" wording and the hammer-on-scrap lead.
- Found-metal sample libraries (ShamanStems *The Eerie Expanse*, https://www.adsrsounds.com/product/presets/shamanstems-the-eerie-expanse-ambient-sound-effects-halloween/; *Metal Worlds*, https://thesoundpacktree.com/sound-packs/MetalWorlds) show that this sound is made by recording objects from scrapyards in big spaces. So the prompt describes objects in a place (girder, oil drum, chain, pipe, foundry hall), not a genre.
- The lurching seven-against-four meter and the clangs getting louder and closer come from the brief: the flicker hits land on the louder strikes and the end card gets the heaviest hit, then a hard stop. Lo-fi tape hiss and mains hum stand in for the shop's fluorescent buzz.
- Round 1's reference (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LGD79pQY1kI): the fetched page gives only the title "Damned" and no description. Its piano and music box are excluded here, along with strings and a drum kit.
- Settings: v6, 17 s, variety high and weirdness 65 so it stays strange without turning into a song, style influence 75 to keep the objects literal. No game, composer or track is named in the prompt.
