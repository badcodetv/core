# inharmonic-vibraphone: notes

- The timbre words come from an honours thesis that analysed the series' ambient pieces spectrally: predominantly dark timbres, inharmonicity, beating harmonics, and spectral fullness from overlapping, clashing frequencies (https://ro.ecu.edu.au/theses_hons/1554). That is why the lead is detuned bars that beat, and the bed is built to thicken into a dense clash rather than a smooth pad.
- Beating needs a small gap: two bars a hertz apart beat once a second, and beyond ~15 Hz apart the ear hears two pitches instead of a pulse (https://physicslabs.colorado.edu/demos/oscillations-and-waves/wave-motion/beats/vibraphone-bars/, https://demonstrations.wolfram.com/BeatsWorking/). So the prompt asks for "a few cents off", not "out of tune". Close-pitch interference reads as grating (https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/how-composers-make-horror-movie-music-sound-terrifying/).
- The first game's score is industrial noise plus dark ambient, much of it percussion or sound effects used percussively, with machine samples (https://vgmonline.net/silenthill/, https://scrapmetalarchive.blogspot.com/2023/01/akira-yamaoka.html). Hence the grinding motor / bowed sheet metal bed under the bells, kept rhythm-free.
- Minimal, sketch-like, best heard loud (https://vaguely-offensive.com/music/akira-yamaoka-silent-hill-original-soundtrack/): sparse single notes with long silences suit a 17-second piece that has to leave room for three flicker hits and an end-card cluster.
- Suno craft (community guides, not official): instrumental stated in the box, genre and mood first because early tokens weigh more, short concrete instrument words (https://howtopromptsuno.com/style-lyrics/how-do-i-make-instrumental, https://roo.beehiiv.com/p/150-suno-ai-prompts-that-actually-work-in-2026).
- Settings: v6, 17 s as fixed. Variety normal and weirdness 65 to allow strange textures without losing the bells; styleInfluence 75 to hold the instrument list. Exclude adds piano, strings, choir and synth pad so the bells stay the lead and it does not slide into round 1's piano or trailer-strings directions. Game, composer and track names are kept out of the prompt.
- Round 1's "Damned" page was fetched (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LGD79pQY1kI): only the title came back; nothing taken from it.
