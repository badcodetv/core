# detuned-bell-piano: notes

What shaped it:

- The ambient half of that soundtrack is described as "dark" timbres with **inharmonicity and beating harmonics**, plus a fullness from clashing frequencies (ECU honours thesis "Silent chill", https://ro.ecu.edu.au/theses_hons/1554). Hence a bell-like electric piano asked to be *slightly out of tune with itself*: the beating between partials is the point, not a mistake.
- Reviewers separate the quiet ambient pieces (ominous, low melody, droning household sounds such as "a boiling kettle sound droning on constantly") from the loud industrial barrages (https://squareenixmusic.com/reviews/kerohazel/silenthill.shtml). This experiment takes the quiet, melancholy side only; the mains hum stands in for the domestic drone and fits a corner shop at 3am.
- A blog review hears a softly played, half-remembered piano in it (https://grimoireofhorror.com/?p=601, via search summary; not read in full).
- Suno craft (community guides, not official): short comma-separated descriptors, genre then mood then instruments then tempo; name the subtype and texture rather than a bare "ambient"; keep excludes to two or three or they fight the positive tags (https://jackrighteous.com/en-gb/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/where-to-put-your-suno-prompt-guide, https://www.layer3labs.io/guides/how-to-write-suno-prompts, https://freeacademy.ai/lessons/prompting-genre-style-and-mood).
- The previous reference, "Damned" (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LGD79pQY1kI), was a lone piano in reverb too; this one is moved away from it by swapping the acoustic piano for a detuned electric/bell timbre and loneliness for threat, with no music-box or strings.

Settings: v6 (not Wild) so the ending instruction is followed; variety normal; weirdness 40 for some tape-wobble strangeness without losing the sparse figure; styleInfluence 70; duration 17 (about 15 is fine). No game, composer or track named in the prompt.
