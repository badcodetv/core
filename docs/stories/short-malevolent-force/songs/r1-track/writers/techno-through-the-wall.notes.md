# techno-through-the-wall: notes

**What shaped it**

- The reference (YouTube LGD79pQY1kI) is the "Damned" Zombies menu theme. The YouTube page itself gave only its title, "Damned". From search summaries of the wikis and reviews: the original is instrumental and mostly piano, with echoey, creepy piano notes, simple melody lines, and menacing growls and clicks underneath. One wiki says the background piano samples the 1978 *Halloween* theme. The later sequel version **adds a "future-esque techno beat"**. This experiment takes that techno layer and leaves the piano to the lone-reverb-piano writer.
- I kept the reference's habits: a simple stepwise melody, a cold and sparse feel, and machine-like clicks (closed hats ticking). The lead is an analog square-wave arpeggio, not piano, as the brief asks.
- I wrote the cue against the picture by the second. The muffled kick sits "behind the wall" for the open door and the dead tube (0-6 s). Three stabs open the filter wider each time, one per price flicker (6-10 s). The kick comes through dry when the money appears and the stockroom opens (10-15 s). One sub drop, then silence for the end card (15 s).
- I chose 124 bpm so roughly two bars land in each section of a 17 s cue. Weirdness is moderate (35) so it stays cold without turning chaotic. Style influence is high (75) so Suno follows the timeline. The excludes ban the other writers' lead voices (piano, music box, strings, guitar/metal) and the classic Suno failure modes for a short cue (a euphoric build, a long fade).
- No game, composer or track name appears in the prompt.
