# distorted-industrial-theme: what shaped it

- **The reference**: the "Damned" menu theme from a 2010 zombies game mode. Fan wiki and review sources describe it as purely instrumental, mostly piano, with a haunting repeated piano melody whose background figure borrows the restless repeated-note ostinato of the 1978 *Halloween* theme (fan-wiki claim, unverified). Hooktheory transcribes it in **E minor, 180 BPM, 4/4** (unofficial). A later version put it over a techno beat. The YouTube page itself returned only the title "Damned"; nobody has listened to it in this session.

- **What I took**: the key (E minor), the fast nervous high-octave piano ostinato, and a tempo of 180 written as 90 half-time so Suno leaves room between the hits. The piano is kept and then mangled (saturation, bitcrush); the guitar sits on the tonic E so it locks to the theme.
- **Suno craft**: industrial-horror prompts lean on concrete words (metallic percussion, distorted, harsh mix edge, cold); "bitcrushed texture" is a term Suno already knows; shorter and concrete beats adjectives; put "No vocals, instrumental only" at the end; negatives go in exclude.

- **Picture timing**: filtered rumble for the street and door (0-6 s); three guitar and anvil slams two seconds apart on the price flickers (6-10 s); the filter opens for the mirror and the doorway; one huge slam at about 15 s, then silence under the end card. Exclude bans fade-outs and reverb tails so the cut stays hard. The game, the composer and the track title are named nowhere in the prompt.
