# dying-music-box: what shaped it

- The reference (Jack's "dammed bo1 zombies", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LGD79pQY1kI) is "Damned", the opening track of the 2010 zombies-mode soundtrack. The YouTube page itself returned only its title ("Damned"), so nothing here comes from listening; it comes from written descriptions. Unverified beyond what those say.
- Search summaries of a fan wiki (https://callofduty.fandom.com/wiki/Damned, mirrored at https://breezewiki.discard.no/callofduty/wiki/Damned; both refused a direct fetch) and of a soundtrack review (https://www.sputnikmusic.com/album/70867/Treyarch-Sound-Call-of-Duty-Black-Ops---Zombies-Soundtrack/, 403 on fetch) say: instrumental, menu theme, a haunting piano melody with echoey creepy notes and simple lines, menacing growls and clicks behind it, and a background piano figure borrowed from the 1978 *Halloween* theme (the famous ostinato in an odd 5/4 meter).
- Sheet music listings (https://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/damned/109904) are piano and harp solos: the tune survives as a bare single-instrument line, which is what a music box can carry.
- Taken from that: a short repeating minor figure, a limping odd meter (the 5/4 feel, asked for as "lopsided limping" rather than named), clicks under it (here the clockwork ticks), and something low and menacing at the end (the struck bell). Dropped: the piano itself (another writer has lone-reverb-piano) and the later techno version (another writer has techno-through-the-wall).
- Nothing names the game, composer, track or *Halloween* in the prompt.
- Settings: v6 and 17 s fixed by the story. Variety high and weirdness 60 to let the wind-down and pitch sag come out strange rather than polished; styleInfluence 70 so the prose scene is followed. Excludes keep out voice (shorts are music and sounds only) and the things a horror tag tends to summon (drums, swells, choir); "happy, major key" guard the lullaby turning sweet.
- Unverified: whether Suno will actually slow the tempo across 17 s or stop dead on cue; a stop at 15 s with hum to 17 s may come out as a fade.
