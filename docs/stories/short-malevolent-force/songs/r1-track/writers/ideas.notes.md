# Research notes: the reference track (r1-track ideas)

Nobody listened to the track; these are written sources only. Marked where unconfirmed.

- The YouTube page (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LGD79pQY1kI) returns only the title "Damned"; no channel or description came through the fetch.
- It is the first track on the 2010 zombies-mode soundtrack, ~1:38 long, instrumental, composer credited with co-composers (search summaries of https://vgmdb.net/album/29513, https://squareenixmusic.com/albums/c/callofdutyzombies.shtml, https://breezewiki.discard.no/callofduty/wiki/Damned). The soundtrack credits list guitars and keyboards; no vocalist on this track (squareenixmusic.com).
- Sound: "consists mainly of the piano"; described as "echoey, creepy piano notes and simple melody lines" with "menacing background growls and clicks"; called "unsettling, unnerving and chilling"; the "haunting piano melody" of the zombies menu (search summaries of the wiki page, https://www.sputnikmusic.com/album/70867/Treyarch-Sound-Call-of-Duty-Black-Ops---Zombies-Soundtrack/, https://matthiashattlem.blogspot.com/2021/11/avenged-sevenfold-not-ready-to-die.html). Wiki claim (unverified): the background piano borrows from the 1978 Halloween main theme, i.e. a fast, repeating, odd-feeling minor piano ostinato.
- Theory: user transcription gives E minor, 180 BPM, 4/4; melody B4-D6, stepwise, about one note every half beat, 100% diatonic; key chords Em, Am, Bm (https://www.hooktheory.com/theorytab/view/kevin-sherwood/damned). Unofficial.
- Later versions: a sequel version swaps in "a future-esque techno beat"; a later track uses "industrial textures and a distorted version" of the theme (search summaries of the wiki and https://songtell.com/treyarch/abracadavre, https://www.destructoid.com/?p=546093). So the theme is known in three skins: bare reverb piano, techno, industrial-distorted.
- Not found anywhere: music box, whispers, heartbeat, detuning. Those would be our invention, not the reference.
