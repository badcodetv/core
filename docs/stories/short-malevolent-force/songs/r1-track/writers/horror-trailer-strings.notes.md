# horror-trailer-strings: notes

**Reference.** The YouTube page (LGD79pQY1kI) only returned its title, "Damned"; no description was readable. Wiki sources: an instrumental opening the zombies soundtrack, played very quietly under the maps, audible only when nothing else is making noise (https://breezewiki.discard.no/callofduty/wiki/Damned). Reviews describe a haunting, echoey piano melody with menacing background growls and clicks (search summary of https://www.sputnikmusic.com/album/70867/... , which returned 403 to a direct fetch: unverified first-hand). Nobody has listened to the track.

**What I took.** The "barely there, under the room" quietness and the growls-and-clicks bed. The col legno tapping stands in for the clicks, a low cello drone and distant growls for the menace. The piano melody is left to the lone-reverb-piano writer and is excluded here, so this experiment stays strings.

**Technique sources.**
- Col legno as a ticking clock; sul ponticello for eerie overtones; slow glissando = menace: https://hub.yamaha.com/strings/s-how-to/eight-spooky-string-techniques , https://sharmusic.com/blogs/all/a-symphony-of-horror
- Stabs as punctuation, braams as the low menace hit, silence after the climax so the last sound hangs: https://www.rareformaudio.com/blog/horror-trailer-music-trends

**Timing.** Stabs are placed at ~6, 8 and 9 s to meet the three flickers (6-10 s), braam after the third, final hit at 15 s, then silence and one harmonic over the end card. Suno will not place hits to the second; expect to nudge in the edit.

**Settings.** v6, variety normal, weirdness 45 (some atonal strangeness without losing the structure), styleInfluence 75 (hold the techniques), no vocal gender, 17 s, maxMode off. No game, composer or track name in the prompt.
