# aleatoric-swarm-strings: what shaped it

- **The model for the sound:** Penderecki's *Threnody to the Victims of Hiroshima* (1960/61) and the sonorism around it. It uses 52 separately written string parts, sustained tone clusters with quarter tones, playing behind the bridge and on the tailpiece, slapped instruments, aleatoric passages and timings in seconds instead of bars. Its excerpts have been used under horror and dread in film (*The People Under the Stairs*, *Children of Men*, *Twin Peaks*). Those details became the style tags: clusters at the top of the range, glissandi like screams, col legno tapping, scraping behind the bridge, sul ponticello, and "free time with no steady beat" in place of a tempo.
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Threnody_to_the_Victims_of_Hiroshima
  - https://www.Wikipedia.org/wiki/Penderecki
  - https://classical.music.apple.com/ca/work/krzysztof-penderecki-1933-pp11
- **Waves, not one long crescendo:** "short swarms that swell in seconds then collapse" and "sudden dead silences between waves" give the editor about six gaps to cut to the scares at roughly 3.0, 4.8, 6.0, 7.8, 13.2 and 14.0 s. "Every chord left hanging" pushes against Suno's habit of resolving dissonance.
- **Suno prompt practice:** guides advise concrete, audible tags instead of "epic". They also say unwanted things belong in Exclude rather than as "no X" in the Style box, and that "instrumental" should be stated. So the style box has no negations except the meter cue, and Exclude carries the epic, trailer, braam, drums, synth, choir, melody and tonal tags. The horror example tags ("dissonant strings, quiet creeping dread, minimalist") confirmed that sparse plus dissonant reads well.

  - https://diyai.io/ai-tools/audio-generation/suno-exclude-styles/
- **Settings:** weirdness sits above the roughly 50 baseline (68) because atonal sound-mass is far from Suno's defaults and needs room to explore. Style influence is high (78) so the long technique list holds. Variety is "normal", since in v6 it can rewrite the style input and "high" risks drifting back to tonal cinematic strings. Model is v6 and length 17 s, as the story fixes.
