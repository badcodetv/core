# Research notes: suspense strings round (r3-strings)

- Suno's own mood pages suggest tremolo strings for horror, a motif the listener can follow, one part of the harmony made unstable, and for suspense a repeating low line (pursuit) broken by a high cluster or tremolo; also leaving a sound hanging after the rhythm stops. https://suno.com/music-library/moods/horror , https://suno.com/music-library/moods/suspenseful
- Horror prompt keyword sets that work in Suno: "dissonant strings, quiet creeping dread, unsettling, minimalist, dark"; for stingers "brief, intense, sudden". Horror is restraint, not noise; avoid contradicting descriptors. https://hookgenius.app/learn/suno-horror-music-prompts/
- Exclude Styles in v5+ handles exclusions well; keep it short (a few plain items) and keep negatives out of the style box. https://hookgenius.app/learn/suno-negative-prompting/ , https://blakecrosley.com/guides/suno-ai-music-generation , https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/how-to-write-better-suno-prompts-without-random-tags
- Instrumental: use the switch, plus the word "instrumental" in the style; vocal hallucinations can still slip in. https://howtopromptsuno.com/style-lyrics/how-do-i-make-instrumental
- No source confirms Suno reliably obeys Italian technique terms (sul ponticello, col legno); plain sound descriptions ("near the bridge, glassy, scraping", "wood of the bow tapping") are safer, so ideas should describe the sound as well as name the technique.
- The classic slasher stab: muted violins, short repeated shrieking high notes with slashing glissandi, heard "as knife stabs"; a cluster chord with alternating semitones; later echoed by a famous shark-film ostinato. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Murder , https://royalholloway.ac.uk/media/19436/herrmann%20teachinghub.pdf , https://classapps.chass.ncsu.edu/com304/?p=1581
- Avant-garde string devices used in horror: highest-possible-note tone clusters, high glissandi that read as distant screams, sul ponticello tremolo (glassy, eerie), striking the wood. https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Main/PsychoStrings , https://hub.yamaha.com/strings/s-how-to/eight-spooky-string-techniques , https://www.sharmusic.com/blogs/all/a-symphony-of-horror , https://philipbrophy.com/projects/secrethistoryoffilmmusic/10.html
- Two-note semitone ostinato doubled in octaves, quiet menace growing to frenzy, is the model for "rising two-to-four-note ostinato". https://dyerbolical.com/how-film-music-creates-fear-and-suspense/ , https://premiumbeat.com/blog/what-makes-music-sound-scary
- Brief's own warning (library entry): Suno drifts to epic trailer braams and resolves dissonance; exclude epic, hybrid, braam, trailer, drums.
