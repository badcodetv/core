# lone-violin-near-the-bridge: notes

What shaped the prompt:

- **The near-the-bridge sound described in plain words.** Bowing right by the bridge brings out the upper harmonics while the fundamental drops away. Sources call the result thin, brittle, metallic and glassy, and it is traditionally played as tremolo. That is where "glassy, whispering shiver, airy and metallic, barely a pitch" comes from. With heavy pressure the tone breaks into crackle and high partials, which became "the bow digs in hard ... scraping and grinding". Following the brief, the prompt uses no Italian terms.

- **Keeping Suno on one instrument.** Suno's genre associations tend to fill a "solo" request with an ensemble. The advice is to repeat the solo idea in several places, to describe the opposite of what you don't want rather than writing "no X" in the style box, and to put the unwanted things in Exclude. So the style says "solo violin only", "one player alone", "bone dry", "silences completely empty". Exclude covers section, cello, double bass, piano, synth, choir and reverb wash, plus the brief's epic, trailer, braam, hybrid and drums.

- **Settings.** Variety is off because guides say low variety keeps the style tags tight. Weirdness is 55, a little above the middle, since guides recommend the centre and raising it only when results come out generic, and extended violin noise is not a generic sound. Style influence is 80 so the dry solo description outweighs Suno's pull toward a full orchestra. Max mode is on. Model and duration (v6, 17 s) are fixed by the story.

- **Fitting the edit.** The cut has scares at about 3.0, 4.8, 6.0, 7.8, 13.2 and 14.0 s. The prompt asks for empty silences broken by sudden shrieks, and for a semitone figure that keeps creeping upward, so the dread rises and stabs can be cut to picture. It also asks for an ending on a high note that just stops, because the end card plays in silence.
