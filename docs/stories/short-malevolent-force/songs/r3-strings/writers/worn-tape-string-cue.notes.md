# worn-tape-string-cue: what shaped it

- **Sound target:** a 1970s British production-library suspense cue (the dramatic-suspense-for-orchestra records that cheap horror films licensed), heard from a worn tape. The style asks for a small, slightly detuned quartet-sized section rather than a big orchestra, so it sounds cheap and boxy and fits the subtle VHS picture. Sources: De Wolfe vinyl catalogue of 1970s suspense moods (https://cl205-119.dewolfemusic.com/page/de_wolfe_vinyl), library music in horror (https://music.copyright.com/blog/film-tv/apm-at-the-movies-production-music-in-historic-horror-movies), sustained tremolo and pulsing chords as the core horror string devices (https://lbbonline.com/news/the-history-and-psychology-of-spooky-music-and-sound-design-in-film).
- **Shape for a 17 s cue with edit points:** near-silence with hiss, a two-note rising cello figure, tremolo violins, then clipped stabs that cut into dead air. The gaps give the edit places to land stabs on the 3.0 / 4.8 / 6.0 / 7.8 / 13.2 / 14.0 s scares. It ends on a sagging, unresolved chord.
- **Lo-fi in plain words:** "mono", "muffled top end", "wow and flutter", "tape hiss" and "overdriven". Guides say musical words work better than technical ones (https://medium.com/@aitooldiscovery/suno-reddit-tips-and-prompts-what-r-sunoai-users-actually-do-65b59d51bcad).
- **Exclude:** the brief's list (epic, hybrid, braam, trailer, drums) plus synth, choir, vocals, brass, piano, hi-fi, and a major-key resolution. Guides advise putting each unwanted element in Exclude and naming its replacement in Style (https://jackrighteous.com/fr/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/negative-prompting-suno-v5-guide, https://diyai.io/ai-tools/audio-generation/suno-exclude-styles/).
- **Settings:** variety off, because at Normal Suno was seen rewriting the style prompt, and at Off it kept the text on 52 of 54 takes. styleInfluence 75, because the default of 50 can make a detailed prompt come out generic (https://hookgenius.app/learn/suno-v6-guide/). Weirdness is a moderate 45 to let the detune and warble through without wandering off-genre. Model v6, 17 s, instrumental with empty lyrics.
