# quartet-cluster-ostinato: what shaped it

- **Small and close, not big.** The brief asks for an intimate chamber sound, so the style says "solo string quartet only", names the four players, and adds "close-miked and dry, small room". This steers Suno away from its habit of turning strings into a big orchestral trailer cue. Crumb's *Black Angels* (an amplified, harsh-sounding quartet) shows that four players can be frightening without an orchestra: https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Angels_(Crumb), https://www.theabsolutesound.com/articles/the-legacy-of-george-crumb/
- **Articulations named concretely.** The prompt asks for tremolo, playing near the bridge (sul ponticello, which sounds gritty and metallic) and upward glides, the standard horror string toolkit. They are written in plain words that the model is likely to follow: https://hub.yamaha.com/strings/s-how-to/eight-spooky-string-techniques
- **Structure written as behaviour.** The prompt spells out the structure as a sequence: the cello figure, a stab from all four, a beat of silence, then the figure creeps back in. That way the editor gets clean stabs and gaps to line up with the scares at about 3.0, 4.8, 6.0, 7.8, 13.2 and 14.0 s. The ending is a "harshest clash, cut off dead", so nothing resolves.
- **Negatives only in Exclude.** Guides say that "no X" written in the style field is unreliable or even backfires, and that Exclude is the place for unwanted elements. Exclude covers the drift the brief warns about (epic, trailer, braam, hybrid, drums) plus orchestral, synth, choir, vocals and piano: https://blakecrosley.com/blog/suno-style-field-style-influence, https://jackrighteous.com/de/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/negative-prompting-suno-v5-guide
- **Settings.** The style field is precise, so styleInfluence is high (80), following the advice to use Strong for a precise field (same Crosley source). Weirdness is moderate (55): high enough to keep the harmony unsettled, low enough not to wander off the quartet. The model is plain v6 with variety normal and maxMode off, and the duration is 17 s as the story fixes. Lyrics are empty, since the track is instrumental.
