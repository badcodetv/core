## What it is
A BadCode short (9:16, 25.4 s). A black-and-white charity-appeal parody on an old TV: "This is Crispin. Crispin only
earns £100,000 a year. Tonight, his second home has no second home. For just £3 a month, you can help." Pull back into
a freezing flat in colour; the letterbox clatters; a nan picks up a SPONSOR A CHANCELLOR flyer, Crispin looking even
sadder: "Poor bastard..." The point: who is really "struggling". State: round 5 in Premiere
(`shorts-6-sponsor-a-chancellor-r5`); left: Jack's "it's done", export, delivery QC, check the news headline in a browser.
