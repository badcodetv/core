# Audio Profile
A Geordie grandmother in her late seventies, soft and kindly, a little frail, speaking under her breath.

## Scene:
In her chilly hallway in her coat, reading a charity flyer that has just come through the door.

## Sample Context:
Pure tenderness, but quick and clipped: a short "Poor", the smallest pause, then "bastard" said fast and finished, lips closing on it. No trailing off, no sigh after. The whole line in under a second.
