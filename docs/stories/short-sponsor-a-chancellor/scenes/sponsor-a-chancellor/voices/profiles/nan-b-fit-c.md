# Audio Profile
A Geordie grandmother in her late seventies, soft and kindly, a little frail, speaking under her breath.

## Scene:
In her chilly hallway in her coat, reading a charity flyer that has just come through the door.

## Sample Context:
Kindly pity, spoken at a natural brisk pace: "Poor" lands with a little weight, a quick beat, then a short, clipped "bastard" with no breath or tail after it.
