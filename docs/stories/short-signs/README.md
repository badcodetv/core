# Signs (a BadCode short)

> **A short is a project** (Kai, 2026-11-10): ID `short-signs`, Drive `Shorts/Signs` (row in `scripts/drive/folders.json`),
> and each machine points the ID at its own folder (`drive.sh where`). Made with `new-short`, which runs `new-scene` on
> the one scene in `scenes/signs/`. The list of every short: [`docs/marketing/badcode-shorts.md`](../../marketing/badcode-shorts.md) (#33).

## The idea (Jack, 2026-11-11, his words)

> "we should make a simple short of literally using the image for this as a reference image someone takes their bins out,
> it zooms into the bin lid, the camera is in the bin looking up at the binlid then the name "Badcode" is illuminated in
> the bin lid with the logo"

**Reference:** [`reference/signs-street-ref.png`](./reference/signs-street-ref.png), a UK terraced street at dusk, every front
garden with a different party's election sign (Green, Labour, Conservative, Lib Dem). Jack named it "Signs".

**Rough beats:** the street of signs, someone wheels their bin out (≈4 s) → push into the bin lid (≈3 s) → inside the bin,
looking up, the lid lights up: the BadCode logo and name (≈4 s). **≈11 s.** No words yet.

## Checks owed
- 🔴 **The logo is the real logo** (`docs/brand/logo/`), laid in by ffmpeg/Premiere, never drawn by Flow (the logo is not a loose guide).
- ⬜ **What it says:** every party's sign out front, BadCode inside the bin. Is the bin the joke on the parties, or on us? Jack's call; the reader rules (`docs/marketing/the-reader.md`) and `marketing-gatekeeper` check it before posting.
- ⬜ Real party names on the signs: fine as text (no real person, no likeness); keep the signs generic-made, no real candidate names.
