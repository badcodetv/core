## What it is
A BadCode short (9:16, 12 s, no words). A UK terraced street at dusk, every front garden with a different party's
election sign; someone wheels their green bin out; push in on the bin lid; from inside the bin, looking up, the BadCode
logo and name light up in the lid. State: rough cut 1 on the timeline page and in Premiere (`shorts-33-signs-r1`).
Left: Jack's notes or "it's done", export, delivery QC, gatekeeper.
