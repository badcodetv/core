## Facts (read at source, 2026-11-22)
- Petrosino, Turpin-Petrosino, Hollis-Peel, Lavenberg (2013), "'Scared Straight' and other juvenile awareness programs
  for preventing juvenile delinquency", Cochrane CD002796 and Campbell Systematic Reviews 2013:5: nine randomised
  trials (946 young people, US); the programmes raised the odds of offending about 1.6-1.7 times against doing nothing.
  https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD002796_scared-straight-and-other-juvenile-awareness-programs-preventing-juvenile-delinquency
- Not in the draft, available: NAO, Evaluating government spending (HC 860, 2 Dec 2021): 8% of major-project spend
  (£35bn of £432bn) had robust evaluation plans; 64% had none. https://www.nao.org.uk/reports/evaluating-government-spending/

## The show being parodied (research, 2026-11-22)
Pitchers climb a staircase (later a lift) into a converted warehouse loft; the investors sit in a row with cash on
show; one pitch, one take; "I'm out"; when all are out the pitcher leaves with nothing. UK parody is fair dealing
under CDPA s30A (2014). Pollution check on the draft: `fresh.py` "nothing close among 4689 past prompts".
