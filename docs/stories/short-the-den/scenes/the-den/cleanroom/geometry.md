# The den: where everything is (every writer of every shot gets this)

- One room: a converted warehouse loft. Brick walls, wooden floorboards, tall gridded factory windows along ONE long
  wall (the window wall), warm lamps, an iron cog machine in a corner.
- **The panel** sits in a gentle arc of five mismatched leather armchairs with their backs to the window wall, so the
  windows glow behind them. Left to right as the pitcher sees them: Investor A (burgundy suit), Investor B (navy
  three-piece), BadCode (balaclava, centre), Investor C (mustard cardigan), Investor D (black polo-neck). A small round
  glass side table with stacks of cash beside each chair.
- **The mark**: an open patch of floorboards in the middle of the room, three or four metres in front of the panel,
  facing them. Pitchers stand there.
- **The staircase**: an iron staircase comes down from a raised walkway on the wall OPPOSITE the windows, behind the
  mark. A pitcher walks down it, crosses to the mark, and faces the panel.
- **Cameras.** Shots of a pitcher: from beside the panel, looking across at the pitcher, the staircase and dark brick
  wall behind them (no windows behind a pitcher). Shots of the panel: from near the mark, looking at the panel, the
  windows glowing behind them.
