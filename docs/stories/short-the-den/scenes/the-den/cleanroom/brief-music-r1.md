# Brief: The Den, background music, round 1

You write a Suno (v6) prompt for an instrumental music bed. Read ONLY this brief. Research freely on the web how the
background music of British TV investors'-pitch shows sounds: the tense underscore while a pitcher stands on the mark,
the stings when an investor declares, how it sits under speech.

## What Jack asked for, verbatim
"please make dragons den like backround music. thanks"

## The facts
- A 9:16 short, about 40 seconds, a parody of a TV investors' pitch show set in a dark converted warehouse loft.
- Story: a smooth government minister asks the panel for two billion pounds to take every teenager round a prison and
  "scare them straight"; asked how he'll know it works, he says "I've got a good feeling"; three investors say "I'm out"
  one after another. A nervous nurse pitches one ward for six months with the number that would prove her wrong; the
  masked investor says "I'm in". An end card with a fact, then a logo.
- The music sits UNDER spoken dialogue the whole way: nine short spoken lines over ~35 s. It must leave room for voices.
- Instrumental only: no singing, no spoken words, no vocal chops.
- Parody rule: the feel of that kind of show, never its actual theme tune or a recognisable copy of any show's music.
  Do not name any TV show, broadcaster or composer in the prompt.
