# analog-synth-pulse: what shaped it

- The style text follows the order one v6 guide recommends: genre, then rhythmic feel, then two or three signature sounds, then mood, then production. It is kept under about 100 words, with one main genre and no competing genres, because that guide says a short, clear prompt does better than a long list of adjectives. https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/optimize-suno-v6-style-of-music-prompt
- Exclude is treated as a boundary, not a guarantee (Suno's release note and the guides say the same). The positive prompt itself never asks for a melody or a drop, and it says "no melody" and "midrange left empty" so the bed stays out of the speech band. https://suno.com/release-notes/exclude-styles
- Lyrics are left empty for a pure instrumental. Section tags were left out because v6 tends to ignore bare tags, and the bed should stay level the whole way through rather than build. https://github.com/mttkllr/suno-field-guide
- Berklee's piece on TV music describes beds that add mood and momentum "without getting in the way of the dialogue," built from ticky percussion, atmospheric pads and a repeated single-note ostinato varied by octave. That led to the one-root bass with octave hops, the clock-like hat and no kick. https://www.berklee.edu/node/2348071
- Library pitch and tension cues (Sonoton "The Final Pitch", Sascha Ende "Low Energy Tension Vol. 4") pair cold analog synths with ticking percussion for factual entertainment and game shows. That confirmed the palette. https://sonoton.com/kr/album/LSYN0105 , https://ende.app/en/song/13572-low-energy-tension-vol-4
- Settings: v6 (not wild) for a steady loop, weirdness 25, styleInfluence 80 so the take holds close to the brief, and 60 s so there is room to trim it to the 42.8 s cut. maxMode is on.
