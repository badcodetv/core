They also say to change one thing at a time and keep Variety low so Suno sticks to the style. So the quartet's two jobs (plucked replies in the verses, bowed lines in the bridge) and the tenor's final-hook-only entry are described in the Style box and then pinned to their sections in the lyrics. Variety stays off ([where to put your prompt](https://jackrighteous.com/en-gb/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/where-to-put-your-suno-prompt-guide), [optimize v6 style prompt](https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/optimize-suno-v6-style-of-music-prompt)).
