English psych folk funk, 100 BPM: a deep, warm South London baritone with a Brixton lilt, rich, rounded and unhurried, glottal stops and dropped H's, rolling through the verses in easy spoken patter, half-crooned, never rapped, a crafty music-hall raconteur leaning on the bar, slow and swaggering, then breaking into a big theatrical, soulful full-throated melody for the hook, a crowd of mates chanting back. Tin whistle up front over hand drums, congas and a slinky funk bass. Dubbed-out psych layers: spring-reverb guitar skanks, fuzz bass doubling the bassline in the hook, echo-chamber delay throws trailing off line endings.
