=== SCENE 1 ===
Single continuous shot, no scene cuts, real-time speed, vintage film camera. From behind the police driver's gloved hands, the black getaway car ahead pulls away under the elevated railway, rear tyres slipping on wet cobbles, its body rolling heavily on soft springs as it swerves right; the gunman on the running board keeps his tommy gun pointed straight up at the sky and it spits blue-white flashes. The wiper blade sweeps across the windscreen twice. Every raindrop on the glass is a tiny lens: each one holds an upside-down copy of the muzzle flash and the purple sparks, and each wiper pass drags those hundreds of little images into long violet and amber streaks that fan across the glass and slowly bead back up. After 6 seconds the camera leans past the wheel toward a lit amber shop window on the right, until one fat raindrop fills the frame with flickering orange light. Sound: engine roar, tyres hissing on wet stone, wiper thump. No dialogue, no on-screen text.
HANDOFF: one raindrop full of flickering amber shop-window light, matching the orange fire in the oven mouth of scene 2.
WHY: the raindrops are real lenses already on the glass, so the trails have a physical cause and the wiper gives them a rhythm instead of a generic smear.

=== SCENE 2 ===
Single continuous shot, no scene cuts, real-time speed, vintage film camera, low angle along the floured brick floor. The tired baker lifts the dough and slaps it down hard on the table; a cloud of flour bursts up and drifts toward the open oven, where every grain catches the firelight and glitters like sparks. Above the burning logs, real heat haze rises: the hot air ripples like oil on water, and through it the hanging copper pans and the brick arch wobble and bend, their edges splitting into thin bands of amber, magenta and teal where the shimmer is strongest. The bakers behind keep working, faces unchanged. In the last 2 seconds the camera rises slowly toward the largest copper pan, until its round polished base, glowing orange through the heat ripple, fills the frame. Sound: dough thud, fire crackle. No dialogue, no on-screen text.
HANDOFF: the round copper base of a pan filling the frame, which match-cuts to the falling coins in scene 3.
WHY: heat shimmer above an open fire is a real optical distortion, so the psychedelic bending grows straight out of the oven the scene is built around.

=== SCENE 3 ===
Single continuous shot, no scene cuts, real-time speed, vintage film camera. The last coin tips out of the brown paper envelope and lands on the floured palm with a small bounce. Low dawn sun shines through the crate of green, blue and amber bottles behind, throwing pools of coloured glass light across the brick and the hand. The moth on the palm opens its wings and lifts off, fluttering slowly up through those coloured beams: its pale wings turn green, then blue, then amber as it crosses each one, and dust shaken from its wings hangs in the light like tinted smoke. The camera follows the moth up and to the left, toward the bottles, then pushes into the round mouth of a blue bottle, whose thick glass rings spiral inward around a dark centre. Sound: coin clink, a soft flutter. No dialogue, no on-screen text.
HANDOFF: looking down the neck of a blue bottle, concentric glass rings around a dark centre, matching the spiralling stairwell and round fishbowl of scene 4.
WHY: the coloured bottles act as real lighting gels, and the moth carrying their colour gives the poverty beat one fragile, beautiful movement.

=== SCENE 4 ===
Single continuous shot, no scene cuts, real-time speed, vintage film camera, looking straight down the stairwell. Far below, the baker climbs one flight, pay envelope in hand, eyes up; above, the landlord in the fur collar leans further over the banister and stretches both palms down toward him. In the foreground the fishbowl acts as a liquid lens: the goldfish flicks across it and its tail stirs the water, and the stairwell seen through the glass twists into a slow turning spiral, the red landing and the hanging bulbs smearing into rings of red and gold that wobble with each ripple. As the fish passes, an orange streak of its body sweeps across the whole bent stairwell. In the last 2 seconds the camera drifts into the bowl until the glass edge fills the frame, splitting the light into a thin horizontal rainbow fringe against dark green-grey. Sound: footsteps on wooden stairs, water lapping. No dialogue, no on-screen text.
HANDOFF: a thin horizontal rainbow fringe on the bowl's glass edge against dark green, matching the prismatic light in the door slot of scene 5.
WHY: the fishbowl is already in the still as a curved water lens, so the stairwell distorting and turning through it is optics the camera could really film.
