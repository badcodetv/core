Use the provided image as the first frame. One continuous 8-second shot with no cuts. The camera is locked off on a tripod at puddle level and never moves. Keep the layout, buildings, lighting and colours the same as the first frame. Both cars are already visible in the first frame. They drive at a steady, believable 40 mph along one straight line down the cobbled street. They grow larger at a smooth, constant rate, never jump or skip position, and stay fully on the ground with their weight bouncing on the old suspension over the cobbles.
[0–3s] The black Packard races toward the camera with its headlights flaring into the lens. The man in the rear window holds the Thompson gun angled steeply up at the sky the whole time, with the barrel never lowering. === WHY (two sentences) ===
I found that the main AI giveaways are cars that pop in and out, everything moving at once, and colours with no visible source, so the still shows both cars already in frame on one straight line, the camera stays locked, and the video has timecodes that send the cars out of frame one after the other. The psychedelic effect is a real oil film on water that gets broken up by real splashes, and the lens, film stock, imperfections and sound are all spelled out, because Google's guidance and the practitioner guides say that kind of detail is what makes these models look filmed, not generic.
