=== STILL PROMPT (Nano Banana 2.1, 16:9) ===
A real 35mm film frame from a 1920s Prohibition-era crime film, shot at night in heavy rain from the passenger seat of a pursuing 1928 police touring car. We look forward through its upright, rain-streaked flat glass windscreen. In the lower foreground is the worn dashboard: scratched black-painted steel, a few small round brass-rimmed gauges with glowing needles and no numerals, a chrome toggle switch. On the left, the driver's hands sit on a thin black steering wheel. He is a uniformed police officer in a dark wool sleeve with a brass button at the cuff and wet leather gloves, seen from the wrist down only. A single wiper arm hangs from the top edge of the glass. It has just swept the left half of the windscreen clean, so that side is sharp and clear. The right half is still covered in beaded, running rain that smears every light into soft, sprayed arcs of colour, like airbrushed paint.

About fifteen metres ahead, centred in the frame, a black 1927 four-door sedan races down a narrow wet cobblestone street. It has tall rounded fenders, a spare tyre on the back and one glowing red rear lamp. A gangster in a dark overcoat and fedora leans out of its rear side window. He holds a Thompson submachine gun with a round drum magazine straight up, the muzzle pointed at the night sky and at no one. Spray kicks up from the sedan's rear wheels. Both sides of the street are lined with brick buildings, iron fire escapes and blurred neon tubes in abstract shapes: magenta, electric cyan, amber and blood red. They glow and reflect in long streaks on the wet stones. The police car's headlamps throw two warm yellow cones through the falling rain.

Look: 40mm anamorphic lens at f/2, focus on the sedan, the glass droplets softly out of focus, Kodak Vision3 500T film grain, slight halation around every light, deep inky shadows, high contrast, exaggerated saturated neon over an otherwise natural, realistic scene. Real-world imperfections: grime at the edges of the windscreen, a small chip in the glass, wet streaks, uneven light. No readable text, letters, numbers, signs or licence plates anywhere. No glossy CGI sheen, no plastic surfaces, no extra cars, no people on the street.

=== VIDEO PROMPT (Omni Flash, image-to-video, 8 s, sound on) ===
Use the image as the first frame. One single continuous shot, no scene cuts. Keep the layout, lighting and colours consistent with the image. The camera is locked to the police car's dashboard in the passenger seat and only shakes slightly with the road and the engine. No pans, no zooms.

The black getaway sedan stays centred and at exactly the same distance ahead for the whole 8 seconds. Both cars drive steadily forward on the same wet cobblestone street. The sedan never jumps, flickers, changes shape or leaves the frame, and no other vehicles appear. Buildings and neon signs slide past on both sides at a steady speed. The driver's gloved hands stay on the wheel and make small steering corrections.

The single top-hinged wiper sweeps slowly and mechanically across the windscreen once every two seconds, with a steady pendulum rhythm. Each sweep wipes the glass clear and sharp for a moment. Fresh raindrops then land, bead up and run, smearing the neon, the red rear lamp and the headlamp glare back into soft, airbrushed arcs of magenta, cyan, amber and red that bloom and drip down the glass until the next sweep.

[0–3s] The gangster leans out of the sedan's rear window with the tommy gun held straight up at the sky.
[3–5s] He fires a long upward burst at the sky. Bright orange muzzle flashes strobe off the falling rain and the wet street. Every flash flares through the water on the windscreen and turns into stars and smeared streaks of light. Brass shell casings tumble off the sedan's roof.
[5–8s] The firing stops and the wiper sweeps the glass clear again. The sedan is still exactly the same distance ahead, its red rear lamp glowing.

Real 35mm film look, natural physics, realistic motion blur, film grain.

Sound: heavy rain drumming on the roof and glass, the low roar of two old engines, the rubber squeak and thunk of each wiper sweep, tyres hissing on wet cobblestones, a 1920s police bell clanging, and the sharp rattling crack of the Thompson gun echoing off brick walls. No dialogue, no voices, no music. No readable text anywhere.

=== WHY (two sentences) ===
Both models respond better to concrete film language than to "cinematic" buzzwords, which is why the prompts name the lens, the film stock and small flaws like grime and a chipped windscreen. They also ask Omni Flash outright for one continuous shot, a single camera position, a fixed distance between the cars, a set time for the gunfire and specific sounds, so the cars can't teleport and the colour effect comes from real rain and a wiper instead of a basic overlay.
