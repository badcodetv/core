=== SCENE 23A ===
A frame from a 35mm feature film, full-bleed, 16:9: Chicago, 1928, a bakery on the South Side just before sunrise. The camera sits low behind the counter, right at the lip of an open trapdoor in the floorboards, so the rough edge of the hatch and its iron ring fill the bottom left corner. Up out of the hole pours a hard, sour green light, the only strong light in the frame: it rakes up the underside of the scarred wooden counter, the cast-iron till, the flour sacks and the bread racks, throwing their shadows up onto the pressed-tin ceiling. Hot air from the furnace below rises through that light as a visible shimmer, bending everything beyond it like a mirage, so the open front door ripples and sways. In the doorway, off to the right and walking out onto a dry, grey, coal-gritted street, two American police officers in peaked caps and long blue coats, one with a walrus moustache, one with ginger sideburns, each with a fat loaf tucked under his arm, half-turn back and tip their caps. Flour dust hangs in the green air. Outside, a thin pale dawn, overcast, starless.
WHY: The basement's green is the one motivated light, coming up from below and colouring the whole shop, and the furnace's heat haze is a real physical warp that turns the picture strange instead of a pasted effect.

=== SCENE 23B ===
A frame from a 35mm feature film, full-bleed, 16:9: Chicago, 1928, first light. The camera looks steeply down from a second-floor window across a narrow side street, the frame tilted, the bakery's doorway in the lower left third. The street is dry: cracked paving, horse dung swept into the gutter, a milk crate, coal ash blown against the kerb. Two American police officers in peaked caps and long blue coats, one with a walrus moustache, one with ginger sideburns, stroll away up the right of the frame, each with a loaf under his arm, both lifting their caps back toward the shop. Two lights cross on the pavement: a low copper sunrise from the far end of the street, and a soft green spill from the open bakery door, where, deep inside, a trapdoor behind the counter glows. Because of the two lights, each officer throws two long shadows that fan out behind him, one tinted rose, one tinted cold cyan, stretching back across the paving to the bakery step like coloured ink. The shop's painted name is sun-bleached, flaked and illegible. Fine grain, deep unlit shadow in the doorways.
WHY: A quieter, Lynchian overhead angle where the psychedelic layer is real optics, coloured double shadows from two light sources, linking the officers' walk away back to the secret glowing in the door.
