=== SCENE 21 ===
Single continuous shot, no scene cuts. Straight overhead on the mayor lying spread-eagled on the mountain of banded cash. The violet spots of light on him come from a punched-tin lantern hanging just above the camera, out of frame, and it starts to turn on its chain. For the first four seconds the spots sweep slowly round him in circles, and the coloured gel inside the lantern swings past so the spots move from violet through magenta to amber as they cross his suit and the gold chain. The mayor's chest rises and falls slowly, and the banknote on his face lifts on each breath and settles again; his fingers twitch lazily in the money. Camera locked off for four seconds, then rises slowly straight up. In the last two seconds the lantern spins faster, the spots shrink to hard white pinpricks scattering over the dark brick. Real-time speed, vintage film camera. Sound: a creaking chain, paper rustling. No dialogue, no on-screen text, no captions.
HANDOFF: the whole frame becomes a scatter of tiny white light specks on darkness, matching the mirror-ball specks on the basement walls in scene 22.
WHY: a turning patterned lantern with a gel is a real, causal light source, so the dreamy colour sweep stays physical, and its pinpricks hand straight to the mirror ball.

=== SCENE 23 ===
Single continuous shot, no scene cuts. The bakery at dawn, the two officers in the open doorway with a loaf under each arm, tipping their caps. They turn and walk out onto the street, heavy coats swinging, and walk away from the door. Green light pours up out of the open trapdoor in the floor through a rising vapour. The psychedelic layer is a liquid light show: the trapdoor light is shining up through something bubbling below, so slow blobs of emerald, teal and gold slide and split across the pressed-tin ceiling and over the counter, like coloured oil swirled between glass plates on a projector. The blobs grow bigger and move faster as the vapour thickens. Camera locked off for five seconds, then tilts down and pushes slowly into the glowing trapdoor until green light and vapour fill the frame. Real-time speed, vintage film camera. Sound: a shop bell, a low bubbling from below. No dialogue, no on-screen text, no captions.
HANDOFF: a frame full of green light and rising vapour, matching the green and violet flames of the furnace in scene 24.
WHY: an oil-projection look caused by light through a bubbling liquid makes the secret downstairs glow, and diving into it is the last drop.

=== SCENE 24 ===
Single continuous shot, no scene cuts. Overhead on the stoker at the furnace. He swings one full shovel of banknotes into the open firebox. As the notes hit, the flame flares up in bands of colour as the inks burn, copper green, then violet, then sodium gold, like a chemistry flame test. Sparks shoot up toward the camera and leave short glowing streaks. The big riveted pipe beside him stays dark and cold all the way up. The glowing rectangle of light on the brick wall changes colour with the flame, and as the green burns brightest it starts to ripple like light through water. Camera locked off for five seconds, then drifts slowly sideways toward the wall. In the last second the rippling green rectangle of light on the brick fills the centre of the frame. Real-time speed, vintage film camera. Sound: the shovel scraping, a roar of flame. No dialogue, no on-screen text, no captions.
HANDOFF: a rippling green rectangle of light on dark brick, matching the glowing green aquarium in scene 25.
WHY: colour from burning chemicals is a real flame effect seen in footage, distinct from the other three techniques, and the cold pipe keeps the story point in shot.
