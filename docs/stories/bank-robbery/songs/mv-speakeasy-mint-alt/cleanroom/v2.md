=== SCENE 5 ===
Single continuous shot, no scene cuts, real-time speed, shot on a vintage film camera. The heavy-set gangster in the fedora leans to the door slot and exhales a long breath of cigarette smoke into it. The bevelled glass edge of the slot acts as a prism: a thin band of spectrum light from inside cuts across his cheek, and as the smoke rolls through the beam the band fans out into a solid ribbon of red, green and violet that slides and twists in the drifting smoke. The eyes behind the slot narrow, then blink once. The camera pushes slowly in toward the slot, holding the man's face steady. After 6 seconds the smoke fills the slot completely, a pale grey cloud lit from inside by a soft rainbow ring. Sound: a cigarette crackle, a muffled band through the steel. No dialogue, no on-screen text, no captions.
HANDOFF: the slot filled edge to edge with pale smoke and a soft rainbow ring, matching the frosted pane and the rainbow halo round grandma's candle.
WHY: the prism already sits in the still's slot, so the colour has a physical cause, and smoke is the one thing that makes a prism beam visible and moving.

=== SCENE 6 ===
Single continuous shot, no scene cuts, real-time speed, shot through a frosted attic window on a vintage film camera. Grandma cups her hands closer round the single candle and breathes out; her breath steams in the cold. On the window glass in front of the lens, new frost crystals grow inward from the edges, feathering across the pane in real time, and each fresh crystal catches the candlelight and splits it into tiny rainbow points, so the halo round the flame turns into a slowly spinning ring of spectral spikes. The flame gutters lower and leans in her breath. The camera pushes slowly in through the gap in the frost toward the flame. In the final 2 seconds the flame fills the centre of frame and its starburst blooms bright white. Sound: a faint wind, ice ticking on the glass. No dialogue, no on-screen text, no captions.
HANDOFF: a hard white starburst of candle flame filling the centre of frame, matching the white bloom of the photographers' flashbulbs.
WHY: frost growing on glass is a real diffraction grating, so the psychedelic layer grows from the cold itself, which is the point of her scene.

=== SCENE 7 ===
Single continuous shot, no scene cuts, real-time speed, looking up through the bars of a storm drain on a vintage film camera. The mayor flicks his wrist and lets go of the last envelopes. On the balcony, the photographers' magnesium flashbulbs fire one after another; each pop freezes the falling envelopes for an instant and leaves a fading coloured afterimage of them in the smoke, so the drop becomes a stuttering staircase of red, white and blue ghosts tumbling straight down toward the lens. The hands of the crowd at the grate edges snatch at the air and close on nothing. The camera stays locked off. In the last 2 seconds a final flash lights the wet bars of the grate, and the water drops on the iron glitter like cut crystal. Sound: flashbulb pops, papers fluttering, rain dripping into the drain. No dialogue, no on-screen text, no captions.
HANDOFF: the wet iron bars glittering with faceted water drops in the last flash, matching the cut-glass decanter that fills the left of the poker room.
WHY: a magnesium flash is a real strobe in the period, so the multi-exposure trail of the envelopes going down the drain is caused by the photographers in the frame.

=== SCENE 8 ===

The gangsters and the mayor sit frozen at the poker table, every head turned to the baker in the doorway holding his small loaf. The camera dollies slowly sideways behind the decanters; their facets split the room into sliding, repeated slices, so the mayor's face and the baker's figure multiply, drift apart in amber and green, and snap back together as each facet passes, like a prism lens turning. The whisky inside sways with a faint amber glow. The only movement at the table is ash dropping from one cigar onto the chips. In the last 2 seconds the camera clears the glass and tilts up to the green-shaded lamp swinging gently over the table. Sound: a clock ticking, the lamp chain creaking. No dialogue, no on-screen text, no captions.
HANDOFF: the green-shaded lamp swinging over green felt, matching the green lamp hanging above the pool table.
WHY: the decanter is a real multi-image lens already in the still, and splitting the frozen room into facets makes the baker's interruption land as a crack in their world.
