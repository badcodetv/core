**What shaped it**
- **"Best one" page:** I fetched it, and its public tags are just "psych folk, funk (V6)". Nothing else there needs carrying over, so I kept the verbatim box as the base, as Jack asked. [suno.com/song/024b82e8…](https://suno.com/song/024b82e8-378c-47bd-bf73-bb5eafea2ed5)
- **Naming instruments:** Prompt guides say Suno reacts strongly to named instruments, and one guide's vintage-funk prompt names "wah guitar riffs". So I listed every layer by name instead of writing "funky textures". These are blog guides, not Suno's own docs. ([Medium guide](https://medium.com/@abhisheksd2003/the-ultimate-suno-ai-prompt-guide-with-clear-tested-examples-2d827ffe8b3a), [Jack Righteous guide](https://jackrighteous.com/en-gb/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/finish-strong-suno-ai-prompts-guide-t-z-for-complete-music-creation-mastery))
- **The psych-funk stack:** An envelope filter (auto-wah) follows how hard you play, and it was the 1970s funk-guitar sound. The Mu-Tron III envelope filter, from 1972, is the classic psych-funk unit, used by Jerry Garcia among others. A clavinet through an envelope filter is the "Higher Ground" sound, and a phaser is another standard clav pedal. That's why it's written as wah guitar plus clavinet through an envelope filter and a phaser, all sitting over the bass. ([Auto-wah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auto-wah), [Mu-Tron III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mu-Tron_III), [Keyscape wah](https://support.spectrasonics.net/manual/Keyscape/11/en/topic/wah-wah), [Reverb clavinet history](https://reverb.com/news/history-of-the-clavinet))
- **No Indian opening:** "sitar, tabla, Indian" are now in the Exclude box. The intro tag now starts on bass and wah guitar, with the tin whistle coming in later, so the first sound isn't a drone.
- **Voice:** He's now a velvety pub-lounge crooner. He still talk-sings the verses with glottal stops and dropped H's, then opens into a chorus with lots of vibrato. The gang voices and the big, grand chorus are written into the box so they don't get lost.
- **Lyrics:** Every word and spelling is unchanged, including "heaven’s" with its curly apostrophe. I only added square-bracket tags.
