**What the research said:**
- One guide says Suno pays less attention to vocal tags at the end of a long Style box ([roo.beehiiv](https://roo.beehiiv.com/p/suno-prompting)). So the MC voice and the choir come straight after "100 BPM", and the voice notes are repeated in the tags next to the lines they apply to.
- Another guide says chorus, pre-chorus and final-chorus tags tend to pull in group vocals ([jackrighteous](https://jackrighteous.com/en-gb/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/stop-suno-adding-crowd-vocals-choirs-backing-voices)). Here we want that pull, so the choir is named in every chorus tag ([learnprompting](https://learnprompting.org/blog/guide-suno) shows choir requests made this way). Each verse tag says "no choir" so the verses stay solo talk.
- A roundup of r/SunoAI tips ([medium](https://medium.com/@aitooldiscovery/suno-reddit-tips-and-prompts-what-r-sunoai-users-actually-do-65b59d51bcad)) says to label call-and-response by section, so the chorus tags say who calls and who answers.
