Here's what I found and the experiments for this round. Nothing came from old prompts or any Suno skills/repo, only from the pages below.

**What the research found**
- **Where things go in Custom mode.** Genre, instruments, voice and production go in the Styles box. Section tags and short cues go in the Lyrics box. Unwanted traits go in Advanced Options → Exclude. Lowering the Variety slider is said to keep v6 closer to your style tags, and Suno says v6 understands more musician vocabulary. ([Jack Righteous, v6 prompting](https://jackrighteous.com/en-gb/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/where-to-put-your-suno-prompt-guide), [v6 style optimisation](https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/optimize-suno-v6-style-of-music-prompt))
- **One build order for the style prompt.** Genre, then rhythm, then 2–3 signature instruments, then vocal role, then mood, then production texture. Keep it under about 100 words. ([Medium, Reddit tips](https://medium.com/@aitooldiscovery/suno-reddit-tips-and-prompts-what-r-sunoai-users-actually-do-65b59d51bcad))
- **Describe the voice in three layers.** Who is singing, how they deliver it, and how the mix treats it. Put the dominant genre first, blend only 2–3 genres, and give an exact BPM so the genre doesn't drift. Suno ignores "don't" in prompts, so describe the wanted sound clearly enough to crowd out the unwanted one and put the rest in Exclude. Don't repeat the same vocal cue in every section. Start [Intro] with instruments, not a singer. ([HookGenius 2026 guide](https://hookgenius.app/learn/suno-prompt-guide-2026/))
- **Put vocal tags early.** They get deprioritised when buried at the end of a long style field. Use about 5–15 tags. "Laid-back male rap" and "90s West Coast" are known working phrases. Don't use artist names; describe the sound instead. ([roo.beehiiv](https://roo.beehiiv.com/p/your-suno-ai-prompts-are-failing-here-s-the-fix-most-guides-won-t-tell-you), [Medium guide](https://medium.com/@abhisheksd2003/the-ultimate-suno-ai-prompt-guide-with-clear-tested-examples-2d827ffe8b3a), [HookGenius cheat sheet](https://hookgenius.app/suno-cheat-sheet/))
- **Keeping the opera without the singer.** The instruments can stay: full orchestra, timpani, harp, French horns. Choir counts as a vocal, so leave it out. "Epic strings over hard 808s" is an example of a cinematic hip-hop fusion that works. ([roo.beehiiv prompt list](https://roo.beehiiv.com/p/the-suno-ai-prompts-list-that-actually-works-2026-genre-artist-style-and-use-case), [frankx guide](https://www.frankx.ai/blog/suno-prompt-engineering-complete-guide))
- **Real classical + funk fusions to borrow from.** Hip-hop often uses orchestral elements; "Planet Rock" took from Stravinsky's *Firebird* ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classical_music_in_hip-hop)). Other models: funk/hip-hop with "orchestral psychedelia" ([Clutchy Hopkins](https://clutchyhopkins.bandcamp.com)), harpsichord opening into funk on a Bernie Worrell record ([Something Else](https://somethingelsereviews.com/?p=227)), baroque orchestration with boom-bap ([Treble on Ghost Funk Orchestra](https://www.treblezine.com/ghost-funk-orchestra-a-song-for-paul-review/)), and funk bass and brass under legato strings with psychedelic trumpet ([Sonoton](https://sonoton.com/en/track/SCDV149774)).

**Rules every idea keeps.** No operatic or choir vocals. No screaming. Slow, unhurried flow with no double-time. Every take is a fresh Create. The lyrics stay word for word; only bracketed tags and directions are added.
