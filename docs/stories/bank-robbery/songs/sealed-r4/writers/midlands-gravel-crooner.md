**What shaped it**
- **Best one's public page** only shows the tags "psych folk, funk" and the V6 model, so Suno's stored box is the real starting point ([page](https://suno.com/song/024b82e8-378c-47bd-bf73-bb5eafea2ed5)). The AI listening pass describes a different-sounding track (acoustic blues, hip-hop, "Cash flow!" chants), so I didn't use it.
- **The voice is phrased as a list of sound qualities**, because a style-box guide says that works better than artist names. That guide recommends 4–7 descriptors in the order genre → tempo → instruments → voice, and the existing box already follows that ([Crosley](https://blakecrosley.com/blog/suno-style-field-style-influence)).
- **"Black Country" sits early in the voice part** to fight the American drift Jack heard. Guides say to name the accent region in the style box, and that Suno doesn't promise accent accuracy, so this has to be checked by ear ([Jack Righteous](https://jackrighteous.com/es-gb/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/how-to-make-suno-sound-like-your-real-voice-2026)). I wrote "Black Country baritone", not the word "accent", because ElevenLabs' prompting guide warns that "accent" can trigger unwanted dialect shifts ([ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/creative-platform/voices/voice-design)).
- **I kept "untrained", "warbling ornaments" and "climbing to a cry on the chorus"** from the original. They're part of Best one's sound and also fit this voice; I only added "cracked". I couldn't see what the other five writers wrote, so I can't confirm these shared original words don't overlap with theirs. My new wording is the rest: "low, gravelly", "Black Country baritone", "husky and close-miked", "lazy behind the beat".
- **Things you don't want go in the Exclude box, not the Style box**, because the guides say Suno handles negatives in the Style box badly ([sliders guide](https://jackrighteous.com/fr/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/how-to-use-suno-s-advanced-sliders-weirdness-style-audio-influence)). I kept the original exclude list and added "screaming" and "american accent".
- **Settings match Best one except Duration.** Kai's lyrics are about 1.5× as long, and squeezing them into 170 s is likely why the earlier vocals came out faster. I raised it to 255 s (170 × 1.5) so the singer can sit back.
- **I opened the song with tin whistle, hand drums and bass**, so it doesn't start on sitar (the "Indian beginning" Jack didn't like). Every word of Kai's lyrics is unchanged; I only added section tags and short bracketed directions.
