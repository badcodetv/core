**Research and how it shaped the prompt**
- **Best one's public page** ([suno.com/song/024b82e8…](https://suno.com/song/024b82e8-378c-47bd-bf73-bb5eafea2ed5)): it's tagged only "psych folk, funk" and laid out as Intro / Verse / Pre-Chorus / Chorus / Verse / Chorus / Monologue / Verse / Final Chorus. I used the same layout, with a Monologue slot where the second-verse "sorry, did I interrupt your meeting?" aside lands.
- **Why it went American last time:** a University of Bern paper on British pop notes that British singers usually switch to American pronunciation when they sing, even when their speech is clearly British ([Liverpool to Louisiana](https://www.morrissey.unibe.ch/personal/Liverpool_to_Louisiana.pdf)). A user on a different AI voice tool reported that its singing still came out mostly American however the accent was described, and that the wording mattered as much as the accent label ([OpenAI forum](https://community.openai.com/t/british-accent-and-pronuciation-in-gpt-4o-mini-tts/1150812)). So the accent is described with concrete sounds (glottal stops, dropped H's) rather than just "English", and "American accent" is added to Exclude.
- **Voice detail:** music-hall Cockney acts sang in London slang about everyday working-class life, playing set characters ([MMU thesis](https://e-space.mmu.ac.uk/632228); [Victorian Web](https://victorianweb.org/mt/musichall/cockney.html)). I left out the word "patter" on purpose, because it means rushed, tongue-twister singing ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patter_song)), and Jack wants this unhurried.
- **Being specific:** voice-design guides say to name the accent together with tone, age and pace, and to avoid vague words ([Dubformer](https://help.dubformer.ai/en/articles/8847778-voice-design-prompting), [Resemble](https://docs.resemble.ai/guides/voice-design/how-to-prompt)).

**What's kept and what changed**
- **Kept from Best one:** the genre, the 100 BPM, the sitar / tin whistle / hand drums / slinky funk bass line, every Exclude term, and the settings (v6, Variety off, weirdness 30, style influence 75, male).
- **Wording that failed the check:** the voice no longer starts with "a deep", and none of the lyric tags repeat the instrument names.
- **Intro:** the Intro tag asks for a short tin-whistle opening, to steer away from the Indian-sounding start Jack didn't like.
- **Length:** I set it to 270 s. Kai's lyrics are about 1.5× as long as the ones Best one used, which works out to about 252 s at the same pace. The extra time gives the slow talk-singing room, because the earlier takes came out faster in the vocal.
