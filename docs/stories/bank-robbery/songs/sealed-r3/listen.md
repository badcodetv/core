## Overall impression
A gritty, mid-tempo acoustic blues and hip-hop hybrid built around a driving acoustic guitar riff, heavy stomping drums, and an aggressive, raw male vocal. The mood is dark, rebellious, and tense, balancing organic folk-blues picking with boom-bap dynamic impact. To recreate its feel: set a mid-tempo groove around 90 BPM with a straight, heavy kick-and-snare pattern. Combine clean acoustic guitar picking with gritty, raspy British male rap-singing vocals, layer shouted gang-vocal hooks ("Cash flow!"), and use subtle room reverb to keep the production upfront, punchy, and raw.

## Drums
- **Groove:** Straight, mid-tempo stomping groove with a boom-bap feel.
- **Kick:** Punchy, round mid-bass thud with solid chest impact.
- **Snare:** Tight, acoustic snare hit on beats 2 and 4 with crisp top-end crack and light room resonance.
- **Hats & Cymbals:** Fast acoustic guitar strumming acts as the primary high-frequency rhythmic driver; hi-hats are minimal/sparse.
- **Feel:** Tight, driving, and heavy.

## Bass
- **Sitting:** Sits in the mid-bass range supporting the kick drum and guitar root notes.
- **Tone:** Round, warm acoustic/electric bass tone with subtle low-end body.
- **Movement:** Locks in directly with the kick drum on key beat downbeats.
- **Clarity:** Clear and clean; does not clutter the low end.

## Lead and vocals
- **Voice:** Male lead, raspy and gritty texture, mid-register, distinct British accent.
- **Delivery:** Energetic, rhythmic rap-singing delivery; conversational in verses, shouted in choruses.
- **Clarity:** Distinct and clear throughout.
- **Backing Vocals:** Group/gang vocals shouting "Cash flow!" on chorus hooks, alongside smooth background vocal harmonies ("Oohs") in transition sections.
- **Spoken audio:** Brief telephone-filtered spoken interruption at 1:41 ("Sorry, did I interrupt your meeting?").

## Pads, texture and effects
- **Instrumentation:** Acoustic guitar (strummed and picked blues/folk riffs) serves as the main harmonic engine.
- **Effects:** Short plate/room reverb on the lead vocal and snare to keep the sound close and dry.
- **Textures:** Distorted room air, subtle vocal swells, and transient acoustic strumming noise providing rhythmic texture.

## Mix balance
- **Loudest:** Lead vocals, kick drum, snare, and primary acoustic guitar.
- **Buried:** Sub-bass frequencies are minimal; focus is on punchy mid-bass.
- **Frequency Balance:** Mid-range is busy but clear; top end is bright from acoustic guitar transients, low end is tight and controlled without boomy buildup.
