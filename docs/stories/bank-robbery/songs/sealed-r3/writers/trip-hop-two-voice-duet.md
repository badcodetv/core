**What shaped it**
- **Duet tags:** Tagging each voice works better than putting "duet" in the Style box alone. Short sections help Suno keep the two voices apart, and voice changes should stay at about four to six per song. So the voices change five times: Voice 1 on the verses, Voice 2 on the first choruses, and both together on the last chorus. The Style box also says plainly that it's two different men. ([jackrighteous duet tags](https://jackrighteous.com/fr/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/duet-harmony-theme-meta-tags-suno), [multi-persona workflow](https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/can-suno-use-multiple-personas-duets-bands-ai-casts) — that second page wouldn't load for me, so this point rests on the search summary, [Reddit tips summary](https://medium.com/@aitooldiscovery/suno-reddit-tips-and-prompts-what-r-sunoai-users-actually-do-65b59d51bcad))
- **Short Style box, Variety off:** A good v6 prompt is a short brief with one clear centre, and setting Variety to 0 keeps Suno closer to your tags. Things you don't want go in Exclude. That also fixes problem 7 on your list. The Style box is about 75 words, much shorter than the 120–140 of the last two rounds. ([v6 style guide](https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/optimize-suno-v6-style-of-music-prompt))
- **Bristol sound:** Massive Attack and Tricky came out of the Wild Bunch crew. The style mixes breakbeat with dub and film-soundtrack samples, and uses vinyl crackle and a lot of reverb. Men on those records often sang in low, menacing, nearly whispered voices. That fits Voice 1, the murmured baritone. ([Epigram](https://epigram.org.uk/get-to-know-bristols-music-scene-a-history/), [Crossfadr](https://crossfadr.com/2011/11/25/genre-overview-trip-hop/), [Trip hop overview](https://encycloreader.org/r/wikipedia.php?q=Trip_hop), [Cambridge / Organised Sound](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/organised-sound/article/just-dont-call-it-trip-hop-reconciling-the-bristol-sound-style-with-the-trip-hop-genre/B4944FEFB7C30977DA7CF0CB3AC07465))
- **The "Best one" page:** The [public page](https://suno.com/song/024b82e8-378c-47bd-bf73-bb5eafea2ed5) only shows the tags "psych folk, funk". That backs up your point that its stored text doesn't describe how it sounds, so I took nothing from it.

- **"Voice 2" may not be honoured:** None of the guides covered two male voices, so it's untested whether Suno keeps two men apart with these labels. Listen for whether the chorus voice is clearly a different man.
