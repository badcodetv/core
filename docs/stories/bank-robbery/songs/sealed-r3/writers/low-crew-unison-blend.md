**What shaped it**
- **Style box (6 tags):** One guide says Suno's Style box works best with 4–7 short descriptors. With fewer, Suno falls back on its usual defaults; with more, the descriptors fight each other. So the box names the beat, the tempo, the guitar, a deep English lead and a low male crew, and nothing more.
- **Style influence 60:** The same guide pairs a short, sparse box with a looser setting and a detailed box with a stricter one. That supports 60 here, and weirdness 35 leaves Suno room to add its own colour.
- **The blend is driven from the lyrics:** Another guide notes that section labels suggesting group energy can pull a choir or chant into a song even when you don't ask for one. I'm using that on purpose. A `[Crew in unison]` label sits only on the "Robbery, robbery!" and "Debasement!" lines, and a `[Lead]` label goes back on every other line.
