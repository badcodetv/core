**What shaped it**
- **Best one's public page** ([suno.com/song/024b82e8…](https://suno.com/song/024b82e8-378c-47bd-bf73-bb5eafea2ed5)) shows only "psych folk, funk" as its style. That's even further from how the take sounds than the 29-word stored prompt. It backs up mistake 6, so I described the music by era and texture, not by Suno's own tags.
- **Suno v6 tips** ([Jack Righteous: optimize v6 style prompt](https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/optimize-suno-v6-style-of-music-prompt), [where to put your prompt](https://jackrighteous.com/en-gb/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/where-to-put-your-suno-prompt-guide), [2026 prompt guide](https://jackrighteous.com/ja/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/best-prompts-for-suno-ai-2026-guide-to-better-results)):
  - If you don't name a voice type, Suno picks one at random.
  - Stacking adjectives or mixing clashing ones weakens the result.
  - So I kept the Style box short (28 words) with one clear voice, and words you can hear, like "chopped" and "stomping".
  - These tips come mostly from one blog, so treat them as opinion.
- **The northern deadpan voice:** write-ups of Mark E. Smith describe a dry wit and a cryptic, chant-like delivery over primitive rhythms ([AV Club](https://avclub.com/remembering-the-falls-mark-e-smith-rocks-most-uncompr-1822391824), [NPR/WHYY](https://whyy.org/npr-story/mark-e-smith-uncompromising-essential-voice-musics-fringe/), [The National](https://www.thenational.ae/arts-culture/music/mark-e-smith-voice-of-the-fall-dies-aged-60-1.698525)). I kept the dryness and the near-reciting. The same sources call him snarling, ranting and shouting, so I left that out to keep to Jack's no-screaming rule. "Bored" and "droll" replace "sneer", and the bracketed directions ask for sung choruses that stay on pitch and never get shouted.
- **The era goes first** ("1979 northern English rehearsal room") instead of a genre tag, as the brief asked. The lyrics are Kai's words exactly, with only section tags and short directions added in square brackets.
- **Settings follow the brief:** Variety off and Max Mode off. Duration is left unset, which I've written as `null` because the brief says unset rather than giving a number. Check that your runner accepts `null` there.
