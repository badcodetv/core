I built this from mostly third-party Suno guides. I couldn't find Suno's own v6 documentation, so treat the tag advice as community-tested, not official.

**What shaped it**
- **Style box:** Community advice from r/SunoAI is to keep the style text under about 100 words, or Suno starts treating parts of it as optional. The order follows one guide's suggestion: genre, then feel, instruments, voice, mood and production. Section directions go in the lyrics, not the style box ([Medium roundup](https://medium.com/@aitooldiscovery/suno-reddit-tips-and-prompts-what-r-sunoai-users-actually-do-65b59d51bcad), [Jack Righteous v6 style guide](https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/optimize-suno-v6-style-of-music-prompt)). That page wouldn't load in full, so I worked from its search summary.
- **Exclude:** The advice is to say what you want first, then exclude only a few specific things you can hear. Here that's screaming and fast rap, which are Jack's rules, plus trap and EDM habits ([Jack Righteous negative prompting](https://jackrighteous.com/en-gb/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/negative-prompting-suno-v5-guide)).
- **Tags:** Short tags placed right where a change happens, like [Spoken], [A Cappella] and [Call and Response], work best. A duet guide suggests the answering voice sings short replies of about 3–6 words. So the lead sings "Robbery, robbery!" and the crowd answers with the shorter lines ([Jack Righteous meta tags](https://jackrighteous.com/en-us/blogs/music-creation-process-guide/REPLACE_NEXT_URL), [roo.beehiiv](https://roo.beehiiv.com/p/suno-prompting), [duet guide](https://jackrighteous.com/fr/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/duet-harmony-theme-meta-tags-suno), [hookgenius](https://hookgenius.app/learn/suno-metatags-complete-list/), [musci.io](https://musci.io/ja/blog/suno-tags)).
- **The psych side:** Fuzz and wah guitar, sitar, tabla and drones are the classic British psych-folk markers. Kikagaku Moyo is a modern band that mixes sitar and wah with fingerpicked guitar ([Wikipedia: Psychedelic music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychedelic_music), [Psychedelic rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychedelic_rock), [Magic Carpet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magic_Carpet_(band)), [mxdwn review](https://music.mxdwn.com/2016/05/12/reviews/kikagaku-moyo-house-in-the-tall-grass/)).
- **The reference songs:** "I Ain't Worried" is about 140 BPM with a 16th-note funk groove, so roughly 70 BPM is its half-time feel ([musicnotes](https://www.musicnotes.com/l/Vzrhw), [jamzone drums](https://jamzone.musicwill.org/Songchart/Drums-IAintWorried-OneRepublic.pdf)). "Eminence Front" runs on a repeating loop with a bassline at its centre. That's where the steady sitar riff and the deep bass come from ([Something Else Reviews](https://somethingelsereviews.com/2011/07/16/something-else-featured-artist-series-the-who/), [American Songwriter](https://americansongwriter.com/story-behind-the-who-eminence-front)). Following Jack's "clean room" request, I didn't name any artists in the prompt.
- **Settings:** I chose v6 over v6-wild because the drop to almost a cappella has to land on cue. Duration is set to the 300-second maximum because the lyrics are long for 70 BPM. Even so, Suno may cut the end short; if it does, try another fresh Create. The outro has no words because the lyrics end at "cake" and I couldn't add any.
