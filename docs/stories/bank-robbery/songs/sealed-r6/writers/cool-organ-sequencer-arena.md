- **The original arrangement:** Wikipedia says Townshend built "Eminence Front" on a chord progression he found on his Yamaha E70 home organ. It credits Entwistle on bass, Tim Gorman on Rhodes, Glyn Johns as producer (1982) and a running time of 5:39. Cash Box praised the "cleanly slicing" guitar and the "strong bottom" from bass and drums. A DJ blog describes a sequenced synth riff doubled on electric guitar. I turned these details into plain descriptions for the style field.
- **Settings:** Suno's v6 FAQ, quoted in the sunowatermark guide, says setting Variety to 0 keeps your style tags as written. My style field is detailed, so I turned Variety off.
