I wrote this as a Rossini buffo scene built like Basilio's "La calunnia è un venticello." In that aria a bass voice stays steady and the build comes from the orchestra, which fits the brief: a big Rossini lift without any speed.

**What shaped it**
- **The crescendo:** The "Rossini crescendo" (also called the "Rossini rocket") isn't just getting louder. A short phrase repeats, instruments come in a few at a time, the phrases get shorter, and the lines jump up an octave. That makes it feel faster when it isn't, so the build can come from the orchestra while the singer stays at moderato. That's why I set it to "steady unhurried pulse, never accelerating" and put [Crescendo] before the last Robbery refrain. ([onmusic dictionary](https://dev-dictionary.onmusic.org/terms/2945-rossini_crescendo), [Houston Grand Opera](https://www.houstongrandopera.org/backstage-pass/rossinis-the-barber-of-seville-top-5-musical-moments))
- **The buffo voice and patter:** Buffo singers are known for patter, the fast stream of words in arias like Bartolo's. That clashes with Jack's no-fast-rap rule. So the style asks for clear diction and long, mocking phrases, and the exclude list rules out patter and anything sped up. ([Lumen Learning](https://courses.lumenlearning.com/vccs-tcc-mus121-1/chapter/opera-buffa/), [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/EBchecked/topic/510222))
- **Suno v6 prompts:** Guides suggest writing the style as genre, then feel, then key instruments, then the voice, then the mood. They also say performance cues belong in brackets in the lyrics and unwanted things go in the Exclude field, kept short. ([Jack Righteous – v6 style prompts](https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/optimize-suno-v6-style-of-music-prompt), [Jack Righteous – where prompts go](https://jackrighteous.com/en-gb/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/where-to-put-your-suno-prompt-guide), [roo – suno prompting](https://roo.beehiiv.com/p/suno-prompting))
