**What shaped the prompt**
- **Suno advice (unofficial guides):** Put the positive description first, then keep Exclude short and limited to things you can actually hear, such as "large crowd choir". Treat Exclude as a guide that Suno may not always follow. Section labels go in the lyrics, not the style box. Order the style text as genre, feel, signature instruments, vocal role, mood, then production. v6 is the "controlled" model and v6-wild is for exploring, so I chose v6 to keep it classical and clean.
- **Heldentenor:** This voice is known for stamina, volume and a dark tone that can sound almost like a baritone in the middle range. It has to cut through a deliberately huge orchestra where recurring musical themes (leitmotifs) carry the drama. So the style asks for a dark, baritonal middle with a ringing top.
- **Wagner tuba:** Wagner invented it for the *Ring* to sit between the horns and trombones. It first carried the Valhalla theme, and its sound is described as "smoky, metallic, unearthly, majestic". That gave me the printing-press theme: a smoky, grinding chorale in the low brass that comes back at every Refrain.
- **Jack's rules:** No choir, so the Refrain is marked as solo voice and choir is excluded. No screaming, and the tempo is slow and broad, with no rap or double-time. The only tags are [Verse] and [Refrain], plus a few short directions. One of these makes "it is the devil, with a radio station" the hushed, darkened climax. Every lyric word is unchanged.
