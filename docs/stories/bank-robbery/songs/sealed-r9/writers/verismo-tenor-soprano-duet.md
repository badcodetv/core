They also say a long mix of styles comes out muddled. So the style is one programme-note scene plus one opera sound, and the lyric cues are a few words each ([Soundverse](https://www.soundverse.ai/blog/article/how-to-write-effective-suno-ai-prompts-1314)).
- **"Puccini" isn't in the Style field.** Suno blocks many artist names, so I described his sound instead: Italian verismo opera from around 1900. I kept your other sound words (divided strings, harp, swelling melodies, rubato, big romantic climaxes). This was my call. If you want the name back in, it's an easy swap ([Jack Righteous 2026 prompts](https://jackrighteous.com/ja/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/best-prompts-for-suno-ai-2026-guide-to-better-results)).
- **The ending.** It's modelled on the end of Act 1 of La Bohème, the duet "O soave fanciulla". The soprano holds a high C that fades away, the tenor often joins her on the same note, and the harp falls softly over the last chord ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/O_soave_fanciulla)). I wrote that into the final climax and the quiet postlude.
- **No rap.** The lyrics are written like rap, so the style asks for long, smooth sung lines with no drums. Rap, spoken word and screaming are all in Exclude.
- **Settings.** Variety is set low because Suno says that keeps it closer to the style ([Jack Righteous 2026 prompts](https://jackrighteous.com/ja/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/best-prompts-for-suno-ai-2026-guide-to-better-results)).
