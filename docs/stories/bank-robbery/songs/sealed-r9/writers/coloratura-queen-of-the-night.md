- **Orchestra:** Mozart scored it for strings, pairs of flutes, oboes and bassoons, natural horns, trumpets and timpani. I kept your list (strings, oboes, horns, harpsichord) and added only timpani, because it's authentic and still isn't "modern drums". The original aria has no harpsichord, so I gave it the dry "recitative" sections, where Mozart would have used it.
- **The final verses:** The climax of Mozart's aria has the Queen singing alone, alternating with loud orchestral chords. I copied that idea in the closing "hungry people… don't just eat cake" verses, marked as stabs from the full orchestra.
