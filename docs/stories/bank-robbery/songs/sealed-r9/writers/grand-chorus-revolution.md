I wrote this as a grand opera crowd scene, not a song with a backing choir. The crowd is the main voice and the baritone only leads it into each refrain.

**What shaped it**
- **The genre.** French grand opera at the Paris Opéra (about 1828–1860) was built on big choruses, "large choral tableaux", rival choruses, crowd spectacle and huge endings, like the volcano that erupts at the end of *La muette de Portici*. I wrote the style text to sound like a staged mob scene and gave the closing verses a big final scene. (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_opera)
- **The build.** A forum discussion of *Les Huguenots* praises its chorus writing and orchestration, and one commenter calls the 4th and 5th acts "one long crescendo". I copied that shape: the last four verses (assets, wealth gap, revolution, cake) keep growing into the climax and don't drop back down. (https://unsungcomposers.com/forum/index.php?msg=1431)
- **How Suno reads tags.** The guides say vocal tags at the end of a long style field often get ignored, so I put the choir and baritone first in the style and repeated them in the section tags. They also say to keep exclusions to two or three, to describe a sound rather than name artists, and that a low Variety setting keeps the model closer to your tags. None of these guides cover v6 choir tags specifically, so treat that part as my judgement. (https://medium.com/@aitooldiscovery/suno-reddit-tips-and-prompts-what-r-sunoai-users-actually-do-65b59d51bcad, https://frankx.ai/guides/suno-prompt-playbook, https://learnprompting.org/blog/guide-suno, https://alexanderweichart.de/2_Areas/Music/Suno-Instructions)
- **Settings.**
  - **vocalGender "male":** the baritone leads, and the choir is written into the style and tags.
  - **Length:** the lyrics are long, so it's set to the 300-second maximum. The ending may still get cut off.
  - **Your rules:** no screaming, no rap or fast delivery, no shouting. Every refrain is marked as sung, and this is a fresh Create.
- **Lyrics.** Every word and spelling is unchanged, including "dispraxic", "da basement" and the curly apostrophe in "heaven’s". Only bracketed tags were added.
