I based this on Bizet's actual *Habanera* score and on general Suno v6 prompting advice. No source covered opera prompting in Suno specifically.

- **The score:** In Bizet's aria each verse is in D minor and each refrain switches to D major. A D pedal tone sits underneath the whole time, the flute plays low and the vocal line has triplets in it. Carmen sings the refrains herself, and when she finishes the women's chorus sings the refrain again. The orchestra uses tambourine and triangle, and two trumpets play only the very last chord. All of that went into the style prompt and the directions in brackets: minor verses opening into a major refrain, a held low note under everything, a chorus of women answering her, and trumpets saved for the last chord. ([Wikipedia – Habanera (aria)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habanera_(aria)), [Met Opera – The Global Habanera](https://www.metopera.org/learn/education/the-global-habanera/))
- **The rhythm:** The Met's guide says the cellos play the habanera pattern first, under Carmen's opening lines, and she and the chorus then pick it up. So the intro starts on cellos alone. The Minnesota Opera teaching guide says her phrases are short and come in four-bar blocks, which is why I asked for clipped, teasing delivery. ([MN Opera guide](https://www.mnopera.org/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/CarmenOperaBox.pdf))
- **How Suno reads prompts:** One v6 guide suggests putting the genre and rhythm first, then signature instruments, then the voice, then the mood and production. It also says to keep the exclude list short, because negative terms can fight with what you're asking for. So the style box starts with your rhythm-and-voice phrase, and the exclude list is just rap, screaming and modern drums, with three closely related terms. Song sections and stage directions go in the lyrics field, not the style box. ([Jack Righteous – optimize v6 style prompt](https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/optimize-suno-v6-style-of-music-prompt), [Jack Righteous – Inside Suno V6 prompting](https://jackrighteous.com/en-gb/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/where-to-put-your-suno-prompt-guide))

To have the chorus echo "Debasement! Debasement!" without adding or repeating any of Jack's words, I gave that exact line to [Chorus Response]. The Escobar verse gets Spanish guitar, castanets and a trumpet solo. I set the length to the 300-second maximum because the lyrics are long, but the song may still be cut off before the end.
