=== CLIP d02 ===
Locked-off camera, framing holds. The white flash fades to dim night light over two seconds. The masked man finishes his stumble: one heavy step down onto the next stair, gloved arm still shielding his head, then he holds still, crouched. The sack drops onto the step and stays there. The few banknotes drift slowly down and settle on the stairs. Mask, gloves and suit unchanged; the wall and stairs stay the same; nothing else enters. SFX: a flash bulb pop, a boot on stone, papers fluttering.
ENDS_ON: The robber crouched on a lower step, arm over his head, the sack and a scatter of notes lying still on the stairs in dim light.
WHY: One stumble that finishes by four seconds, with the flash fading and the notes settling, gives a clean out-point.

SFX: a hushed theatre murmur, soft wing flutter, a faint gasp from the crowd.

WHY: One small motion on stage and one small shared motion in the stalls shows the trick and the theft side by side without crowd movement that could morph.
