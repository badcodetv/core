=== CLIP y01-b ===
Locked camera fixed in the back seat, moving with the car; no zoom, pan or tilt. The car is already rolling fast. From the first moment the driver on the left hauls the wheel hard left with both gloved hands, his shoulders and elbows leaning into the turn; the heavy car body rolls on its springs and both men sway with it, then settle as it straightens. Through the windscreen the brick street swings and slides past. Both men keep facing forward. The masks and the grey hat stay exactly as they are: white ring, red triangle, no holes. Engine roar, tyres drumming on cobbles. No speech.
ENDS_ON: The same back-seat framing, both masked heads large and facing forward, the car straightened and the street running ahead through the windscreen.
WHY: The world outside the glass does all the moving while the two heads stay big and nearly still, so the masks never turn or shrink and the car carries real weight.

=== CLIP y02-a ===
Locked camera low at the wet kerb; no zoom, pan or tilt. The car is already sliding broadside, its rear wheel spinning in place and pouring thick white smoke that rolls left along the cobbles. The heavy car slides only a short way right, staying in frame, then the tyre grips and the body rocks once on its springs and settles. The man leaning from the window keeps the gun pointed straight up at the sky in both gloved hands and pumps it upward twice, head steady toward the lens. The driver stays at the wheel. Both masks stay exactly as they are: white ring, red triangle, no holes. Tyre screech, engine.
ENDS_ON: The car settled close to the lens, the green-masked man still leaning out with the gun pointing at the sky, the driver behind him, smoke drifting across the street.
WHY: One dominant motion, a short heavy slide that grips and settles, keeps both masks large and three-quarter to the lens for the whole clip.

=== CLIP y03-a ===
Slow motion, locked camera; no zoom, pan or tilt. He keeps flying sideways through the air, travelling only a little to the right and sinking slightly as gravity takes him, his cream jacket flapping and his trouser legs rippling. He holds the gun pointed straight up at the ceiling in both gloved hands the whole time. His head stays square to the lens and large in frame. Behind him glass crystals fall steadily, glint in the window light and bounce off the marble counter. The mask stays exactly as it is: white ring, red triangle, no holes. Tinkling glass, cloth snapping. No speech.
ENDS_ON: Still mid-dive, a little lower and further right, mask facing the lens, gun up, crystals scattered on the counter behind him.
WHY: Holding the dive in slow motion with only a short drift keeps the mask face-on and big, and the falling crystals give the shot its motion without any landing or head turn.
