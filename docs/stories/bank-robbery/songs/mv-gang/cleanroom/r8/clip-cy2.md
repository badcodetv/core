=== CLIP y04-a ===
Locked-off camera, tripod-stable, no zoom, pan or tilt; one continuous shot. The man stands still in the window frame of the fallen house front. At once his gloved hand brushes the dust off his lapel in two firm strokes, then he squares his shoulders, tugs his jacket straight with one hand and sets his feet, the carpet bag held steady at his side. A last puff of plaster dust settles round his boots. His head stays square to the lens throughout; his knitted mask does not change. Sound: settling timber creaks, a cloth brush, a distant dog.
ENDS_ON: The man upright and square to camera in the window frame, jacket straightened, bag in hand, the dust settling around him.
WHY: One small, weighty action in the shoulders and hands lets the gag play while the large face-on mask stays untouched.

=== CLIP y05-a ===
Locked-off camera, tripod-stable, no zoom, pan or tilt; one continuous shot. The man leans on the standing barrel with both gloved hands flat on its rim. Whiskey keeps glugging from the split barrel at his feet and spreads slowly across the cobbles in a widening amber puddle. He straightens his back, plants both hands on the barrel lid and gives it one firm, heavy pat, then rests there, deadpan. His head stays still, angled down at the spill; his knitted mask does not change. Sound: liquid gurgling, a dull wooden thump.
ENDS_ON: The man straightened, both gloved hands resting on the standing barrel, the amber puddle wider across the cobbles.
WHY: Spreading liquid is a strong pattern for the model and the single heavy pat gives weight without moving the head or crossing the mask.

=== CLIP y06-a ===
Locked-off camera, tripod-stable, no zoom, pan or tilt; one continuous shot. The two men hold the canvas sack between them over the basket rim and give it two hard, shared shakes, so a thick stream of banknotes tumbles out and flutters away down towards the clouds. On the third beat they lift the empty sack and hold it out flat, deadpan, facing the lens. Their heads stay square to the camera and do not turn; both knitted masks stay exactly as they are. The ropes sway a little. Sound: wind, flapping paper, creaking wicker.
ENDS_ON: Both men facing the lens holding the empty sack out over the rim, the last notes drifting down to the clouds below.
WHY: All the energy is in the arms and the falling notes, below both masks, so the large face-on masks have nothing to redraw.
