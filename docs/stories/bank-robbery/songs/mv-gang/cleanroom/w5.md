=== SHOT a03 ===
A music-video still: a black 1928 sedan skids sideways round a city street corner at noon, its rear wheels smoking, spokes blurred to discs, body rolled hard onto its outer springs. Shot from a low 24mm lens at kerb height as the car slides toward camera, a fruit stall's crates tumbling in its path. RUST drives, gloved hands crossed on the wheel: rust-gold hand-knitted wool balaclava with a black chain-stitched ring outlining the face, seamless knit where the face would be, a small rounded red felt triangle high over his right eye, on the left of the picture; black double-breasted suit, white tie. MARINE clings to the running board, cream linen suit flapping, marine-blue balaclava with a cream ring, red triangle over his right eye. Hard overhead sun, short black shadows, brick and green-painted shopfronts with blank awnings. Grainy 1950s Technicolor film, slight lens softness.
WHY: A low, close, hard-noon kerbside angle makes the corner skid feel violent and keeps both masks big enough for the triangle to read.

=== SHOT x01 ===
A music-video still at blue dusk: two black 1920s sedans race side by side down a wide city avenue, shot on a long 135mm lens from a camera car running alongside, so both cars stay sharp while the lamp posts and lit bakery windows behind streak sideways. On the near car's running board CRIMSON stands braced, grey fedora on top of his crimson hand-knitted balaclava with a cream face-ring and a rounded red felt triangle over his right eye, on the left of the picture; charcoal pinstripe double-breasted suit. He fires a 1920s Thompson prop gun with a drum magazine straight up at the sky, an orange muzzle flash lighting the knit. On the far car's running board SILVER, oxblood suit and bow tie, silver-grey balaclava with a crimson ring, fires his Thompson into the air too. Black leather gloves, cuffs down, collars up. Cool dusk sky, warm window light, coarse film grain.
WHY: A long-lens tracking angle stacks both running boards into one frame, and the guns firing skyward keep the shootout about noise and nerve, not targets.

=== SHOT x02 ===
A music-video still: three stripped-down 1920s roadsters, fenders off, engines bare, cut-down windscreens, race flat out across a cracked white dry lake bed at sunset, each dragging a long ochre dust plume. Shot wide on a 35mm lens from ground level ahead and to one side, the sun low behind the cars so every plume glows amber and the bodies go dark against it. RUST drives the lead car, a bare-chassis speedster with two bucket seats and a round fuel tank behind, leaning into the wheel with black-gloved hands: rust-gold hand-knitted balaclava, black chain-stitched ring round the face, smooth knit where the face would be, a small rounded red felt triangle over his right eye, on the left of the picture as he heads toward camera; black double-breasted suit, white tie whipping back. Distant purple mountains, nothing else on the flat. Warm grainy 1930s colour film, slight halation.
WHY: The open lake bed, low backlight and wide ground-level angle give a pure speed-and-dust image unlike the city shots.

=== SHOT x03 ===
A music-video still: night, a blizzard over a frozen river, the white ice cracked in long dark lines. Shot on an 85mm lens from just behind the lead car's front fender, so the edge of a headlamp and a doorless black 1920s sedan fill the right of the frame, two more doorless cars queued behind. In the twin headlight beams, snow streaking through them, three gang members step out of the dark toward the convoy, 1920s Thompson prop guns held pointing up at the sky: CRIMSON in a grey fedora and charcoal pinstripe, BOTTLE in brown tweed with a bottle-green balaclava and oatmeal ring, SILVER in oxblood with a silver-grey balaclava and crimson ring. Each hand-knitted mask covers the whole head with seamless knit, a contrasting ring outlining the face and a rounded red felt triangle over the right eye, on the left of the picture. Black gloves, collars up, snow on the shoulders. Cold blue-white light, deep black beyond, heavy grain.
WHY: Shooting from behind the headlamp makes the hold-up read instantly as the gang ambushing a whiskey convoy on the ice, lit only by the cars they are stopping.
