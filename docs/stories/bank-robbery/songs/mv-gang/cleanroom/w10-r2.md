=== SHOT f02 ===
A music-video still set on a grey-green 1920s harbour at overcast noon, seen from water level with a 35mm lens, the waterline cutting across the bottom third of the frame. Three portly bankers in black silk top hats, frock coats and gold watch chains sit in a small varnished wooden rowing boat that is sinking stern first, brown water already over the thwarts. Each banker scoops water out over the side with a tin bucket packed full of green banknotes, and the notes slosh out with the water and float around the hull in soggy drifts. The nearest banker's top hat is tipped back, cheeks puffed, sleeves soaked to the elbow. Behind them a tall steam liner's rust-red hull and a white wooden pier fill the background, softened by haze. None of the six masked men in the attached reference image appear in this shot. Flat diffuse daylight from the left, muted Technicolor colour, coarse film grain, slight softness at the edges.
WHY: The bankers literally bail water with buckets of cash from a boat that sinks anyway, so "bail" and "bail out" collide in one picture.

=== SHOT f04 ===
A music-video still on a mansion lawn at golden late afternoon, shot from a low angle with a 24mm lens close behind SILVER. SILVER's mask and suit are exactly as in the attached reference image, where he is the man on the far right. He stands in the lower right foreground in his oxblood-red suit and bow tie, black leather gloves, arms folded, his head tipped back and turned three-quarters toward camera: a seamless hand-knitted silver-grey rib wool hood covers his whole head and neck, tucked into a turned-up collar, smooth knit where the face would be, a large white wool ring stitched round the face from brow to chin, and one small rounded red felt triangle sewn high inside the ring over his left eye, on the right side of the picture at about two o'clock. Above him a huge hot-air balloon stitched together from thousands of green banknotes swells as it inflates, burners roaring orange. In its wicker basket a pair of rich couples in furs and boaters wave. A white-columned mansion and clipped hedges behind. Warm low sun from the right, long shadows, fine grain.
WHY: Money literally inflating into a balloon that lifts the rich away while the robber watches unimpressed shows "asset inflation" at a glance.

=== SHOT f05 ===
A music-video still inside a small 1920s corner bakery, a cramped warm room with mint-green tiled walls and empty wooden shelves, lit by one bare bulb hanging over the counter. RAP: RUST leans right into a wide-angle 18mm lens at chest height, close and slightly low, his gloved left hand thrust toward the camera counting off on three leather fingers, his right fist pressed to his chest. RUST's mask and suit are exactly as in the attached reference image, where he is the fifth man from the left. He wears his black double-breasted suit, white tie, black leather gloves, collar up; a seamless hand-knitted rust-gold rib wool hood covers his whole head and neck with smooth knit where the face would be, a large white wool ring stitched round the face from brow to chin, and one small rounded red felt triangle sewn high inside the ring over his left eye, on the right side of the picture at about two o'clock. Behind him a worn working man in a flat cap and patched overalls has pushed a heaped wooden wheelbarrow of banknotes to the counter, and the baker hands him a single tiny bread roll. Plain film grain, soft lens falloff.
WHY: A wheelbarrow of cash buying one tiny roll, with Rust counting it out to camera, makes "print more" and what it does to wages instantly readable.

=== SHOT f09 ===
A music-video still of a candlelit 1920s banquet in a dining room with deep teal walls and gilt mirrors, shot from table height with a 50mm lens looking up past the head of the table. A stout dowager in a lilac silk gown, ropes of pearls and a feathered headband sits in the foreground, a silver fork plunged into a towering five-tier white iced cake, her mouth open in mid-bite. Above her, through an opened glass skylight black with night, three gang members descend on taut ropes, gloved hands gripping, legs braced: PLUM, CRIMSON and MARINE, their masks and suits exactly as in the attached reference image. PLUM in his chalk-striped mustard-yellow suit and plum hood; CRIMSON in his charcoal pinstripe, crimson hood and grey fedora; MARINE in cream linen and a marine-blue hood. Each hood is seamless hand-knitted rib wool, smooth where the face would be, with a large white wool ring round the face from brow to chin, looking down at her, each with one small rounded red felt triangle high inside the ring over his left eye, on the right side of the picture. Warm candlelight from below, cool moonlight from the skylight, grain.
WHY: The rich woman gorging on cake while the gang drops in from above shows "don't just eat cake" as a literal threat to the banquet.
