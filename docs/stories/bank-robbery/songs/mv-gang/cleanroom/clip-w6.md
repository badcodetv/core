Locked-off camera, framing holds. The masked man takes two slow steps along the line toward the lens, head nodding on a steady beat. === CLIP v04 ===
Locked-off camera, framing holds. The masked man in the foreground shrugs on the beat, palms up, shoulders rising to his ears and dropping, three times, head tilting with each one, then holds the last shrug. The few loose banknotes already in the air drift slowly on the wind toward the bank at the end of the street. The worker stays where he is, looking down at his envelope. Mask, suit and gloves stay unchanged; no new objects appear. SFX: wind gusts, papers fluttering.
ENDS_ON: The masked man frozen mid-shrug, palms up, the last notes floating near the bank's open window.
WHY: The shrug is the lyric's question, and the drifting notes answer it without any new motion being invented.

=== CLIP d03 ===
Locked-off camera, framing holds. The crowd at the gates shakes raised fists on a steady beat, four times, shoulders surging forward together. One man grips the bars with both hands and rattles the chained gate; the gate stays shut and the chain holds. Smoke rolls slowly from the chimneys, furnace glow flickering in the mill windows. Every person stays in place; no one enters or leaves. SFX: iron gate clanking, chain rattling. Ambient noise: a wordless angry crowd roar, distant furnace rumble.
ENDS_ON: The crowd pressed at the closed, chained gate with fists raised high, smoke still rising behind.
WHY: A single rhythmic surge of fists and one rattled gate shows the angry mob plainly, with only smoke as background motion.

=== CLIP f08 ===
Locked-off camera, framing holds. The black 1920s open touring car rolls slowly forward down the street at walking pace, staying fully in frame and staying the same car. The two masked men standing in the back fling a few banknotes upward with gloved hands, twice each, and the notes flutter slowly down onto the cobbles. The people on the stoops and at the windows wave their arms and reach up. Masks and suits stay unchanged; no vehicles appear. SFX: engine putter, notes fluttering. Ambient noise: cheering street crowd.
ENDS_ON: The car a little further down the street toward the lens, the two men with arms raised after a throw, notes settling on the cobbles.
WHY: One slow car roll plus a simple repeated throw shows the gang giving the money back, with the car kept whole and in frame.
