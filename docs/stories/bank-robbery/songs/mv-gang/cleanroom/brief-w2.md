## C. Performing rap to camera

- **Direct address is the core convention.** In performance videos the artist plays to the lens, and "the awareness of audience, the direct mode of address" is the defining trait. [reported, classroom-grade] https://media-studies.com/music-video-genre/
- **Beat-marking gestures are the most common.** A study of 50 rap videos (136 gesture types, more than 3,000 occurrences) found that gestures marking the beat dominate. Then come iconic gestures (miming what the words say) and pointing gestures, plus a category that opens or closes a verse. [tested] https://is.muni.cz/publication/1686116
- **Pointing:** most rappers who point do it "into the void or at the camera". [reported] https://djbooth.net/features/2018-08-16-best-worst-hand-gestures-in-rap-history/
- **Fisheye plus low angle plus ultrawide.** The 1990s signature: the lens bulges the centre, so a face or hand pushed toward the lens grows huge while the room bends away behind it. [reported] https://www.artforum.com/columns/sasha-geffen-on-willow-and-olivia-rodrigo-250233/ ; https://djbooth.net/features/2020-02-04-an-ode-to-hype-williams-rap-video-director
- **Close-ups are the convention.** Labels want lots of close-ups of the artist; low angles read as power over the viewer. [reported, **thin**: student material] https://www.slideshare.net/freddiegass/technical-and-symbolic-conventions
- **Crew shots.** The group is framed as a unit, often moving toward a tracking camera. [reported, **thin**] https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/media-part-2/12161072
- Physical words for the gestures, ready for prompts. All [inference] from the study's categories and common practice:
  - chopping the beat: a flat hand slicing down on each bar;
  - a forearm pumping with a loose fist;
  - a straight-arm point into the lens, the glove filling a third of the frame;
  - two-handed framing: palms out, fingers spread, pushing toward the camera;
  - counting off on gloved fingers;
  - the thumb pointing back at the rapper's own chest ("me");
  - a hand flat at the chest then flung open (a reveal);
  - the head and shoulders leaning in over a low lens, so the frame looks up a nostril line (here, at the balaclava's knit);
  - the whole crew behind the lead rapper, arms folded, one man mirroring the lead's gesture;
  - a hand rubbing finger and thumb (money);
  - throwing imaginary bills off the fingertips;
  - a hand cupped behind the head (ear) to the camera.
- For a figure with no face, gestures carry all the expression: make hands and shoulders the acting [inference].

## E. Economics lines shown literally in a 1920s setting

1. **Money printer running hot.** A 1920s rotary press glowing red, steam off the rollers, sheets of banknotes pouring onto the floor like a waterfall, robbers shovelling them. Based on the Weimar presses [inference].
2. **Wheelbarrows of cash.** Shoppers did carry banknotes in buckets, bags and wheelbarrows in 1923. **Warning:** the famous wheelbarrow photo is misattributed (it is US, 1910).
