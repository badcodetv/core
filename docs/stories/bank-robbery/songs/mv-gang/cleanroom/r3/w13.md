=== SHOT k17 ===
A steep low-angle shot looking up the stone face of a 1920s bank tower toward a huge clock, with a wide lens and hard late-afternoon sun raking from the left. The masks and suits are exactly as in the attached reference image; this shot shows PLUM alone. He hangs at full stretch from the long black minute hand with one gloved fist, legs swinging, his mustard chalk-stripe suit pulled tight across his broad back. The clock hands sit at a quarter to three. His other hand holds a fat canvas cash bag, and he has lifted that wrist to check the watch strapped over his glove cuff against the clock, head tilted in mild disappointment. The clock face is plain cream enamel with black tick marks only, no numbers. His knitted plum balaclava has a white wool ring round the face and a small red felt triangle over his left eye, on the right of the picture. Black leather gloves, no skin. Far below, black Model T traffic, out of focus. No readable words anywhere. Full frame, 16:9.
WHY: The man who is late against the clock that robs everyone's money of value plays "asset inflation" as a stunt played straight with a petty deadpan.

=== SHOT k20 ===
An aerial shot from slightly above and behind a flying yellow-and-silver 1920s biplane over endless green cornfields at golden hour, a 24mm wide lens, the low sun throwing a hard warm rim light on everything and long shadows across the fields below. The masks and suits are exactly as in the attached reference image; this shot shows SILVER alone. He stands tall on the top wing, one black-gloved hand on a single strut, his oxblood suit and bow tie whipped flat by the gale, a canvas cash bag swinging in his free hand as he steps out toward the wingtip of a second plane that just enters the edge of the frame on the right. His grey fedora sits perfectly still on top of his head, untouched by the wind. He turns toward the camera; his knitted silver-grey balaclava has a white wool ring round the face and a small red felt triangle over his left eye, on the right of the picture. No skin anywhere. Doped canvas wings show stitching and oil streaks. No readable lettering. Full frame, 16:9.
WHY: Carrying the money from one plane to the next in a gale with an unruffled hat makes "stacking up the cash by the grand" a too-cool stunt where the rich never lose their composure.
