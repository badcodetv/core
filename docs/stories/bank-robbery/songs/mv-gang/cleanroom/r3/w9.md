=== SHOT k13 ===
A high three-quarter view looking down into a cramped 1920s speakeasy back room. Five masked robbers stand in a tight circle on a scuffed black-and-white tiled floor: PLUM, BOTTLE, CRIMSON, MARINE and SILVER, their masks and suits exactly as in the attached reference image. Each man aims a gloved finger-gun at the man beside him, arm locked straight, shoulders squared, dead serious, everyone frozen. BOTTLE holds a ripe banana in exactly the same grip, aimed just as gravely. The masks are hand-knitted rib wool with no holes, a white wool ring round each face and one small rounded red felt triangle over each man's left eye, which sits on the right side of his face as he turns toward the camera. Black leather gloves, collars up, no skin. One bare bulb swings overhead on its flex, throwing hard moving shadows across stacked gin crates, a dented copper still and cigar smoke hanging in the beam. Shot on a 28mm lens, deep blacks, film grain.
WHY: The stand-off is played completely straight, so the banana does all the joking, and the circle of aimed hands is pure tension for that line.

=== SHOT k15 ===
A steep low-angle wide shot from the polished terrazzo dance floor of a grand art deco ballroom, looking up past fluted gold columns and a stepped sunburst ceiling. MARINE, his mask and suit exactly as in the attached reference image, swings across the room hanging one-handed from a huge tiered cut-crystal chandelier, cream linen suit rippling, two-tone shoes kicked out, legs scissored like a star taking a bow. In his other gloved hand a Thompson submachine gun points straight up at the ceiling. His hand-knitted rib-wool mask has no holes, a white wool ring round his face and one small rounded red felt triangle over his left eye, on the right side of the picture as he faces us. Black leather gloves, no skin. Below him couples in satin evening gowns and tailcoats duck and cover their heads, champagne glasses tipping. WHY: The spotlight tracking a man on a chandelier is the most self-regarding stunt there is, which is exactly the real star.

In the foreground, seen from behind and slightly to the side, CRIMSON, his mask and suit exactly as in the attached reference image, fires a Thompson submachine gun upward along the line of lamps, grey fedora on top of his mask, charcoal pinstripe double-breasted coat flaring, braced wide. WHY: The dark rolling down the hall splits the two sides of the room, and the lyric's factions sit on either side of his gun.
