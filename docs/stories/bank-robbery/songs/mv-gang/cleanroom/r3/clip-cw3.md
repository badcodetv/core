=== CLIP k12-a ===
Locked-off camera, framing holds. From the first frame the cream roadster rolls slowly forward toward the lower left and stays fully in frame. The standing man keeps flinging fistfuls of notes from the sack in a steady rhythm, his raised arm pumping. The notes flutter and drift down onto the crowd, who snatch at them. The driver stays still, hands on the wheel. Same car, same masks and gloves throughout; no one new appears. SFX: idling engine, crowd cheering, paper rustling.
ENDS_ON: The roadster a little nearer the lower left, still whole in frame, the man mid-fling with notes drifting over the reaching crowd.
WHY: One steady fling-and-flutter carries the set piece while the slow roll gives motion without a car that can teleport or leave the frame.

=== CLIP k13-b ===
Locked-off camera, high angle holds. From the first frame the bare bulb swings hard on its flex, its hard shadows sweeping across the tiled floor and crates. All five men stay frozen, arms locked, dead serious. On the second second, all five drop their thumbs at the same instant like a pistol hammer, and the man with the banana does the same, then they hold perfectly still. Smoke drifts in the beam. Masks and gloves unchanged; no one else enters. SFX: creaking flex, five tiny clicks in unison, low hum.
ENDS_ON: The five still locked in the circle, thumbs down, the bulb still swinging and shadows moving over them.
WHY: The swinging light supplies the tension from frame one, and the synchronised thumb-cock is a single deadpan beat that plays the stand-off cliché too seriously.

=== CLIP k14-a ===
Slow-motion bullet time, locked-off camera, framing holds. From the first frame the man in the mustard suit keeps gliding slowly forward toward frame left, body still horizontal above the baize, jacket rippling. The glass shards and champagne beads drift outward and sink gently; the burst chips tumble slowly toward the empty chair on the right and begin to drop onto the table. The onlookers stay where they are. Mask, gloves and suit unchanged; nothing new enters. SFX: deep slowed whoosh, tinkling glass, chips clattering.
ENDS_ON: The man still airborne over the left half of the table, shards and chips falling around him, several chips landing near the empty chair.
WHY: The still is a bullet-time freeze, so the clip simply continues the dive in exaggerated slow motion with one direction of travel and debris that falls with gravity.

=== CLIP k16-a ===
Locked-off camera, framing holds. From the first frame the man keeps firing the prop drum-magazine submachine gun straight up at the hanging globe lamps, braced wide, the barrel staying pointed upward. Bright flashes at the muzzle, small recoil in his shoulders. Each lamp he aims at bursts into falling glass, sparks drop and die out, and the near end of the hall grows darker while the far lamps glow. Plaster dust drifts. Mask, hat and gloves unchanged; no one else appears. SFX: rapid rattling bursts, glass shattering, echo in the marble hall.
ENDS_ON: The man silhouetted and still firing upward, the nearest lamps gone dark, glass and sparks falling, far lamps still lit.
WHY: The shooting-out-the-lights cliché is one continuous action from the first second, aimed only at the lamps, with debris that falls with gravity.
