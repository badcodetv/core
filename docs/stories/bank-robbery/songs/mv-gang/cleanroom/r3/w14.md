=== SHOT k21 ===
A wide, high view from just behind and above a varnished mahogany rum-runner speedboat at dawn, 1920s Atlantic coast, sea flat and pewter, low sun rising behind the camera so warm light rakes across the boat's chrome cleats and brass rail. CRIMSON and MARINE stand at the stern facing the camera, the masks and suits exactly as in the attached reference image, the red triangle over each man's left eye, on the right side of the picture. Black leather gloves, cuffs over the wrists, no skin. Together they tip a wooden crate over the transom, a slab of banknotes sliding out mid-pour. Behind them, toward us, a straight mile-long wake glitters with floating pale green paper notes, no legible print. Far off on the horizon, a tiny grey patrol boat. CRIMSON's other hand licks-and-counts one note before it goes. Shot on 35mm film, 28mm lens, fine grain, the paper catching gold rim light. 16:9, edge to edge.
WHY: Daylight wide keeps it clear of the night boat chase and makes "stacks and stacks" literal as a sea of paper, with the note-counting as the deadpan giveaway.

=== SHOT k22 ===
Low angle from the cobbled street looking up at a 1920s elevated railway as a wooden train car leans into a tight curve overhead, hard midday sun slicing through the steel trestle and throwing striped shadows across everything. RUST, mask and suit exactly as in the attached reference image, stands on the car's outside step, one black-gloved hand on the brass grab rail, the other holding a fat canvas cash bag swinging out over the drop, his white tie blown sideways. His red triangle sits over his left eye, on the right side of the picture; no skin shows, cuffs over the wrists, collar up. Through the open window behind him a bored commuter in a bowler hat, reading a folded paper with no legible print, holds out a ticket to him without looking up. Riveted girders, soot, chipped green paint, no readable signs. 135mm long lens compressing the trestle, slight halation on the sunlit rail, 35mm film grain. 16:9, edge to edge.
WHY: The commuter's bored ticket hand-off turns a train-hanging stunt into the daily grind of "earning that dough, but where does it go".

=== SHOT k23 ===
Blue hour over 1920s Manhattan, seen from a steep low angle on a skyscraper's art deco observation deck, chrome-capped parapet and fluted stone in the foreground. Above, a silver-grey blimp with a plain, unmarked envelope noses against the tall mooring mast, its belly lit warm by a deck floodlight, city towers glowing below in amber windows. MARINE slides down a single taut rope from the gondola, one gloved hand above the other, two-tone shoes pointed, cream linen suit flaring. On the deck PLUM, BOTTLE, CRIMSON, RUST and SILVER already stand in a loose row, looking up, hands in pockets; the masks and suits exactly as in the attached reference image. Every red triangle sits over the man's left eye, on the right side of the picture for those facing us. Black leather gloves, no skin anywhere. No lettering anywhere. 24mm wide lens, deep focus, hard floodlight rim on the knitted wool, cool sky against warm light, 35mm film grain. 16:9, edge to edge.
WHY: Arriving like a state delegation by airship on top of the city's tallest tower makes "state level manipulation" look grand and absurd at once.

=== SHOT k24 ===
Bright high noon above an endless white sea of cloud, a hard unfiltered sun overhead and a deep cobalt sky, a striped canvas gas balloon filling the top of the frame. Medium shot from level with a wicker basket, as if from a second balloon alongside, 50mm lens. PLUM and BOTTLE stand in the basket, the masks and suits exactly as in the attached reference image, both red triangles over the left eye, on the right side of the picture as they face us. Black leather gloves, cuffs over the wrists, no skin. BOTTLE heaves a burlap sack of banknotes over the rim, pale green paper notes with no legible print spilling and fluttering down into the clouds; PLUM, chest puffed, throws both arms wide in a showman's raise-the-roof at the sky, delighted, chalk-stripe mustard suit blazing in the sun. Ropes, sandbag hooks, worn wicker with frayed ends, brass valve cord. Crisp shadows, sun glint on the gloves, fine 35mm film grain. 16:9, edge to edge.
WHY: Throwing money out to rise is the cleanest picture of "crime is asset inflation": the less the cash is worth, the higher they go.
