=== CLIP k07-a ===
Locked-off overhead camera, framing holds. From the first frame the black sedan keeps sliding sideways in its arc, rear wheels spinning, and rolls steadily toward the lower left of frame. The passenger keeps his arm straight up, prop pistol pointed at the sky, perfectly still and bored. Thick white tyre smoke billows and spreads behind the car under the lamp. The same one car the whole time; the street stays empty. SFX: tyre squeal, engine roar, rain hiss.
ENDS_ON: The same sedan near the lower left corner, still sideways and fully in frame, with a wall of glowing smoke filling the crossroads behind it.
WHY: The handbrake slide is the whole set piece, so one continuous slide in one direction carries it with nothing to morph or teleport.

=== CLIP k09-a ===
Locked-off camera, framing holds. The wooden speedboat on the left keeps leaping through its wake, bow high, then drops and slaps back onto the water in a wide fan of white spray. The man in the stern foreground holds his point at the horizon, dead still, and lowers his chin to glance at the pocket watch in his other hand. The searchlight beam stays fixed. Wardrobe and crates unchanged. SFX: engine snarl, a heavy splash, wind and spray.
ENDS_ON: The speedboat back down in a cloud of falling spray, and the man in the foreground still pointing, head tipped down to his watch.
WHY: One splash-down gives the beat its hit while the deadpan watch-check plays the cool against the chaos.

=== CLIP k10-a ===
Locked-off long-lens side view, framing holds. The steam train rolls steadily toward the left across the trestle, smoke pouring back over the carriages. On the roof the two men keep sprinting toward the right in long strides, cash sacks swinging, and the two hats tumble away behind them on the wind and drift out of the top of the frame. The man on the right keeps his sarcastic point at the camera. SFX: chugging engine, rattling rails, a steam whistle, wind.
ENDS_ON: The two men still running along the same carriage roof, both bareheaded, the train still crossing the trestle in gold light.
WHY: Running against a moving train is the train-roof cliché played as a treadmill gag, and one continuous run is all the cut needs.

=== CLIP k11-a ===
Locked-off low camera, framing holds. The silver three-engine plane keeps climbing slowly upward, propellers spinning, wheels rising further off the field. The man hanging from it swings gently, legs dangling, and lifts his free gloved hand into a slow, bored, regal wave, side to side. The empty ladder sways. Far behind, the same three small black sedans bounce through the furrows, kicking up dust, never getting closer. SFX: roaring propellers, rushing wind, distant engines.
ENDS_ON: The plane higher in the frame with the man hanging below it mid-wave, the three sedans still small and far back in the dust.
WHY: The slow lift and the lazy wave make the escape too easy, which is the punchline on "the end".
