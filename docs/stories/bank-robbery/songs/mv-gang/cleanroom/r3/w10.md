PLUM is in the shot alone, and his mask and suit are exactly as in the attached reference image: the plum hand-knitted rib-wool balaclava with no holes, the white wool ring round the face, the small rounded red felt triangle high over his LEFT eye, so it sits on the RIGHT of the picture at two o'clock. He stands in a gilded bank vault, an 18mm lens a foot from him at a low angle, so his flat black-gloved right hand chopping down at the lens looks huge and his left glove sits on his hip, chin up, mock-lecturing. Behind him, brass-faced safe-deposit drawers, one hanging open, and a stepped gold sunburst on the round vault door. WHY: The beat chop caught at its lowest point, foreshortened by a wide lens in a room full of money, turns "you can't make money from a tree" into a smug lecture from the one place that proves it wrong.
