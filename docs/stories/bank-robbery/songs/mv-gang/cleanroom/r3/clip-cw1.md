=== CLIP k01-a ===
From the first frame the six men keep walking down the steps toward the camera at one steady, unhurried pace, one step per beat, arms loose, heads level, nobody looks back. Behind them the orange fireball keeps rolling slowly upward and fades to smoke. A few blank paper notes flutter down and settle on the steps. The paper in the left man's glove keeps burning gently. Locked-off camera, framing holds; masks, suits and gloves stay exactly as they are. SFX: a deep low rumble, crackling fire, footsteps on stone. No speech, no music.
ENDS_ON: The six men two steps lower and closer to the lens, still in a line, smoke and a few settling notes behind them.
WHY: The walk-away-from-the-blast cliché only needs the walk to continue, so the one motion is the stroll and everything else just settles.

=== CLIP k02-b ===
From the first frame the dark-green 1920s truck keeps rolling slowly forward across the lobby toward the lower left, then brakes and stops by the third second, rocking once on its springs. The barrels already on the floor roll a short way and come to rest; the split barrel keeps foaming. Dust drifts through the light shafts. The driver stays at the wheel; the man on the running board holds on, his prop gun still pointed at the ceiling. Locked-off camera, framing holds; the same truck, the same two men, nothing new enters. SFX: engine rumble, a brake squeal, bricks settling, barrels thudding. No speech, no music.
ENDS_ON: The truck stopped a short way further into the lobby, dust hanging in the light, the barrels still, the gun still raised.
WHY: One plain move (truck rolls in and stops) delivers the crash-through-the-wall gag without the barrels multiplying or morphing.

=== CLIP k03-a ===
Slow motion, as in the classic sideways-dive cliché. From the first frame the man keeps gliding through the air toward the left of frame, body horizontal, sinking very gently, both prop guns still pointed up at the ceiling, jacket tails rippling. The chandelier crystals keep falling slowly and twinkle; a few land and skitter on the marble. He stays fully in frame. Locked-off camera, framing holds; mask, suit and gloves unchanged. SFX: a slowed-down whoosh, tinkling glass. No speech, no music.
ENDS_ON: The man still mid-air a little left and lower than he began, crystals still falling around him.
WHY: The parody is a pointless amount of slow-motion drama, so the clip simply carries the dive on, slowly, with nothing landing.

=== CLIP k04-a ===
From the first frame the six men keep walking straight at the lens in their V formation, one stride per beat, open overcoats flaring behind them. The grey-masked man tugs his cuff straight once; the man on the far right glances down at his pocket watch, then snaps it shut. Nobody turns his head. The marquee bulbs keep chasing; rain glints on the cobbles. Long-lens compression, so they grow only slightly larger. Locked-off camera, framing holds; masks, suits and gloves unchanged. SFX: heavy footsteps on wet stone, light rain, a faint electric hum. No speech, no music.
ENDS_ON: The V formation a few strides closer, filling a little more of the frame, the watch closed, the marquee glowing behind.
WHY: The power-walk cliché is the walk itself, and the two tiny bored gestures carry the deadpan joke.

=== CLIP k06-b ===
From the first frame the black 1920s sedan pulls away to the right along the cobbles, the two masked men inside sitting perfectly still, and it drives out of frame right by the fourth second. The policeman stays on one knee and shakes his raised fist twice. The upturned police car's wheels keep spinning. The oranges finish falling, bounce once and roll to a stop along the kerb. Locked-off camera, framing holds; nothing new enters the street, the bystanders stay where they are. SFX: a period car engine revving away, oranges thudding, a whistle in the distance. No speech, no music.
ENDS_ON: The empty cobbles on the right, the policeman kneeling with his fist up, oranges scattered and still, wheels slowing.
WHY: One direction of travel for the getaway car and one gesture for the cop keep the car-flip gag readable without cars popping in and out.
