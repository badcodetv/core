=== CLIP k23-a ===
From the first frame the man in the cream suit slides steadily down the single rope, hand under hand, legs together, shoes pointed, dropping smoothly toward the deck at an unhurried pace. The five men on the deck stand completely still, hands in pockets, watching him. The blimp holds its place at the mast and the city lights glow steady below. Masks, gloves and suits unchanged; no one new appears. Locked-off camera, framing holds. SFX: rope creak, high wind, a distant ship horn.
ENDS_ON: The cream-suited man lower on the rope, near deck height, the five still standing in their row looking up.
WHY: One continuous slide down a rope is the whole set piece, and the deadpan row of watchers is the joke.

=== CLIP k24-a ===
From the first frame the man in green keeps tipping the burlap sack over the basket rim, and a steady stream of pale green notes pours out and flutters down into the cloud sea, each note drifting and tumbling slowly on the wind. The man in mustard holds his arms wide and pumps them up twice at the sky, delighted. The balloon, basket and ropes hold steady. Masks, gloves and suits unchanged. Locked-off camera, framing holds. SFX: wind, rustling paper, creaking wicker.
ENDS_ON: The sack nearly empty, a long trail of notes falling into the clouds, the mustard man still mid raise-the-roof.
WHY: Money poured out of the sky from on high is asset inflation in one plain image, played as a showman's celebration.

=== CLIP k26-a ===
From the first frame the three axe heads jolt and wrench in the splintered door as if worked from the other side, more wood chips bursting out and falling to the floor, dust puffing into the lamp light. At the table everyone stays perfectly calm: the man in the hat holds his raised finger still, the others hold their cards without looking round. The lamp sways gently; cigar smoke curls. Masks, gloves and suits unchanged. Locked-off camera, framing holds. SFX: heavy thuds, cracking wood.
ENDS_ON: The door more splintered with all three axe heads still stuck in it, the man in the hat still holding up one finger, cards in hand.
WHY: The chaos stays behind the door while the gang refuses to react, so the parody reads instantly in a short cut.

=== CLIP k27-b ===
From the first frame the clerk at the press freezes mid-turn, then slowly raises both hands beside his head, stiff with alarm. Through the round vault doorway the two masked men stand completely still, upright prop guns pointing at the ceiling, as if posing, the shelves behind them bare. The vault door, press and stack of green paper stay exactly where they are. Masks, gloves and suits unchanged; no one new appears. Locked-off camera, framing holds. SFX: lamp hum, a slow metal creak from the door.
ENDS_ON: The clerk with both hands up beside his head, the two masked men still framed motionless in the doorway with guns pointed up.
WHY: The robbers find an empty vault while the man printing the money gives himself up, and only he moves, so nothing on the masks or the vault can drift.
