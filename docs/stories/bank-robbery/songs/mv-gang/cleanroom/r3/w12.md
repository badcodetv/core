=== SHOT g09 ===
A 1920s political rally hall in the afternoon, shot from very low and very close on a wide 18mm lens, so CRIMSON's lunge fills the frame. The mask and suit are exactly as in the attached reference image; only CRIMSON is in the shot. He is caught at the extreme of a forward lunge, one two-tone foot planted on a wooden crate of gin bottles, his body thrown toward the lens, shouting through a dented brass megaphone so its bell looms huge and distorted in the foreground. His free gloved hand is flung back and open behind him. His crimson knitted balaclava has no eye, nose or mouth holes, a white wool ring round the face, and a small red felt triangle over his LEFT eye, on the right side of the picture. Behind him, faded red, white and blue bunting sags from a balcony, empty folding chairs and dust hanging in hard sunlight from tall arched windows. Black leather gloves, no skin.
WHY: The wide lens and megaphone bell turn a stage-rapper's lean-in into a mock demagogue's rally speech for "state level manipulation".
