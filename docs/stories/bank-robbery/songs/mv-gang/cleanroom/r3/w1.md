=== SHOT h01 ===
A low-angle shot from a front table in a cramped 1920s speakeasy. A woman stands on a little wooden stage, singing straight into the lens. Her whole head and neck are covered by a hand-knitted emerald wool balaclava with fuzzy, visible stitches and no holes for eyes, nose or mouth. A large white ring is stitched around the whole face, and one small solid red felt triangle with rounded corners sits high inside the ring over her left eye, which puts it on the right of the picture at two o'clock. She wears a knee-length champagne-gold beaded dress with fringe swinging mid-shimmy, ivory opera gloves to the shoulder, opaque stockings and scuffed satin T-bar shoes. A dark, worn hickory bat lies across her shoulders with one wrist draped over it, and her other gloved hand is flung out palm-up in a big, mocking shrug toward a scrawny potted fig tree next to her. A single hard spotlight cuts through cigar haze from above left. Oxblood velvet curtain, glowing bare-bulb footlights, gin bottles on a brass rail, deep blacks, 35mm film grain, 16:9 filling the frame.
WHY: The shrug toward the bare little tree acts out "you can't make money from a tree", and the emerald and champagne keep her well clear of any red-and-black jester look.

=== SHOT h02 ===
A wide 24mm shot from floor level in a grand 1920s bank lobby by daylight. A woman stands on top of a polished veined marble counter in front of brass teller grilles, lit by hard sun shafts slanting through tall windows and dust. Her whole head and neck are covered by a hand-knitted peacock-blue wool balaclava with visible yarn texture and no holes for eyes, nose or mouth. A large white ring is stitched around the whole face, and one small solid red felt triangle with rounded corners sits high inside the ring over her left eye, on the right of the picture at two o'clock. A cream fedora is tipped over the mask. She wears a dove-grey chalk-stripe man's three-piece suit cut down to fit her, mustard braces, butter-yellow kid gloves, spats and two-tone brogues. Weight on one hip, she slaps a dark worn bat into her open palm and cups her other hand beside the mask like she's yelling for help, chin up, mid-holler. Behind her, soft and out of focus, masked men in dark suits hold Thompsons up at the ceiling. The deco sunburst clock and the brass plaques are blank, 16:9 edge to edge.
WHY: She's the robber pretending to yell for the police, which plays "call the police, it's a robbery" as a sarcastic joke, and the cold daylight on marble is a different place, lens and light from h01.
