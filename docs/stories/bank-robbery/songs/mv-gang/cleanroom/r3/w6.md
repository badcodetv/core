=== SHOT k01 ===
A low-angle wide shot on a 24mm lens at dusk: all six robbers, PLUM, BOTTLE, CRIMSON, MARINE, RUST and SILVER, stroll down the chipped granite steps of a columned 1920s bank toward the camera, unhurried, while the vault behind them erupts in an orange fireball and a storm of banknotes. Their masks and suits are exactly as in the attached reference image: hand-knitted rib wool with no holes, a white wool ring round each face, and one small rounded red felt triangle over each man's left eye, which sits on the right of the picture. Black leather gloves, collars up, no skin. Nobody looks back. PLUM holds a cigar to a burning ledger page in his gloved hand, deadpan, though his mask has no mouth. The fire rims their hats and shoulders in hard orange light against a cold blue sky; drifting notes are blank. Film grain, edge to edge 16:9.
WHY: The most mocked action cliché played dead straight, with the mouthless cigar as the joke that gives it away on the outro shout.

=== SHOT k02 ===
A high-angle view from a brass-railed mezzanine looking down into an empty marble bank lobby as a plain dark-green 1920s beer truck reverses through the far brick wall. Bricks, dust and oak barrels burst inward and tumble across the terrazzo; one barrel splits and foams. Hard white daylight blasts through the hole in solid shafts through the dust. RUST grips the wheel in the cab; CRIMSON rides the running board, one gloved hand on the door, a Thompson pointed straight up at the ceiling. Their masks and suits are exactly as in the attached reference image: knitted wool with no eye, nose or mouth holes, a white ring round the face, a small rounded red felt triangle over each man's left eye, on the right of the picture. Black gloves, cuffs over wrists, no skin. The truck carries no lettering. 35mm lens, frozen debris, film grain, filling the 16:9 frame.
WHY: A brutal entrance gives the first "Robbery!" an impact, and the overhead angle keeps it clear who is driving and who is posing.

=== SHOT k03 ===
A worm's-eye shot on a wide 18mm lens from the polished veined marble floor of a bank lobby: MARINE flies sideways through the air above the camera, body horizontal, cream linen jacket flaring, two-tone shoes kicked out, a Thompson in each gloved fist pointed up at the ceiling. Above him a huge cut-crystal chandelier has shattered and its crystals rain down, frozen mid-fall and catching sparks of light. His mask and suit are exactly as in the attached reference image: rib-knit wool over head and neck with no holes, a white wool ring round the face, a small rounded red felt triangle over his left eye, on the right of the picture. Black leather gloves, no skin. The only light is the warm tungsten chandelier glare and gold sconces on fluted columns, deep blacks between. Fast shutter, every crystal sharp, a pointless amount of slow-motion drama. Film grain, full-bleed 16:9.
WHY: The double "robbery, robbery" gets a two-gun dive that is far too much, seen from below so the crystals fall on the viewer.

=== SHOT k04 ===
A long 135mm telephoto shot at night, compressing a rain-wet cobbled street: all six robbers walk straight at the lens in a V formation, CRIMSON at the point, PLUM and SILVER at his shoulders, BOTTLE, MARINE and RUST at the wings. Their masks and suits are exactly as in the attached reference image: knitted wool with no holes, a white ring round each face, a small rounded red felt triangle over each man's left eye, on the right of the picture. Dark overcoats hang open over the suits and flare behind them. Black gloves, collars up, no skin. Behind them a theatre marquee of chasing bulbs glows warm gold, its letter boards blank, its light pooling in the wet stones; the rest is cool blue shadow and haze. Mid-stride, SILVER straightens a cufflink and RUST checks a pocket watch, unbothered. Oval bulb bokeh, film grain, edge to edge 16:9.
WHY: The power walk is where the music kicks in, and the long lens stacks the gang into one slab of swagger with two bored details for the laugh.
