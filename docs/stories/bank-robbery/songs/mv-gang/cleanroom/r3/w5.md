=== SHOT h09 ===
A wide eye-level shot on a 35mm lens of a woman in a 1920s widow's mourning dress, black lace over black silk, standing in the rain beside an open nickel-plated coffin heaped with white lilies outside a grey granite mausoleum with blank, uninscribed headstones. Her whole head and neck are covered by a hand-knitted charcoal-black wool balaclava with visible stitches and fuzz, no eye, nose or mouth holes, a large white ring stitched round the whole face, and one small solid red felt triangle with rounded corners high inside the ring over her left eye, on the right of the picture. A sheer black veil hangs from a small hat over the mask. Black opera gloves, opaque black stockings, no skin anywhere. She leans her weight on a dark, worn hickory bat like a walking stick and raps to the camera, her free gloved fist pressed to her heart in grief held far too long, head tilted. Flat overcast daylight, wet stone, masked mourners under black umbrellas soft behind her. Film grain, 16:9, full frame.
WHY: The banker's funeral turns the bailout line into mock grief, and her held fist-to-heart is the sarcastic stage gesture.

=== SHOT h10 ===
A low-angle shot on a 24mm lens from the foot of a grand 1920s art deco staircase of black and white veined marble, brass rails and a sunburst mirrored panel, looking up at a woman at the very top. She wears a sharply cut white dinner jacket with satin peak lapels, a black bow tie, high-waisted black trousers, patent leather shoes and a glossy black top hat over her balaclava. The balaclava is hand-knitted deep peacock-green wool with visible stitches and fuzz, covering her whole head and neck, no eye, nose or mouth holes, a large white ring stitched round the whole face, and one small solid red felt triangle with rounded corners high inside the ring over her left eye, on the right of the picture. White kid gloves, no skin anywhere. She twirls a gold-painted bat like a showman's cane in one hand and flings the other arm wide, palm up, rapping to camera as a shower of blank banknotes flutters down the steps. One hard spotlight from above through cigar haze, deep blacks, crisp specular highlights on brass. Film grain, 16:9, full frame.
WHY: The showman throwing money down the stairs is the giving-not-taking line, and the open-palmed flourish at full extension is the big performer's gesture.
