=== SHOT h05 ===
A 1920s bank vault at night, seen from a low wide angle on a 24mm lens. A woman robber in a hand-knitted emerald wool balaclava covering her whole head and neck, with no eye, nose or mouth holes, sprawls back on a waist-high heap of stolen banknotes. A large white ring is stitched round her whole face, and one small solid red felt triangle with rounded corners sits high inside the ring over her left eye, on the right of the picture at two o'clock. She wears a huge ivory fox-fur coat slipping off one shoulder over a champagne silk slip dress, a long rope of pearls, ivory opera gloves and sheer champagne stockings, so no skin shows. A dark hickory bat lies across her knees and one gloved hand flicks the back of its fingers at the lens, bored. Behind her a small hand-cranked printing press spits out sheets of notes. A single bare bulb overhead throws hard light on brass, steel bolts and the fur. The notes carry no legible lettering. Film grain, deep blacks, 16:9 full frame.
WHY: The fur coat and the printing press turn "you don't feel winter" into one lazy, rich gesture, and the throwaway flick is her sarcasm without a face.

=== SHOT h06 ===
A tenement stoop in a 1920s city street in late afternoon, shot at eye level on an 85mm lens, washing lines and fire escapes soft behind. A woman robber sits on the worn brownstone steps, one scuffed boot up on the step above, knees wide, leaning into the camera. Her hand-knitted oxblood wool balaclava covers her whole head and neck with no eye, nose or mouth holes; a large white ring is stitched round her whole face, and one small solid red felt triangle with rounded corners sits high inside it over her left eye, on the right of the picture at two o'clock. A tweed flat cap sits over the wool. She wears an oversized camel man's overcoat with turned-up sleeves, grey wool gloves and thick brown stockings, so no skin shows. A bat wrapped in grubby friction tape rests on her shoulder while her free hand counts off on its fingers, deadpan, beside an empty tin plate. Hard low sun cuts between the buildings, catching dust and the yarn's fuzz. No signs or lettering anywhere. Film grain, 16:9 full frame.
WHY: The empty plate and the slow finger count make "just about eating" a sarcastic tally of a landlord's take, and the shabby daylight is the opposite world to the vault heiress.
