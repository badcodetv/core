=== CLIP g08-b ===
Locked-off camera, no camera movement. The plum-masked man in the mustard suit performs to the lens from the first instant, mock-grand like a stage magician. On the first beat he wiggles his spread glove at the lens; on the next he snaps it into a fist and pops it open again; then he swirls it in a slow showy circle, head tilting the other way with each beat, shoulders bouncing. His hidden hand keeps the folded banknote and slides it deeper into his jacket pocket. Mask, gloves and suit never change. No speech; footlight hum, cloth rustle.
ENDS_ON: The big glove spread wide at the lens again, head tilted, the banknote half inside his pocket behind his back.
WHY: The trick is in the action itself: the front hand plays to the crowd while the back hand pockets the money, all of it on the beat.

=== CLIP g12-b ===

WHY: A fake-sincere hand on the heart between two cash throws makes the generosity line come off as mockery.

=== CLIP v14g-a ===

On the first beat his raised palm pushes firmly toward the offered glass, refusing it; on the next his pointing finger jabs at the camera twice; then he wags that finger side to side in a big mock-stern no, rolls his shoulders, and throws a hand flick at the glass. The plum-masked man beside him keeps holding out the same glass and only tilts his head. Masks and suits never change. No speech; creaking boards, cloth rustle.
ENDS_ON: Rust's finger wagging at the lens, other palm up toward the glass, Plum still offering it.
WHY: A schoolteacher's finger-wag aimed at the camera, from a man running a still, makes the refusal both big and hypocritical.
