Behind him the man in the top hat hauls the brass lever down once, slowly, and the workers stay pressed against the wall. Masks, gloves and faces stay unchanged; nothing new enters the frame. SFX: a heavy lever clunk, machinery hum. Ambient noise: furnace roar.
ENDS_ON: The masked man mid-nod, glove chopped low, thumb pointing back at the man holding the lowered lever.
WHY: The chop carries the beat and the thumb points the viewer to the owner, so the lyric's meaning plays out in one plain action behind him.

Mask and gloves stay the same; nothing new enters the frame. SFX: paper fluttering. Ambient noise: murmuring crowd.
ENDS_ON: The man holding the cheque at his chest, mask tipped up toward the lens, cheques lying on the hats and cobbles.
WHY: A single catch-and-nod reads clearly from four floors up, while still cars and slow paper avoid the teleporting and morphing he flagged.
