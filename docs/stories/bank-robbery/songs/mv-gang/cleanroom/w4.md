=== SHOT c01 ===
A music-video still from 1920s America. Six masked robbers burst into a marble bank lobby, shot from waist height on a very wide 14mm lens a few feet from them, so the nearest gloved hands and knitted heads swell toward the camera while the columns bow away behind. Each wears a seamless hand-knitted rib wool hood over the whole head and neck, tucked into a turned-up collar, smooth knit where the face would be, a thin contrasting ring stitched round the face and one small rounded red felt triangle high over his right eye, on the left of the picture. Plum with cream ring in mustard chalk-stripe; bottle green with oatmeal ring in brown tweed; crimson with cream ring, grey fedora, charcoal pinstripe; marine blue in cream linen; rust-gold with black ring in black and white tie; silver-grey with crimson ring in oxblood. They thrust prop Thompsons and revolvers straight up at a brass chandelier, free fists pumping. Behind them customers in bowler hats lie flat on green-veined marble. Hard morning sun slants through tall windows in dusty shafts; grainy 1920s colour film.
WHY: The line is a shout, so all six bellow with their bodies and guns raised while the room lies on the floor, the textbook lobby takeover.

=== SHOT d11 ===
A music-video still: BOTTLE, a quiet robber in a bottle-green hand-knitted rib wool hood covering his whole head and neck, smooth knit where the face would be, an oatmeal chain-stitched ring outlining the face and one small rounded red felt triangle sewn high over his right eye, which sits on the left of the picture as he faces us, shoves a wooden builder's wheelbarrow out through a massive round steel vault door. The barrow is heaped far above its rim with banded bundles of green banknotes, and bundles slide off the front and tumble across the threshold. He wears a brown tweed suit, collar up, black leather gloves gripping the worn handles, cuffs down. Camera low on the floor at the door's hinge, 35mm, looking up the barrow at him. The only light is a caged bulb inside the vault behind him, rimming his wool in gold and throwing the open door's bolts into long shadows; grainy period colour film.
WHY: A literal wheelbarrow overloaded with vault cash rolling out of the vault door is the line pictured word for word.

=== SHOT e02 ===
A tight macro still on a steel-topped vault counter: two hands in black leather gloves, the shirt cuffs and a brown tweed sleeve pulled down over the wrists, stretch a tan rubber band and snap it round a fat brick of green banknotes, the band caught mid-recoil and blurred. The engraving on the top note is soft and out of focus, an ornate border with no legible lettering. Around the brick lie more loose rubber bands, a brass teller's stamp and a heap of unbanded notes. Shot on a 100mm macro lens from just above counter height, shallow focus so the far edge of the counter dissolves into the gloom of the vault. Light comes from one green-shaded brass desk lamp to the left, pooling warm on the leather creases and the band's tension, the rest falling to deep shadow. Visible film grain, slight softness, nothing glossy.
WHY: The line is about elastic bands and hands, so the frame is nothing but gloved hands, not plastic ones, snapping a band round a brick of cash.

=== SHOT v13 ===
A music-video still, 1920s bank archive basement: SILVER, a tall robber in a silver-grey hand-knitted rib wool hood covering his whole head and neck, smooth knit where the face would be, a crimson chain-stitched ring outlining the face and one small rounded red felt triangle high over his right eye, on the left of the picture, sits on top of a towering mound of thick bound ledgers with blank spines and loose paper slips, the whole heap wrapped round and round in heavy iron chains and padlocks. He wears an oxblood-red suit and bow tie, collar up, black gloves. He raps into the lens: leaning down toward a fisheye camera placed low at the foot of the pile, one gloved arm straight out pointing into the lens, the other hand a fist on his chest. Steel shelving bends away behind. Cold daylight falls from a single high barred window, dust hanging in the beam; grainy colour film.
WHY: The debt system shown as a chained mountain of ledgers and IOUs, with SILVER enthroned on it rapping like it will never go away, makes "insistent on debt's existence" literal.
