=== SHOT d02 ===
A music-video still at night on a brick bank's back steps in 1920s America, shot head-on as a press photographer's bulb flash fires straight into the lens: a hard white burst blooms over the left of the frame, washing the brick to chalk and throwing a sharp black shadow behind. BOTTLE, a robber in a brown tweed suit, collar up, black leather gloves, stumbles toward camera mid-step, knees buckling, one gloved arm thrown across his head. The mask and suit are exactly as in the attached reference image; this is BOTTLE alone. His mask is a hand-knitted bottle-green rib-wool hood, smooth unbroken knit where the face would be, a large white wool ring stitched round the face from brow to chin, and one small rounded red felt triangle high inside it over his left eye, on the right side of the picture at about two o'clock. A canvas sack tumbles from his other glove, banknotes fanning into the air. Speed Graphic look, 35mm wide, frozen motion, harsh frontal flash, coarse grain, blank wall behind.
WHY: The flash fires into us and he stumbles and spills the cash, so "distracted by a dyspraxic flash" reads at once.

=== SHOT d07 ===
A wide high-angle still from a gilded box seat over a 1920s vaudeville theatre: on the far stage a single hard white spotlight falls on a magician in tails sawing through a red-and-gold lacquered cabinet, a smiling woman's head and stockinged feet poking out of either end. Everything else is warm darkness: rows of velvet seats in deep burgundy, a packed audience of men in bowlers and women in cloche hats all staring at the stage, their faces lit only by spill. Among them, crouched in the aisles and leaning between rows, the gang works: PLUM, MARINE and RUST, gloved fingers lifting wallets and pocket watches from coats. Their masks and suits are exactly as in the attached reference image: hand-knitted hoods in plum, marine blue and rust-gold, each with a large white ring round the face and a small red felt triangle over the left eye. 28mm lens, deep focus, amber footlight glow, film grain.
WHY: Every eye is on the trick while the gang empties the pockets, which is the line "distraction is the attraction" made literal.

=== SHOT d08 ===
A close fisheye still on a crowded 1920s railway concourse at dusk, low camera tilted up: SILVER, the tall robber in an oxblood-red suit and bow tie, raps into the lens, pushing one black leather glove open-palm toward the camera, fingers spread, the glove filling a third of the frame and bulging in the lens like a conjurer's flourish. The mask and suit are exactly as in the attached reference image; this is SILVER alone. His mask is a hand-knitted silver-grey rib-wool hood, seamless knit where the face would be, a large white wool ring stitched round the face from brow to chin, and one small rounded red felt triangle high inside it over his left eye, on the right side of the picture at about two o'clock. Just past the big glove, his other gloved hand slides a fat leather wallet out of a businessman's coat pocket behind him, the man gazing up at a station clock. Low window light from the right, cool blue dusk outside, warm lamps inside, grain.
WHY: The open palm in our face holds the eye while the other hand takes the wallet, which shows "look at the hand" literally.

=== SHOT d10 ===
A medium-close still in a cramped 1920s radio studio padded with quilted maroon fabric, lit by a single deep-red lamp low on the right so everything glows scarlet and falls into black. CRIMSON, the loud robber in a charcoal pinstripe double-breasted suit with a grey fedora pulled over his mask, raps over a big chrome ring-mounted radio microphone on a stand, leaning in, one gloved fist pumping beside it, the other gloved finger jabbing at the lens. The mask and suit are exactly as in the attached reference image; this is CRIMSON alone. His mask is a hand-knitted crimson rib-wool hood, smooth knit where the face would be, a large white wool ring stitched round the face from brow to chin, and one small rounded red felt triangle high inside it over his left eye, on the right side of the picture at about two o'clock. On the padded wall behind him the low lamp throws his huge black shadow, and in the shadow two curved devil's horns rise from his head. 40mm lens, slight low angle, grain, a glowing valve set on a desk.
WHY: A gang member on the airwaves with a horned shadow behind him is "the devil, with a radio station" shown straight.
