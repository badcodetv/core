=== SHOT a01 ===
A music-video film still on slightly grainy colour film, taken through a long telephoto lens from the middle of a 1920s city street early on a clear morning. Six masked robbers walk side by side straight toward the camera. A low sun behind them throws their long shadows forward across the cobbles and streetcar rails. The six men are PLUM, BOTTLE, CRIMSON, MARINE, RUST and SILVER, left to right, and their masks and suits are exactly as in the attached reference image. Each wears a hand-knitted rib wool balaclava over his whole head and neck, with smooth unbroken knit where the face would be. A large white wool ring is stitched round the face from brow to chin, and a small rounded red felt triangle sits high inside the ring over his left eye, which puts it on the right side of the picture at about two o'clock. All six wear black leather gloves with their collars up. Each rests a 1920s Thompson prop gun on his shoulder, muzzle to the sky. Behind them, out of focus, rise the pale stone columns of a bank.
WHY: The intro shows the whole crew once, facing the lens and readable, with the telephoto squeezing six men and the bank into one wall of colour.

=== SHOT a02 ===
A music-video still from a 1920s bank lobby, shot with a wide-angle lens from knee height on the marble floor. The tall brass-and-glass front doors burst inward. Shards of glass hang in the air, and hard morning sunlight blasts through the doorway behind the gang, so they arrive half-silhouetted with a rim of light. CRIMSON leads, with the rest of the gang crowding the doorway behind him. His mask and suit are exactly as in the attached reference image. His crimson hand-knitted balaclava has a large white ring stitched round the face from brow to chin, smooth knit where the face would be, and a small rounded red felt triangle high over his left eye, on the right side of the picture. He wears a grey fedora over the mask, a charcoal pinstripe double-breasted suit, black leather gloves and an upturned collar, and holds a 1920s Thompson prop gun straight up at the coffered ceiling. In the foreground, customers in mismatched period clothes flinch and duck: a woman in a cloche hat drops her handbag and a clerk flings up a ledger. Dust swirls in the light shafts.
WHY: "Robbery!" is the moment of the doors bursting in, shot from floor level so the glass, the light and the flinch all hit at once.

=== SHOT v10 ===
A music-video still inside a walnut-panelled 1920s boardroom at mid-morning, shot on a wide-angle lens close to the doorway. MARINE has just stepped through the open door, the only gang member in the shot. His mask and suit are exactly as in the attached reference image. He leans into the lens in close-up on the left of the frame, one black-gloved hand raised flat beside his head in a polite little apology wave. His marine-blue hand-knitted balaclava has a large white ring stitched round the face from brow to chin, smooth knit where the face would be, and a small rounded red felt triangle high over his left eye, on the right side of his masked face in the picture. He wears a cream linen suit with the collar turned up. Behind him on the right, a long mahogany table recedes, lined with old bankers in wing collars sitting in green leather chairs, all frozen. One holds a lit cigar halfway to his open mouth, another has stopped pouring from a decanter. Window light slices sideways through the hanging blue cigar smoke.
WHY: The apologetic raised glove up close, set against a whole table of bankers frozen mid-cigar, plays "did I interrupt your meeting?" literally.

=== SHOT v11 ===
A music-video still at noon on the wide granite steps of an enormous columned central-bank building, shot with an ultra-wide lens from a few steps below, looking up. The overhead sun drops short, hard shadows under every column. SILVER, the tall one and the only gang member in the shot, leans down into the lens and wags one black-gloved index finger at the viewer. His mask and suit are exactly as in the attached reference image. The finger is large and slightly bulged by the lens, and his other gloved hand is planted on his hip. His silver-grey hand-knitted balaclava has a large white ring stitched round the face from brow to chin, smooth knit where the face would be, and a small rounded red felt triangle high over his left eye, on the right side of the picture. He wears an oxblood-red suit, a bow tie and an upturned collar, with the cuffs down over the gloves. Far behind him, at the top of the steps between the giant columns, a stout banker in a top hat hugs a fat cloth moneybag to his chest and peers back warily.
WHY: The scolding finger in the lens on the steps of the money temple, with the clutching banker behind, reads at once as a warning about who the bank is really for.
