=== CLIP p02-a ===
Locked-off camera, framing holds. The masked man performs from the first instant, head bobbing hard on every beat. On the first beat his pointing finger jabs at the lens twice, sharp. On the next he snaps the hand back and chops it flat down through the air. Then a huge sarcastic shrug, both palms up, head cocked. Then he leans in and jabs at the lens again. The porter keeps slowly pushing his loaded cart, staying in frame. Mask, hat, gloves and suit unchanged; no new objects. SFX: leather glove creaks, cloth swishes, cart wheels, echoing station hall.
ENDS_ON: The man leaning toward the lens, finger jabbing close at camera, porter still with his cart at right.
WHY: Jab, chop, shrug, jab gives a new readable move every beat or two, sarcastic over-commitment, and the deadpan porter carries on.

=== CLIP p06-b ===
Locked-off camera, framing holds. The six masked men perform at once from the first instant. On the first beat all raised fists pump up twice in unison; the man in the orange mask is late, his fist only reaching shoulder height each time. On the next beat they all drop to flat palms chopping down together. Then they all bounce at the knees, heads nodding hard. Then all fists punch up again, orange still late. Behind the grille the grey teller keeps counting banknotes, never looking up. Masks, suits and gloves unchanged; nobody new appears. SFX: shoe stamps, cloth, rustling banknotes, marble echo.
ENDS_ON: Five fists high overhead, the orange-masked man's fist at shoulder height, the teller still counting.
WHY: The unison crew with one man always late is the parody, and the uninterested teller undercuts it.

=== CLIP p11-b ===
Locked-off camera, framing holds. The crouched masked man performs from the first instant, bouncing on bent knees with every beat. On the first beat his raised finger wags a stern "no" at the lens. On the next he springs forward from the hips, finger jabbing at the camera. Then his hip hand flips up into an over-the-top shrug. Then back to the deep crouch, finger wagging again. The dockhand on the barrel keeps sipping his tea; the men behind keep lifting crates slowly. Car and boat stay still. Mask, gloves and suit unchanged. SFX: boots thudding on wet planks, cloth, clinking bottles, lapping water.
ENDS_ON: The man in a deep crouch, finger wagging at the lens, the dockhand lowering his teacup.
WHY: The wag, jab and shrug read instantly as a mock-stern lecture, while the dock carries on as if nothing is happening.

=== CLIP p16-a ===
Locked-off camera, framing holds. The six masked men in the ring perform at once from the first instant, staring up at the lens. On the first beat every raised fist pumps up twice in unison; the green-masked man in front is a beat late, his fist only reaching chest height. On the next beat they all bounce at the knees, heads nodding hard. Then all thump a fist to their chest together. Then every fist punches straight up again, green still late. Smoke drifts slowly through the window light. Masks, suits and gloves unchanged; nobody new appears. SFX: stamps on canvas, creaking ropes, cloth, echoing gym.
ENDS_ON: Five fists high overhead, the green-masked man's fist halfway up, smoke drifting behind.
WHY: The revolutionary fist done in eager unison with one man always late is big, sarcastic and readable in any half-second of the cut.
