=== CLIP p03-b ===
Locked-off camera, framing holds. The masked man performs to the lens on a fast beat from the first instant. First beat: his shrug drops, both gloved palms slap down flat on the counter. Next beat: he leans in over the counter, head pushed toward the lens. Next: his right hand chops down through the air twice. Next: his left hand jabs a finger at the lens. Next: a huge slow shrug, palms up, head cocked. The old woman sips her coffee, staring ahead. Mask, gloves and suit stay unchanged. SFX: palms on counter, tweed rustle, a mug set down, quiet diner hum.
ENDS_ON: The masked man at the top of a second big shrug, palms up, head cocked at the lens, the old woman still staring ahead holding her mug.
WHY: The shrug becomes the punchline he returns to, while the woman's indifference sells the cold-grandma lyric.

=== CLIP p12-a ===
Locked-off camera, framing holds. The six masked men perform in unison to the lens on a fast beat from the first instant. First beat: the five raised arms chop down to point at the lens together; the man in the mustard mask finally throws his arm straight up, alone. Next beat: he snaps it down, late again. Next: all six bounce on bent knees and roll their shoulders. Next: all six fling both arms out wide. The band keeps playing; the dancers sway in place. Masks, gloves and suits stay unchanged. SFX: brass band, stamps on the stage, applause.
ENDS_ON: All six with arms spread wide across the bandstand, the man in the mustard mask a beat behind, his arms still rising.
WHY: The one late man in a synchronised routine is the deadpan joke, and big unison moves read in a wide shot.
