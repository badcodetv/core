=== CLIP p04-b ===
Locked-off camera, framing holds. The masked man performs straight at the lens from the first instant, mock-serious. First beat: two gloved fingers tap his temple twice, sharp. Next beat: the same hand snaps out, index finger jabbing at the lens. Next: palm flat, edge down, chopping the air twice. Next: both shoulders shrug high, gloved palm turned up, head cocked. Then the two fingers return to his temple, tapping. The elevator operator stays still behind him, holding the tray level, coin in place. Mask, gloves and suit unchanged. SFX: leather glove creaks, cloth rustles, quiet lobby hum.
ENDS_ON: The masked man close on the right, chin dipped, two gloved fingers back on his temple; the operator unmoved at the bronze lift door with the coin on his tray.
WHY: The "think" tap becomes a sarcastic lecture loop (tap, jab, chop, shrug, tap) that sells "the Fed is not your friend" with the hand alone.

=== CLIP p08-b ===
Locked-off high camera, framing holds. The masked man lands from his jump within the first half second, knees bending deep, and keeps bouncing on the beat. On each landing his arms swing: both index fingers point left, then both point right, then both jab up at the lens. Next beat: arms flung wide again, shoulders bobbing, heels lifting. He stays on the same spot of cobbles. The two old men in the windows stay leaning out, sip their tea once, blank. Steam drifts. Suit and mask unchanged. SFX: shoe soles slap wet cobbles, cloth flaps, distant drip.
ENDS_ON: The masked man mid-bounce in the centre of the alley, arms wide, fingers pointing at the two old men in the windows, who still hold their teacups.
WHY: Pointing left and right at the identical tea-drinkers turns "the right and the left both witness" into a literal, bouncing joke.

=== CLIP p13-a ===
Locked-off low camera, framing holds. The masked man leans over the table and performs at the lens from the first instant. First beat: his raised gloved fist chops down hard toward the felt, stopping just above it. Next beat: back up, fist pumping twice beside his shoulder. Next: he hunches forward, head pushed toward the lens, shoulders rolling. Next: flat palm chops down again. His other hand stays gripping the table rail. Behind, the croupier keeps raking chips, eyes down. Dice and chips stay where they lie. Mask, gloves, suit unchanged. SFX: glove thuds near felt, chips click, faint rake scrape.
ENDS_ON: The masked man hunched over the rail, gloved hand at the bottom of a chop just above the felt; the croupier still raking, eyes down.
WHY: The table-pounding chop is the gambler's swagger, and the croupier ignoring him undercuts the bravado.
