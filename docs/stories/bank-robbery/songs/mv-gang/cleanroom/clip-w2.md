=== CLIP v06 ===
Locked-off static camera, framing holds. The masked man in the tweed suit nods his head on a steady beat and jabs his gloved pointing finger toward the lens on each beat, four times, shoulders rolling with the groove, then holds the point. His mask and gloves stay exactly as they are. The three people in the doorway stay where they are with only small shifts of weight. The street stays empty. SFX: wind along the street, faint creak of the door. No voices.
ENDS_ON: The masked man frozen mid-point into the lens, head tipped forward, the landlord and the couple still in the doorway behind him.
WHY: One repeated jab on the beat reads as rapping from the first second and leaves no room for extras or morphing.

=== CLIP v07 ===
Locked-off static camera, framing holds. The masked man slowly settles the mustard jacket over the old woman's shoulders and smooths it down with both gloved hands, then rests one hand on her shoulder and stays still. She shivers once and her breath leaves a small white cloud in the cold air. The frost on the window, the empty grate and the cup stay unchanged. SFX: quiet room tone, a thin wind against the glass, wool rustling. No voices.
ENDS_ON: The jacket resting round her shoulders, his gloved hand on it, her breath fading in the cold air.
WHY: The visible breath and the shiver show the freezing plainly, and the one slow tender action keeps it simple.

=== CLIP v09 ===
Locked-off static camera, framing holds. The masked man in the fedora nods his head on a steady beat and lifts the fork toward the lens in small jabs on each beat, while his other gloved hand keeps two fingers raised and wags them in time, four beats, then he holds still. The single bean stays on the fork. The bulb, the tin and the paper on the wall stay unchanged. SFX: the bulb's faint electric hum, a slow ticking clock. No voices.
ENDS_ON: The man leaning toward the lens with the fork held out, one bean on it, two fingers still raised.
WHY: Fork jabs and counted fingers turn the lone bean into the punchline while the body carries the rap.

=== CLIP f03 ===
Locked-off static camera, framing holds. The masked man in the armchair stays reclined, then lazily flicks the single banknote from his gloved hand toward the fireplace; it flutters down and lands in the flames, curls and burns. He sinks back into the chair and stays there. The fire flickers, snow keeps falling gently past the window, and the stack on the hearth stays put. SFX: the crackle and soft roar of the fire, a quiet whoosh as the note catches. No voices.
ENDS_ON: The man settled back in the armchair, the note burning in the grate, snow falling outside.
WHY: One lazy flick into a warm fire against falling snow says owning the printer means never fearing winter.
