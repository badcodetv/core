=== SHOT v01 ===
A 1920s music-video still in an apple orchard at late afternoon, low sun behind the trees. The mask and suit are exactly as in the attached reference image; the only man in this shot is PLUM. PLUM, a broad robber in a chalk-striped mustard-yellow three-piece suit, wears a chunky hand-knitted plum wool balaclava covering his whole head and neck, smooth unbroken knit where the face would be, a large white wool ring stitched round the whole face from brow to chin, and one small rounded red felt triangle high inside the ring over his left eye, on the right side of the picture at about two o'clock. Black leather gloves, collar up. He leans into a low 18mm lens, left glove filling a third of the frame, pointing straight into it, while his right glove grips a branch and shakes it hard. Green banknotes with plain engraved borders rain from the branches instead of leaves, backlit gold, a few blurred near the lens. Kodachrome-like colour, soft grain.
WHY: The tree literally makes money while PLUM raps the line straight down the lens.

=== SHOT v02 ===
A wide harvest-field still at hard high noon, 1920s Midwest, shot from far off on a 135mm lens so the stubble field and a red barn stack up flat behind. The mask and suit are exactly as in the attached reference image; the only man in this shot is BOTTLE. BOTTLE, a robber in a brown tweed suit, wears a chunky hand-knitted bottle-green wool balaclava covering his whole head and neck, seamless knit where the face would be, a large white wool ring stitched round the whole face from brow to chin, and one small rounded red felt triangle high inside the ring over his left eye; he is in three-quarter profile with his left side toward the camera, so the triangle is on the near side of his face. Black leather gloves, cuffs down. He drives a wooden pitchfork into a square hay bale and it bursts open, a fountain of banknotes with plain engraved borders spraying out among the straw. Short black shadows, dry dust, slight grain.
WHY: Making hay turns out to be making money, shown in one burst of a bale.

=== SHOT v05 ===
A cavernous 1920s factory machine room lit only by an orange furnace glow from the left and a row of caged bulbs overhead, iron flywheels and belts vanishing into smoky dark. Shot on a 24mm lens from a low angle. The mask and suit are exactly as in the attached reference image; the only gang member in this shot is MARINE. MARINE, a thin robber in a cream linen suit and two-tone shoes, wears a chunky hand-knitted marine-blue wool balaclava covering his whole head and neck, smooth knit where the face would be, a large white wool ring stitched round the whole face from brow to chin, and one small rounded red felt triangle high inside the ring over his left eye, facing the camera so it sits on the right side of the picture at about two o'clock. Black leather gloves. He raps into the lens, one glove chopping down on the beat, the other thumb jabbing back over his shoulder. Behind him a factory owner in a top hat hauls a big brass lever while adult workers in overalls shrink back against the wall, made small. Grain, deep shadows.
WHY: The owner at the controls and the shrinking workers make the reduction literal.

=== SHOT v08 ===
Looking straight down from a fourth-floor window onto a crowded 1920s city street under flat grey overcast light, 50mm lens, a stone ledge cropping the bottom edge. Hundreds of blank pale-green paper cheques with fine engraved borders flutter down like ticker tape over bowler hats, cloche hats and a stalled Model T. The mask and suit are exactly as in the attached reference image; the only gang member in this shot is RUST. In the middle of the crowd RUST, a robber in a black double-breasted suit and white tie, wears a chunky hand-knitted rust-gold wool balaclava covering his whole head and neck, seamless knit where the face would be, a large white wool ring stitched round the whole face from brow to chin, and one small rounded red felt triangle high inside the ring over his left eye; he looks up at the lens, so the triangle shows on the right side of the picture at about two o'clock. He snatches a cheque out of the air with a black-gloved hand stretched high above the hats. Muted brick reds and greens, soft grain.
WHY: Cheques falling from on high over the street, and one robber grabbing his, is the line in a single frame.
