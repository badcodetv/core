=== CLIP f02 ===
Locked-off camera at water level, framing holds. The bankers keep bailing: each dips his bucket into the boat and tips it over the side, water and a few soggy banknotes sloshing out, then dips again. The boat settles slowly lower in the water. The floating notes bob gently where they are. The ship, pier and boat stay exactly as they are; nothing new appears. SFX: water sloshing, buckets clanking, gulls far off. Ambient noise: quiet harbour lapping. No voices.
ENDS_ON: The same three bankers mid-scoop in the same boat, sitting slightly lower in the water, notes drifting around the hull.
WHY: One repeated action, bailing into the sea the boat is sinking in, shows the lyric plainly and gives the editor a loop to cut on.

=== CLIP f04 ===
Locked-off camera, framing holds. The masked man in the red suit stays facing the balloon, arms folded, and nods his head slowly on a steady beat. The burner flames pulse and the banknote balloon swells a little fuller. The couples in the basket wave. Mask, gloves, suit, mansion and lawn stay unchanged; the basket stays on the grass. SFX: burner roaring in bursts, canvas creaking. Ambient noise: birdsong, light breeze. No voices.
ENDS_ON: The masked man still arms folded, head tipped up at a fuller balloon, flames bright, couples waving from the grounded basket.
WHY: The swelling money balloon is the asset inflation, and the folded-arms nod keeps the gang in the groove without risking the mask.

=== CLIP f05 ===
Locked-off camera, framing holds. The masked man in front raps with his body: his raised hand pumps toward the lens on each beat, still showing three fingers, his fist stays on his chest, his head and shoulders nod in a steady groove. Behind him the baker places the single small roll in the worker's hand and the worker looks down at it. Mask, gloves and room stay unchanged; nobody else enters. SFX: a deep steady kick drum, bulb hum. No voices.
ENDS_ON: The masked man leaning into the lens with three fingers up, fist on chest; behind, the worker holding one small roll beside his barrow of notes.
WHY: The gang member counts the beat to camera while the trade behind him lands the joke, a barrow of money for one roll.

=== CLIP f09 ===
Locked-off camera, framing holds. The three masked men lower themselves slowly and steadily down their ropes, hand under hand, the same short distance, staying above the table. The woman in lilac carries on eating cake, unaware, fork to mouth again. Candle flames flicker. Masks, suits, ropes, cake and guests stay unchanged; nobody new enters. SFX: ropes creaking, a fork clinking on china, quiet string music from the room. No voices.
ENDS_ON: The three masked men hanging a little lower above the woman, who is still eating cake beneath them.
WHY: One slow descent over a woman too busy eating to notice is the whole "don't just eat cake" warning in a single action.
