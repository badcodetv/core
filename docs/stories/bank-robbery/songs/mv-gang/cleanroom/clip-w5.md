=== CLIP a03 ===
Locked-off camera at kerb height, framing holds. The black sedan finishes its sideways skid, rocks once on its springs and stops in place. Grey tyre smoke drifts and thins; the loose oranges roll a little and settle. The man on the running board grips the door frame and nods his shoulders on a steady beat. Same car, same street, masks unchanged, gloves on, nothing enters the frame. SFX: tyre screech dying out, a wooden crate thud, engine idle. No voices.
ENDS_ON: The sedan at rest beside the toppled crates, smoke thinning, the passenger still on the running board nodding.
WHY: Stopping the skid inside the frame gives one clear action that cannot teleport or leave and return.

=== CLIP x01 ===
Camera rides at the same speed as the two 1920s sedans, locked off, framing holds; both cars keep their place in frame while the lit street behind streaks past. Each man fires one short upward burst, a bright flash at the muzzle, then both pump their prop guns at the sky in time with a steady head-nod beat. Masks, hats and gloves unchanged, nothing new on the road. SFX: two short gun bursts, engines roaring, wind. No voices.
ENDS_ON: Both men braced on their running boards, guns raised skyward, muzzles smoking, cars level in frame.
WHY: Holding the cars fixed in frame and giving the men one burst then a beat pump keeps the action readable and the cars stable.

=== CLIP x02 ===
Camera travels alongside the lead roadster, locked off, framing holds; the three 1920s roadsters keep their places while the cracked lake bed streaks past and the amber dust plumes billow behind. The masked driver nods on a steady beat, then lifts his right gloved hand from the wheel and points it into the lens, holds, and returns it to the wheel. His white tie whips back. Mask unchanged, no new vehicles. SFX: engines roaring, gravel spray, wind. No voices.
ENDS_ON: The driver with both hands back on the wheel, head nodding, the two other roadsters in place behind in the glowing dust.
WHY: One point into the lens turns the race into a performance while the cars stay put in frame.

=== CLIP x03 ===
Locked-off camera, framing holds. The three masked men walk two slow steps toward camera through the blizzard, prop guns held straight up, then stop together. They nod heads and shoulders on a steady beat while their free gloved hands chop down in time. Snow streaks through the headlight beams. The parked sedans stay exactly where they are, nobody else appears, masks unchanged. SFX: howling wind, boots crunching on snow, idling engines. No voices.
ENDS_ON: The three men standing still in the headlights, guns up, nodding, the sedans parked beside them.
WHY: A short walk that stops, then a shared beat nod, gives the gang one simple swagger in a frame that holds.
