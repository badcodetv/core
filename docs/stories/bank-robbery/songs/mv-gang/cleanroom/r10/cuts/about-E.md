# Cut E: Energy curve

The cutting speed follows how loud each bar is. The film opens on one six-second hold of the gang having lunch on the girder (your candidate for the beginning), slows through two calm shots, and changes picture exactly on the drop at 10.82. From there it steps up again at 13 s and again on the first word at 20.14. The choruses are the fastest part, a cut on every beat, and the loudest stretch of verse 5 (164–176 s, the loudest bars in the song) cuts just as fast. Quiet bars get long holds: the explosion on "did I interrupt your meeting?", the dilution shot, the ledge after chorus 1, and the whole break (144–155), which runs four shots of 2.4–3.1 s.

The strongest moment is 2:56. At 175.98 the cut goes from half-beat cutting straight into a long hold on the snowy night field (h11-b), a place shown nowhere else in the film. After that the cut slows down: the walk away from the fireball, Plum's jacket on "It's time to give", then 5.1 s on the man on the bank steps (v11x-a) to 187.0.

All the keeps are in place: q01-b, z03-a, q04-a, d06m-a, d10-a and v11x-a. Chorus 2 repeats chorus 1 shot for shot, shifted 61.88 s. Every cut lands on a beat, a half beat or a strong hit, and 51 of the 143 land on a strong hit.
