# Cut C: On the words

Every sung line starts on its own cut (its `start_snapped`), and each line gets the clip that shows what it says. Sometimes that is literal (the wheelbarrow on "stack up the vaults like a wheelbarrow", MARINE on a narrow ledge for "risk of default is really rather narrow", the bundles in a crate for "elastic bands") and sometimes it is a pun ("debasement" is PLUM and BOTTLE lowered through the floor into the vault, "bailing out" is MARINE sliding down a rope from the blimp in place of the train, "inflation" is the balloon filling behind the crew). When a line names two things it gets a second cut on a strong hit: QE money then grandma, "standards bleeding" then the tenement roof for "landlords", "fiat" then the ticking dynamite clock for "debt", "left and right" either side of the stolen safe. Where nothing shows the line ("Oh, oh, oh", "Biggie said, wait!", "divide and conquer") the crew raps to camera. It is strongest in verse 3 and the break, where almost every line has its own picture, and every keep from Kai's notes is in place: q01-b at 24.5, z03-a at 29.6, q04-a at 49.9, d06m-a at 96.9, d10-a at 110.5 to 113.4 and v11x-a at 183.5. The choruses cut into the chase on "Robbery!", give call-the-police only its own line and keep the second half on basement and printing. Chorus 2 is chorus 1 shot for shot.

Where I broke a rule:
- The intro has no sung lines, so I cut it on a bar (4.06), the drop (10.83) and a strong hit (16.30), building calm (the girder lunch Kai suggested, then PLUM and the barrel) up to medium action. One clip cannot cover the 6.6 s from 1.3 s to 7.9 s.
- The chorus cut sits at 61.79, not 61.86. 61.79 is the beat, with the strong hit at 61.75 just before it, while 62.11 is a whole beat late. Chorus 2 follows its own line starts (about 61.93 s later), not exactly 61.88.
- q08-a (the gramophone) is held from "Rockin' 97" through "Don't get high off your own supply" (120.19 to 123.72), with no cut at 121.45, so Kai has the whole stretch to time the gramophone moment in Premiere.
- v11x-a runs from "But for heaven's sake" to the end, with no cut on "Don't just eat cake", because Kai wants the film to end on the man on the bank steps. He mimes the cake slice, so the shot still shows the line.
- Mid-line cuts on "grandma freezing" (37.26) and "don't feel winter" (163.07) are on beats, because those lines have no strong hit.
- Repeats: q04-a is used three times (on every printing line: "stimulus checks", "it prints all the dollars", "own a printer", each a different stretch), and c03g-b four times, all of them in the choruses. v07m-b (grandma) still has the triangle fault Kai flagged, and no other clip shows her. cn:safe4-b has the wrong mask, but Kai picked it as the basement shot.
