# Cut B: strict alternation

From the first word (20.18) to "Don't just eat cake", the picture swaps on every sung line: the gang rapping at the camera, then a story shot that acts out the line, then rapping again. Every cut sits on the line's `start_snapped` or on a strong drum hit. The choruses are the exception: car chase on "Robbery!" and "Call the police", then basement, underground and printing on "Debasement / basement / printer". Chorus 2 repeats chorus 1 shot for shot. The story shots are picked to say the line: workers counting notes on "work can get paid", money flung on "quantitative easing", grandma only on "grandma freezing", the rapping crew round a printing press on "stimulus checks", the fireball on "interrupt your meeting", the floor dropping into the vault on "shitty little drop", taking cover on "sick little cover", the wheelbarrow on "wheelbarrow", the narrow ledge on "Biggie said, wait!", notes poured out of the balloon on "Bailin' out" (instead of the train), and Plum giving grandma his jacket on "time to give". It is strongest in verses 3 and 4 (82.98 to 123.7) and in verse 5, where every rapping line has a different clip and the 2:56 change goes to a slow-motion chandelier flight, then the crew in the night snow. The intro is calm until the drop at 10.83 (the girder lunch Kai suggested, the distillery, Plum on his barrel), then medium action from 13.7, with no rapping shots.

Where it breaks the rule, and why:
- **One rapping shot holds two lines, four times**: q01-b (20.18 to 25.36), h20-a (31.16 to 35.97), c03g-b (38.56 to 43.39) and q04-a (46.31 to 51.78). The keepers make this necessary. q01-b at 24.5 and q04-a at 49.9 are rapping shots, and the lines that need their picture (QE/grandma, the explosion) have to be story shots. Strict alternation can't fit all of them. Holding one rapping shot across two lines keeps the picture alternating without ever cutting from one rapping shot to another.
- The same happens at d10-a (held to 113.08, Kai's "exactly here", which is 0.3 s before the wheelbarrow line) and at q08-a (the gramophone shot, held over "Rockin' 97" and "own supply" so that it is on screen at 122). The last shot, v11x-a, starts on the strong hit at 182.49 in the middle of "not just to take" and runs to 187.0.
- Verse 3 opens on a story shot straight after the chorus's last basement shot. This is needed so that d06m-a (96.9) and the magician's hand both land on rapping lines.
- Some story clips repeat in verse 5, each from a different stretch: y05-a, k36m-b, a03m-a, k12m-a, and v07m-b, which comes back on purpose for "time to give". All the rapping lines in verse 5 use clips seen nowhere else in the cut. c02-a appears four times because both choruses use it twice.
