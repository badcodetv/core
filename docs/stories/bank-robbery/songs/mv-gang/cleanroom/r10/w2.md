=== SHOT h05 ===
A frame from a 1920s feature film: six masked robbers rap at the lens on the platform of the elevated railway in the attached frame from the film, the wooden train car standing behind them with its doors open. The masks and suits are exactly as in the attached line-up card, masks copied exactly from its close-ups: PLUM, BOTTLE, CRIMSON, MARINE, RUST and SILVER. Each knitted balaclava has a white ring round the face and one small red triangle over the man's left eye, on the right side of the picture. CRIMSON leads, hunched forward with knees bent, his gloved right index finger jabbing at the lens, large in the foreground. The other five crowd behind him in a tight wedge, gloved arms swinging down on the beat. In the open car door a conductor stands holding his ticket punch, utterly unmoved. Low angle, 24mm, a metre from CRIMSON. Hard midday sun slices through the steel beams in stripes across their suits, deep shadow between. Shot on 35mm film, fine grain, 16:9, edge to edge.
WHY: The crew-wedge pushing at a low lens is the core street-video move, and the stripes of sun from the place keep it a real 1920s station.
