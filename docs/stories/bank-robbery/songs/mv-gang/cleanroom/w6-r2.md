=== SHOT v03 ===
A 1920s music-video still, 16:9, shot on an ultrawide lens from hip height inside a brick factory entrance hall at dawn. Cold grey light falls from tall wire-glass windows high on the left wall; one caged bulb glows amber over a brass punch clock on a wooden post. A long line of factory men in faded blue overalls and flat caps waits shoulder to shoulder, each holding a blank buff time card. In the foreground CRIMSON walks the line toward the lens, rapping straight into it. His mask and suit are exactly as in the attached reference image; only CRIMSON of the six is in this shot. Crimson hand-knitted rib-wool balaclava, smooth unbroken knit where the face would be, a large white wool ring stitched round the face from brow to chin, one small rounded red felt triangle high over his left eye, on the right side of the picture. Grey fedora, charcoal pinstripe double-breasted suit, collar up, black leather gloves, one gloved hand chopping down on the beat, the other counting off fingers. Fine film grain, soft edges.
WHY: Workers queue to clock in for their pay while the loudest robber walks the line counting it off, so "work and get paid" reads instantly.

=== SHOT v04 ===
A 1920s music-video still, 16:9, eye-level from a narrow cobbled street on a windy overcast afternoon, 28mm lens. Mid-ground left, a tired factory worker in a patched brown jacket and cloth cap has just torn open a small brown pay envelope; his few banknotes are lifting out of it on the wind. The notes stream away in a long loose ribbon down the street, tumbling past a fruit barrow and a lamp post, and fly in through the open tall window of a grand pale-stone bank with fluted columns at the far end of the street, its plain stone frieze bare. In the right foreground, close to the lens, PLUM shrugs enormously at the camera. His mask and suit are exactly as in the attached reference image; only PLUM of the six is in this shot. Plum hand-knitted balaclava, seamless knit face, a large white wool ring round the face, a small rounded red felt triangle high over his left eye, on the right side of the picture. Chalk-striped mustard-yellow three-piece suit, black leather gloves, palms up, shoulders at his ears. Flat soft daylight, muted brick reds, grain.
WHY: The wage literally blows out of the worker's hand and into the bank while the robber shrugs, so "where does it go? nobody knows" is answered on screen.

=== SHOT d03 ===
A 1920s music-video still, 16:9, shot from across a cinder road on a long telephoto lens that stacks the scene flat, at dusk. None of the six gang members from the attached reference image is in this shot; it shows only workers. A dense crowd of grown factory men and women in overalls, shawls and cloth caps presses against tall closed wrought-iron gates bound with a heavy chain, fists raised and shaking, mouths open shouting, one man rattling the bars with both hands. Behind the gates a soot-black brick mill rises with three tall chimneys pouring thick grey and orange-lit smoke into a bruised violet sky. The only strong light is the furnace glow spilling through the mill's lower windows, rimming the raised fists and caps in hot orange while the faces nearest us sit in cool blue shadow. Grimy red brick, rust on the gate scrolls, wet cinders underfoot. Heavy film grain, slight lens softness.
WHY: An angry mob shaking fists at the gates of the place that works them makes "mob, angry at their shitty little job" literal.

=== SHOT f08 ===
A 1920s music-video still, 16:9, looking steeply down from a second-floor iron fire escape with a 35mm lens onto a narrow tenement street in low golden late-afternoon sun raking in from the right. A black open-topped 1920s touring car with spoked wheels rolls at walking pace down the middle of the street. The masks and suits are exactly as in the attached reference image; RUST, MARINE and PLUM are in this shot. RUST drives in his rust-gold balaclava and black double-breasted suit with white tie. In the back seat MARINE, marine-blue balaclava and cream linen suit, and PLUM, plum balaclava and chalk-striped mustard-yellow suit, stand and fling handfuls of banknotes into the air with black-gloved hands. Each mask is seamless hand-knitted wool with a large white ring round the face and a small rounded red felt triangle high over the wearer's left eye. Grown men and women in worn coats and aprons crowd the stoops and lean from windows, cheering, arms up, catching notes. Washing lines strung overhead, peeling green and ochre paint, grain.
WHY: The robbers handing the money back to a poor street shows "time to give, not just to take" at a glance.
