=== CLIP c01 ===
Locked-off camera on a tripod, framing holds. The six masked men stand their ground and bob together on a steady head-nod groove, knees giving on each beat. Their free gloved fists pump up twice in time, props held still and pointed at the ceiling. The masks, gloves and suits stay exactly as they are. The men on the floor lie still; the room stays the same and nobody enters. SFX: a heavy drum beat, shoes on marble, a hush of breath. No voices.
ENDS_ON: The six men in the same line, fists high and props up, held mid-nod, with the people on the floor unmoved.
WHY: Six bodies nodding and punching the air on the beat gives the chant energy from the first frame with no walking or new arrivals to go wrong.

=== CLIP d11 ===
Locked-off camera, framing holds. The masked man pushes the loaded wooden wheelbarrow forward over the threshold in one slow, heavy shove, then stops and sets it down. Two banded bundles slide off the front and land flat on the floor beside the others; the rest of the heap stays put. His mask, gloves and suit stay unchanged. The vault door and bulb stay still. SFX: a creaking wheel, a thud as each bundle lands, a low hum from the bulb. No voices.
ENDS_ON: The barrow at rest just past the threshold, two more bundles lying flat on the floor, the man standing behind it.
WHY: One push and two falling bundles shows the vault being emptied by the barrowload, with too few moving objects for them to multiply.

=== CLIP e02 ===
Locked-off macro shot, framing holds. The gloved hand holding the stretched rubber band lets it go once; the band snaps tight round the brick of notes and the brick gives a small jolt on the counter. Both hands then rest flat on the brick and stay still. The gloves, sleeves, lamp and loose bands stay unchanged. SFX: a sharp rubbery snap, a soft thump of paper on steel, a quiet room tone. No voices.
ENDS_ON: The brick of notes bound tight with the band, both gloved hands resting on it in the lamplight.
WHY: A single snap of the band is the lyric's image and lands in the first second, a small physical cause and effect that holds up.

=== CLIP v13 ===
Locked-off low camera, framing holds. The masked man stays crouched on the chained pile and raps with his body: his head nods on a steady beat, and his pointing hand jabs toward the lens four times in rhythm while his other fist stays pressed to his chest. His mask, gloves and suit stay exactly as they are. The chains, ledgers and shelving stay still; dust drifts in the window light. SFX: a deep drum beat, chains creaking faintly. No voices.
ENDS_ON: The man leaning toward the lens, his finger pointing straight into it and his fist on his chest, held on the last beat.
WHY: Repeating one jab into the lens on the beat keeps the delivery readable for a man with no mouth, and leaves the mask and the chained pile undisturbed.
