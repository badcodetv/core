=== SHOT d06 ===
A music-video still in a cramped 1920s basement speakeasy, shot low and close on a wide-angle lens. In the foreground, a robber called BOTTLE leans his elbows on a scuffed mahogany bar and raps straight into the lens. His thumb jabs back over his shoulder at the barman, his other gloved hand chops flat on the beat. His head is a chunky hand-knitted bottle-green wool hood, smooth knit where the face would be, ringed with oatmeal chain stitch from brow to chin, and one small rounded red felt triangle sewn high over his right eye, on the left of the picture. Brown tweed suit, collar up, black leather gloves. Behind him, a waistcoated barman calmly tips a tin water jug into an amber whiskey bottle with a blank label, the spirit visibly paling. Two bare amber bulbs on the back bar, plum walls, smoke, coarse film grain, deep shadow.
WHY: The watering of the whiskey happens in plain sight while Bottle's thumb points at it, so "dilutional" reads at once.

=== SHOT v14 ===
A music-video still inside a timber barn turned bootleg distillery, framed at eye level on a 35mm lens, hard afternoon sun slicing through gaps in the plank walls and catching floating dust. A tall hammered copper pot still with a coiled copper worm fills the right of the frame. RUST stands centre facing the camera, rapping: one black-gloved palm pushes a small glass of clear moonshine away to the side, the other hand points straight down the lens. His head is a hand-knitted rust-gold wool hood, smooth knit over the face, a black chain-stitched ring outlining it, a small rounded red felt triangle high over his right eye, on the left of the picture. Black double-breasted suit, white tie, collar up. Beside him PLUM, broad, in a chalk-striped mustard-yellow three-piece suit and a plum knitted hood with a cream ring and the same red triangle over his right eye, offers the glass with a gloved hand. Warm wood, green copper patina, film grain.
WHY: The driver literally refusing a glass of the gang's own brew beside the still is the line, word for word.

=== SHOT x06 ===
A music-video still at night on open water: a long, low varnished mahogany speedboat tears across a black bay, bow lifted, white spray fanning from its hull, seen from low on the waterline with a telephoto lens so the distant shore lights compress behind it. From off-frame left, a Coast Guard cutter's searchlight throws a hard white beam that pins the boat and turns the spray to glitter. Stacked wooden liquor crates fill the stern under a loose tarp. MARINE, thin, in a cream linen suit and a marine-blue hand-knitted hood with a cream chain-stitched ring, grips the wheel; CRIMSON, in a charcoal pinstripe double-breasted suit and a crimson knitted hood with a cream ring under a grey fedora, kneels on the crates, a Thompson held pointing at the sky. Both look back into the light, so the small rounded red felt triangle over each man's right eye sits on the left of his face in the picture. Black gloves, collars up. Cold moonlight, grain.
WHY: Searchlight, speedboat and crates in one frozen chase moment make the "action" chorus beat a real rum-running run.

=== SHOT x07 ===
A music-video still of a police raid on a 1920s roadhouse, seen from high in the rafters looking down on a wide lens. Uniformed policemen in long blue coats and peaked caps swing fire axes into a row of oak whiskey barrels; staves split and amber liquor gushes across the plank floor in a spreading flood, soaking scattered playing cards and overturned chairs. Car headlights blaze through the front windows from the left, throwing long shadows of the axes across the room. In the far right corner, a back door stands open to blue night: SILVER, tall, in an oxblood-red suit with a bow tie and a silver-grey hand-knitted hood with a crimson chain-stitched ring, holds it while BOTTLE, in brown tweed and a bottle-green hood with an oatmeal ring, slips through carrying a crate. Silver glances back up toward the camera, the small rounded red felt triangle high over his right eye showing on the left side of his face. Gloves on, collars up. Grain, hard shadows.
WHY: The cops are busy smashing barrels in the flood while the gang walks out the back with the goods, so the raid and the escape read in one look.
