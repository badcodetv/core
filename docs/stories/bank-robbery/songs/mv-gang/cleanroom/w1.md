=== SHOT a01 ===
A music-video film still on slightly grainy colour film, taken through a long telephoto lens from the middle of a 1920s city street early on a clear morning. Six masked robbers walk side by side straight toward the camera. A low sun behind them throws their long shadows forward across the cobbles and streetcar rails. Each man wears a hand-knitted rib wool balaclava that covers his whole head and neck, with smooth unbroken knit where the face would be. A thin chain-stitched ring in a contrasting wool outlines the face, and a small red felt triangle with rounded corners sits high over his right eye, which puts it on the left side of the picture. From left to right: a plum mask with a cream ring over a mustard chalk-stripe suit; bottle green with an oatmeal ring over brown tweed; crimson with a cream ring, a charcoal pinstripe and a grey fedora; marine blue with a cream ring over cream linen; rust-gold with a black ring, a black suit and a white tie; silver-grey with a crimson ring, an oxblood suit and a bow tie. All six wear black leather gloves with their collars up. Each rests a 1920s Thompson prop gun on his shoulder, muzzle to the sky. Behind them, out of focus, rise the pale stone columns of a bank.
WHY: The intro shows the whole crew once, facing the lens and readable, with the telephoto squeezing six men and the bank into one wall of colour.

=== SHOT a02 ===
A music-video still from a 1920s bank lobby, shot with a wide-angle lens from knee height on the marble floor. The tall brass-and-glass front doors burst inward. Shards of glass hang in the air, and hard morning sunlight blasts through the doorway behind the gang, so they arrive half-silhouetted with a rim of light. CRIMSON leads. His crimson hand-knitted balaclava has a cream ring stitched round the face outline, smooth knit where the face would be, and a small rounded red felt triangle high over his right eye, on the left side of the picture. He wears a grey fedora over the mask, a charcoal pinstripe double-breasted suit, black leather gloves and an upturned collar, and holds a 1920s Thompson prop gun straight up at the coffered ceiling. In the foreground, customers in mismatched period clothes flinch and duck: a woman in a cloche hat drops her handbag and a clerk flings up a ledger. Dust swirls in the light shafts.
WHY: "Robbery!" is the moment of the doors bursting in, shot from floor level so the glass, the light and the flinch all hit at once.

=== SHOT v10 ===
A music-video still inside a walnut-panelled 1920s boardroom at mid-morning, shot on a wide-angle lens close to the doorway. MARINE has just stepped through the open door. He leans into the lens in close-up on the left of the frame, one black-gloved hand raised flat beside his head in a polite little apology wave. His marine-blue hand-knitted balaclava has a cream ring chain-stitched round the face outline, smooth knit where the face would be, and a small rounded red felt triangle high over his right eye, on the left side of the picture. He wears a cream linen suit with the collar turned up. Behind him on the right, a long mahogany table recedes, lined with old bankers in wing collars sitting in green leather chairs, all frozen. One holds a lit cigar halfway to his open mouth, another has stopped pouring from a decanter. Window light slices sideways through the hanging blue cigar smoke.
WHY: The apologetic raised glove up close, set against a whole table of bankers frozen mid-cigar, plays "did I interrupt your meeting?" literally.

=== SHOT v11 ===
A music-video still at noon on the wide granite steps of an enormous columned central-bank building, shot with an ultra-wide lens from a few steps below, looking up. The overhead sun drops short, hard shadows under every column. SILVER, the tall one, leans down into the lens and wags one black-gloved index finger at the viewer. The finger is large and slightly bulged by the lens, and his other gloved hand is planted on his hip. His silver-grey hand-knitted balaclava has a crimson ring chain-stitched round the face outline, smooth knit where the face would be, and a small rounded red felt triangle high over his right eye, on the left side of the picture. He wears an oxblood-red suit, a bow tie and an upturned collar, with the cuffs down over the gloves. Far behind him, at the top of the steps between the giant columns, a stout banker in a top hat hugs a fat cloth moneybag to his chest and peers back warily.
WHY: The scolding finger in the lens on the steps of the money temple, with the clutching banker behind, reads at once as a warning about who the bank is really for.
