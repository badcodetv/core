I did the research first. Below is what I found, then the six prompts.

## What the research found and how it shaped the prompts

1. **The prompt for "Best one" is probably why its intro sounded Indian.** Its style text asks for sitar, and Suno's v6 FAQ says v6 is built to be "precise". Exclude guides also warn that if your style text contains something, the exclude box can't remove it. So I didn't reuse the old text. Each new style describes what the listening passes actually heard: boom-bap around 90 BPM, a bluesy acoustic guitar, a dirty bass, wah guitar stabs, a gang-chant chorus, a drum drop at 0:25 and vinyl crackle. Sitar, tabla and raga go in Exclude. https://help.suno.com/en/articles/13924481 · https://usesuno.com/guide/negative-prompts/
2. **Variety rewrites your style text before it generates. At 0 ("off") your tags stay as typed.** All six are set to off so the "Best one" sound isn't rewritten. https://help.suno.com/en/articles/13924481 · https://undetectr.com/blog/suno-v6-changing-your-prompt
3. **Max Mode is recommended for songs over 2 minutes and for "keeping vocals and style consistent through the whole track."** These lyrics need about 4.5 minutes, so all six are on, at 285 s. https://help.suno.com/en/articles/13924481 · https://siliconsense.club/guides/suno-v6-controls
4. **Suggested starting settings are Weirdness about 20–30 and Style Influence about 70–80.** I used 20–25 and 80–85. https://undetectr.com/blog/suno-v6-changing-your-prompt · https://sunostyles.com/blog/suno-v6-settings
5. **The likely fix for "same voice every time":**
   - Put who the singer is in one clause (age, range, texture), placed right after the genre.
   - Describe how he performs separately, with verbs like "talks", "croons", "whispers", "barks".
   - Repeat the delivery in the section tags inside the lyrics.
   - A single clause like "gritty male vocal" leaves the voice to chance.

   Each prompt here gives a different age, range, texture and verb. https://undetectr.com/blog/suno-v6-vocal-prompts · https://musicpromptpro.com/topics/suno-v6-vocal-prompts
6. **Mood words get absorbed into Suno's default sound, while concrete structure gets followed.** "Drums enter immediately, no atmospheric intro" was the biggest single lever in one test. So each prompt spells out the 0:25 drop, the chorus chant and the guitar-only break. https://www.jgbeatslab.com/ai-music-lab-blog/why-suno-songs-sound-the-same
7. **Keep Exclude short and don't contradict it in the style.** "Rap, hip hop" is no longer excluded, because "Best one" is a boom-bap track. https://usesuno.com/guide/negative-prompts/ · https://songsmith.studio/blog/suno-negative-prompts-guide
8. **v6-wild is "less predictable and more varied".** One user got clearer vocals on it with low Weirdness and high Style Influence, but it fails about half the time in busy genres. Only the quietest voice (the whisperer) uses it. https://help.suno.com/en/articles/13924481 · https://replayedstudio.com/blog/why-suno-v6-sounds-different/ · https://undetectr.com/blog/suno-v6-vocal-prompts
9. **Don't name real artists. Describe the voice instead.** https://undetectr.com/blog/suno-v6-vocal-prompts
10. **Basic voice gender tags don't stop one voice coming back every time.** Giving each take its own detailed voice identity is a better bet. https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/keep-same-singer-across-ai-songs-suno

Jack's rules are covered in every prompt. "Screaming", "double-time" and "fast rap" are excluded, and the chorus chant is written as "punchy, not screamed". Kai's words are unchanged; I only added bracketed tags.
