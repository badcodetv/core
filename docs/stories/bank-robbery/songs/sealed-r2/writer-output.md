**What the research found that shaped the prompts**

1. **Suno tags "Best one" as "psych folk, funk" (model V6), not hip-hop.** The lyrics shown on its page don't match the backing. So every prompt names a psych-folk flavour and keeps a strummed acoustic guitar under the wah/funk chops. https://suno.com/song/024b82e8-378c-47bd-bf73-bb5eafea2ed5
2. **Psychedelic funk uses wah, fuzz, echo chambers and vocal distorters,** and has a deadpan Madchester branch. That's where the Manchester voice and the phaser voice come from. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychedelic_funk
3. **v6 follows the style box literally and works better with sentences than tag lists.** Aim for about 250–600 characters (limit 1,000), put tempo and groove early, and put unwanted things in Exclude. https://roo.beehiiv.com/p/best-suno-prompts-v6
4. **Describe each part with verbs and in a set order:** genre, then vocals, then instruments and arrangement. v6 won't add doubles or ad-libs unless you ask, and bracket tags are only suggestions. One user only got good vocals on v6-wild with low Weirdness and high Style Influence. https://undetectr.com/blog/suno-v6-vocal-prompts
5. **Voices differ through register, texture, delivery and placement, not character names.** That's why six gangster characters came out as one voice. https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/mastering-vocal-diversity-in-suno-a-detailed-tutorial-for-unique-sounds
6. **No keyword guarantees a bass voice, and many genres default to high male vocals.** So each prompt describes the low register in its own words and excludes tenor/falsetto (or soprano). https://roo.beehiiv.com/p/the-suno-ai-prompts-list-that-actually-works-2026-genre-artist-style-and-use-case
7. **Variety rewrites your style text and can even change the vocal gender; Off keeps it as written.** Max Mode keeps vocals consistent on songs over two minutes. So Variety stays off and Max Mode stays on. https://popularaitools.ai/blog/suno-v6-variety-style-influence-weirdness-2026 · https://www.ytyng.com/en/blog/suno-v6-review-variety-max-mode-spectrum · https://github.com/bitwize-music-studio/claude-ai-music-skills/issues/562
8. **Voices drift, and prompted male voices sometimes come out female.** So gender is set in the settings, stated in the style text and excluded for the opposite gender. https://vantaige.io/ai-tool/suno
9. **Accents work better combined with tone, age and pacing.** So each voice names an English region plus those traits. https://help.dubformer.ai/en/articles/8847778-voice-design-prompting
