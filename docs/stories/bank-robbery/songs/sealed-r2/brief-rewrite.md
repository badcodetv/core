2. **Psychedelic funk** traditionally uses wah pedals, fuzz, echo chambers and "vocal distorters". There is also a "stiff" Madchester version (Happy Mondays), which suggested voice 1 and the treated voice 6. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychedelic_funk
3. **v6 reads the style box literally and prefers sentences to tag lists.** Aim for about 250–600 characters (limit 1,000), put the BPM and groove near the start, and put unwanted things in Exclude rather than writing "no X" in the style. https://roo.beehiiv.com/p/best-suno-prompts-v6
4. **Tell v6 what each part does, using verbs** (holds, breathes, pulls back). The suggested order is genre, then vocals, then instruments, arrangement and ending. v6 no longer adds doubles or ad-libs by itself, so you have to ask for them. Bracket tags are suggestions, not commands. One user only got usable vocals on **v6-wild with low Weirdness and high Style Influence**. https://undetectr.com/blog/suno-v6-vocal-prompts
5. **Voice formula: role + register + texture + delivery + articulation + energy arc + how the voice relates to the arrangement.** Voices separate through register, texture and delivery, not character names. "Low baritone", "conversational low placement", talk-singing and vocal fry are all usable options. That explains why six gangster characters sounded like one voice. https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/mastering-vocal-diversity-in-suno-a-detailed-tutorial-for-unique-sounds
6. **"Basso profondo" works only in some genres. Many genres default to high male voices, and no single keyword guarantees a bass voice.** So each prompt also states a low key and a low register in its section tags, and excludes tenor and falsetto. https://roo.beehiiv.com/p/the-suno-ai-prompts-list-that-actually-works-2026-genre-artist-style-and-use-case
7. **Variety rewrites your style text and can add details or change the vocal gender; Off keeps your text as written.** Style Influence controls how closely Suno follows the text, and Weirdness 50 counts as "normal". Max Mode is meant for songs over two minutes and keeps the vocal consistent. This is why Variety stays off and Max Mode stays on. https://popularaitools.ai/blog/suno-v6-variety-style-influence-weirdness-2026 · https://www.ytyng.com/en/blog/suno-v6-review-variety-max-mode-spectrum · https://github.com/bitwize-music-studio/claude-ai-music-skills/issues/562
8. **Even with identical settings, Suno voices drift, and prompted male voices sometimes come out female.** So the vocal gender is set in the settings, stated in the style text and excluded for the opposite gender. https://vantaige.io/ai-tool/suno
