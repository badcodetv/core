# The Bank Robbery music video: colourful noir

Jack, 2026-10-30. A third version of the music video, in a new Premiere sequence when it gets that far. His words in full: [`cleanroom/jack.md`](./cleanroom/jack.md).

**This round: a bank of cool general shots. We make it make sense later.** No story yet, and no lip sync yet.

## The look

Colourful noir: a mob boss's den in a colourful cartoon, crossed with a neon-lit 1940s horror-noir game city. The look is written up in `../../look.md` (Per part) once Jack has picked. EXPLORING.

## The shots Jack asked for

1. The speakeasy den and its band.
2. The same band under a bridge at night: city lights on the water, car headlights lighting them, gangster style.
3. Fast car chases.
4. The first-person driver from the last version, followed through the film as one of many threads.
5. A car pulling a small safe out of a wall and dragging it, sparks flying, fleeing the cops.
6. Robberies by people in balaclavas with the BadCode logo on them, with people being robbed.
7. Panning shots of pretty Prohibition-era cities, lights and reflections, interesting shots.
8. Old-school hot rod racing.

## Later, not now

- **Lip sync:** the characters talk the lyrics in conversations; the band sings the chorus and some of the song. Held until a song take is locked.
- **Transitions:** keep them cool, make them realistic. Handled in the clips; the stills only note a way out of each shot.
- **Song:** no take is picked. Jack will screen-record one with OBS as a stand-in and swap in the real download later.

## What Jack did not like in the last version

AI slop · psychedelic transitions · the blue and red scarf teams · the bakers walking in randomly · the story in general.
