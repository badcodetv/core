=== SHOT pov1 ===
A man's own view from the driving seat of a closed 1931 sedan, his eyes level with the top of the steering wheel, travelling down an empty straight country road at night. Along the bottom of the picture are a large thin black steering wheel and his two hands resting on its rim at ten and two, relaxed, in oxblood leather gloves, a narrow strip of mustard check cuff showing at each wrist. Behind the wheel a flat painted steel dash, the dull green of old enamel, holds two round cream-faced gauges marked only with ticks. Above it, a tall upright windscreen divided by a slim metal frame, with a dark cloth roof lining across the top of the picture and thick dark pillars at both sides. Through the glass, a long bottle-green bonnet with a centre hinge and a small upright radiator cap, and past it the domed tops of two separate mudguards with a round lamp standing on each. The headlamps throw a weak yellow pool down thirty yards of pale dry macadam, which fades to black beyond. A row of bare wooden telegraph poles runs along one verge. Far down the road, one small building stands on the verge, its wall painted crimson, lit by a single bare bulb over a shut door. Above a black line of low hills, the last of the dusk is a band of deep indigo with some stars. The road is otherwise empty and nothing is in the air. Dye-rich colour, soft grain, honest darkness in the cabin with only a little of the headlamp glow on the gloves.
WHY: With one straight road, one lit building and nothing loose in frame, the only thing the clip has to do is drive forward, which removes every object that morphed last time.

=== SHOT pov3 ===
A driver's view from behind the wheel of a 1932 open tourer rolling at steady speed along a dockside street at dusk, heading straight for the mouth of a brick road tunnel. At the bottom of the picture, a slim black steering wheel with one hand at the top of its rim in an oxblood leather glove, and the other glove resting on the round black knob of a long floor gear lever, a line of mustard check cuff at each wrist. A folding upright windscreen in a plain nickel frame stands square to the road, and beyond it a long mustard-yellow bonnet with side louvres runs out to two round lamps standing on separate black mudguards. The road is dry granite setts with a pair of tram rails running dead straight down the middle, carrying the eye to the tunnel a hundred yards off: a tall arch of soot-dark brick, its inside true black except for a line of small amber lamps curving away along the roof. One small ruby tail lamp shows far inside, the only other vehicle in the picture. On one side, a row of tall warehouses in flat painted colours, oxblood, faded teal and tobacco brown, with their big loading doors shut and one lit lamp over each door. On the other side, a low stone wall with black water beyond it and one moored barge, unlit. The sky over the warehouses is a dim plum going to rust at the horizon, and that light falls from ahead onto the bonnet and the backs of the gloves. The horizon is level. The street is empty of people. Fine grain, heavy dye colour, deep shadow under the wall.
WHY: The level car, straight rails and fixed tunnel keep it grounded with no jump to fake, and the black arch gives an 8-second drive a physical dark to end in for a cut.
