One man leans from its window: his whole head in a hand-knitted cream wool balaclava with no eye holes and no mouth hole, one thin black ring stitched around the face, and high inside it, over where one eye would be, pushed up near the ring's edge and well off centre, one small solid red felt triangle with rounded corners. Collar up, gloved. A tight chain runs from the bumper to one small green safe, half out of a broken hole in the wall. The rest of the alley is empty.
MOTION: Locked camera, no movement. The car creeps forward and the taut chain slides the one green safe out of the wall and across the cobbles. Exactly one car, one safe, one man. Nothing else moves, nothing enters the frame.
SECONDS: 4
WHY: Starting with the safe already half out and the alley stripped bare leaves the model only one object to slide, so there is nothing to duplicate.
