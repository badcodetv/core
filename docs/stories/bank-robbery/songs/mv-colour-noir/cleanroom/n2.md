=== SHOT rods1 ===
Two stripped-down 1929 racing roadsters run side by side across a dry alkali lakebed in the last hour of sun, seen in side view from a camera at hip height on a car keeping pace thirty metres away. Both cars point to the right of the frame, parallel, on the same heading. The nearer car, in oxblood red enamel, is half a length ahead; the farther car, in mustard yellow, sits just behind it and beyond it, its whole outline clear of the red one. Each is a tall, narrow, upright machine: a square radiator shell, separate curved mudguards, a flat running board, thin wire-spoked wheels on narrow tyres pressed firmly on the ground, and a small upright windscreen. Each driver is a grown man in a brown leather cap and goggles, both hands low on a wooden wheel. Bodies are plain and unlettered. A low, even trail of pale dust stretches straight back to the left edge from each rear wheel. The sun sits behind the camera and lays long black shadows under the cars on the cracked pinkish-white ground. The mountains behind are a band of dark plum beneath a deepening blue sky, softened by a gentle horizontal blur, while the cars stay sharp and their spokes blur into grey discs. Clear lakebed fills the space ahead of the lead car. Coarse colour film grain, dense reds and yellows, edge to edge in 16:9.
WHY: Two cars on one parallel heading, held level by a pacing camera, can simply keep running in place while the background slides, leaving nothing to reverse, jump or collide.

=== SHOT rods4 ===
A long lens looks up a wide concrete flood channel at dusk in 1931, from knee height on the dry floor, a little right of the centre line so the three road bridges step away toward the left instead of lining up. The nearest arch crosses the top of the frame, its underside almost black. Two 1930 roadsters come toward the camera along the flat floor, one behind the other, just emerging from the shadow of the middle arch and still well down the channel. The lead car is chalky turquoise, the second mustard yellow, offset a little to its left so both can be seen. They are tall, narrow and upright: square radiators, separate curved mudguards, running boards, thin spoked wheels and small upright windscreens, with two round lamps lit a dull yellow on each. All four wheels of each car sit level on the concrete. Each driver is a grown man in a leather cap, sitting straight up. A thin, still ribbon of water runs along the middle of the floor beside them, carrying one long dim bar of the sky; the concrete around it is pale, stained with rust and only damp in patches. Behind the far bridge the sky is deep plum fading to dull rose at the horizon. One iron lamp on the far bridge throws a small amber pool on the wall below it. The sloped banks are empty and already in deep blue-grey shadow. Fine colour film grain, edge to edge in 16:9.
WHY: Two level cars driving straight up a flat floor with one still strip of water and empty banks gives one plain event, with no wall-riding, spray or onlookers to exaggerate or morph.
