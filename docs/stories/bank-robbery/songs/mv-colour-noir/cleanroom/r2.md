=== SHOT l03 ===
Head-on from the foredeck of a mahogany rum-runner's speedboat, 35mm lens at his chest height, night fog tearing past. A Black seaman of about 55 stands at the spoked wheel, the wheel low in frame below his chest: broad flat face, wide nose, heavy cheeks, grey stubble, gap in his lower teeth, navy watch cap, open neck. His mouth is wide open in song, his face filling a third of the frame and squared to us. Behind his head a hard blue searchlight from an unseen patrol boat burns a cone through the fog. Amber light from the instrument lamps lifts onto his mouth and jaw. The picture fills the frame edge to edge, with bare unmarked hull and deck.
MOTION: Camera stays locked to the boat in a single continuous shot, no cuts. The blue searchlight beam sweeps slowly through the fog behind him. His head barely moves. Nobody else sings. He sings: it is state level manipulation
SECONDS: 4
WHY: The searchlight closing behind a man who won't turn round puts the danger behind him and leaves his broad, amber-lit face clear for the mouth tool.

=== SHOT l04 ===
Long 135mm lens from the far rail of a low grey rum boat at night, the deck compressed and the sea stacked up behind him. A pale Irish American of about 30 stands three-quarter to us, his face filling a third of the frame: freckles across a narrow nose broken once, red hair under a black fedora, a boxer's split through one eyebrow, clean-shaven, chipped front tooth, shirt open at the throat. He sings mid-word, unbothered. Behind his shoulder, out of focus and huge, a column of water from a warning shot stands lit red and pink. A green running lamp beside him throws clean green light across his cheek and mouth. Wet deck to every edge of the frame, no markings on the boat.
MOTION: Camera holds still on the long lens, single continuous shot, no cuts. The column of water collapses slowly behind his shoulder. His head barely moves. Nobody else sings. He sings: it is the devil with a radio station
SECONDS: 4
WHY: A flat long lens makes the water column loom right at his shoulder while the green lamp keeps a freckled, unmistakably different face readable.

=== SHOT f3 ===
Camera a hand's width above black water, 24mm lens, close off the stern quarter of a small wooden skiff crossing the frame at night. A slim Filipino American deckhand of about 28 sits at the oars, three-quarter to us and leaning toward the lens: wide cheekbones, small flat nose, narrow chin, smooth clean-shaven skin, hatless, soaked black hair stuck to his forehead, wet undershirt. His face fills a third of the frame, oar handles low below his chest. Far behind him the crew's own boat burns against a timber pier, orange fire spilling across the water toward us. A cold blue-green lantern on the skiff's stern seat lights his mouth from below. The water runs unbroken to the frame's edges.
MOTION: Camera floats steady at the waterline, single continuous shot, no cuts. Only the flames move, rising on the burning boat behind him and flickering on the water. His head barely moves. Nobody else sings. He sings: that drives the situation
SECONDS: 4
WHY: The waterline view keeps the burning boat small and far behind a young, slight face, and he sings with his back to what he has just lost.

=== SHOT f4 ===
Looking almost straight down from high in a schooner's rigging at night, 18mm lens, the masts converging to a point far below. A barrel-chested Portuguese American of about 60 clings to the ropes just under the lens, face tipped up and square to us, looking just above the camera: heavy single brow, big jug ears, bulbous nose, deep-cut jowls, white stubble, tweed cap pushed back, missing eye tooth. His face fills a third of the frame, ropes running beside it and never across it. Far beneath his shoulders, sacks of bottles swing out over the side toward two small motorboats with red lamps on violet water. A hanging yellow lamp beside the lens lights his whole face. Dark sea fills all the corners, nothing written on anything.
MOTION: Camera hangs fixed in the rigging, single continuous shot, no cuts. One sack of bottles swings slowly over the side far below him. His head barely moves. Nobody else sings. He sings: working people get paid in money
SECONDS: 4
WHY: The dizzying overhead angle gives this old, big-eared man a drop behind him that no other shot has, and his upturned face still meets the camera for the sync.

=== SHOT e4 ===
Square-on and dead level with the open door of a boxcar in a wet freight yard at night, 50mm lens, the door frame set symmetrically like a stage. A very thin, elegant Black Pullman porter of about 65 sits in the doorway facing us, hatless: long narrow skull, white cropped hair, sunken cheeks, high hollow temples, long upper lip, clean-shaven, bow tie hanging undone from an open collar well below his chin. His face fills a third of the frame. Unmarked whiskey crates are stacked in the dark behind him. Through the far door, small and soft, a yard guard's lantern approaches in the steam. Magenta signal light falls full across his mouth and cheek, green signal light rims his other side, both glowing in steam and wet ballast to the frame's edges.
MOTION: Camera holds level with a barely perceptible push in, single continuous shot, no cuts. Steam drifts slowly across the coloured signal lights behind him. His head barely moves. Nobody else sings. He sings: ill gotten gains, no need to explain
SECONDS: 6
WHY: Formal stillness and an old man's gaunt face give the cut a slow, elegant breath, and the far lantern coming for him keeps the threat in frame.

=== SHOT c2 ===
Chest-height 28mm lens tilted a few degrees off level, inside a timber dock warehouse at night under sagging strings of bare bulbs, whiskey barrels stacked into the dark. Nearest the lens, face filling a third of the frame and turned three-quarter to us, an Italian American of 25 shouts: sharp jaw, thin hooked nose, slick black hair, clean-shaven, one arm straight up holding a revolver at the rafters, well clear of his face. Behind him to the right, a head taller than anyone should be, an enormous Samoan dock worker of 35 with a broad flat nose, head thrown back, lifts a Thompson one-handed. Further back at left, barely reaching the big man's elbow, a tiny old Jewish bookkeeper of 70 with thick round glasses and big ears stands on tiptoe, revolver raised in both hands. Amber bulbs overhead, cold cyan moonlight through the slats behind, unmarked barrels to every edge.
MOTION: Camera holds with a slight handheld shake, single continuous shot, no cuts. The strings of bulbs swing gently overhead. Guns stay pointed up. Nobody else sings. The men shout together: Call the police, it's a robbery!
SECONDS: 4
WHY: Three bodies that cannot average out, staggered in depth with one clear front face, turn the duplicate crew into a mismatched gang and still give the mouth tool its target.
