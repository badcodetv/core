=== SHOT chase1 ===
One continuous shot at bumper height, chasing the red sedan down the wet cobbles. [0-5s] The sedan accelerates hard, its rear squatting on its springs, tyres smoking and skittering over the tram rail, while sparks keep raining from the same spot on the girder overhead. [5-8s] The camera swerves right at speed; the riveted pillar rushes up and passes inches from the lens, its black iron filling the frame.
ENDS ON: Full-frame black iron from the pillar passing the lens, cut inside the black.
WHY: Timed beats give the car's weight time to read before a physical object, already in the frame, closes the shot.

=== SHOT chase2 ===
Locked off on the rooftop beside the water tank, no camera movement for six seconds. Far below, the yellow roadster slides sideways across the junction, tail swinging out and dust trailing, straightens with a lurch, and runs left. The black sedan brakes and turns after it, the green tram rolls steadily on, and pedestrians hurry clear. Steam drifts; the city lights hold steady. In the last two seconds, the camera whips left and down past the tank's iron leg, smearing to blur at the peak. One continuous shot.
ENDS ON: Peak leftward motion blur past the tank's leg, travelling the same way as the roadster, ready to cut into a shot moving in that direction.
WHY: A still, distant camera keeps the cars small and credibly heavy, so the single whip at the end hits harder.

=== SHOT chase3 ===
Camera tracks alongside at the same speed, one continuous shot. The teal sedan fishtails on the ice, its heavy back end stepping out and catching, its crates shifting against the windows as spray blasts from the rear wheels. It pulls ahead, sliding out of frame left. The police motorcycle and sidecar skid outward, directly toward the camera, until the rider's headlamp swings into the lens and blooms to white. His hands stay on the bars.
ENDS ON: The motorcycle's headlamp flaring the whole frame white, cut on the flare into the next shot's brightest light.
WHY: The handover from sedan to motorcycle gives the side-on tracking shot a reason to end in the headlamp, so the flare is a motivated exit.

=== SHOT chase4 ===
The camera stays fixed to the car's interior, moving only as the cabin jolts. The car bounces over cobbles; the man grips the seat back and is thrown sideways, and the oranges drop, bounce on the velvet and roll along the seat under gravity. Through the oval window, the motorcycle closes in as market stalls fall behind. Then the car swings hard under a stone railway arch: darkness sweeps through the cabin, and black stone slides across the oval window until it fills it. One continuous shot, with the rider's hands on the handlebars.
ENDS ON: Cabin and oval window both gone to black under the arch, cut on that black.
WHY: Tying the camera to the car lets every jolt and rolling orange prove the speed and weight, with no need to show the tyres.
