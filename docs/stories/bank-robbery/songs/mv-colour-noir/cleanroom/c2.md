=== SHOT mask1 ===
One continuous shot, camera locked. The man in the mustard suit slowly tilts his head to the left, pauses, then slowly tilts it to the right, admiring himself in the hand mirror, like someone trying on a hat. The tiara stays in place and the mask keeps its shape. The jeweller holds the mirror steady, mouth closed. Everyone else stays still.
ENDS ON: The man holding his final tilt, no camera move, a clean cut point.
WHY: A vain little head tilt is one small action that gets the joke across without giving the pearls, the mirror or the second man a chance to morph.

=== SHOT mask3 ===
One continuous shot, camera locked. Snow falls and the pink flare burns. At the carriage door, the sack is lowered into the waiting man's hands and he takes its weight. Everyone else holds still, masks unchanged. Near the end, steam from under the locomotive rolls left across the frame until it fills the lens with white.
ENDS ON: Locomotive steam covering the whole frame, cut at full coverage.
WHY: The steam is already in the picture, so it gives the editor a physically motivated wipe that needs no new object.

=== SHOT mask4 ===
One continuous shot from the moving boat, which rides gently up and down on the water while its wake churns alongside. A thin trickle of small chips keeps falling from the tilted table into the river, and the chips keep their shape. The man with the cigar and the crowd at the rail hold still. In the last second, whip pan right along the steamer's dark hull, blurring at the peak.
ENDS ON: A short rightward whip pan, cut at peak blur.
WHY: Earlier falling objects morphed, so the fall is reduced to a thin trickle and the boat's gentle motion carries the life of the shot.

=== SHOT safe4 ===
Inside the rocking car, the only thing that changes is the light. Through the cracked oval rear window, the two headlamps slowly grow brighter and nearer, drifting a little from side to side. The blind's tassel sways with the road. The man stays frozen, his head and gloved hands unmoving, his mask unchanged. One continuous shot. Finally the window burns white and flares across the lens.
ENDS ON: Headlamp glare blooming from the rear window until the lens flares out.
WHY: Telling the chase entirely through light keeps the masked figure and his hands still, and the flare hands the editor a bright exit.

=== SHOT chase2 ===
Locked off on the rooftop, one continuous shot. Far below, the tiny yellow 1920s roadster, with its separate mudguards, running boards and spoked wheels, drives steadily left along the road and out across the junction, a little dust trailing behind it. It stays the same car. The tram, other cars and pedestrians wait where they are. In the last second, the camera pans quickly left until the dark iron leg of the water tank fills the lens.
ENDS ON: The tank's dark iron leg covering the frame, cut at full coverage.
WHY: Last time's slide, pursuit and hurrying crowd are removed so a single tiny car can stay period-correct and hold its shape, with a dark object already in the frame as the way out.
