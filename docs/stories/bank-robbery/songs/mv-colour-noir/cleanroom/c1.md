=== SHOT den1 ===
The camera stays locked at the drummer's shoulder. She sweeps her wire brush in small, slow circles on the snare, moving only her wrist. The singer stands still with his mouth closed. The other players and the crowd hold their places. Smoke drifts slowly above the tables. One continuous shot, no cuts.
ENDS ON: The same framing, the brush still circling; it simply ends.
WHY: The only motion is the small one nearest the lens, so the busy room has nothing to morph.

=== SHOT den4 ===
A locked shot. The furnace fire on the left flickers and its orange light moves softly over the bricks. A thin line of smoke rises from the cigarette in the seated man's still hand. He and the seated musicians stay motionless, mouths closed. In the final second, the camera whips left to the furnace, blurring at the peak. One continuous shot.
ENDS ON: A fast leftward whip pan, at peak blur on the orange furnace light.
WHY: The stillness makes this read as a band waiting, and the fire gives the whip pan a physical place to go.

=== SHOT bridge1 ===
The camera slides slowly and steadily to the left across the boat deck, at an even speed. The near black funnel passes across the frame from left to right, becoming larger, until it covers the whole lens. The band, parked cars, bridge and river keep their exact shape and stay where they are, and the cars do not move. One continuous shot, no cuts.
ENDS ON: The black funnel fully covering the lens.
WHY: A dark object already in the frame makes a realistic wipe, and the sideways parallax gives the wide view its life without asking anything of the small figures.

=== SHOT bridge2 ===
From a low position just above the water, the camera pushes forward very slowly toward the singer in the plum suit, arriving late. He stands still at the microphone, mouth closed, breathing gently. Behind him the two cars stay parked with steady headlamps and the band holds still. Dark water laps at the bottom step. In the final two seconds his jacket fills the frame. One continuous shot, no cuts.
ENDS ON: The dark plum wool of his jacket filling the frame.
WHY: A slow push into dark cloth gives the editor a clean exit and keeps the cars and players out of the action.

=== SHOT bridge3 ===
Fixed camera, symmetrical frame unchanged. The bass player and drummer play with small natural movements, and their huge shadows on the brick vault sway slightly in time with them. Nobody sings; the singer stands still with his mouth closed. Both black cars stay parked. The man in the hat stays still, a thread of smoke rising from his cigarette. Single continuous shot.
ENDS ON: The same symmetrical frame with the shadows still swaying; it simply ends.
WHY: The shadows carry the movement at wall scale, so the picture feels alive while the figures barely have to move.

=== SHOT bridge4 ===
The large car headlamp on the left slowly grows brighter as its filament warms, over the full eight seconds. Its glow spreads across the singer's suit and, by the end, blooms across the whole frame. The singer stays still, eyes closed, lips unmoving, not singing, his hand steady on the microphone. The players behind him and the city lights hold. One continuous shot, no cuts.
ENDS ON: The headlamp's warm glare washing over the frame to near white.
WHY: A single change of light avoids any risk to the close-up face and ends on a physically motivated flare.
