=== SHOT rods1 ===
One unbroken shot, tracking sideways at the roadster's speed across the cracked lakebed. The driver stamps the throttle: the tail squats, the rear tyres bite and throw dirt, and the car pulls away out of frame right, pursued by the yellow truck. The camera, too slow, is left behind in the plume as the dust wall rolls left to right across the lens until the whole frame is pale dust. Engine noise only.
ENDS ON: Full-frame pale dust with no car or horizon visible, ready to cut to the next shot's camera clearing steam, smoke or fog.
WHY: The car's own acceleration makes the dust wall arrive, so the white-out has a physical cause and shows the weight of the car squatting.

=== SHOT rods2 ===
High on the rusted tower at night, camera locked off.
[0-6s] Far below, the yellow and crimson cars race away down the lit strip side by side toward the city, shrinking, their exhaust smoke hanging over the road. The crowd stays by the parked cars, heads turning to follow.
[6-8s] Without warning the camera whips left and down in one violent move until the upright rusted girder fills the frame, smeared with motion blur at the peak of the pan.
No cut.
ENDS ON: Mid-whip, the vertical girder blurred across the full frame, ready to cut on the blur to a matching lamp post, pillar or exhaust stack.
WHY: Holding still for six seconds lets the tiny distant cars read as real speed and scale, so the sudden whip hits harder when it comes.

=== SHOT rods3 ===
The camera is bolted to the back of a car ahead, so the black bumper bar stays fixed in the foreground and everything shudders with the cobbles. The green roadster chases hard down the wet tram tracks, closing the gap the whole time. Its nose pitches over each bump, the scraping chassis throws sparks from the rail, and the driver hauls on the wheel. The right headlamp keeps growing until its white glare blooms over the lens and fills the frame. The left lamp stays dead. Shop signs stay soft and unreadable. Continuous shot, no cuts.
ENDS ON: The live headlamp's round white flare filling the frame, ready to match into a bright round lamp in the next shot.
WHY: A rigid bumper mount makes the shake do the speed and turns the closing gap into the single action, so the flare is simply the car getting too close.

=== SHOT rods4 ===
Camera low and still at water level in the concrete channel. The turquoise roadster charges straight at us through the shallow water, heavy, its tyres ploughing up sheets of spray that fall back with real weight, and sunset reflections break apart in its wake. The yellow car stays up on the sloped wall behind it, tilted, and the men in hats stay on the ledge. The turquoise car swerves just left of the lens and its shadowed flank passes at arm's length, blocking the whole frame in darkness for a moment. All one shot.
ENDS ON: The car's dark flank covering the full frame at arm's length, a real wipe ready to open on the next shot as the car clears.
WHY: A locked camera at water height gives a near miss that only the car moves through, so the wipe comes from the car and not from a camera trick.
