=== SHOT den3 ===
The camera sits level with a round card table in a windowless back room in the 1920s, its lens only a hand's width above billiard-green baize, looking across the table at three men playing cards. A wide green enamel lamp hangs low over the middle of the table with its top cut off by the upper edge of the frame, and it is the main light, dropping straight down in a hard pool that ends just behind the men's shoulders. A short stack of ivory and red clay chips stands large and soft at the lower left, close to the lens. The man in the centre wears a mustard felt fedora and a brown-and-cream houndstooth suit. The man on the left wears an oxblood fedora and a green plaid suit, and the man on the right, cut off by the right edge of the frame at his shoulder, wears a violet fedora and a charcoal chalk-stripe suit. All three are heavy, ordinary, middle-aged, sitting perfectly upright with their forearms on the table's padded rim, each holding a small fan of cards close to his chest. The hat brims put their eyes in shadow and the lamp catches their chins and knuckles. A single cigar rests in a thick glass ashtray in the centre of the table, and one thin line of smoke climbs straight up from it and flattens into a green haze under the lamp. Behind the men, a wall painted deep plum fades into true dark, with one small amber bulb in a brass wall fitting glowing far off to the left. Fine film grain and deep, velvety blacks, with the colour in the hats, the suits, the chips and the cloth. The picture fills the frame edge to edge.
WHY: A level, unposed card game with three visible men and one line of smoke gives the engine nothing to read as staged or sinister, and only the smoke needs to move.

=== SHOT mask4 ===
Dusk on a wide, calm river in the 1920s, seen from low on the water beside a small mahogany runabout lying still at anchor, with its flat upright brass-framed windscreen, its varnished deck planks, a brass stern lamp and an open cockpit of buttoned oxblood leather. The boat fills the left half of the frame, cut off by the left edge and the bottom edge. A man in a cream linen three-piece suit and tan leather gloves sits at ease on the stern deck with his legs crossed and his gloved hands resting on one knee, his head turned to face us. His whole head is in a hand-knitted balaclava of teal wool in a slightly uneven rib, its long neck tucked into his collar, with unbroken knit running across the place where his eyes and mouth would be. A thin cream wool ring is chain-stitched around the face, as wide as the face from brow to chin. High inside the ring, over his left eye and on the right of the picture, pressed against the ring's inner edge, sits one small triangle cut from a single piece of solid red felt, its corners rounded, sewn flat with small stitches around its edge. The middle of the ring is bare teal wool. The small brass lamp beside him warms his chest and the front of the mask, and the last of the sky rims his shoulders from behind. Far behind him on the right, small and quiet, a tall white-and-tomato-red wooden paddle steamer with twin black smokestacks and a big stern wheel lies moored against a dark line of trees, its decks empty, strings of amber bulbs along each rail. Those bulbs fall onto the flat water as long, broken bars of gold, stretched toward the camera and dimmer than the bulbs above. The sky has gone rose and plum with one long bar of apricot at the horizon, and the water between boat and steamer holds that colour, glassy and barely moving. Fine film grain, saturated painted colour, true dark under the boat's hull. The picture fills the whole frame, edge to edge.
WHY: With the crowd, the undressed men, the raised hands, the money and the falling table gone, this is one still figure in a costume on a moored boat, and it can move with nothing more than a slow rock of the hull and the water's shimmer.
