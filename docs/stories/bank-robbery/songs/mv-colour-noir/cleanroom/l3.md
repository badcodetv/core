=== SHOT l05 ===
Medium close-up at eye level on a 50mm lens: a clean-shaven barman of about seventy, creased face, grey hair oiled flat, waistcoat and sleeve garters, three-quarter to camera, head and shoulders filling half the frame. His right hand is out of sight on the hidden button under the bar. Behind him the whole back shelf has flipped on its hinge and unlabelled bottles slide down a dark chute, smashing in bursts of liquor and glass. The tilted bevelled mirror shows the bulb-lit room and not him. A magenta lamp out of frame left lays colour across his cheek and mouth; hard gold bulbs along the brass rail streak across the mirror. Shadows fall to true black.
MOTION: Locked camera. Liquor keeps running down the chute behind him. He says: Biggie said way back in ninety seven. He talks at an unhurried pace and nobody else speaks.
SECONDS: 4
WHY: The barman dumps his stock mid-raid at the press of a button, and the lyric's name is kept exactly as written although it breaks the no-real-names rule, so it needs a human call and may draw a refusal.

=== SHOT l06 ===
A low angle from bench height on a 35mm lens, near-frontal: a bald moonshiner of about sixty-five with white stubble and a burn-scarred, weathered face, collarless shirt and leather apron, chest-up and filling the left half of a brick cellar. Behind his shoulder the copper still has split along its seam, a tall orange fireball standing in the dark, curved sheets of copper and rivets flung through the air. A sheet of blue spirit flame runs along the wet bench at his elbow and throws cold light up onto his mouth and jaw. Furnace orange rims his head from behind. An unbroken polished copper pot beside him mirrors both fires as warped streaks. Everything else falls to true black.
MOTION: Locked camera. The blue flame flickers along the bench beside him. He says: don't get high off your own supply. He talks at an unhurried pace and nobody else speaks.
SECONDS: 4
WHY: A distiller calmly stating the rule while his own still blows up behind him makes the line a joke without any extra staging.

=== SHOT c3 ===
A wide 24mm lens almost on the floor of a raided speakeasy, looking up at a crew of four men aged sixty to seventy-five, clean-shaven or stubbled, shirtsleeves, braces and battered hats, shoulder to shoulder, the two nearest faces huge in the frame, all mouths open and clear, shouting just past the camera. Firemen's axes are held high above their heads, blades up. Behind them a wall of oak barrels stands staved in and split, and amber liquor sheets down across the floor toward the lens. The flooded boards mirror them head to foot. A crimson lamp from the left and an ice-cyan lamp from the right cross on their faces, and the rest of the cellar falls away to black.
MOTION: Locked camera. Liquor keeps running from one split barrel behind them. The men shout together: Debasement! Debasement! They shout at an unhurried pace and nobody else speaks.
SECONDS: 4
WHY: The axe raid on the barrels is destruction of property with no one in danger, so an old crew can bellow the chant with the wrecked stock behind them.

=== SHOT c4 ===
A high angle from halfway up a cellar stair on a 35mm lens: five weathered men of about sixty to seventy, clean-shaven, dark overcoats and fedoras pushed back, packed at the foot of the steps and looking up just past the camera, the front three faces large, mouths open and clear, shouting. Drum-magazine submachine guns hang low at their hips, muzzles toward the side wall. Behind them a huge camouflaged door faced with shelves of hams and plain tins stands swung wide, its polished steel edge mirroring the bulbs. A severed lamp cable rains sparks over rows of glass demijohns, several burst. Sulphur yellow bulbs light their faces from the front; an ultramarine lamp fills the hidden room behind. Deep blacks.
MOTION: Locked camera. Sparks keep falling from the broken cable behind them. The men shout together: It happened in da basement! They shout at an unhurried pace and nobody else speaks.
SECONDS: 4
WHY: The hidden cellar behind its two-and-a-half-ton false wall is a real raid detail and literally is the basement the line is shouting about.
