=== SHOT c3 ===
Wide lens almost on the flooded floor of a raided speakeasy, looking up, ankle-deep spilled liquor mirroring everything. Nearest the lens, face filling nearly half the frame height, a small man of 60 with a sharp hooked nose, hollow cheeks, jutting ears and thin grey hair roars at us three-quarter on, clean-shaven, shirt open at the neck, axe high above his head. Red light from the smashed bar falls full on his mouth; teal glows from the far wall. Further back and smaller: a giant blond Swede of 30, long-boned and pale-browed, axe raised, towering at left; a bald Black man of 50 with grey stubble, axe on his shoulder, at right; deep in the room a chubby baby-faced man of 19, wading, axe up in both hands.
MOTION: Camera creeps slightly closer, single continuous shot. Liquor ripples around their shins; everything else keeps its shape, heads nearly still. Nobody else sings. The men shout together: Debasement! Debasement!
SECONDS: 4
WHY: The tiny old man up front with the giant looming behind makes four impossible-to-merge bodies and leaves one clear mouth for the sync tool.

=== SHOT c4 ===
From a few steps up a narrow cellar staircase, a 28mm lens looks slightly down at a crew gathered at the foot. Closest, his face a good third of the frame, a long-faced Black man of 40 with a high forehead, narrow nose and long chin, clean-shaven, white fedora pushed up, sings up at us, overcoat open, collar flat, revolver hanging low at his thigh. On the right a smashed fuse box showers white-gold sparks that light his mouth; the left sinks into deep blue. Smaller behind him: a square-headed German American of 55, cropped grey hair, duelling scars on one cheek, Thompson aimed at the floor, standing tall on the left; a wiry Greek American of 25 with heavy eyebrows, crouched at right, looking back at the sparks.
MOTION: Camera holds, drifting a touch closer, single continuous shot. Sparks keep falling on the right; bodies stay in place, heads nearly still. Nobody else sings. The men shout together: It happened in da basement!
SECONDS: 4
WHY: Looking down the stairs gives a new angle, and the sparks stay beside him so the danger never crosses his mouth.

=== SHOT c5 ===
Tight and crowded in a low cellar print shop, a 40mm lens at chest height. Front and centre, face filling almost half the frame, a stooped Jewish printer of 70 with wild white hair, deep creases, large ears, a long nose and a few worn teeth, clean-shaven, ink-black apron over a collarless shirt, sings straight at us. A green glass-shaded lamp hanging just above the lens lights his face and mouth in sickly green. Behind him a counterfeit press burns orange, curling blank sheets lifting off it. At his shoulders, further back and smaller: a skinny young Black apprentice of 18, big-eyed, sleeves rolled, beating at flames with a rag; a beefy red-faced Irish American of 45 with a broken nose, hauling on the press lever.
MOTION: Camera stays locked, single continuous shot. Flames flicker on the press behind them; the men and machine keep their shape, heads nearly still. Nobody else sings. The men shout together: Printer is burning, now now what's occurring?
SECONDS: 6
WHY: The green lamp against the orange fire gives this shot its own pair of colours, and the old man's face carries the sync while the others are busy with the fire.

=== SHOT e1 ===
A long 135mm lens at eye level picks out a dapper Cuban American of about 35 in the foreground of a night-time hold-up, his face a third of the frame height, turned three-quarter to us, singing as if ordering coffee. Thin face, long fine nose, narrow chin, small neat ears, clean-shaven, one gold tooth, cream fedora, cream suit, tie loosened and open at the throat. A warm amber headlamp lights his mouth from the side. Behind him, soft with distance, a riveted 1920s armoured truck stands with rear doors hanging open, split canvas sacks on the wet street, unreadable banknotes hanging in the air, teal neon wash over the whole city block. Nobody else near him; two far blurred figures carry sacks.
MOTION: Camera slides slowly sideways, keeping him the same size, single continuous shot. Banknotes drift down behind him; the truck stays whole and still. His head barely moves. Nobody else sings. He sings: stacking up the cash by the grands
SECONDS: 4
WHY: The long lens turns the robbery into soft teal and gold behind him, so the heist can't morph and his calm is the joke.

=== SHOT e2 ===
Looking down from the landing above with a wide 24mm lens tilted off level, onto a tall gaunt Black man of about 45 on a rain-soaked iron fire escape, mid-chase, one long arm flung wide along the railing. His face fills a third of the frame, tipped up to us front-on: sharp jaw, sunken cheeks, narrow nose, small close ears, stubble, soaked homburg with water running off the brim behind his jaw, coat collar flat. The window right beside him is bursting outward, glass hanging in the air, amber room light spilling across his mouth. Magenta neon rain fills everything else, the wet iron stairs zigzagging away to a dark street far below where small torch beams search upward.
MOTION: Camera sinks slightly toward him, single continuous shot. Rain falls straight down in magenta light; the fire escape stays rigid, his head nearly still. Nobody else sings. He sings: standard plans are for the working man
SECONDS: 4
WHY: The steep high angle and vertical drop give a chase feeling that comes from height and rain, with nothing needing to run.

=== SHOT f7 ===
Dead centre and symmetrical, a 50mm lens at chest height in a grand marble bank hall at midnight. A stocky Korean American of about 40 stands square to us, face a good third of the frame: wide square jaw, broad flat cheekbones, thick neck, small ears, short straight nose, clean-shaven, bowler hat dead level, dark suit with the shirt open at the neck. His right hand holds a revolver up beside his ear, muzzle at the ceiling, clear of his face. A work lamp on the floor throws hard cold white up onto his mouth and chin. Far behind him, exactly on his axis, the round vault door hangs blown open, thick smoke rolling from it, one red alarm lamp turning the smoke and marble columns deep red.
MOTION: Camera pushes in very slowly, single continuous shot. Smoke rolls out of the vault behind him; door, columns and man keep their shape, head nearly still. Nobody else sings. He sings: hungry people feed a revolution
SECONDS: 4
WHY: A flat formal frame stands apart from the tilted shots around it and lets the vault door sit behind his head like a halo.

=== SHOT f8 ===
On a tar rooftop at night, a 35mm lens held low and to his left, tilted a few degrees. A young soft-featured Italian American of about 24 sings three-quarter on, his face a third of the frame height: round cheeks, small upturned nose, full lips, a dimple in a smooth chin, wide-set eyes, stubble just starting, hat tipped too far back so his whole face shows, collar open. A Thompson is held up on his far side, muzzle to the sky. Behind him a bullet-riddled wooden water tank on iron legs sprays a dozen thin jets, which catch purple neon from the skyline. A pale yellow bulb on the stair-head to the side lights his mouth clearly. Tar glistens wet around his shoes.
MOTION: Camera arcs a little around him, single continuous shot. Water jets spray steadily from the tank behind him; tank and man keep their shape, head nearly still. Nobody else sings. He sings: then it's retribution, perhaps some execution
SECONDS: 6
WHY: The sweet face against the punctured tank is the contrast the line wants, and the jets give motion that stays well behind his mouth.
