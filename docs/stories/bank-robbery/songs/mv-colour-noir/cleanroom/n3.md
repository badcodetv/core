=== SHOT safe3 ===
Seen from the catwalk of a rooftop water tower, looking steeply down over a corroded iron rail that crosses the lower right corner close to the lens and out of focus, an inland brick district of a 1928 city an hour after sundown, with a sliver of dull violet sky along the top edge of the frame. One wide, straight avenue runs through the picture from the upper right to the lower left, paved in pale dry granite setts the colour of old bone, with a raised kerb and a strip of pavement down each side, iron lamp posts at even intervals throwing round pools of yellow light on the stone, and closed shopfronts under oxblood and bottle-green awnings along its length, so that it shows as the brightest, plainest shape in the picture and can only be a street. Three black sedans stand parked and dark against the kerb. In the middle of the avenue a single tall, upright sedan in egg-yolk yellow, small in the frame but large enough to read its boxy cabin, separate black mudguards, running boards and spoked wheels, points straight down the road toward the lower left with its two round headlamps laying short beams on the setts ahead of it. Behind it a taut chain runs back along the centre of the road to a small dark iron safe lying flat on the stone, and from the point where the iron grinds on the granite one thin bright thread of orange sparks stretches back along the same line for the length of a block, dulling to red as it cools and tinting the setts beneath it. Far up the avenue, two small white headlamps of one following car point the same way along the road. Every other part of the view is rooftop: tar and water tanks and chimney stacks, mostly dark, with a few long skylights glowing amber, one rooftop painted in faded jade, and a row of bulb signs seen from behind so they show only as scaffolding and rows of dots. The whole district is dry land, roofs and paved road from edge to edge, with no river, canal or bridge anywhere in it. A long-lens view with flattened layers of buildings, deep plum shadows, saturated film colour and a fine grain, filling the entire 16:9 frame without any margin.
WHY: The canal and bridge are gone and the road is now a pale, kerbed, lamp-lit street, which leaves nothing dark and wet for a car to appear to drive on, and the car, chain and spark line all sit on one straight axis so the only motion is forward.

=== SHOT safe1 ===
The instant a small safe leaves a wall, seen from across a narrow back alley at night in 1928, the camera at chest height against the opposite wall with a rusted iron drainpipe cut off by the left edge of the frame. An egg-yolk yellow sedan with a tall upright body, separate black mudguards, a running board and spoked wheels stands at a hard angle on the right, its rear wheels spinning on wet-dark cobbles and throwing thick grey tyre smoke, lit deep red by its two small tail lamps. From its rear axle a heavy chain runs dead straight, rigid as a bar, across the alley and into a ragged hole in an oxblood brick wall with a flaking bottle-green painted band. The safe, a squat cast-iron box in chipped forest-green enamel with a brass dial, hangs tilted in the air a foot outside the hole with the chain wrapped around it, while bricks, mortar and pieces of green wall fall around it through a cloud of pale dust. One bare amber bulb in a tin shade above a wooden back door, high on the left, is the only other light, so the dust glows amber on one side and red on the other, and the far end of the alley drops into true black. A man leans far out of the sedan's rear window, big enough in the frame to read clearly, dark overcoat collar turned up, both leather-gloved hands gripping the door. He has twisted round so the front of his head faces the camera almost square on, turned only slightly toward the hole in the wall, and the whole front of his mask is lit by the amber bulb. His whole head is inside a hand-knitted balaclava of marine-blue wool, slightly uneven, with a ribbed neck tucked into the collar and continuous knit running unbroken across the place where eyes, nose and mouth would be. One thin ring of cream wool, chain-stitched by hand, is a wide oval that fills the whole front of his head, reaching from his brow to his chin and from cheek to cheek. Inside that ring, level with his left eye and close against the ring at the two o'clock position as we look at him, sits one small triangle of solid red felt with rounded corners, point downward, its hand-cut edge sewn down with tiny black running stitches. The middle of the ring and everything to the left of the triangle is bare blue knit. Falling bricks are slightly blurred, while the chain, safe and mask are sharp. Rich saturated colour film with visible grain and hard shadows on the brick, the picture running edge to edge across the whole 16:9 frame.
WHY: The scene stays word for word apart from the period wording on the car, while the head now faces the camera and the triangle is anchored to his left eye and a clock position on a much larger ring, which is where it sits on the approved jeweller's-shop mask.
