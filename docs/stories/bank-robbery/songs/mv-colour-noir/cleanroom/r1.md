=== SHOT l01 ===
Night, a long black 1920s sedan at full speed down a city street. A lean Black man of about 35 rides its running board: high cheekbones, narrow clean-shaven face, long neck, dove-grey fedora pushed up off his brow. The lens sits low on the door, wide, looking up at him three-quarter, his face a third of the frame's height, mouth open mid-note. One arm hooks the door pillar; the other holds a drum-fed Thompson straight up beside his head, muzzle at the sky. Bars of magenta shop neon with no lettering streak along the wet black door, right out to the frame's edges. A small cyan lamp on the pillar lights his mouth and jaw plainly.
MOTION: Camera stays locked to the door, same framing. Only the magenta neon moves, streaking along the bodywork beneath him, while his head barely shifts. He sings: don't be hating on the credit rating. Nobody else sings.
SECONDS: 4
WHY: Holding the car still against the lens puts all the speed in the door's reflections, and the gun stays upright and clear of his chin.

=== SHOT l02 ===
A stout Italian American man of about 45 stands in the back of an open 1920s touring car at night, round-faced, heavy-jowled, stubbled, a wide gap between his front teeth showing as he sings, hat knocked to the back of his skull. Long lens from level with his shoulders on the car's far side, flattening everything: he turns three-quarter to us, face over a third of the picture's height. Directly behind him the dark green riveted flank of an armoured money truck, unmarked, fills the frame side to side. Blurred banknotes whirl against it, all behind his shoulders. Strings of amber bulbs overhead melt into soft discs and glint on the wet street below. Warm amber falls across his mouth; green shadow holds his far cheek.
MOTION: Camera rides level with him at matching speed, framing unchanged. The banknotes keep whirling behind his shoulders and never cross his face. He sings: or have resistance to the facts I'm stating. Nobody else sings.
SECONDS: 6
WHY: The long lens makes the truck a looming wall right at his back, and his gap teeth and jowls cannot average into anyone else in the film.

=== SHOT f1 ===
A country road at night, a fake police roadblock. A skinny Irish American man of 22 crouches beside one burst wooden beer barrel, facing us square on: hollow cheeks, pale smooth skin, big jug ears sticking out under a peaked cap too large for him, crooked front teeth, mouth wide. His oversized dark police tunic hangs open at a thin neck, its collar far below his chin. The lens lies at road height, in the foam. Beer spreads toward us in a shining sheet that runs off every side of the picture. A red road lantern at his knee lights his face from beneath, mouth and chin clear. Far behind him, a stopped 1920s flat-bed truck, its load tarped, sits small in cold blue moonlight.
MOTION: Camera stays dead still at road level. Beer creeps slowly across the road toward the lens; barrel, truck and lantern hold their shape and place. He sings: crime is asset inflation. Nobody else sings.
SECONDS: 4
WHY: The road-level frontal angle gives the mouth tool a clean face with the spreading beer as the single moving thing and only one barrel to keep intact.

=== SHOT f2 ===
Rain-wet highway at night, a bootleg convoy on the move. We look in from outside a moving 1920s sedan, through its open front side window, at a Chinese American man of about 50: thin face, sunken cheeks, sharp chin, clean-shaven, round wire glasses, homburg, three-piece suit, sitting very upright and turned three-quarter toward us. A normal lens, eye level, the dark window frame edging the picture on all sides and his face a good third of its height. A chrome wing mirror low in the corner, well clear of his chin, holds two hard white headlights from the truck behind. A pale gold dash lamp lights his mouth from below left. Past him, the far window shows cobalt night smeared sideways.
MOTION: Camera moves with the sedan at matching speed, holding him in the window. Cobalt night smears past the far window behind him; the headlights hold in the mirror. He sings: passive creation of a wealth gap. Nobody else sings.
SECONDS: 4
WHY: His calm, neat stillness inside a frame-within-a-frame is the joke, and the danger stays small and bright in the mirror beside him.

=== SHOT e3 ===
Dead-on, symmetrical, from a camera bolted to the bonnet of an open 1920s roadster at a level crossing at night, windscreen folded flat. A huge bull-necked Polish American man of about 40 fills the driver's seat, his shoulders running to both edges of the picture: wide square head, flat broken nose, one cauliflower ear, heavy stubble, flat cap, chipped teeth, mouth open. His face takes a third of the frame's height. The steering wheel sits low, below his chest. A smashed striped crossing gate lies across the bonnet at the bottom of the shot, beneath his chin line. Hard pink signal light strikes his face and mouth from the front. Behind him a black locomotive bulk, white steam and orange sparks.
MOTION: Camera sits fixed on the bonnet with a faint engine tremble. Steam rolls slowly behind him, a few sparks in it; the gate stays where it lies. He sings: and this right here is a next level scam. Nobody else sings.
SECONDS: 6
WHY: A flat head-on frame is the mouth tool's easiest face, and his size against the wreck and the locomotive makes him seem unbothered by all of it.

Further still, a wiry Mexican American man of 20 crouches on a 1920s sedan's bonnet, head thrown back. That sedan burns against a lamp post in orange flame, under a blank bulb-edged theatre marquee whose teal light falls on the bald man's face.
MOTION: Camera creeps in very slightly. Flames flicker on the burning car behind them while each man keeps his own place and pose. The men shout together: Robbery, robbery! Nobody else sings.
SECONDS: 4
WHY: Three heights at three depths let the cut land on one clear front face, while the group reads as nothing like the same man repeated.
