=== SHOT o1 ===
Night, 1920s America, a wide empty street of wet black cobbles. Seen straight on from low down, a huge rooftop sign on a riveted steel frame fills the upper frame: a black porcelain-enamel board carrying the single word badcode. The letters follow the reference image exactly and nothing else is written anywhere. Each letter is a cream-enamelled sheet-metal trough packed with warm amber bulbs; the solid disc is glossy red enamel washed by gooseneck lamps, the only red in the picture. An argon-blue glass tube edges the board. A lamp box waits behind it. The cobbles mirror everything in long streaks. Thin haze, true black shadow, full frame edge to edge.
MOTION: Single continuous shot, locked camera. Haze drifts slowly across the sign. The reflections on the wet cobbles shimmer. The letters stay exactly as in the first frame. Nothing else moves. No speech.
SECONDS: 8
WHY: The name gets one long calm look, whole and lit, so the audience knows exactly what is about to be destroyed.

=== SHOT o2 ===
Night, 1920s American street of wet cobbles, camera at knee height, directly behind three men standing in a spaced row, seen only from behind as black shapes in fedoras and long overcoats, faces unseen, each holding a drum-fed submachine gun low at his hip. Amber headlamps of a parked dark sedan, out of frame behind the camera, rim their shoulders and throw three long shadows forward. Far beyond them, soft and high, the black sign with the single word badcode glows amber, its letters following the reference image exactly, its blue tube bright; nothing else is written anywhere. Fine rain lit from behind. Full frame edge to edge, deep shadow.
MOTION: Single continuous shot, locked camera. The three men raise their guns together toward the sign and hold still. Rain falls through the headlamp beams. The sign stays exactly as in the first frame. No speech.
SECONDS: 4
WHY: Three shapes with their backs to us turn the empty street into a firing line without giving the shot a face or a voice.

=== SHOT o3 ===
Night, wet 1920s street. Camera locked directly behind one man's shoulder: his fedora and overcoat are a black, out-of-focus mass on the left, a drum-fed submachine gun with a finned barrel raised and aimed up at the sign, away from camera. White-orange flame jets upward from the muzzle, lighting the rain and the brim of his hat. Sharp behind him, straight on, the rooftop sign with the single word badcode, letters following the reference image exactly, every amber bulb lit, the red disc glossy, the blue tube bright. Nothing else is written anywhere. Haze hangs in the light. Saturated amber against blue, shadows true black, full frame edge to edge.
MOTION: Single continuous shot, locked camera. He holds still and fires one steady burst; flame stutters at the muzzle and brass casings spray to the right. The sign stays intact and unchanged. Gunfire only, no speech.
SECONDS: 4
WHY: The first burst lands on an unharmed sign, holding the damage back for one more beat.

=== SHOT o4 ===
Night, extreme close view, locked and front-on, of one straight cream-enamelled sheet-metal stem of the sign, a trough packed with a dense double row of glowing amber bulbs, running top to bottom through the frame against glossy black enamel. It is only a part of the reference letters and follows them exactly; no complete word shows and nothing is written anywhere. A blurred thread of argon-blue tube glows at the frame's edge. Thin haze curls past, lit amber. Beads of rain sit on the bulbs and on the gloss, each holding a tiny point of light. Full frame edge to edge, deep black around the glow.
MOTION: Single continuous shot, locked camera, slow motion. From top to bottom, bulbs burst one after another, each in a puff of glass and a small fan of white-orange sparks, then goes dark. The trough keeps its shape. No speech.
SECONDS: 4
WHY: Letting the letter die bulb by bulb in close-up makes the destruction plain and legible as progress.

=== SHOT o5 ===
Night, close and straight on: the solid red porcelain-enamel disc of the sign fills most of the frame, glossy as wet paint, washed by a gooseneck lamp from above, the only red in the picture. At its edges, parts of the neighbouring cream letter troughs follow the reference letters exactly, their amber bulbs still burning; nothing is written anywhere. The black board around it mirrors the bulbs as rows of warm points, with a hard line of argon-blue tube light along the bottom edge. Drifting haze and fine rain catch the glow. Shadows fall to true black. Full frame edge to edge.
MOTION: Single continuous shot, locked camera, slow motion. Six hits strike the disc one at a time. At each, a ring of red enamel chips away, showing dark iron around a clean hole, with a fan of sparks. The disc keeps its round shape. No speech.
SECONDS: 4
WHY: The red disc is the heart of the name, so its wounds get their own slow, beautiful moment.

=== SHOT o6 ===
Night, wide and straight on, the whole rooftop sign with the single word badcode, the letters following the reference image exactly, nothing else written anywhere. Half its amber bulbs are dead, and the unlit troughs still read as pale cream enamel. A level line of bright bullet holes crosses the black board at one height, each pouring a thin white beam from the lamp box behind into the haze. The red disc is cratered, ringed with dark iron. The blue tube is still lit. Below, wet cobbles mirror it all in long broken streaks. Saturated amber, blue and red in deep black, full frame edge to edge.
MOTION: Single continuous shot, locked camera. One sweep of hits runs left to right along that same line, sparks falling in a curtain. As it passes, the blue tube breaks, glass drops, and it goes dark. Letter shapes stay unchanged. No speech.
SECONDS: 4
WHY: A single level sweep along the board gives the barrage the shape of a line being crossed out without losing the word.

=== SHOT o7 ===
Night, straight on, the rooftop sign after the firing has stopped, huge and still above the wet cobbles. The single word badcode remains readable, its letters following the reference image exactly and nothing else written anywhere: dead cream troughs of broken bulb sockets, the red disc pitted with dark iron craters, the black board riddled with hundreds of holes. Every bulb and lamp is dead except one lamp behind the board, the one light left, sending thin white beams through every hole into slow smoke. The beams' soft glow keeps the letters and the red disc just visible. The street mirrors the points of light. True black all around, full frame edge to edge.
MOTION: Single continuous shot, locked camera. Smoke drifts very slowly through the still beams of light. The last lamp flickers once and steadies. Every letter and hole stays exactly as in the first frame. Silence, no speech.
SECONDS: 8
WHY: The film ends on the name wrecked but still legible, with one light refusing to go out as the guitar fades.
