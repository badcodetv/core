=== SHOT pov1 ===
One continuous first-person shot from the driver's eyes, fast, no cuts. [0-3s] The heavy open car charges down the cobbles, the bonnet shaking; barrels bounce towards us off the truck ahead and burst on the stones, sparks kicking up. [3-6s] Both gloved hands haul the wheel left, the whole car leaning and lurching on its springs as the biggest barrel slides past the right side of the windscreen. [6-8s] The swerve carries us at the left-hand green iron girder, which rushes up and passes right against the lens, filling the frame with black. Signs stay unreadable.
ENDS ON: The green girder crosses the lens and the frame is fully black; cut inside that dark.
WHY: A swerve around the barrel is the one action, and it physically causes the girder wipe, so the transition is earned by the driving.

=== SHOT pov2 ===
The camera stays perfectly still at the driver's eye for six seconds, looking out of the side window of the idling car, which trembles slightly with the engine. Behind the policeman the theatre crowd mills and laughs, and the marquee bulbs chase. He stands in profile, mouth closed on his cigarette, smoke drifting. The red glow on his cheek turns green. The glove on the door tightens. Then, in the last two seconds, the camera whips hard to the right past his profile and across the dark window pillar, smearing everything into streaks of light. Nobody speaks. Lettering stays unreadable. A single unbroken shot.
ENDS ON: The whip pan to the right at peak blur, streaks of light crossing the dark pillar; cut at the peak.
WHY: Six seconds of held nerve make the sudden whip feel like the driver's eyes snapping away, giving a fast film one tense breath.

=== SHOT pov3 ===
The car is already in the air, having left the raised bridge deck, and for a moment it hangs weightless above the green river, the wind flicking the driver's cuffs. The engine's weight then wins: the long cream bonnet tips slowly downward, the horizon and skyline climb out of the top of the frame, and the far tilted deck of the drawbridge swells towards us. The gloved hands stay locked on the wheel. Far below, the barge keeps moving and the water stays calm. The dark planks fill the whole view. On impact the car slams down and the view jolts once, hard. Filmed as one shot.
ENDS ON: Dark deck fills the frame and the car slams onto it with one hard jolt; cut on the impact, matching a jolt at the start of the next shot.
WHY: Told as gravity acting on a heavy car, so the fall has weight and the camera move comes from the nose dropping rather than from a floating camera.

=== SHOT pov4 ===
Looking backward over the rear seat of the fleeing open car, the camera bouncing with the cobbles, the alley shrinks away behind us. The driver in the tweed cap keeps glaring back, her glove clamped on the leather. The black car behind accelerates through the spilled lemons, scattering them, its nose dipping and rising on its suspension, the torn shirt flapping on its radiator. The woman in the apron shakes her fists. Laundry swings in our wake. The big crimson sheet overhead sags, then drops, slowly at first, and spreads over the lens until red cloth covers everything. Single shot, faces stay the same, no words appear.
ENDS ON: The crimson sheet covers the lens completely, a full frame of red cloth with light through the weave; cut there onto a red shape or light.
WHY: Every moving layer is pushed back by the car's speed, so the frame reads as a real chase and the falling sheet arrives as the last thing it knocks loose.
