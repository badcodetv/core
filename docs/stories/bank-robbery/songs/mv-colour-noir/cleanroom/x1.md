=== SHOT n01 ===
Night on the teak afterdeck of a private yacht, 1920s, seen from knee height through a wide lens. A heavy, jolly white banker of 60, clean-shaven, round red cheeks, double chin resting above an open wing collar, white dinner jacket, stands three-quarter to us with his face taking up a third of the frame, mouth open on a big happy note. Far behind him over black water a wooden motor launch burns, small, its fire doubled on the swell. A sea-green deck lantern lights his face and mouth; the fire rims his shoulder orange. Full frame, no bars.
MOTION: Camera creeps slowly in from knee height. The distant launch burns; everything else holds. His head barely moves. He sings: They say, ya can't make money from a tree. Nobody else sings.
SECONDS: 4
WHY: The contented rich man sings the opening lie with someone else's boat on fire behind him, and the distance keeps the fire safe from warping.

=== SHOT n02 ===

Directly behind him, compressed and out of focus, a ladle pours a molten stream into the pit, blazing orange, with five small men in leather aprons silhouetted beside it. A cold steel-cyan arc lamp hits his face from the front, clean across the mouth. Full frame, no bars.
MOTION: Camera holds still on the long lens. Behind him the molten stream keeps pouring; nothing else moves. His head stays almost still. He sings: work and get paid though, earning that dough. Nobody else sings.
SECONDS: 4
WHY: Telephoto stacks the pour directly on his shoulders, so labour and the man who does it share one flat wall of colour.
