## What it is
Politicians and media people rob a bank by carrying money *into* it, arguing left against right about
immigration the whole way while holding one end of the same pallet each. The only person who notices is
the cleaner, Denise, because she counts. The point: printing money is a robbery nobody breaks in for, and
the argument is the distraction. Two parts: a narrated story film and a music video. They share the
lyrics' subject and nothing else: **don't carry one part's look or sound into the other.**

## Story film

**Look — LOCKED ("The bank in the haze").** A heist film shot like the news at dusk in a declining
English town. Each place has two or three named saturated colours and two lights of different colours,
named with where the shadow falls. Red and blue arrive by accident (flare smoke, flags, scarves, ties),
never as cards. The camera is always at a named physical place (floor, table top, ceiling corner, inside
an object), with something out of focus at one edge. Prompt shape: feature-film frame on 35mm with a lens
and a place → the out-of-focus foreground → each person's position and face → what is behind them "and
nothing else" → the two lights → the colours. With Characters attached it opens `The named people are the
attached Characters, with their own faces and clothes.` and closes `Visible 35mm film grain. Nobody looks
at the camera. 16:9. Thanks.` Cast: thirteen Flow Characters (ids in [look.md](./look.md#cast)), Barry
`491aad1f…` for the short. Stills NB2 16:9; clips Omni 1.2 Flash, 720p, 8 s, camera locked.

**Sound — narrator LOCKED for now:** AI Studio `Sadaltager`, deadpan Walthamstow note (verbatim in
[sound.md](./sound.md#narration--voices)). Open: several narrator voices, one per character, no lip-sync
(how many and which not decided). Music: six HoliznaCC0 lo-fi tunes (CC0), played sincere; the jokes are
the narrator's. Unclear: whether the short's music tracks are the same lo-fi set.
