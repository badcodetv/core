## Why a plain portrait and not a still in the film's look

A Character's portrait is an identity anchor, not a shot. The repo's rule
([`consistency.md`](../../../flow/consistency.md) §2) and Google's own advice (neutral pose, simple
background, light that keeps detail visible: [veobulk](https://veobulk.com/en/blog/consistent-characters-google-flow),
search summary only) agree: one person, plain background, face fully lit. The dusk-and-smoke look
comes back when the Character is cast into a scene. The recipe is the one that worked for Money
For Something on 3 October.

**Against the plastic look:** unretouched, pores and lines, slight asymmetry, an expression written
as muscles and not as a feeling, and clothes that have been worn.

## The prompt (one template, three clauses change)

```text
A character reference portrait of {WHO}, head and shoulders, body turned slightly to one side and eyes on the camera, {FACE}. {WEARS}. Background: a plain dark grey studio backdrop, empty. Lighting: one large soft light from high on the left and a little fill from the right, so the whole face is clearly seen with a gentle shadow on the far cheek. Capture: a real photograph on an 85mm portrait lens, sharp focus on the eyes, unretouched, natural skin texture with pores, lines and slight asymmetry, true colours. Constraints: alone, hands out of frame, no props, no text, logos, captions or watermarks. Compose for a 3:4 portrait frame. Thanks.
```

### The bodies

One full-length image each, made from the picked portrait as a reference (`flow_edit_image`,
Nano Banana 2, 9:16, one candidate, 768×1376). The template, with the stance and clothes clause
changing per person:

```text
Using the provided portrait, make a full-length character reference photograph of this same {man|woman} standing, seen from head to toe with {his|her} whole body and both feet inside the frame and a little space above {his|her} head and below {his|her} feet. Keep {his|her} face, hair, age and build exactly as they are. {BUILD AND STANCE}. {CLOTHES AND SHOES}. Background: a plain mid-grey studio backdrop and floor, empty. Lighting: large soft even light from the front left, with a soft shadow on the floor behind {him|her}. Capture: a real photograph on a 50mm lens at chest height, sharp from head to shoes, natural fabric texture, true colours. Constraints: alone, no furniture, no text, logos, captions or watermarks. Compose for a 9:16 vertical frame. Thanks.
```

Things the bodies added that were not in the portraits: The Proprietor leans on a black walking
stick; Mr Turquoise has tan shoes and open arms; The Platform is in black jeans and white trainers;
Mr Blue stands with his arms folded; Mr Red has one hand raised to make a point.

### The Governor's face was changed a third time

Flow **refused the second Governor portrait as an upload, twice** (the picker never showed the
file; every other portrait uploaded first time). It was the face flagged as a possible lookalike
of a television actor. Cause not proven, but a refused upload of a photoreal face is a known Flow
behaviour. He was remade with a different face (bald crown, white hair at the sides, white
moustache, round face), which uploaded at once. The refused pair is in `superseded\`.
