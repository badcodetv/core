# Denise — the one who counts

**Working name. First pass, 6 October 2026, not ruled.**

She is the reader's seat in the story ([`the-reader.md`](../../../marketing/the-reader.md), rule 1:
hero, not pupil). Nobody explains anything to her. She works it out with the one skill both her
jobs gave her: she counts. Tips, change, floor tiles, the bags going past.

**What she wants:** the flat above the café. She has been saving the deposit in a passbook for
years, and the number in it is the costed object of the whole story
([`story.md`](../story.md), "The one object").

**The Killing Zoe borrow:** in that film, Zoe turns out to work in the bank being robbed. Denise
is the woman the crew did not tip at breakfast, and she is in the building that night.

**Rules for writing her:**

- She is never the butt of a joke, and she is never fooled. The crew is.
- She is not on a side. Mr Red and Mr Blue both try to claim her; she belongs to neither.
- She does not deny that her street has changed. She says how big that is, next to the vault
  (rule 4: proportionality, never denial).
- She gets the line for the pub: **"One bill. You were all at the same table."**
- She does not make a speech. The bill is the speech.

**Two jobs is the point, not a detail.** She is at the first scene and the last because she is
always at work.
