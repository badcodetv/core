# The Bank Robbery

> **Source of truth** for the Bank Robbery story. Started 6 October 2026 with the `new-story`
> and `story-craft` skills, graduating the inbox seed
> [`docs/ideas/bank-robbery.md`](../../ideas/bank-robbery.md). **As of 12 October 2026 the film is at cut 10,
> and Jack is now cutting it himself. Kai went through it with Jack on the 15 Oct 2026 call: the rulings and
> Jack's to-do list are the last section of [`assembly.md`](./assembly.md).**

> **First release, 22 October 2026 (Kai):** The Bank Robbery is one of the four stories released
> first (with Camping, Karen and GitPush Origin Master): a film and a song on YouTube. See
> [`ep1.md`](../gitpush-origin-master/ep1.md).

Eleven politicians and media people rob a bank by carrying money into it. Left and right argue
about immigration the whole way through, holding one end of the same pallet each. The only person
who notices is the cleaner, because she counts.

A heist comedy in three borrowed shapes: **Reservoir Dogs** (the breakfast, the colours, the
standoff), **Ocean's Eleven** (the recruiting, the plan, the fountain) and **Killing Zoe** (the
night before, the chaos, the woman who works in the bank).

## Before anything is made

1. **Jack and Kai go through the storyboard** scene by scene.
2. **Rule on the nine open calls** in [`story.md`](./story.md). The first one matters most: does
   this story agree with The Magic Money Tree about whether making money is the problem?
3. **Check the figures marked unverified** in [`research.md`](./research.md), and run the Bank
   of England's inflation calculator by hand before any "a pound buys less" number is used.
4. **Then** shot design (`shot-craft`), one prompt at a time.
