## What was and was not found

- **No openly licensed photograph of empty dinghies on a Kent beach or in the Dover storage
  compound was found.** The well-known compound and drone pictures are PA / Getty (not usable).
  Wikimedia Commons has no such file (its "English Channel migrant crossings" category holds three
  charts; the ICIBI Tug Haven / Western Jet Foil set is OGL but shows buildings, not boats).
  Geograph's search returned nothing relevant. NCA press images show boats boxed in a lorry and the
  licence on that site was not established (unverified, not pursued).
- **The only genuine Channel boat found under a usable licence** is one UK Home Office photograph
  at Dover (candidate 3) — it has a minister and officers in frame, so it is supplied with a crop.
- **The best pictures are from Kos, Greece, September 2015** (candidates 1, 2, 4): the same
  black/grey rubber dinghies, on a shingle beach and in a harbour. In newsprint halftone they read
  as "abandoned dinghies on a beach". They are **not** the English Channel — fine on a prop
  newspaper in a satire, but do not caption them as Dover or Kent anywhere that claims to be fact.

## Notes for whoever uses one

- **Credit goes on screen**, in the film's credits, as the string above, and the Flickr/Commons
  URL goes in the video description. CC BY requires saying the picture was changed — the
  "cropped and converted to black and white" clause does that; amend it if the treatment differs.
- CC BY does not let us imply the photographer or the Home Office endorses the film. A credit line
  is fine; "courtesy of" is not.
- The two `…_CROP-no-people.jpg` files were made here with ffmpeg from the originals alongside
  them; they are our adaptations, not source files.
- Rejected on sight: Freedom House's Flickr "Public Domain Mark" refugee pictures (they are agency
  photographs of people in distress, and the mark is not a licence from the photographer); the
  other Home Office frames (ministers in every one); Geograph and most of Commons (nothing on
  subject); `adamansel52`'s Therma boats (a ship's lifeboat full of life jackets).
