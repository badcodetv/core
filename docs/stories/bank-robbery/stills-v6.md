# The Bank Robbery — sixth-pass shots (8 October 2026, late)

**Asked for by Jack, 8 October 2026,** after cut 4. His words are quoted in
[`script.md`](./script.md), "Jack's notes on cut 4". In short: the old eating-and-pushing clip back; a montage that
is not only men with lettering; every model-town shot out; the café open at the start, so the shutter at the end
closes a circle; a fast proud-to-be-British opening with a full English, pints, football and real war footage;
Denise cleaning round the robbers with bleach and a rag; more on left and right, and the politicians laughing;
and "more david lynch interesting angle stuff and cinematically pretty shots".

  protesters; `br5-room` (the window still) for the laughing shot.
- **Research:** this afternoon's sweep, [`docs/google-flow/2026-10-08-anti-slop-sweep.md`](../../google-flow/2026-10-08-anti-slop-sweep.md),
  was used again and not repeated. From it, for these shots: lead with the capture, one positioned light, ordinary
  subjects with dirt and wear, humble gear, no golden hour, fire and steam small and leaning one way, a crowd as
  one sharp anchor with the rest soft.

## The design of each shot

| Shot | Job | Camera | Light | The detail that could only be here |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `s00a-cafe-open` | The café alive, so its shutter means something at the end | Knee height across the road, as the SOLD shot | Its own gold light on a wet grey dawn | A builder leaving with a bacon roll; the flat's lit window and pot plant |
| `s00b-full-english` | The thing itself, put down in front of you | Macro, level with the plate rim | Window behind, backlit steam | The hand with a tea-towel still on the plate |
| `s00c-tea`, `s00d-fry` | The café at work | Mug height; the edge of the grill | Window behind; one bulb under the hood | The crusted sugar pourer; a burn scar |
| `s00e-pint` | A proper pint | On the bar, looking up past the drip tray | One frosted pub window through the beer | Pork scratchings, a flat cap on a peg |
| `s00f-football` | Park football, not the Premier League | In the mud at the touchline, tilted | Flat rain light | A leather ball, a man with a dog, terraces behind |
| `s00g-flags`, `s00h-chippy` | Bunting; fish and chips on the front | On the road looking up; close on a railing | Grey sky | A white van on the kerb; vinegar drops in the air |
| `s05h-table` | The plan, with nothing on the table | On the table top, looking along it | One low bulb, shadows up the wall | A mug and a packet of biscuits where the model was |
| `s10e-bleach` | Denise cleans round a robber | Lying on the counter, past a green desk lamp | That lamp only | Yellow rubber gloves; the Fixer eating with his mask pushed up |
| `s08f-argue` | Two men with one face, filming each other while the money goes by | 85mm at the barrier, the gap between their noses | One flare, off left | The pallet, soft, exactly in the gap |
| `s08g-laugh` | The two politicians, off duty, laughing at the street | Locked off, low, the same dark room | The riot's glow through the window | Their two megaphones dumped by the lamp |

**On the reader rule.** `s08f-argue` gives both men the same weathered face and the same anger on purpose; the
joke in the frame is the pallet, not them. The laughing is given to Mr Blue and Mr Red. See the 🔴 note in
`script.md`.
