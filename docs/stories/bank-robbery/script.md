## The register (Jack, 7 October 2026)

Jack, on draft 1: the script "needs to be as brutal and as funny as possible, a social commentary
on how the right and left are squabbling over the distractions that the puppet masters that are
the rich and powerful / elite cause them on purpose so they can take all the money and power. I
need it to sound like Frankie Boyle vs the world, or Nish Kumar in the Mash Report, a mixture of
dark and brutal, yet silly and ridiculous."

How that is being done. The first three are my reading of the two comedians, not something a
source confirmed (the web search found reviews and no transcripts):

- **The narrator is the Boyle voice.** Flat, certain, and cruel in complete sentences. He talks
  from the first frame now, not from scene 2. The joke shape is a plain statement, then a picture
  that is worse than you expected.
- **The Kumar note is disbelief.** Once or twice a scene the narrator stops being flat and cannot
  believe what he is looking at. Rationed, or it turns into shouting.
- **Brutal and silly in the same line.** The dark half is true and the daft half is small and
  specific (a racehorse, a mint, a sausage).
- **From the repo's own comedy research**
  ([brief 10](../../../design/research/2026-08-22-story-craft/10-comedy-craft-and-satirical-news.md)):
  fix the point first and then run it through a filter (hyperbole, misplaced focus, analogy);
  rule of three with the surprise third; the bigger the build, the harder the drop; and aim at
  the structure, never at a party's voters.

**Who the brutality may land on:** the eleven, every time, left and right to the same depth.
**Never** Denise, either crowd, or anyone who is not in a suit. That is the existing rule 1 in
`story.md`, and it matters more now the jokes are harder.

**Kept from the story rules, on purpose:** no real living person named or drawn; the row still
has no content; nothing is claimed about immigrants so it can be knocked down; Denise keeps the
one straight beat in the film.
