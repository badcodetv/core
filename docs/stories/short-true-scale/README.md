## The idea

Source: an Instagram reel Jack sent on 2026-11-16 (https://www.instagram.com/reels/Deo9IgphLIw/; not ours, nothing from it is used). Three people
talk through US numbers: about 600,000 homeless people against 16 million unoccupied homes ("We could solve the homeless crisis
tomorrow"), and the reply people give: "Well, who owns those homes?" ("If your response is, 'But who owns those places?' It tells
me everything I need to know.")

Kai, 2026-11-16: "fuck me I mean that is 100% a short that links to camping. I can imagine a video where it's like here is the
true scale of GodZilla and they put him next to Empire State. here is the true scale of homelessness and we do a three.js render
of 600k people, when then zoom out and side by side a three.js render of 16million homes."

Jack, 2026-11-16: "we should make a short but talk about the style and script/story beats." Picked: **England's numbers, by
household**, and the **night, fluorescent-teal look of The Malevolent Force**.

**Point:** There are far more empty homes than homeless households; homelessness is a decision about who those homes are for,
not a shortage. It links to Camping.

## The numbers (checked on primary sources 2026-11-16: [`numbers.md`](./numbers.md); the list below is the first search summary)
- 134,210 households in temporary accommodation, England, 31 Dec 2025 (gov.uk, Statutory homelessness Oct-Dec 2025).
- 382,618 people homeless on a given night, England, 2025 (Shelter).
- 303,185 long-term empty homes, England, 2025; 1,022,158 empty in all (Action on Empty Homes, from council tax data).
- 4,793 people sleeping rough on a single night, autumn 2025 (official snapshot).
**Checked:** 134,210 households in TA (gov.uk) and 303,185 long-term empty / 754,264 all empty (MHCLG) are official. **Not safe:** "1 million empty" (AoEH adds second homes), and 382,618 people beside homes. Not to say: the US figures.
