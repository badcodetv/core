# Audio Profile
A pub landlady in her fifties running the Thursday quiz. Warm, low, unforced voice; dry humour under it. London accent, softly spoken. She is not a performer: she reads into a cheap mic the way you'd talk to friends across a small room, relaxed, a little amused, never shouting, never sing-song. Pace: keeps it moving at an easy conversational speed, short natural pauses only, one small beat before the name.

## Scene:
A small, busy local pub on quiz night. She is behind the bar with the mic held loosely, reading from a sheet.

## Sample Context:
She has run this quiz for years. Understated and natural; the drama is in the room, not in her voice.
