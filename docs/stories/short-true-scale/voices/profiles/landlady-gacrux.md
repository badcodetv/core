# Audio Profile
A landlady in her sixties running her pub's quiz night. Big, warm, smoky voice, a natural performer with a cheap microphone. Working-class Midlands accent. Loves the drama of the tiebreaker. Pace: brisk and punchy, words tumbling out fast like a host who wants last orders, no long pauses, only a tiny beat before a name.

## Scene:
A packed local pub on quiz night, carpet, fruit machine, meat raffle on the bar. She is on the mic behind the bar.

## Sample Context:
She has run this quiz every Thursday for twenty years. She reads answers like a game-show host and has never once been surprised, until now.
