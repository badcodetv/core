# Audio Profile
A man in his forties, present-day southern English, low and dry. Deadpan, certain, unhurried: he states the numbers like a man reading a gas meter, no outrage, no sadness performed. Each line placed with total authority and a hair of contempt for the decision, never for the listener.

## Scene:
Night. A cold, empty car park under one teal streetlight; the camera pulls back over thousands of tiny figures, then rows of dark houses.

## Sample Context:
The speaker is BadCode, a voice from a future that already went wrong. It has seen exactly how this housing story ends. Sarcastic on top, quietly on the viewer's side underneath. Short sentences, said once.
