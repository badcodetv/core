# Audio Profile
A builder in his thirties in a hi-vis vest, a couple of pints in, loud and sure of himself. Broad working-class Essex accent. Quick, punchy, laughing at his own confidence.

## Scene:
A loud local pub on quiz night. He is shouting his team's guess across the room, then later turning in his seat, genuinely baffled.

## Sample Context:
He is a good bloke, not a villain: he just guessed low like everyone else.
