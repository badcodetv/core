# True Scale: England numbers, checked 2026-11-16

Marks: (primary) = I fetched the page itself; (read) = search summary only; (unverified) = not seen.

## 1. Households in temporary accommodation
- **134,210 households** on **31 Dec 2025** (primary). Snapshot of the last day of the quarter. Counts HOUSEHOLDS, not people. Also on that page: 85,800 households with children; 176,130 dependent children.
- Released 30 Apr 2026. Up 5.0% on a year earlier, down 0.4% on the previous quarter (read, search summary).
- URL: https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/statutory-homelessness-in-england-october-to-december-2025/statutory-homelessness-in-england-october-to-december-2025
- Safe wording: "134,210 households in temporary accommodation in England, 31 Dec 2025."
- A newer quarter (Jan-Mar 2026, due ~July 2026) may exist by now; I did not look. Check before publishing.

## 2. People homeless on a given night (Shelter)
- **At least 382,618 people**, "on a given night in 2025", about 1 in 153; 175,025 are children (primary, Shelter press release 11 Dec 2025).
- It is Shelter's ESTIMATE, not an official count. Built from MHCLG data plus FOI replies: 350,480 in council temporary accommodation, about 16,300 in hostels, 7,146 "homeless at home", 4,667 rough sleeping (the 2024 count), 4,031 in social-services accommodation. Sofa-surfing is NOT included.
- No official total of homeless people exists (the gov.uk page says its figures are not suitable for that).
- URL: https://england.shelter.org.uk/media/press_release/at_least_382000_people_are_homeless_in_england_today_
- Safe wording: "Shelter estimates at least 382,618 people homeless in England on a given night, 2025."

## 3. Empty homes (MHCLG council tax data, snapshot 6 Oct 2025)
- **303,185 long-term vacant** dwellings, up from 264,884 (7 Oct 2024). (primary: figures on the gov.uk release below.)
- **754,264 vacant dwellings**, up from 719,470 (primary, same page). This is ALL vacants: "reported as empty (unoccupied and unfurnished) for council tax", and it includes properties with a council tax exemption (the page says so). That means short-term empties are in it, for example between tenants or sales.
- **1,022,158 = 754,264 + 267,894 second homes** (arithmetic checks; primary for the AoEH page, read for the split). It is Action on Empty Homes' total, NOT an MHCLG figure. The ~1 million therefore includes second homes (267,894), which are furnished and used part of the year. Do not call them "empty".
- Long-term definition: the usual one is empty more than 6 months. I could NOT confirm that on the gov.uk pages; the technical notes don't define it. It comes from search summaries and the Table 615 notes, which I did not open (unverified). I also did not open the Table 615 spreadsheet.
- URLs: MHCLG https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/dwelling-stock-estimates-in-england-2025/dwelling-stock-estimates-england-31-march-2025 ; table page https://www.gov.uk/government/statistical-data-sets/live-tables-on-dwelling-stock-including-vacants ; AoEH https://www.actiononemptyhomes.org/the-last-decade
- Safe wording: "303,185 homes in England have been empty long-term (council tax data, Oct 2025)." or "754,264 homes empty in England, Oct 2025."

## 4. Rough sleeping snapshot
- **4,793 people** on a single night in autumn 2025, a record, up 3% (primary). Each council picks one night between 1 Oct and 30 Nov. Mostly evidence-based estimates (83%), not street counts. Excludes hostels, squatters, travellers.
- Published 26 Feb 2026 (read).
- URL: https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/rough-sleeping-snapshot-in-england-autumn-2025/rough-sleeping-snapshot-in-england-autumn-2025
- Safe wording: "4,793 people estimated sleeping rough on one night, autumn 2025."

## Which comparison is fair for 13 seconds
- **Best: 303,185 long-term empty homes vs 134,210 households in temporary accommodation.** Both are official MHCLG figures, both in homes/households, both late 2025. Long-term empties are more than twice the households in TA. Say "households", and "homes", not people.
- **Also fair, but flag it:** 754,264 empty homes (all kinds, includes short-term empties and exempt ones). It beats 134,210 by 5.6x, but critics will say most are in turnover; the long-term number avoids that.
- **Avoid:** "1,022,158 empty homes", because it includes 267,894 second homes and is a charity's sum. Avoid putting 382,618 people beside homes: people against dwellings, and an NGO estimate against official data. Avoid claiming empties could house the homeless one-for-one. Many are in the wrong place, derelict or in probate. The fair claim is scale only.
- Same-night caution: the empties are 6 Oct 2025 and TA is 31 Dec 2025; say "late 2025".
