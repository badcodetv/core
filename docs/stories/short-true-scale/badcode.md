## What it is
A BadCode short (9:16, 40.8 s), "Nearest Wins": a pub-quiz tiebreaker, "How many homes in England have been empty six
months or more?" Every table guesses low; the nearest answer is Bob's, a team of one with his rucksack and sleeping mat.
"How'd you know that?" Bob: "I sleep opposite one." He hands on the meat raffle and walks out into the rain; a plain stats
card, then the logo as a pub fruit machine. The point: there are far more empty homes than homeless households;
homelessness is a decision about who homes are for, not a shortage. State: rough cut 5 on the timeline page; next is
Jack's notes, then Premiere and export. (The earlier true-scale 3D graphic version is scrapped.)

## Story-only rules
- Numbers (checked, [numbers.md](./numbers.md)): 303,185 long-term empty homes (MHCLG, Oct 2025), 267,894 second homes
  (call them "nobody's main home", never "empty"), 134,210 households in temporary accommodation (31 Dec 2025). Say
  households and homes, not people. Never "1 million empty"; never put Shelter's 382,618 people beside homes; never the
  US figures; never claim the empties could house the homeless one-for-one. Use one quarter for every number on screen and re-check for a newer one before posting.
