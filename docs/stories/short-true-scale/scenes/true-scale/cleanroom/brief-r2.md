## The point
England has far more homes standing empty or kept as second homes than it has homeless households. Homelessness is a decision
about who homes are for, not a shortage. Never mock the viewer; never blame homeless people; never a real person, politician or
company. The viewer is a working-class UK person the economy has failed.

## The numbers (England, official, late 2025)
- **134,210** homeless households in temporary accommodation (B&Bs, hostels, short lets). A household = a single person, a couple or a family; 85,800 have children. Only about 4,800 people sleep rough.
- **303,185** homes empty long-term (6+ months).
- **267,894** second homes (furnished, nobody's main home).
- So: more than **2** long-term empty homes per homeless household; about **4** counting second homes (571,079 homes that are nobody's main home).

## What we want now

demonstration, piece of British life, or visual trick, where the viewer gets "there are more than enough homes" in one look,
without needing to read numbers. Think of how a comedian or a great infographic or an advert would land it. Dark humour is
welcome (BadCode's voice: sarcastic, certain, on the viewer's side). Each idea must be honest to the numbers (the ratio is about
2 to 1, or about 4 to 1 with second homes; don't exaggerate it).
