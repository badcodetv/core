## The scene (about 25 s)
A British local pub on quiz night: carpet, fruit machine, answer sheets, tiny pencils, a meat raffle on the bar, a quizmaster with a
mic.
1. **Tiebreaker:** the quizmaster holds up a sheet: "Nearest wins the meat raffle. How many homes in England have been empty six months or more?"
2. **The guesses:** quick cuts of confident wrong guesses being scribbled and shouted across tables. One table's sheet says **50,000**. In the corner, **Bob**, a team of one, writes his answer without hesitating.
3. **The answer:** the quizmaster reads the answer from the sheet: **303,185** (plus 267,894 second homes); groans; "Nearest… table nine. Bob."
4. **How'd you know?:** the whole pub turns to look at Bob for the first time. He is standing, rucksack and rolled sleeping mat on. His sheet on the table says **300,000** (his guess, the nearest).
5. **The prize:** Bob hands the meat-raffle prize to the next table and walks out into the rain.
