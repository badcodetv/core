# pub-upright-piano: what shaped it

- **Instrumental wording.** "instrumental, no vocals" go together at the front of the style box, and the box names the production, not a genre that implies singing. "Solo" makes it one instrument. Source: https://hookgenius.app/learn/suno-instrumental-music/
- **Name the exact instrument, not just "piano".** Tag lists say a bare "piano" drifts towards a generic ballad, and honky-tonk is listed as its own piano type. So the box says "honky-tonk upright" and "saloon piano" and gives the feel. Sources: https://sunoaime.pages.dev/blog/suno-instrument-prompt-tags/ , https://roo.beehiiv.com/p/i-tested-200-suno-prompts-for-detective-music-30-actually-sound-cinematic-here-s-why
- **Where the honky-tonk sound comes from.** One string per key is pulled out of tune, and tack hammers or a mandolin rail add a jangle. I wrote "slightly detuned" and "tack-hammer jangle" so it reads as a pub piano and not a broken one. Sources: https://perfessorbill.com/help/faq9.htm , https://boards.straightdope.com/t/what-gives-19th-century-saloon-style-pianos-their-characteristic-sound/334305
- **Stride left hand.** The left hand plays a bass note on beats 1 and 3 and a chord on 2 and 4. I kept the bass low and limited the right hand to sparse high stabs, so the middle stays empty for the voices (roughly 300 Hz to 3 kHz). Source: https://hub.yamaha.com/keyboards/k-how-to/the-basics-of-stride-piano
- **The ending.** The lyrics stay empty, so the slow-down, the rain, the held chords and the bright run up the keys for the fruit-machine landing are written into the style box as one "ending:" clause. Whether v6 follows that order is untested.
- **The exclude box.** Besides the brief's list, it blocks crowd singalongs, spoken word, ballad or grand-piano reverb, any lead melody, drums and synth, so nothing else takes over from the piano and the room.
- **Settings.** v6 with normal variety. Weirdness is 35 so the take stays close to the brief. Style influence is 70 so the room sounds and the ending are more likely to be followed. Duration is 40 s and Max Mode is off.
