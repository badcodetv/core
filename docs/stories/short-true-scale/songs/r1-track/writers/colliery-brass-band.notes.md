# colliery-brass-band: what shaped it

- **The band itself.** A "colliery band" is the coal-field name for the standard British brass band (cornets, tenor horns, baritones, euphoniums, E-flat and B-flat basses, trombones). I named the instruments by their real brass-band names (E-flat / B-flat basses, baritone horns, cornet) so the model reaches for that ensemble rather than an orchestra or a New Orleans band. Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_brass_band , https://www.stapesilverband.co.uk/post/instruments-of-a-brass-band , https://bbe.org.uk/node/7074
- **Through the wall.** The muffling is written as production words a model can hear (lo-fi, boxy, dull top end, space in the middle), which also keeps the 300 Hz-3 kHz band clear for the AI Studio dialogue. The cornet gets "short held tones, never a tune" so nothing melodic fights the voices.
- **Prose, not tags.** The brief asked for a scene description; Suno v6 guides say the style field reads natural language but rewards structure, so the sentences go in order: who, where, low end, cornet, room, mix, ending. Source: https://blakecrosley.com/blog/suno-style-field-style-influence , https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/optimize-suno-v6-style-of-music-prompt
- **Exclude kept short and paired with positives.** Guides say a short exclude list works better and that an exclusion should have a positive filler in the style box (the basses and baritones fill the role strings or trailer brass would take). "Murmur with no words" replaces crowd speech rather than only banning it. Source: https://jackrighteous.com/de/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/negative-prompting-suno-v5-guide , https://usesuno.pages.dev/guide/negative-prompts
- **Settings.** v6 at 40 s (fixed). Style influence 70: the description is precise, and the same guide says precise fields want the slider toward Strong. Weirdness 40, a touch under centre, so the structure (thin out, then the stab) is respected rather than reinvented. Variety normal, max mode off. The ending's timing is the riskiest ask: Suno may not land the stab in the last two seconds, so the stab can be trimmed onto the logo in the edit.
