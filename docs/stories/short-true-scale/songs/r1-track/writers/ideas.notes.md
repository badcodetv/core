# r1-track: research notes (ideas writer, 2026-11-16)

- Instrumentals in Suno: put "instrumental" / "no vocals" early in the style box; the Instrumental switch alone does not always stop hallucinated vocals; describe what you want rather than leaning on bans; earlier tags weigh more (third-party claims, unverified). https://howtopromptsuno.com/style-lyrics/how-do-i-make-instrumental , https://hookgenius.app/learn/suno-instrumental-prompts/ , https://blakecrosley.com/guides/suno
- Too many structure tags conflict or do nothing; simple prompts give Suno room. https://howtopromptsuno.com/style-lyrics/how-do-i-make-instrumental
- Ambience inside a song: a style tag like "rain ambience" gives a continuous texture with imprecise placement; bracketed SFX cues are for lyrics boxes (we have none here). Suno Sounds (separate experimental feature) does rain/room tone/crowd beds as loops. https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/suno-sounds-ai-sound-effects-guide , https://help.suno.com/en/articles/10625537
- Underscore under dialogue: texture over melody, no long tune on one instrument, keep out of the voice band or the mixer turns you down; "never draw the conscious attention of the listener". https://thinkspace.ac.uk/blog/blog-how-to-underscore/
- Speech band figures vary by source: ~500 Hz-1 kHz core, 1-3 kHz cut on music, 2.5-5 kHz intelligibility. Sparse music with room between notes sits best under voice. https://www.prosoundweb.com/in-the-studio-balancing-dialog-in-post-production/ , https://sonilo.com/blog/guides/background-music-for-interview-videos
- Fruit machine sound: reel whir and mechanical clunk as each reel stops, then a bright ascending chime run for the win; payout bells only after the last reel lands. Fruit machines spread through British pubs in the 1960s-70s, heard over glassware and chatter. https://audiosolace.com/blog/why-slot-machines-use-specific-audio-cues , https://beerconnoisseur.com/?p=68979 , https://fusehive.itch.io/mechanical-fruit-machine-slots-sound-effects-library-retro-analog-slot-game-soun
- Pub music culture: jukeboxes near universal in UK pubs late 70s-80s; pub rock was the back-to-basics small-venue sound. https://davehaslam.substack.com/p/the-rise-and-fall-of-jukeboxes , https://www.britannica.com/art/pub-rock
