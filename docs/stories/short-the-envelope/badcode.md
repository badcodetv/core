## What it is
A BadCode short (9:16, 15 s), the "Sting switcheroo" version: a 1930s betting parlour where the race is the election.
The dealer fans two envelopes, APPROVED BY THE CONSERVATIVE PARTY and APPROVED BY THE LABOUR PARTY; the working man picks
one; under the counter both hold the same arms order beside fresh money; BadCode rips back the curtain on blue tie and
red tie shaking hands over the rifle crate. The point: some decisions don't change whoever you vote for; the vote is
the distraction. **State: PARKED at rough cut 1** (Jack: "this one doesnt work lets do a different short", no reason
given; ask him before reviving).
