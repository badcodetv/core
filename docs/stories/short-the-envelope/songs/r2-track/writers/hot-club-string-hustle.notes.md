# hot-club-string-hustle — what shaped it

- **Instrumentation from the 1930s Paris quintet model**: two rhythm guitars, upright bass, violin lead, played acoustically with no drummer; la pompe is a fast up-down stroke then a down stroke, accenting 2 and 4, replacing the drums. Described in my own words ("pumping a tight chopped offbeat") and no artist names, since Suno dislikes named artists. Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gypsy_jazz , https://www.premierguitar.com/style-guide-django-reinhardt-and-gypsy-jazz?page=2
- **Lead instrument first, then rhythm, then texture** (genre, feel, two or three signature instruments, mood, production): https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/optimize-suno-v6-style-of-music-prompt
- **No negatives in the style box; unwanted things go in Exclude**, and exclude is checked against the positive prompt so nothing summons a voice (no "scat", "call", "chant" words in the style). Instrumental toggle should be on; lyrics left empty: https://alexanderweichart.de/2_Areas/Music/Suno-Instructions , https://jackrighteous.com/en-gb/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/negative-prompting-suno-v5-guide , https://jackrighteous.com/es-gb/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/stop-suno-adding-crowd-vocals-choirs-backing-voices
- **Timed cues written as second-ranges in the style box** so the switch (pizzicato + falling glissando, guitars stop dead) lands near 7-10.5 s and the last 4.5 s stay almost empty for the line laid over it outside Suno. "No piano, no drums" in the style is the one negative kept, because it defines the ensemble; piano/drums/ragtime are also in Exclude to keep it away from anything Joplin-like.
- **Settings**: v6, Duration 15 s (fixed by the story). Variety "normal" and Style Influence 75 to hold the timed wording; Weirdness 35 for a little character without losing the period sound; no vocal gender; Max Mode off.
