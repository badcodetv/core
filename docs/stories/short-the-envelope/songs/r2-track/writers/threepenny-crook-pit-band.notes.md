# threepenny-crook-pit-band: notes

What shaped it:

- **The band is the 1928 seven-player pit band, not an orchestra.** Publisher listings for the original score give seven doubling players: alto sax (fl, cl, bari), tenor sax, trumpet, trombone (double bass), banjo (guitar, Hawaiian guitar, mandolin, bandoneon), timpani/percussion, harmonium (celesta, piano). So banjo, harmonium, alto sax, muted trumpet and timpani are all authentic to the line-up. The timpani is a band member, which is why the 7 s switch thud is written as a timpani hit "inside the band" and not as a sound effect pasted over the top. Sources: https://www.schott-music.com/en/die-dreigroschenoper-no308754.html , https://www.guidetomusicaltheatre.com/shows_t/threepenny_opera.htm , https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/Barbara_Song
- **No composer or work named in the box.** "Weimar Berlin underworld cabaret pit band" describes the sound without naming Weill/Threepenny (artist names tend to be blocked or ignored), and nothing is quoted from any score, in line with the "invent it, copy nothing" rule.
- **Not piano-led, on purpose.** Round 1 leaned on piano; "piano lead, ragtime, stride piano" go in Exclude to keep this away from the banned Sting/Joplin territory and from earlier rounds.
- **Instrumental is enforced three ways.** Guides agree on: Instrumental on, "instrumental" first in the style box, vocal textures (singing, speech, narration, choir) in Exclude, and an empty lyrics box. Sources: https://howtopromptsuno.com/style-lyrics/how-do-i-make-instrumental , https://diyai.io/ai-tools/audio-generation/suno-exclude-styles/ , https://suno.com/release-notes/exclude-styles
- **Exclude holds the "no" items.** The positive box describes what to play; bans go in Exclude so the style box never names the thing to avoid (https://jackrighteous.com/es/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/negative-prompting-suno-v5-guide).
- **Settings.** v6 and 15 s are fixed by the story. Weirdness 45 and variety normal leave some looseness for the lopsided, out-of-tune feel. Style influence is at 70 so the instrument list and the cut-off at 10.5 s are followed (unverified whether Suno honours timestamps). maxMode is off for a 15 s cue.
