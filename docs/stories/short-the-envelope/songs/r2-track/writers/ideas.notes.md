# Round r2-track: research notes (ideas writer)

## Getting a true instrumental out of Suno
- Switch the Instrumental mode on even when the style box already says instrumental. Text alone ("no vocals") is not a guarantee: faint vocals, humming or choir pads can leak. https://alexanderweichart.de/2_Areas/Music/Suno-Instructions , https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/stop-suno-adding-crowd-vocals-choirs-backing-voices
- Put unwanted sounds in the Exclude box, not as "no X" in the style box. Keep the list short and specific, and give the removed element a positive replacement in the style box so the role isn't left empty. https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/negative-prompting-suno-v5-guide , https://diyai.io/ai-tools/audio-generation/suno-exclude-styles/ , https://hookgenius.app/learn/suno-negative-prompting/
- Sound effects inside a song prompt are possible (bracketed cues like [Vinyl Crackle] between sections, or "rain sounds" in style), but Suno may apply them subtly. Suno Sounds is a separate one-shot/loop mode for single foley effects. Unverified for v6 specifically. https://help.suno.com/en/articles/10625537 , https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/suno-sounds-ai-soundeffects-guide

## Period palettes that aren't The Sting (no Joplin, no Hamlisch)
- Weill's Threepenny Opera (1928, a play about crooks and the respectable bourgeoisie): a pit band of saxophones, banjo (doubling guitar, Hawaiian guitar, mandolin, bandoneon), harmonium (doubling celesta, piano), trumpets, trombone, timpani and percussion. https://www.kwf.org/works/die-dreigroschenoper/ , https://www.eamdc.com/composers/kurt-weill/works/die-dreigroschenoper/ , https://universaledition.com/en/Works/The-Threepenny-Opera/P0013809
- Hot Club string jazz (Paris, from 1934): violin and lead guitar over two rhythm guitars ("la pompe") and upright bass, acoustic, no drums. Hard-picked runs and swing feel. https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/Quintette_du_Hot_Club_de_France , https://www.premierguitar.com/articles/Style_Guide_Django_Reinhardt_and_Gypsy_Jazz
- 1930s tango (its golden age): the bandoneon is the signature voice, over the habanera cell (dotted-eighth, sixteenth, eighth, eighth) and marcato accents that work like cues. https://www.masterclass.com/articles/guide-to-tango-music , https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tango , https://interlude.hk/tango-beyond-piazzolla-ii-troilo-di-sarli/
- 1930s cartoon scoring ("Mickey Mousing", Carl Stalling): the music hits each on-screen action, using glissandi, bounces and a stinger chord (a sharp shock chord for a reveal). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mickey_Mousing , https://wikipedia.com/wiki/Carl_Stalling , https://filmmusiccentral.com/2016/01/23/film-music-101-stinger-chords , https://www.erudit.org/en/revue/cine/2013/v24/n1/1023110ar.html
