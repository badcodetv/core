# back-room-whisper-upright — what shaped it

- **Scene in plain sentences, not genres.** Suno's own Sounds help says ambience drifts into music when the setting is general ("coffee shop" gave lofi) and holds better when you name concrete sources ("background chatter and clinking cups"). So the style box names each sound: clinking glasses, murmur, riffled banknotes, a spun coin, a piano heard *through a wall*. https://help.suno.com/en/articles/10625537
- **Speech, not singing.** The spoken-narration guide: describe delivery as speech, keep the bed restrained and voice-forward, strip hook/chorus words, add a positive direction ("no melody in the voice") beside the excludes, one clear cue per section ([Whispered spoken word]), short punctuated lines. If the cue gets spoken aloud, move it into the style box. https://jackrighteous.com/de/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/suno-ai-spoken-narration-workflow
- **Expect leakage.** Speech in v6 is a tendency, not a mode; generate both takes and keep the spoken one. Suno's separate Speech beta (Oct 2026) exists but has no duration control, which this 15 s cut needs, so this stays in v6 Custom. https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/suno-speech-beta-spoken-audio-background-music
- **The room.** Interwar off-course cash betting was illegal (Street Betting Act 1906 until 1960); bets were taken through back doors into smoke-filled back rooms by runners, so the speaker is a runner letting you in on the fix. https://www.sportsjournalists.co.uk/view-from-the-pressbox/when-back-street-bookies-were-an-innocent-flutter/ , https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/historical-journal/article/abs/police-and-the-people-gambling-in-salford-19001939/2FDA6B1298BAE529425DBA2A227C18A6
- **Timing.** The [Intro] ambience tag gives the room ~9 s before the voice. The one line is split at its comma (every word kept, spelling unchanged) so a [The room falls silent] cue can sit before "everything else is a distraction." If Suno reads the cue aloud, rejoin the line and keep the silence only in the style box.
- **Settings.** v6 core (more consistent than Wild for speech), styleInfluence 70 so the plain-sentence description outweighs Suno's song habits, weirdness 35, male voice, 15 s duration as fixed. Excludes ban singing, drums, orchestra, and ragtime/Joplin to keep clear of The Sting.
