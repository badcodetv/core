# novelty-flurry-then-violin-jab — what shaped it

- **The piano is novelty piano, not ragtime.** Novelty piano (1920s, Art Deco polish) leans on triplets, dotted figures, chromatic runs, "characteristic breaks" and consecutive fourths, and drops ragtime's steady oom-pah. So the style box asks for chromatic right-hand runs, triplet figures and glissando sweeps with the pulse carried by a woodblock, and the exclude bans ragtime, stride and Joplin by name (the story's Never list).
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novelty_piano
  - https://www.alfred.com/collections/zez-confrey-sheet-music.oembed (runs over a steady pulse; no composer named in the prompt)
- **Speech, not singing.** Guides agree: describe the delivery and a restrained bed, keep song words (hook, chorus, anthemic) out, put a [Spoken word] cue on its own line above the line, and pair the ban on singing with positive pacing words ("plain conversational pacing, no melody"). Hence the bed collapses to a lone cello drone before the voice enters.
  - https://jackrighteous.com/ja/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/suno-ai-spoken-narration-workflow
  - https://roo.beehiiv.com/p/suno-commands
- **Order and placement.** Earliest style tokens weigh most, so the timing frame ("15-second timed cue") leads; negatives live in Exclude; section cues in the lyrics box carry the timed events, since v6 has been seen to act on lyric-box section cues (single anecdotal test, unverified).
  - https://www.layer3labs.io/guides/how-to-write-suno-prompts
  - https://sunowatermark.com/blog/suno-v6-vocal-prompts-2026/
- **The pause.** The words are fixed, so the pause before "everything else is a distraction" rides on the existing comma and "slow, weary" in the cue; no bracket was put mid-line because directions inside a line risk being read aloud (jackrighteous guide).
- Settings: v6 core (consistency over Wild), styleInfluence 75 to hold the four-part event order, weirdness 35, male, 15 s fixed.
