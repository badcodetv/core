## The idea (from the slate, picked by Jack 2026-11-12)

The 1919 *Life* cartoon: a top-hatted fat cat on a park bench tells a one-legged veteran "The war is over my boy. Forget it."
Same bench today, the same fat cat, a nurse: "Forget it." He hasn't aged a day.

Jack, 2026-11-12: *"lets do this, using the art style, but lets change it slightly so it doesnt get copywrighted."*

**Rough beats (20-second cut, two eras, Jack 2026-11-12):** 1919, the veteran: "Forget it." (≈5 s) → the same bench today, the
same fat cat, a nurse in scrubs: "Forget it." (≈5 s) → he hasn't aged a day (≈4 s). **≈14 s.** 2054 dropped.

**Point:** the people who carry the cost are always thanked, then forgotten. The fat cat is the decision-maker; never the soldier's or nurse's fault.

## Checks owed

  is probably public domain in the UK and US anyway; we are not relying on that.)
- ⬜ **No real person.** The fat cat is a type, not a likeness.
- ⬜ **Nurse line:** pay and the clapping, not the pandemic as news (no news cycle); wording is Jack's.
