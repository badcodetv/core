# Audio Profile
A soft, prissy country squire from East Anglia, late fifties, a high, slightly wheezy voice kept to a near-whisper, as if his lips are at your ear, with a small giggle tucked inside the words.

## Scene:
Leaning in towards a stranger on a bench in the dark, as if telling a secret.

## Sample Context:
Polite, almost tender, which is what makes it horrible.
