# Audio Profile
A wealthy old English gentleman in his sixties, plummy upper-class southern accent, a low purr with gravel in it. Velvet contempt: amused, unhurried, never raises his voice.

## Scene:
Late night on a park bench under a gas lamp. He is speaking to the man beside him without looking at him.

## Sample Context:
He has said this to a great many people and enjoys it a little more each time.
