# c-portrait-straight-to-camera — notes (redo)

Rewrite after the sameness gate flagged the shared phrase "for a caption". The new prompt never uses the word "caption": the headroom is asked for as "the upper third of the sheet, above the hat, is left as clean empty paper with nothing drawn in it". All other wording is fresh against the previous draft.

What shaped it:
- Character-sheet prompts for Nano Banana work best with a single figure on a plain ground with even light, so the figure can be reused as the reference ("clean neutral background, full body"). The invented "costume study" framing does that job without asking for a multi-view sheet, which would break the bench scene. Sources: https://lilys.ai/en/notes/nano-banana-20260127/consistent-character-sheets-nano-banana , https://www.ssw.com.au/rules/consistent-ai-generated-characters , https://morphic.com/resources/prompts/character-design-prompts ("clean ink line ... plain cream ground").
- None of the prompt libraries reserve empty space at the top, so the instruction is spelled out as a fraction of the frame plus "nothing drawn in it" (per the search summary of those sites).
- Period technique: Life-era drawings were pen ink over graphite, some with wash; this prompt chooses pure line (cross-hatch, single-direction hatching, stipple) and bans wash so the plate stays clean and copyable across beats. Sources: https://www.loc.gov/exhibits/cartoonamerica/cartoon-checklist.html , https://findingaids.library.upenn.edu/records/PRIN_MUDD_MC068 , https://americanheritage.com/funny-us
- Distance from the source cartoon: no soldier present, he faces the viewer rather than a companion, empty seats with only the dropped crutch and hospital pass carry the story; "invented ... resembling no real person" covers the no-likeness rule. No artist or title named.
- Settings fixed by the story: Nano Banana 2, 9:16. Ends with "Thanks." per house convention.
- Same-prompt check (fresh.py): swapped the habit phrases "in the middle of" and "anywhere in the image" for fresh wording.
