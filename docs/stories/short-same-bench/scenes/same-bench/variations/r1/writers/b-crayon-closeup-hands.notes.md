- Medium: grease (lithographic) crayon was the 1910s editorial cartoonist's tool, chosen because it imitated lithography's soft multi-shade line and its broad strokes read as immediate; Library of Congress catalogue entries for 1910s cartoons list crayon with ink and opaque white. Hence crayon on toothy paper + India ink wash + scratched white highlights.
