# b-crosshatch-hospital-dusk: notes (redo)

Redo after the sameness gate flagged the phrase "for a caption" (shared across most of the round). The new prompt never uses the word "caption": the clear band is described as "an open band where words will later be set", and the whole paragraph is reworded from scratch rather than patched.

What shaped it:

- **Lit windows as reserved white.** In pen-and-ink, a building's windows read bright only at night, lit from inside, and the white of the paper can do part of the work while the hatching around it does the rest. Hence the hospital tower inked near-black with its windows left as untouched white oblongs. Sources: https://artyfactory.com/pen_and_ink_drawing/pen_and_ink_drawing_1.htm, https://www.walkingboston.com/2020/02/06/making-pictures-with-tiny-lines/
- **Night sky by hatching density.** Pen night skylines build darkness with layered cross-hatching, so dusk is carried by the hatching thickening toward the ground, with the last light behind the tower. Source: https://condrengalleries.com/chicago-skyline-199a
- **Negative space has to be placed and named.** Nano Banana guidance says to say where the empty area sits and what fills the rest, and that "minimal" or "bare" wording keeps the model from filling the frame. Hence "top fourth of the page is left as bare, almost unmarked paper sky". Sources: https://10b-ai-blog-frontend.pages.dev/blog/nano-banana-composition-tips, https://uxplanet.org/a-designers-guide-to-prompting-google-nano-banana-954d5b73fe71
- **No text in the picture.** The prompt ends by excluding lettering, balloons and a signature, because the cartoon genre invites captions and speech balloons that the model would otherwise draw.
