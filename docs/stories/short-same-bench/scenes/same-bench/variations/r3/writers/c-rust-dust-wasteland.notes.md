# c-rust-dust-wasteland — notes (redo)

Redo after the sameness gate flagged "the top third of the frame" (shared with another prompt in round r3). Only the caption-space sentence changed: it now describes the dust haze overhead as a blank band of murky air kept bare for words laid over it later. The rest of the prompt stands.

What shaped it:
- Dystopian surrealist painting of the 1970s-80s: meticulous oil-on-hardboard surfaces, desolate ruined landscapes, deep atmospheric distance; the later work in a restricted, subdued palette.
  - https://www.thecollector.com/zdzislaw-beksinski-the-dystopian-surrealist-painter-you-should-know/
  - https://artincontext.org/dystopian-surrealism
- A design guide drawn from that work: hold the palette to three or four related tones (ochre, rust, brown) and add one lighter accent for the focal point. That is why the man is the single clean, cool note in the frame. (This is a designer's reading, not a documented account of the painter's own method.)
  - https://madegooddesigns.com/?p=111817
- The dread comes from scale and absence: the clean man, small, dead centre, brushing off nobody in a ruin he has outlived, unaged. No game imagery, no named artist in the prompt.
