# c-the-only-still-thing: notes (redo)

Rewritten to drop the phrase "in the picture" that the sameness gate flagged; the rest of the wording is fresh too.

What shaped it:
- Daguerre's Boulevard du Temple plate (1838): the street's traffic vanished over a minutes-long exposure and the only person recorded was the man who stood still. That is the gag: the rich man is the one fixed thing while generations blur past him. https://smarthistory.org/daguerre-paris-boulevard , https://www.history.com/articles/oldest-known-photograph-person
- Glass-plate decay for the surface: silver mirroring reads as a bluish metallic sheen, worst at the edges and in dark areas; plus fading, abrasion, broken corners. https://psap.library.illinois.edu/advanced-help/negative-glass , https://www.conservation-wiki.com/wiki/Gelatin_Dry-plate_Negative
- Horror comes from the stillness: an unblinking fixed smile, ghosts with no shadows, and only he casts one. No gore and no game imagery. Pale empty sky kept at the top for the caption.

Settings: Nano Banana 2, 9:16 (fixed by the story).
