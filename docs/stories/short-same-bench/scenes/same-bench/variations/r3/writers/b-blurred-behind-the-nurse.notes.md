# b-blurred-behind-the-nurse: notes (redo)

Rewritten after the sameness gate flagged "man in a", "of the frame" and "one gloved hand (lifted in a)". All three phrases are gone, and the prose has been reworded throughout instead of just patched.

What shaped it:
- Shallow focus phrasing: Nano Banana prompt galleries get a sharp subject with a soft background by naming a wide-open portrait lens and saying which subject is sharp, e.g. "shallow depth of field, bokeh background", "sharp focus on subject", 85mm at f/1.4-1.8. I kept the idea ("lens wide open so only the closest subject holds focus") and left out the numbers. Sources: https://carat.im/en/prompt-gallery/p/29076, https://youmind.com/en-US/nano-banana-pro-prompts?id=930, https://youmind.com/prompts/night-city-dash-portrait-35565
- Sodium light as dread: a low-pressure sodium lamp emits almost all its light at one wavelength (~589 nm), so colour rendition collapses and white balance cannot bring it back. The prompt asks for "flat amber monochrome, so colours die and skin turns waxy", which makes the light itself unsettling and not just orange. The gallery search turned up no sodium-specific prompts, so this wording is untested. Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sodium-vapor_lamp, https://cinematography.net/edited-pages/Streetlights.htm, https://www.uniquephoto.com/community/qa/how-ambient-light-spectrum-affects-color-rendition-and-white-balance
- The horror comes from what you can only half see: he is a blurred bulk you have to squint at, and he leans slightly too close. Nothing in it comes from any game's imagery.
- The caption gets an empty top third of wet black sky. No text is drawn in the image. The settings are fixed by the story: Nano Banana 2, 9:16.
