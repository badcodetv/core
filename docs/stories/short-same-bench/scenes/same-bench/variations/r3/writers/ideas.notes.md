# r3 ideas: research notes (2026-11-12)

Web research only; nothing read from the repo.

- **Fog marks where you stop knowing.** It hides how far a street or a creature goes, so a threat can't be placed. A short draw distance means empty space is never proven empty. Fixed camera angles leave blind spots. Low-poly, half-made forms and warping, dithered textures stop the eye from fully recognising a shape. Familiar forms bent a little are scarier than monsters. https://www.itechguides.com/what-makes-ps1-style-survival-horror-games-scary/
- Fog keeps horror "constantly possible"; decay (rust, flaking paint, stagnant water, dust) carries isolation and entropy; cool blue-grey slows perception. https://www.superjumpmagazine.com/drafting-through-the-mists/ · https://www.creativebloq.com/3d/video-game-design/how-unreal-engine-5-3-made-still-wakes-the-deep-more-terrifyingly-beautiful
- **German Expressionism:** hard contrast, stark silhouettes, unmotivated shadows, canted angles, exaggerated depth. Nosferatu's shadow arrives before the vampire does, and the film turns real, familiar places sinister. https://www.bfi.org.uk/lists/10-great-german-expressionist-films · https://howtofilmschool.com/cinema-studies/german-expressionism/
- **Silent-horror prompt terms** (one blog, untested): orthochromatic nitrate stock, chalk-white skin, crushed blacks with no mid-grey, iris vignette, heavy silver-halide grain, uncoated lens, "no digital smoothing". https://budgetpixel.com/blog/dark-expressionism-prompting-1920s-silent-horror-cinema
- **Horror ink:** all in black ink on white, dense crosshatching and stippling, eye sockets shaded for dread, close-ups that fill the panel with no background (the sources are fan-level). https://www.overthinkingit.com/2018/07/24/junji-itos-eyes/ · https://www.thepromptindex.com/skill/1193-illustration-style-junji-ito · https://www.retrosupply.co/blogs/tutorials/how-to-ink-like-a-horror-comic-artist
- **Goya's Black Paintings:** palette held to ochre, gold, brown, grey and black, white used only for accents; the subject pushed to one side with heavy empty space; oil on plaster. https://www.artgallery.co.uk/blog/17620-unveiling-darkness-francisco-goyas-black-paintings-1819-1823 · https://www.thecollector.com/gallery/saturn-devouring-his-son/
- **Dystopian painting palette:** three or four related tones (ochre, rust, brown) plus one light accent on the focal point; barren places choked with dust or fog. https://madegooddesigns.com/?p=111817 · https://www.thecollector.com/zdzislaw-beksinski-dystopian-world/
- **Cinematography:** a soft, out-of-focus figure behind a sharp subject makes the viewer strain to read the danger. A backlight at 10 or 2 o'clock gives a sharp outline over a black face. An ambiguous figure that stays unidentified works best. In Kairo, the horror is a figure you can barely make out. https://looper.com/656584/the-real-reason-horror-movies-have-so-many-blurry-shots · https://theasc.com/article/terror-through-lighting/ · https://faroutmagazine.co.uk/the-horrifying-ghost-walk-in-kiyoshi-kurosawas-kairo/
- **Prompt craft** (blogs, untested): name the fog ("thick low-lying fog", "drifting ash"), film stock and grain, and "chiaroscuro". https://www.atlabs.ai/blog/27-cinematic-lighting-looks-ai-prompts-guide · https://morphic.com/resources/prompts/horror-prompts

What carries over: **he is the thing that is too clear, too still or too big.** The world around him is fogged, rusted, blurred or decaying. The comedy stays in the dismissive wave and the polite smile.
