# a-gaslamp-silent-film-still — notes (redo)

Redo after the sameness gate flagged the shared phrase "in the picture". Rewritten from scratch in new wording; that phrase is gone (the pair now sits "in the lower half of the frame").

What shaped it:
- Orthochromatic stock was blind to red, so red lips print near-black. The prompt keeps that as the detail that makes his polite smile look wrong: a white face with an ink-dark mouth. Source: forum discussion only, anecdotal ([Photrio, "Red lips, black lips"](https://www.photrio.com/forum/threads/red-lips-black-lips.109599/unread)). The forum posters agree on the mechanism but not on the makeup history.
- Expressionist horror got its "Stimmung" (mood) from chiaroscuro and staging, not from moving the camera. That is why the camera is locked off and frontal, and the dread comes from one hard light source. Sources: [BFI, Shadow and substance](https://bfi.org.uk/sight-and-sound/features/shadow-substance-f-w-murnaus-nosferatu), [Film Inquiry, German Expressionism](https://filminquiry.com/german-expressionism).
- Iris fades and masks were standard in that era's camerawork (blog-level source: [Dyerbolical](https://dyerbolical.com/shadows-that-devour-murnaus-mastery-of-light-and-dread-in-nosferatu/)). Unverified beyond that.
- The eye-sockets-as-pits effect comes from a hard top light shadowed by the hat brim. This is my own lighting logic and is unsourced; the search found nothing specific on it.

Settings: Nano Banana 2, 9:16, both fixed by the story. No banned words, no film titles or artist names in the prompt.
