# a-crayon-dismissing-hand: notes (r2 redo)

What shaped it:
- Around 1910-1920, American radical and antiwar cartoonists drew in black grease or litho crayon over ink wash. The crayon gave a soft line with many shades, like a lithograph, and the broad strokes read as immediate. So the prompt asks for broad greasy crayon over washes, with a grain that breaks up on a textured board. Sources: https://kiechelart.com/?p=54590, https://spartacus-educational.com/ARTcesare.htm, https://archives-exhibits.rit.edu/exhibits/show/editorial-cartoons/essays/history-cartoons
- Laid or toothy paper is what makes crayon marks go grainy: https://www.culturalheritage.org/docs/default-source/annualmeeting/2014am_poster04_from_pen_to_press.pdf
- No artist names are in the prompt. It describes the medium only.
