# b-flat-colour-hospital-night — notes (r2 redo)

Rewritten to drop the wording the sameness gate flagged ("no lettering captions", "of any kind", "silk top hat", "or marks of any kind drawn"): the hat is now a "tall black stovepipe hat", the no-text line is "carries no writing at all: no words, numbers, signs or signatures", and the rest of the paragraph is reworded from scratch.

What shaped it:
- Munich satirical weekly covers were colour lithographs with bold flat graphics on solid black grounds (LACMA record of the 1896 red-and-black cover): https://collections.lacma.org/object/123105
- Printing of the day pushed a limited palette of flat colours defined by a few chosen lines; big bold shapes (Gurney Journey on Thöny): https://gurneyjourney.blogspot.com/2019/06/eduard-thonys-caricatures.html?m=1
- Clean, incisive line of essential strokes, Jugendstil elegance plus satire: https://www.channeldraw.org/2025/09/03/simplicissimus-the-legendary-satirical-voice-of-fin-de-siecle-munich/
- Hence: four flat inks, even-weight black contours, an empty solid-black sky band (also the caption room), caricatured figures against geometric hospital architecture. No artist or title named in the prompt.
