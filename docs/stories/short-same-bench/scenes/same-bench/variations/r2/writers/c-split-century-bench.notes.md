# c-split-century-bench (redo): notes

Redo after the sameness gate flagged the caption-room phrase ("words can be laid over it later"), which matched other prompts in the round. That clause is gone. The tear is now described as a strip of pale card showing through the rip, kept empty as the place for the caption. Nothing else in the draft was flagged, but I reworded it all anyway so no wording carries over.

What shaped it:
- Split-screen prompts that work in Nano Banana keep the layout, camera angle and pose identical on both sides, so the change is the only thing that differs. Here the bench's legs line up across the rip and the man is a point-for-point duplicate. Sources: https://veprompts.pages.dev/nano-banana-before-after and https://blog.laozhang.ai/en/posts/how-to-prompt-nano-banana
- A torn-paper divider is a known pattern that NB renders reliably, and it says plainly where the line runs (horizontal, at the waist). Source: https://prompts.oomol.com/p/split-photo-and-sketch-portrait-effect-815436be
- Keep the background and light constant and change only the era's props. That is why one low sun from the left rakes both halves. Source: https://youmind.com/en-US/nano-banana-pro-prompts?id=874
- The no-text request is written as a positive instruction ("bare of any marks") rather than a list of bans, to keep away from the flagged "no words letters..." pattern.

Settings: Nano Banana 2, 9:16 (fixed by the story).
