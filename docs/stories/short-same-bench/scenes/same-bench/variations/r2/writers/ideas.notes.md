# r2 ideas: research notes (2026-11-12)

- Pen and ink was the norm for 1919 newspaper cartoons; two camps: the dense, "meticulously crosshatched", perfect-perspective McCay school (Winsor McCay, Harry Murphy, who inked folds, fabric wrinkles and musculature precisely) versus the looser majority line (Hal Coffman). Source: https://bergetoons.blogspot.com/2019/06/gladly-crosshatched-bear.html
- Period originals were "pencil and ink on board"; LoC entries also list crayon and opaque white over graphite underdrawing. Sources: https://digital.library.missouri.edu/node/186647 , https://www.loc.gov/exhibits/cartoonamerica/cartoon-checklist.html
- Boardman Robinson (NY Tribune from 1910, The Masses 1916-17) popularised black grease/lithographic crayon with ink washes: soft, tonal, smudgy blacks instead of hatching. Robert Minor pushed the same crayon approach. A real period alternative to pen line. Sources: https://ebsco.com/research-starters/history/boardman-robinson , https://www.annexgalleries.com/artists/biography/2012 , https://loc.gov/exhibits/goldstein/goldrad.html
- The fat capitalist as a stock type: Art Young drew Capitalism as a fat bald man tipping his chair over a cliff (1920), and a fat capitalist on a pyramid baiting a labourer (1913); the type descends from L'Assiette au Beurre and Simplicissimus. Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_Young , https://newpol.org/issue_post/radical-art-art-young-and-cartoons-american-socialism/ , https://www.cartooningcapitalism.com/art-for-the-masses
- Simplicissimus (Th. Th. Heine): bold flat graphics, a limited palette of flat colours defined by a few selected lines, "essential lines". A period-true route to colour. Sources: https://gurneyjourney.blogspot.com/2019/06/eduard-thonys-caricatures.html?m=1 , https://www.radnorshire-fine-arts.co.uk/brand/heine-thomas-theodor-1867-1948/
- Style is not copyrightable, but a specific panel's composition is: so vary camera distance, angle, setting, and medium (pen hatch, crayon, flat colour) rather than restaging the 1919 panel.
- Nano Banana consistency: build a character reference (bust / sheet) early and feed it to later beats; one prompt per beat; label references; name the art style and the emotion; be specific about environment and light. Sources: https://www.leonardo.ai/news/nano-banana-prompt-guide , https://llamagen.ai/lt/prompts/nano-banana-comic-prompts , https://ccforpms.com/nano-banana/consistency-style , https://framia.converge.ai/page/en-US/blog/nano-banana-2-tips
- Nano Banana 2 renders text readily, so "no lettering" must be stated explicitly (no official Google doc on negatives; third-party advice, unverified). Sources: https://fliki.ai/blog/nano-banana-2-prompting-guide , https://content.getdarwin.ai/en/nano-banana-2-prompt-guide-how-to-write-better-prompts
