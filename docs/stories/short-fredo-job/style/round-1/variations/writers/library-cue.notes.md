# library-cue: notes

Redo. The earlier draft shared the phrase "a speaking voice" with other prompts in the round, so the style box is now in new wording. Room for the narrator is now asked for as "the groove thins to bass and hi-hat in places so a narrator can talk on top".

- Sound palette: 1970s KPM-era jazz-funk is described as fuzz/funk bass, wah guitar, clavinet, aggressive horns and Hammond, with big jazz arrangements and funk breaks. That gave the instrument list, the breakbeat and the trumpet-and-trombone fanfare with a flute answer. I also kept the "British telly theme" framing ("Saturday-teatime telly thriller") so it stays the small-screen British cousin of the heist sound.

- Prompt shape: community guides say to keep the style box to about 100 words or less and to lead with genre, tempo, mood, then instruments and era. The arc (swagger, then a suspense stab, then a major-key lift, then one tight final chord) is written in plain words in the style box, because the lyrics box has to stay empty.
