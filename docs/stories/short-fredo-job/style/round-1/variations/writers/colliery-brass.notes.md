# colliery-brass: what shaped it

- Instruments: a British brass band uses only a fixed set of instruments, with cornets, flugelhorn, tenor horns, baritones, euphoniums and basses, and no trumpets or French horns. So the style names that exact line-up, and trumpet and French horn go in the exclude box. Euphonium and flugelhorn have a conical bore, which gives them their mellow sound, so the euphonium is the one left playing alone and the flugelhorn helps the lift. Sources: https://Www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silver_band, https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/Bandsman
- Form: in the traditional brass band march the solo cornet carries the main theme, and hymn arrangements are slow and sustained with rich harmony. Those two halves map onto the job (march) and the jar (hymn). Sources: https://conductit.eu/wp-content/uploads/sites/41/2021/10/Brass-Bands-A-Brief-Overview.pdf, https://www.countrylife.co.uk/out-and-about/brass-bands-a-northern-british-tradition-229881
- Prompt length: Reddit users find that once a style prompt goes much past about 100 words, Suno starts treating parts of it as optional, and that naming instruments works better than adjectives. The style is kept close to that length and leads with instruments. Source: https://medium.com/@aitooldiscovery/suno-reddit-tips-and-prompts-what-r-sunoai-users-actually-do-65b59d51bcad
- Sliders: raising Style Influence helps when the arrangement keeps drifting, so it is set at 75 to hold the falter and the hymn return. Source: https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/instrumentation-and-arrangement-in-suno-v5
- No v6-specific guidance turned up in the search, so the settings stay moderate (v6, variety normal, weirdness 30).
- Fitting the picture: the lyrics box has to stay empty, so the order of events is written into the style in words: two taps, the march, the falter down to one euphonium, the hymn, then a clean final chord. Duration is 30 s so the 18 s cut has spare to trim. The band is kept mid-low and sparse so the four spoken lines have room. A slow 80 BPM march keeps the step-by-step feel under the slow-motion walk.
