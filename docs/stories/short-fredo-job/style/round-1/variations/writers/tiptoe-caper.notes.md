# tiptoe-caper: notes

- Rewrite after the overlap check: the phrases about leaving space for the spoken lines are gone. They are replaced by "arrangement thins to bare plucks wherever a line gets read over it". The rest is reworded so it stands apart from the other six prompts.
- Instruments: production-music descriptions of "sneaky" cues keep putting staccato pizzicato strings together with a "conspiratorial" bassoon and clarinet counter-lines to suggest tiptoeing. That is why the bassoon and pizzicato lead, and why the clarinet carries the hopeful lift at the end. https://ende.app/en/song/13547-1-dramedy-the-quiet-sneak-cinematic-underscore-loops-the-core-collection , https://ende.app/en/song/12176-sneaky-snitch
- Hitting each footstep on a plucked note is "mickey mousing": music synced to on-screen action, a technique that goes back to early Disney. https://www.Wikipedia.com/wiki/Mickey_Mousing
- Prompt craft: the style box is kept at roughly 100 words or less, with named instruments and a BPM, and ends with "No vocals". The lyrics box is left empty, and variety is set to normal so the wording holds. https://medium.com/@aitooldiscovery/suno-reddit-tips-and-prompts-what-r-sunoai-users-actually-do-65b59d51bcad , https://jackrighteous.com/es/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/optimize-suno-v6-style-of-music-prompt
- The order follows the locked picture: opening taps, sneak and freezes, held breath at the laser, smug rise at the prize, tuba droop at the price sting, warm clarinet at the jar, then a single button ending. The track is 30 s so the 18 s cut can be trimmed out of it.
