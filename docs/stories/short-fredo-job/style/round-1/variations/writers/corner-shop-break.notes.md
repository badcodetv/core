# corner-shop-break: what shaped it

- Big beat (100-140 BPM; loud, compressed breakbeats; distorted bass; funk/soul loops; builds, drops, filters) gave the core. I picked 108 BPM so it swaggers instead of racing, and so the four spoken lines have room. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_beat
- Big beat usually leans on spoken-word and film dialogue samples. Those would fight the narrator, so they go in the exclude box, along with vocal chops. The prompt also asks for a lean, dry midrange.
- v6 style advice: put the lead genre first, then the rhythmic feel, then two or three signature instruments, then the emotion, then the production texture. Keep it short, name sounds you can hear, and don't stack genres. I also kept the exclude box free of anything the style asks for. Sources: https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/guides-using-suno-ai-music-creation/optimize-suno-v6-style-of-music-prompt , https://suno.com/release-notes/exclude-styles , https://github.com/mttkllr/suno-field-guide
- The lyrics box has to stay empty, so the shape (groove, turntable stop at the sting, a glockenspiel major arpeggio as the "coins", the break coming back, one stop hit) is written as one sentence in the style box. Suno won't hit 12.5 s exactly. Plan to cut the stop and the return onto the picture by hand, and lay the real till drawer, coin jar and shop-door bell over the top on the timeline, as the brief expects.
- Settings: v6 (controlled) rather than wild. Weirdness is moderate. Style influence is fairly high to hold the shape. durationSec is 30 so there's material to trim to about 18 s.
