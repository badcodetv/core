# Brief: The Fredo Job, the last shot (the logo in the snow)

You write prompts for Google Flow, Nano Banana 2.1, 9:16 portrait stills. Read ONLY this brief and look at the images
it names (all in this folder). Research freely on the web how marks, rings and symbols look when made in real snow
(trodden by footprints, pressed with a hand or a boot, drawn with a stick, ploughed, sprinkled, lit at night).

## The story's facts
- A short film: a crew of four masked thieves pulls a slick, deadly serious heist on a British corner shop at night, all
  for one cheap chocolate bar. This is the last shot: the maker's mark, made in the snow, after the job.
- The director, verbatim: "lets make one in the snow".

## The mark (fixed, never redesign it)
`logo-mark.png`: a thick white ring, and inside it one solid red triangle with rounded corners, upper right of centre
(about 2 o'clock), pointing down with its flat edge on top. Only the triangle is red. Made in snow, the ring is the snow
itself (pressed, trodden or cut in); the red triangle is one small red thing inside it, never dead centre. Keep the ring
a clean circle, seen from above or close to above, so it reads at once. Exactly one ring and one triangle in the frame.
Leave the lower third of the frame plain snow: the name is added there afterwards.

## The look: one line
Real-looking photographs that follow each reference photo's layout and light; quiet, cold, night where the photo is night.

## Images in this folder
- `ref-snow-path.jpg` (a snowy footpath at night with footprints and a street lamp), `ref-snow-street.jpg` (a city
  pavement in snow at night outside shops, warm lamp light), `ref-snow-flat.jpg` (flat fresh snow, close),
  `ref-snow-drift.jpg` (blue drifts at dusk), `logo-mark.png` (the mark).

## What to write
Four stills, one per reference photo, each a genuinely different way of the mark being made in the snow (your call from
your research): ids `SNOW-path`, `SNOW-street`, `SNOW-flat`, `SNOW-drift`. Each attaches its reference photo and
`logo-mark.png`. No people in the frame; traces of people (footprints, a glove print) are fine.

## Rules
- One paragraph per prompt, your own words, under ~120 words. 9:16.
- Describe only what should be in the picture; never name the thing to leave out. No readable text anywhere.
- No sign-off at the end of a prompt.

## Output
Write `prompts-snow.json` in this folder: `[{"id": "SNOW-path", "refs": ["ref-snow-path.jpg", "logo-mark.png"], "prompt": "..."}, ...]`.
