## The way out on screen: three options for the stills round (by: claude)
- **A. The pay packet ticks:** the price tag and a pay slip flip side by side like a heist-film departure board, both
  climbing together; the frog stays the same few minutes of work.
- **B. Heist cancelled:** the next crew pins up the blueprints, the pay goes up with prices, the plan's binned and one of
  them just walks in and buys it, coins on the counter.
- **C. The piggy bank that keeps up:** the crew's savings jar fills as the price rises, the level always at the tag.
