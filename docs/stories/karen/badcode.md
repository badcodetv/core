## What it is
A New York middle manager fired by an AI (Sean AI) weaponises complaining: "I want to speak to your manager"
climbs from a Midtown phone box to the President, at the moment the AI fires him too. Point: act, don't lie
there. Two videos: a **narrated story film** (ends on Sean AI's card, the sacked President calling Karen back,
"To be continued"; its one stated moral, alone on the end card with the logo (r11), is **"They're counting on you to hang up." then "Don't."** (Kai, 2026-11-27: "don't say please don't, just say don't"; was "They're counting on you to hang up, please don't...", 2026-11-26, from a Fable reviewer's five; the quality is persistence, never the word "complain")) and a **music video** for the song "All Day to Complain" (about 4:10).
