## Tone, in one paragraph

Old narrator: a dry Western storyteller, warm and amused. New narrator: the thing from the future that
watched all this happen and is **angry about the inaction**, talking straight to the viewer. The
aggression goes at the **doing nothing**, never at the viewer (address that accuses the viewer gets
pushback; [`narrator.md`](../../../story-craft/narrator.md) §3) and never at Karen once she starts
fighting. Karen is still mocked in Act 1, because the bait still has to work. The Susan contrast does
the "and you?" job without pointing a finger at anyone watching. It knows how this ends, so it can say
so: foreknowledge makes good tension; a withheld twist doesn't.
