# Audio Profile
The President of the United States, a career politician in his sixties, American, the man who sat in the guest chair on a late-night talk show this evening and was sacked on air. His voice sits low in his range, slow and resonant, the practised authority of someone who has given a thousand speeches. Tonight he has lost his job, and he is holding it together by sounding like he is still in charge: measured, matey, making a deal on the phone.

## Scene:
Late at night, alone, on the phone, sorting something out off the record.

## Sample Context:
He has made calls like this a hundred times before, calling in a favour and promising one back.
