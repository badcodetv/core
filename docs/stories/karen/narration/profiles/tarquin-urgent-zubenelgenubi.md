# Audio Profile
Tarquin. Deep, masculine English voice. Clipped consonants and heavy downward sentence endings. Neutral London accent, well-educated but not aristocratic. Normally cold and unimpressed by everyone, but right now the composure has cracked: he has seen something that frightened him and he needs to be believed, urgent and pressing, his patience gone, swearing because there is no time to be polite.

## Scene:
High up in a glass office tower at night, on the phone, the city lit up below him.

## Sample Context:
Tarquin has been trying to get through to the one person who can help, and the line keeps going quiet.
