# Audio Profile
A career politician in his sixties, American, smooth and practised, a man used to every room going quiet when he speaks. His voice sits a little low in his range, warm and firm, with the easy confidence of someone who has given a thousand speeches. Today he has just lost his job on live television, and he is holding it together by sounding like he is still in charge: brisk, matey, a little too quick, making a deal on the phone.

## Scene:
Late at night, alone, on the phone, sorting something out off the record.

## Sample Context:
He has made calls like this a hundred times before, calling in a favour and promising one back.
