# Karen story film: the new ending (r13 writing exercise)

Kai, 2026-11-27 (verbatim idea, paraphrased only in the beats below): the President, raced out of the studio in the
Beast, would not call Karen first; he calls his Chief of Staff "to ask what the fuck is going on". Show it on the
escalation ladder, with the Chief of Staff at the top: the boss's boss, a continuation of the joke. No character
voices: the narrator says everything. The Chief of Staff: "we need to get the public on our side". A few hours later
Karen gets a call from the Chief of Staff asking her to come to the Situation Room. Last scene: Karen walks into the
Situation Room and the President says "Karen, the fight back has begun. We need you on our side."

Held fixed: the narrator tells the ending; the boom bap runs to the end; the moral alone on the end card; Karen always
in the box when she is at the phone; faces in the ending withheld (look: one warm lamp, cold city glow).

## Draft A: straight, Kai's beats (recommended)

- E1: "In the back of the Beast, the President does the only thing he can think of. He asks to speak to his manager."
- E2: "His Chief of Staff." (the sixth rung lights) "He asks what the fuck is going on. The Chief of Staff says they need to get the public on their side."
- E3: "A few hours later, a phone rings in a Midtown phone box. For once, someone is calling Karen. It's the Chief of Staff. They need her in the Situation Room."
- E4: no words; the boom bap carries it.
- E5: "The President stands up. 'Karen... the fight back has begun. We need you on our side.'"

## Draft B: the public has been calling all along

As A, but E2 ends on a payoff line for the whole film:
- E2: "...The Chief of Staff says they need to get the public on their side. The public, as it happens, has been trying to get through for weeks."
- E3: "A few hours later, the phone in the Midtown box rings. Karen picks up. It's the Chief of Staff..."

## Draft C: lean, the ladder does the joke

- E1: "In the back of the Beast, the President asks to speak to his manager."
- E2: no words: the rung lights to a single phone ring.
- E2b: "The Chief of Staff has one answer: get the public on our side."
- E3: "A few hours later, a phone box in Midtown rings."
- E5: "'Karen. The fight back has begun. We need you on our side.'"

## Checklist rows run (story-craft build mode)

- Spine: but/therefore holds (the President is sacked, *therefore* he escalates, *but* the top of the ladder has no
  answer except "the public", *therefore* they ring Karen).
- Plant and payoff: "I want to speak to your manager" (the film's opening) pays off as the President's own move; the
  ladder (Act 2's device) pays off with one rung more; the phone that only ever rang out now rings in.
- Closing image answers the opening: the film opens on Karen ringing up; it ends on someone ringing her.
- Moral never stated in the narration (the end card carries it, by Kai's choice).
