## Act 1 — Meet Karen (the bait)

- **1.1 The zoom-in** — Manhattan aerial, drop into the office building, find Karen mid-flow being awful.
- **1.2 Karen at work** — two quick office beats where the narration and the visuals say opposite things.
- **1.3 The fish lunch** — the microwave, and her condescending to the cleaner about the spot she missed.
- **1.3b Inside the microwave** — the view flips to the cleaner's side: *what a knob.*
- **1.4a Called in** — the manager walks her to the meeting room; they sit, laptop on her lap.
- **1.4b "Look at your laptop"** — his odd, awkward ask.
- **1.4c Into the screen** — her POV tilts down, the lid opens, the screen swallows the frame.
- **1.4d Sean AI fires her** — the UI, the 8-bit moniker, *"I now run the HR department."*
- **1.4e She turns the laptop round** — one gesture; the narrator supplies *"can I speak to your manager?"*
- **1.4f His reaction** — webcam POV: a small recoil, then irritation. He's been fired too.
- **1.4g Karen's side-eye** — deadpan, judging, the camera pushing slowly into her eyes.
- **1.5a The bar** — hard cut to night, Susan consoling her, two seconds.
- **1.5b The Polaroids** — ten half-second stills, both of them getting steadily destroyed.
- **1.6 The phone in the river** — she drops it; *we* see where it went, she won't.
- **1.7a Karen wakes** — hangover, no memory, no phone.
- **1.7b Susan wakes** — the same shot, mirrored, in her bed.
- **1.7c The call** — the phone box: *"Yesterday you got sacked. By an AI."*
- **1.7d The pep talk** — *"Then get ringing."* The hinge of the film.
