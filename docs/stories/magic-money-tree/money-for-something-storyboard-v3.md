**What this pass of research added to round 10's rules** (web, and `docs/cinematography/frame.md`):

- Quality words ("4k", "perfect lighting", "high definition") are what bring the plastic look, so
  none are used. The capture line names a real, poor camera instead
  ([Pixova](https://www.pixova.io/blog/how-to-make-ai-images-look-less-like-ai), a blog; one source).
- Broad, even light is what makes skin look like plastic. Every plate has one hard source and a
  dark side ([note.com](https://note.com/monophyllaea/n/n80ba3017ed9d?hl=en), a blog; one source).
- Full sentences that name subject, setting, light and camera, not keyword lists
  ([Google Cloud's Nano Banana guide](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/ultimate-prompting-guide-for-nano-banana)).
- New in these prompts: "Nothing is retouched or smoothed" in the capture line. Whether it does
  anything was not tested against a prompt without it.
- From the cinematography file: every frame has a near layer, one place for the eye to rest, and a
  named light with its falloff. The two adverts and the trench each keep one small bright region
