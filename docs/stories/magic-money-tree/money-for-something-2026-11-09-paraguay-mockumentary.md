## The shape of every short (15–25 s)

1. **REAL (4–6 s): what's happening.** The real clip, credited on screen to the uploader, with one plain-fact line
   (presenter voice-over in a straight news read, or a caption).
2. **HARD CUT → REMADE (8–15 s): the joke.** The same moment recreated in Flow, matched to the real clip's camera
   (vertical phone, night, handheld), with the presenter (`pp2-06-a`) in the middle of it, mic out, taking the piss.
   Real people (the president, Cubas) are men in suits with faces **pixelated "for legal reasons"**: the caption says
   so. Real and AI meet only at the cut, never in one frame.
3. **On top of both:** the BadCode bug in the corner, cheap lower-thirds, half-second meme flashes on the punchlines
   (our own Flow stills, never copied memes of real people).

🔴 **Using the real clip in the edit is amber** (no free licence exists, `…-paraguay-footage.md`). The route is UK fair
dealing (quotation / reporting current events): short, credited, commentary on it. Defensible, not safe (a court ruled
8 s commercial clips unfair in 2016), and YouTube may claim it. **Jack and Kai decide before anything is posted.**
Backup per short: ask the uploader (Nación Media covers Universo 970 and Hoy), or replace the real clip with a
headline card.
