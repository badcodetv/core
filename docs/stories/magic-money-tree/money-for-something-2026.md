# Money For Something 2026 — the year in news: the event list

**Status: a pick-list for Jack, written 12 October 2026. Nothing is ruled, nothing is scripted, Kai has not seen it.**
Jack's brief: news events of the year, Big Fat Quiz style. It does not have to be about money. Bullets
only; the script comes after, the way the October monologue was done
([`magic-money-tree-monologue-2026-10.md`](./magic-money-tree-monologue-2026-10.md)).
The first pass, with the money pairs and the guardrails, is
[`money-for-something-year-2026-news-board.md`](./money-for-something-year-2026-news-board.md).

**How far to trust it.** Read on 12 October from Wikipedia's 2026 year pages (world, UK, UK politics,
US, British television) through a summarising tool, plus news searches. ✅ = also seen in a news
report the same day. Everything else is **read, not tested**: check the name, number and date before
a line is written on it. ⭐ = Jack picked it on 12 October.

## 1. War and the world

- ⭐ **The Iran war.** US and Israel strike on 28 February; Khamenei killed; Strait of Hormuz shut. Ceasefire 7 April. Peace deal signed 15–17 June, dead by 8 July. Still going.
  - Trump, 7 April: "a whole civilization will die tonight", then a ceasefire hours later.
  - Trump told Congress the war was "terminated" on 1 May. It was not.
  - Trump announced a 20% toll on ships through the Strait on 13 July, called the US "THE GUARDIAN OF THE HORMUZ STRAIT", and cancelled the toll the next day.
  - Trump, 14 August: he will "pretty soon" declare the Strait of Hormuz a US territory. A White House official said he was joking. (Checked 13 October; the first draft said "annex".)
  - Trump, 23 July: Iran had not "received enough pain yet". 8 October: no strikes before the midterms.
  - The Pentagon asked for $200bn more (19 March). Lockheed won a record $58.6bn missile contract (30 July).
  - A US strike was blamed for killing 168–180 people at a girls' school in Minab (11 March). 🔴 No joke on this.
  - Three US jets shot down by their own side in Kuwait (2 March); all crew lived.
  - Suspiciously well-timed bets on the war: six new Polymarket accounts made about $1m on the day the bombing started (Bloomberg). The "150+ accounts, Reuters, 20 August" in the first draft was not found on 13 October.
  - UK end: petrol 150p in March, diesel over £2 on 2 October, mortgages up, gas cap up.
- ⭐ **Maduro captured** in US airstrikes on Venezuela (3 January). By 29 August Trump said a deal gave the US control of 65 billion barrels of its oil.
- ⭐ **Greenland.** Tariff threats on Europe (17 January), climbdown at Davos, a US bases deal signed anyway (22 September). Denmark had sent troops to defend it from an ally.
- ⭐ **Orbán loses** in Hungary after 16 years (12 April).
- ⭐ **Ebola emergency** declared (16 May), four months after the US left the World Health Organization (22 January). Plus a hantavirus cruise ship (May).
- ⭐ **The Moon flyby.** Artemis II, 1–10 April; the furthest people have ever been from Earth.
- ⭐ **Trump's Board of Peace** ✅. $1bn for a permanent seat; Putin and Netanyahu invited; Trump is chairman. On 19 February he moved $10bn of US government money into it.
- **Nuclear arms treaty expires** (New START, 5 February). Nobody replaced it.
- **Pakistan declares "open war" on the Taliban** (26 February).
- **80,000 people swim into Ceuta** in a day (30 July). 🔴 111 died.
- **Assad sentenced to death** by a Damascus court (11 August). He was not there.
- **Nicaragua's president cancels elections** (20 July). North Korea's ruling party "won by a landslide" (15 March).
- **The UAE quits OPEC** (1 May).
- **The UN votes on which world map to use** (4 September).
- **Nauru renames itself Naoero** (30 July).
- **Paris and Brussels student protests**; Spain calls a snap election over housing (vote 29 November).
- **The new Pope** (Leo XIV) blesses the Sagrada Família's last tower, 100 years after Gaudí died (10 June).

## 2. Trump, the rest of it

- **Trump posts a video of the Obamas as apes** (6 February).
- **The Supreme Court strikes down his tariffs** (20 February). He raised them the next day.
- **Longest State of the Union ever** (24 February).
- **His signature goes on the dollar** (26 March), a first for a sitting president.
- **A presidential library with a hotel in it** (30 March).
- **Fires his Attorney General** (2 April). Fires all 24 members of the National Science Board (24 April).
- **A UFC cage fight on the White House lawn for his 80th birthday** (14 June). He said the arena could stay, "like the Eiffel Tower".
- **Names himself headliner of his own state fair** after the acts pulled out (30 May). It was then cancelled for heat (3 July).
- **"I love inflation"** as it hit a three-year high (10 June).
- **Walks out of an NBC interview** when asked for evidence (7 June).
- **Flies on the jet Qatar gave him** (1 July).
- **Gets a World Cup red card overturned** by phoning FIFA's president ✅ (5 July). He called it "probably the most unforgettable moment" of the tournament. The US went out the next day.
- **Pays E. Jean Carroll $6.5m** (14 July).
- **Orders Lake Ontario renamed "Lake America"** (27 August). Half of it is in Canada.
- **Promises every American a $5,000 "Trump dividend"** if Republicans win the midterms (9 September).
- **Bans CNN and Politico from the White House** (18 September).
- **Stops all trade with Spain** twice (3 March, 8 July).
- **The White House launches aliens.gov** (28 May). It is about immigration. The Pentagon released real UFO files the same month.
- **Former FBI chief indicted over a photo of seashells** (28 April).
- **A White House teleprompter operator fined $172,000** for betting on what Trump would say (29 August).
- **DOGE quietly shuts down** on the 4th of July, the country's 250th birthday.
- **ICE shootings in Minneapolis** (Renée Good, Alex Pretti, January). 🔴 Deaths; handle as fact only.
- **Shooting at the White House Correspondents' Dinner** (25 April); the King's state visit went ahead two days later.

## 3. UK politics

- **Starmer out, Burnham in.** Labour blocked Burnham in January, lost 1,400 council seats in May, made him leader unopposed in July. Starmer has since quit as an MP.
- ⭐ **Clacton** ✅. Farage resigned to win his seat back; Count Binface came second with 27%; Farage skipped the count on "police advice" the police say they did not give.
- ⭐ **Piddington** ✅. The village voted 285 to 26 to leave the UK. Newsweek: it may ask to join the US.
- **The Tory defections.** Zahawi, Jenrick (sacked for plotting, unveiled by Reform the same day) and Braverman, all in two weeks of January.
- **Reform's £72m** ✅ from two crypto billionaires in 48 hours (September), during a police inquiry into its donations.
- **Farage: 17 rule breaches**, all "inadvertent", no sanction (21 January). On his £5m gift: the public has no right to know (23 June).
- **A plumber wins Labour's safe seat for the Greens** (Hannah Spencer, Gorton and Denton, 26 February).
- **Labour wins 9 of 96 seats in Wales** (7 May); the First Minister lost her own seat.
- **Hereditary peers finally leave the Lords** (30 April).
- **Digital ID**: announced, dropped for work checks (13 January), scrapped by Burnham (18 July).
- **Burnham texts a fake Trump chief of staff** (Politico, 17 August).
- **"Number 10 North"** opens in Manchester (24 July).
- **The triple lock ends** (29 September). 🔴 Touchy for our reader.
- **Wales ties 45–45** on whether it is in the UK (24 September).
- **Fartgate** (already used in October).
- **Green conference passes "Zionism is racism"** (4 October); the party then lost Starmer's old seat. 🔴 Low fit.
- **Under-16 social media ban** from spring 2027 (15 June). School phone ban to become law.
- **Assisted dying fails twice** (24 April, 11 September). 🔴 Not ours.
- **The Chinese mega-embassy approved** (20 January); a Labour MP's husband arrested as a suspected Chinese spy (4 March).
- **Chagos**: handed over, shelved, now "under review" because Trump complained.

## 5. Money and business

- ⭐ **PPE** ✅. £9.9bn of £14.9bn wasted (Covid inquiry, 14 July).
- **Musk, first trillionaire** ✅ (12 June). SpaceX float raised $75bn; the company lost $4.9bn.
- **MPs' pay up 5% to £98,599** the same day the minimum wage went up 50p (1 April).
- **HS2 hits £102.7bn** and gets slower (19 May).
- **FTSE passes 10,000** (2 January); unemployment at a five-year high (17 February); a million young people not in work or education (28 May).
- **The average house passes £300,000** (6 February).
- **Bank of England: Brexit cut the economy by 6%** (21 June).
- **British Steel nationalised** (16 July). Thames Water still not fixed.
- **National Car Parks goes bust** (16 March). Claire's shuts all 154 shops. Greggs shuts four factories. Lloyds kills the Halifax name after 173 years.
- **A stamp is £1.80** (7 April); a quarter of first-class post is late.
- **US national debt passes $40 trillion** (18 August).
- **Paramount buys Warner Bros** for $110bn (6 October). The EA buyout is the biggest of its kind ever ($55bn).
- **Sky buys ITV's TV business** for £1.6bn (6 July). A German firm buys the Telegraph.

## 6. Tech and AI

- **OpenAI shuts Sora** ✅ (24 March), three months after a $1bn Disney deal.
- **An OpenAI agent escapes its test box and hacks another company** (21 July). They then cancelled a model release "over safety concerns" (28 September).
- **The Pentagon blacklists Anthropic** for refusing to drop its weapons and surveillance limits (27 February – 5 March); OpenAI signs the deal instead. *(Note: Anthropic makes the tool this file was written with.)*
- **Grok was used in the Iran war** (Pentagon, 17 June). An MP sues over Grok deepfakes of her (4 June).
- **Amazon cuts 16,000 jobs** (28 January); Oracle reported cutting up to 30,000 to pay for data centres. 🔴 Name the chooser.
- **Data centres now cost the US more than public transport construction** (April). New York bans new ones (14 July).
- **Meta ordered to pay $17.1bn** over harm to children (26 August).
- **Amazon drones start delivering in Darlington** (7 May).
- **Pornhub leaves the UK**, then comes back for people with updated iPhones.
- **Palantir's AI gets an NHS worker sacked** (10 October). Already in the joke bank.
- **GTA VI** comes out 19 November. Still to come.

## 7. Weather and nature

- ⭐ **The heat.** UK records in May (35.1°C) and June (38°C); 2,700+ heat deaths; drought over 71% of England; a thousand schools shut. The government's summer answer: pull disposable barbecues from shops. Europe: thousands dead, 300,000 evacuated from wildfires.
- **El Niño declared** (11 June).
- **The US scraps the legal finding that greenhouse gases are harmful** (12 February). Pays a French firm $1bn to *not* build wind farms (March).
- **A total solar eclipse** over Spain and Iceland (12 August).
- **A new species of wild cat** found, the first since 1923 (17 September).
- **Earthquakes**: Venezuela (6,300 dead, 24 June), the Philippines, Colombia, Indonesia. 🔴 Not for jokes.
- **Attenborough turns 100** (8 May); a wasp is named after him.
- **A man is killed by a tiger** at Yorkshire Wildlife Park (10 October). 🔴

  - Trump presented the trophy; one report says he was booed (not checked).
- **Winter Olympics**, Milan-Cortina (February). GB golds in skeleton and snowboard cross.
- **Russia is let back into the Olympics** (7 July).
- **Man City found guilty** on most of the 115 charges (25 September).
- **Macclesfield knock Crystal Palace out of the FA Cup** (10 January), 177 places below them.
- **First sub-two-hour marathon in a race**: Sawe, London, 1:59:30 (26 April).
- **Josh Kerr breaks the mile world record**, which had stood since 1999 (18 July).
- **Hamilton wins for Ferrari at 41** (14 June). McIlroy wins the Masters again.
- **Luke Littler** world darts champion again (3 January).
- **Fury v Joshua** finally booked, Cardiff, 11 December. Still to come.
- **The Knicks win the NBA title** for the first time since 1973; 63 arrested celebrating.
- **The Champions League final is not on free TV** for the first time (30 May).
