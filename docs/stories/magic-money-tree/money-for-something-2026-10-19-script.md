# Money For Something — 19 October 2026: the script

**Status: first draft, written 19 October 2026 at Jack's request ("make a money for something, 19th oct version").
Nothing here is FINAL. Kai has not seen it.**

**How this draft differs from the process.** [`money-for-something-process.md`](./money-for-something-process.md) says
Jack writes the jokes and the last column stays empty. This time he asked for a script, so every row carries a **draft
line (mine)** for him to keep, change or bin. The "what to joke about" column is there so he can write his own instead.
Where he writes his own, his wins.

Shape: the October set, host only, plate B (television on beer crates), one clip = 8 s, one speaker, about 20 words.
Cold open, fact first with the number, then the turn, a different exit word each beat. About **23 clips, roughly
3 minutes**. Rows marked *(optional)* can go if it runs long.

**Optional extras, briefed, no draft line yet** (use if Jack wants a different beat):

- **House prices.** Rightmove: asking prices cut on 3,000+ homes a day in September, most for 15 years; average cut nearly
  7% (~£21,000); mortgage rates at a three-year high. Angle: prices are falling and you still can't afford one.
- **Heating oil.** Up to 82% dearer since the Iran war began (energy minister Polly Billington); a Devon woman's 500 litres

  the website; Burnham has asked for a "manual trawl". Club found guilty of "systematically" breaking Premier League rules.

  No who-got-paid fact checked this week (water company money), so not briefed as a beat.
