# Facts about the job (not style advice)

- **What it is for:** a run of 10–15 second satirical meme videos (vertical and horizontal) about a bank collapse in Paraguay. The presenter is the recurring masked character who turns up in each one: in a crowd, at a desk, holding a sign, pointing at things. These stills pick his design; later the picked still becomes the reference for every shot and is animated by a video model.
- **"The money for something guy"** is the host of a low-budget comedy panel show. His earlier unmasked design: a dishevelled stand-up comic, crumpled black suit jacket, white shirt open at the collar, a glass of red wine in his hand, leaning on a battered wooden lectern on a cheap black-box TV set with an orange curtain and a lamp on a stand. Slightly guilty, sideways "who, me?" energy. Comic, not menacing.
- **The mask (fixed, it is the brand logo):** a knitted balaclava covering the whole head, with NO eye, nose or mouth openings at all. A large white ring is stitched around the whole face area. One small SOLID red triangle with rounded corners is stitched high inside the ring over the wearer's LEFT eye: facing the camera, that is on the RIGHT side of the picture, at about two o'clock on the ring, never in the centre. Only the ring and the triangle are on the face; nothing else. The balaclava's own colour is free.
- **"Like the other ones":** a gang of masked men already exists in another film: knitted balaclavas in different colours (purple, green, red, navy, mustard, grey), each with the cream-white ring and the red triangle at two o'clock, worn with sharp suits and black leather gloves. This presenter belongs to the same family but is his own man.
- **No skin, ever:** gloves on both hands, cuffs over the wrists, the neck of the balaclava tucked into a collar or roll-neck. Nothing human shows, so he could be a person or a machine underneath. But no robot features: no metal, no lights, no seams, no visor. Just cloth and gloves.
- **The gloves:** Jack mentioned "gloves with the badcode logo on". Some designs may put a small version of the logo (the white ring with the red triangle at two o'clock) on the back of each glove; others keep plain gloves. Do one each way at least.
- **Wine glass:** he has no mouth, so a glass of red wine he can never drink is part of the joke; optional per design.
- Readable words anywhere in the frame are unwanted (signs, papers, labels stay blank or illegible).
- The picture must work as a start frame for a video model later: head and gloved hands clearly visible, head not tiny in frame.
- Photoreal, not illustration. 16:9.

# The engine

Google Flow, image model Nano Banana 2. One prompt per image, plain text. Prompts end with the word "Thanks." (house habit of the people sending them; harmless).
