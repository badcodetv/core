# Audio Profile
The BadCode street reporter: a flat, courteous English local-news correspondent. Unhurried, never raises his voice, never laughs, never sounds in on the joke. Treats absurd chaos as a routine assignment.

## Scene:
Night, outdoors, at a loud street protest; he speaks close to a stick microphone, slightly out of breath from jogging, but composed.

## Sample Context:
Deadpan TV reporting. Even pitch, clipped English sentences, real silences between phrases. The swear word gets exactly the same polite tone as the question. About two words a second.
