# Facts (sourced; still moving)

- Ueno Bank (Paraguay): young, fast-grown; ~3 million customers, about 60% of the country's adults, signed up with supermarket rebates; for over a million it was their first bank account.
- Seized by the central bank 2–3 Nov 2026: loans to the owners' own companies, poorly documented; expenses booked as assets. App, cards, branches dead.
- ~US$800m of state money inside, including pension-fund money; 32,000 retirees paid through it.
- President Santiago Peña was a shareholder in the bank's holding company until 2025, and made the bank's former chairman governor of the central bank (its regulator).
- Thu 5 Nov: Peña resigned; Congress refused the resignation and impeached him 78–0. Fri: Senate removed him 44–0; he was charged (not convicted) and put under house arrest.
- Thu 5 Nov evening: Paraguayo "Payo" Cubas, ex-presidential candidate and ex-senator, tried to join the protest; the crowd booed him, shoved him, threw water and ran him out, chanting "¡Fuera!" and "oportunista" (opportunist). Why: senators elected on his lists had crossed over to Peña's side.
- Four days from the bank's seizure to the president's removal: the public won.
