# Judge: Paraguay short, clean-room round 1

Read only: brief-facts.md, research-comedy.md, research-engine.md, script-w1/w2/w3.json. 2026-11-09.

## Ranking

### 1. W2, "Sir, Quick Question": the spine
- **Funny:** the only one built on the brief's own premise, a reporter taking the piss *with a microphone*. It has a deadpan straight man, the held-out mic with nobody filling it (the pause), and a therefore/but chain: the bank dies, THEREFORE a politician turns up, BUT the crowd carries him off, THEREFORE the question goes unanswered. "What's your plan for the pensioners?" asked to a man riding on palms is the best gap joke of the three. `PENSIONER_PLAN.EXE IS NOT RESPONDING [WAIT] [WAIT]` is the best flash.
- **UK viewer:** yes. Pensioners, savers and a politician dodging a question need no Paraguay.
- **Ending:** "bank owners don't get to inspect banks" is the right fix, but it is said flat, as a statement. The second suit with a clipboard is a good image, but it costs a clip and can read as "the cycle repeats", which is the cynicism trap.
- **Rules:** clean. It punches up: the woman in the crowd is the competent one.
- **Faults:** six generated videos, about three times the budget. The woman pointing while passing legs overhead is two actions on an extra, which is morph bait. The arrow labelled OUT, the map pin and "Thank you. Very clear." all explain jokes that already landed. "SIR, THIS IS A BANK RUN" is a good meme shape, but the bank was seized, not run on, so it is slightly false.

### 2. W3, "Return Him to the Wild": the best lines
- **Funny:** the funniest single lines. "The herd is not fucking having it", whispered, is the biggest laugh in the round. `OPPORTUNISTUS SUITUS / STATUS: NOT ENDANGERED` echoes the crowd's real chant ("oportunista") without naming anyone. "Catch. And release." is good too.
- **Faults:** the narration voice sidelines the mic. The reporter crouches and whispers instead of putting the mic in a face, so it is less mockumentary and more nature doc. Calling the protesters "the herd" edges toward punching down at the crowd. The ending ("never hire the fox to inspect the henhouse") is a stock proverb, and the index card then explains it again: the two together are preachy. Crouching among legs is an extras-morph risk. Four videos plus two stills.

### 3. W1, "Subbed Off Before Kick-Off": the best ending, buried
- **Funny:** football is a strong UK register, and `HOME 3,000,000 – 0 AWAY` is good. Every joke comes with its own explanation, though: red card twice, a VAR flash, a fourth-official board, a league table, a tactics whiteboard. It is a list of football references, an "and then" chain, not a story.
- **Ending:** **"The ref can't own a team"** is the best way-out line in the round: concrete, deadpan, true to the facts (the shareholder-regulator link), and it lands instantly for a UK viewer. "Bank regulator. Bank owner. Pick one." is good too.
- **Faults:** the most text on screen of the three (summary, lower-third, sign, board, monitor, table, whiteboard), which fails sound-off reading at 15–20 s. "The gaffer getting sacked" tangles the man in the clip with the president. Five videos plus two stills.

## What the final takes from each
- **From W2 (spine):** the polite mic question, the pensioner line, the held pause, the .EXE flash (made in post, not generated), the slow nod, and "So a politician turned up to help."
- **From W3:** "not fucking having it" (now said by the polite reporter, not a whisperer, about the crowd's verdict, not the crowd), the OPPORTUNISTUS SUITUS species card as the hard-cut punch, and "I'M HERE TO LISTEN" as the literal cliché on the sign.
- **From W1:** the ending line, "the ref can't own the team", plus "Bank owner. Bank inspector. Pick one." as its sound-off card. The four-days fact stays as the agency proof, so the short ends on "the public won" rather than "they're all the same".
- **Cut:** the arrow, the map pin, "Thank you. Very clear.", the second suit, all the football props, the herd, the fox, and both whiteboard/index-card explanations.
- **Changed:** W2's lilo still stays as a meme flash, but it now reads `OUT OF OFFICE` instead of the inaccurate "bank run". The end moves to the streamer desk full-frame, so the last frame rhymes with the facecam in frame 1 and the short loops.
