# Magic Money Tree research

**Evidence library, not competing story canon.** The documentary's only current storyboard is
[The Future He Never Saw](../storyboard.md), chosen 11 September 2026.

Start with the [completed September investigation](./documentary-austerity-emergency-investigation-2026-09-11.md).
Its audit and correction register qualify earlier files. Where an older claim conflicts with a
correction, use the corrected account; do not treat an old “binding” or “verified” label as enough.
Research documents preserve the record of investigation rather than silently rewriting every draft.
The September report's proposed full-film and vertical structures have themselves been superseded
by the canonical storyboard; its economics and evidence remain useful.
