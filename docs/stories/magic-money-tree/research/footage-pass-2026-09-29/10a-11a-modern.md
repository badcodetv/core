## 1. Licence findings

- **Parliament portraits.** Not verified on parliament.uk (403 Cloudflare from curl and WebFetch). A WebSearch summary said portraits are CC BY 3.0, Lords portraits released 2018: read, not fetched. Commons records the May portrait as "CC BY 3.0", credit https://members.parliament.uk/member/8/portrait. Members API Portrait endpoint 404 for Osborne (id 1458) and Sunak (id 4483); Mone (id 4544) thumbnail is a 2.5 KB PNG that looks like a placeholder.
- **Number 10 Flickr.** Red today. Live licence fields: photo 55554342741 = `https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/`; photo 49948534748 (Sunak briefing 29 May 2020) = `https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/2.0/`. Commons holds that same photo as "CC BY 2.0" (bot check passed March 2023). Any Number 10 Flickr file on Commons has a provenance conflict: amber at best.
- **gov.uk.** Verified: "All content is available under the Open Government Licence v3.0, except where otherwise stated © Crown copyright" (2017 election statement page, 23 March 2020 address page, Budget June 2010 page, Contracts Finder). The 23 March 2020 page has no video embed.
- **Number 10 YouTube.** Three 2020 briefing pages fetched (Pc8yOc0mFUU, 5i0pIRmjdm0, eH_dhcS6yIQ): no Creative Commons licence field, so standard YouTube licence. Whether OGL covers the videos: unconfirmed, no statement found.
- **Bank of England (https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/legal).** Not OGL for text or images. Quote: "You may … download, display or print the Resources for personal use or internal use within an individual organisation for non-commercial purposes. Requests for further authorisation … should be addressed to: Head of Communications Division". OGL applies only to the Database (data series) and Rulebook. Bank Flickr photo 54979808020 is CC BY-NC-ND 4.0: red. Consequence: typeset quotes need fair dealing or written permission; the Bank "typically grants permission for non-commercial re-use" and we are commercial. Article URLs load (200): https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/quarterly-bulletin/2014/q1/money-creation-in-the-modern-economy (14 March 2014, McLeay, Radia, Thomas) and https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/monetary-policy/quantitative-easing ("What is quantitative easing?").
- **DVIDS (https://www.dvidshub.net/about/copyright).** Notice now branded Department of War. Quote: "DoW VI may not be used in a manner that could imply endorsement of … any political party or candidate for partisan political office." Required disclaimer: "The appearance of U.S. Department of War (DoW) visual information does not imply or constitute DoW endorsement." US government works uncopyrighted, but some items are third-party. Green for generic PPE, American only; nothing fetched from DVIDS itself.
- **Open Justice Licence v2.0 (https://caselaw.nationalarchives.gov.uk/open-justice-licence).** Permits "copy, publish, distribute and transmit the Information", including commercial exploitation. Requires attribution "Contains information licensed under the Open Justice - Licence v2.0.", no misrepresentation, regard for the dignity of the courts. Computational analysis needs separate permission (not relevant).
- **Contracts Finder.** Footer: OGL v3.0 "except where otherwise stated".
