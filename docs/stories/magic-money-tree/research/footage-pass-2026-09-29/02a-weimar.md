# Scene 02a, Germany 1923: footage sweep report

TL;DR: Scene 02a can be pictured free with still photographs (public-domain Bain, Meurisse and Rol press photos, plus Reichsbank note scans), but I found no free moving footage of the 1923 inflation or the Ruhr. The only moving footage found is French (INA), in copyright and unpriced. No live price could be fetched from any paid vendor.

## 1. Verdict
Free routes cover priorities 2, 3 and 4 (Ruhr occupation photos, high-resolution photos, banknote scans) with slow push-ins. Priority 1 (moving footage) is not available free.

I could not read loc.gov, British Pathe, Pond5 or Getty directly. Every request returned HTTP 403 (Cloudflare challenge or a bot block). LoC rights text was taken from the Commons file pages that mirror LoC, plus a WebSearch snippet of the loc.gov page.

## 3. Top 3 picks
1. Bain Essen set (#2) with "In a Berlin Bank" (#1): LoC, "no known restrictions", 5000px, covers the occupation and the bank.
2. Meurisse bread crowd (#4) plus the Rol Essen set (#3): wage-queue and chalked-prices feel, plus more occupation shots. Confirm the rights on the Gallica pages first.
3. NMAH Reichsbanknote scans (#5) and the CC0 20-milliard note (#6): clean high-resolution notes for the "more notes" line.

## 4. Dead ends
- curl and WebFetch to loc.gov (`/pictures/search`, `/search`, `/item/...?fo=json`, `cdn.loc.gov`) returned 403 or a 301 to `tile.loc.gov`. Cloudflare "Just a moment..." from this machine.
- britishpathe.com (`/licensing/` and search) returned 403 via curl and WebFetch.
- pond5.com search returned 403 ("Please enable..."); gettyimages.co.uk returned 403 (ERROR page).
- archive.org NARA search "germany OR ruhr OR inflation OR reichsbank" gave 144 hits, all WWII or later. The 1919-1926 date-scoped NARA search returned only `gov.archives.arc.88967`.
- Prelinger scoped search found nothing for the period.
- `bottrop1925_1`, `bottrop1925_2`, `kreisfest1925` on archive.org carry `by-nc-nd` licences: red (hard kill), also wrong year.
- `stuttgart.-lollizei`: 2020 modern political rant video, unrelated.
- `DeutschlandsHyperinflation1923`: text PDF from a personal-email uploader, not footage.
- Commons `filetype:video` searches returned nothing relevant. Categories "Videos of the Weimar Republic" and "Videos of the Occupation of the Ruhr" empty. Only video hit: `Die Flamme (1923).webm`, a feature film, off topic.
- Bundesarchiv Bild 102-xxxxx and 146-xxxxx photos exist (wallpapering, money transport, bread queues), all CC-BY-SA 3.0 de. Noted only, as instructed.
- Europeana's other hit: a 1958 Hans Luther interview, CC-BY-NC-ND (red, off topic).
- Not tried: Smithsonian's own site, Deutsche Digitale Bibliothek, filmothek.bundesarchiv.de. WebSearch calls used: 5 of 15.
