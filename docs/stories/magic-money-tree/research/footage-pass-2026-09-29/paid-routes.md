# Paid footage routes: what £100-£200 could buy (fetched 2026-09-29)

**Verdict: the realistic buy within £100-£200 is one editorial still or a few Pond5 clips. No British 1945-55 film fits.**

- British Pathé's cheapest online licence is £275 + VAT, above the £200 push limit. It rises to £565 + VAT for YouTube plus our own site.
- The only UK-archive item that fits is an Alamy still: £80 or £170 for one image (£170 is the documentary tier).
- Pond5's £29 clips are the only moving footage under £100. Their licence has a political-use clause that could bar our use.
- I did not check whether the Pond5 clips are genuinely free at source (the seller says "public domain"), so check that before paying.
- I bought nothing and downloaded no media. Two Alamy prices are the ones I read on live pages (item 1 below).

## 2. The five subjects

1. **British moving footage 1945-55.**
   - Pathé holds it: VE Day 93856 (7:00) and 94311 (4:04); NHS story 72340 (price not checked); prefabs 78496 (1:13); Attlee 69041, 67545 and 95981 (prices not checked).
   - The cheapest confirmed route is £275 + VAT for a film of about a minute, single site. That is over budget, and it needs a "non-commercial entity" and no sale.
   - The Pathé shop showed no in/out selection, so the price is for the whole film.
   - Pond5 £29 is the only sub-£100 route. It is editorial-only, and it may be resold public-domain material, so it may buy nothing that a free source doesn't.
2. **High-resolution still.**
   - Alamy 458482108 is a 1948 nurse-training photo at 4979 x 3924 px. That is above 2,000 px, unlike the roughly 800 px Commons copies.
   - It costs £80 at Standard or £170 at Editorial Enhanced (the documentary tier).
   - The council-housing photo I found is only 1750 px wide and is about the housing shortage, not a family moving in. No true council-house-building image was found.
3. **1930s dole and Jarrow.** Pathé 97696 (Jarrow 1936, 0:35) is £275 / £565. 50460 "The Queues - Unemployment" is dated 1923, not the 1930s. 96206 "Dole Queue" is dated 1947.
4. **German hyperinflation 1923.** Pathé 101679 "Trocquer and Maginot (1923)", an item on industry and inflation in Germany. I did not price it.
5. **BBC Question Time (2017) and Baroness Mone (2023) clips.**
   - No price exists in anything I could reach.
   - Getty returned 0 results for "BBC Question Time Theresa May nurse 2017".
   - Getty's 12 "magic money tree" results included no Question Time clip.
   - `bbcmotiongallery.com` gave ERR_CONNECTION_RESET.
   - I guessed a `bbcstudios.com` licensing URL and got a 404; it may be a wrong guess. The Baroness Mone clip was not searched at any vendor.

## 3. Traps: clauses that bar our use

- **Storyblocks** (`/license/individual-license`): "exclude media to be used in Broadcast, Television, or OTT platforms, or in connection with political campaigns or other political purposes, unless explicitly set forth in the subscription plan that you selected". Its "No Sensitive Use" clause (2.5) covers "a subject that would be unflattering or unduly controversial to a reasonable person".
- **Pond5** (`/legal/license`, "Sensitive Use"): "you may not use any Content in any context that would be unflattering or unduly controversial to a reasonable person, including use related to the promotion, advertisement or endorsement of any political party, candidate, or elected official, or in connection with any political policy or viewpoint". That clause says "use of Editorial Content in accordance with Section 7 would not be prohibited by this section". Section 7 limits editorial content to "events or topics that are newsworthy or of general public interest" and bars "endorsement, promotion, advertorial, or other commercial Production". Whether a political-argument film fits Section 7 is unresolved, so only Pond5 can rule on it.
- **Shutterstock** (`/license`): the bar is "in a political context, such as the promotion, advertisement or endorsement of any party, candidate, or elected official, or in connection with any political policy or viewpoint". It sits in a clause about depicting a Model, so it may not apply to newsreel. Editorial items add "Editorial Use Only. Single use in context."
- **Getty** (`/eula`): "you may not use content marked 'editorial' ... for any commercial, promotional, advertorial, endorsement, advertising, gambling/betting/gaming uses, or merchandising purpose." Prices are hidden behind an account, so I could not test the cost.
- **Huntley** (`/includes/licensingterms.html`):
  - "These terms do not constitute a licence of the Intellectual Property Rights in any Content. These must be obtained from the owner of such rights."
  - Clause 6.9: the customer "may only amend the Content by: (a) altering the colourisation; (b) altering the aspect rations". Clause 6.10 bars other changes without consent.
  - Under 5.3-5.5, usage above the amount paid for is charged at "triple the amount of such shortfall".
- **Pathé:** the licence is limited to a "NON-COMMERCIAL ENTITY", and the footage must not be "available to buy, rent, or download". The file arrives as SD 720x576 "with the British Pathé logo visible in the top right-hand corner of the screen". The logo is on the screen for the whole clip.
- **Alamy:** the terms bar "any way that creates or implies an endorsement or commercial association" (in the sports-data clause) and any use "in a pornographic, defamatory, fraudulent, lewd, obscene or otherwise illegal manner". Editorial images are "Available for Editorial use only." The page says "Restrictions stated before or at the time of purchase may limit the scope of permissible usage" (video page wording). I found no political bar.

## 4. Dead ends

- **Plain curl and WebFetch:** British Pathé, Getty, Pond5 and Shutterstock returned 403, or a Cloudflare, CloudFront or DataDome challenge page. TopFoto returned an "Attention Required! | Cloudflare" block. Alamy image pages returned a WAF 202 challenge with no body.
- **Pathé rate limit:** one direct navigation returned HTTP 429 with "Just a moment...". A retry about seven minutes later worked.
- **Getty:** prices are hidden ("Join today to view pricing"). `/pricing` returned a 404.
- **Shutterstock:** editorial pages show no price.
- **Huntley:** the price calculator needs a sign-in. Keyword searches for "VE Day", "prefabs" and "housing" (1940s) redirected to `noresults.asp`. That may be a search quirk rather than absent holdings, so I cannot say whether Huntley holds the subjects.
- **Science & Society:** search results need a click-through to reach an item; the price sits behind "Order Image".
- **Storyblocks:** I did not check for British 1940s archive.
- **CriticalPast and Footage Farm:** UNCONFIRMED. CriticalPast's homepage only says "royalty-free archival footage", and its about page returned a 404. I did not visit Footage Farm. I did not confirm they resell US government material.
- **BBC:** `bbcmotiongallery.com` gave ERR_CONNECTION_RESET, and I found no published floor price for a BBC clip.
