Strongest true line: *£203m of contracts, £122m of which bought 25 million gowns the NHS could never use, while £65m of profit left the company.*

## 1. Proven / court-established facts

- **The company.** PPE Medpro Ltd incorporated **12 May 2020**, £100 capital, owned on paper by **Anthony Page** (a Barrowman associate). No PPE trading history. Seven weeks from incorporation to contract.
- **The referral.** Mone emailed **Lord Agnew** on **8 May 2020** — four days *before* the company existed — offering PPE via "my team in Hong Kong"; the company entered the **VIP lane**. She also lobbied Michael Gove. (Established via FOI, revealed Nov 2021.)
- **The "no benefit" declaration.** Emails of **22 May 2020**: Mone told government she was not entitled to "any financial benefit."
- **The gowns failed.** DHSC rejected the 25m gowns **December 2020**; in storage since (~£8m storage costs). Testing of a 60-gown sample: **55 not sterile**.
- **The judgment.** *SSHSC v PPE Medpro* **[2025] EWHC 2486 (Comm)**, Mrs Justice Cockerill, **1 October 2025**: gowns "not, contractually speaking, sterile, or properly validated as being sterile"; no validated sterilisation process; missing Notified Body numbers. Repay **£121,999,219.20** by 15 Oct 2025. Storage costs not recovered. https://caselaw.nationalarchives.gov.uk/ewhc/comm/2025/2486
- **The insolvency.** PPE Medpro entered administration **30 September 2025 — the day before judgment**; missed the deadline; converted to **liquidation December 2025** (Judge Sebastian Prentis). Company held under £1m; ~**£600k** available against a £148m judgment debt plus a **£39m HMRC claim**. Prentis: the debt "has been incurred through the supply of defective equipment at a time of national crisis."
- **The VIP lane was unlawful** — *R (Good Law Project & EveryDoctor) v SSHSC* [2022] EWHC 46 (TCC), 12 Jan 2022: breached equal-treatment obligation. **But** relief refused ("highly likely" contracts would have been awarded anyway), and that case concerned **PestFix and Ayanda, not PPE Medpro**.

**The admission — BBC, *Sunday with Laura Kuenssberg*, 17 December 2023** (+ parallel Guardian interview): she **lied to the press** about involvement ("regrets" it, framed as protecting family); **Barrowman was ultimate beneficial owner**; she **stands to benefit from ~£60m** in his trusts. Simultaneously: "I don't honestly see there is a case to answer." Barrowman: they are a "scapegoat." After the Oct 2025 judgment: Mone called it a win for "the establishment"; Barrowman "a travesty of justice." Both deny wrongdoing.

## 4. The yacht — there are three vessels; get this right

**Barrowman's *Lady M*:** 38.9m (127ft) sailing yacht, Fitzroy Yachts 2006, Ed Dubois design, ex-*Mia Cara*. Bought **May 2021** by **LM Yachts Ltd** (IoM) — director **Anthony Page**, PPE Medpro's nominal owner. Reported price **~£5–6m**. Mone posted Instagram photos aboard **3–29 August 2021**. Context vessels: Barrowman **sold** a 187ft yacht for £17.5m (Oct 2023); Jan 2024 reports of an 87m, £50m *Vento* megayacht — **unconfirmed**, broker neither confirmed nor denied. Fire sale: ~£80m of assets (St Barts villa £63m→£41m, the yacht, £7.5m Cessna CJ4).

## 5. Legal state of play (2024–2026)

- **Criminal:** NCA investigation opened **May 2021** (suspected conspiracy to defraud, fraud by false representation, bribery — all denied). Homes raided 27 Apr 2022. June 2024: unnamed 46-year-old man arrested. **No charges as of latest reporting.** Gove and Hancock questioned (Nov 2023).
- **Asset restraint:** Jan 2024, ~**£75m** frozen under POCA restraint order, **consented to** by Mone and Barrowman.
- **Civil recovery:** June 2026 — liquidators (Interpath Advisory / Simmons & Simmons) filed High Court claims against **six individuals and five companies including Mone and Barrowman**, seeking the ~£122m personally. Neither was ever a director; claims turn on roles behind the corporate veil. **Live and unresolved — no personal judgment exists.** https://www.scottishfinancialnews.com/articles/interpath-pursues-mone-and-barrowman-in-bid-to-recover-ppe-medpro-millions
- **Parliament:** leave of absence from the Lords since Dec 2022; lost Conservative whip. After Oct 2025 ruling Badenoch said strip the peerage — but that needs an Act of Parliament; she remains a life peer.
- **Covid-19 Inquiry:** rejected her late application; will publish PPE Medpro findings only after any criminal proceedings conclude.

## 6. The wider VIP lane

- ~**493 suppliers** referred; **47–51** won contracts. Referred firms ~**10x** (Inquiry: 13x) more likely to win. VIP-lane contracts total **£1.7bn** (PAC, Feb 2022); ten senior Conservative referrers accounted for ~£1.6bn. Others: Uniserve £876m (Agnew), Randox £479m, PestFix £313m, Ayanda £252m, Meller Designs £164m (Gove), Excalibur £135.4m (Hancock).
- **Quality:** Spotlight on Corruption — **25 of 50** VIP companies supplied ~**£1bn** of unfit PPE = **59%** of VIP-lane money vs **17%** through the normal channel. NAO: 53% of 51 VIP suppliers unsuitable.
- **Write-offs:** **£8.7bn of £12.1bn** early PPE spend written off (£673m totally unusable, £2.6bn unsuitable for NHS, £750m expired, £4.7bn overpaid). Total write-downs ~£15bn over two years. Peak: 500+ lorry loads/month incinerated.
- **Covid-19 Inquiry Module 5 (14 July 2026):** UK spent ~£15bn on Covid PPE — **more than the rest of Europe combined** (65% of European PPE award value in 2020); ~£10bn write-downs; VIP lane a "misguided attempt" that "heightened the risk of abuse" — should never have happened and must never happen again. TI-UK: 135 contracts worth £15.3bn carried 3+ corruption red flags. £324m PPE fraud identified; ⚠️ "195 fraud referrals, zero prosecutions, only one supplier taken to court (PPE Medpro)" — verify against the primary report before quoting.

## 7. Writer's guardrails (mandatory)

1. **No criminal conviction exists. Nobody has been charged.** The £122m ruling is a civil contract judgment **against a company**, not a fraud finding against a person.
2. **Mone personally has not been found liable for anything by a court.** The liquidators' claim is filed, untested, unresolved.
3. **Safe to state flatly:** she referred the company into the VIP lane; told government she'd get no financial benefit; denied involvement for three years; admitted on the BBC (Dec 2023) that she lied and stands to benefit from ~£60m. Her own on-camera admission is the most defensible material.
4. **Attribute the money trail** to the Guardian's reporting, not to a court.
5. The yacht is ~£5–6m, and no court has found it was bought with PPE money — the case is **timing and common ownership** (LM Yachts' director = PPE Medpro's nominal owner). Strong enough without embellishment.
6. The VIP-lane "unlawful" ruling concerned PestFix/Ayanda, not PPE Medpro, and relief was refused.
