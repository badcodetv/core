# Paraguay special: real footage and copyright (research 2026-11-09)

Jack asked: "find the real footage that is copywright free, or a way around copywright like altering the video somehow."
Web research by a sub-agent, 9 Nov 2026. VERIFIED = licence/law page read; UNVERIFIED = not confirmed.

## Answer
- **No free-licensed footage of the Payo Cubas moment, or of the Ueno protests, was found.** Commons: nothing (VERIFIED).
  IP Paraguay (ip.gov.py): no licence notice, treat as all rights reserved (VERIFIED). YouTube CC filter: not checked
  (needs a browser; search "Payo Cubas" → Filters → Creative Commons, and check the uploader filmed it).
- **Altering a clip does not remove copyright** (mirror, recolour, speed, crop, rotoscope, AI style transfer): it is
  still a copy of a substantial part, and dodging Content ID only hides it.

## Routes, safest first
1. **Re-stage it** (our plan already): facts and news of the day are not protected (Paraguay Ley 1328/98 art. 8.3–8.4,
   VERIFIED). Masked presenter in an anonymous Flow crowd + an on-screen line ("5 Nov, Asunción: the crowd ran
   Payo Cubas out"). No clearance needed. Never generate Cubas.
2. **Ask permission.** Universo 970 and Hoy are both Nación Media (VERIFIED): one ask covers both
   (Av. Mariscal López 2948, Asunción, +595 21 603 400, UNVERIFIED as current). ABC TV bars commercial use and
   modification without written consent from Editorial Azeta S.A. (VERIFIED). Raúl Bogarín looks like an
   individual journalist: DM on X (UNVERIFIED). Ñandutí owner not found.
3. **4–6 s clip under UK fair dealing, credited** (CDPA s.30(1ZA) quotation; s.30(2) current events covers film;
   s.30A parody). Defensible, not safe: ECB v Tixdaq [2016] EWHC 575 (Ch) held commercially motivated 8 s clips were
   not fair dealing. Expect Content ID claims; a manual takedown is a strike; a counter-notice hands over name and address.
4. A still frame with credit; using the platforms' own Stitch/Remix tools on TikTok/Instagram.
5. Worst: an altered clip with no permission.

## Cubas himself
UK defamation risk low if it is clearly comment on true, widely reported facts: say "booed, water thrown, run out",
never "assaulted". Paraguay criminalises defamation (Cubas is combative); enforcement on a UK collective unlikely.
