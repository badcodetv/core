# Money For Something 2026: screen sources

Pictures and clips for the television and the full-screen cards in the 2026 year-in-news cut. Sourced 14 October 2026,
the same way as October's set ([`money-for-something-screen-sources.md`](./money-for-something-screen-sources.md)), which
this cut also draws on: ten of its files are reused and their rows stay in that file.

New files live outside the repo in `...\animation\money for something\2026\screen\photos`.

## How to read this

- 13 new files, all from Wikimedia Commons. Every licence was read from the Commons API on the day (`extmetadata`), per file.
- **Green**: public domain, CC0 or CC BY, read on the day. CC BY needs the credit shown; the cut builder puts it on the credits card.
- **Amber**: the tag is there but the chain is weak. Not used.
- CC BY-SA, NC and ND were skipped, which is why there is no picture of Count Binface, a Channel dinghy, PPE or Piddington: nothing free was found.
- Pictures wider than 1920 px were saved as Commons' 1920 px rendering.
- I looked at every file on a contact sheet before using it.

## The same three warnings as October

1. **US military and government pictures** are public domain on copyright; the endorsement clause is Kai's judgement.
2. **Real people.** A clean licence does not clear what the cut implies. In this cut the television shows Xi Jinping, Dick
   Cheney (dead), Donald Rumsfeld (dead), Trump, Farage and Epstein beside lines about them. The unnamed "British minister"
   and the unnamed "baroness" are deliberately NOT pictured. Kai's call, shot by shot.
3. **Marks.** The Taliban picture carries the Voice of America logo; the Maduro picture carries a broadcaster's caption.
