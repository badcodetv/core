## Notes

- **Scene 11a wording (important).** The court's conclusion (para 330) says DHSC "can nonetheless recover the full value of the Gowns **as damages**" after finding breach of contract; it could not recover the £8m storage costs. The figure claimed and in the order is £121,999,219.20. So "a court orders a hundred and twenty-two million repaid" is the press shorthand; the judgment's word is damages equal to the price. Suggest "a court finds the company in breach and awards the government the price of the gowns, about £122m", and feed this to the legal read. The same judgment calls Medpro "a relatively new company which had access to the so-called 'VIP lane'" (para 2): a usable on-screen line.
- **Scene 11a notice dates.** Gowns £122m: awarded 25 Jun 2020, published 7 Sep 2020. Masks £80,850,000: awarded 30 May 2020, published 20 Oct 2020. £122m + £80.85m = £202.85m, matches "two hundred and three million".
- **BoE 2014 Q1 page refs (printed page / PDF page).** p.14 (PDF 1): "the majority of money in the modern economy is created by commercial banks making loans"; "banks do not act simply as intermediaries, lending out deposits that savers place with them". p.15 (PDF 2): "rather than banks lending out deposits that are placed with them, the act of lending creates deposits — the reverse of the sequence typically described in textbooks". p.16 (PDF 3): "Commercial banks create money, in the form of bank deposits, by making new loans." p.26 (PDF 12): "banks create money whenever they lend ... Bank of England does not directly control..." Re-check the exact wording against the PDF before quoting.
- **Scene 12 is not a typeset card.** The TNA image is usable and legible at 1800 px. The quoted sentence spans three lines in the centre panel.
