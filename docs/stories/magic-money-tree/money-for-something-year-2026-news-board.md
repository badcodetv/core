# Money For Something — the year in news (2026 board)

**Status: research for Jack, written 12 October 2026. Nothing here is ruled, and Kai has not seen it.**
Jack's ask: a Money For Something version that is "basically news events of the year" — the most
impactful and the most piss-takable. This is the board to pick from. No jokes are written here
([Jack writes the jokes](./money-for-something-voice.md)); each row says what there is to joke about.

**How far to trust it.** Most rows were read from Wikipedia's year pages (`2026`,
`2026 in the United Kingdom`, `2026 in United Kingdom politics and government`) through a
summarising tool on 12 October. **Read, not tested.** Rows marked ✅ were also checked against a
news report the same day. Every number needs one more look at a primary source before it goes on a
card. The year is not over: the Budget (28 October), the US midterms (3 November) and the Spanish
election (29 November) are still to come.

## The year in one breath

A war started in February and arrived in the gas bill by spring. A man became a trillionaire in
June. The Prime Minister changed in July. It was 38 degrees and there was a hosepipe ban. A party
took £72m from two crypto billionaires and a bin came second in Clacton.

## Guardrails (from `the-reader.md`)

- Every automation or AI line names the person who chose it, in the same breath.
- Immigration stories (Ceuta, Piddington, small boats, net migration down to 171,000): proportion, never denial, never a joke on the people in the boat.
