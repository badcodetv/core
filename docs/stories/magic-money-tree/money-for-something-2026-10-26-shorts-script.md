## How a short is built

Each short is one story, 25–40 s, vertical, with overlay B over real footage and photos the whole way through.

| Layer | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Signal box** (top) | Ring bug · `RECEIVED 26.10.2026` · `SENT 2054 · SIGNAL nn%`. The signal starts high and **drops at the turn** (two-frame glitch) |
| **Tag** | Red triangle · `ARCHIVE · ITEM 2610-nn` |
| **Chyron** (black) | The day's quote or headline, as the news put it. On screen from frame one: **it's the hook** |
| **Correction** (yellow, `›`) | The future's reply. **It lands on the voice's last word.** It's the punchline, so it's the line that matters most |
| **Voice** | BadCode, text-to-speech, flat and unhurried. Burnt-in captions sit **above** the chyron, never on it |
| **Host tail** | 1–2 s of plate H1, silent: **T1** pushes the pint away · **T2** turns his head to camera · **T3** raises one gloved finger · **T4** squares his papers. Then the logo |

Voice runs about 60–80 words a short. A line that has to be rushed to fit is too long.

## Headline slams (Jack, 25 Oct: "have headlines flash on screen like memes")

The YouTube format: real headlines as tilted press cuttings that punch in with a shake, a highlighter swipes the key
words, the next one lands on the pile. Research was thin on paper (no write-up of the convention found); the format is
my reading of current video essays and news explainers, p-grade.

- **Kit:** `scripts/money-for-something/oct26/build-headline-slams.py SPEC.json OUTDIR [BACKGROUND]`. Specs in
  `oct26/slams/`. Output: `slams-<name>.mov` (ProRes 4444 with alpha, goes over the footage in Premiere) and a
  `-preview.mp4` (on a blurred placeholder background with overlay B's signal box).
- **Short 1:** `slams/short1-checklist.json` → `money for something\2026-10-26\slams\` (12.2 s). Five checklist
  cuttings (The Sun 14 Aug, BBC 23 Oct, Guardian 22 Oct, BBC 22 Oct, Streeting quote), each tagged `n/5 … · DONE`, then
  LBC 14 Oct; the voice's dinghy line plays over the LBC cutting; the yellow line closes (placeholder: Jack's to write).
- **Rules:** headlines verbatim with outlet and date; outlet as plain type, never its logo. Timings are guesses until
  the voice is recorded: set each card's `hold` to the voice's word times then.
- **Not done yet:** sound (a thud per slam, a marker squeak on the highlight); real footage underneath.
