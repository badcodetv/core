## 2. The coverage sheet

paid somebody. **Verdict:** the middle (1:10 to 1:38) is the show working: five short lines, one joke each, five
different shapes of joke (a budget gag, a callback, a future aside, a crude simile, a bin). Four other stretches are
facts waiting for that treatment, and the back half has a worse problem: one shape repeated.

Gates: the reader rules hold (each money beat names who got paid; no joke lands on the voters or on the dead).

## 5. The interview segment: the strongest choice

Research: [`money-for-something-2026-interview-and-meta-research.md`](./money-for-something-2026-interview-and-meta-research.md).

**My pick: a fake podcast, Cunk rules, the powerful as guests.** A clip from "the biggest podcast of 2026" plays on
the host's television, then full screen. A fictional podcast-bro host (dark studio, two big microphones, a neon sign,
the "closing tradition" question, an ad read for something he owns) interviews one powerful guest an episode (a
minister, a water boss, a crypto donor, a hedge-fund man). **Both are idiots, as Jack asked**, and both are sincere.
Our host watches it and says one flat word after.

Why this one:

- **Comedy:** it is Kaplan's straight line / wavy line: the podcast is the wavy line, our deadpan host is the straight
  line, and the laugh is him watching it. Cunk's own engine (literal questions, wrong-century questions, false
  equivalence) works on a guest who answers seriously.
- **Reader rule:** the stupid people are the powerful, never members of the public. High-street vox pops would make
  the public the joke, and the public is our reader.
- **No slop:** two people seated under one light, a locked camera, one speaker a clip, eight seconds or less: what
  Omni does best. Crowds on a high street are what it does worst.
- **Legal:** a pastiche of the *format* with a fictional host, name and face. Never Steven Bartlett's name, face or
  voice (`docs/using-ai.md` §6 already bans generating real people; passing off is a separate risk). Kai's call before
  anything is published.
- **Cost:** about 6 to 10 clips an episode, the same Flow pipeline, a new plate and two new Characters.

Ranked behind it: a straight Cunk sit-down (our host asks, the powerful answer); the correspondent alone; vox pops
(no).
