# Money For Something — how we write a script (the process that worked)

**What this is:** the steps we used for the October 2026 monologue
([`magic-money-tree-monologue-2026-10.md`](./magic-money-tree-monologue-2026-10.md)). Jack, 13 October
2026: "that process worked, please do the same." Use it for every Money For Something script unless
Jack says otherwise.

## The steps

1. **News board first.** Bullets only, no jokes. Mark what was checked against a news report and what
   was only read. Jack picks the events.
2. **One subject at a time.** Never the whole script in one go.
3. **I write a beat brief.** A table, one row per line of the script:

   | # | What the line must get across | What to joke about | Jack's line |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- |

   The first column is the plain, checked fact. The second is ways in, not written jokes. The last
   column is left empty.
4. **Jack sends a rough line.**
5. **I fix grammar and facts only**, split it into clips, and say what worries me. Once.
6. **Jack decides.** If he says "keep this" or "this will do", it is marked **FINAL** with the date, and
   the worry is written beside the line. It is not argued again.
7. **If he is stuck he asks for "angles".** Ten or twelve one-line ways in, facts above them.
8. **Then the next subject.** When every beat has a line: one clean read-through, then Kai's rulings,
   then stills and clips.

## Rules for a brief

- **Jack writes the jokes.** I never fill in the last column.
- **Check before the brief.** Re-search each fact on the day. Put how well it stands in a table at the
  bottom: ✅ two or more outlets, 🟡 one source, 🔴 not found. A fact that fails is cut or flagged.
- **One clip is 8 seconds, one speaker, about 20 words.** A longer line is split across clips.
- **Open cold.** No hello, no welcome, no goodnight. Trim everything not needed.
- **Fact first, with the number, then the turn.** End on a throwaway exit word, a different one each beat.
- **The host is from the future.** One short aside a beat at most, thrown away. Never name GitPush
  Origin Master.
- **Read straight.** No wink, no studio laughter.
- **Reader rules** ([`the-reader.md`](../../marketing/the-reader.md)): name who got paid in the same
  piece; the joke lands on the powerful, never on the voters; no joke on the dead.
- **Kai's open calls:** naming living people, and real footage. Any line with an unsourced claim about a
  living person goes to Kai before it is recorded.
