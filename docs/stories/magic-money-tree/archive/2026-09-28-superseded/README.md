# Superseded Magic Money Tree storyboard — 20 September 2026 draft

Archived on **28 September 2026**, when Kai agreed the film's point and ruled its ending, and
[the storyboard](../../storyboard.md) was revised to match.

[`storyboard-2026-09-20.md`](./storyboard-2026-09-20.md) is a **verbatim copy** of the storyboard as
it stood before that revision. It was never committed to git, so this copy is the only record of it.
It is a historical record, **not alternative active canon**.

## What changed on 28 September 2026

| The 20 September draft | The current storyboard |
| --- | --- |
| Central idea: learn to tell a resource shortage from a financing limit from a priority | The point: “we can't afford it” is a choice; the real limit is the work, not the money |
| Ended on a list of questions | Ends on a statement, then an act of care, then a short coda |
| No verbs | Shake, starve, plant on a card at every dated beat |
| 17 scenes in one run | 19 scenes in five movements |
| Covid shown through Eat Out to Help Out | Covid shown through wages kept paid, and one contract in the priority lane |
| “Nobody needs to be secretly enriched for this film's argument to work” | Who paid and who got paid is named three times |
| The debt figure was posed and left | Scene 10b: the debt was outgrown, and the cost is named |
| About twelve and a half minutes planned | About fifteen minutes planned |

Its evidence notes, source links and restraint notes were carried into the current storyboard
unchanged wherever the scene survived. Its narration for scenes 01, 06, 07, 08, 09, 10 and 14 is
unchanged.

Earlier treatments are in the [11 September archive](../2026-09-11-superseded/README.md).
