# Money For Something, 26 October 2026 shorts: screen sources

Real photos for the six vertical shorts (1, 2, 5, 6, 8, 9). Sourced 26 October 2026 the same way as the
[2026 year cut](./money-for-something-2026-screen-sources.md): every file from Wikimedia Commons, every licence read from
the Commons API on the day (`extmetadata` plus licence categories), per file.

Files live outside the repo in `...\animation\money for something\2026-10-26\pictures`, named `s<short>-<key>-<nn>-<slug>.jpg`.
Machine-readable list for the cut builder: [`scripts/money-for-something/oct26/pictures.tsv`](../../../scripts/money-for-something/oct26/pictures.tsv)
(`key`, Linux path, credit; credit empty when none is owed).

## How to read this

- 63 files covering all 42 keys (the weak ones are flagged in "Not found" below). All looked at on a contact sheet.
- Pictures larger than about 3000 px were saved as Commons' 3840 px rendering; smaller ones are the original. PNG originals were converted to JPEG.
- **Green**: public domain (US federal, PD-UKGov, PD-self), CC0, CC BY, or UK OGL v3.0, read on the day. CC BY and OGL need the credit shown; carry the "Exact credit owed" string onto the credits card.
- **Amber**: six files. Four are CC BY-SA (ShareAlike: editing the picture into a short arguably makes the short an adaptation that must be relicensed CC BY-SA). Two carry a Flickr Public Domain Mark the photographer applied (a mark, not a formal dedication). Per the find-footage hard stop, **none ships until a human has said yes.**
- NC and ND were never taken.

## Warnings

1. **Real people.** Burnham, Macron, Zuckerberg, Piers Morgan, Kushner, Musk, Kanishka Narayan and Prince William are all
   pictured in official or public settings. A clean licence does not clear what the cut puts beside them: Kai's call, shot by shot.
   Crowd and protest pictures also show identifiable members of the public.
2. **Narayan is AI minister.** Checked 26 October 2026 by web search: Andy Burnham (PM since 20 July 2026) made Kanishka Narayan the first
   cabinet-level Minister for AI on 20 July 2026. No source dated October 2026 was found, so "still in post" is unverified.
3. **Brands and named places.** The Freddo, Ozempic pen, Barclays branch, Meta sign, Etihad Stadium and FANUC robot carry trademarks
   (fine for editorial comment, never as endorsement). `s9-carehome-01` is a real, named care home (Dukes Court, Avery Healthcare): if the
   short says anything bad about care homes, use it blurred or not at all.
4. **Not quite the brief:** `s1-siren-01` is a modern Finnish civil-defence siren, not WW2; `s1-siren-02` is an Australian test alert, not a
   UK one; `s2-totnes-01` is a kite at Oxwich (Wales), not Totnes; `s9-gran-01` reads as 50s-60s rather than a gran; `s6-chatbot-01` is a
   laptop with no chat on screen; `s8-ozempic-01` is the Chinese-market pen.
