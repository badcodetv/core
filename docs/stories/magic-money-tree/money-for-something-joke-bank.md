# Money For Something — the joke bank

**What this is:** every line that made the show, every joke and angle that did not, and the
running gags, kept so the next episode starts from a full cupboard. Add to it after every episode.
How Jack writes: [`money-for-something-voice.md`](./money-for-something-voice.md).

**Who wrote what.** Lines under "The show" are Jack's (grammar and facts fixed by me). Lines and
angles under "Unused" are marked **J** (Jack) or **C** (offered by me, not picked). An unused **C**
line has never been approved by anyone; treat it as a suggestion.
