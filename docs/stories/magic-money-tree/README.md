# The Magic Money Tree

> **Source of truth** for this story. All media derive from this folder.
> See [`../../storytelling.md`](../../storytelling.md) for the method and
> [`../../voice.md`](../../voice.md) for tone.

**Re-founded 2026-08-05/06 (Kai + Claude):** the story is now **John Maynard
Keynes talking to a tree** — a friendship that begins on a bench in St James's
Park in the darkest year of the war, invents the system that saves Britain (and
the NHS), outlives Keynes as a ghost at the same bench, and watches the century
misuse what they built. Format: **narrated YouTube video** — AI-generated film
for the fiction, **real archive footage for real people**, song as the ending.
Load-bearing idea: *the tree is real and it is good — the crime is where the
money comes to rest.*

**The ending (reworked 2026-08-06):** not the May clip — the **fascism
gut-punch**. The wealth gap is dismantling the anti-fascism machine the WWII
generation built; the May clip is demoted to "the nod that they've forgotten";
the last scene is the winter bench, tree bare, ghost head-in-hands, cutting to
the Bank of England's four mulberries in leaf in a graveyard, being shaken.

**Grove lore added 2026-08-07 (Kai):** money trees **fruit** on command and live
**inside central banks**, going back to the Bank of England's 1694 war charter.
They have one limit — *only as much as there is real work to buy* — and past it
the fruit comes out **hollow**. Our tree is the feral one: grown from a £1 note a
Bank clerk planted in a park in **August 1914**, so young, not ancient, and the
only money tree in history anyone has ever *talked to* instead of shaken. **And
the premise is documented, not invented:** the Bank of England really does have
**four mulberries growing in a graveyard in its courtyard**, really did plant
them **during the Blitz**, and really chose the species because **its oldest
banknote is printed on mulberry bark**. See [`the-grove.md`](./the-grove.md).

Previous spines (the v1 Dawn comic, the 2026-07-22 Question Time set-crumble
rework) are superseded; the v1 comic remains live as the pipeline-spike
artifact, and git history preserves the old canon.
