> **Source of truth** for this story. All media derive from this folder.
> See [`../../storytelling.md`](../../storytelling.md) for the method and
> [`../../voice.md`](../../voice.md) for tone.

**Re-founded 2026-08-05/06 (Kai + Claude):** the story is now **John Maynard
Keynes talking to a tree** — a friendship that begins on a park bench in the
darkest year of the war, invents the system that saves Britain (and the NHS),
outlives Keynes as a ghost at the same bench, and watches the century misuse
what they built — ending cold on the real Theresa May clip, the yacht, and a
worldwide grove of central-bank trees. Format: **narrated YouTube video** with
real archive footage, song as the ending. Load-bearing idea: *the tree is real
and it is good — the crime is where the money comes to rest.*

Previous spines (the v1 Dawn comic, the 2026-07-22 Question Time set-crumble
rework) are superseded; the v1 comic remains live as the pipeline-spike
artifact, and git history preserves the old canon.
