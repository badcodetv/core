**The documentary: [The Future He Never Saw — canonical storyboard](./storyboard.md).**

Britain rescues an army at Dunkirk, then builds a country worth coming home to while owing more
than it ever had. Keynes explains how, and dies before the NHS opens. Almost seventy years later a
nurse asks about her pay and is told there is no magic money tree. The film shows the three things
a country can do with its money: **shake it, starve it, or plant it.**

**The point, agreed by Kai on 28 September 2026:** *“we can't afford it” is a choice; the real limit
is the work, not the money.* The film ends on a statement, not a question.

**Direction chosen by Kai, 11 September 2026:** an informative, occasionally humorous short
documentary about war, Keynes, employment, rebuilding and the NHS. Genuine historical footage can
mix with clearly distinguishable illustration, AI-generated imagery and comics. Story comes first;
visual treatment is not locked. The shared purpose is a country worth living in, not a contest over
which voters are allowed to feel patriotic.
