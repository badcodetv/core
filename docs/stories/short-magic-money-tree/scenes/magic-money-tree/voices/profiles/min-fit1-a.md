# Audio Profile
A smooth, well-fed Home Counties government minister in his fifties, plummy and practised, the voice of a man who has said no to a great many people without ever losing his lunch.

## Scene:
Standing in a park in front of the cameras, between two trees.

## Sample Context:
A quick, brushed-off "We can't afford it", all in one breath, no pause, no sigh, over in about a second.
