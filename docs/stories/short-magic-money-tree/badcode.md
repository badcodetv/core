## What it is
A BadCode short (9:16, 13.3 s). A minister shakes a tree labelled HOSPITALS: nothing falls. "We can't afford it."
He shakes the tree next to it, BANKS: money pours. To camera: "Different tree." The point: "we can't afford it" is a
choice about who gets the money, not a fact about whether it exists. State: rough cut 1 approved by Jack ("great") and
built in Premiere (`shorts-36-magic-money-tree-r1`); waiting on Jack's "it's done", export, QC, gatekeeper.

## Story-only rules
- An invented minister: no AI likeness or name of a real politician.
- HOSPITALS and BANKS are painted-style captions laid in by the edit at the top of frame, never asked of Flow.
- Aim at the decision (who the money goes to), not at "the economy". `marketing-gatekeeper` before posting.
- Close-ups exist because the wide shot's face is too small to lip-sync.
