Galileo didn't break faith with reason. The men who refused to look through his
telescope were **professors, not priests**, and what they were defending was
Aristotle — the last generation's science, canonised. Four hundred years later
we've done it again, and this time nobody needs a tribunal, because we recant our
own lives unprompted, mid-sentence, with an apology attached. *"I don't mean to
sound spiritual, but—"* is an abjuration. Nobody made you sign it.

## The core idea, in one line

**Dogma is not a set of beliefs. It's a rule about what you're allowed to report**
— and it's always the previous generation's correct answer, fossilised and
defended by the people who make a living from it.

## Scope guard (load-bearing)

- **The target is fossilised consensus, not science.** Science is the honest part
  — it publishes its own failures and hands out medals for breaking its favourite
  assumptions. The villain is the priesthood that accretes on top of anything that
  works. Materialism wasn't wrong as science; it was wrong as a *stopping point*.
  Get this wrong and we lose exactly the audience we want.
- **Adjacent to the [Storyverse](../gitpush-origin-master/storyverse.md), never an
  explanation of it.** The frontier beat stops at *your physics goes quiet here.*
  No collapse mechanism, no multi-dimensional time, no hierarchical entanglement —
  that's a different story.
- **Politics first, per [`voice.md`](../../voice.md).** The load-bearing political
  idea is *the licence on reality is the most profitable asset in any economy* —
  the tithe then, the grant now. A beat with no power or money in it is off-brief.
- **Bright lines (inherited):** *science side — borrow the credible lineage, never
  the woo. Theology side — point at the commonalities, never found a church.*

## What's committed vs open

The **key concept, the twist, the double-casting device and the accuracy rules are
committed.** The ten beats are a strong first pass, not a lock — order, count and
the exact narration are open. Medium is unassigned.
