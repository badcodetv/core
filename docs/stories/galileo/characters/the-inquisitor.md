# The Inquisitor

**Double-cast.** Same face, twice: the tribunal that takes Galileo's abjuration
(beat 4), and the committee chair who declines Priya's proposal (beat 9).

**Historical note (declared in `story.md`):** he is a composite. Bellarmine gave
the 1616 warning and was dead by 1621; the 1633 trial ran under Urban VIII with
Vincenzo Maculano as commissary. We compress to one face because the whole device
depends on one face. Bellarmine's bearing is the model.

**He is never a monster and he must never enjoy it.** He is polite. He is
genuinely sorry it's come to this. He believes — accurately — that he is holding
a civilisation together, and that a man with a tube is a smaller thing than the
order that keeps eight million people from each other's throats. He would very
much prefer that Galileo simply signed, and he says so, warmly, several times.

That is what makes him the correct villain: **institutions do not defend
themselves with cruelty. They defend themselves with reasonableness and a
calendar.**

**Modern variant** — the chair. Identical function, identical warmth, zero
theatre. He doesn't threaten anyone. He mentions, in passing and with real
sympathy, when her contract comes up. That's it. That's the entire rack.

## Visual (house style: hyper-realistic documentary photography)

- 60s–70s, ascetic, dry-skinned, thin-lipped. Steady eyes. A face that has heard
  everything already. No scowl — the resting expression is mild attention.
- **1633 variant:** cardinal's red, worn plainly, no gold theatre. Seated. A
  document and an inkwell in front of him, turned to face the accused. The room
  is stone and enormous and mostly empty; the emptiness is the threat.
- **Modern variant (beat 9):** the same face, in a grey suit that fits badly at
  the shoulder, and a lanyard. Seated at the head of a laminate table. A printed
  proposal and a pen in front of him, **turned to face her — match the 1633
  framing exactly.** The room is a beige committee room with a wall clock.
- **Locked rhyme:** in both eras the shot is over the accused's shoulder,
  slightly low, with him centred and the document between them. Same composition,
  four hundred years apart. Red robe / grey suit is the only thing that changes.
