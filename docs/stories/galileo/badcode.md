## What it is
Galileo didn't break faith with reason: the men who refused to look through his telescope were professors, not
priests, defending the last generation's science. Four hundred years later we do it again, and recant our own lives
unprompted ("I don't mean to sound spiritual, but—" is an abjuration). The point: **dogma is a rule about what you're
allowed to report**, and the licence on reality is the most profitable asset in any economy (the tithe then, the grant
now). A first-pass spine of ten beats; concept, twist and double-casting are committed, beats are open. Medium
unassigned (comic / short / song); nothing made yet.

## Story-only rules
- **The target is fossilised consensus, not science.** Science is the honest part; the villain is the priesthood that
  accretes on anything that works. Materialism was wrong as a stopping point, not as science.
- The frontier beat stops at "your physics goes quiet here": no collapse mechanism, no multi-dimensional time.
- A beat with no power or money in it is off-brief.
- Accuracy (binding): say "Scientific Revolution", never "Galileo began the Enlightenment"; the refusers were
  Aristotelian philosophers, not clerics; the Jesuit astronomers confirmed the observations within a year (include it).
- No torture, no dungeon: house arrest, then Arcetri; the 1633 threat was formal and verbal. Play the room as the threat.
- Never conflate with Giordano Bruno. "E pur si muove" is legend: use only as self-aware myth.
- The tribunal is a declared composite (Bellarmine / Urban VIII / Maculano compressed to one face).
- No established physics says consciousness is fundamental: say what's unsolved, never claim what's unproven.
