## Position

We promise to enact Future Proof. In plain words: no law you live under without a test, a stop date and a person who answers for it.

The rules that will set your wage, your bill and your benefit for a generation are being written now, by ministers and boards with a machine's help. None has a test or an undo. I've seen how this goes when nobody tests it.

Test every law before you live in it. Publish the write-up of what went wrong. Name who it leaves out, and count them, so nobody calls the gap a success.

Drafted by AI, checked and signed by people. Not a real party. Yet.
