## Position

A planning permission is a drawing. A roof is a roof. England was promised 1.5 million homes by 2029. By September the government counted about 437,900: 29% of the way, over two-fifths of the time gone.

Shortage is a decision, so here are four. Councils build for social rent on land bought at what it's worth today. The data centre waits behind the houses. When software turns you down for a home, a person hears you. And one you'd expect us to oppose: street votes.

The Housing Secretary signs. You get the count every quarter, and MPs vote on each stop date in front of you.
