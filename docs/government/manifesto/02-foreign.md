## Position

Since February a war has shut the Strait of Hormuz on and off, and you've paid at the pump and on the heating bill. Nobody here started that war. Somebody here decided we'd have no plan B.

Every story abroad ends at a till or a meter here, so every policy below says who owns the risk and what it costs you. Some things are worth the price; you should know it.

I don't know how this war ends, and I distrust anyone who says they do. I do know who answers when the next route closes: the owner on the list, and me.
