## Position

A data centre wants as much power as a town. Brilliant machine. Wrong queue.

Households first: homes keep their power, the data centre obeys the hosepipe ban, and the standing charge comes down, prepayment first. Where there's one set of wires and no rival, the public owns it.

Net zero stays, security first: home-grown power is the one kind nobody can blockade.

Who it leaves out: homes on heating oil and prepayment meters, who pay most and get counted least. Counted separately, and first.

Ministers chose the data-centre discount this year. Your MPs choose whether it stays.
