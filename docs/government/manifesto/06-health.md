## Position

Your mum's hip is one of 7.4 million waits for planned treatment in England. Hitting a date is not
fixing a queue.

NHS England has told the health service to adopt AI note-takers that listen to your appointment.
Nobody has published whether they buy you more of the doctor, or the hospital fewer doctors. So
nothing goes national until it has been tried on a ward, and every hour it saves is owed to a
patient.

Bed 4 first, then the press release. Your hospital board chooses that order this year.
