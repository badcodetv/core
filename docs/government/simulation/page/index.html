Future Proof Live

What if the people who live with a decision set its test? Here's a machine pretending, every day, until real people do it.

Every day one real news story goes round the Future Proof loop. Simulated citizens set the test, three answers are written blind, a gate checks them against every test, a simulated jury settles the values question, and a simulated minister signs one small trial. Then the calendar marks the predictions.

Nothing here is real people taking part. The citizens and jurors are AI stand-ins drawn by lot from a pool built from real census figures. Real polls sit beside them where they exist. The news, the sources and the dates are real. Not a real party. Yet.
