A policy works for most people, the average goes up, and the people it missed vanish into the
average.

Who decided. Departments, which choose what an impact assessment counts and whom it leaves blank.

The rule. Name who it leaves out, and count them, so nobody calls the gap a success. Every policy
prints who it leaves out, named groups, the reader's people first, counted on their own line, and
the line is never empty.

The ledger. None: it moves no money. It moves the people a scoreboard misses onto the page.
