You get one vote every five years, for a whole package. Everything else you think goes unasked.

Who decided. Parliament, which sets the agenda itself and answers the public when it chooses.

The rule. A say on more than one thing. People picked like a jury and paid for their time choose a
few questions a year. Parliament answers every point they agree on within three months. Not a
referendum on everything and not phone voting on anything binding: a panel, and an answer.

The ledger. Small: the panel's pay and running costs. The public gains questions it chose itself.
