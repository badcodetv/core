judgement.principle: The state pension keeps the triple lock for the rest of this parliament.
judgement.review: at the end of this parliament
judgement.change_mind: Published evidence that the same money would do more for the poorest pensioners spent another way.
stop: Stops at the end of this parliament unless Parliament votes to keep it, on the published review.
leaves_out: Working-age claimants; people below pension age with no pension yet
signs: The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions
check.claim: The state pension rise for April 2028, announced in autumn 2027, will be set by earnings growth: higher than both September 2027 CPI inflation and 2.5%.
check.prior: 0.5
check.rule: Take the percentage rise in the full new State Pension for 2028 to 2029 from DWP's published rates and the ONS CPI 12-month rate for September 2027; held if the rise is above both that CPI rate and 2.5%.
check.source: DWP, Benefit and pension rates 2028 to 2029 (gov.uk); ONS, Consumer price inflation, September 2027
check.if_revised: The rates as first announced count; if no 2028 to 2029 rate is announced by the check date, the check is void.
check.check_on: 2027-12-31
check.result: pending

The triple lock stays this parliament.
