Nobody's job gets taken by weather. A board decides, on a weekday, and the saving goes somewhere.
The law makes it consult the forty people it was about. Being told isn't a say.

Before a firm automates jobs, the people in them pick who speaks for them and choose the split: a
shorter week on the same pay, a profit share, or paid training for a job that exists. Binding,
where employers sign up.

The ledger. The firm's saving is shared with the people it was about; the public pays for running
the trial. Small: under £100m a year.

The board decides on a weekday. So can the forty people it was about.
