judgement.principle: No rise in the state pension age without published figures for how long people stay healthy, region by region, and a vote on that rise alone.
judgement.review: before any rise in the state pension age is put to MPs
judgement.change_mind: The regional figures show healthy years rising in every region the rise would reach.
stop: Stops on its date unless Parliament votes to keep it, on the published review. Passes, and it goes national.
leaves_out: Anyone whose health fails before their region's average; people already past pension age
signs: The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions
check.claim: The next ONS release of healthy life expectancy will show a wider gap between the English regions with the highest and lowest male healthy life expectancy at birth than the release before it.
check.prior: 0.5
check.rule: In the first ONS health state life expectancy release published after 2026-11-11, take male healthy life expectancy at birth for the nine English regions; held if the highest minus lowest is larger than the same gap in the previous release.
check.source: ONS, Health state life expectancies, UK
check.if_revised: The figures as first published in each release count; if no new release appears by the check date, the check is void.
check.check_on: 2027-12-31
check.provisional: yes
check.result: pending

Before the pension age rises, we publish how long people stay healthy, region by region, and MPs
vote on that rise alone.
