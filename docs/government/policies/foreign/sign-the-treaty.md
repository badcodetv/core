No machine decides who dies. No UK weapon selects a person to attack. Incoming missiles and drones
are fair game for a fast machine. People are not.

Over seventy countries want a treaty saying so; Britain calls itself unconvinced. We'd sign, and
keep machines out of nuclear decisions.
