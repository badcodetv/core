Police cameras. On 4 November the Home Office promised a regulator, bias tests and £11 million to
triple the live facial recognition vans. Right rules, wrong order.

The rule. The new vans go first to volunteer forces drawn by lot, against forces like them. A match
is a lead, not a fact about you: an officer decides, and says so, at the stop.
