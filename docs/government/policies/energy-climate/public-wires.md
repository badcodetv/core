Own what can't compete. One set of wires reaches your door; with no rival, the market is a toll
booth.

Who decided. Successive governments left the local wires in private hands: 14 licence areas, six
operator groups, five owners.

The rule. One network region goes public first, priced by an independent valuer.

The ledger. The public pays the valuer's price once; the region's bill payers stop paying the
toll's profit.
