A bust water company stays public: our judgement call, reviewed in five years.

Who decided. Whoever is in government when a water company runs out of money decides whether the
public rescues it and hands it back, or keeps it.

The rule. When a water company goes bust, it stays in public hands.

The ledger. The public takes on the company; bill payers stop paying for its owners' profit.
