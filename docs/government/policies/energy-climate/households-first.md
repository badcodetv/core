A data centre wants as much power as a town, and ministers have decided it should pay less than
you do: up to £24 off every megawatt-hour in the AI zones, from 2027. Brilliant machine. Wrong
queue.

Who decided. Ministers chose that discount this year. The discount still needs legislation.

The rule. In a shortage, homes and the factories already here keep their power; big new loads
switch down first and pay for their own wires. Ministers say your bill doesn't fund that discount;
we make them prove it, yearly.

The ledger. Big new loads pay for their own connections; household bills stop carrying them.
