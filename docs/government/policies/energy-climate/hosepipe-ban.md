The hosepipe ban applies to the data centre.

Who decided. Ministers, when they chose where the AI Growth Zones go and gave their data centres
cheaper power.

The rule. The host town writes the deal, not the company: local ownership of the new power, water
use published, the campus cut first in a drought. The deal is Housing's host-town compact; this is
its grid and water section.

The ledger. The data centre gives up first call on water in a drought and a share of the new power;
the host town gains both.
