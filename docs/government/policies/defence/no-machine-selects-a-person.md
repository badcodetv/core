The rule. No UK weapon selects a person to attack. A named officer answers for every shot, the way
a soldier always has. Incoming missiles and drones are fair game for a fast machine. People are
not.

Today the UK's rule is a phrase in a policy paper. We make it law, a right with no stop date. One
shot approved too fast to be a decision, and the minister answers to Parliament within 30 days.

Who decides who answers for a shot today: a policy paper. Who will: Parliament, and an officer with
a name.
