Threat: in 2024 a cyberattack sent London hospitals' blood tests back to paper. The head of the
National Cyber Security Centre calls it highly likely that by 2028 attackers will use AI to break
into the old systems running your power and water. In October, France says, Iran's Revolutionary
Guard hacked the fridges in more than 700 French shops: food spoiled, fires started. US agencies
warn the attacks are live; UK supermarket units were reportedly hit.

Budget: 3% of national income by 2030, spent on kit, not on keeping the lights on.

The rule. Water, power, hospital labs, payments, ports and the cold stores that keep the food prove
in a published drill that the essentials hold with the main systems off. Whitehall's own 2027
exercise, Albiston Shadow, the largest home defence drill in decades, tests the War Book. Ours
tests the pump room.

The ledger. Paid a dividend and ran no fallback? The dividend pays, not your bill. Our share comes
from NATO's resilience pledge, already made.
