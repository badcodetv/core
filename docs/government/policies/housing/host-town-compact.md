Homes before servers. A housing site should never sit in the queue for a grid connection behind
a server farm.

Who decided. Ministers, when they named the AI Growth Zones and offered their data centres cheaper
power.

The rule. A data centre pays for its own wires, under Energy's grid rule, and waits behind the
houses. The town writes the deal, and residents have the last word. The compact is the one form
every host town uses: planning is its legal hook and Housing owns it; Energy fills in the grid and
water section; the Chancellor sets how the money splits between the town and the national fund.

The ledger. The developer pays for its own connection; the houses keep their place in the queue.
