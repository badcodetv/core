One you'd expect us to oppose: street votes, law since 2023, never switched on.

Who decided. Ministers, who left the street-votes powers in the Levelling-up and Regeneration Act
2023 unused; in October 2025 they said no final decision had been taken.

The rule. Commence the street-votes provisions where councils volunteer.

The ledger. Streets that vote yes add homes on their own plots; nothing moves from the public purse
beyond running the vote.
