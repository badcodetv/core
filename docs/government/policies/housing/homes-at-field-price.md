Councils built 9,241 homes for social rent in 2024-25.

Shortage is a decision. When a council buys a field, the law pays the owner for a permission the
public hasn't granted yet.

Who decided. Parliament, in the Land Compensation Act 1961. Since 2024 (the Levelling-up and
Regeneration Act 2023) councils building affordable homes may ask to leave that out.

The rule. We make it the default for social rent, as the new towns did: the rise pays for the
road, the school and the surgery. Small farms their owners work are exempt. New council homes stay
out of Right to Buy, for good.

Who gets them: people who live, work or grew up in the town, alongside those in greatest need.
People escaping abuse and care leavers keep their right to a home wherever they need one.

The ledger. The landowner loses a windfall; the waiting list gains homes; rents repay the
borrowing. How councils are funded at all: Not ready.
