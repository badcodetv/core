If software turns you down for a tenancy, a named person hears your appeal within ten working days.

Who decided. Parliament, in the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025: since 5 February 2026 the
safeguards in UK GDPR cover only decisions made solely by a machine. A decision a machine helped
with, with a person in the loop, carries none of them.

The rule. The right to a human is the Prime Minister's, one statute for every decision about a
person that a machine helped make. This is its tenancy deadline: ten working days.

The ledger. Whoever uses the software pays for the person who hears the appeal; the person turned
down gets a hearing. Small.
