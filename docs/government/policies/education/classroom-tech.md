Ministers are consulting on safety certificates for classroom tech. Safe is a start; does it teach?

The rule. No school buys an AI tool until it has beaten classes like it, in volunteer schools drawn
by lot. No machine sets a grade without a teacher's name on it.

Who decided. Ministers, by choosing to certify classroom tools for safety, not for learning.
