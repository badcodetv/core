NHS England has told the health service to adopt AI note-takers that listen to your appointment.
Nobody has published whether they buy you more of the doctor, or the hospital fewer doctors.

Most note-takers fall outside today's checks on medical software. We make a ward trial compulsory
before the whole NHS buys anything. If it fails, it comes out.
