Every saving lands in somebody's account, every shock on somebody's bill, and nobody prints the
names. That is a decision, and decisions have addresses.

Who decided. The Treasury, which decides what a Budget's documents print and what they leave out.

The rule. Every Budget and public deal with a tech firm comes with who pays and who gains, by
household, in pounds, within four weeks. The Bank stays independent; we ask it to do the same
yearly, as it already writes to the Chancellor whenever inflation is more than a point from target.

The ledger. Small: the cost of the tables. Everyone who pays a bill gains the receipt.
