"We can't afford it" is never true as stated: who can't, and who decided?

Who decided. The Treasury, which sets the fiscal rules.

The rule. Fiscal rules kept, every measure scored by the forecaster, no rise in income tax,
employee NI or VAT rates on working people. Overspend and new roll-outs pause.

The ledger. None of its own: it is the rule every other measure on these pages is scored against.
