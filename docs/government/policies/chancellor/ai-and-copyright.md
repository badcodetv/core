You made the song. Someone else's machine learned from it, and the profit went to the machine's
owner.

Who decided. The firms that train machines on other people's work without asking, and the
ministers who leave the asking to them.

The rule. Training a machine on your songs or photos for profit needs your permission, and sellers
say what it learned from.

We make our own pictures and music with these machines.

The ledger. Firms that train for profit pay for the work they use; the people who made it gain.
