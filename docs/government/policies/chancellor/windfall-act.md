Your energy price cap: up 4% in October, driven by gas prices in the Middle East. Somebody's
receipts went up by the same shock that put up your bill.

Who decided. Ministers, who set the Electricity Generator Levy to end on 31 March 2028.

The rule. One standing Windfall Act: when a shock abroad hands power or oil firms a windfall, the
levy switches on, every pound goes on the standing charge, and it switches off when prices fall
back. Two schedules. Electricity: the existing Electricity Generator Levy, kept and raised while
the shock lasts. Oil and gas: the Energy Profits Levy. One destination: the standing charge,
prepayment customers first.

The ledger. Generators and oil and gas firms with windfall receipts pay; households gain, starting
with prepayment meters. Large: over £1bn a year while a shock lasts.
