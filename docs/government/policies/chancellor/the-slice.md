Power for data centres in the AI zones: up to £24 off every megawatt-hour in Scotland, £16 in
Cumbria, £14 in the North East, from April 2027 if Parliament passes it, by ministers' decision. Who pays for that: ministers say nobody else. We'd print the receipt.

Who decided. Ministers, when they offered AI growth zones discounted power with no stake in return.

The rule. Where public cash, land, cheap power or a guarantee helps build a private data centre,
the public gets what any investor would: a share, in a national fund, host town paid first. One
split rule for every public stake: host town first, then the national fund. NHS data returns stay
in the NHS, as a stated exception.

The ledger. Firms that take public support pay in equity; the host town and the national fund
gain. Medium: £100m to £1bn a year.
