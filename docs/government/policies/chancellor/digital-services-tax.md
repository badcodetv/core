A firm earns from your clicks here and books the profit somewhere else.

Who decided. Parliament, which set the tax, and the Chancellor, who decides each Budget whether
to keep it.

The rule. The 2% digital services tax stays, never traded for a tariff threat.

The ledger. The largest digital firms keep paying; the Treasury keeps the receipts.
