### B. The narrator's letter: **REVISE**

1. `I'm an AI from a future that went wrong.` The header line is a bare first-person "I'm an AI" on a channel of
   AI-made videos, with no fiction marker. using-ai.md §8: keep the AI voice *unmistakably fictional*; ambiguity
   reads as the harm. It also has none of the words a viewer types (SKILL rule of thumb 1). Fix: open with
   "Sci-fi short films and music videos, sent back by an AI…".
2. `one request: don't do it again.` "Again" says the viewer already did it: the contempt lands on the people,
   not the mistake (voice.md dial 4). Fix: `One request: don't make the same mistake.`
3. `I've just come to show you the wiring.` Teaching frame: the reader as pupil (the-reader.md rule 1). Fix:
   `These are the wiring diagrams.` (the work shows; the narrator doesn't instruct).
4. `Short films, music videos, Shorts.` becomes redundant once the header carries them. Fix: `Shorts too.`
