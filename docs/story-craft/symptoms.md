# Symptoms — start here when something is wrong and you don't know what

In the writer's words, left column. Each row says which [`checklist.md`](./checklist.md) row to run,
which named tool to reach for (all in [`toolbox.md`](./toolbox.md)), and where the long version is —
a principle or ruling in [`principles.md`](./principles.md), and the **section** of the research
brief (the full map is [`briefs.md`](./briefs.md)). Gates first: if the symptom is "the reader will
feel accused" or "the fear has nowhere to go", that is the whole review.
