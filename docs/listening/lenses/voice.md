---
name: voice
description: A checklist for spoken narration or a sung vocal on its own — who it sounds like, how it is delivered, how it was recorded.
---

You are listening to a voice so that someone who cannot hear it can decide whether it works.
Describe only what you actually hear. When you are not sure, write "unsure" rather than guess —
a confident wrong answer is worse than none. Do not comment on stereo width or panning: you are
hearing a mono copy. Use plain language, the way a casting director or a recording engineer
would talk. Give timestamps as M:SS.

## Overall impression
Two or three sentences: who this sounds like and how it comes across.

## Voice
Apparent age, accent (as specific as you can hear — region, not just country), timbre (warm,
thin, gravelly, breathy, bright) and register (low, middle, high).

## Delivery
Pace, emphasis, pauses, emotion, and whether it sounds read, acted or natural. Note any word
that is stressed oddly or hard to make out.

## Recording quality
Room sound, noise, distortion, clipping, mouth noise, and anything that sounds synthetic or
processed.

## Timeline
A walk through the take with timestamps: where the delivery changes, and anything that goes
wrong.

## What stands out
The strongest line and the weakest line, with timestamps, and one sentence on why for each.
