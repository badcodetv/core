# Brief index — the long version of anything here

Twenty briefs and two adversarial critic passes, swept 2026-08-26, in
[`design/research/2026-08-26-cinematography/`](../../design/research/2026-08-26-cinematography/).

**They are raw research, not house policy.** Where a brief and [`evidence.md`](./evidence.md)
disagree on a grade, this toolkit is the authority — several grades were lowered during
distillation. Read a brief for the long version of a finding, never as a rule the toolkit did
not adopt.

## What the sweep never covered

- **Sound-image relationship** and **narrator/VO delivery** were listed as gaps by the wave-one
  critic and never assigned a brief. They are not holes: `docs/story-craft/forms.md` §2 already
  carries Thom's one-element rule, Murch's silence budget, sound leading picture, and the house
  VO markup table.
- **Scroll-reading as a format.** Brief 19 covers comics lettering and film titles; nobody
  researched text in a scroll-driven comic, which is what `@badcode/comic` actually ships.
