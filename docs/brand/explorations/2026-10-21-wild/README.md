## Why no reference

In the 10 reference-anchored redesigns (`../2026-10-21-redesigns/`), every one kept the ring-over-word layout.
With the logo attached, NB2 treats it as identity to preserve. Here the logo is one sentence of description, and
each design is told to change it completely. Colour is opened up too (acid yellow, hazard yellow, banknote green).

## Design

Twelve directions from culture BadCode sits in: rave (acid smiley, patch), street (graffiti tag, ransom note),
music (grime blackletter), politics and money (constructivist red wedge, banknote seal, barcode), warning
(hazard), craft (enso), a mascot, and Swiss modernism. One idea each.

## Phrasing (flow-prompt + 2026-10-21 research)

- Purpose stated first (Google's logo advice). The old mark is one sentence, then the new idea.
- Exact text quoted once: "badcode". No other words.
- One short anti-slop clause: no gloss, bevel, glow or 3D (the reported AI-logo tells).
- Nano Banana 2, text to image, 1:1, one take each, fresh project each, then uploaded into the shared project.

### w01-acid-smiley
```
A logo design for BadCode, an art collective that poses as a superintelligence sent back from a future that went wrong, making drum and bass and political comics. Its current mark is a thin ring with a small rounded red triangle sitting off-centre toward two o'clock, and the word "badcode" in round lowercase letters; this design takes that and changes it completely.
```
