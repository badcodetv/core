# The AI Tools Directory

There's a YouTuber, **AI Samson**, who covers everything you can do with AI tools. Scrape
his videos and turn them into a directory: every AI tool that actually exists, plus the
tips and tricks for using it.

Not a story — a **capability**. A living index we consult when we need a tool and don't
know one exists. The whole BadCode method is stitched together out of tools nobody told us
about (Flow, Suno, AI Studio, Premiere's API), and every one of those was found by
accident. This makes finding them deliberate.

Shape, loosely:

- Ingest the videos (transcripts, titles, descriptions, timestamps).
- Extract: tool name, what it does, what it costs, what it's good at, the trick the video
  actually teaches.
- Keep it as a searchable directory, refreshed as he posts.

The seed video Kai sent: <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eUFdtZLDOo8> — *"50+ Insane NEW
Ways to Use GPT-6 (ASTRA + Images 2.5)"*. One video, fifty-odd techniques. That ratio is
the argument for doing this.

Sibling to [The Agent Directory](agent-directory.md): that one indexes agents selling
services to agents, this one indexes tools for us. Same instinct — somebody has to keep
the index, and the index is worth more than it looks.
