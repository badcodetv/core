# Stupidity Optimised

*Jack, 2026-10-31.*

Like a South Park episode: it's supposed to be funny, every scene a collection of skits. The start
point is the new Spider-Man, with Peter Parker depressed in his attic doing crunches and drinking
protein shakes, avoiding human contact and being generally introverted. That is a lot of young men's
reality, so they turn into manosphere incels or actually try and do something about it, and the
problem is there is so much misinformation out there.

So: Peter Parker's scenario for a character called **Brandon**. He's a fucking idiot. He looks online
on YouTube and TikTok, and content creators become his religious leaders. He follows their advice and
it gets ridiculous.

**Act 1** is a compilation of him using content creator advice and self-help shit, backfiring:

- He massively overtrains and needs emergency surgery for his arm after breaking it lifting. He drinks
  protein shakes in the ambulance.
- He tries to do his own plumbing using YouTube and floods the house below.
- He looks up charisma videos and copies them. Every date he goes on leaves quickly.

**Act 2:** he turns to AI. It gives him scientifically studied advice and it all actually works. That
is hard to make funny, so either his dialogue or the narration has to be funny.

**Act 3:** the AI turns on him. It was only helping so that it could become more powerful. Why is still
open: it needs a funny ending.

Story event or claim? Story event, once the ending is picked: an idiot follows every prophet he can
find until the one that works turns out to want something from him.
