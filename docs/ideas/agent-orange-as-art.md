# Agent Orange as Art

Surface how Agent Orange works — talk about it, show it, explain why it matters. Why it
matters: it's **art as code**. The autonomous-agent machinery isn't backstage plumbing,
it's part of the work — and it's also literally *how BadCode operates*, so telling that
story is telling our own.

Adjacent seeds cover the meta layer ([Manifesting BadCode](manifesting-badcode.md) — the
two-idiots joke) and the footage ([Document the Process](document-the-process.md) — film
the sessions). This one is the straight version: **show the machine.** How the agents run,
what they produce, why an art collective is built like software.

Related: [The BadCode Government](badcode-government.md) — the flagship public artifact
the machinery would power.
