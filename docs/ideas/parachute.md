# The Parachute

We have already leapt out of the aeroplane. The argument about whether to jump is over —
the carbon is in the air and the ground is coming up. So the parachute had better work.

The parachute is the technology we haven't finished inventing yet: clean energy abundant
enough to suck the carbon back out of the air and desalinate the sea water. A story about
a parachute jumper, weaving that metaphor — mid-fall, working the canopy.

Crucially this is a **message of hope**, not doom: solar is doing so well that the strong
belief is we *will* reach the stage of using clean energy to desalinate and decarbonise —
reversing climate change, inventing the technology before things get too bad. The chute
opens.

Pairs with the GPOM good-branch node **An Optimistic Lens**
([`future-proof.md`](../stories/gitpush-origin-master/future-proof.md) names desalination
and decarbonisation as physically possible) — this is a story vehicle for that node.
