# Ask Not

The JFK line, run backwards. The original: *"Ask not what your country can do for you —
ask what you can do for your country."* Ours:

> **"Ask not what a politician can do for your country — ask what your country can do
> for a politician."**

Play it as a **skit**: the podium, the crowd, the hand-on-heart cadence of a speech
everybody already knows the shape of — and the words come out the wrong way round. The
delivery stays completely sincere. Nobody in the room notices.

The joke is that it isn't a slip. It's the honest version. Politicians are self-serving
and exploitative, and the country is the resource being asked from. Once the sentence is
inverted it stops being satire and starts being a job description.

## The character

Not JFK — **adjacent**. Someone in that register (the era, the podium, the flag behind,
the accent) without being the man himself. Who exactly is **open** — that's the thing to
decide before this gets made.

## Format thoughts

**Short-form video.** One beat, one line, out. Could run as a repeating format if the
inversion trick has more famous speeches in it — but it stands alone fine.
