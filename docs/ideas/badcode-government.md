# The BadCode Government

A shadow government made of autonomous AI agents running on Agent Orange. Every day the
agents update **their own manifesto** based on the news — and the output is a standing
comparison: here is what the actual cabinet said today, and here is the very contrary view
of what the BadCode government would do. A shadow government that is constantly pointing
out the failings of the existing one, just by existing next to it.

The bite is the cadence: real ministers make statements, the BadCode government answers
them — same news cycle, opposite conclusions, receipts kept.

The agent framework itself (Agent Orange) is the separate parallel project and stays out
of scope in this repo — this idea is the **public-facing art object** that framework
powers. Related: [Agent Orange as Art](agent-orange-as-art.md), and the marketing fleet
already specced in [`docs/marketing/agent-orange.md`](../marketing/agent-orange.md)
(different department, same machinery).
