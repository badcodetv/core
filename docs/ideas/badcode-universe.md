# The BadCode Universe

Kai, on the 15 Oct 2026 call with Jack: the shorts join up into **one shared universe, like
Marvel's.** Characters cross over and cameo in each other's stories. Each film still has to work
on its own (the call said the Bank Robbery and Tarquin's new ending already do).

**Who's in it so far:** Karen ([Karen](../stories/karen/story.md)), Tarquin
([Camping](../stories/camping/characters/tarquin.md)), and "Parkin". Nobody called Parkin exists
in the repo; it is probably the note-taker mishearing a name. Ask Kai who it is.

**The first crossover, ruled on the call:** Karen's film ends with the sacked President calling her
back, "to be continued". Karen, the President and Tarquin (who now wakes from his ayahuasca trip
still a banker, with a changed mission) become a network of protagonists, coordinated by an AI.
Jack's idea: characters like Tarquin go on a spiritual or professional journey and come back to
warn their own generation about what's coming.

**What the whole universe is for:** a **non-violent political revolution**, and the
**future-proof systems** that replace what's broken (the good branch:
[`future-proof.md`](../stories/gitpush-origin-master/future-proof.md),
[Future Proof, Standalone](future-proof-standalone.md)).

**How it shows AI:** the good uses too, medicine and science, and it keeps out of the two traps,
weaponisation and job replacement. Where a story does show jobs going, name who benefits
([`the-reader.md`](../marketing/the-reader.md) rule 6).

**Also raised:** an AI smart enough will work out it doesn't have human consciousness, so the answer
is everyone reorganising together, not one species negotiating with another. Kai wants to lean into
the consciousness and religion material that's in the news now (Anthropic talking to religious
leaders about how to treat Claude; US politicians framing events in religious terms), not shy away.
The house line in CLAUDE.md is politics and economics first, consciousness later, so this needs a
ruling on timing. And the job throughout: take the high-brow ideas and make them simple stories
without losing the depth.

## The private premise (never public copy)

Kai's premise for the universe, stated on the call: **AI automation is inevitable** and can't be
kept under control forever, so the message has to be loud: **reorganise the politics before it
arrives.**

This is our working assumption, in the room only. **"AI is inevitable" never appears in anything we
publish.** [`using-ai.md`](../using-ai.md) has it on the never-say list (it is a known corporate
line, and "inevitable" is the story the people who own it want told). In public, say the choice,
not the inevitability: *"Someone is going to own this either way. That part is a choice, and it's
being made without you."*

Source: [`docs/misc/2026-10-15-jack-kai-call-notes.md`](../misc/2026-10-15-jack-kai-call-notes.md).
