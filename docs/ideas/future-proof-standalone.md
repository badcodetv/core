# Future Proof, Standalone

The good-branch epic — [`future-proof.md`](../stories/gitpush-origin-master/future-proof.md),
redesigning politics with software-engineering tenets — should get its own front door, the
way the Storyverse has one ([Storyverse, Standalone](storyverse-standalone.md)). Not just a
chapter inside GPOM: **lore webpages that the GitPush Origin Master story links out to.**
Maybe a story, maybe an idea, maybe just a page — the point is it becomes addressable on
its own.

And a breakdown: the three tenets could each be **their own story** —

1. **Composition over inheritance**
2. **Continuous integration**
3. **Data-driven decisions**

— each told as *what goes wrong when you don't do it*. Three cautionary tales instead of
one essay. The canon already argues each tenet (`future-proof.md` §1–3); the stories would
dramatize the failure modes.

Canon source of truth stays `stories/gitpush-origin-master/future-proof.md`; this idea is
the front door plus the three-story breakdown. Split into per-story seeds if one starts
developing.

## Format thoughts

Webpages first (the lore front door); each tenet-failure story is a comic candidate.
