# Fact check: welfare and the economy

2026-11-05. One fact-checker's pass over the welfare and economy claims in this strategy
(`README.md` §6, §7 and §8; `ministers/01-chancellor.md`; `ministers/07-work-pensions.md`;
`absorb/landscape.md`). Kai ruled every §8 item option A, so the NI holiday is in reserve and the
Rung Fund is the one trial; the NI-holiday facts are checked anyway because the reserve option
still cites them.

**Grades:** A = primary (gov.uk, ONS, Bank of England, legislation.gov.uk, Hansard, Ofgem, OBR);
B = reputable secondary (IfG, trade press, major outlets, a named MP on the record);
C = weak (single trade or advocacy site, partial page text).
**Verdicts:** TRUE / FALSE / OUTDATED / UNVERIFIED. "Fetched" = the page was opened this session;
"not fetched" = the fact is well established background but no page was opened (about 15 searches
was the budget). Every "not fetched" row must be opened before it goes public.
