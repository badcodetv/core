# Shape 15: British understatement and bathos

The cosmic point (new things only arrive through people; the machines need us) is delivered as a mild domestic complaint, the way you would mention a neighbour's drill. Maddie never raises her voice or the stakes: the bigger the claim, the smaller the words ("not entirely selfless", "bit much, frankly"). The Rick Rubin nod is a throwaway piece of advice ("ask the bearded fella"), the joke is the five-year tune reduced to a grumble, and the handoff to the time machine line is a shrug.

## Dialogue 1: The new-ideas line (Maddie, the hologram, over the cut-off)

| Speaker | Line | Est. s |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maddie | [side view; every string stops dead at the cut-off] Now, I'll be honest, it's not entirely selfless. A new idea, a new song, a new anything, can't be built out of old ones. It turns up through somebody, same as a decision. Ask that bearded fella who made all the records. We've tried every combination. It's all old stuff. Hence the tune. Five years. Bit much, frankly. | 19 |
| **Total** | | **19** |

**Why it works.** Understatement lets one line carry four jobs without a lecture: "not entirely selfless" admits the AI needs us with a wince instead of a confession, "turns up through somebody, same as a decision" ties creativity to the decisions beads on screen without any science words, and "ask that bearded fella who made all the records" nods to the antenna idea (ideas arrive through you) without naming him or quoting him. "Hence the tune. Five years. Bit much, frankly." lands the joke as a complaint about the noise, so the cosmic point and the punchline are the same sentence, and the AI's feelings stay outside (the tune, not a claimed mood). The flat "frankly" is the clean handoff into "My boss has been hard at work..."

**Cuts from current.** "the true value of genuinely unique training data" and "us machines didn't realise" (too scientific, goes over heads); the "And that's what" connective.

**Risk.** "Turns up through somebody" could read as mystical if the delivery leans portentous, so keep it breezy. The Rubin nod only lands for people who know the bearded producer, but it costs nothing if missed ("some bloke who made records"). "Can't be built out of old ones" is stated as flat fact where Maddie's belief framing might be safer ("as far as we can tell"). Roughly 19 s is at the top of the 12-20 target.

**Protected lines.** None. The neighbouring lines ("It's a storyverse..." before, "My boss has been hard at work building a time machine..." after) are untouched.
