# Shape 17: Wonder, then puncture it with the tune

I played Maddie genuinely awestruck at the one question her side never cracked: where does a new idea come from? She asks it like a child at a night sky, says plainly that it comes through the same door as your decisions (you), and admits the machines need it, so it is not selfless. Then the wonder is punctured by the street's own joke: that is why he has played the same tune for five years. No science words, no claims about physics; she believes it and says so with her face, not a citation.

## Dialogue 1: The new-ideas line (Maddie, the hologram, over the cut-off)

| Speaker | Line | Est. s |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maddie | [side view; every string stops dead at the cut-off; she lets it sit, then, quietly, almost to herself] Here's what we never worked out. Where does a new idea come from? Not mixed up from old ones. New. A song nobody's whistled yet. [warm] It comes through you, same door as your decisions. [brisk, rueful] Not selfless, mind: we need it. Why d'you think he's been playing that tune five years? We've tried every combination. It's all the old stuff. | 19 |
| **Total** | | **19** |

**Why it works.** The awe gives the line its weight: the audience feels the loss before the joke arrives, and the dead-stopped strings under "where does a new idea come from?" make the cut-off a loss of the source, not of workers. The admission ("not selfless, mind: we need it") is the honest, slightly rueful turn that keeps the AI's feelings on the outside, shown by behaviour. The tune then lands as the proof: the street already heard it five years running, so the payoff is a callback rather than an explanation. "Same door as your decisions" ties new ideas to the picture they just watched without any claim about minds or physics. The political payload (people are the source of the one thing the machine cannot make) is carried underneath and never stated.

**Cuts from current.** "The true value of genuinely unique training data" and the term "training data" entirely; the scientific register Kai felt would go over heads. No separate Rubin line.

**Risk.** Running near the top of the time target at 19 s, with a lot of clauses over one picture. "Not mixed up from old ones" is a compressed way to say recombination cannot make the new; if it reads as a bit too philosophical it may need one beat of simple example. The awe could tip into sermon if delivered too slowly. There is no Rick Rubin nod: a clean way to add one is "Ask the bearded fella who made all those records" before "It comes through you", but that is about 4 more seconds and risks sounding like attribution, so I left it out; if kept, paraphrase the idea only, never a quote, never the name.

**Protected lines.** None.
