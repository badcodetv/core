# Shape 07: Songwriter testimony

Maddie does not argue; she calls a witness. Every songwriter says the good ones "just arrived", so the songwriters' own testimony carries the claim that new ideas come through you, the same door decisions use. The old-ideas-can-only-be-shuffled point follows from it, then the admission (it's not selfless), then the tune joke closes. Rick Rubin is nodded at as "the big-bearded wizard behind all the hit records" and credited with nothing in quotes, only the general idea that you tune in and receive.

## Dialogue 1: The new-ideas line (Maddie, the hologram, over the cut-off)

| Speaker | Line | Est. s |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maddie | [side view; every string stops dead at the cut-off] Ask anyone who's ever written a song where it came from. They'll tell you it just arrived. Even that big-bearded wizard behind all the hit records says he only ever tunes in. Same door as your decisions, that. | 9 |
| Maddie | [beat] And before you get misty, it's not all for your sake. You can't shuffle old ideas into a new one. We've tried, every combination going. Hence the tune. Five years. Same tune. It's all the old stuff. | 9 |
| **Total** | | **18** |

- **Why it works.** Testimony does the argument without a single claim about physics: Maddie only reports what songwriters say, so she never states anything a physicist could object to, and the audience supplies the "so that's consciousness" themselves. The wizard-producer nod gives anyone who knows the reference a small thrill and costs nobody else anything. "Before you get misty" admits the AI needs them in Maddie's own brisk, unsentimental register, and the tune joke lands the line on the callback the street planted.
- **Cuts from current.** "The true value of genuinely unique training data" goes entirely: no jargon, no "training data", no "us machines didn't realise".
- **Risk.** "Ask anyone who's written a song" is a generalisation (some songwriters say they grind it out); the wizard line may read as a puzzle to viewers who don't know the man, and at about 18 s it sits at the top of the target with little room for the picture to breathe. "Hence the tune" must not sound like a stated moral. Rubin is referenced, not quoted: "says he only ever tunes in" paraphrases his published idea and must stay that loose.
- **Protected lines.** None. The handoff ("My boss has been hard at work building a time machine...") is untouched; this line ends on "the old stuff" so it flows in.
