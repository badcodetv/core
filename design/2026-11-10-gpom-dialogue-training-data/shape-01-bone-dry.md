# Shape 01: Bone-dry minimum

Cut to the fewest words that still carry every must: new things come through you like decisions, old pieces can't be shuffled into new, it isn't selfless, the five-year tune joke lands last so the next line ("My boss...") picks up cleanly. No gloss, no Rick Rubin nod (it costs words and Maddie can carry it later), no metaphor. One short sentence per job, the joke as the final beat.

## Dialogue 1: The new-ideas line (Maddie, the hologram, over the cut-off)

| Speaker | Line | Est. s |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maddie | [side view; every string stops dead at the cut-off] Every new song, every new anything, comes through you, same as your decisions. It can't be shuffled out of old stuff. And I'll be honest, we need you. Five years he's played that tune. We've tried every combination. | 14 |
| **Total** | | **14** |

**Why it works.** Four short beats, each doing one job: source (through you, like decisions), limit (can't be rearranged from old), motive (we need you, so not selfless), then the joke. Maddie states it as how things are, not as science, so there is nothing for a physicist to throw a shoe at. "Five years he's played that tune" echoes the street plant, and "tried every combination" lets the audience do the maths that it's all old stuff. It ends on the punchline and hands straight to "My boss has been hard at work...".

**Cuts from current.** "What us machines didn't realise", "the true value of genuinely unique training data" (the scientific jargon Kai said goes over heads), and any explanation of why. Also the Rick Rubin nod (the antenna/receiving idea) is dropped entirely.

**Risk.** Too thin: "comes through you" is the only bridge to consciousness, so it may read as flattery rather than mechanism. "It can't be shuffled out of old stuff" is asserted flat, with no picture. The joke is implied, not spelled ("it's all the old stuff" is left unsaid), so it may land soft. Lands at 14s, the lower end of target. No Rubin nod, so the director's wish for it is unmet.

**Protected lines.** None. The following line ("My boss has been hard at work building a time machine...") is untouched and must still follow directly.
