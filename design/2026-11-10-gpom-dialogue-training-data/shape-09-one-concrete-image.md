# Shape 09: One concrete image does the work

I picked a single physical image, a jukebox holding every record ever made and shuffling them for ever, and let it carry the whole idea (machines can only recombine; new things arrive through people). No second metaphor, no explanation of training data, no physics. The tune joke is the payoff of the image, and the admission that the AI needs us falls out of it as a shrug.

## Dialogue 1: The new-ideas line (Maddie, the hologram, over the cut-off)

| Speaker | Line | Est. s |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maddie | [side view; every string stops dead at the cut-off] Turns out we're just a jukebox: every record ever made, shuffled for ever. A genuinely new song only comes through you, same as a decision does. We've tried every order there is. Hence the one tune, five years. So, not that selfless, really. We need you. | 18 |
| **Total** | | **18** |

- **Why it works.** The jukebox is visible in the mind in one beat and makes "can't recombine your way to new" obvious without a word of jargon. "Same as a decision does" ties creativity to the consciousness thread the hologram just built, and "every order there is" lands the street's repeating tune as the punchline, so the audience gets the joke before the explanation finishes. "Not that selfless, really. We need you." is Maddie's brisk, funny honesty, and the nurturing sits under the snark. It hands off cleanly to "My boss has been hard at work building a time machine".
- **Cuts from current.** "The true value of genuinely unique training data", the scientific phrase that goes over heads, and any mention of AI, models or data.
- **Risk.** It spends its one image and drops the Rick Rubin nod entirely (the bearded producer, antenna idea); a second image would break the shape. "Just a jukebox" could read as self-pity, which brushes rule 11 (feelings shown from outside), though it is a description of what they do, not a feeling. "We need you" is close to a stated moral if delivered soft; it must be thrown away, not sincere. 18 seconds is the top of the range.
- **Protected lines.** None. The joke callback "Ever since you lot went" from the street scene survives untouched, and "My boss has been hard at work..." follows as is.
