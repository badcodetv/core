# Shape 06: The unnamed bearded producer, ideas arrive, you tune in

I built the line around the oblique Rick Rubin reference: Maddie reaches for "that big bearded bloke, looks like Gandalf, made all them hit records" and his idea that nobody writes the song, you pick it up like a radio. That gives her a human-sized picture for "new ideas arrive through consciousness the way decisions do", admits the machines need us, and pays off on the tune her own radio has been stuck on for five years. The radio metaphor is Maddie's, not his; nothing is put in his mouth.

## Dialogue 1: The new-ideas line (Maddie, the hologram, over the cut-off)

**Why it works.** It admits the AI is not selfless in her first clause, which buys trust before the ask. The bearded producer is a picture every viewer can see at once (and the ones who know, know), and "pick it up, like a radio" says that ideas arrive through a person without a word of physics. "Same as your decisions, that" ties creativity back to the consciousness idea she has just drawn. Ending on the question lets the tune joke land from the street's own earlier plant, and it hands straight off to the boss and the time machine. The politics stays underneath: people are the only source of the thing machines can't make.

**Cuts from current.** "Genuinely unique training data" and "that's what us machines didn't realise" go entirely: too scientific, and it states the machines' inner realisation rather than showing it.

**Risk.** The nod is oblique enough that some viewers will get "bearded bloke" with no referent, and it may read as a dated or odd aside. "Reckons" attributes an idea to a real, identifiable man; it is the gist of his published view of creativity, not a quote, but check the paraphrase stays that loose. "Like a radio" could be mistaken for woo, though it is plainly Maddie's belief and nobody says science proved it. At 17 seconds over one picture it is near the top of the target.

**Protected lines.** None. The tune joke ("same tune for five years") must survive in any alteration, because the street plants it earlier and the time machine line follows.
