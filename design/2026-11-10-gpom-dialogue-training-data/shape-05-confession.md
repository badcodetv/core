# Shape 05: The confession, Maddie owns the selfish motive first

Maddie opens by admitting the selfish motive, almost embarrassed, before any explanation, so the audience trusts her before the idea arrives. The idea then comes in her own plain words: new things arrive through a person (the aerial picture, which is the Rick Rubin idea without the name), machines can't build a new idea out of old ones, and the tune joke lands it. Her belief is stated as belief; no physics claims; the AI's feelings are shown only through the tune.

## Dialogue 1: The new-ideas line (Maddie, the hologram, over the cut-off)

| Speaker | Line | Est. s |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maddie | [side view; every string stops dead at the cut-off; she doesn't look at the hologram] Right, confession: it's not all for your sake. A new song, a new anything, comes in through a person, same as a decision does. You lot are the aerials. We've got no signal. You can't build a new idea out of old ones, see. Five years he's played that tune. We've tried every combination. It's all the old stuff. | 18 |
| **Total** | | **18** |

**Why it works.** The embarrassed admission up front disarms the room and makes "he needs you" a human, funny stroke rather than a boast or a threat; it answers "so it's not selfless" before anyone can think it. "Aerials" carries the Rubin idea (creators receive, they don't invent) with no name and no invented quote, and it hands the gleaming old-man-with-a-beard producer to anyone who knows. "You can't build a new idea out of old ones" is plain enough for a high street, and the tune lands as the punchline, so the time machine line follows cleanly as the next confession of what he's tried.

**Cuts from current.** "The true value of genuinely unique training data" and every trace of machine jargon: no "training data", no "synthesise". The line no longer sounds like a lecture slide.

**Risk.** Opening on "confession" could read as comic rather than quietly sad over the dead strings; the aerials line is a metaphor and could be heard as claiming science (it is framed as Maddie's picture, "see", not a fact); "we've got no signal" might be mistaken for the AI's own feelings (it is a plain report, shown from outside). Dense for 18 seconds: if it runs long, drop "You lot are the aerials" last-but-one clause first.

**Protected lines.** None.
