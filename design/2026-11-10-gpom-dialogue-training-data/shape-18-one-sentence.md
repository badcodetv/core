# Shape 18: One sentence, under 8 seconds

One breath of line: the whole job (not selfless, new ideas only arrive through people, recombining old ones fails, the tune joke) squeezed into a single sentence-pair that lands on "tune" and hands straight to the time machine line. Everything that can be implied is left to the picture (the dead-stopped strings) and the street's tune from earlier.

## Dialogue 1: The new-ideas line (Maddie, the hologram, over the cut-off)

| Speaker | Line | Est. s |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maddie | [side view; every string stops dead at the cut-off] We need you, love: new ideas only come through you. Shuffling the old ones got us five years of that tune. | 7.5 |
| **Total** | | **7.5** |

- **Why it works.** "We need you, love" is the admission that it isn't selfless, in Maddie's warm West Country register, and it is the only emotion claimed (said outright by her, shown by the tune). "New ideas only come through you" carries the consciousness link without a single science word, since the previous beats already established that decisions come through you. "Shuffling the old ones" is the recombination point in plain speech, and it pays off the street's tune joke on the last word, so the laugh is the final beat before "My boss has been hard at work...".
- **Cuts from current.** "True value of genuinely unique training data" (jargon, above people's heads); the explicit "arrive the same way decisions do" bridge (left implied by the picture of beads); the "we tried every combination" list; any Rick Rubin nod (no room; the "come through you" phrasing is the antenna idea in passing, unnamed).
- **Risk.** Too compressed: "new ideas come through you" may read as flattery rather than a claim linked to decisions, and a viewer may not connect "that tune" to the street line unless it was heard recently. "Shuffling" is slightly lossy for "can't be made from old ones". No Gandalf/producer wink.
- **Protected lines.** None. The following lines ("My boss has been hard at work building a time machine...") are untouched and the handoff works because the last word is a punchline pause.
