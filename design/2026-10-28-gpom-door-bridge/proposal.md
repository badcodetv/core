## Why B is dull (so the bridge does not repeat the mistake)

1. **B replays A beat 0 to a second audience.** Maddie already gave *us* the whole pitch on the high street:
   "he misses you something rotten", "nobody's home in any of them", "consciousness is the missing part".
   The doorstep says it again to the humans. The viewer sits through a scene they have already had.
2. **Nothing turns** (story-craft principle 7). 2J has one line of resistance, then a sales pitch nobody pushes
   against. That is exposition wearing a scene's clothes.
3. **D already carries the facts.** D03 "It's the time machine. You do understand what this will cost you." and
   D04 the lady's "worst possible outcome for humanity… I want to take that chance" are the machine, the cost and
   the vote, said once, in the room, in front of the thing itself. B pre-empts both and blunts them.

## The continuity contract

For the jump not to read as an error the viewer must be able to add up, from picture, that (a) the humans know a
robot is at the door, (b) they know whose robot it is, (c) she asked them something, (d) they decided. None of
that needs a word about the time machine. (c) is the trick: **show the pitch, never let us hear it.** The viewer
already holds the content (A beat 0); watching it land is Hitchcock's bomb, hearing it again is the wait.

What B owns that A and D do not, and the bridge keeps: the tannoy joke (Kai: "Love it"), the lady's lip-synced
"Your boss killed everyone we ever loved." (kept through every draft), and the doorway shot Kai likes (B15r4 / V11).
Everything else in 2J is explanation and goes.

## Ranking

1. **5, the door shut.** The only one that gives a hundred people a night to decide and still says nothing.
2. **1, the short door.** 5 without the night: same footage, ten seconds shorter, and a doorstep decision.
3. **4, the smash cut.** Kai's instinct, costed: zero new footage, two best shots lost, naive humans.
4. **2, the door three times.** The most cinematic on paper and the most likely to come back from Flow wrong.
5. **3, the gesture.** Sentiment on a shot Kai has already cut once.

**Recommend 5.** Build it as a new cut of the B folder (tag `bridge`, Premiere `gpom-3-B-door-r5`, r4 to
"zz old sequences"), note it on B's timeline page, and if Kai wants it faster drop steps 7–8: that is option 1,
no re-work.

The one craft line to hold whichever he picks: **in C, Derek's "We're not coming back, are we?" is now the first
time the audience hears there is a cost.** It arrives inside a joke about a toilet seat, which is story over
sermon, and it is why the walk works without the talk.
