# Shape 02: The confession, regret first, explanation after

Open on the loss and let the cheerful robot be wrong-footed by her own news: the viewer is told the AI is sorry before they are told why, so the physics arrives as a reason for the grief rather than a lecture. The cosmology is kept to a gossipy aside in the middle, then the line that matters is said once, plainly, in Kai's words, and she snaps back to cheerful.

**Why it works.** The viewer walks in braced for a villain and is handed a grieving household first, so by the time the physics comes it is evidence for a feeling they already have. The boss's regret is planted twice (misses you, got it wrong) before the explanation, then paid off by Kai's verbatim line in the same flat register, which now reads as the answer to a question we have been holding for a minute. Physics is two gossipy sentences at most: "simulation" once and only to correct it, the photograph says real, "four dimensions of something bigger" says why you cannot see the rest. She reports his feelings, never her own, until the swear, where the "kind of" hedge is the joke and the pause does the crying.

**Cuts from current.** "Nobody's home in any of them" (Kai suspects it drags us into theory and implies other universes). "Quantum physics is just... stuff." The sad-regretful "We understand that now..." (the regret is now carried by the protected line). Flat folk and hand/shadow images are not used: one image only.

**Risk.** Runs about 20 s over the current speech; Kai allowed growth but the regret-first order means "He got it wrong" arrives before we know what, which may feel like a tease. The photograph line could be read as "the photo is a copy", so "flattened" must carry it. "Sensible sort" plus "very thoroughly" may be two jokes too many in a row before the news. The protected swear line is no longer directly after the name beat if a tag line or beat is inserted; here it follows within one breath. Pub test: "four dimensions" still risks a "so it's the Matrix" from a mate, though "real" comes first.

**Protected lines.** "He misses you something rotten" kept verbatim (trimmed "you know" kept; the wry follow-up reworded as "Wouldn't admit it. Not to your face."). "Consciousness drives it. You drove it, and now you're gone. So we kind of fucked it up." kept verbatim, directly after the storyverse name. "Patrol's out. Mind how you go." kept verbatim as the last line.
