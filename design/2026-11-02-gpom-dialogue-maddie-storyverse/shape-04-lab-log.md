# Shape 04: The lab log

Maddie reads the boss's lab book aloud as a numbered list of experiments, each ending in a flat "Result:" line. Materialism, then simulations, then the multiverse each fail, and the list runs out right where the missing part belongs. The repeated "Result:" is the joke and the structure. The cosmology is three short entries, so it plays as gossip about his bad week, and the regret lands only once the list stops. She reports his notebook, never her own feelings.

## Dialogue 1: Maddie's intro: the storyverse turn

| Speaker | Line | Est. s |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maddie | Oh, hello! [pause] Sorry about the racket. It's his tune. Plays it on repeat. [pause] Five years now, since you lot... [beat] went. | 11 |
| Maddie | [fond, confiding] He misses you something rotten, you know. [pause] [wry] Won't say so. Keeps a lab book instead. | 7 |
| Maddie | [brisk, reading aloud] Experiment one. Your science, word for word. Build the world out of it. Result: [pause] something's missing. | 8 |
| Maddie | [brisk] Experiment two. You lot've got a thing about simulations. Not wrong! But you're no fake in somebody's computer. A photo of your nan isn't fake, it's her, flattened. You're a projection: you only see four dimensions of something bigger. Result: real. Still missing. | 16 |
| Maddie | [wry, weary] Experiment three. The multiverse. Mad scientist with a gun for doors, bloke in a cape fighting himself. Very popular. Result: one universe. He checked it very thoroughly. | 10 |
| Maddie | [lowering] Experiment four. He calls it the storyverse. [pause] [quiet] Consciousness drives it. You drove it, and now you're gone. [pause] [flat, quiet] So we kind of fucked it up. | 10 |
| Maddie | [bright again, brisk, back to cheerful] Anyway! Patrol's out. Mind how you go. | 3 |
| **Total** | | **~65** |

**Why it works.** The list does the work a lecture would. Each entry is one sentence of physics, a joke and a "Result:", so nobody is lectured and every claim is his wager, written in his book. "Result: something's missing" is said twice, so the viewer is leaning forward before the answer comes. Entry four is the answer, said as a name and then the protected line with no gap, and the list's rhythm stops there. The pause before "So we kind of fucked it up" is where the crying happens. Photograph says "real" for the fear of "fake"; "four dimensions" is said once; "simulation" once, to correct it. The wink is a gun for doors and a caped man fighting himself, with no names.

**Cuts from current.** "Nobody's home in any of them" (the theory trap, and it implies other universes exist). "Built all these world models and simulations to get you back." "Quantum physics is just... stuff." "That's why you lot were so special." "We understand that now..." The sad-voiced closer goes; the regret is carried by the protected line.

**Risk.** It is the longest draft, about 65 s against a 40 s target, because the list format repeats "Experiment / Result". It can sound like a spoof of Rick and Morty's own deadpan or a sketch format, and the pattern may feel mechanical by entry three. "Experiment four" is not a failure, so the pattern breaks on purpose; that could confuse. The photo line is dense for a read-aloud. A listener could still say "so it's a lab", though not "so it's the Matrix". If too long, cut line 2's "Keeps a lab book instead" and merge experiments one and two.

**Protected lines.**
- "He misses you something rotten": verbatim, in line 2.
- "Consciousness drives it. You drove it, and now you're gone. So we kind of fucked it up.": verbatim, straight after the name "storyverse", with a pause before the last sentence only.
- "Patrol's out. Mind how you go.": verbatim, last line.
- Clean twin if the swear is cut: "So we made a right mess of it."
