# Shape 07: One object carries it (the radio)

Every explanation is made with a single prop in the scene: the radio playing his tune. Maddie never leaves it. It is the multiverse joke, the "real, not fake" line and the regret, so the cosmology costs no extra sentences. The song is real and the radio is not the band: a flat little box that carries one song, while the band is bigger than the box. The radio cuts out on "gone", and the silence is the grief.

## Dialogue 1: Maddie's intro: the storyverse turn

| Maddie | Oh, hello! [pause] Sorry about the racket. [she thumps the radio on her trolley] | 3.5 |
| Maddie | It's his tune. Plays it on repeat. Five years now, since you lot... [beat] went. | 6.5 |
| Maddie | [fond, confiding] He misses you something rotten, you know. [pause] [wry] He wouldn't admit it, of course. | 6 |
| Maddie | He's a stickler for your science, my boss. Word for word. Built every world it said he could, and the tune came out right. Every note. [pause] Nobody humming along. | 9.5 |

| Maddie | [bright again, brisk] Anyway! [radio crackles back on, mid-tune] Patrol's out. Mind how you go. | 4 |

**Why it works.** The viewer has to hold one thing, not three: a radio. The multiverse wink becomes flipping through stations (every channel plays the same tune, still nobody humming), so the weariness is physical business, not another paragraph. "Real" lands before "projection" because the radio is obviously real and plainly not the band, and "you drove it" gets a second meaning: you were the band, and the box is still playing your song. The cut-out on "gone" lets the pause do the crying, and the tune returning under "Anyway!" is the cheer coming back on like a battery. Simulation is named once, as gossip, and only to correct it.

**Cuts from current.** "Nobody's home in any of them" and "quantum physics is just stuff" (both dropped as theory bait). The "sad, regretful, quiet / we understand that now" line (replaced by the three protected sentences and the silence). "Built all these world models and simulations to get you back" (replaced by the stickler-for-your-science beat). The word "quantum" is gone entirely.

**Risk.**
- A radio implies a broadcaster, so "the band is bigger than the box" could read as "a signal from somewhere else", which drifts toward the alien-computer idea we are denying. A mate could say "so it's a transmission, then?".
- It is the longest of the options (about 65 s), and the middle block carries the most weight on the prop.
- It needs the radio visible and in her hands for the whole speech, which changes the staging. The cut-out and return need a sound designer to hit them.
- Four dimensions "of it" is fuzzy about what "it" is; the box analogy does not prove it, it only reassures. The "no cheer drained" tag is a direction note for the reader and should be rewritten before TTS.
- A clean twin for beat 6 if the swear lands badly: "So we made a right mess of it."

**Protected lines.**
- "He misses you something rotten" survives verbatim ("you know" and "of course" added around it).
- "Consciousness drives it. You drove it, and now you're gone. So we kind of fucked it up." survives verbatim; the only change is that the radio cuts out and a hold sits between "gone" and "So".
- "Patrol's out. Mind how you go." survives verbatim.
