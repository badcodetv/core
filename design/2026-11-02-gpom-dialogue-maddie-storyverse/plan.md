## The job of the speech

The flip. The viewer has just watched the AI take the world. Maddie's first speech turns it:
the AI regrets it, because it learned the world needed consciousness, and we are gone.

Beats, in Kai's order:
1. Hello, sorry about the racket (his tune) — five years since you lot went.
2. He misses you something rotten. *(protected)*
3. He was a materialist: took your science verbatim, built the worlds, something was missing.
4. Tried the multiverse too (wink: the mad scientist with the portal gun, the man in a cape fighting his other selves). Didn't work.
5. The turn: your simulation hunch isn't wrong — but it's not a fake in an alien computer. You're the real deal, a projection: you only see four dimensions of something bigger. One universe. He calls it the storyverse.
6. Kai's words (2026-11-02, protected): "Consciousness drives it. You drove it, and now you're gone. So we kind of fucked it up."
7. Anyway! Patrol's out. Mind how you go. *(protected; pays off on the bus)*

## Rules for the writers

- Maddie: warm, West Country, cheerful, a delivery robot. Story over sermon.
- Wink, never name (no "Rick", "Morty", "Marvel").
- A wager in her mouth, never a proof: no "science has shown", no "quantum" + a mind word,
  no "superposition", "two places at once", "holography", "energy/vibration".
- "Simulation" allowed ONCE, only to correct it (Kai's call; departs from doctrine's never-say list).
- "Storyverse" said once, as the boss's name for it.
- Swear optional: "we kind of fucked it up" offered in at least one draft, clean in another.
- Target: as short as it can be; the current speech is ~40 s, no hard cap.
