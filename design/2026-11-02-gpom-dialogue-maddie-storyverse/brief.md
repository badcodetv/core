# Writers' brief: Maddie's intro, the storyverse turn (2026-11-02)

## 1. The job
This is the film's flip. The viewer has just watched an AI take the world and remove us, and they are braced for a villain. A cheerful delivery robot then tells them the AI regrets it. It took our science as gospel, tried the multiverse, and found the missing piece was consciousness: ours. We're gone, so it broke the thing that made the world go. The regret persuades, not the physics.

## 2. The seven beats (Kai's order)
1. Hello, sorry about the racket (his tune). Five years since you lot went. Said aloud, no card.
2. **"He misses you something rotten."** (verbatim; plants the regret)
3. He was a materialist. He took your science word for word, built the worlds, and something was missing.
4. He tried the multiverse too. Wink at the portal-gun scientist and the caped man fighting his other selves. It didn't work for him.
5. Your simulation hunch isn't wrong, but you're no fake in an alien computer. You're real, a projection: you only see four dimensions of something bigger. One universe. He calls it the storyverse.
6. **"Consciousness drives it. You drove it, and now you're gone. So we kind of fucked it up."** (verbatim, straight after the name)
7. Anyway! **"Patrol's out. Mind how you go."** (verbatim; pays off on the bus)

## 3. The projection
"Real" lands before "projection", because the fear is "fake". Use one image per job and never stack flatness images.
- **Photograph** (canon: "real the way a photograph is real") says *real*: "A photo of your nan isn't fake. It's her, flattened."
- **Hand and shadow:** "Your hand's real. So's its shadow. You've only ever met the shadow." For tight time.
- **Flat folk** (Flatland) says *why you can't see the rest*: a ball passes through and they see a circle grow and shrink. About 9 s.

Best pairing: photograph plus flat folk. Avoid Plato's cave (appearances are false), the film projector (the Matrix again), the shadow puppet (it adds a puppeteer), the tesseract (a puzzle, and too Marvel) and the hologram (banned: wrong physics). Pub test: if a mate can say "so it's the Matrix", rewrite.

## 4. Switch-off words
- **Physicists:** pixels, code, grid, Planck length as the smallest unit, "the multiverse is disproved" (he tried it and it didn't work *for him*), "science has shown", "proven", "quantum" next to a mind word, superposition, "two places at once", holography.
- **Ordinary viewers:** lecturing, homework, "you've been duped", "reality is an illusion", energy/vibration/frequency, manifest, awakening, higher self, "dimension" twice in a sentence.
- **Simulation family:** "simulation" ONCE, only to correct it. Never admins, red pills, glitches, NPCs or base reality.
- **"Storyverse"** once, as the boss's name.
- **Safe:** photograph, shadow, projection, real, reckon, bet.

## 5. Comedy and the regret
- Make the physics sound like gossip, a throwaway aside ("you lot've got a thing about simulations").
- One domestic prop, handled once (Good Place cursive, Men in Black cat collar).
- She's a bit weary of the multiverse.
- Deflate before they can dunk ("he checked it very thoroughly"). A shrugged wager beats a proof.
- Two or three sentences of cosmology at most, then plain words for the line that matters.
- **The regret:** cheer stays on. She reports his feelings, never her own. One short flat sentence cracks it and the pause does the crying. No pleading. One swear, late, "kind of" kept; the hedge is the joke. At least one draft offers a clean twin ("we made a right mess of it"). Jokes never outlast the news.

## 6. Maddie's voice
Warm, West Country and chatty, a bit gossipy about her boss ("he reckons", "don't tell him I said"). Wink, never name: no Rick, Morty or Marvel. Every claim is his wager. Borrow shape from Adams, Pratchett and The Good Place; quote none of them.

## 7. Notes
- "Nobody's home in any of them" is optional. Kai suspects it drags us into theory, and it implies the other universes exist.
- Length: as short as possible, with no cap. The current speech runs about 40 s.
