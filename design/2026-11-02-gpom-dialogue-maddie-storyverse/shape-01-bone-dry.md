# Shape 01: Bone-dry, fewest words that carry all seven beats

I wrote the speech as seven beats and gave each the fewest words that still land it. Every aside, repeat and second example is cut. The physics is a throwaway, with one image for "real" (the photograph) and one clause for "why we can't see it" (four dimensions of something bigger). The regret gets the room that the cuts free up. Cheer stays on throughout, she reports his feelings and never her own, and the swear stays late with "kind of" kept.

## Dialogue 1: Maddie's intro: the storyverse turn

| Speaker | Line | Est. s |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maddie | Oh, hello! [pause] Sorry about the racket. His tune. Five years now, since you lot went. | 5.0 |
| Maddie | [fond, confiding] He misses you something rotten. | 2.5 |
| Maddie | He was a materialist, see. Built worlds off your science, word for word. [pause] Something was missing. | 5.0 |
| Maddie | [wry, weary] Tried the multiverse, too. Portal guns. Blokes in capes fighting themselves. Nothing. | 4.5 |
| Maddie | You lot reckon it's a simulation. Not wrong. But you're not fake. You're real. Like a photo of your nan's real. | 6.0 |
| Maddie | You only see four dimensions of something bigger. One universe. The storyverse, he calls it. | 5.0 |
| Maddie | [quiet] Consciousness drives it. You drove it, and now you're gone. [pause] So we kind of fucked it up. | 7.0 |
| Maddie | [bright again, brisk] Anyway! Patrol's out. Mind how you go. | 2.5 |
| **Total** | | **37.5** |

**Why it works.** Each beat is one or two short sentences, so nothing gets to feel like a lecture. The physics sounds like gossip: "you lot reckon it's a simulation. Not wrong." is the only time "simulation" appears, and it is there to be corrected. "Real" lands before "projection", which answers the fear of "fake". The photograph says real, and "four dimensions of something bigger" says why we can't see the rest. The multiverse wink is a list of two images and a flat "Nothing", so she deflates it before anyone can dunk. "Storyverse" is delivered as the boss's name, once. The saved seconds go to the pause before the protected line, so the regret has room to land.

**Cuts from current.**
- "Plays it on repeat" and "Ever since you lot... went", folded into a single line.
- "He wouldn't admit that, of course."
- "Nobody's home in any of them" (Kai suspects it drags us into theory and implies other universes exist).
- "Built all these world models and simulations to get you back."
- "Quantum physics is just... stuff", and "That's why you lot were so special".
- "We understand that now...", replaced by the protected line.
- No domestic prop, no "don't tell him I said", no "he reckons" gossip beyond one "see".
- The word "projection" is also cut. The photograph carries the idea, and "real" is the point.

**Risk.**
- Too clipped and a bit brisk: with the regret this thin, the gap between "multiverse. Nothing." and the storyverse reveal may feel hurried, and the cheerful voice may not carry the crack.
- "Not wrong" is a small claim, and a physicist could hear "simulation... not wrong" and stop listening before the correction arrives.
- "Projection" is never said, so "a photo of your nan" has to do all the work. A mate could still say "so it's like a picture, so we're flat?" and the four-dimensions line only half answers it.
- The wink is thin: "portal guns" and "blokes in capes" must be read as a gag, and without the weary tone it may not register as a joke.
- 37.5 s is only slightly under today's ~40 s, since the new must-haves (five years, the multiverse, the storyverse) fill the room that the cuts made.

**Protected lines.**
- "He misses you something rotten." survives verbatim, with only a stage direction added.
- "Consciousness drives it. You drove it, and now you're gone. So we kind of fucked it up." survives verbatim, with a [pause] inserted before the last sentence (the pause does the crying).
- "Patrol's out. Mind how you go." survives verbatim, with "Anyway!" ahead of it.
