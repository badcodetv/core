# Shape 08: The photograph as spine

One physical photograph (a stranger's nan, found in a skip) is planted early as gossip, doubted by the boss's materialism, held up as the answer to "fake", and lowered at the regret. Every cosmology beat hangs off that one prop, so there is a single image, handled for several jobs. Flat folk is the only second image, and it does a different job: why you can't see the rest. The physics is gossip, the regret is the quiet line, and the prop is the only domestic joke.

**Why it works.** Kai's own image ("real the way a photograph is real") stops being a throwaway and becomes the one thing the viewer can see: Maddie literally holds the answer to "am I fake?" in her hand. "Real" lands before "projection" because the nan is a person we already believe in. The materialist's line ("paper and silver") is the same prop seen coldly, so the turn from his view to the storyverse is the same photo seen warmly. A skip-found stranger's nan is also the regret in miniature: he keeps a picture of people because the people are gone, and he doesn't know whose they are. Physics stays gossip ("a thing about simulations", "fair play"), and "simulation" is said once, only to correct it. The multiverse wink is two descriptions and a shrug, with a deflating "checked it very thoroughly". The regret has no pleading: the cheer stays on, the pause does the crying, the hedge in "kind of" is the joke, and the photo going down is the visual cue for it.

**Cuts from current.** "Nobody's home in any of them" (the theory trap, and it implies empty universes). "Quantum physics is just... stuff" and "That's why you lot were so special" (replaced by beats 3 and 6). "We understand that now..." (the regret now rides on the protected swear line instead).

**Risk.** At about 75 s it is nearly double the current 40 s, the longest of any shape, and the middle (beats 5 and 6) carries two images and a number. "Flattened" used for the photo and "flat folk" for the ball can read as a stacked flatness image, and the pub test may produce "so we're a picture, like the Matrix". The nan prop needs a physical photo on screen, which is a visual commitment for scene A. "Projected" is the only place the word lands, so it is easy to miss. "He keeps a photo of somebody's nan" could also make the boss sound sweet before the regret has earned it, or make a viewer ask why a stranger's nan. And "Won't say so" slightly pre-empts the regret by hinting it twice.

**Protected lines.** All three survive verbatim: "He misses you something rotten" (with ", you know" kept from the current draft), "Consciousness drives it. You drove it, and now you're gone. So we kind of fucked it up." (straight after the storyverse name, the only change being a [pause] before the swear), and "Patrol's out. Mind how you go." A clean twin for the swear, if wanted: "So we made a right mess of it."
