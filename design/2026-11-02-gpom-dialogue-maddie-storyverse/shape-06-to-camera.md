# Shape 06: Straight to camera, the simulation fixation

Maddie drops the scene and talks to the lens, as a gossip talking to you about your own habit: "you lot've got a thing about simulations". The physics is her aside, the photograph and the flat folk carry the "real, not fake" job, and the cheer holds until one flat sentence cracks it. It follows Kai's own spoken version beat by beat (his order, his protected lines), pushed so far that she addresses the viewer directly the whole way through.

- **Why it works.** It is Kai's own speech, so it sounds like someone who means it. Talking to the lens lets "you lot" land as the viewers themselves, and it makes the fixation a piece of gossip rather than a lecture. "Real" arrives before "projection" (the photo of your nan), the flat folk explain why we cannot see the rest, and "simulation" is said once, only to be corrected. The regret is one flat sentence and a pause, with the cheer back on straight after; the swear is late and the "kind of" is the joke.
- **Cuts from current.** "Nobody's home in any of them" (Kai suspects it drags us into theory and implies other universes exist). "Built all these world models and simulations to get you back." "Quantum physics is just... stuff." "That's why you lot were so special." "We understand that now..." and the sad-regretful tag, replaced by the flat line.
- **Risk.** Longest of the shapes at about 73 s against the current 40, and the middle is a run of cosmology (two images plus the multiverse wink) before the line that matters; the flat-folk sentence is the first to cut if the director wants it shorter. Talking down the lens for over a minute may read as a lecture if the TTS delivery is not light. "Dimensions" appears once, but "something bigger" must not drift into woo. The pub test: someone can still say "so it's the Matrix", which the photo line is there to block. She also reports the boss's wager as fact in places ("Took your science word for word"); keep "he reckons" energy in the read.
- **Protected lines.** "He misses you something rotten" survives verbatim, with ", you know" kept. "Consciousness drives it. You drove it, and now you're gone. So we kind of fucked it up." survives verbatim, with a long pause inserted before "So" (Kai's order has it straight after the name, which it still is). "Patrol's out. Mind how you go." survives verbatim. "Five years since you lot went" is said aloud, no card.
