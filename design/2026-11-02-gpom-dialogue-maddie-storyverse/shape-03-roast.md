# Shape 03: The roast, the multiverse wink leads

I let the multiverse be the first real laugh and made the joke do the arguing. Maddie sounds weary of it, like it is a fad she has had to hear about for years. She winks at the portal-gun scientist and the caped man fighting his other selves by describing them and never naming them. Then she says "he checked it very thoroughly" before anyone can dunk on it. The physics is one gossipy aside, the photograph says "real", and the two protected regret lines land after the jokes have stopped. She reports the boss's feelings and never her own, and the pause does the crying.

**Why it works.** The multiverse is the one bit of the cosmology the audience already has a feeling about, so it gets the laugh and buys the trust. "Mad scientist with the door gun" and "fella in a cape" are winks that anyone who has seen the films will get, and anyone who hasn't will still hear a tired robot being rude about a fad. "He checked it very thoroughly" deflates the objection in advance, and it is his wager, so no physicist is told the multiverse is disproved. "Simulation" is said once, only to correct it. "A photo of your nan isn't fake. It's her, flattened" answers the fear of being fake first, and "two dimensions, you've got four" turns the photograph into the four-dimension point without a lecture or a second image. The jokes stop at "storyverse" and nothing funny follows the news. The swear arrives late, flat, with "kind of" kept, and the hedge is the joke.

**Cuts from current.** "Nobody's home in any of them" goes, because it implies the other universes exist and drags us into theory. "Quantum physics is just... stuff" goes, because it puts quantum next to a mind word. "That's why you lot were so special" and "We understand that now..." go, because they have Maddie speak her own feelings. Her sad, regretful voice is replaced by the flat line and the pause.

**Risk.** It runs about 58 s against the current 40. The roast could read as the film mocking the Rick and Morty and Marvel fans we want. "Door gun" is a clumsy description, so it may need a better prop word. Winking at two famous things might also feel like a parody. "Fella in a cape" could be taken for Superman or Batman instead of the Marvel multiverse. "Four dimensions" is a bare claim that a physicist might nitpick, since the photo is two-dimensional and we are described as four, but it is the director's own wording. The photo-of-your-nan image is the only one in use, so there is no flat-folk explanation of why we can't see the rest.

**Protected lines.** All three survive verbatim. "He misses you something rotten" is kept inside "He misses you something rotten, you know." The Kai line is kept word for word, with a pause and tags only. "Patrol's out. Mind how you go." is untouched. The surrounding "Wouldn't admit it" and "Anyway!" are kept from the current script.
