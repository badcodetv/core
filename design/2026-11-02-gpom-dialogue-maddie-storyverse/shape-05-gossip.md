# Shape 05: Gossip, Maddie telling tales on her boss

Maddie is the office gossip and the boss is out of the room. Every piece of cosmology comes out as tattling, so the physics is only ever "what he reckons". "Don't tell him I said" runs through it. The regret is the one moment she stops gossiping.
