# R2: the projection idea in plain clothes

Sources: repo = `docs/stories/storyverse/` (doctrine.md, confession.md, research/dimensions-and-time.md, research/word-resistance-and-register.md, research/sf-precedent-and-craft.md). Web = 4 searches. Solidity: SOLID (primary/textbook or ruled canon), OK (several secondary sources agree), MINE (my judgement, untested on audiences).

## The floor

- It is a wager, not a result. Doctrine pillar 6 grades it B/C: "projector = consciousness is the bet" (doctrine.md). SOLID.
- Not holography: that arrow runs the other way, and a physicist "will catch it immediately" (research/dimensions-and-time.md, intro and 1.3). Honest sources for our arrow are Kaluza-Klein, Bars's 2T "shadows" and plain Flatland allegory, "not physics, and that's fine" (same file). SOLID.
- "Not a simulation, a projection; real the way a photograph is real" is canon. word-resistance 8.1 calls the photograph line "the best line in the canon" and bans the simulation family. Kai allows "simulation" once, to correct it (plan.md, kai-maddie-intro.md).
- "Four dimensions" means three of space plus time. Never "4+2" (banned, doctrine pillar 3).

## The three best for a robot, under 10 seconds

These are my drafts, not quotes. The writers should beat them.

1. **Photograph.** "A photo of your nan isn't fake. It's her, flattened. That's you." Canon, one breath, says "real" by itself.
2. **The bigger hand.** "Your hand's real. So's its shadow. You've only ever met the shadow." Makes the shadow real first, then turns it into "you only see part".
3. **Flat folk.** "Picture flat folk. A ball comes through. All they see is a circle, growing, shrinking. They'd swear that's everything. Four dimensions, you lot. There's more." The only one that explains why you cannot see the rest. About 9 seconds.

Best pairing: 1 for "real", 3 for "why you can't see it". Use 2 if time is tight.

## Never-say check

- Pass: photograph, shadow, "projection", "real the way" (word-resistance 8.2: "Keep. These are the best assets").
- Fail: hologram, "reality is an illusion", energy/vibration, quantum with a mind word, "science has shown", "two places at once", superposition, simulation-family words other than the single correcting use.
- "Consciousness" must stay a wager ("he reckons", "his bet"); never unhedged (word-resistance 8.2).
- "Nobody's home in any of them": Kai flagged it as suspect; it also implies the other universes are empty, which fights the one-universe point.

## Takeaways for the writers

- "Real" must arrive before "projection". The audience's fear is "fake".
- Photograph leads; find it a fresh body, not a fresh image.
- A second image must do a different job: photograph says "real", Flatland says "why you can't see more". Do not stack flatness images.
- Skip cave, projector, puppet, bank-card hologram. Each implies illusion, a puppeteer or the wrong physics.
- "Four dimensions" is a number she says, not a lesson. Never "4+2" or "higher self".
- Use "reckon", "bet", "my boss's bet". Never "proven" or "science says".
- Pub test: if a scientist can say "that's holography" or a mate can say "so it's the Matrix", rewrite.
- Concrete and warm wins: nan, hand, Greenland. Never "dimension" twice in one sentence.
