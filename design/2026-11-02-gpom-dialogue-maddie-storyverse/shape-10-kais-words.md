# Shape 10: Kai's own words, barely touched

I took Kai's voice-note (feedback/chat-2026-11-02/kai-maddie-intro.md) and trimmed it into a speech, changing as little as possible. His order, his phrases ("you lot", "the real deal", "fake simulation in some alien computer", "four dimensions", "my boss calls this the storyverse", "we kind of fucked it up") stay almost as spoken. I only cut stumbles and repeats, added the West Country warmth and the pauses, and dropped "Nobody's home in any of them" as he suspected it was the theory trap. Nothing new is invented except the stage directions.

- **Why it works.** It is the director's own thinking, so the order is already right: regret planted early, the wink, the correction of simulation, the name, then the hit straight after it. Because the words are Kai's, they sound spoken, not written, and the physics reads as gossip ("you lot have this thing about simulations"). The cheer stays on until the one flat sentence, and the pause before "kind of fucked it up" does the crying.
- **Cuts from current.** "Nobody's home in any of them" and the "built all these world models and simulations to get you back" line. "Quantum physics is just... stuff" and "that's why you lot were so special". The sad "We understand that now..." (the regret now lives in the swear line instead).
- **Risk.** It is the longest of the shapes (about 58 s against 40 s now) and the middle is one dense run of cosmology: simulation, real, projection, four dimensions, in one breath. "A projection" is dropped in with no image, so a mate could still say "so it's the Matrix" until "one universe" lands. "Dimensions" appears once, which is safe, but "projection" is bare and could sound like jargon without a photograph line. "Mad scientist" and "bloke in a cape" are close to naming, though no show is named. Kai's spoken "you only operate in four dimensions" is slightly physics-flavoured.
- **Protected lines.** All three survive verbatim: "He misses you something rotten" (with "you know" after it), "Consciousness drives it. You drove it, and now you're gone. So we kind of fucked it up." (a pause and a flat delivery tag inserted, wording intact), and "Patrol's out. Mind how you go."
