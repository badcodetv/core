# Shape 08: The place is the argument (the dark street lamp)

I picked one concrete object on the empty Swindon high street and let it carry the whole physics: a motion-sensor street lamp that only comes on when somebody walks past. Maddie points at it before the hologram starts, and it is the "renders only when you look" idea, said in a way a working-class reader already knows from every dark car park. She never explains the physics in the abstract; every abstract claim is "same as the lamp". It returns at the end as the regret (five years, not one light). The hologram stays as written; the lamp is the real-world rhyme sitting under it.

**Why it works.** The viewer is standing in the street the lamp is on, so the "idea" is already in their eyes before the hologram starts: a thing that is only decided when somebody is there to trigger it. That lets "Nothing's alive and dead at once. It just isn't decided yet" land as common sense ("like the lamp") instead of a physics claim, and it keeps Maddie a delivery robot noticing her surroundings, not a lecturer. The lamp is also the regret: a dark lamp for five years is the sound of nobody looking, which says "we didn't realise how important you were" without Maddie having to state a feeling. The payoff, "You were writing it", gets a clean run (one universe, one story, then the line) with nothing after it but the string going dead.

**Cuts from current.** "This is your life, see" and "One decision at a time"; "Said sorry first" and "Got married" (the beads appear silently); the "storyverse / so it really matters / and so did you" run (the lamp and the regret carry it); "now we've worked all that out we feel we've messed this up" (folded into the bridge and the lamp); "Back to just before it all went wrong"; the separate last two lines (merged into one sassy conclusion). Added: the lamp at the top, the cat is a joke only implied (the lamp does that job), and the lamp callback at the five-years line.

**Risk.** It runs about 72 s, over the 60 s target; the lamp costs about 10 s, so to hit 60 the lamp's opening line and the beads line can go, leaving only the callback. It also needs a motion-sensor lamp plate in the street shot (a real object, not a hologram moment), and a lamp turning on by itself would be a second thing to animate. If the viewer does not clock the lamp as sensor-driven ("only comes on when somebody walks past"), the analogy is lost; that first lamp line has to be read plain and early. "Render when you look" is a game idea that this deliberately sacrifices for the lamp. The word "consciousness" appears only once in the pick line and the final line, so the time-machine logic ("runs on consciousness") is the least explained beat.
