# Shape 10: Ending first, built backwards

I wrote the last ten seconds first ("We can't run it though... You were the conscious ones, that's the whole point. Anyway! Patrol's out.") and made it the thing every earlier line is paying toward. Then I walked backwards, asking of each line only: does the ending need this to land? Anything the ending does not need is cut. The word "conscious" is planted early so the closer lands as a callback, and the regret sits in one short beat just before the machine, so the sass has something to cut against.

- **Why it works.** The ending is a joke with a bruise in it: Maddie is sassy about the one thing she can't fix, and "the conscious ones" is the only line that says what humans were for, in the voice of someone who now misses them. Because it was written first, everything before it is a runway. "Conscious" is introduced as the thing that picks (line 8), becomes the thing the universe runs on (line 13), then is turned on the human in the doorway (line 14). The viewer is never asked to follow physics, only to carry one word forward. The ruled idea ("It wasn't decided until you looked") gets its own line at the collapse flash, right where the hologram shows it, and "You were writing it" lands straight after "everybody, all at once". The regret is two short lines (the bridge and "messed this up") with silence round them, so it reads as feeling, not lecture.
- **Cuts from current.** "This is your life, see." "Said sorry first." "Got married." (toast stands alone as the example). "And every time, there were loads of others" is folded into the multiverse line. "A storyverse" and "So it really matters. And so did you." are cut: "You were writing it" does that work. "He made me learn a little presentation / gather round" is cut because the bridge now carries the move into the hologram. "Now we've worked all that out, we really feel like we've messed this up" is shortened to "We really have messed this up." "In case any of you ever came back" is dropped (the scene already sets it up).
- **Risk.** At 66 s it is a few seconds over the 60 s target; the multiverse line (9 s) is the first thing to trim if more is wanted, though it holds the two kept jokes. "Storyverse" as a named word is gone, which loses the distinction from "multiverse" except by contrast ("One universe. One story."). "Consciousness" is said twice, close together, which may feel like a physics word; the hope is that it reads as the plot rather than a lecture. Engineering the whole speech for the last line means the middle is thinner on character colour than other shapes. The "cat was a joke" line is the one place that name-checks a physicist idea; if the viewer does not know the cat, "alive and dead at once" must carry it alone. It does not claim the physics is proven: Maddie is reporting what the boss worked out ("He can draft... Only your consciousness can pick").
