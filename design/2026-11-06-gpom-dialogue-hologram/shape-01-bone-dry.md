# Shape 01: Bone-dry minimum

Fewest words that still carry every beat. Each line does one job and names the hologram moment it rides on; anything explanatory is cut and left to the picture. The regret is carried by short flat lines and silence, not by words. The sass of the ending is kept because it is the payoff of the whole bit.

**Why it works.** Every line is a single picture beat, so the hologram does the explaining and Maddie only labels it. The bridge ("we didn't realise how important you were") states the regret once, up front, then the toast opens fast as Kai asked. "Like a game that only draws what you're looking at" gives a working-class reader the render-when-you-look idea in one image, with no physics vocabulary and no cat. "You were writing it" lands straight after "everybody's doing it, all at once", so the payoff comes before the dead strings, which then hurt. The regret is then a flat six words, and the time machine line is the sass that closes it.

**Cuts from current.**
- "This is your life, see. One decision at a time."
- "Said sorry first." and "Got married." (toast alone carries the bead).
- "He can draft a billion of these. He can't pick one." folded into "Only your consciousness picks."
- "He tried that an' all, got it off your cartoon" and the "fighting versions of himself" tail, though both jokes survive in short form.
- "A storyverse" and "So it really matters. And so did you." (the bridge and the payoff already say it).
- "Now we've worked all that out" and "Back to just before it all went wrong".
- "And, well, you lot aren't here any more" (the sass line replaces it).

**Risk.**
- The multiverse jokes are now name-checks, so they may not land for anyone who has not seen the shows. The picture must show the portal gun and the cape.
- "Storyverse" is never said, so the one-story idea rests on "one universe, one story" and the picture.
- There is little room for the regret to breathe. It depends on the performance and on holding the silence after "not one bead on it", which the timing must leave.
- The time machine line carries three ideas (built it, runs on consciousness, can't run it) in one breath and could feel rushed.
- "Like a game that only draws what you're looking at" is a strong claim stated as plain fact. It is Maddie reporting the boss's view, not proven physics, so the delivery should be matter-of-fact and not lecturing.
