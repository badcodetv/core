# Shape 02: The picture carries it

I treated the hologram as the narrator and Maddie as the caption writer. Every idea the three.js animation already shows (beads, cloud, flash, circle river, dead string, drawn machine) loses its explaining sentence, and Maddie only says what a picture cannot: the names ("commits"), the one idea ("it wasn't decided until you looked"), the payoff ("you were writing it") and the regret. Each line is pinned to a hologram moment so the director can see what the image is doing while she speaks. Pushed to the extreme, this is the shortest version that still has every beat.

- **Why it works.** The viewer is watching a hologram do the explaining, so the words never describe what is on screen; they label it and land the one idea. "Toast, not cereal" plus a lit bead is a whole premise in six seconds. The cloud that hangs and then collapses on a flash is Kai's "renders when you look" shown rather than told, so "It wasn't decided until you looked" arrives as the click of the flash and gets front-loaded into the middle of the piece without a physics sentence. "You were writing it" plays over the river settling, which is the payoff. Regret is carried by the dead string and by the drop in Maddie's voice, not by a sentence saying "we messed up". Nothing here says "multiverse", "Schrodinger" or "quantum", so it cannot become a lecture.
- **Cuts from current.** "Said sorry first" and "Got married" (the beads tick past, labelled on screen). "This is your life, see." "One decision at a time." "And every time, there were loads of others you could've picked" (the cloud says it). The words "multiverse", "cartoon", "storyverse" and the cat. "He can draft a billion of these. He can't pick one" (the flash does it). "So it really matters. And so did you" and "we really feel like we've messed this up" (regret moved into delivery and the dead string). "Back to just before it all went wrong" (the drawn machine implies it). "Needs a consciousness to drive it" folded into one line.
- **Risk.** It leans hard on the animation: if the cloud-collapse flash or the circle river is weak or unclear, the Schrodinger line has nothing to land on and "it wasn't decided until you looked" becomes abstract. The labels (7:42 toast, sorry, wedding) must be legible on the beads. "Storyverse" is no longer said, so the word is lost; the Storyverse tie-in rests on "one universe, one story". "Consciousness" is now stated twice in quick succession with no gloss other than the flash, which could sound like jargon to a non-physics viewer. Regret has no spoken line of its own, so a flat read would lose the tone. The final time-machine line is long (11 s) at the end of a brisk piece. Nothing here claims the physics is proven; Maddie is only reporting what he worked out, and "Runs on consciousness, same as everything" is the nearest it gets to asserting it.
- **Protected lines.** Verbatim: "He misses you something rotten, you know." / "Toast, not cereal." / "You lot call 'em decisions. He calls 'em commits." / "Nah. Nothing's alive and dead at once. It just isn't decided yet." / "And that's just you. Everybody's doing it, all at once." (now split by the circle-river moment, with a deadpan stage direction) / "Five years of string, and not one bead on it." / "Anyway! Patrol's out. Mind how you go." Altered: "You were the conscious ones, that's the whole point" is kept verbatim as the final clause but now follows "We can't run it though, because you lot aren't here" in one sentence, as Kai asked.
