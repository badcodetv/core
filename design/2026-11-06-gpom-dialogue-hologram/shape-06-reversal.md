# Shape 06: Reversal, the human is a step ahead and Maddie confirms

Every line is written as if the returning human has already half-got it: Maddie reads their face, says "yes, that, you've got it", and confirms instead of explaining. Maddie still speaks every line; the human's thoughts only exist as her guesses ("I can see you looking at the maybes"). This kills the lecture feel because nothing is taught from scratch, it is only ratified. The warm regret sits in the confirming ("you were writing it") and the snark in her catching them out before they speak.

- **Why it works.** The viewer is never lectured; they are caught being clever. Each beat is a guess Maddie lands on the human's behalf ("don't say it", "go on, say it", "you can see where this is going"), so the physics arrives as recognition, which reads as respect for a reader who is not into physics and never talks down. The single idea ("it wasn't decided until you looked") is front-loaded right after the cat joke and the Nah, then "you were writing it" pays it off at the circle, and the regret is carried by the shortest lines (the bead-less string, the "important you were" bridge), with the snark confined to her reading the human's face.
- **Cuts from current.** "This is your life, see. One decision at a time." (Kai's note 2); "Said sorry first." and "Got married." (the two later beads are folded into a flick-past under the commits line); the separate "loads of others" and multiverse lines are merged into the maybes pair (jokes kept); "A storyverse. So it really matters. And so did you." (the bridge now says it up front, and "storyverse" is carried by "one universe, one story"); c13 world-model line stays out; "we really feel like we've messed this up" (regret folded into the bridge and the five-years delivery); "Back to just before it all went wrong" and the two time-machine lines merged into one confirm plus the sassy conclusion.
- **Risk.** Putting thoughts in the viewer's head can slide into the patronising "you were thinking X" (against the-reader rules) if the delivery is smug rather than fond; "I can see you looking" needs the returning human actually shown looking at the maybes in the picture. The reversal assumes the viewer has guessed the multiverse; some haven't, and "say it" then confuses. "Love" in the cat line risks twee, and the cat sentence is still the most physics-flavoured one: it must stay one breath. At 62 s the render-when-you-look image is tight against the flash, so the collapse needs about 1.5 s of silence beat. The time-machine line leans on "same as everything" to carry "runs on consciousness", which is thinner than the old explicit sentence. Nothing here claims the physics is proven: it is Maddie reporting what the boss worked out, but "It wasn't decided until you looked" is stated flat and may need a "he reckons".
