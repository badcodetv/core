# Shape 03: The plain logic, pub-style, one step at a time

Maddie reasons it out like a pub explanation. Each line is one small step that follows from the one before: decisions, options, "not decided yet", "someone has to look", "that's you", "everyone", "so you were writing it", "so the machine needs you". No physics terms; she says each thing once and moves on.
