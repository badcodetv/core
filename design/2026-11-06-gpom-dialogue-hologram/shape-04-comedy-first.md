# Shape 04: Comedy first, bluntness and bad timing carry the facts

Every hard fact is delivered by Maddie as a punchline with the wrong timing: too blunt, too soon, or an "oops" where an apology should be. The physics never gets a sentence to itself, because each fact arrives as a gag, so nobody can feel a lecture starting. The regret is held back and hits only in the last beat before "Anyway!", where the jokes stop for one line.

- **Why it works.** The cat line, the portal-gun and cape jokes, "Oops" and "Truman Show, but everyone" keep the viewer laughing at the speaker rather than learning from her. Every idea is a short blunt sentence, so nothing lectures. "You were writing it" lands flat and fast, as a fact she says in passing, which is why it can hurt. Because the whole piece is jokey, the one unjoking line, "We really did mess this up", stands out against it, and "Anyway!" then drags us out of it the way someone changes the subject when they've nearly cried.
- **Cuts from current.** "This is your life, see." / "Said sorry first." / "Got married." / the separate "he misses you" follow-up ("He made me learn a little presentation...gather round") / "And so did you" / "It really matters" / the separate "now we've worked all that out" line / "needs a consciousness to drive it" as its own sentence (folded into "runs on consciousness"). Cat, game and Truman Show are compressed into one run. Still over the 60 s target by about 10 s at normal pace; at Maddie's fast patter it should land near 62 s. If more is needed, cut the "Daft." joke line first and then the Truman Show line.
- **Risk.** (1) "Oops" could make the regret sound insincere if it is read too jokey; direct it as a wince, not a shrug. (2) "Schrödinger's cat was a joke, you know" asserts a claim about the physicist; the doctrine says not to claim the physics is proven. It is framed as Maddie reporting what the boss worked out and sits on the cat only, but check against the never-say list. (3) Nothing says "decided when you look" in plain words except the game and Truman Show line, so that line carries the single ruled idea and must be clear in the hologram (a flash when the viewer's gaze lands). (4) Pushed to the extreme, there is only one quiet line before "Anyway!", and if the actor rushes it, the regret is lost.
- **Protected lines.** Kept verbatim: "He misses you something rotten, you know." / "Toast, not cereal." (inside the new opener, "You humans go through life making decisions. For example: toast, not cereal.") / "You lot call 'em decisions. He calls 'em commits." / "Nothing's alive and dead at once. It just isn't decided yet." (with "Nah." and the cat joke added in front) / "And that's just you." / "Everybody's doing it, all at once." / "Five years of string, and not one bead on it." (with "Since you went?" and the stage direction) / "You were the conscious ones, that's the whole point." / "Anyway! Patrol's out. Mind how you go." Only the "Nah." line is altered, by the insertion of the cat joke between "Nah." and the verbatim sentence.
