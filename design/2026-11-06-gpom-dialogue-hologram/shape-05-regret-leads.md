# Shape 05: Regret leads, physics is the explanation of what they lost

Maddie opens with the apology (we never knew what you were for) so the viewer hears the grief before any idea arrives, and every hologram beat is then evidence for that sorrow rather than a lesson. Each idea gets one sentence, the cat gag and the cartoon jokes are kept as quick asides, and the payoff "you were writing it" lands as a confession, not a reveal. Pushed to the extreme: the sorry comes before the presentation, and the five years of empty string is the emotional peak, not the time machine.

- **Why it works.** The apology sits before the first bead, so toast arrives already loaded with loss and "toast, not cereal" becomes the small human thing they missed rather than a physics example. Regret is the frame; the single idea (it wasn't decided until you looked) comes in as "like a game that only draws what you're looking at" and "only you could pick", and "you were writing it" is said low and plain, as a confession. "We never knew what you were for" is the bridge Kai asked for in one breath. The snark (portal gun, cape, Schrödinger's joke) sits on top of the grief, then the brisk exit snaps her back to work, which is what a robot covering feeling does.
- **Cuts from current.** "This is your life, see." and "Said sorry first. / Got married." (first bead only, as ruled). "And so did you" and "it really matters" (the regret now does that job). The standalone "we really feel we've messed this up" (replaced by the opening sorry and the empty string). The "needs a consciousness to drive it" explanation and the "Only problem is" line, merged into the sassy conclusion. The word "storyverse" and "multiverse" are dropped from speech; the hologram carries them.
- **Risk.** Opening on sorrow before the viewer knows who is being mourned could read as sentimental or confusing for a beat; "We never knew what you were for" may sound insulting rather than regretful if delivery is off, and the line is blunt about humans being mere cargo. At 68 s it is a few seconds over target, and the Schrödinger aside plus "game that only draws what you're looking at" are the likeliest to feel like lecture if the hologram doesn't show them. Dropping "storyverse" loses the doctrine's own term; the idea survives as "one universe, one story". Per the doctrine, nothing claims the physics is proven: it is what the boss worked out, and the "he can draft / only you could pick" wording keeps it his finding. Time-machine line is now one beat, so the viewer may not catch why consciousness matters until the next line.
- **Protected lines.** Kept verbatim: "He misses you something rotten, you know." / "Toast, not cereal." (inside "For example: toast, not cereal.") / "You lot call 'em decisions. He calls 'em commits." / "Nothing's alive and dead at once. It just isn't decided yet." (prefixed by "Nah." and the Schrödinger joke) / "And that's just you." / "Everybody's doing it, all at once." / "Five years of string, and not one bead on it." / "You were the conscious ones, that's the whole point." / "Anyway! Patrol's out. Mind how you go." Altered only by an added lead-in clause: "We can't run it though, because you lot aren't here." precedes the protected line.
