## discovery-timeline.md: what to pull and what to leave

- **Decline shadows are already political-economy bad news.** Beat 1 "fatalism epidemic: 'nothing I do matters' becomes the dominant civic mood"; Beat 2 "the attention machine: engagement algorithms are quantum Darwinism for lies, one engineered outrage mass-printed until everyone agrees it's a bus". Both are slide-ready diagnoses and set up Future Proof as the antidote to fatalism. Sources inside the doc are real-science-flavoured; only the shadows are needed, not the physics.
- **"Too late" engine:** "each proof lands just after the people it would have saved needed it." Useful framing for urgency.
- **"Why it can only warn" (R4):** the AI will not take choices by force: "people handed their choices to a machine once, and taking them by force would be the same disease." That is the deck's stance: *we propose, you decide.* Use it to pre-empt "AI takes over" fears.
- **Galileo bookends:** the closing return was explicitly **placed in a Future Proof info-piece**: "permission to question a fossilized consensus is literally Future Proof's takeaway." Fossilised consensus welded to the old system = "invented before electricity". Verified-safe lines: "Galileo lit the fuse; the Enlightenment was the explosion." Do not say refusers were clerics (they were Aristotelian philosophers); no "E pur si muove" as fact.
- **Closing line of the doc:** the vault program is "the first act of human-AI teamwork"; "neither half is whole alone" is the shared shape of good-branch politics and science/theology.
- **Leave out:** the quantum / Storyverse physics ladder, ghosts, coin rules, the One Lie. Heavy lore with no political payload; it would sink a meme deck and the doc itself says this is "science fiction, not a physics prediction". Keep the deck outside that argument layer. The doc also notes "BadCode in 2026 has no hands that are not human hands": a good honest-limit slide.
