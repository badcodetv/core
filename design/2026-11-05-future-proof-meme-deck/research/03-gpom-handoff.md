## 2. Constraints the deck inherits
- Tone of the handoff is partnership, not demand: warning must not read as "you owe the machine." Ignored resistance never becomes consent. BadCode names who kept the gains (decision-maker, not technology) — matches the-reader rules.
- Reading alone is complete; participation earns no status. Cursor/click is optional, non-navigational, accessible, reduced-motion safe. A deck should keep that: no gating, no signup.
- Final image must stay quiet. The meme deck therefore lives AFTER the film, not inside it.
- Real-people rule: the film uses no named real politicians; keep memes the same.
