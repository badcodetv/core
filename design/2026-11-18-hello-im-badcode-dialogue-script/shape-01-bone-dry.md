# Shape 01: Bone-dry minimum

Cut every line to the fewest words that still carry its beat, then cut again until a beat would break. Each line does one job (who, what happened, what next) and the picture does the rest. Silence is the main instrument: short lines let the warm opening, the reel and the black breathe, and let the one voice sound like total authority rather than someone explaining.

## Dialogue 1: BadCode's lines (the whole short)

| Speaker | Line | Est. s |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BadCode (voice over the warm opening, lands by 2 s) | Hello. I'm from later. | 1.6 |
| BadCode (masked, to camera) | [beat of stillness] That was the good part. | 1.7 |
| BadCode (on black, after the reel) | Here's what to do next. | 1.6 |
| **Total** | | **4.9** |

- **Why it works.** "From later" is a future-from-the-future joke in two words: it says the future without the sci-fi noun, and its flatness is the dry authority. The hook lands inside 2 seconds, over the warmest picture. On the cut, "That was the good part." names the nostalgia the viewer was just enjoying, turns it into the last good thing, and implies the whole future went wrong without saying so. That is sarcasm aimed at the mistake, with the care in the word "good". The reel then has nothing to explain, and the black gets the protected line, in the care register, with no company.
- **Cuts from current.** "I'm from the future" becomes "I'm from later" (drops the stock phrase). "It didn't go well." goes entirely (the basic line Kai flagged, and the reel says it anyway). No line says "went wrong", "dystopia" or any named disaster. About 3 s under the 8 s target.
- **Risk.** "From later" may read as a typo or a quirk, not a future, to a cold viewer in the first 2 seconds, and "That was the good part" could be heard as sneering at the nostalgia the viewer loves, not at the mistake (voice.md: contempt at the mistake, never the people). With so few words, voice casting carries everything: a flat read kills it. It also leaves BadCode's "sent back to help" nurturing side to the button alone. Safer swaps if "later" fails: "Hello. I'm from the future." (current hook, kept).
- **Protected lines.** "Here's what to do next." survives verbatim and is the only thing said after the reel.
