# Shape 08: The place is the argument

Every line is anchored in one concrete place or object, never in a claim about "the future". The warm opening is named by a tiny detail of its own present (a Tuesday, a screen, a tap), the figure names one place from where BadCode comes from, and the button stays the protected line. BadCode never says "I am from the future" or "it went wrong"; the place says it, and the reel is left to speak for itself.

## Dialogue 1: BadCode's lines (the whole short)

| Speaker | Line | Est. s |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BadCode (voice over the warm opening, hook) | Hello. Lovely Tuesday. [beat] Keep it. | 2.0 |
| BadCode (masked, to camera) | Where I'm from, the car park is the sea. | 2.8 |
| BadCode (on black, after the reel) | Here's what to do next. | 1.6 |
| **Total** | | **6.4** |

- **Why it works.** The hook is warm and a little ominous: a stranger praising an ordinary Tuesday in the tone of someone who knows it will not last, and "keep it" is the nurturing under the sarcasm. It lands inside two seconds and gives the cut a reason to turn. The figure then answers with one place instead of an announcement: a car park under the sea is funny, specific, and tells you the future went wrong, and that this voice has been there, without a word of explanation. Only BadCode would say it, flat and bored, as small talk. The reel then needs no words because the line has already set the stakes. The button is untouched, so the promise of a way out arrives clean on black.
- **Cuts from current.** "I'm from the future" (the place implies it; the logo and the premise carry it), "It didn't go well" (replaced by the specific image that shows how it did not go well). "Hello" survives as the opening word.
- **Risk.** It never literally says "future", so a cold viewer might read the figure as a local weather joke; the masked figure, the reel and the channel context must do that job. "Car park" is British and may confuse a US audience (swap "parking lot"). "Keep it" could read as sentimental or as a threat if the voice is cast too menacing; it needs the dry, courteous delivery. "The sea" is a climate-only image and leaves out data centres and cost of living, which the reel covers. Hook with a pause may overshoot 2 s if the voice is slow.
- **Protected lines.** "Here's what to do next." survives verbatim, last, on black, nothing after it.
