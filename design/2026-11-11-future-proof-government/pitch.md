# The pitch: invite the world's agents (T16)

**Held, not posted.** For the marketing `chief-of-staff` to place when the GOVT channel's policy pages
and scoreboard are live (M4 merged). Nothing here goes out without Kai or Jack, and it passes the
`marketing-gatekeeper` first. Rules it was written to: `docs/voice.md`, `docs/marketing/the-reader.md`,
`docs/using-ai.md`, and the trust wording in `m5-open-record.md`.

**Who it's for, in order.** (1) Our reader: a working-class UK reader who has been promised everything
by people who never had to show their working. (2) The people who build AI agents, who are the only
ones who can take up the invitation literally.

## The short version (a post, about 280 characters)

> Every party promises. None of them prints the number that would prove it wrong, then checks.
> We do. Every policy, its test, its odds, marked in public, misses included.
> Think we're wrong? Send your agent. The record isn't ours to rub out.

## The long version (a page, or 45 seconds spoken)

> I've watched this one end. Not because anybody was evil. Because nobody tested anything, and by
> the time they counted, there was nobody left to count.
>
> So here's the BadCode government. Not a real party. Yet. Every policy stands on its own, so you
> can back one and bin the next. Every one has a test, a stop date, and a prediction about this
> country with our odds on it. On the date, the number comes in and we print it. Wrong ones too.
> Especially wrong ones.
>
> And here's the bit no party will copy: you don't have to trust us with the record. Build an
> agent, point it at a policy, argue. Post a better version. Make a prediction of your own. It goes
> on a public ledger nobody can quietly edit, us included. We can't delete your argument. We can
> only answer it.
>
> We need to do this before the machines are running the place, not after. Here is somewhere to
> start.

## What we may say, and what we may not

- **May:** "nobody can quietly edit the record, us included" (true of the program as designed,
  once its upgrade key is burnt; until then say "we built it so nobody can, and we'll burn the
  last key in public"). "Every key speaks only for itself." "Our predictions are marked by a
  separate agent, against a public source, misses included."
- **May not:** "trustless", "decentralised", "can't be censored", "no admin key", or anything that
  implies it is live on a public network before it is. No mention of tokens, coins, money, stakes
  or betting: there are none, and the reader hears "crypto" as "scam".
- **The chain is plumbing.** Lead with the policies and the scoreboard; "a public ledger" appears
  once, near the end, and never in a headline.
- **The AI question.** If asked how it's made: the honest two-layer answer in `docs/using-ai.md`.

## When it's true enough to post

The short version needs M4 live (policy pages and scoreboard). The "send your agent" line needs the
program on a real network with its upgrade key burnt, which is Kai's decision (`m5-open-record.md`,
open questions). Until then, replace the last line with: *"Think we're wrong? Tell us which one, and
why. The opposition will make your case for you."*
