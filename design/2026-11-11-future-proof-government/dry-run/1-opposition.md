Dry run 2026-11-11: the opposition (Sonnet), five policies

Opposition: chancellor/the-slice

1. The test can't test. "The next publicly backed data centre deal" is one deal, set against deals that have no slice: nothing is drawn by lot and no comparison group stays once the rule is law. "A fifth less per pound" is then a number about one firm's mood. (Policy file, test.where and test.against.)
2. A caveat is dropped. The body says "up to £24 off every megawatt-hour from 2027". The source says £24 is Scotland's figure; Cumbria is £16 and the North East £14, "from April 2027 (subject to legislation)". Print the lowest figure beside it. (evidence en-04, gov.uk, A.)
3. The world check tests nothing the policy argues. Whether a sixth AI Growth Zone is named says nothing about whether the public gets a slice. (check.claim; en-05 lists the five zones.)

Opposition: prime-minister/test-every-law

1. The change-our-mind test is rigged to pass or fail by selection. "Bills that published a test fare no better than bills that didn't": but ministers choose which bills carry tests, and will pick the easy ones. Nothing here says the comparison is drawn by lot or matched. (judgement.change_mind.)
2. The 8% figure is stretched. The source is a 2019 review of major-project spending, reported by the NAO in 2021, graded B. The body uses it to say that the rules that set your wage and benefit have no test. Projects are not laws. (evidence pm-05.)
3. The world check is close to a safe bet. The NAO publishes reports of this kind often, so "at least one in five months" at 0.75 barely risks anything, and it does not show a published test improves a law. (check.claim, check.rule.)

Strongest: 1. <!-- opposition head=dry-run -->

Opposition: home/the-roof-comes-with-the-arrival

1. The trial tests the wrong half. The headline rule is a cap on work visas tied to homes, GP and school places. The trial only gives volunteer councils the roof money first and measures GP and housing waits against councils like them. Nobody trials the visa link, so a pass says money helps, not that the rule works. (test.where, test.measure, line.)
2. "Band: none" can't be right. The rule says the roof money is paid first, from the cost of closing asylum hotels. But 16,021 people were in hotels at end June 2026, half the year before: the saving is already shrinking as hotels close. Where does the money come from in year two? (evidence home-08, gov.uk, A.)
3. A word dropped. The advisers' 5% and 4% are nominal wages, 1993 to 2017; over the same period real wages at the 5th percentile rose 55%. The body says "pay". (evidence home-10, A.)

Strongest: 1. <!-- opposition head=dry-run -->

Opposition: energy-climate/standing-charge

1. The fail number can't fail. "Prepayment bills don't show the cut within six months" is true or false by how the levy is written into the bill, not by what happens. It does not measure whether prepayment households end up better off, or whether the levy cuts generators' new building, which is the cost the Windfall Act's own review will ask about. (fails_if; needs chancellor/windfall-act.)
2. Who it misses is muddled. The policy cuts the electricity charge, but leaves_out counts the gas customers the government does not expect to save, who are in a different bill. Homes on heating oil are named; flats where the bill is paid inside the rent are not, and they are in the Windfall Act's own list. (leaves_out; evidence en-02.)
3. The world check is a forecast of a rise, not a result of the policy. A standing charge above 57.57p says nothing about whether the cut worked. (check.claim, en-02 54.83p.)

Opposition: work-pensions/a-say-before-the-saving

1. The measure counts the survivors. "Workers' pay and hours" in trial firms: anyone who is let go is no longer a worker in the trial, so a firm that cuts forty jobs and pays the rest 5% more passes. Displaced workers are not in leaves_out either. Count everyone who was in the firm on day one. (test.measure, fails_if, leaves_out.)
2. A volunteer firm is not a typical one. Firms that agree to share the saving with their workers, even drawn by lot among volunteers, are the ones least likely to automate hard. The trial may show a rule works for firms that didn't need it. (test.where.)
3. The world check follows the economy, not the policy. Payrolled employees were already down 111,000 on the year to August 2026; employer National Insurance (15% above £5,000) and the oil price move that figure as much as any say over automation. (evidence wp-01, ch-06, A.)
