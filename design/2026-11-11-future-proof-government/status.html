The government's new founding rules

Choose issue by issueEvery policy stands alone, so you can back one and reject the next.
composition over inheritance
Small, checked, undoableLaws change in small tested steps, each with a stop date (rights excepted).
continuous integration
No policy without its testEvery proposal ships with its experiment, the number that kills it, and who it leaves out.
data-driven decisions
