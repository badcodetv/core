# Choruses: ideas (6 Nov, from Kai's brief after the Jack meeting)

Ruling it builds on: `decisions.md` #5. The robot army and the eye are cut, and the choruses show the humans.
Kai's words (6 Nov): chorus 1 = homelessness, joblessness, poverty, money. Chorus 2 = Last of Us style downfall, implied violence, subtle, not Michael Bay. **Chorus 2 must not outdo the Swindon explosion** (e07, 156.3 s), which stays the film's one big hit and the drop.

## Slots (01 GPOM start, 174 BPM, 0.345 s a beat)
- **Chorus 1:** 62.58–74.08 (11.5 s). Now: 4 robot/eye shots on V2. It sits after the push and the sackings, before the banks are emptied.
- **Chorus 2:** 106.92–128.71 (21.8 s). The chant repeats at 117.71, so it has two passes. Now: 12 night robot/eye shots.
- **War:** 128.71 onward. No humans. It ends on the SWINDON sign and the explosion.

## The ladder (how loud each step gets)
1. **Chorus 1, quiet: the money goes.** Daylight, the opening's documentary 35mm. Faces, no fire.
2. **Banks (kept):** the rooms are empty and the money moves to the machine.
3. **Chorus 2, pass 1, uneasy: things stop working.** Dusk into night.
4. **Chorus 2, pass 2, dread: people leave.** Night, the Last of Us flight. Any fire is far off: a glow on cloud, never a flame on screen.
5. **War:** the people have gone. The M4 is empty. **The explosion is the first fire we see up close.**

## Chorus 1 (11.5 s, 4 shots, one per chant line)
- **A. The same people, a week later.** The opening's humans after the sackings: the trader carries his box out into the rain; the corner-office worker sits in a job-centre queue; a sleeping bag lies in the rain plaza's doorway. Then the beneficiary: the same tower, lit and busy, with nobody at a desk. The reader already knows these people, which makes it land hardest.
- **B. Job, home, street.** Four rungs down: badge denied at the barrier, keys posted back through a letterbox, a sleeping bag in a shop doorway, a food-bank queue under a billboard for the AI.
- **C. Hands only.** Jack's "a hand coming out": a card declined, coins counted on a kitchen table, a hand out of a sleeping bag, a P45 folded into a pocket. Intimate and the cheapest to make look real.

## Chorus 2 (21.8 s, two passes)
- **Pass 1, 106.9–117.7 (things stop working):** a cashpoint screen dies; a crowd at a bank's shutter; empty shelves; streetlights flicker along a terrace; a phone shows no signal.
- **Pass 2, 117.7–128.7 (the flight):**
  - night gridlock on the M4 out of Swindon, red tail-lights for miles;
  - a child in a back seat looks back at an orange glow on the clouds;
  - far off, small and silent, a helicopter's light spirals down (Kai's idea);
  - people on a footbridge look up at lights moving in the sky;
  - **the town's lights go out street by street**;
  - the last frame: an empty street, a car door left open, the engine still running.
- **The match cut:** the jammed M4 at night (chorus 2) → the empty M4 by day (war, wr-w03 MW1). "There are no humans left", without showing what happened.

## Limits that keep the explosion the peak
- No fireball, no hit, no weapon pointed at a person, no injury, no body.
- Fire only as a glow behind something.
- Fear on faces, never pain.
- Sound stays the track; no sirens louder than the music.
- Flashes at 3 a second or fewer (the house limit).

## Open choices for Kai
- Chorus 1: A, B or C, or a mix.
- Keep one abstract beat or none: e.g. the eye as the very last frame of chorus 2, when the town lights go out (Kai in the meeting: "alternate"; decisions #5: cut). Default: none.
- The end of chorus 2: the lights going out → black → war, or keep the white flash.
