## What changed from 1I

## Doctrine check
- Choosing and committing, never observing or collapsing. No "superposition", no "everywhere at once".
- "Only your consciousness can" is Maddie's (and her boss's) belief, said with her certainty, not a physics claim.
- The "one long line that only grows" picture is the growing block. The future is not drawn at all.
