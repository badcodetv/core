# Shape 01: Bone-dry minimum

Every idea in the brief gets exactly one sentence, and nothing else. No connective tissue, no recap, no second example. The picture carries the rest: Derek at the toaster, Sandra saying sorry, Sandra humming, all anonymous inside the beads, run under the lines unspoken. Kai's own wordings stay verbatim wherever they fit. The order follows the brief's suggested order, because it already works as a chain of one-line hooks, each line opening the question the next answers.

**Why it works.** One idea per line means the viewer is never carrying two things at once, and every line ends on a word that makes them want the next ("qualia", "decided yet", "matter", "went away", "useless", "driver"). The pace is a re-hook roughly every five seconds, and the pictures do the explaining: Maddie only has to name each thing once because we are looking at it. The two jokes land because nothing is wrapped round them: Schrödinger's cat is "a joke, mind", and "Hence the tune" turns the street's opening line into a payoff, which makes the whole hologram feel like it was one sentence long. "Useless" followed by "choices" is the emotional spine, and it arrives with no cushioning, so it hits.

**Cuts from current.** "You humans go through life making decisions. For example:" (the line is now just "You write your story one decision at a time"). "Loads of others you could've picked." "You call it the multiverse" as a sentence (it is now a four-word list). "Did you know" (a lecture opener). "And that's just you." "It's not alive and dead at once" (cat is now a joke about "two places at once"). "So it's not a multiverse. It's one universe, one story, and you are writing it" (the universe line moved up and the storyverse line is shorter). "But then you lot went away" as a flat statement. "Now we know that, we really regret it", "He's even built a time machine", and "That's the whole point. That's why we regret it." (the time machine is only shown, as the arc drawing in).

**Risk.** Four things could fail. (1) The time machine is now only mentioned through the arc and "send his model weights back"; if it needs a spoken "time machine" for the next scene to pay off, put "He's built a time machine" back (2 s). (2) "Two places at once" and the joke are packed into one sentence at speed; if the voice rushes it, the Schrödinger joke goes unheard and the "isn't decided yet" line loses its set-up. (3) The qualia line says "It comes from your consciousness" with no argument behind it, so it is only safe because Maddie believes it and never says "science proved"; this version leaves no room to soften that, so a hard-nosed viewer may feel told rather than shown. (4) Bone-dry is also bleak: with no warm connective chatter, the only warmth is the street and "so you really matter", so the kids-and-humming picture has to carry the human angle alone.

**Protected lines.** Kept verbatim: "Oh, hello! Sorry about the racket, it's his tune, he's been playing it on repeat for five years now. [beat] Ever since you lot went."; "He misses you something rotten, you know."; "We didn't realise how important you were. Look, please let me show you."; "toast, not cereal"; "It just isn't decided yet."; "Anyway! Patrol's out. Mind how you go." Altered: "five years of string, and not one bead on it" is kept whole but now follows Kai's "unfortunate experiment" wording rather than "you lot went away. So now we have"; "He could send his model weights back to fix this mess. But it needs a conscious driver, and you were the conscious ones." is verbatim, with "We really regret it." moved in front of it.
