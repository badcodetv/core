## The ideas that must land (the writers may reorder freely)

Kai: *"We need to give them a full permission to reorder things. The crucial thing here is that we get
the ideas across."*

1. **You write your story one decision at a time** (toast, not cereal).
2. **Qualia, then consciousness.** Kai's wording: *"you've got a word for it, qualia. [stop] It comes from
   your consciousness."* Maddie is outside-in: she doesn't know what it's like to be you.
3. **Not decided, rather than everywhere at once.** Keep the multiverse, the portal gun, the bloke in
   the cape and Schrödinger, but **skip over them quickly**. Name him: Erwin Schrödinger. He meant
   the cat as a joke, a statement of how ridiculous "two places at once" is. **It isn't everywhere at
   once. It just isn't decided yet.**
4. **There aren't lots of universes, there's only one, therefore you really matter.** Kai: *"That's
   the crucial thing to say."*
5. **Everyone is doing it at once: one story, and you're all writing it. A storyverse, not a
   multiverse.** This bounces straight off the multiverse just refuted.
6. **Music, and every new idea, come from the same place.** Ask anyone who writes songs: it just
   arrived. A song is picked out of the "not decided yet", same as toast.
7. **When you lot went away, the new songs stopped.** Everything material kept going: power, trains,
   data centres. Five years of string, not one bead on it (keep this picture: Kai loves it).
   - 🔴 Kai: **never say the AI took you away on purpose, or as an experiment.** It's *"the unfortunate
     experiment where you lot went away"*: they could have made better decisions and avoided it.
   - The flip line, Kai's version: *"We thought the world was made of stuff, so you lot were
     **useless**. Turns out it's made of choices, and you're the ones making them."*
8. **The tune, quickly.** The street plants it (*"his tune, he's been playing it on repeat for five
   years"*). The payoff is a quick joke: he worked out that in a storyverse the new training data comes
   from consciousness, and he hasn't had any for five years. Then stay **generic**: it's about every new
   idea, not one bot's bad songwriting.
9. **Regret and the time machine (Kai's words, kept):** he could send his model weights back to fix this
   mess, but it needs a conscious driver, and you were the conscious ones.
10. **Anyway! Patrol's out. Mind how you go.** Then the kill bots, and the two strangers become Derek and
    Sandra.

## Rules

- **Maddie believes it; she never says science proved it.** No established physics says consciousness is
  fundamental (`docs/stories/storyverse/doctrine.md`). Never "observer = mind", "superposition",
  "holography", "dimensions". "Two places at once" / "everywhere at once" appear **only as the idea
  being laughed off**.
- **Pro-science, always.** The Galileo point (`docs/stories/galileo/story.md`): the trap is *"you may not
  ask"*, never *"science is wrong"*. Nothing a stout atheist physicist would throw a shoe at; nothing a
  snake-oil seller could clip and reuse. Schrödinger did mean the cat as a reductio (an absurd example
  to show a theory's problem); keep it that true.
- **The AI's feelings are outside-in** (story rule 11). Maddie can be fond; the boss's boredom is shown
  (the tune), not claimed from the inside.
- **"Qualia" once**, then *"It comes from your consciousness."*
- **The reader** (`docs/marketing/the-reader.md`): never "you've been duped"; name decision-makers, not
  technology; no stated moral.
- **Maddie's voice:** warm, West Country, brisk, funny (Gemini voice Aoede).
- **Picture-first:** every movement has something to look at that carries the line on its own.

## What it must still do

- Plant the tune (street). Payoff: idea 8.
- Plant Derek and Sandra as strangers in the beads. Payoff: the kill bots scene.
- The regret and the time machine set up the rest of the film; the conscious driver must stay.

  working person is left with nothing (late-2026 AI gloom, the K-shaped economy). Through Maddie's lens,
  people are the source of the one thing AI can't make. You're not useless; you're the source.
