# Shape 08: Music-led, the river plays and then stops

The whole presentation hangs off the tune. The street plants one repeating song; inside the hologram every bead is a note, so the string is a melody and every idea is something you hear before it is explained. Decisions are notes, qualia is the thing a listener has and a player-piano doesn't, "not decided yet" is the held breath before a note, the storyverse is everyone playing one song, new ideas arrive the way songs arrive, and the cut-off is the melody stopping dead with only the old tune looping. The hook is the sound itself; the joke (the tune) is planted in the first ten seconds and paid off at the end.

**Why it works.** A viewer who has never heard the word "qualia" can still hear the difference between a tune and silence, so each idea is carried by something they can hear and the explanation only confirms it. The song is the hook (a repeating tune they can't escape in the first ten seconds, with the reason withheld), the re-hook is every bead being a note that plays (toast, sorry, wedding, then a melody), and the biggest beat is sonic: the whole river stops dead and only one looping tune is left, which makes "five years of string, not one bead" land as an absence you can hear. Qualia sits exactly where a listener has something a transcript doesn't, so "it comes from your consciousness" is a stop after a held note rather than a lecture. Schrödinger and the multiverse are skipped at pace to a single flash note ("not decided yet" is the breath before it), and "only one universe, so every note you play, you matter" is placed straight after, before the river, so the multiverse refutation bounces directly into the storyverse. Songs-arriving-from-nowhere is the human angle the brief asked for (Sandra at the sink, a kid and a chord), and it makes "you're the source" feel like a fact about anyone who has ever hummed. The tune joke closes the loop planted on the street, and "every joke, every recipe" turns it generic immediately.

**Cuts from current.** "One story, and you are writing it" is folded into "one song, all of you playing it" (the story word survives only inside "storyverse"). "That's the whole point. That's why we regret it." goes: the regret is now carried by the silence and the line before it. "You call it the multiverse" loses its setup sentence; "He's been playing it" gets a payoff. Derek and Sandra stay anonymous people inside beads, never named.

**Risk.** Length: this runs about 153 s against 1D's 80 s, nearly double, because every idea gets a sound and a bead; the measuring pass will have to cut, and the first cut candidates are the "ask anyone who writes songs" line and the second half of the multiverse line. The metaphor is carrying a lot: if "every bead is a note" is read as "the universe is music", it drifts toward the woo the doctrine bans (frequency, vibration), so the lines must stay with "notes you could have played", never "the universe vibrates". "Training data comes from consciousness" is the one claim Maddie states flatly; it is the boss's belief reported outside-in, but it needs the line to stay a joke. Sound design is load-bearing: if the tune and the stopped river can't be mixed cleanly under a narrated voice, the middle third has no picture to hold. Finally, "Moving on" is a throwaway the director may find too glib for the cartoon scientist and the bloke in the cape, and Schrödinger's cat as a reductio is true but is explained in one breath.
