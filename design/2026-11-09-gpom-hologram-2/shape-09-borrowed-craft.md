# Shape 09: Borrowed craft (montage, myth-bust, silence, reframe)

I took four tricks from work that squeezes hard ideas into feeling, and pushed each one to its extreme. From Pixar's opening montage in Up (2009): a whole life told as a run of small choices, so the beads carry the feeling and the words only name them. From Kurzgesagt: bust a popular myth the viewer already half believes (the multiverse), then replace it with a cleaner picture (one story). From Attenborough: understatement over the empty shot, so the silence does the work. From The Good Place (the season one reveal, and the Trolley Problem episode): a joke that is secretly a premise, then a flip that recasts everything before it. The result is a re-hook every 6 to 9 seconds, one planted object (toast) that returns at the worst moment, and the ideas ordered as: choices, then what it feels like, then the myth, then one universe, then everyone, then music, then silence, then the flip, then the tune, then regret.

**Why it works.** Each borrowed move does a job the physics lesson could not. The Up-style run of beads makes "decisions" a feeling before it is a concept, and the toast is planted so it can come back as the sharpest line in the film ("Five years since anybody picked toast"), which is the callback that turns the silence into loss. The multiverse is treated as a Kurzgesagt myth-bust: name it fast, laugh it off, replace it with something simpler, so it costs 20 seconds and not a lecture. Schrödinger is a joke about a joke, which is both true to him and a laugh. "One universe, no spare toast" is the stake in four words. The cut-off is Attenborough: say it plainly and then stop talking. The "stuff or choices" line is the Good Place flip, recasting the whole five-year silence as a category mistake rather than a crime, and the tune joke then pays off the street in one sentence before widening to every idea so it does not stay about one bot's songwriting. The re-hooks are the new beads, the cloud, the flash, the humming, the silence, and the reframe, one every 6 to 9 seconds, and the viewer is always looking at something that carries the line.

**Cuts from current.** "Loads of others..." is kept but trimmed to one list. The old "And that's just you. Everybody's doing it, all at once." is folded into the river line. "But then you lot went away" and "Now we know that" are dropped as connective tissue. "So it's not a multiverse. It's one universe, one story, and you are writing it" is split: the universe point moves before the river, the story point stays in the river. "Anonymous dots" become a man at a toaster and a woman saying sorry.

**Risk.** It is long (about 142 s against 1D's 80 s), and the second half is dense with abstract claims, so the pictures have to do real work in the music and silence sections. "Five years since anybody picked toast" depends on the toast bead having a face and a mouthful; if the beads are generic it reads as a gag and not a gut punch. "Training data comes from consciousness" is the one line a physicist or an AI person might push on, so it is said by Maddie as the boss's working conclusion, never as science. "Same place as the toast" may stretch the picture if the music bead is not visibly different from the toast bead. The silence beat may feel like a dead patch at this length.

**Protected lines.** Kept verbatim: the street opener ("Oh, hello! ... Ever since you lot went."), "He misses you something rotten, you know.", "We didn't realise how important you were.
