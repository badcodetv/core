# Shape 07: Retention, YouTube-style (hook, open loops, re-hook every ten seconds)

Every dialogue is built like a video that has to hold a stranger who can leave at any second: a hook inside five seconds, loops opened out loud ("I'll come back to his tune") and always paid off, a re-hook roughly every ten seconds (a promise, a question, a joke or a turn of the picture), and curiosity planted before every answer so the idea lands as relief, not as a lesson. Kai's wordings are kept near verbatim; the structure around them is pushed hard toward "what happens next?". Three loops run through the whole piece: the tune (opened in the first line, paid off at the end), "the one thing we got wrong" (opened on the street, paid off at the cut-off), and "the bit I promised you" (the music, opened in the toast, paid off in the river).

- **Why it works.** The first line is itself the hook (a mystery noise, five years, "ever since you lot went"), and Maddie immediately names a promised answer, so the viewer has a reason to stay for the first two minutes. From there every beat ends on an open question or a turn: "Small stuff, right? Hang on." (toast looks trivial, so the payoff must be big), "Keep hold of that" (qualia becomes a plant), "Different question" (the multiverse is dismissed in one breath so we can ask the better one), "the bit I promised you" (the music), then the silence of the cut-off lands because we have just been shown what is being lost. The tune joke pays off a loop opened in the first line and is stated as a joke, then handed straight to the generic version: new ideas come through people. Kai's two crucial points ("there's only one universe, so you matter" and "one story, you're all writing it, a storyverse, not a multiverse") each get their own re-hook and their own picture (flash to one bead; ring then river). The joke density (quiz-show multiverse, "taking the mick", "group project", "cry for help") gives the audience a laugh roughly every fifteen seconds, which is what carries the heavy beats.
- **Cuts from current.** "That's the whole point. That's why we regret it." (the picture and the earlier flip now carry it). "Did you know Erwin Schrödinger meant his cat as a joke?" becomes a flat statement of character, not a quiz. "So it's not a multiverse. It's one universe, one story..." is split: "one universe, you matter" moves up to the flash, "one story, a storyverse" moves to the river. "He misses you" is kept, but the old bridge now carries the promise.
- **Risk.** (1) Length: about 160 s against 80 s, double the current run. Retention shape lives or dies on pace, so the voice must be quick and the loops cut hard if the measure comes back long (cut order: the "Group project" joke, the "Keep hold of that" plant, the generic ideas line). (2) Too many explicit promises ("I promise", "the bit I promised you") can read as YouTuber patter, not a patrol robot; it works only if Maddie delivers it warm and slightly flustered, not salesy. (3) "Unfortunate experiment" is Kai's phrase and risks reading as on purpose; the line after it ("we could've made better decisions and avoided it") is there to hold that. If the line fails in the voiced take, drop "experiment" first. (4) "Two places at once" appears once, only as the idea laughed off, as the brief allows. (5) Schrödinger as "taking the mick" is a fair reductio, but "everyone thinks he was being clever" must not slide into mocking science; kept pro-science by naming him and his intent, not the physics.
- **Protected lines.** All survive verbatim: the street opener ("Oh, hello! ... Ever since you lot went."); "He misses you something rotten, you know."; "We didn't realise how important you were. Look, please let me show you." (moved after the new promise line, wording unchanged); "toast, not cereal"; "It just isn't decided yet." (the "It's not both." is added before it and does not change it); "Five years of string, and not one bead on it." (only the leading "So now we have" is replaced by a lead-in with the new-songs line); "He could send his model weights back to fix this mess. But it needs a conscious driver, and you were the conscious ones."; "Anyway! Patrol's out. Mind how you go." Derek and Sandra appear only as the man at the toaster and the woman saying sorry and humming, never named.
