# Shape 03: The logic said plainly (a mate down the pub)

Maddie reasons it out one numbered step at a time, counting on her fingers, each step earning the next: pick, qualia, skip the multiverse, "not decided yet", only one universe so you matter, everyone at once, music comes from the same place, you went away, stuff versus choices, the tune joke, regret and the time machine. The hook is a promise ("I'll go slow, like explaining it to a mate down the pub"), the open loop is "why would a robot need to explain this?" (paid off at the cut-off), and every step ends on a small turn so the next one has a reason to exist. Pushed to the extreme: the steps are literally counted out loud.

**Why it works.** It is the only shape that treats the viewer as a smart mate, not a student: the counted steps give a hook and a built-in re-hook every ten or so seconds ("step three, and this is the one I'd put on a mug"), and each step is a thing you can see in the hologram. Every wobble in the science gets the same one-breath treatment: the multiverse is laughed past in a single line so Schrödinger's cat can turn it ("it just isn't decided yet"), which is what earns "one universe, so you matter". "Everyone's doing it, all at once" then bounces straight off the multiverse just dismissed, and music arriving "from the same place" turns the toast into a song, so the cut-off hurts (songs stopped, five years of string). The stuff-versus-choices line carries the politics without a sermon (a stuff-lens that makes people useless, versus people as the source), the tune joke is quick and the next line goes generic, and "you're not in the way" is the human payoff.

**Cuts from current.** "Loads of others you could've picked. You call it the multiverse" (folded into the quick step two); "He could send his model weights back... that's the whole point. That's why we regret it" (the second half goes: the stakes are already earned); "you humans go through life making decisions" (becomes step one); "That's just you" deadpan (replaced by step four's "everybody's doing it, all at once"); "the cartoon scientist" (now just "portal gun").

**Risk.** Length: this is by far the longest option (~170 s against 1D's ~80 s), and counted steps can feel like a lecture slide if Aoede reads them flatly; the joke density has to carry it. "Step six" is the weakest-numbered line, as it carries the sensitive "unfortunate experiment" wording: if the AI sounds too sheepish it reads as an admission, if it sounds too flip it reads as callous. The qualia line ("You've got a word for that") sits close to the outside-in rule, so check it stays what Maddie cannot know, not what she knows. "One universe, so you matter" must stay a bet in Maddie's mouth, never "science says"; Schrödinger's cat as a reductio is kept honest. Cut candidates if it must shrink: the "Sorry first. Married." aside, and the "every cure, every joke" line.
