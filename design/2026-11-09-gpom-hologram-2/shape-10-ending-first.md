# Shape 10: Ending first, every line a setup

Wrote the last fifteen seconds first (the regret, the time machine, a flash of the boss getting restless, then "Anyway! Patrol's out.") so the kill bots feel like the only thing that can happen next, then worked backwards: every earlier beat exists to plant one word or feeling the ending cashes in (a hand on the wheel, the one copy, a hunger for new ideas, "driver"). Kai's wordings are kept close to verbatim; the order follows his notes (decisions, qualia, multiverse skipped fast, Erwin's joke, one universe, storyverse, music from the same place, the cut-off, the tune, regret). The hook is a promise made early that the ending pays: one thing he can't make, and what it needs.

**Why it works.** The ending is the engine. "Hand on the wheel" in the qualia line and "the one copy" in the pick plant the driver and the stakes, so when Maddie says "it needs a conscious driver" it lands as the answer to a question the viewer has been holding since toast. Re-hooks come every 8 to 13 seconds and each one is a picture: toast with Derek, the cloud blooming, the flash on the pick, the river, Sandra humming (the turn the whole thing hangs on), the melody stopping dead. Jokes pay off by escalation: the multiverse is brushed off, Erwin's joke earns "daft", the tune joke closes the loop the street opened. "He's getting a bit restless, mind" is the new move: one outside-in observation that makes the kill bots arrive as consequence, not a swerve, and the bright "Anyway!" becomes a cover over unease.

**Cuts from current.** "Loads of others you could've picked" is folded into the cloud line, not its own beat. "And that's just you" is kept but "everybody's doing it, all at once" now has "one story, and you're all writing it" attached. The old standalone "So it's not a multiverse. It's one universe" is split in two: the universe count now sits on the pick, and the storyverse on the river. "Now we know that" is kept only in the regret.

**Risk.** (1) "Unfortunate experiment" can still be heard as deliberate: only "unfortunate", the stuff-vs-choices line and the regret protect it, and the writers should check by ear. (2) At about 132 s against 1D's 80 s the middle (qualia, Schrödinger, music) is the longest run without a new picture-turn; Sandra humming has to earn its twelve seconds. (3) "Restless" gives the boss a feeling; it is held to what Maddie can see, but it is the one line a purist on story rule 11 could challenge. (4) "Two places at once" and "everywhere at once" appear only as the idea being laughed off, per the doctrine, but the Schrödinger line sits close to that edge. (5) The tune joke is the only place the training-data idea is named, so it must be read fast and dry or it becomes a lecture.
