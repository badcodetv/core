# Shape 07: The chorus, side characters voice it

I took the reporter's mouth away. The reporter is a straight-faced professional who barely speaks; the paramedic, the outrider and a bystander say everything that needs saying, in the flat, practical register of people doing their job. Nobody states whose time counts. The paramedic's dry numbers do it by implication, and the button lands on the paramedic, not on a caption or the reporter. Extreme version: the reporter gets two words and a look.

## Dialogue 1: Priority Lane: the whole short

| Speaker | Line | Est. s |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OUTRIDER | [gloved hand up, bored] Hold there, love. Won't be a minute. | 2.2 |
| PARAMEDIC | [leaning out of the cab, calm] Mine's got about four. | 1.8 |
| REPORTER | [straight to camera, regional TV voice] Normal service. | 1.0 |
| BYSTANDER | [umbrella, not looking up] Every Tuesday. | 1.0 |
| MINISTER | [on the phone, seething] Lemon curd. LEMON CURD. I said raspberry. | 3.0 |
| PARAMEDIC | [watching the tail lights go] **That was the four.** | 1.3 |
| CAPTION | RASPBERRY SITUATION: ONGOING | 0 |
| **Total** | about 31 words | **about 10.3 s** |

**Why it works.** The reporter is the BadCode authority, and the funniest thing an authority can do is stay professionally blank while ordinary people say the quiet part in practical terms. The paramedic's "about four" gives the audience a number with no adjective on it, so they do the maths themselves; "That was the four" pays it off as the punchline (marked in bold) without any moral. The minister's trivial fury is the contrast, and "I said raspberry" is untouched. The caption is a deadpan news-style label that treats the jam as the live story, which is the real crime of the piece, said by no one. The bystander's "Every Tuesday" is a throwaway that tells the reader this is routine, not an exception, with no one sneering at the viewer.

**Cuts from current.** The reporter's monologue goes entirely (Jack's line was never written, so nothing is lost except the idea that the reporter carries the joke). The caption becomes a label, not a lecture. Nothing explains the gap between the two vehicles.

**Risk.** The reporter is the brand, so leaving the reporter with "Normal service." may feel like a waste of the character and thin on the BadCode "knows how this ends" authority. "That was the four" could read as a death joke too bluntly for some platforms, or be missed without the preceding "about four" landing cleanly in 1.8 s. Four voices means four lip-syncs in 20 s, more than the others. Beat 2 is only 2 s of speech in a 5 s slot, so the shot needs a held look to camera.

**Protected lines.** "I said raspberry." survives verbatim, as the last sentence of the minister's line. Only "Lemon curd. LEMON CURD." is added in front of it, for context.
