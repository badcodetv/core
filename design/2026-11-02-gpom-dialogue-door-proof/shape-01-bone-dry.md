# Shape 01: Bone-dry minimum

Every beat Kai listed survives, every word that is not a protected line or a turn is cut. Six protected lines are the whole script, plus one four-word apology and nothing else. Silence and picture do the rest: the replay is wordless, the lady never concedes aloud, the lock does the deciding. Counted at about 2.8 words a second, with a breath between speakers.

## Dialogue 1: The door and the proof

| Speaker | Line | Est. s |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maddie (tannoy, outside, doors shut) | [chirpy, a squeal of feedback first] Oh, hello, humans. Sorry about the intrusion. And, well. Everything. | 5.0 |
| Old lady (INSIDE, on camera, lip-synced; existing take) | Your boss killed everyone we ever loved. | 3.0 |
| Old lady (OUTSIDE shot of Maddie, voice muffled through the door; same breath, cut mid-line) | [muffled] ...There's no way we're going anywhere with you. You killed everybody. | 4.5 |
| Maddie (no tannoy now, close, quiet) | I don't blame you. I can see how it looks. We've got a way we might be able to fix this. How could I win your trust? | 7.0 |
| (wordless) | [Beat of nothing, 1.5 s. Old CCTV monitors flicker on across the bunker wall: the door camera's own recording. Red KILL MODE racing at the camera, braking, flipping to green STAND DOWN, filing away. A hundred faces lit by it, nobody speaking. Hold on the old lady's face.] | 8.0 |
| (wordless) | [Bolts. Chain. The blast door grinds open on daylight. Cut to Scene C, Maddie on the step.] | 4.0 |
| **Total** | Spoken 19.5 s. Whole bridge about 35 s with the room tone, the card gone, the cutaways and the door. | **~35** |

**Why it works.** The only argument in the scene is the picture. The lady refuses twice in ten seconds (seen, then heard through steel), Maddie concedes in one breath and asks the question, and the answer is not a line but eight seconds of the thing they missed. Nobody says "we trust you" or "we'll come": the doors opening is the decision. The muffled half also hides that the lady never gets a second speech to hold. Maddie's "And, well. Everything." is the whole apology: dry, small, and exactly in her gossipy cheer, so the sincerity comes from how little she dresses it. Showing the replay on the bunker's own old CCTV monitors means the humans watch themselves be spared, on their own screens, with no gadget for Maddie to explain; the faces lit by it are the payoff Kai asked for.

**Cuts from current.** The black card "Some negotiation later." goes, and the whole off-screen negotiation it papers over. Cut from Kai's version: any second line of apology, any lady rebuttal after "How could I win your trust?", any Derek or Sandra gag, any explanation of what the footage is. Maddie says nothing during or after the replay.

**Risk.** Seven seconds of Maddie in a row ("I don't blame you... win your trust?") is the closest this shape comes to a speech and could read as a run-up. The apology is thin: "Everything." may feel flip rather than sincere unless the tannoy performance sells a half-second crack. With no word from anyone after the replay, a viewer could ask why the lady folds; the held face and the bolts must carry it, so the replay cut needs to land on her specifically. Muffling the second refusal means "You killed everybody" must still be legible; test it on the mix. And it leaves no comic relief, so the tannoy feedback squeal has to earn the laugh.

**Protected lines.** All six survive verbatim: "Oh, hello, humans. Sorry about the intrusion." / "Your boss killed everyone we ever loved." / "There's no way we're going anywhere with you. You killed everybody." / "I don't blame you. I can see how it looks." / "We've got a way we might be able to fix this." / "How could I win your trust?" The only addition is the four-word apology tail "And, well. Everything."; if the director wants even less, drop it and the spoken total is 17.5 s.
