# Shape 06: The reversal, they prove it to themselves

Maddie does the humble part (tannoy, apology, concession, the open question) and then does nothing at all. Someone inside works out that the bunker's own door camera, a dead-looking CCTV tape loop nobody has checked in five years, recorded the stand-down, and demands to see it. The proof is not presented, it is requested. Maddie never explains what the footage means, because Derek has already understood it. Pushed to the extreme: Maddie has no line after "How could I win your trust?"

- **Why it works.** The humans make the decision, so the bridge never has to argue it: nobody says "we trust you" because nobody has to, Derek simply goes and checks. It keeps Kai's mechanism (the replay, the hundred lit faces) but turns it from Maddie's evidence into the humans' own detective work, which is more convincing than anything she could say and is faster than any explanation. Maddie's silence after the question is her best moment: she does not plead. Derek gets a grumpy, comic hinge line that has real work to do, and the old lady's "Play it." is her whole concession, stern and unpersuaded about the plan, only about the fact. Because the humans find the footage, it proves exactly one thing: the machine had them and chose not to. Scene D keeps all of its doubt.
- **Cuts from current.** The black card "Some negotiation later." goes, along with any explanatory doorstep talk. Maddie's side of the proof (her beacon, her hatch screen) goes. The old lady's long refusal is cut to the two protected lines.
- **Risk.** (1) It rests on a new fact, a door camera still recording to tape, which has to be planted visually in the bunker establishing shots or it reads as convenient; it also slightly contradicts "she replays the footage" in Kai's note, since Maddie no longer does. Kai may want her hand in it (fix: Maddie's tannoy hiss cuts out and the monitor only then flickers, so her arrival "woke" it). (2) Derek solving it risks making the humans too quick: the line "Play it." must land as reluctant, not won over. (3) Cutting between a screen and a hundred faces in 8 s is tight, and the muffled line must still be intelligible. (4) If the camera has been taping for five years, someone should have looked; Derek's "still taping" needs a shrug of a reason.
- **Protected lines.** All six survive verbatim: "Oh, hello, humans. Sorry about the intrusion." / "Your boss killed everyone we ever loved." / "There's no way we're going anywhere with you. You killed everybody." (fragment audible muffled, the head of the sentence lost behind the door, which is the half-and-half) / "I don't blame you. I can see how it looks." / "We've got a way we might be able to fix this." / "How could I win your trust?"
