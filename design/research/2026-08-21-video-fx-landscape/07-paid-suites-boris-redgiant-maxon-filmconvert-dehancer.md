# The paid plugin suites, compared and priced

## What this covers

Paid effect/transition plugin suites for Premiere Pro 2026: Boris FX (Continuum, Sapphire, Mocha
Pro, Silhouette), Maxon/Red Giant (Universe, Magic Bullet, VFX Suite, Trapcode), FilmConvert
Nitrate, Dehancer, FxFactory, Video Copilot (**After Effects only**), NewBlue Titler Pro, Motion
Array, CoreMelt, proDAD Mercalli — price + date, licence model, Premiere 2026 compatibility,
whether it's a standard Premiere effect (match-name automatable), trial availability. Excludes free
transition packs and native Premiere tooling — other briefs cover those.

## Named tools

### Boris FX Continuum
300+ effects, 40+ transitions (glow, blur, keying, particles). Perpetual $365–$2,195 by host
bundle; subscription $325/yr or $48/mo for 2026.5 (Toolfarm; CG Channel 2026-05; seen 2026-08-21).
Windows/Mac; Premiere, AE, Avid, Resolve, FCP. Native plugin, appears in Effects panel. Industry
standard, actively updated (2026.5 shipped May 2026 with an "AI and FX Editor").

### Boris FX Sapphire
270+ builder-based optical/stylize effects; 2026 added a film-burn effect and Mocha-tracked
whiplash transition. PRICE not published on fetched product/store pages (2026-08-21) — **gap**;
practical route is the Boris FX Suite bundle. Windows/Mac; Premiere, AE, Avid, Resolve, FCP, Nuke.
Native plugin. Free update for existing subscribers (CG Channel, 2025-11).

### Boris FX Mocha Pro (2026.5)
Planar tracker, object removal, roto; 2026.5 adds automatic point tracking and AI matte refinement
(Matte Refine ML) — pitched directly against Premiere's native Object Mask. Perpetual $765 (plugin
only) to $1,645 (standalone+all plugins); subscription $325–$655/yr, $48–$96/mo (Toolfarm/
MotionMedia, 2026-08-21). Windows/Mac; plugin for AE + Premiere CC 2023+. Native plugin. Category
leader for planar tracking.

### Boris FX Silhouette & the Boris FX Suite bundle
Silhouette: node-based roto/paint/comp, the deepest BFX tool — standalone price unconfirmed
(**gap**), normally bought via the Suite. The Suite bundles Sapphire, Continuum, Mocha Pro,
Silhouette, Optics, Particle Illusion Pro, CrumplePop, SynthEyes + audio tools (11 products);
its price also did not resolve from fetched pages (**gap** — needs a direct store check).
Continuum/Mocha/Sapphire/Silhouette are all confirmed Premiere-compatible on the product page
(borisfx.com/products/bfxsuite/, 2026-08-21).

### Red Giant Universe
100+ native transitions/effects (glitch, light leaks, chromatic aberration). **Inconsistent across
Maxon's own pages**: $30/mo or $199/yr on one page (VFXER, 2026-08-21); other pages same day showed
$32/mo, $214/yr, $85/yr-as-part-of-Complete. Cite the exact page, never a bare figure. Windows/Mac;
Premiere, AE, other NLEs. Native plugin. Subscription-only.

### Red Giant / Maxon VFX Suite, Magic Bullet, Trapcode
Three lines under Maxon: VFX Suite (Knoll Light Factory, Composite Wizard); Magic Bullet (Looks,
Denoiser, Cosmo — largely Premiere-compatible); Trapcode (Particular, Form, Mir, Shine) is
**primarily After Effects-native** — per-plugin Premiere support not individually verified here
(gap). Bundled only — Red Giant Complete $599/yr, or Maxon One $1,199/yr with Cinema 4D, Redshift,
ZBrush (maxon.net, 2026-08-21).

### FilmConvert Nitrate
Camera-profile film-stock colour match + grain from real scans. $119 sale/$149 regular (Adobe
single-host), $179 all-platform bundle, $29 upgrade from Pro (filmconvert.com/purchase,
2026-08-21). Perpetual, 3 seats, no GBP shown. Windows/Mac; Premiere, AE, Resolve, FCP. Native
effect. Free trial. Lighter-weight than Dehancer per reviews.

### Dehancer (Lite / Pro)
Multi-stage analogue emulation (stock, grain, bloom, halation, vignette, defringe). Lite $199
subscription-only; Pro $449 perpetual; Studio sub $49/mo ($588/yr); Production $184/mo
(dehancer.com/pricing, 2026-08-21). Lite cannot upgrade to Pro — must exchange for a subscription.
Windows/Mac; Premiere, AE. Native plugin. Most granular emulation in this set per 2025/26 reviews;
steeper curve than FilmConvert.

### FxFactory Pro
Marketplace unlock + first-party catalogue (170+ filters/generators/transitions from many
developers). $199 one-time unlock (fxfactory.com/news, 2026-08-21); individual third-party plugins
sold separately, roughly £20–£200+ each per a 2026 roundup (secondary source, unconfirmed by
vendor). Mac-centric; Premiere, AE, FCP, Motion, Resolve. Installs as native effects.

### NewBlue Titler Pro
Standalone 3D titling app (extrusion, kinetic type, lower-thirds) bridged into Premiere as a
plugin. From $284 for 7.9 (Novedge, 2026-08-21); Elite/Ultimate tier breakdown unconfirmed (gap).
Windows/Mac; Premiere plugin + standalone app.

### Motion Array (Everything plan)
Subscription MOGRT/stock/LUT/plugin library. $24.99/mo annual commitment, $39.99/mo monthly, ~2
bonus months on annual (photutorial.com, 2026-08-21). Cross-platform; MOGRTs import via the
standard Premiere route — the second thing the UXP bridge already drives.

### CoreMelt (Free pack + Lock & Load)
Free pack = 44 transition/text effects. Lock & Load = motion-tracked overlay tool, available for
Premiere. SliceX/DriveX/TrackX/LUTx (mocha-powered) are **FCPX only**, not Premiere
(support.coremelt.com, 2026-08-21). Free pack £0; paid tiers unconfirmed (gap). Mac-centric.

### proDAD Mercalli V6
Frame-by-frame stabilization + rolling-shutter correction with an AI auto-correct ("AiA") mode;
reviewers rate it above Warp Stabilizer on difficult handheld footage (Cutjamm, 2026). Price
unconfirmed in GBP/USD — page routes to a FastSpring checkout (gap). Perpetual, free trial
confirmed on-page. **Windows only** — no Mac version found in this pass.

### Video Copilot (Element 3D, Optical Flares) — AE ONLY
3D renderer and lens-flare generator. Compatibility confirmed directly (videocopilot.net/support/
category/compatibility, 2026-08-21): *"our Plug-ins are only compatible with Adobe After Effects."*
Only Stock FX/Sound FX (pre-rendered assets) work with Premiere. **Not usable** here; the
equivalent look has to come from Continuum Particles or Sapphire's flare/glow builders, or be
pre-rendered elsewhere.

## Automation hook

**Premiere side:** no vendor page found publishes a match-name list for Continuum/Sapphire/Mocha —
treat every third-party effect as **undocumented, discover don't assume**:

```
# UXP/ExtendScript discovery skeleton — UNTESTED (no Premiere host in this environment)
# 1. Install the plugin so it registers with Premiere.
# 2. In a UXP panel / ExtendScript console:
app.project.activeSequence.videoTracks[0].clips[0].components
  .forEach(c => $.writeln(c.matchName));   // or getMatchNames() per the
                                            # Adobe CC Developer Forums thread
# 3. Filter for the vendor prefix (e.g. "BCC" for Continuum); confirm by adding
#    the effect via the UI and re-dumping.
```
Native Premiere effects are prefixed `PR.ADBE.*`, AE-ported ones `AE.ADBE.*` (per the forum thread
in Sources). No shortcut exists until a vendor publishes their own list.

**MOGRT insertion**: no match-name lookup needed — Motion Array templates use the standard
`.mogrt` import path, which the bridge already drives per the brief.

**ffmpeg side** (free equivalents for when a paid plugin isn't installed — run against a synthetic
1376×768 clip in scratchpad, 2026-08-21, `ffmpeg 4.4.2`, libvidstab enabled):

```bash
# Vignette (Sapphire/Continuum vignette builders) — TESTED
ffmpeg -i in.mp4 -vf "vignette=PI/5" out.mp4
# Film grain (Dehancer/FilmConvert grain stage) — TESTED
ffmpeg -i in.mp4 -vf "noise=alls=12:allf=t+u" out.mp4
# Denoise (Neat Video-class cleanup) — TESTED
ffmpeg -i in.mp4 -vf "hqdn3d=4:3:6:4.5" out.mp4
# Stabilization (Mercalli/Warp Stabilizer-class, two-pass) — TESTED
ffmpeg -i in.mp4 -vf vidstabdetect=shakiness=5:result=transforms.trf -f null -
ffmpeg -i in.mp4 -vf vidstabtransform=input=transforms.trf:smoothing=10 out.mp4
```
Not a substitute for Dehancer's stock-matched grain or Mocha's planar tracking — the zero-cost
stand-in when a shot doesn't need suite-grade precision.

## BadCode fit

- Grain/film-look plugins are the highest-value category on near-black 8s AI clips — Veo footage
  is clean by default, and light grain + halation often sells "documentary" over "AI-generated."
  Keep strength low: presets assume daylight footage and will warm BadCode's cool-neutral shadows.
- Mocha's AI matte-refine may be redundant with Premiere 2026's native Object Mask — **not
  verified in this pass**; reach for Mocha only where the native tool fails on a hard edge against
  near-black crush.
- Stabilization matters less here: Flow/Veo clips are camera-simulated, not shaky handheld footage.
  Try native Warp Stabilizer first; budget Mercalli/Mocha only for a specific jittery clip.
- Don't stack film-emulation plugins (Dehancer + FilmConvert + Sapphire grain) on one clip —
  near-black frames band fast under compounded noise. Preview strength cheaply with the ffmpeg
  `noise` skeleton before committing a paid render.
- Trapcode's particle strength (AE-native) is the one real capability gap for dense atmospheric
  particles beyond Continuum's Particles Unit or free Particle Illusion — flagged, not solved.

## Traps

- **Red Giant/Universe pricing is not one number** — five figures ($199/yr, $30/mo, $32/mo,
  $214/yr, $85/yr-in-Complete) turned up across Maxon's own pages. Cite the exact page and date.
- **CEP extensions are actively breaking now**, not eventually — Premiere 2026 no longer natively
  loads legacy CEP panels by default (GitHub issue #571; Hyper Brew's "CEP Migration Clock" post,
  both 2026). No hard deprecation date from Adobe, only informal "several years." Prefer UXP-native
  or match-name automation for anything new.
- **Video Copilot will not run in Premiere at all** — not degraded, absent. Vendor-confirmed.
- **CoreMelt's mocha-powered tools are FCPX-only** despite CoreMelt otherwise supporting Premiere —
  easy to wrongly assume suite-wide compatibility for the planar-tracking tools that matter most.
- **Dehancer Lite can't upgrade to Pro** — a one-way purchase unless exchanged for a subscription.
- **proDAD Mercalli's Mac availability is unconfirmed** — every source implies Windows-only.
- Several exact prices (Boris FX Suite bundle, Silhouette standalone, CoreMelt paid tiers, NewBlue
  Elite/Ultimate) didn't resolve in this pass — treat as unknown, not $0 or "included."
