# Logo sweep — what should the BadCode logo be?

Sixteen research briefs, commissioned 2026-09-29 by Kai, on one question: **what mark is simple,
unmistakably BadCode, and carries the character of a somewhat threatening AI from the future —
without being the curly-brace cliché the current logo turned out to be.**

**Start at [`direction.md`](./direction.md)** — the proposed direction, the decisions owed, and the
hand-over to the design session. It is **proposed, not ruled**. The current logo in
[`docs/brand/`](../../../docs/brand/README.md) stays official until Kai rules.

**See it:** [`sketches/direction-sheet.png`](./sketches/direction-sheet.png) — twelve napkin-quality
sketches on one sheet. Geometry drawn by a script to judge ideas by eye. Not finished design.

## How it was done

- **Sixteen Sonnet agents, one angle each**, run as one workflow. Each was capped at 8 web searches
  because the search allowance is shared across a whole session (about 200). **123 searches used.**
- Fifteen agents were given only the brand description and told to propose their own marks. One
  (brief 15) was given five candidate concepts and told to find who already owns something similar.
- Every finding carries a grade: **verified** (the agent fetched a page that says it), **secondary**
  (a search snippet or a secondary source), or ***(unverified)*** (from the model's own memory).
  Of 191 findings: **122 verified, 60 secondary, 9 from memory.**
- 🔴 **The agents cannot see images.** Every description of someone else's logo came from text.
- 🔴 **No trade mark register was searched.** They all need interactive forms. *Nothing found*
  here means *nothing looked at*.

**They are raw research, not house policy.** Each brief ends with logo ideas that agent invented.
Those are suggestions from one agent, not recommendations. The only file that recommends anything
is `direction.md`.

## What the sweep found, in ten lines

1. **The brace is a cliché and an old one.** A 2012 gallery of curly-brace logos is nearly all
   companies with "code" in the name (02).
2. **A red dot on its own can never be ours.** Leica, Target, Japan, HAL 9000, every record light
   (04, 15). What can be owned is *what the dot is doing*: where it sits, what it is joined to, how
   it moves.
3. **HAL's menace is not in the shape.** It is a real camera lens, holding still, with a calm voice
   (03). Menace is stillness, voice and sound.
4. **An eye says "I am watching you".** That is the message of the things our reader distrusts.
   Frame the light as the warner, not the watcher (04, 05, 13).
5. **Six agents drew the same mark unprompted**: the house blade of light, ending in red
   (01, 04, 08, 10, and cousins in 12 and 16).
6. **The name is a gift.** Its letters are all round, so the logo can be a rule — *colour the O* —
   instead of a drawing (11).
7. **Idents that stuck lock one behaviour** and change everything around it (10).
8. **Plain type is how British authority sounds.** Wide science-fiction capitals are the look of a
   fictional company, and BadCode is not a company (09).
9. **Every AI company's logo is soft, round and glowing.** Flat and hard-edged is empty ground (16).
10. **The reader evidence is thin.** Nobody has measured what a working-class UK viewer makes of any
    logo. The "blunt object" advice is a heuristic to test (13).
