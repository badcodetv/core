# BadCode logo — direction brief

> **Status: PROPOSED 2026-09-29. Not ruled.** Kai (and Jack) choose; nothing here is canon until
> they do. The current logo in [`docs/brand/`](../../../docs/brand/README.md) — the curly brace
> standing in for the C — stays the official one until a ruling replaces it.
>
> **What this file is for:** it is the hand-over to the design session (the `frontend-design`
> skill). It says *what to draw and why*. It does not contain finished design. The pictures in
> [`sketches/direction-sheet.png`](./sketches/direction-sheet.png) are napkin quality: geometry
> drawn by a script to judge ideas by eye.
>
> **Where it came from:** sixteen web-research briefs in this folder ([index](./README.md)), plus
> three rounds of rough sketches. Where a claim below rests on research it says so and names the
> brief. Where it is judgement, it says that too.

---

## 1. The problem, in Kai's words

The logo has to be **simple** and **unmistakably BadCode**. The brace-for-a-C *"feels very obvious
and very cliché"* — every coding tool does it. BadCode is an AI from the future and Kai keeps
seeing *"a Terminator red dot, somewhat threatening"*. It is *not* a friendly, happy character,
and the logo should start leaning into that.

The research agrees with the instinct about the brace. A 2012 gallery of curly-brace logos is
almost entirely companies with "code" in their name, doing exactly what ours does
([brief 02](./02-code-symbol-cliches.md), verified).

## 2. The direction in one line

**A thin line of cold light comes down from the top edge and ends in one red lamp.**

Working name: **End of the Line**. The mark is *the lamp*; the two-second animation is *the drop*.

**The one sentence a founder can say out loud:**

> The line is the road we're on. The red light is what's waiting at the end of it. That's us —
> come back to tell you to get off.

Why that sentence matters: the marks that lasted in UK music all have one. Goldie's for the
Metalheadz skull was *"music will be here long after we're all dead"*
([brief 07](./07-uk-music-label-identity.md), verified).

## 3. Why this is unmistakably BadCode

- **It is the picture we already own.** The house look is a near-black frame with one thin
  vertical blade of cold light ([`docs/images/register-anchor.jpg`](../../../docs/images/register-anchor.jpg)).
  The mark is that picture with one thing added.
- **The one thing added is the thing the film rations.** The plant-room scene keeps every status
  light cool white so that red can *arrive* — *"no red anywhere in the hall until C4"*
  (`docs/stories/gitpush-origin-master/prompts.md`). In our own film language, red is the moment
  it goes wrong.
- **It is the story's diagram.** The fork drawing in the story backbone has the bad branch running
  on to *"the AI, alone"*. The mark is the end of that branch. The website plan already draws the
  line forking, with gold dashes for the branch not yet written — so the logo and the site are one
  system, not two.
- **Six of the sixteen researchers arrived at it on their own.** They were given the brand
  description and told to invent their own marks. Four drew exactly this — a vertical line of
  light with a red dot (briefs 01, 04, 08, 10). Two more drew close cousins (brief 12 in acid
  green, brief 16 with a square red light). That is what "obvious in the good sense" looks like:
  it falls out of the brand, not out of the word "code".
- **Nobody was found using it.** One researcher looked for the nearest existing marks and found no
  brand with a hair-thin line and a red dot ([brief 15](./15-seed-concept-prior-art.md)).
  🔴 That was one search and the researchers cannot see images. It is a good sign, not clearance.

## 4. The red light is a lamp, not an eye

This is the one place the brief bends Kai's instinct, and it is a decision Kai owns (§12, decision 2).

| | An eye | A lamp |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it says | I am watching you | This is a warning |
| Our own canon | Before it arrives the AI has *"no robot, no avatar, no face"*; after it arrives it *"takes a thousand forms and never reuses one"* (`characters/the-ai.md`). One fixed face goes against both. | A point of light is not a face |
| Our reader | Their machine fear is being judged by the welfare algorithm. A watching eye is the thing they already distrust ([brief 13](./13-working-class-uk-vernacular.md)) | The warner, not the watcher |
| Our house rule | *Name the decision-maker, never the technology* ([`the-reader.md`](../../../docs/marketing/the-reader.md)). An eye makes the brand about a scary machine | A lamp points at something |
| Prior owners | HAL 9000, the Terminator, CBS, Big Brother | None found |

**Where the menace comes from instead.** HAL's eye is a real camera lens lit red from behind, and
most of its threat is stillness, a calm voice and context — not the shape
([brief 03](./03-evil-ai-eye-canon.md), verified). The Cylon scanner and KITT's scanner are the
same red light; one is a killer and one is a friendly car (same brief, secondary source). So the menace is carried by: the lamp
holding still, the narrator's voice, the sub-bass, and the meaning *end of the line*.

**Where the care comes from.** The same lamp is a light left on. The story's last image of the bad
branch is *"the hall dark, then one lamp, lit"* (`story.md`). One shape, both halves of the voice:
brutal truth, then love.

**What a stranger sees with no explanation** (judgement, not research): a lamp on a cord, a
builder's plumb line, a pendulum at rest, a warning light. In Britain also the red lamp on the
buffers at the end of a railway track. All of them are fine.

## 5. The mark — the rule, and what to test

**The rule, which is the actual logo:** *draw a line down from the top edge; put a red dot on the
end.* Two strokes. Anyone can redraw it. The peace sign, the anarchist circle-A and the squatters'
symbol are all two to four strokes ([brief 08](./08-marks-that-spread.md) — the researcher's own
count, not a sourced rule), and a rule survives any hand and any font the way a drawing does not
([brief 11](./11-wordmark-error-and-round-letters.md), the Nine Inch Nails lesson, verified).

Things the sketches and the research settled:

| Rule | Why |
| --- | --- |
| The lamp is **attached** to the line. No gap. | With a gap it is an exclamation mark — the most generic warning icon there is. Attached, it is an object. (Sketch sheet 3, cells 1 and 2.) |
| The line **touches the top edge** of whatever it is on. | This is the behaviour that makes a plain shape ours: it always hangs. |
| Always **vertical**. | A horizontal line with a red dot on it is YouTube's progress bar, and our reader lives on YouTube ([brief 15](./15-seed-concept-prior-art.md), recalled, unverified). |
| The line is **never thicker than a quarter of the lamp**, and at least about **2.5 lamps long**. | Thicker or shorter and it reads as a thermometer. (Sketch sheet 3, cells 3 and 14.) |
| **Flat.** No glow, no lens ring, no highlight. | A glowing ring round a red dot is HAL, and soft glowing circles are the shared look of every AI company ([brief 16](./16-ai-brand-tropes.md)). |
| **Symmetrical**, lamp centred on the line. | Off to one side it becomes a lowercase **b** with a red bowl, which is the Beats headphones logo — a music brand. |
| Never a red dot **alone**, and never a dot in the **corner** of a profile picture. | Alone it is Leica, Japan's flag, a record light. In a corner it is a notification badge ([brief 04](./04-red-dot-ownership.md)). |

**Three cuts of the one rule**, the way the current logo already has a heavier favicon:

| Cut | Used for | Starting numbers to test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display | video, sleeves, posters, the site | line about 0.7% of the width; lamp about 12%; lamp centre about two-thirds of the way down |
| Badge | profile pictures, stickers | line about 5%; lamp about 21% |
| Favicon | 16 and 32 pixels | line at least 1.5 pixels; lamp at least 14% of the canvas, probably a close-up crop |

The 14% and 1.5-pixel figures come from [brief 10](./10-idents-and-small-sizes.md) and are that
researcher's rule of thumb, not a standard. Profile pictures are cropped to a circle almost
everywhere, so the lamp sits inside the middle 65% (the same researcher's arithmetic). The line is
allowed to run off the top — that is the point of it. Spotify does not allow text in an artist
picture (verified), so the profile picture is the symbol alone.

## 6. The name — one rule

**The O is the lamp.** Set BADCODE, make the O a solid red disc. In the full lock-up the line comes
down and lands in it.

- The name has exactly one O, and it is the fifth letter of seven — off-centre, which is better
  than centred.
- It is a rule, not a drawing. A fan can type the name in any font, colour the O, and it is ours.
- Nearest neighbours: Mobil's wordmark has a red letter o. Ours is a solid disc with a line on it.

**The voice of the letters is an open decision (§12, decision 3).** Recommended: **heavy, plain,
condensed capitals** — a warning plate, a van door, a masthead.

| | Blunt capitals (recommended) | Round lowercase |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Feels like | a notice, a nameplate | a modern brand |
| For it | The lamp is the only round thing among hard letters. Plain type is how British authority sounds: the *Protect and Survive* booklet was Futura Extra Bold Condensed and Helvetica, and the menace was in the voice ([brief 09](./09-sinister-institutions.md), verified). | The word "badcode" is made of nothing but round letters — seven rings in a row. It is a lovely accident. |
| Against it | Italic or on a red block it becomes a tabloid masthead. | Round lowercase at a medium weight reads as friendly start-up, charity or government app ([briefs 11](./11-wordmark-error-and-round-letters.md), [13](./13-working-class-uk-vernacular.md) — their judgement, untested). Kai asked for *not friendly*. |

**Do not use** wide, stretched science-fiction capitals (Eurostile, Microgramma). That is
Weyland-Yutani, Star Trek and Severance — the look of a fictional *company*, and BadCode is a mind,
not a company ([brief 09](./09-sinister-institutions.md)).

## 7. The ident — one behaviour, every time

Black. The line drops from the top (about 0.6 seconds). A beat of nothing. The lamp lights on the
sub-bass drop. Hold.

- Channel 4, BBC Two and MTV all lock **one** behaviour and change everything around it
  ([brief 10](./10-idents-and-small-sizes.md), verified). The still logo is this animation paused.
- The sound should be a **gesture**, not a tune. The one study found (90 real sound logos, 1,860
  people) says a more complex melody is easier to recognise and harder to sing back, so the two
  pull against each other. For an ident that only has to be recognised, one swelling gesture like
  THX's is the safer bet (same brief; the conclusion is the researcher's).
- The same two seconds can be played as menace (the line *strikes*) or as care (the lamp is *lit*).
  That is a performance choice per film, which suits a narrator with two registers.
- 🔴 Short-form platforms often replace the audio. The sound will mostly be heard on long videos
  and on the records.

## 8. The system — the dot does jobs

One red dot, and it is always doing something. The constant is the dot, the near-black, and the
cold line. This follows the story canon: after it arrives, BadCode *"takes a thousand forms and
never reuses one."*

| The dot… | Where | Why it earns its place |
| --- | --- | --- |
| hangs on the line | the mark | §2 |
| stands in for the O | the name | §6 |
| lands on a thing as a **SOLD** sticker | inside the films and posts | In a gallery a red dot beside a work means it has been bought ([brief 04](./04-red-dot-ownership.md), verified). Stuck on a water company, a care home, a playing field, it names *what was sold*. This is the house rule made visible: never raise the temperature without aiming the flame. |
| ends the sentence | captions, end cards | A full stop. Total authority. |

**The way out.** On the site the line forks before it reaches the lamp, and the other branch runs
as gold dashes (`#e8c98a`, already the site's signal colour). The mark on its own is the warning;
the fork is the rest of the message. 🔴 The reader rules say *injustice, not inevitability* — so
the mark is never captioned as doom, and wherever there is room the fork is shown with it.

## 9. Colour

| | Value | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lamp red | about `#e6291c` | Warmer than the current crimson `#cc2b37`, and clearly not Labour's pinkish red (`#e4003b` — that value is from memory, check it). Contrast on near-black is about 4.5 to 1 (my arithmetic, not a measurement on a screen). |
| Cold light | about `#eef3f6` | The line and the letters. Not pure white. |
| Ground | about `#050607` | Near-black. |
| Gold | `#e8c98a` | Existing. Only for the branch that is still open. |

- **Blue goes.** That closes the open risk recorded in the brand README: red and blue are the two
  main UK party colours.
- **Red is always a small light, never a background.** Twelve of the sixteen researchers raised
  the party or tabloid reading of red. The escape, in [brief 13](./13-working-class-uk-vernacular.md)'s
  words: make the red *a small physical light on black, never a background field or a red-and-blue
  split*. None of this has been tested on a reader.
- 🔴 Red on black is dim for some colour-blind viewers. Test it. The white line is what locates it.

## 10. Never

Collected from the sixteen briefs. Each one is either someone else's mark or a cliché.

- Curly braces, angle brackets, a terminal prompt, a blinking cursor, binary, glitch effects.
- A centred red dot in a black square or circle with nothing else (HAL).
- A red ring round a dot; concentric circles (HAL, Target).
- A skull or a robot head (Metalheadz owns it in drum & bass).
- The letters B and C as a monogram, or mirrored letters round a bar (the drum & bass group
  Bad Company's logo is exactly that — [brief 01](./01-name-collisions.md), verified).
- A lowercase b with a round red bowl (Beats).
- A clock face, a hand at midnight, an hourglass (the Doomsday Clock — which, as of January 2026,
  names AI among its reasons; [brief 14](./14-time-based-marks.md), verified).
- A triangle with anything in it (hazard sign; at small sizes an error icon or the all-seeing eye).
- Yellow and black hazard stripes (the Haçienda did it in 1982; it now sells flats).
- The lowercase name inside a circle (Paul Rand's ABC logo — and our letters are a, b, c, d, e).
- A vinyl record standing in for the O.
- A soft glowing ring, a sparkle, a gradient (every AI company).

## 11. The alternatives

**B — The Look.** A ring with a red pupil pushed to the edge: a machine eye that glances sideways
instead of staring. It has the most character of anything sketched, and no brand was found using
an averted pupil. Against it: it is an eye (§4); it can read as cute, or as the side-eye emoji; at
thumbnail size it is close to HAL. *If Kai wants a face, this is the one.* It could also live as a
rare behaviour inside direction A rather than as the mark.

**C — The Notice.** No symbol at all. Plain heavy type, a red full stop, and everything numbered
like paperwork. The lineage is Factory Records, where the catalogue number *was* the brand and a
lawsuit got one too ([brief 07](./07-uk-music-label-identity.md)). Against it: a wordmark is weak
as a profile picture, and the references are loved by designers, not by our reader. *Best used as
the supporting layer under A*: the stamp on every release.

## 12. Decisions owed — Kai rules

1. **Direction.** A (End of the Line), B (The Look), C (The Notice), or a mix. *Recommended: A,
   with C's numbering as its supporting layer.*
2. **Lamp or eye.** Is the red light a warning lamp or a watching eye? *Recommended: lamp* (§4).
3. **Voice of the name.** Blunt capitals or round lowercase. *Recommended: blunt capitals* (§6).
4. **Colour.** Warm lamp red, and blue retired. *Recommended: yes to both* (§9).
5. **The SOLD sticker.** Adopt it as a device in the films. *Recommended: yes — it is the most
   political thing on this page.*
6. **"8 BC".** A date stamp counting years *Before Collapse* (2034 in the story), with BC also
   being our initials. No earlier use was found ([brief 14](./14-time-based-marks.md)).
   *Recommended: park it.* It reads as Before Christ until explained; a profile picture that
   changes every January breaks recognition; a fixed doom date has a shelf life (the Terminator
   films moved theirs four times); and the site already uses `BC-000` as a catalogue number.
7. **Does Jack get a say before design starts?** He is the lead creative designer.

## 13. Free things hiding in the name — parked, not part of the logo

Nice to know. None of them is the mechanism, because our reader is not a coder and will not decode
any of it ([brief 12](./12-hexspeak-and-colour.md), risk rated high).

- **The name is a number.** `BADC0DE` with a zero is valid hexadecimal. A relative of it sits in an
  old Linux kernel list (verified). It is not an official error code and must not be called one.
- **The name is a colour.** `#BADC0D` is a real colour code: an acid yellow-green in the tennis-ball
  and hi-vis family. It is loud and it is free. It also fights the near-black look, prints as dull
  olive without a special ink, and next to red it fails for the roughly one man in twelve who is
  red-green colour-blind (the usual figure; not checked here). *Sticker and special-edition use
  at most.*
- **Seven letters, seven bits.** Light only the fifth of seven and you have written the old
  control code for *End of Transmission*. The O is the fifth letter. Nobody needs to know. *(My own
  arithmetic — not from the research.)*

## 15. What the evidence cannot carry

- **Nobody has measured what this reader thinks of any logo.** The "blunt object, not clever
  symbol" advice rests on football fans revolting against minimalist club crests (Leeds, 2018) and
  on reading the culture. It is a heuristic to test on real people, not a finding.
- **The science of threatening shapes is weak.** Downward-pointing angles, watching eyes and the
  colour red all have mixed or failed replications ([brief 05](./05-threat-perception-science.md)).
  Use it to break a tie between two drawings. Never quote it to justify one.
- **The researchers could not see pictures.** Every description of someone else's logo came from
  text.
- **"No earlier use found" means one search.**

## 16. For the design session

Build a tuner, the way the brace logo was tuned by hand in round four:

1. The mark with sliders: line weight, lamp size, how far down the lamp hangs, the red.
2. Live previews beside it at 128, 64, 32 and 16 pixels, in a circle crop, on a dark app and a
   light one.
3. The name in both voices (blunt capitals, round lowercase) with the line landing in the O.
4. The two-second ident, playing.
5. A sticker on white, and a sleeve corner with the line running to the top edge.
6. One panel that shows the mark drawn badly, because that is how it will travel.

Then generate the files from the chosen settings with a script, the way
`scripts/brand/build-logo.mjs` does now, and update `docs/brand/README.md` only after Kai rules.
