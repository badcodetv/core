# Shape 10: Ending first, work backwards

I wrote the last ten seconds first and let each earlier beat exist only to make that ending inevitable. The ending is a sentence cut off mid-number by the flatline tone; silence from 11 s means the viewer finishes the sentence themselves. Working backwards, the minister's line arrives while the crew are still shouting numbers, and the red-light stop is the one moment the crew's words are about the stop, not the patient. The opening plants "BP 60 over 30" so the cut-off figure reads as a fall from it.
