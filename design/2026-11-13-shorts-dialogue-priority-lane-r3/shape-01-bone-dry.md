# Shape 01: Bone-dry minimum

Cut every line to the fewest words that still carry its beat: one clipped, shoutable phrase per beat, no two crew lines in one breath, and the only full sentence is the minister's. Medical language is reduced to the one number the audience already knows from hospital dramas (BP), and everything else is carried by siren, monitor and the red light. Spoken words are about 5 s, which leaves the flatline and the silence before it to do the work.

## Dialogue 1: Priority Lane round 3, the whole short

| Speaker | Line | Est. s |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PARAMEDIC 1 | [shouting over siren] BP 60 over 30! We're losing him! | 2.0 |
| DRIVER | Move! | 0.4 |
| PARAMEDIC 2 | [at the windscreen, disbelieving] Why's he stopping us?! | 1.0 |
| MINISTER | [on the phone, through the glass] I said raspberry-shaped hors d'oeuvres! | 2.4 |
| MONITOR | [flatline tone, no words, 3 s of silence from the crew] | 0.0 |
| **Total** | | **5.8** |

**Why it works.** Five seconds is enough to believe a man is dying if the audience has seen a hundred ER scenes: "BP 60 over 30" is the number, "losing him" is the stake, and the monitor sells the rest. "Move!" is one syllable the driver can scream over a siren, so it reads as a reflex rather than a line. The crew never explain the red light; the raised glove is on screen and the paramedic's "Why's he stopping us?!" is a question, not an exposition, which lets the outrage belong to the viewer. The minister's line then arrives into a gap with nothing competing with it, so the triviality is louder than any amount of crew dialogue would have made it. The final three seconds contain no words at all, so the audience supplies "he's going to die" themselves, which is what Kai wants. No moral, no narrator.

**Cuts from current.** "we're gonna" becomes "we're" (faster to shout); "Come on, get out of the way!" becomes "Move!"; "What's he doing?!" becomes "Why's he stopping us?!" (one more word, but it names the outrage: they are being stopped, not just delayed). No swearing in this version, which is the edge to test: Kai's swearing ("get the f*** out of the way", "f***ing hurry up") is dropped entirely to see whether the shorter, cleaner shout is stronger. Also cut: oxygen dropping, sats, "look, we're gonna lose him, hurry up".

**Risk.** Kai asked for the swearing and for the crew to feel like heroes in trouble; a bone-dry cut may feel under-written and too polite, and "Move!" alone may not read as the driver's desperation without the sweary escalation. With so few words, the 0-5 s window might not establish "dying" if the Suno voices are not shouty enough, and the whole short rests on the monitor and the acting. Suno speech mode may also slur a 0.4 s line. "Why's he stopping us?!" may be read as the crew being angry at the outrider rather than at the system, which is fine. Fallback: restore "get the f*** out of the way" for the driver (about 1.3 s) and the total is still under 7 s.

**Protected lines.** "I said raspberry-shaped hors d'oeuvres!" survives verbatim. "BP 60 over 30" survives verbatim inside the first line.
