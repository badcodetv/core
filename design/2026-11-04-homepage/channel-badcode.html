Channel BadCode

The whole website is a television. You switch it on, and BadCode has already taken over the signal.

This TV works. Press the red button on the remote to switch it on, change channels with the numbers, press TEXT for teletext, GUIDE for the listings. Keyboard: 0–4, arrows, T, G, M. On a phone the screen fills the width and the remote sits below it. Under the set are all the ideas for growing this, to vote on. The stills are frames from the films; the figures are examples.
