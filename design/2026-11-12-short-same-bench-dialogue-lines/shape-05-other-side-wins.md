# Shape 05: The other side wins

The fat cat never moves; the veteran and the nurse do. Each one opens with a modest ask, hears "Forget it.", and concedes something, bargaining themselves down against a man who has already won. He sets the terms in both eras with the same two words, so the echo is the joke, and the concessions get smaller and more absurd. The button is the extreme end: the next person sits down and concedes before anyone speaks.

## Dialogue 1: Same Bench: the whole short

| Speaker | Line | Est. s |
| --- | --- | --- |
| VETERAN (1919) | A pension. For the leg. | 1.5 |
| FAT CAT | Forget it. | 1 |
| VETERAN (1919) | [lowering the crutch] Keep the leg, then. | 2 |
| NURSE (today) | A pay rise. Just the one. | 1.5 |
| FAT CAT | Forget it. | 1 |
| NURSE (today) | [softly] ...A clap? | 1.5 |
| NEXT PERSON (silhouette, sits before speaking, hat in hands) | [silent] | 1 |
| FAT CAT [not looking up, unaged] | Forget it. | 1 |
| CAPTION | SAME BENCH. SAME MAN. | 2 |
| **Total** | | **12.5** |

- **Why it works.** The ask shrinks each time, so we see the people who carried the cost bargaining against themselves while the fat cat gives up nothing. "Keep the leg, then" is the dark punchline of beat A, and "A clap?" calls back to the thanks that was the only thing they were ever given, so beat B lands as the same joke with the same loser. The button is a silent action: a third figure sits down and concedes by posture, and the fat cat says "Forget it." before a word is spoken. That is the same-man-every-time point with no moral stated, and the contempt lands on the decision-maker and never on the soldier or nurse. Alternate buttons: (a) he says "Forget it." to the camera, as if it were us; (b) a caption only, "Thanked. Then forgotten. Same man."; (c) his top hat swaps for a modern cap, the face stays identical, with no line.
- **Cuts from current.** The bare repeated "Forget it." as the only content is gone, since the victims now speak and bargain. The open-ended button becomes a specific silent action plus a caption.
- **Risk.** "Forget it." is spoken three times and may feel like padding if the TTS reads it identically; it needs the same flat, bored read each time for the echo to work. "Keep the leg, then." could read as the soldier being too sardonic for a victim; the line may need to be played as defeat, not wit. The silent third figure needs a clear visual (hat in hands) or the button is lost. 12.5 s leaves 1.5 s for a pause before the caption.
- **Protected lines.** "Forget it." survives verbatim, spoken three times by the fat cat (twice as the reply, once as the button), and never altered.
