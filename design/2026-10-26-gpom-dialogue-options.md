## 5. Open questions for Kai

1. Is the kill-bot explosion audible or visible from the bunker? Drafts 2A, 2B and 2C lean on it ("He blew up his own bots"); if the lady can't know it, we need a different trust line.
2. Should "consciousness" be spoken in the intro? All three intros avoid the word and use "nobody there to notice / nobody's home"; the doorstep's "You're the conscious ones" then names it for the first time.
3. Do you want the whisper to be a quotation of the AI ("As he says... [ahem]") or Maddie's own promise? Only 2A keeps "As he says"; it costs about 1.5 s.
4. Is the ending allowed a button after the slam (biscuits, humming), or silent roll-away only? 2C offers it; 2A and 2B are silent.
5. Is a new voice take acceptable (Sandra for 2B), and is the doorframe tally drawable in the bunker door shot?
