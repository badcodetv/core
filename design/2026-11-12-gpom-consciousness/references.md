## The four

### Donald D. Hoffman

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Who | Professor of Cognitive Sciences, University of California, Irvine (checked, his UCI page; the page does not say whether he is emeritus) |
| Known for | *The Case Against Reality* (2019). Perception is a user interface, not a window on the world. "Conscious realism": consciousness is fundamental and physical objects are interface features (checked, UCI news and the Edge essay) |
| His challenge, in his words | "There's not a single theory that's been proposed that can explain even one specific conscious experience." His examples in that essay: "the taste of chocolate or the smell of garlic", and chocolate against vanilla (checked: "Realism Is False", Edge, 2020) |
| **Mint** | **Not found in his own words.** It turns up in second-hand podcast summaries only. Treat mint as Maddie's example. Never caption it as his or as a quote. |
| Link | https://sites.socsci.uci.edu/~ddhoff/ (checked, loads) · the essay: https://www.edge.org/node/28195 |
| Honest note | A minority position. A 2022 paper in *Synthese* argues his scepticism defeats itself (repo). |

### Federico Faggin

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Who | Physicist and engineer. Led the design of the Intel 4004, the first commercial microprocessor (1971); founded Zilog (the Z80) and Synaptics (repo; the 4004 confirmed in this pass). National Medal of Technology and Innovation, 2009 (repo; this pass only saw "US national medal") |
| Known for here | *Irreducible: Consciousness, Life, Computers, and Human Nature* (2024, published by Essentia Books). Consciousness is an irreducible property of nature; a computer cannot have it (checked via reviews and summaries; **no verbatim quote was verified**, so paraphrase only) |
| What he actually proposes | With the physicist Giacomo Mauro D'Ariano: "quantum-information-based panpsychism" (checked, Closer To Truth's summary; repo). That is not the same view as Hoffman's or Kastrup's. |
| Link | https://www.fagginfoundation.org/ (the Federico and Elvia Faggin Foundation, founded 2011; the domain is confirmed, its `/about` page returned "not found" on 12 Nov, so check the exact page before printing) |
| Honest note | Towering as an engineer; no standing in philosophy of mind. Reviewers say the hard problem is moved, not solved (repo; *Philosophy Now* review seen in this pass). |
| The line it earns | The man who designed the first microprocessor says the chip can't have this. True as a description of his position. Do not put words in his mouth. |

## The thing a critic will spot first

These are not four independent voices.

- Kastrup runs Essentia.
- Faggin's book is published by Essentia Books.
- Hoffman sits on Essentia's academic advisory board (repo).

So "four names" is close to one school. They also hold three different theories. Doctrine already
grades this: tier D, "serious minds, not settled science", and the refutation page says agreement
among them is not evidence.

## Do not

- Do not have Maddie say any living person's name.
- Do not write "scientists have shown", "science now accepts" or "leading scientists".
- Do not call the four "physicists" or "neuroscientists". Hoffman is a cognitive scientist, Faggin
  an engineer and physicist by training, Kastrup a philosopher and computer engineer.
- Do not quote any of them unless the exact words are on a page you have open.
- Do not imply they have seen, helped with or endorsed the film. Say "not affiliated" in the description.
