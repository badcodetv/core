**The wank tank is a black BMW X8, and Flow prompts must say so.** Name the marque and
the model, and describe the blacked-out kidney grille that identifies it. Do not
anonymise it into "a large black luxury SUV".

**Why:** the car is a character, not set dressing — the disparaging nickname *is* the
point, and the joke needs the audience to recognise the specific vehicle. Camping's
prompts otherwise carry a blanket *"no badges, brand names or readable lettering"* rule
(see [[always-new-prompt-never-edit-an-image]] and the ledger conventions), and applying
that rule to the car strips the thing that makes it read. **The car is the exception.**

**How to apply — every shot, inside and out.** **Any time Tarquin is in a car, the car is
the BMW X8 and the prompt names it** *(extended 2026-08-26)*. That covers scene 5's drive
home, scene 8 at the tent, and the ruined clamped variant in scenes 10–12.

- **Exteriors:** *"a blacked-out BMW X8"* — slab sides, the huge blacked-out double kidney
  grille, dark alloys, privacy glass.
- **Interiors:** name it too, and give the cabin its identifying furniture — the wide curved
  digital display sweeping from the driver across the dash, the thick flat-bottomed wheel
  with the round BMW badge at the centre of the hub, the faceted crystal-effect gear
  selector and rotary controller on the console, thin illuminated trim. **The badge on the
  wheel hub is explicitly allowed to be visible** — it is the only thing that identifies the
  car from inside.

Keep the no-lettering rule for everything else in the frame, including every OTHER number plate.

**🔑 Exception ruled 2026-09-17 (Jack): the X8's own plate READS `BAD C0DE`.** It's the film's BadCode reveal, planted at the Shard (5a) and when he parks (8a), and paid off at the end with the car dead and clamped in the rain. Quote the string and spell it character by character. Tracker: `docs/stories/camping/prompts.md` § *The BAD C0DE plate*.

**⚠️ Naming it is not enough — BMW never built an X8** (the range is X1–X7 plus the XM, and
the X8 was only ever a rumour). The name has no referent, so the engine returns a generic big
black SUV. **Always pair the name with the shape:** the enormous vertical stacked double-kidney
grille filling an upright nose, split headlights (thin LED daytime-running strips high on the
wings with separate main units below), a tall flat front, a long slab flank and a shallow
greenhouse. And frame it so the **front three-quarter** is visible — a car driving away shows
only its tailgate, the least identifiable angle there is.
