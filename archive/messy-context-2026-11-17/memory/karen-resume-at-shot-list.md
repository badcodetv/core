The **Karen Will Lead the Revolution** film has an assembly-order shot list at
`docs/stories/karen/shot-list.md`, with a **"Next session — start here"** block at the top.
As of 2026-08-20 that block owes: **1.4e** (*"Can I speak to your manager?"*), **1.4f** (the
laptop-cam 180° reverse, *"Sorry mate — you too"*), **1.7a** (Karen wakes), **1.7c** (the call
from the box), and **re-pinning the phone-box location to the new subway-entrance spot**.

**Why:** Kai asked for these to survive the session boundary, and the shot list is the only
document that holds the film in *cut* order — `story.md` holds canon, the `prompts-act*.md`
files hold prompt history, neither says what to shoot next.

**How to apply:** at the start of a Karen session, read that block before anything else, and
tick items off it as they're fired. The phone-box re-pin propagates to every act that shows the
corner (Act 2's box, Act 3's stairs and crowd, Act 5's statue). Related:
[[flow-hand-driven-not-automated]], [[every-flow-prompt-ends-with-thanks]].
