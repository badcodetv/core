Don't composite AI characters into real footage (a real plate with an AI person) for Bank Robbery or similar.

**Why:** Jack, 2026-10-18, after three rounds: "forget all of this real footage with ai stuff, it doesnt work at all. when i say that i dont mean real footage in general, i just mean comnbing that with ai."

**How to apply:**
- Real footage on its own is welcome (archive, ECB inserts).
- AI shots on their own are welcome.
- Join them by the cut only: never in the same frame.
- For machines and processes Flow can't render (printing presses), use the real footage for the machine and keep the AI characters in their own shots, lit or sounded by it.
