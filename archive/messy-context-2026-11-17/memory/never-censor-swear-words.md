Never censor swear words anywhere: repo docs, narration scripts, canon files, or
chat replies. Write `fuck`, `cunt`, `shit`, `bollocks` in full. Jack, 2026-09-03:
*"dont censer swear words in any of it"*.

**Why:** BadCode's register is working-class UK and the profanity is load-bearing
(see [[the-reader-is-working-class-uk]]). An asterisked spelling softens a line
that canon wrote hard, and it propagates — camping's narration had `hell`
substituted for `fuck` purely because Suno's filter demanded it, and the
softening then outlived Suno.

**How to apply:** If a generation engine actually filters a word, substitute a
different real word and record why — never an asterisked spelling. When quoting
canon back to Jack, quote it uncensored.
