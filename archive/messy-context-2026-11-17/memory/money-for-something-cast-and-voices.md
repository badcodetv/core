On 2026-10-03 Jack ruled **"split the roles"** for [[money-for-something-game-show]]: the voice over real clips is **the MFS narrator** (its own AI Studio voice, recorded in `docs/stories/magic-money-tree/sound.md` — not a BadCode-wide narrator; no other story inherits it); the host and the guests speak only on camera, with voices made in Flow. He asked for voices that "sound how they look".

- **Flow Characters with custom voices** in project `cb27208c`; ids, performance texts and portraits: `docs/stories/magic-money-tree/money-for-something.md` § "The cast and their voices in Flow" and `look.md` § Cast.
- **Room reference:** see [[money-for-something-resume]].

**Why:** two voice engines would give the host two voices; making the clip voice a separate person removes the problem.

**How to apply:** [[never-dialogue-in-flow-videos]] still stands for every other film; for this show on-camera speech in Flow is allowed. One speaker per shot, short lines (unverified community advice). Jack, 2026-10-03: "I like the voices"; 2026-10-04: "the voice is fine". The show's prompt rules (sterling not dollars, no audience words) are in MMT's `look.md`. Attaching a voice by hand: `docs/flow/automation-2026-09-rebuild.md` items 37 to 41.
