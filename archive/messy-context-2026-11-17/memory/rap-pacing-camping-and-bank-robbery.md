Jack, 2026-09-24, on Camping: *"never do double time with the rapping, remember that please."* The Bank Robbery song carries the same rule.

**Scope:** Camping and The Bank Robbery only. It is not a BadCode rule: another story's song may want a fast flow, and that story's `sound.md` decides (Magic Money Tree's jump-up song asks for one).

**How to apply:** for either story, read *Rules for this story* in `docs/stories/camping/sound.md` or `docs/stories/bank-robbery/sound.md` (the Exclude list and the summoning words to strip are there). Related: [[no-house-style-story-files]], [[a-ban-loses-to-a-positive-instruction]].
