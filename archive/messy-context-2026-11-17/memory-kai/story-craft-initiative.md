exhaustively and kept as a skill — like suno-prompt/flow-prompt — then applied as an adversarial
review of GitPush Origin Master *without changing the arc, backbone or conclusion*.

re-derived per story. The rulings (e.g. the moral is never stated, the beneficiary always is; tell
the audience about the bomb; irony decodes through prior belief → pair every sarcastic line with a
consequence) are synthesis rulings marked overturnable by Kai/Jack.
