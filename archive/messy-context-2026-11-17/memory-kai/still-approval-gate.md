On any Flow scene work, generate stills, show Kai **every candidate**, and wait for his
verdict before spending a single video credit. "Accept exactly one as the plate" means *he*
accepts it — not me. Confirmed by him 2026-08-22: *"it's really good, this process, of us
making images before we generate videos. That's working."*

**Why:** on GPOM cut 4 I generated plates, picked one myself and went straight to video — and
shot an entire beat of the wrong scene (a dead street, which was scene 8's image, for scene
7's crowd-watching-the-news). The still-first rule in [[flow-automation-integration]] was
already written down; what was missing was the *human* in the gate. A wrong plate costs one
cheap image to catch and a full video round to discover.

**How to apply:** build a contact sheet per subject (2x2 for a/b candidates), state my pick
and my reasoning, name every defect I can see, then stop. Reordering, relocating and
re-scoping all happen at the still stage — Kai has redrawn cut 4's shape three times off
stills alone, each time for the price of a few images.
