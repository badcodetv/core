Kai, 2026-10-23: "we're starting to get a really useful scene editor app put together just in the form of skills." The layers are: **overview-board** (the whole film: timelines.json → overview page, skeleton / status / reshape / keep current) → **new-scene** (one part: brief → boards → clips → timeline page) → **provenance** (cards, trail.json, final.json). Skill: .claude/skills/overview-board/SKILL.md.

**Why:** Kai wants to initialise the skeleton shape of a whole story, then go into each scene and edit its timeline.

**How to apply:** a new film starts with a skeleton timelines.json (every part "not made", each with a one-line `beat`). "Let's work on <part>" hands that part to new-scene, with its beat as the stage-1 sentence. Every timeline change → edit timelines.json, rebuild, republish to the same URL.
