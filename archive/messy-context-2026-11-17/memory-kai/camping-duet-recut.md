The "Camping" track was re-cut as a **true duet** and **accepted 2026-08-21 at round 17**.
The released take used a saved Voice, so Bob and Tarquin came out sounding like the same man.
Settled, pushed, and not to be reopened without a fault someone can name in a take.

**The three files in `docs/stories/camping/songs/`:** `camping.md` is the accepted prompt
sheet (four boxes + settings + §4a–§4f reasoning); `camping-released.md` is the published take
and the **lyric-fidelity reference**; `camping-prompt-history.md` is the 17-round log with the
summary table and previous cue sets as revert targets.

**Two things to know before touching it.** The sheet is **no longer verbatim** — Kai made
three deliberate word changes at round 17, listed in §4, and one of them (dropping `fast
start`) costs the setup for `the AI does the fast part`. And §4 carries a **verification
script that must be run after any lyric edit**: three words once drifted from the released
lyric and nobody heard it across nine rounds of close listening.

**Why:** the words are what Jack's video is cut to, so lyric fidelity is a hard constraint
that the ear demonstrably cannot police.

**How to apply:** treat the sheet as frozen; if a re-cut ever happens, the first thing to try
is splitting `in having the manager / or any of the c-suite about` — 15 syllables in an
11-syllable bridge slot, documented as split but never actually applied.
