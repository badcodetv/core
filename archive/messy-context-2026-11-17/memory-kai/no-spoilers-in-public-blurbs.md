Public-facing story descriptions and pictures must not spoil the story: setup and hook only, never
the turn, the twist or the ending.

**Why:** Kai, 2026-10-22, on the badcode.tv blurbs: Camping's "five years on, he's waking up in the
tent next door" and Karen's "all the way to the top" gave the stories away. He also swapped Camping's
camp-fire still for the car-park argument for the same reason.
**How to apply:** for each blurb or still, ask "does this tell them where it goes?" Stop at the
inciting moment. Canon summaries (ep1.md etc.) are internal and may spoil. Related: [[first-release-four-stories]].
