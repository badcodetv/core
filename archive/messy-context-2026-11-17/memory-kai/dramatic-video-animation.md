**Scope (2026-10-28):** this is GPOM's direction, recorded in `docs/stories/gitpush-origin-master/look.md`. Another story animates however its own look.md says ([[no-house-style]]).

When turning GPOM plates into Veo video, make the animation **dramatic** — a dive through cloud, a
storm with a lightning flash, a hard push into the glass — not "vague small animation" (twinkling
lights, drifting haze, faint flicker on a locked camera).

**Why:** Kai, 2026-09-13, watching the gentle pass on scene 2's descent ladder: *"when we do videos
here we should make them dramatic… at the moment we are trying to kind of get some vague small
animation going."* The hybrid-method default (camera locked, subtle world motion) produced correct
but inert clips.

**How to apply:** offer dramatic options per shot up front. Keep the hybrid-method safety where it
earns it — a glass curtain wall in a moving Veo camera still regenerates (push-a/push-b both did
after ~3–4s), so either lock the camera and make the WORLD dramatic (storm, fog, lightning) or let
Veo move and cut before it breaks. Where there's no repeating grid (aerials, cloud), let Veo move
the camera. Relates to [[hybrid-video-method]] and [[still-approval-gate]].
