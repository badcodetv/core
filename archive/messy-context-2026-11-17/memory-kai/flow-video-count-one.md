Generate Flow video at `count: 1` by default. On 2026-09-28 I re-rolled the gaze night-site clip (`25-f3-a`) at count 2 on my own judgement; Kai asked where the "two clips" came from, and noted both takes were basically the same.

**Why:** a second candidate doubles the credits, and on a sealed plate with a locked camera the two takes come back near-identical — the choice isn't worth buying.

**How to apply:** count 1 unless Kai asks for options, or it's the first roll of a genuinely new setup and I say up front that I'm buying two. The flow-automation skill already says "count 1 unless you are buying a choice" — lean harder on the 1. Related: [[still-approval-gate]].
